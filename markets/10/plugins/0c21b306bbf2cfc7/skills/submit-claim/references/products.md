## Unresolved commercial terms

These points are not approved terms or guarantees. Confirm them with Delos before quoting them; do not infer an answer from indicative pricing or the product description.

- **collections-partial-recovery-fee** (Collections, Portfolio collections): How the success fee applies to partial recovery is to be confirmed in the agreed terms. Explain the indicative fee and that payment terms follow review.
- **factoring-fee-period-and-timing** (Factoring): The precise fee start, end, cap and overdue treatment, and a universal funding time, have not been confirmed. The quote confirms the fee period and charges; do not promise 48-hour funding or an automatic 90-day rule.
- **payment-protection-final-fee** (Payment protection): 0.5–2% of invoice value is an indicative fee for securing the purchase commitment. The exact commitment fee and any factoring charges are confirmed after contract review. The agreed Certificate sets the fee schedule and invoice purchase terms; do not assume the ordinary Factoring daily rate applies.
- **lien-quoted-scope** (Lien filing): From $150 is the confirmed starting price. Included work, filing charges and additional costs require a quote. MCP lien intake and uploads remain paused.
- **claim-sale-servicing-fee-and-purchase-settlement** (Claim sale, Portfolio sale): Recovery fees and the included work are agreed before collection starts. Do not import the standalone Collections rate, assume an offset against the purchase price or calculate a guaranteed net payout. Any offer and agreement determine the purchase terms and treatment of prior recoveries.

## Payment protection

After reviewing your contract, Delos agrees in advance to buy future invoices on agreed terms. If you aren’t paid, you already have a buyer: you can sell the invoice to Delos, receive up to 90% upfront and let Delos handle collection.

**Pricing:** 0.5–2% of invoice value. You pay a fee to secure Delos’s commitment to buy your invoices. The 0.5–2% range is a guide. Delos reviews your contract and confirms the exact fee and purchase terms before you agree.

**Suitable when:**

- You have not issued the invoice yet and want a buyer agreed before payment becomes a problem.
- You are negotiating a contract, have signed it or are already doing the work, and want Delos’s purchase commitment arranged in advance.

**How it works:**

1. **Send your contract.** Delos reviews the contract you are negotiating or have signed, the payer and payment terms.
2. **Secure a buyer.** Agree and pay the commitment fee. Delos reserves funding and commits to buying invoices that meet the agreed terms, up to an agreed total amount and during an agreed period.
3. **Sell the invoice if you aren’t paid.** Offer the invoice under your commitment. Delos purchases it, advances up to 90% of its value and handles collection under the agreed terms.

**Key points:**

- A buyer agreed in advance
- Up to 90% upfront when Delos buys the invoice
- Collection handled by Delos

**Review and limits:**

- The commitment applies to invoices that meet the agreed terms, within the agreed funding limit and period.
- Submitting an enquiry requests review. It does not reserve funding or create a commitment to buy an invoice.
- Applying after an invoice is unpaid without a prior commitment asks Delos to decide whether to make an offer. A purchase is not assured.
- Up to 90% is an advance in exchange for selling the invoice. It is not a coverage percentage, compensation payment or fee.
- Factoring charges and any seller obligations, including recourse, follow the agreement.

**What to prepare:**

- Describe the contract, goods or services, planned payment amount and when the payer should pay.
- Prepare the payer’s full legal name and location.
- If Delos has already agreed a purchase commitment, mention its details or reference if available. Do not invent an agreement or assume a new enquiry activates it.
- If an invoice is already unpaid, clarify whether a prior Delos commitment applies. Without one, an application is a request for review, not an obligation to buy.
- The contract can be provided later through the secure upload link in the confirmation email.

**MCP submission:** Supported as a claim enquiry with review purpose `payment_protection`.

## Factoring

Get 80% of your invoice amount upfront after approval. When the invoice recipient pays in full, you receive the remaining 20%, minus the agreed fee.

**Pricing:** 0.04% per day. Calculated on the full invoice amount. Your quote confirms the advance, fee period and all charges.

**Suitable when:**

- You have issued an invoice that is not due yet or is less than 30 days overdue.
- You would like to receive money sooner rather than wait for the invoice recipient to pay.

**How it works:**

1. **Review.** Delos reviews the invoice and proposes the advance and fees.
2. **Advance.** Once approved and agreed, you receive 80% of the invoice amount upfront.
3. **Balance.** When the invoice recipient pays in full, you receive the remaining 20%, minus the agreed fee.

**Key points:**

- 80% paid upfront
- 20% balance, less the fee
- Non-recourse factoring

**Review and limits:**

- The invoice, advance and fees require review and approval. An enquiry does not release funding.
- The 0.04% daily fee is calculated on the full invoice amount; the quote confirms the fee period and all charges.
- Non-recourse applies to non-payment reasons covered by the agreement.
- The remaining 20%, less the agreed fee, is paid when the invoice recipient pays in full.
- Funding timing follows review and the agreed terms; there is no universal 48-hour funding promise.

**What to prepare:**

- Prepare the invoice amount and the invoice recipient’s legal name and location.
- Describe the invoice, issue date, due date and payment terms.
- Supporting documents can be provided with the enquiry where supported or later through the emailed secure upload link.

**MCP submission:** Supported as a claim enquiry with review purpose `factoring`.

## Collections

The invoice stays yours. Delos works to recover the money on your behalf. You agree the approach and fees before we start.

**Pricing:** ~25–35% success fee. We’ll confirm the fee, payment terms and any additional costs after reviewing your enquiry, before you decide to proceed.

**Suitable when:**

- Your invoice is 30 or more days overdue, or you do not expect it to be paid.
- You want Delos to pursue payment while you retain ownership of the claim.

**How it works:**

1. **Review.** Delos reviews the invoice, payment history and any dispute.
2. **Agree terms.** Approve the collection approach and fees.
3. **Recovery.** Delos pursues payment and keeps you updated.

**Key points:**

- You keep ownership
- Delos handles recovery
- Fee agreed after review

**Review and limits:**

- The approach, fees and any extra costs are agreed after review and before work starts.
- The approximate 25–35% success fee is not a final quote.
- Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee recovery or start collection work automatically.
- The success fee and how it applies to recovered amounts are agreed after review.

**What to prepare:**

- Prepare the amount owed and the debtor’s business or individual details and location.
- Describe the claim, payment terms, previous follow-up and any dispute.
- Supporting documents can be provided with the enquiry where supported or later through the emailed secure upload link.

**MCP submission:** Supported as a claim enquiry with review purpose `collections`.

## Claim sale

You can sell your unpaid invoice to Delos for cash upfront. We start with collection on your behalf to understand the debtor’s position, then give you our purchase decision on an agreed date.

**Pricing:** Up to 60% of the claim value we agree to buy. Indicative purchase price. Any offer follows the purchase review.

**Suitable when:**

- Your invoice is 30 or more days overdue, or you do not expect it to be paid.
- You prefer an agreed payment for selling the claim rather than waiting for an uncertain recovery.

**How it works:**

1. **Initial collection.** Agree the work, review date and fee on money recovered, with no upfront service fee. Delos then contacts the debtor and explores payment arrangements.
2. **Purchase review.** On the agreed date, Delos gives you its purchase decision and explains any offer, progress and next steps.
3. **Sale.** If you accept an offer and complete the sale, Delos pays the agreed price upfront and takes ownership.

**Key points:**

- Cash upfront on sale
- Purchase decision on an agreed date
- Ownership transfers to Delos

**Review and limits:**

- Up to 60% is indicative, not a guaranteed offer. Any offer follows the purchase review.
- Collection begins after terms are agreed. You keep ownership until an agreed sale is completed.
- Even if Delos does not buy the claim, you receive an update on the work done, any payments or arrangements, and a clearer assessment of your options.
- An enquiry does not start collection or complete a sale.

**What to prepare:**

- Prepare the amount owed and the debtor’s business or individual details and location.
- Describe the claim, payment terms, payment history and prior follow-up.
- Supporting documents can be provided with the enquiry where supported or later through the emailed secure upload link.

**MCP submission:** Supported as a claim enquiry with review purpose `claim_purchase`.

## Portfolio sale

You can sell a portfolio of unpaid invoices to Delos for cash upfront. We start with collection on your behalf to understand the debtors’ positions, then give you our purchase decision on an agreed date.

**Pricing:** ~50% of the portfolio value we agree to buy. Indicative purchase price. Any offer follows the purchase review.

**Suitable when:**

- You have several unpaid invoices to consider together.
- You want to explore selling the included claims and handing recovery to Delos.

**How it works:**

1. **Initial collection.** Agree which claims are included, the work, fees and review date. Delos then contacts the debtors and explores payment arrangements.
2. **Purchase review.** On the agreed date, Delos gives you its purchase decision and explains any offer for the included claims, progress and next steps.
3. **Sale.** If you accept an offer and complete the sale, Delos pays the agreed price upfront and takes ownership of the included claims.

**Key points:**

- Cash upfront on sale
- Included claims agreed first
- Ownership transfers to Delos

**Review and limits:**

- The approximate 50% figure is a reference, not a guaranteed purchase price.
- Collection begins after the included claims, work and terms are agreed. You keep ownership until an agreed sale is completed.
- Even if Delos does not buy the included claims, you receive an update on the work done, any payments or arrangements, and a clearer assessment of your options.
- A portfolio review can recommend different routes for different claims. Only the claims included in an agreed sale transfer to Delos.
- An enquiry does not start collection or sell the portfolio automatically.

**What to prepare:**

- Prepare the invoice count and total principal value.
- Describe the receivables, debtors, aging, payment history and where you need support.
- The invoice list and supporting documents can be provided with the enquiry where supported or later through the emailed secure upload link.

**MCP submission:** Supported as a portfolio enquiry with review purpose `claim_purchase`.

## Lien filing

Explore whether a claim against the project’s property could help secure payment for your work or materials. The options depend on your project and location.

**Pricing:** From $150 for lien filing. Your quote sets out the included work, filing charges and any additional costs.

**Suitable when:**

- You are owed money for construction work or materials on a US project.
- You want to explore whether a claim against the project’s property could help secure payment.

**How it works:**

1. **Review.** Delos reviews the project details, location and relevant dates.
2. **Agree terms.** Confirm the proposed work, responsibilities and full cost.
3. **Filing.** If you proceed, the agreed documents are prepared and submitted for filing.

**Key points:**

- Construction work or materials
- Project-specific review
- Scope and price agreed first

**Review and limits:**

- Availability and filing options depend on the project, location and relevant dates.
- The price starts from $150. The quote confirms included work, filing charges and any additional costs.
