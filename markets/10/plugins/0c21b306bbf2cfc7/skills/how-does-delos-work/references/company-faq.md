# Delos company, privacy and support FAQ

This reference carries forward general company information from the published
Delos plugin 1.0.12 FAQ. Use the shared product guide for service availability,
pricing, suitability and product processes, and the enquiry procedure for
submission, documents and results. This file does not establish a new commercial
policy or a current account status.

The privacy-request address below was checked against the
[published privacy policy](https://www.delos.international/privacy-policy) on
21 October 2026.

## What is Delos?

Delos is an AI-native collections and receivables finance company.

## Why was Delos created?

Delos’s stated aim is to make smaller commercial claims economically worthwhile
to pursue.

## Does Delos work with enterprise teams?

Yes. Delos offers workflows for finance and operations teams covering intake,
assessment, routing, reporting and handoffs. Engagements can cover selected
processes or a broader receivables operation.

## Can Delos work with our existing systems?

Delos offers intake from CSV, API and ERP exports and can discuss custom
integration workflows for a business. These are company-level services to discuss
with Delos; this does not mean the plugin imports spreadsheet rows or connects to
an enterprise system.

## What industries does Delos serve?

Delos works with businesses in industries including fintech, marketplaces,
construction, logistics, SaaS and B2B services.

## Does an enquiry create an attorney-client relationship?

No attorney-client relationship is created by an enquiry. Delos’s communications
provide business information rather than professional advice.

## Does a Delos review pause an approaching legal deadline?

No. Submitting a claim to Delos does not pause statutes of limitation, filing
deadlines or other legal deadlines.

## What information does Delos use?

Delos uses the information you provide, including contact details, claim
information, portfolio data, documents and communications, to evaluate and service
your submission.

## Can information be shared with other parties?

Yes, when needed to evaluate, process, finance, purchase or service a claim or
portfolio. This can include relevant service providers and transaction partners.

## How long is information retained?

Delos retains information as needed to operate its services, maintain transaction
records and meet applicable business and legal requirements.

## How can I request access, correction or deletion?

Email **support@delosintelligence.com** with your request, as stated in Delos’s
published privacy policy.

## Does supplying my phone number mean I consent to SMS?

No. Providing a phone number does not automatically opt you into SMS messages.
