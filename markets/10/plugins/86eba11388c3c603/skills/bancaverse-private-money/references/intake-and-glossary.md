# Bancaverse Intake & Glossary

## 1. Intake branches — ask only what's missing, one question at a time

### Residential investment, 1–4 units
**Fix-and-flip / RTL (bridge)** — purchase price · as-is value · rehab budget · ARV · loan amount requested · FICO range · flips/rehabs completed in last 3 yrs · exit (sale or refinance) · target close date.
Leverage note: RTL/bridge requests are commonly sized on LTC (loan ÷ purchase + rehab). Show LTV (on as-is), LTC and ARV-LTV.

**DSCR rental** — purpose (purchase / rate-term refi / cash-out refi) · as-is value · loan amount · monthly market or in-place rent · annual taxes · annual insurance · monthly HOA · FICO range · units (1–4) · occupied or vacant.
DSCR (1–4 units) = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA. Only run PITIA with a rate the user supplies.

**Ground-up construction** — land cost (or owned free and clear) · hard costs · soft costs · completed value · plans & permits status · builder/GC experience · exit (sale or refinance) · timeline.

**Bridge refinance / cash-out** — as-is value · existing payoff · loan amount requested · what the cash is for · exit (sale or refinance). Cash-out only exists on a refinance.

### Multifamily, 5+ units
purchase or refinance · units · price and/or as-is value · loan amount · occupancy · in-place NOI · stabilized NOI (if value-add) · renovation/capex budget · sponsor experience · liquidity · exit (sale or refinance) · close date.
Run: LTV, LTC (if capex), debt yield, DSCR (if user gives a rate or debt service).

### Commercial
Property types: retail · office · industrial · self-storage · hotel · MHC · RV park · medical · mixed use · land · special use.
Ask: property type · purchase or refinance · price/as-is value · loan amount · occupancy · NOI · lease terms (WALT, major tenants — no tenant names needed) · owner-occupied vs investment · exit (sale or refinance).
Hotels: ask flag/independent and trailing revenue. Land: entitlements status and plan. Owner-occupied: still business purpose.

## 3. Fees (Bancaverse)
Bancaverse earns a brokerage fee only when the loan closes. No upfront fees, no application fees. Some lenders on large commercial or multifamily deals may require a deposit; that goes directly to the lender, not Bancaverse.

## 4. Glossary (plain English)
- **LTV (loan-to-value):** loan ÷ as-is (current) value.
- **LTC (loan-to-cost):** loan ÷ total project cost (purchase + rehab/construction + approved soft costs).
- **ARV (after-repair value):** expected value once the renovation is done. ARV-LTV = loan ÷ ARV.
- **As-is value:** what the property is worth today, before work. Needed on every deal.
- **Stabilized value:** value once the property is leased up and operating normally.
- **NOI (net operating income):** income minus operating expenses. Excludes loan payments.
- **DSCR (debt service coverage ratio):** NOI ÷ annual debt service. Above 1.0 means the property covers its payment.
- **Debt yield:** NOI ÷ loan amount. Lenders use it to size loans without relying on rates or value.
- **Debt service:** total loan payments (principal and/or interest) over a period.
- **Cap rate:** NOI ÷ value. A market measure of pricing and risk.
- **Private money:** loans funded by private lenders or funds instead of banks. Faster and more flexible, usually costlier.
- **Hard money:** private loans focused mainly on the property's value; often short-term.
- **Bridge loan:** short-term loan that "bridges" to a sale or a long-term refinance. Can be a purchase or a refinance.
- **Business-purpose loan:** loan for investment or business real estate, not a home you live in.
- **Fix-and-flip:** buy, renovate, sell. Financed with RTL/bridge loans.
- **DSCR rental loan:** long-term rental loan qualified on the property's rent rather than personal income.
- **Ground-up construction:** financing to build new from land.
- **Cash-out refinance:** a refinance that pays off the old loan and returns extra cash to the owner.
- **Exit strategy:** how the loan gets paid off. Only two: sale or refinance.
- **Points:** upfront lender charge expressed as a % of the loan (1 point = 1%).
- **Origination fee:** charge for creating the loan, often quoted in points.
- **Extension fee:** charge to extend a short-term loan past maturity.
- **Prepayment penalty:** charge for paying the loan off early.
- **Interest reserve:** part of the loan set aside to cover interest payments during the project.
- **Rehab draw:** renovation money released in stages after work is inspected.
- **Recourse:** the borrower/guarantor is personally liable if the property doesn't cover the loan.
- **Non-recourse:** lender's recovery is limited to the property (with standard "bad boy" carve-outs).
- **Guaranty:** a personal or corporate promise to repay or perform.
- **Senior debt:** first-position loan, paid first.
- **Mezzanine debt:** subordinate loan secured by ownership interests, behind senior debt.
- **Preferred equity:** investment that sits between debt and common equity, with priority returns.

## 5. Things the assistant never does
- Never names, suggests, compares or ranks lenders or any other company.
- Has no access to lender lists, programs, credit boxes or borrower data.
- Never quotes rates, points or "max leverage".
- Never says approved / pre-approved / guaranteed / "X matches".
- Never asks for SSN, bank or card numbers, passwords or full credit reports.
