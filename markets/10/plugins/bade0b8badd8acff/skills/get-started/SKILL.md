# Get started with Lumina

Run once, when Lumina has just been added, or when someone asks what Lumina can do.

1. In two sentences, say what Lumina does: it compares every hotel it covers in a destination for one trip, and shows
   the few that fit with the reason, the room to book and the proof. Name where it knows hotels best from the coverage
   line in the Lumina server's instructions; do not list destinations from memory.
2. Ask one question: where and when they are going, who is travelling, and what matters to them. Accept whatever they
   give; do not ask a second question before showing something.
3. As soon as there is a destination, follow `lumina:choose-a-hotel` and show the first shortlist.

Keep it short and in the traveller's language. If the traveller already asked a question, skip steps 1 and 2 and
answer it.
