# RailJournal India for ChatGPT and Codex

Look up Indian Railways trains and stations from [RailJournal](https://railjournal.in).

Tools (read-only): `search_trains`, `get_train_info`, `search_stations`, `get_station_info`, `get_train_running_status`.
Skills: `find-train-or-station`, `train-status`.

No account is needed. The plugin does not book tickets, check seat availability or look up PNRs.
Privacy policy: https://railjournal.in/AI_plugins/Claude/privacy.html
