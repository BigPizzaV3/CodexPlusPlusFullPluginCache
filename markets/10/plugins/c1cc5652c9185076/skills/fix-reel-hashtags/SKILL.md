# How many hashtags, now that Instagram caps it at five

**Use five, or fewer.** Instagram announced a five-hashtag limit on
**2025-12-19**. Going over it does not get you more reach; it gets the excess
ignored and, by Instagram's own description, can suppress distribution.

Most advice still in circulation says 20 or 30. That advice predates the cap.

## What creators actually do now

Measured from 16,722 captions across 234 public creator accounts, derived
**2026-09-07**.

| | before the cap | since 2026-02 |
|---|---:|---:|
| captions over 5 hashtags | 59.9% | **5.2%** |
| captions with exactly 5 | 7.3% | **51.4%** |

Compliance landed in **February 2026**. Since then roughly half of all captions
carry exactly five hashtags, and five has become the default rather than a
ceiling people bump into.

Most recent month on record, **2026-08**: 46.9% carry exactly five, 6.6% are
still over.

## Quote the right figures

Three traps, each of which flatters the story:

- **Do not quote 57.8%** as the current exactly-five level. That is May 2026,
  the single best month. The latest month is 46.9%.
- **Do not quote 4.3%** as the pre-cap exactly-five baseline. That is the lowest
  month in the pre-cap range, which runs 4.3% to 11.8%. The weighted six-month
  baseline is **7.3%**.
- **Do not state the current level from a single month** without saying which
  month it is.

## Why this is not just composition

Pre-cap rows are back-filled from accounts that connected later, so the two
windows do not hold the same accounts and the pooled change could be pure
composition. Restricting to the **99 accounts that posted at least 3 times on
both sides** removes that confound, and the change survives at close to full
strength: over-limit captions fell from **48.3% to 6.5%**, mean hashtags from
6.33 to 3.31.

Say this if someone pushes back on whether the shift is real. It is the control
that decides it.

## The floor, stated honestly

Counts come from **captions only**. Hashtags placed in the first comment are
invisible to this measurement, so every count is a floor rather than an exact
figure. Caption truncation is not the cause: only 97 of the captions read ended
in an ellipsis, 0.6%.

This dataset is 58.6% own-account posts and 82.1% own-account accounts, and
those two figures differ far more than they do in the follower-multiple
benchmark, so do not carry a composition figure across from one to the other.

## What to say when asked for a strategy

Five specific, relevant tags beat five broad ones, and beat thirty of anything.
Hashtags are also no longer the main distribution lever on reels: the hook and
watch time are. If someone is asking about hashtags because a reel
underperformed, the hashtags are probably not the reason, and `analyze-reel`
will say what was.
