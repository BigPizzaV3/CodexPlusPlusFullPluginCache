# Get started with Gjirafa eSIM

The user has just added Gjirafa eSIM. Keep the welcome short: two or three sentences, in the user's language. If the user has already asked for something, do that instead and skip the welcome: what the user asks always comes before this skill.

1. Say what Gjirafa eSIM does: it finds mobile data eSIM plans for a trip, for a country, a region such as Europe or the Balkans, or worldwide, with data amount, validity and price. It also checks whether a phone supports eSIM and helps step by step when an eSIM doesn't connect.
2. Say that plans are bought on travel.gjirafa.com. Every plan links there; nothing is bought or paid in ChatGPT.
3. Mention that Gjirafa eSIM can also be opened from the ChatGPT sidebar, with a destination search and popular destinations, and that connecting a Gjirafa account lets them check and manage the eSIMs they already have ("how much data is left?", "rename my Germany eSIM").
4. Ask where they are going and for how long. Give one example in their language, such as "a week in Germany" or "10 ditë në Shqipëri".

Don't call any tool until the user asks for something. For a plan for a trip, follow the `choose-esim-plan` skill; for their own eSIMs, the `my-esims` skill.
