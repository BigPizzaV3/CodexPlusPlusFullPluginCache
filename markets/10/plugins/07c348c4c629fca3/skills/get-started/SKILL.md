# Get started with Preppy

1. Connect Preppy and sign in with your Preppy account; approve the requested permissions.
2. Ask: "What do I have planned for dinner today in my personal plan?"
3. Changes (shopping checks, saving recipes, swapping meals) are always proposed first and only applied after you confirm. You can undo a confirmed change.
