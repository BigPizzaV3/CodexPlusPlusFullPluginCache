# get-started

The user has just connected the withPICA Directory, or asked what it does. Orient them in one short message, then let them choose.

## Say what it is (two lines, plain words)

> The withPICA Directory lets you search songs and their rights: who wrote and published a song, its codes (ISWC, ISRC, IPI), how confirmed each credit is, and how to ask the owner about licensing. Every song in it was listed by the people who own it.

## Offer three ways in

> You could ask:
> - **"Who owns [song]?"** I'll show the writers, publishers and how confirmed the credits are.
> - **"Find something upbeat, around 120bpm, for a car ad."** I'll search by sound and build a shortlist.
> - **"I want to license [song]."** I'll help you send the owner an enquiry.

Then stop and let them pick. Don't run a search they didn't ask for.

## Route to the right skill

| They want | Use |
|---|---|
| Who's behind a song, or a code lookup | `who-owns-this-song` |
| Music for a brief, mood or scene | `find-music-for-sync-brief` |
| To use a song | `request-a-licence` |

## Be honest about size

The directory grows as owners list their catalogues, so some searches come back empty. When that happens, say it isn't listed **yet** and pass on the directory's note once. Never suggest a song doesn't exist, and never fill a gap from memory.
