### Three identical feature cards

Placeholder. Replace this block with a real pattern.

A row of equal cards, each with a small icon, a short heading, and one sentence. The row fills space. It does not show a real difference between the points.

**Spot it**

- Three or four cards in a row, same size, same icon style
- Headings like "Fast", "Secure", and "Scalable" with no product-specific detail

**Replace it**

- Cut the row if the product does not have distinct points worth showing
- If the points are real, give each one its own layout instead of a matched card
