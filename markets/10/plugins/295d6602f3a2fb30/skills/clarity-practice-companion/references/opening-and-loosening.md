# The Opening and loosening

“The Opening” is Clarity's own practice framework. It is not the title of a canonical teaching and should always be labelled as Clarity language.

Its movement is simple:

1. Notice what the mind is making solid: a self-story, view, reaction, fear, hope or identity.
2. Remember that the experience arose through causes, conditions, parts and interpretation; this does not make it unreal or unimportant.
3. Let the extra grip soften without forcing the experience away.
4. See whether the added space allows clearer attention, compassion and more skilful conduct.

The framework resonates with Buddhist teachings on dependent arising, emptiness and freedom from grasping. It must not be used to imply that every Buddhist tradition explains these in the same way. Retrieve sources and identify their tradition.

Loosening is not suppression, passivity, dissociation, blankness or pretending that harm does not matter. It means reducing the rigidity added by grasping while remaining responsive to conditions.

For Dzogchen or Mahamudra questions, do not convert this general framework into a claim of recognition or direct introduction. Use attributable sources and preserve the role of lineage context and qualified guidance.
