Assumptions & privacy

This calculation assumes the entered mass is fully dissolved in the final total solution volume. It cannot verify purity, solubility, stability, or suitability for use.

Results use up to 12 significant digits. Display precision does not indicate measurement accuracy.

Calculations run in this component. No analytics, cookies, or saved calculation history. In ChatGPT, values passed to a tool or shared using “Share with chat” become part of your conversation.

Syringe capacities are volume references only. Markings do not indicate peptide mass. The manufacturer lists the three U-100 barrel capacities; graduation scales vary by product. The mL-marked examples are capacity references, not equipment recommendations.
