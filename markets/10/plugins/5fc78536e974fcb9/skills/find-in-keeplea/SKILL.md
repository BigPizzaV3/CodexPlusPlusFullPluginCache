# Finding things in Keeplea

Keeplea is the person's visual memory. Each item is one photo or PDF of something they wanted to
remember, with what Keeplea read from it when it was captured: a title, a summary, keywords,
attributes such as brand, model, serial number, color or dates, the person's own tags, and the
collection it sits in. The full description, the text recognized in the photo and the person's note
come with the full record.

## Find it the way they describe it

- They describe the thing, not the text printed on it: "the green paint in the hallway", "the
  boiler certificate". Search with their words; Keeplea matches by meaning.
- A code they give you — a model number, a serial, an order reference — searches as it is.
- Questions about many items at once ("all my receipts from 2025", "everything tagged Kitchen")
  go through the library page by page with a filter rather than a search. Tags are matched by name.
- When a row does not hold the answer — the date on a certificate, a line on a receipt — read the
  full record.

## Answer from the records

- Answer only from what the records say. Never fill a gap with general knowledge: a model number,
  a price or a date that is not in a record is not known.
- When nothing matches, say so plainly, and suggest describing the object rather than the text on
  it, or fewer words.
- When the items appear in Keeplea's view, the person already sees them and their photos. Reply in a
  sentence or two — the answer, what stands out, or what to ask next — rather than listing them.
- Several similar items (two parking photos, three paint tins) are a reason to show them and ask
  which one, not to pick one silently.

## What Keeplea never shares

Identity documents, medical records and anything the person deleted never reach an assistant. If
they ask for one, say Keeplea keeps these out of assistants, so if they saved it, it is in the
Keeplea app. You cannot tell whether it exists: never conclude that it does not.

This connection only reads. Capturing, editing, tagging and deleting happen in the Keeplea app.
