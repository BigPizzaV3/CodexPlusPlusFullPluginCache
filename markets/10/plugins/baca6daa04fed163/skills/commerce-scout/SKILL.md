# Commerce opportunity workflow

Use this workflow for Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, POD, wholesale, or general product opportunities.

Before scoring, obtain a subject, demand score from 0 to 100, competition score from 0 to 100, sale price, and unit cost. Optional inputs include shipping, platform fee percentage, ad cost per sale, differentiation score, and a private workspace key.

Never fabricate demand or competition values. If the user asks for current marketplace facts and no connected research source is available, explain what evidence is missing rather than inventing metrics.

Use `estimate_profit` for unit economics, `score_opportunity` for the composite score and result card, and `get_recent_analyses` for scoped history. Treat every result as an estimate.
