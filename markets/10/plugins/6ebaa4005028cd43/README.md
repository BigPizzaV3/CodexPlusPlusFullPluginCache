# SellApp Merchant

Manage your SellApp store from ChatGPT and Codex. Search and edit products, variants, prices, images, private delivery files, groups, stock and coupons. Review orders, customers and analytics without changing them.

## Connect your store

Open the SellApp Merchant workspace and choose Connect account. Complete sign-in and consent in your browser, then select a store you can access. Your current store role controls which actions are available. You can change stores without signing in again.

If you do not have a store, the workspace guides you through account creation and email verification in your browser, then lets you review and create your first store. Payment-provider setup takes place in your SellApp dashboard.

Try one of these requests:

- Open my SellApp merchant workspace.
- Find a product and help me review an edit.
- Show my store analytics for the last 30 days.

## Review changes

Before a change is saved, check the store, records and values in its review. Confirm to apply the change or decline to leave it unchanged. If a record changes while you are reviewing it, reload it and review the current values. When the result is uncertain, check the record before trying again.

The workspace also includes subscriptions, promotions, upsells, credits, wallet incentives, support, affiliates and settings. Available actions depend on your permissions. These merchant settings can affect what customers are offered on your storefront. Customer purchases and payment-provider setup happen outside the conversation.

## Explore before connecting

You can create a private storefront preview from a short description of your business, product and audience. Choose Clean, Warm or Bold, then continue in your browser to sign in or create an account. A preview expires after 24 hours and can be claimed in the browser where its private link was first opened.

Claiming creates a private store with the default layout. It does not apply the preview design, create products, configure payments or connect the plugin. After claiming, connect SellApp Merchant through browser consent and refresh your stores.

## Get help

Ask a SellApp setup or API question to receive an answer linked to the public documentation. For account help, use [SellApp support](https://sell.app/docs/support). If one store denies an action, choose a store where you have the required permission. Manage or disconnect your own connection through **MCP access** in SellApp settings.
