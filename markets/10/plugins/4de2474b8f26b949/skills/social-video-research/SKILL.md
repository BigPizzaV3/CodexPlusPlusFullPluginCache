# Compare public creators across platforms

Read `../hasdata-research/SKILL.md`. Preserve the platforms, public handles/URLs and requested comparison. Choose `../youtube-research/SKILL.md`, `../instagram-research/SKILL.md`, `../tiktok-research/SKILL.md` or `../facebook-research/SKILL.md` for each actual requested source. For a single-platform task use only that focused workflow, not a cross-platform sweep.

A profile, posts, comments, video metadata and transcript answer different questions. Retrieve the smallest requested sample within the shared budget. Do not infer that two accounts belong to the same person from similar display names; use supplied or source-supported associations and label uncertainty.

Keep actual dates, counters and source links, with the sampled page size. Different platforms' views, likes, followers and ratings are not interchangeable measures. Missing values are unknown, not zero. A single live snapshot cannot establish audience growth or whole-history engagement.

A video-content summary needs its actual transcript when available, not a metadata description presented as watched content. Cite segment timestamps. If no transcript is returned, disclose that rather than inventing text or making a silent paid fallback.

For comments, quote briefly without unnecessary commenter identifiers or sensitive inferences. Do not access private profiles, supply cookies, infer private relationships, follow, post or message. Do not investigate private individuals or minors across platforms or compile personal dossiers. Return a comparison of the requested public creators, brands or content.
