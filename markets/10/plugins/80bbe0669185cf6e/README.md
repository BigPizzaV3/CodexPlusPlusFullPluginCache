# People’s Court

Connect your own People’s Court account to manage authorized arbitration cases.
For new cases filed through ChatGPT, an assigned human arbitrator decides and signs any Award.
New filings and Answers prepared through ChatGPT retain the human appeal.
The hosted connection uses OAuth and the MCP endpoint declared in mcp.json.
Review and accept the required filing or Answer packet personally on People’s Court.
Support: https://peoplescourt.ai/docs/api/connectors
