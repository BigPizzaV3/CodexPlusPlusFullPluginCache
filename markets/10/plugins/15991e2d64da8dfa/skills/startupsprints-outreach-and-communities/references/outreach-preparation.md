# Prepare useful outreach and community work

Follow the live execution brief and [MCP workflow](mcp-workflow.md). The objective is a relevant contribution or bounded conversation that can support the founder's mission, with evidence sufficient to assess actual response.

## Qualify the destination

For each recipient, record actual source/date, role or audience context, specific problem/trigger evidence, permitted contact route and uncertainty. A job title alone is not a buying trigger. For communities, record recent relevant discussions, rules URL/check date, membership context, allowed affiliation disclosure, promotion restrictions and moderator guidance. Publicly visible contact information does not by itself grant permission for a campaign.

Respect no-cold-outreach decisions, opt-outs and existing-community-only constraints. If prerequisites block contact, prepare useful drafts or public audience research that does not violate them. Do not infer permission to join a new community from willingness to use a public identity. Current rules matter more than an old recommendation or saved rules excerpt.

## Produce the actual work

| Action type | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Targeted founder/design-partner/trigger outreach | Named supported recipients, exact individual message, useful offer/question, clear next step, contact cap |
| Warm/referral introduction | Specific fit and introduction text the relationship owner can choose to send; no implied consent from the recipient |
| Follow-up | Prior actual interaction and timing, one relevant new reason, exact draft, approved limit and easy decline |
| Editor/podcast pitch | Evidence from current outlet/episodes, audience benefit, concrete story/lesson, individual pitch and affiliation |
| Community mapping | Verified partial shortlist, relevant threads, checked rules and permitted next contribution |
| Thread answer/community feedback | Complete useful reply responding to the actual question, transparent affiliation, allowed links and no disguised promotion |

Use the founder's truthful offer and confirmed capabilities. Do not claim social proof, urgency, a prior relationship or personalization you did not observe. Draft concise finished messages with a real requested next step. Do not turn a community question into a product pitch where it is not appropriate.

## Execute and preserve scope

Confirm the actual authenticated account separately from missing send/post authorization. Preserve earlier explicit confirmations and approvals while account, content, named destinations, costs and volume/follow-up scope are unchanged. Prepare a concrete review package before asking for any missing approval. A broad “research this audience” does not authorize contact.

If authorized tools can execute the bounded action, record the actual receipt/link/date and response. If unavailable, provide manual-ready messages and a log with drafted/sent/responded states, recording only what happened. Stop after declines or restrictions; do not use alternate routes to bypass denied access. Non-response does not authorize additional volume or follow-ups.

For example, an approved one-time send to Pat and Lee can produce one successful send and one explicitly failed attempt. Record the failure in Lee's `outcome`, preserve the existing entry status, and never count it as sent. Do not retry under a one-attempt approval unless the founder explicitly authorized retries; an uncertain send receipt requires checking before any repeat. Additional prospects may be prepared, but named recipients and exact messages need their own missing send approval.

## Save and review

Use live template fields. `outreach` usually separates `recipient`, `evidence`, `route`, `subject`, `message`, `cta`, `followup` and actual `outcome`; community/publication work may use `shortlist` or `content`. Keep drafts and actual responses distinct; do not add unsupported field keys. Preserve existing entries/notes and legacy findings, merge full replacements with current `workRevision`, reread conflicts and verify.

Report verified recipients, drafts, actual sends and responses separately. Evaluate signal against real effort, window and mission. Save summaries without implying action completion. Ambiguous wrap-up needs necessary recording/verdict clarification before an attempt-ending result; the full current-attempt and `measuredMetrics` rules are in the local MCP reference.
