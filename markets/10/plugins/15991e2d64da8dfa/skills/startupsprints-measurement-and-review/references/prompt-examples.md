# Reusable founder prompts

These prompts select work; they do not grant account access or authorize sends, publication, costs or attempt-ending result writes. Resolve startup/action IDs through live tools. Preserve earlier explicit bounded approvals.

- **Continue:** “Help me continue [strategy title] for [startup name] toward our mission. Read the execution brief and saved work, produce the next useful deliverable, and save it when permitted. Ask only for the next step's missing facts or permissions.”
- **Interview candidates:** “Help with ‘Interview five people with the problem’ for [startup name]. Find up to five source-backed candidates in our confirmed audience. Prepare invitations and a neutral interview guide, save research and use a permitted alternative if the preferred integration is missing.”
- **Outreach:** “Prepare relevant outreach for [strategy title]. Read our preferences and current venue rules, verify recipients' relevance, draft exact messages and save them. Present any missing send approval with account, recipients, content and contact limit.”
- **Asset or launch:** “Prepare the actual deliverable for [strategy title]. Use confirmed facts and existing assets, verify current destination requirements, state unresolved claims or eligibility, and save the draft.”
- **Review:** “Review actual notes/results for [strategy title]. Separate measured values from missing information, include cohort/window and recommend repeat, repair or stop. Save a summary; leave the attempt open unless I explicitly request recording a completed or failed result.”
- **Missing integration:** “Continue [strategy title] using an available permitted route. Complete useful independent preparation, explain the remaining limitation and smallest needed input, and keep unsupported access/actions/results absent.”
