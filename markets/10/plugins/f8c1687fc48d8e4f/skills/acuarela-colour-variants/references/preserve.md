# What must not change, and how to check

## Keep exactly as in the original

- The model: face, expression, skin tone, hair, hands.
- The pose and the framing.
- The lighting, shadows and highlights.
- The background.
- Accessories: jewellery, bags, belts, glasses, watches.
- Every other piece of clothing.
- The garment's own fabric: texture, sheen, folds, seams, buttons, pockets, prints and patterns. A pattern keeps its shapes; only its colours shift.

## The only change

The colour of the garment the user asked about. If the garment has two colours (a stripe, a print, a contrast collar), ask which one changes, unless the user already said.

## Check each image against the original

Look at the two side by side and answer each question. Any "no" means the image fails.

1. Is it one image of one colour, with no second panel?
2. Is the person the same person, with the same expression?
3. Is the pose the same, including the hands?
4. Is the background the same?
5. Are the accessories and other clothing unchanged in colour and shape?
6. Does the garment have the same shape, buttons, pockets, seams and pattern?
7. Is the garment the requested colour over its whole surface, with no patches of the old colour?
8. Do the pixel dimensions match the original's? If not, resize before showing; this alone is not a failure.

## When an image fails

Make it again once, from the original photo. If it fails again, show it to the user and say which question it failed. Do not hide a difference.

## Hard cases

- Black to a light colour, and white to a dark colour: check that folds and texture are still visible, not flattened.
- Sheer or shiny fabric: check that highlights are still there.
- Hair or a bag strap lying over the garment: check that it kept its own colour.
- White garment on a white background: check that the garment's edge is still clear.
