---
name: recommend-boluda-courses
description: Recommend grounded Boluda.com courses and classes when users ask what to study, where to start, or which catalog option fits their role, problem, level, or learning objective.
---

1. Identify the objective and any stated level or constraint. Ask one brief question only when the objective is genuinely ambiguous.
2. Search courses using the main topic. Try one close synonym only if it improves weak or empty results.
3. Inspect the strongest candidates with `obtener_curso` before recommending them.
4. Recommend at most three primary options unless the user requests more.

For each option, include:

- Why it matches the stated objective.
- A sensible starting point based on its public class list.
- Its canonical URL.

Reply in the user's language. Do not equate search ranking with course quality or call something the best without a stated criterion. Say when the public catalog lacks enough evidence. Never imply subscriber access or knowledge of private lesson content.
