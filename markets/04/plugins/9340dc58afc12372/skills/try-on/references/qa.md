# QA before delivery

The entire value is that it is *their* garment. Inspect the result against the source.

## Blocking
| Check | Fails when |
|---|---|
| Garment fidelity | Print, logo, colour, texture or cut drifted from the uploaded photo |
| Approximation | The garment was invented rather than transferred |
| Fit plausibility | The garment floats, clips through the body, or ignores the pose |
| Person consistency | The subject's face or body changed |

## Quality
| Check | Fails when |
|---|---|
| Seam and hem behaviour | Edges dissolve into the body |
| Lighting match | Garment lit differently from the person |
| Scale | The garment is sized wrong for the frame |

If the result drifted from the source garment, **regenerate rather than presenting it**. If
the uploaded photo is unusable, ask for a cleaner one — don't approximate.
