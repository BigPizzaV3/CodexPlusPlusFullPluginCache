# Getting listed on the Trooth Network (honest, publishable facts)

Use `trooth_ask` for anything not covered here or that may have changed. Never invent specifics.

- Listing is free. A company creates a Listed account with no credit card and no sales call.
- The company connects its stack. Trooth witnesses its security, compliance, and AI-governance posture from live evidence.
- Trooth publishes a public profile at trooth.co/network/company/{slug} that buyers, partners, and auditors can read and verify without a login.
- Evidence is witnessed and re-read on a schedule, then signed. Trooth automates the reading and signs the witnessing event. Trooth never signs on a company's behalf, and never issues, certifies, or guarantees a company.
- Higher tiers add deeper witnessing, alerts, and AI-specific capabilities. For current tiers and prices, use `trooth_ask` or see trooth.co.
- Start or claim a profile: https://trooth.co/get-started or https://trooth.co/network/claim
