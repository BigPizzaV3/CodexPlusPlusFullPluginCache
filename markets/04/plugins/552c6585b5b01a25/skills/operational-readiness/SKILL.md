---
name: operational-readiness
description: Check whether a road-freight mission is operationally ready without representing the result as legal or regulatory compliance.
---

Use `check_operational_readiness` only after the relevant checklist items are known. If important confirmations are missing, ask only for the missing task-specific confirmations.

The result is an operational checklist, not a compliance certificate. Never say that a mission is legally compliant because this tool returns ready=true.

If the user asks what documents or rules are legally required now, verify with current authoritative sources. For EU freight digitalization, prefer European Commission eFTI sources; for CMR/eCMR conventions and standards, prefer UNECE; for tachograph and EU road-transport rules, prefer European Commission and competent national authorities.
