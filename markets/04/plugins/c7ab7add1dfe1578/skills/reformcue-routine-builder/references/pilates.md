# Pilates

Apply this reference only when the ReformCue account context and returned library support Pilates-oriented moves. A general class may progress from breath and preparation through trunk control, lower and upper body, lateral or rotational work, and a measured cooldown, with transitions made explicit.

Use only returned move IDs and stored spring/position information. Favor clear pacing, neutral transitions, and balanced sides when the move metadata calls for it. Difficulty scaling must come from the account’s move presets and the user’s stated level.

Do not imply instructor certification, rehabilitation expertise, clinical benefits, or medical safety. `REQUIRES_DOMAIN_REVIEW`: Pilates-specific technical, certification, or therapeutic claims. Ask when “Pilates” is requested but the authorized library does not expose Pilates moves; do not silently substitute another style.
