# Looks

A look is the film's whole visual system: surfaces, type, how things move,
what carries across a cut, and what the sound does. Reference videos from
the user are the best source of one. Without them, choose one look below,
name it in the storyboard with a sentence on why it fits the message, and
build the first beat in it before anything else. One look per film.

Every look names what carries across its cuts; a film whose cuts carry
nothing reads as a slideshow whatever its surfaces.

## Pick by the message

| Message | Look |
| --- | --- |
| A line of copy with nothing to show | Kinetic type, Paper editorial |
| A product with real screens | Interface panels, Paper editorial |
| A brand or manifesto film | Dark cinematic, Quiet geometry |
| A design-led or premium brand | Aurora, Paper editorial |
| A tool for builders | Blueprint |
| A playful consumer product | Pop |
| A social ad that has to stop the scroll | Papercut |

## Paper editorial

- Surfaces: the brand's neutral or a warm off-white page, white cards with
  hairline borders, black ink.
- Type: one tight grotesk at display size, one serif italic for the
  emotional word of a line. The brand colour appears on the call to action,
  one highlighted word, and the pointer that stands for the product.
- Devices: key words resolve letter by letter inside hairline boxes drawn
  from each letter's measured `ink`; small labelled pointers that act in the
  frame; real images as tilted collage tiles with construction lines; a
  phrase repeated in rows until it fills the frame.
- Motion: hard cuts on the beat; animation inside the frame steps on twos
  (every animated value on hold keys two frames apart at the film's frame
  rate, sampled from the curve you would otherwise ease) while the camera, when it moves, stays smooth;
  two or three parallax sweeps in a 30 second film and still shots between.
  On a 10 second film, skip the sweeps and keep the stepping.
- Carries across cuts: the word that was resolving opens the next frame, or
  the pointer that pressed something starts the next scene.
- Sound: a soft tick per settled letter, a click per press, one whoosh per
  sweep. Avoid a second accent colour and drawn illustrations.

## Interface panels

- Surfaces: black or white panels on one hairline grid; the product's real
  interface redrawn in code so every label and number is exact.
- Type: one grotesk at interface sizes, one large number per scene.
- Devices: a counter ticking to its figure, a chart line drawing itself, a
  dot grid whose dots become items, one 3D beat of cards passing the camera
  at different depths.
- Motion: panels enter staggered and stop hard; cuts on the beat; one slow
  dolly on the beat that matters.
- Carries across cuts: the focused panel shrinks into the next layout, or
  the finished number becomes the next headline.
- Sound: a tick per counted step, a low hit as a panel lands. Avoid figures
  that could not be true and chrome around chrome.

## Dark cinematic

- Surfaces: near-black with fine grain. Type: a large white serif.
- Accent: the brand's colour, else amber, for brackets and underlines, used
  once in three beats.
- Devices: generated abstract macros as half-second bursts (an eye, ink in
  water, a match, a fern); brackets on visual subjects, never on words; one
  line of copy per beat.
- Motion: slow push-ins on holds, cuts on the music's hits, exits faster
  than entrances.
- Carries across cuts: the accent stays in place while the picture changes,
  or the last word of a line opens the next beat.
- Sound: a riser into each hit, a low impact on it, room tone under holds.
  Avoid stock-looking hero shots and brackets on every cut.

## Kinetic type

- Surfaces: black. Type: bold white sans at small to medium size, centred in
  empty space.
- Devices: words land one or a few at a time in the rhythm of the voice; a
  stressed word grows or flips the frame to white for a beat; product shots
  slide through full frame and leave as fast as they came.
- Motion: several cuts a second in fast passages, no hold longer than a
  breath, a sound on every cut.
- Carries across cuts: the phrase builds in place, or the word that filled
  the frame shrinks into the next line. Avoid anything but type, product and
  black.

## Quiet geometry

- Surfaces: warm white and near-black. Marks: tiny dots, hairline strokes,
  simple shapes, small centred type; monochrome with one quiet accent.
- Devices: the shapes illustrate what the words mean and transform into the
  next scene; nothing cuts.
- Motion: slow, precise, with real stillness.
- Carries across cuts: the dot of one scene is the centre of the next, a
  line becomes a border, a circle becomes a letter.
- Sound: sparse tones on the transformations. Avoid clutter and speed.

## Aurora

- Surfaces: soft gradients grown from the brand colour over white and over
  near-black, frosted glass cards. Type: light, with large numbers.
- Devices: a believable interface a pointer types into and scrolls; few
  scenes, each given time; a full-frame brand card with the wordmark to end.
- Motion: the camera pushes and scrolls rather than cutting.
- Carries across cuts: one element stays on screen through each change and
  becomes the next scene, such as a prompt box that grows as the gradient
  blooms and then shrinks to the top as the interface scrolls under it.
- Sound: soft swells, no hits. Avoid hard cuts and saturated colour.

## Blueprint

- Surfaces: cream and one saturated brand colour, faint grain.
- Devices: design-tool chrome as decoration (selection handles, crosshair
  guides, dotted connectors, dimension lines that draw themselves in);
  schematic diagrams instead of illustrations; an outlined wordmark to end.
- Motion: the film is one canvas the camera pans and zooms across while
  elements are placed, resized and wired together.
- Carries across cuts: a scene change is a move to another part of the
  canvas, never a cut.
- Sound: pencil ticks and clicks as things are placed.

## Pop

- Surfaces: saturated primaries against black and white. Type: chunky bold
  sans.
- Devices: real product screens and chat bubbles, named pointers moving on a
  canvas, a grid of many outputs appearing at once.
- Motion: punchy squash-and-stretch pops; quick and fun.
- Carries across cuts: an object flies out of one scene and lands in the
  next composition (a sticker, a screen, a word).
- Sound: playful pops and snaps on the landings. Avoid long holds.

## Papercut

- Surfaces: an opening of full-bleed vertical social posts over a blurred
  copy of themselves, then an off-white page inside a hairline frame with
  tiny monospaced labels in its corners.
- Type: bold white sans over the feed; a medium grotesk on the page, a few
  words at a time, with one emotional word per line in a serif italic. One
  accent colour, the brand's or else orange, on that word, chips and the
  call to action.
- Devices: the hook line builds over posts that flick past a few frames
  each, then the feed freezes and dims on its last word; a white interface
  card beside the type whose fields type themselves in; post cards with
  typed caption plates; a list becomes full-bleed photos with one giant
  word each.
- Motion: every animated value steps at 12 frames a second (hold keys on
  that grid, sampled from the curve you would otherwise ease); hard cuts on
  the beat; the camera stays still.
- Carries across cuts: the word that ended a line opens the next frame, or
  a card shrinks into the next layout.
- Sound: a chill, airy breakbeat, a soft whoosh on the big cuts. Avoid a
  hook that waits: the first words land in the first second.
