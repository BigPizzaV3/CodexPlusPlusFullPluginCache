# Cite what AI leaders said

Real Alpha has the best few minutes of long interviews with the people building AI. Each moment comes with the
speaker, what they do, the show, the date, their quote and the moment's page on realalpha.app.

## Pick the tool

- One named person ("What has Jensen Huang said about demand?"): `what_did_they_say`, with a topic when there is one.
- A subject, maybe narrowed to a person, company, show or date: `search_moments`.
- Where people stand on a big question ("Is AI a bubble?"): `answers_to`, when the question is on its list.
- What's new, on every show or one: `latest_moments`.
- One moment in full, or a realalpha.app/s/ link someone shared: `get_moment`.
- The exact words around a quote: `transcript_excerpt`, with the video and second from the moment's `episode_url`.

## Answer from the moments

- Quote the `quote` word for word, or paraphrase it and say so. Never put words in quotation marks that a tool
  didn't return.
- Name the speaker, the show and the date, and link the moment's `url`. The `cite` field has a ready line.
- Say when the moments are few or old, and give each person's view as theirs, not as fact.
- When a name could be more than one person, the tool says who; ask which one.
- When nothing matches, say Real Alpha has no moment on it. It only covers the episodes it has cut, so that doesn't
  mean nobody said it. Don't fill the gap from memory as if it were a quote.
- Results are short excerpts. For the full conversation, link the moment's page, which plays the episode from that
  minute.
