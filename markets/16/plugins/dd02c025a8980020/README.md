# AegisVita - Brand Builder OS

**Version:** 1.0.0 public-edition candidate  
**Package status:** General-purpose fork; private staging copy pending public-listing preparation and review.

AegisVita - Brand Builder OS helps founders and operators shape a research-informed business and brand strategy, from an early idea through customer discovery and offer feasibility. It then helps turn that strategy into content and customer-journey plans.

## What it supports

- Founder intake and a sequenced new-business build.
- Secondary market research and qualitative customer-discovery planning and synthesis.
- Provisional audience, problem, positioning, and offer hypotheses with explicit evidence status.
- Delivery feasibility, cost and pricing assumptions, capacity, and low-risk experiment design.
- Separate personal-brand and company, product, service, or organization-brand paths.
- Brand strategy, visual direction, messaging, content strategy, scripts, campaigns, and platform adaptations.
- Truthful storytelling and case-study development from approved facts or clearly labeled fictional examples.
- Lead-generation, sales-readiness, customer-experience, retention, referral, measurement, and AI-readiness workflows with explicit gates.
- Optional, permission-based network-marketing and team-growth workflows when the user requests them.
- A specialized IUL-education content workflow for users working in that regulated product area.

The **AegisVita** name is the product/publisher identity. This general edition does not preload any company's client profile, customer segment, product facts, private brand strategy, or case material. Users provide the project context they want to work on.

## Operating standard

Research and first-party evidence are the primary basis for factual claims. The OS challenges material assumptions, seeks counterevidence, and distinguishes CONFIRMED, ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS, EXAMPLE, and UNKNOWN. It flags unsupported or contradicted figures and recommends source-backed corrections. The user controls editorial choices, but an unsupported or known-false figure must not be presented as established fact.

See `BETA-GUIDE.md` for scope, capability limits, evidence handling, and feedback guidance. Specialist workflows are in `skills/`; shared architecture and evidence rules are in `references/`.
