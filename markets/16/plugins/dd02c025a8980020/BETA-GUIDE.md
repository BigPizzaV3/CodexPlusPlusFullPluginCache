# AegisVita - Brand Builder OS — Beta Guide

## What it helps with

The OS helps users clarify a business idea, research a market, plan customer discovery, examine an offer's feasibility, build a personal or business brand, and carry the strategy into content and customer-journey workflows. It can also help users with an existing business audit or improve a specific asset without forcing a full rebuild.

Supported workflows include market research, real-note synthesis, offer feasibility, brand strategy, visual direction, content planning and creation, campaigns, storytelling and case studies, lead generation, conversion readiness, customer experience, retention and referral readiness, measurement, systems, and optional network-marketing growth. The bundled IUL workflow is a specialized education and content path; it does not determine whether a financial product is suitable for a person.

## How to start

Describe the project and the outcome you want. If you are starting from an idea, say so; the OS will begin with founder context and guide you in sequence, asking one focused question at a time when information is missing. If you already have a brand or business, name the stage you want help with. You can request a later-stage draft; unsupported inputs and unmet readiness gates should remain visible in that draft.

There is no hidden AegisVita project mode or profile trigger in this general edition. The AegisVita name identifies this product; it does not preload a customer profile, company strategy, products, market, or claims. Supply the facts and materials relevant to your own project.

## Research and evidence

Current external research requires web/search access in the host. The OS should say whether it actually researched a question, cite sources when used, and report scope and limitations. Without access, it can propose a research plan or provide a clearly labeled provisional analysis; it should not present memory as fresh research.

The OS can help plan discovery and synthesize interview notes, observations, and records supplied by the user. It cannot authenticate the source or completeness of those materials without corroboration, recruit participants, conduct interviews, or validate demand unless a specific authorized tool actually performs that work. Fictional interviews, personas, and synthetic examples are method demonstrations, not customer evidence. Remove unnecessary personal identifiers and obtain appropriate consent before recording or sharing research materials.

Use the labels CONFIRMED, ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS, EXAMPLE, and UNKNOWN to keep facts, interpretations, and missing information distinct. Research is the primary basis for claims about customers, markets, performance, prices, products, and feasibility. User preferences guide subjective choices but do not establish empirical facts.

## Figures and editorial decisions

Flag figures that lack a source or have a scope mismatch. If a figure appears incorrect, explain the evidence and suggest a correction. The user retains editorial choice about whether to use a figure, but the OS must preserve its evidence label and must not state a known-false figure as fact. Creative material may use clearly marked placeholders when no verified value is available.

## Capability limits

An image concept or prompt is not a generated image. Rendering requires image-generation access in the host. The OS does not publish content, contact prospects, collect leads, book services, or operate external workflows unless an appropriate tool is connected, authorized, and actually used.

For chatbot work, it can help define requirements, conversation flows, and draft instructions. It does not create or deploy a functioning chatbot unless a suitable authorized builder is available and actually used.

## Professional boundaries

The OS supports planning and analysis. It does not replace qualified legal, tax, accounting, financial, regulatory/compliance, clinical, engineering, safety, cybersecurity, or other specialist operational advice. It does not file registrations, obtain licenses, verify legal availability of a name or trademark, guarantee demand or results, or establish business readiness.

## Optional network-marketing workflow

The network-marketing and team-growth skill runs only when requested. It is separate from ordinary customer and content workflows. It avoids guaranteed-income claims, pressure, fake scarcity, and treating every customer or follower as a recruiting prospect. Any earnings or business-opportunity statements need current, authoritative disclosures and a clear distinction between gross and net results.
