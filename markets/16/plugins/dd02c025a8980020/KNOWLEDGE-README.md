# Brand Builder OS — Knowledge and Skill Map

The orchestrator routes each request to the smallest useful set of specialist skills. Shared rules for research, evidence labels, assumption-challenging, and scope live in `references/`.

## Strategy and evidence

- `market-research` — current secondary research, competitors, source evaluation, and unresolved hypotheses.
- `customer-discovery` — study planning and synthesis of real interview, observation, and first-party evidence.
- `business-feasibility-validation` — delivery constraints, cost/capacity inputs, pricing assumptions, and low-risk experiments.
- `brand-builder-orchestrator` — sequencing, routing, state preservation, truth labels, and quality gates.

## Brand and content

- `brand-from-scratch` — personal brands.
- `business-brand-from-scratch` — companies, organizations, products, and services.
- `brand-strategy`, `brand-audit`, `brand-management`, `visual-brand-strategy`, and `executive-brand-summary` — shared brand development and governance.
- `brand-content-strategy` — content tied to a personal-brand transformation.
- `content-strategy`, `content-creation`, `campaign-planning`, `content-manager`, `social-intelligence-seo`, `repurposing-analytics`, and `language-workflow` — content operations.
- `storytelling` and `case-study-builder` — evidence-aware narratives and customer cases.

## Customer journey and optional paths

- `lead-generation` — permission-based engagement, lead resources, qualification, conversion readiness, and handoffs.
- `customer-experience-retention` — service expectations, feedback, retention, and referral readiness.
- `measurement-optimization` — trustworthy metrics and bounded experiments.
- `systems-ai-readiness` — stable manual processes, human review, and data safeguards before automation.
- `network-marketing-growth` — optional, ethical opportunity and team-development workflows.
- `iul-content-tool` — specialized educational content workflow for IUL-related work, with additional product-claim safeguards.

No bundled skill assumes a particular market, customer profile, product, brand voice, or business model.
