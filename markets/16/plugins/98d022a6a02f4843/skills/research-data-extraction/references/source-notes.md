## Method resources consulted (checked 2026-10-03)

- [PRISMA 2020 statement](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020) and [checklist](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-checklist): reporting guidance for systematic reviews; not a quality score or risk-of-bias instrument.
- [Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 8](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-08): RoB 2 framework for risk-of-bias assessment in randomized trials, centered on a specified result and domains/signalling questions.
- [ROBINS-I resource](https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/home): versioned materials for non-randomized studies of interventions; its site announces evolving versions, so check the current release before applying.
- [STARD explanation and elaboration](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-012799): diagnostic-accuracy reporting context; do not treat checklist completion as proof of validity.

The plugin extracts information and supports organization. It does not reproduce complete instrument text, decide all judgments automatically, or replace a trained review team/protocol.
