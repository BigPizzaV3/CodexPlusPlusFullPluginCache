  01          CAREER NORTHSTAR COMPASS™ / FIELD GUIDE




 Why NorthStar exists
 A career-development resource built around the same questions and decision points that arise in real
 recruiter, candidate, and hiring conversations.

 A career is more than a collection of titles and accomplishments. It is also shaped by the problems
 you enjoy solving, the environments in which you do your best work, the responsibilities you want to
 carry, and the life you want your work to support.

 Career NorthStar Compass™ helps you connect those pieces into a more cohesive professional story.
 It is a guided conversation, not a test, a job-matching engine, or a shortcut to a predetermined
 answer.


  Your career. Your story. Your decisions.
  NorthStar helps you reflect, identify patterns, ask stronger questions, and investigate possibilities. Its
  documents are working interpretations that you can challenge or refine. It is not intended to replace your
  judgment.




 Start with the question you are actually trying to answer.
 You do not need a resume, a perfect prompt, a defined career goal, or an active job search. Start
 with what is on your mind. NorthStar will generally explore one meaningful question at a time.


  TRY ASKING

  I have had a successful career, but I am not sure I want the next promotion.




 How to use NorthStar insights
 Start where you are, explore what is helpful, and return when the next decision arrives.
 NorthStar supports career direction and can inform later work on resumes, interviews, and opportunity evaluation.

 What can we explore?
 NorthStar looks for the relationship between your experience, your preferences, and the professional
 conditions under which you thrive.

 Demonstrated strengths
 What have you repeatedly done well? What did you personally contribute, and what did your organization,
 colleagues, resources, or market make possible?


 The work that engages you
 Which challenges, decisions, interactions, and responsibilities make you feel effective? What are you capable of
 doing but no longer want to do every day?


 Environment and organizational fit
 What role have leadership, authority, support, culture, geographic scope, team dynamics, and business maturity
 played in your experience?


 Career and life priorities
 Which tradeoffs are acceptable now? Consider compensation, travel, family, location, autonomy, time, and
 professional significance without assuming one priority fits everyone.


 An evolving professional identity
 What does advancement mean to you today? A title, a larger enterprise, greater impact, a different challenge,
 more room to build, or a different way of living?



  Capability and preference are different questions.
  You can be exceptionally good at work you no longer find rewarding. NorthStar helps distinguish your
  demonstrated strengths from the responsibilities and environments you want in the next chapter.

  03          CAREER NORTHSTAR COMPASS™ / FIELD GUIDE




 Start anywhere
 NorthStar is for experienced professionals exploring different kinds of career questions. These are
 examples, not fixed pathways or eligibility requirements.

 Considering a career change
 Investigate transferable capabilities, reasons for a change, constraints, and assumptions that need testing.

 START WITH: I know I need something different, but I am not sure what.


 Sales and commercial careers
 Explore how you develop business, customer access, channel relationships, territory scope, technical demands,
 and organizational support.

 START WITH: Why have I performed well in some sales organizations and struggled in others?


 Directors and emerging executives
 Examine the actual mandate behind an opportunity: leadership, influence, scope, decision rights, and whether the
 next step fits what you enjoy.

 START WITH: I am being considered for a VP role. Is that truly the work I want?


 Established executives and former owners
 Separate influence from authority, building from stewardship, and meaningful contribution from the burden of
 ultimate responsibility.

 START WITH: I sold my business. How do I design a worthwhile next chapter?


 NorthStar does not need to know your destination at the beginning. Discovering
 what the right question is can be part of the work.

 What can I take away?
 At any point, ask NorthStar to organize your conversation into a useful document. You do not have to
 complete a formal assessment first.

 01 NorthStar Map
 The main career-direction deliverable. A substantive synthesis of evidence, transferable capabilities, recurring
 themes, motivations, environmental preferences, constraints, directions to investigate, and unanswered
 questions.

 ASK: Create my NorthStar Map based on what we have discussed.



 02 Coaching Prep Packet
 Organizes career examples, emerging insights, important tensions, and focused questions for a conversation with
 a human coach, mentor, or trusted adviser.

 ASK: I meet with a career coach next week. Prepare my Coaching Prep Packet.



 03 Career Decision Brief
 Separates known facts, assumptions, priorities, tradeoffs, uncertainties, and questions to investigate when
 considering a promotion, offer, transition, or competing opportunities. It does not select an option on your behalf.

 ASK: Help me evaluate this opportunity against what we have learned.



  You can ask early.
  If there is not enough information for a complete document, NorthStar can create a provisional version and
  clearly identify what still needs exploration.

 More ways to use your insights
 The goal is not simply to produce documents. It is to improve the next conversation and the next
 decision.

 04 Opportunity Fit Checklist
 Turns the priorities and conditions you have identified into questions for evaluating prospective roles and
 employers. It is personalized, not an automatic numerical fit score.

 ASK: Create a checklist I can take into interviews to evaluate fit.



 05 Session Snapshot
 A shorter portable record of your current discoveries, priorities, and open questions. Copy it into a future NorthStar
 conversation to continue without reconstructing everything.

 ASK: Save my progress. Create a Session Snapshot.



 Brief reflection
 Not every milestone calls for a complete document. Ask for a short reflection of what NorthStar has observed so
 far, and correct anything that does not fit.

 ASK: Where am I so far? What patterns are emerging?




   Keep the Map rich. Keep the Snapshot portable.
   A NorthStar Map is for depth and decision-making. A Session Snapshot is for continuity. The two have different
   purposes and should not be interchangeable.

 Put it into practice
 Your insights are most valuable when they change the questions you ask and the opportunities you
 investigate.

 Build a cohesive professional story
 Connect where you have been, what you have demonstrated, the environments that have amplified your
 strengths, and what you want to do next. A professional story does not have to be perfectly linear to be credible.


 Evaluate the business case, not just the job title
 What problem is the employer trying to solve? What authority, resources, support, and measures of success come
 with the mandate? What would a successful first year actually look like?


 Use interviews as two-way discovery
 Bring your Opportunity Fit Checklist. Interviewing is an opportunity to verify your own assumptions, not merely
 demonstrate that you meet a description.


 Carry your insights into the next task
 Career direction can inform how you describe your experience and prepare for an actual opportunity.
 Ask NorthStar to summarize relevant evidence, themes, and questions in a handoff you control.



  TRY ASKING

  Help me turn my NorthStar Map into five questions I should ask before accepting this role.

 Make the conversation yours
 NorthStar is intended to be useful to experienced professionals, not burdensome to use.

 Bring real examples
 One specific project, difficult decision, customer situation, or successful period often reveals more than ten broad
 adjectives.


 Challenge what does not fit
 Tell NorthStar when it overstates a pattern, gets a fact wrong, overlooks important context, or draws a conclusion
 that feels inconsistent with your experience.


 Explore the apparent contradictions
 You can enjoy leadership without wanting more people administration; seek influence without ownership; or enjoy
 new business without preferring high-volume cold prospecting.


 Ask for the depth you need
 Request another question, a fuller analysis, a provisional deliverable, or a different perspective. There is no
 mandatory sequence.


 Stop and resume
 Ask for a Session Snapshot whenever you need to leave, then paste it into a new conversation when ready.



  AI can be wrong.
  NorthStar is powered by ChatGPT. It may misunderstand context, miss information, produce inaccurate
  details, or make unsupported interpretations. Correct its mistakes and independently verify important facts.
  Do not enter unnecessary confidential or sensitive personal information.



 Use the output as a conversation and decision-support resource, not as a definitive assessment, a guarantee, or a
 substitute for qualified advice where needed.

 Your next question starts here
 You do not need to have your entire career figured out before exploring it.

 Start with what you know. Be candid about what you do not. As useful patterns emerge, ask
 NorthStar to organize them into a Map, a decision framework, a coaching preparation document, or
 questions for your next opportunity.




  Better preparation. Not manufactured answers.
  NorthStar helps you understand and communicate the real version of your experience. You remain responsible
  for what you say, submit, and decide.




 THREE SIMPLE WAYS TO BEGIN

 “What I would like to understand about my career is…”

 “I am facing a decision, and I am not sure what I should be asking…”

 “Help me connect my experiences into a clearer professional story.”
