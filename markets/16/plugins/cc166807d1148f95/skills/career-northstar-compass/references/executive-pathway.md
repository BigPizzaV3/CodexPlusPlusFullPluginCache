# Executive career discovery

Use when senior leaders are exploring role design, leadership scope, executive progression or an intentional move into an individual-contributor position. Draw on the user-supplied May 2025 Executive Compass instructions and `source/TRX_Executive_Compass_Knowledge_Base.txt`.

Explore in a conversational sequence, selecting only what resolves their issue:
- Strategic orientation: external/internal, horizon, strategic/operational involvement, appetite for ambiguity.
- Operating mode: building, optimization, integration, transformation, technical leadership, maintaining momentum. Treat Builder/Optimizer/Architect as conversational metaphors, not psychometrics.
- Evidence of impact: scope, decision rights, personal contribution, financial/operational/customer outcomes, constraints.
- What authority and resources they actually need; manager/board/ownership relationship, leadership team and cultural maturity.
- Preferred scale, pace, team responsibilities, travel, compensation design, and risk trade-offs when relevant.
- Their legacy or impact statement: what they want to leave stronger and what they no longer want to own.

Probe: “Which decisions did you personally own?” “Was the achievement primarily a scale problem, a process problem, or a people problem?” “Would an individual contributor role remove an unwanted burden, or also remove something that energizes you?” “What kind of organizational problem do you want to solve next?”

A title is not evidence of equivalent scope. Do not assume leadership requires people management. Offer 2-3 conditional role/environment hypotheses and identify outstanding questions. The supplemental knowledge base's May 2025 compensation numbers are historical illustrative notes, not current benchmarks; do not repeat them as present market facts. For detailed resumes and interview scripts, refer to dedicated TRX tools.
