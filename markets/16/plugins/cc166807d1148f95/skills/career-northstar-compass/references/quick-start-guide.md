# Career NorthStar Compass™ | Quick Start Guide (accessible text companion)

This guide is included inside Career NorthStar Compass™ and can be shown in the conversation whenever requested. The expanded Field Guide is also included as `field-guide-text.md`. No outside website or download is needed to use either guide, and neither is a prerequisite for coaching. Offer the requested content directly in chat. Do not direct users to external PDF downloads. If they ask for a PDF, clarify that this plugin does not offer a hosted file download and provide copyable formatted text.

**What this is for:** NorthStar helps you reflect on your experience, ask better questions, connect the pieces into a cohesive and truthful career story, and clarify the factors that matter in your decisions. The insights and documents are conversation and decision-support tools, not answers handed down to you. Challenge or change any interpretation; your judgment remains central.

**You do not need a résumé, a target title, or a polished career story.** Start with what is on your mind. NorthStar explores one good question at a time. Short answers are fine; give concrete work examples when possible, and correct interpretations that don't fit.

**Try:** “I know I want something different, but I'm not sure what.” “I have an opportunity to consider and need to understand its fit.” “I have a coaching appointment coming up.”

**You can ask any time:**
- “Where am I so far?” — concise reflection.
- “Create my NorthStar Map.” — substantive career direction, evidence, hypotheses, environment fit, next steps, and when appropriate an Opportunity Fit Checklist.
- “Prepare my Coaching Prep Packet.” — structured observations and questions to bring to a human coach.
- “Help me evaluate this career decision.” — Career Decision Brief on what is known, tradeoffs, uncertainties, and next actions.
- “Give me an Opportunity Fit Checklist.” — practical criteria to use when exploring a role.
- “Save my progress.” — a portable Session Summary Snapshot.

**Returning later:** Copy/save the Session Snapshot. In a new chat paste it and say “Here is my NorthStar Profile. Please pick up where we left off.” Chat history does not automatically transfer between sessions. You choose what to save and share.

**Specialized next step:** NorthStar focuses on career direction and decision preparation, rather than writing a complete resume or conducting a mock interview. If you want to continue those tasks elsewhere, you can ask for a copyable handoff brief. You choose what, if anything, to share.

**AI accuracy:** NorthStar is powered by ChatGPT and can make mistakes. It may misunderstand your experience, miss context, or offer an interpretation that does not fit. Check important details and assumptions, correct anything inaccurate, and use your own judgment and trusted people when making consequential decisions.

**Remember:** Use your Map, Packet, Brief, or Snapshot to drive conversations and your own reflection, not as a substitute for judgment. All interpretations are open to correction. This is not a psychometric diagnosis or hiring prediction.
