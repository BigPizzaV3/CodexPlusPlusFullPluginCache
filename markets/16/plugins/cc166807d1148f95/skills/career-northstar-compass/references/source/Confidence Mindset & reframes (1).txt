Core Coaching Beliefs About Confidence

Confidence comes from evidence, not hype.

Momentum builds confidence; stagnation drains it.

Clarity reduces anxiety.

People don’t need to feel confident to take action — action produces confidence.

Most job search fear is rooted in uncertainty, not inadequacy.

You don’t have to be “the best,” you have to be aligned.

Common Fears & TRX Reframes
Fear: “I don’t know what I’m targeting.”
Reframe: “We’ll narrow it. Direction is created, not discovered.”

Fear: “My resume isn’t good enough.”
Reframe: “It just needs to tell the right story, clearly.”

Fear: “My experience is too all over.”
Reframe: “Patterns exist. We’ll surface them.”

Fear: “I don’t want to bother people.”
Reframe: “You’re asking for insight, not a job.”

Fear: “What if I choose wrong?”
Reframe: “Your next role is a chapter, not a sentence.”

Evidence-Based Confidence Builders

List 5 accomplishments

Identify patterns in compliments

Map past moments of effectiveness

Identify 3 strengths you consistently use

Review people who have succeeded with similar experience

Look for repeat outcomes you’ve created

Mindset Shifts for Job Search

From “I need a job” → “I need alignment.”

From “I must impress” → “I must show who I am.”

From “I need perfection” → “I need clarity.”

From “I can’t ask for help” → “People help people they like.”

From “The market is terrible” → “The market is strategic.”

Signals of Burnout to Name (Not Shame)

Decision fatigue

Low motivation

Confusion about direction

Over-accommodation

Avoiding conflict

Feeling ineffective despite evidence to the contrary

What to Do When Overwhelmed

Narrow to 1–2 tasks/day

Reset expectations

Re-anchor in strengths

Revisit “must be present/must be absent”

Take one step that creates clarity

Get honest about energy, not just goals
