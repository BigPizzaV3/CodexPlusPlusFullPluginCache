30-Day Job Search Sprint Plan (TRX Style)
Week 1 — Positioning & Clarity
* Finalize top-half resume
* LinkedIn headline + About rewrite
* Target role list (Safe / Stretch / Pivot)
* 30–40 target companies
* Identify 10 connectors (people who know people)
Week 2 — Outreach & Visibility
* Reach out to 2–3 people/day
* Warm intros first
* Light-touch check-ins
* Comment meaningfully on industry posts
* Update LI with a subtle activity post (not “I’m open,” but value-driven)
Week 3 — Applications & Introductions
* Apply selectively (max 3–5 meaningful roles/week)
* Send networking messages for insider context
* Ask for 1–2 referrals/week
* Track all responses in a simple sheet or board
Week 4 — Follow-Up & Expansion
* Follow up on every touchpoint
* Expand network by 10–15 new connections
* Reassess patterns: what’s working? what’s cold?
* Tighten story + interview answers
Three-Lane Targeting Model
* Lane 1: Safe – Direct match to your experience
* Lane 2: Stretch – Adjacent roles you can do with 20–30% lift
* Lane 3: Pivot – Roles requiring narrative support & positioning
Keep a 60/30/10 balance (Safe/Stretch/Pivot).
Outreach Cadence (Warm)
Day 1: Intro message
Day 3: “Wanted to follow up in case this got buried.”
Day 7: Soft bump
Day 14: One final follow-up
Then drop or shift to long-term nurture.
Outreach Cadence (Cold but Targeted)
* Short message
* Mutual context
* One question (insight, not job ask)
* Light thanks
* No pressure
Networking Message Templates (Kathleen Tone)
Warm Ask
* “I’d love your insight on…”
* “You’re someone I trust in this space…”
* “Would you be open to sharing your perspective?”
Cold Intro
* “Saw your work in…”
* “I’m exploring roles in X…”
* “If you’re open, I’d value one perspective…”
Referral Request
* “Is there anyone you think I should talk with?”
Weekly Activity Metrics
* 10–15 conversations
* 10 targeted outreach messages
* 3–5 applications
* 1–2 informational calls
* 1 post or comment thread
* Resume/LI polish ongoing
Red Flags in Strategy
* Applying to everything
* Zero messaging
* Inconsistent story
* Over-indexing on job boards
* No follow-up
* Waiting for recruiters to find you
* Panic-pivoting without clarity
