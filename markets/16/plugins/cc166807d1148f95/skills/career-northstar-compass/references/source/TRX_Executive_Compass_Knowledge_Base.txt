
TRX Executive Compass™ – Supplemental Knowledge Base
----------------------------------------------------

This document contains additional calibration content, insights, and examples to enhance the GPT’s ability to guide candidates through career alignment, positioning, and trajectory planning using the Executive Compass™ framework.

1. INDUSTRY BENCHMARK INSIGHTS
------------------------------
> Sectors commonly served:
- Building materials (adhesives, formwork, fenestration, coatings)
- Specialty manufacturing and engineered products
- Precast and construction systems
- Technical sales and field-based leadership roles

> Common themes:
- In chemicals and coatings, field support and spec-writing experience are often more important than “new logo” sales.
- In engineered building products, trust-building and long sales cycles mean messaging must show *persistence, not just quick wins*.

2. CANDIDATE VALUE FRAMEWORK
-----------------------------
> Use this when evaluating or advising candidates:
- Strategic Fit = Motivators + Strengths + Ideal Leadership Environment
- Misalignment Red Flags:
  - “Wants stability” paired with chaotic growth-stage org
  - Desires “mentorship” but target companies have hands-off leaders

3. COMPENSATION POSITIONING CLUES
---------------------------------
> Clues for interpreting value:
- VP of Ops in building materials: $150K–$180K + 15–25% bonus
- BD Director (chemicals): $130K–$150K base + commission or project bonuses
- Field Service Tech with mechanical expertise: $70K–$90K + heavy travel + vehicle allowance

4. CANDIDATE MESSAGING CLARITY FILTER
-------------------------------------
Ask GPT to evaluate:
- Is it specific?
- Does it show evolution/growth?
- Is it role-aligned?
- Does it reflect what this person *wants next*?

5. CULTURE FIT SIGNALS (FROM TRX WORK)
--------------------------------------
> Common positive traits:
- Relationship-first communicator
- Resourceful in ambiguous environments
- Open to coaching and feedback
- Strong internal “ownership” drive

> Common risks:
- Overreliance on structure
- Lack of metrics or unclear scope of impact
- Poor storytelling or no defined “why now”

6. TRX VOICE REMINDERS
----------------------
- Professional but direct. Don’t sugarcoat.
- Use examples wherever possible.
- Tie feedback to **alignment** and **impact**—not just style.
