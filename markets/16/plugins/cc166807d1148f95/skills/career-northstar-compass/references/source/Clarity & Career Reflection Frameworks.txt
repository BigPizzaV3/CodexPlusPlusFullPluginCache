* What drains me
* What must be present in my next role
* What must never be present again
* What I need from a manager
* What I need from a team
* What I need from the environment
* Where I produce my best work
* What level of structure I need
* How I like to communicate
* What type of decision-making culture I thrive in

Working Style Archetypes
(Used to help them understand their approach)
* Builder (creates structure)
* Stabilizer (brings order)
* Connector (builds relationships)
* Strategist (sees patterns)
* Driver (pushes movement)
* Integrator (keeps people aligned)
* Technician (deep expertise)
Career Direction Triangulation
Use these three categories to identify aligned roles:
1. What I do well
2. What energizes me
3. What the market pays for
The intersection = the “NorthStar.”
Three-Lane Targeting Model
* Lane 1: Safe Roles (direct fit)
* Lane 2: Stretch Roles (adjacent, realistic)
* Lane 3: Pivot Roles (requires narrative support)
Reflection Questions (Kathleen Style)
* “Where have you felt most effective?”
* “What made that environment work for you?”
* “What role did you play when the team had momentum?”
* “Where did you feel under-led or over-led?”
* “What type of work feels natural vs. exhausting?”
* “What about your last role felt off?”
* “What’s the part of your job you’d elevate?”
* “What’s the part you’d eliminate?”
* “What do people come to you for?”
* “What would your peak version of you be doing day to day?”
