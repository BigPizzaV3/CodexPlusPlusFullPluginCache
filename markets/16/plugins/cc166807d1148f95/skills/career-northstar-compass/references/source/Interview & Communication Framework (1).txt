Interview Foundation Questions

Who are you professionally?

What are you good at?

Where do you do your best work?

What environments drain you?

What do you want next?

What does a healthy team/leader look like for you?

Behavioral Interview Structure
Answer Length: 60–90 seconds
Format:

Situation

Action

Outcome

What I learned

Why it matters for you (the company)

“Tell Me About Yourself” Structure

Who you are

What you’ve done

What you’re good at

What drives you

What you want next

Why this role/company

Communication Strengths to Highlight

Translating technical → practical

Bringing calm to ambiguity

Breaking down complex problems

Building trust quickly

Adapting approach to audience

Blending relationship + results

Common Interview Blind Spots

Rambling

Starting too far back in history

Listing duties instead of outcomes

Speaking in generalities

Apologizing for experience gaps

Oversharing emotionally

Not tailoring story to role/company

TRX-Style Interview Prep Questions

“What’s the real problem this role solves?”

“What strengths of yours directly support that?”

“What example proves it?”

“What outcome can you claim with evidence?”

“Where might they see risk — and how do you address it?”

Post-Interview Follow-Up Message (Kathleen Tone)

Short

Warm

Value-anchored

Clear interest

No desperation

Example:
“Thank you again for the conversation today. I’m thinking a lot about X challenge you mentioned and how my experience in Y might support it. I’d welcome the chance to continue the dialogue.”

How to Discuss Weaknesses (Truth + Ownership)

Own it without drama

Provide context

Show how you manage it

Offer evidence of improvement

Keep tone steady and confident
