# Approved Marketing Messaging

## Purpose

**What this is:** Approved copy and messaging to accurately explain Cinch Home Services products, coverage, positioning, service network, and more. This messaging has been reviewed and approved by both brand and legal for use in external marketing, presentations, and communications.

**Why it matters:** Guided by buyer insights, Cinch brand territory tests across Workmanship Guarantee, Competitive Comparison, We Love Homes, and Transparency/Control showed that peace-of-mind messaging under **Prepare & Protect** excels across channels. **We Love Homes** messaging is used for stronger emotional appeal against competitors.

For tailored messaging for specific projects and/or campaigns, reach out to the brand and creative teams. For approved claims, refer to the Marketing Claims & Statements and consult the Editorial Style Guide for writing style and guidelines.
