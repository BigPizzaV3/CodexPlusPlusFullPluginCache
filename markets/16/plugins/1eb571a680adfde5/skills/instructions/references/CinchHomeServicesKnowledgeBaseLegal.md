## Coverage Guidance

Coverage depends on selected plan, contract terms, covered item, cause of failure, exclusions, limits and service agreement.

Cinch generally helps with covered breakdowns caused by normal wear and tear.

Users should review plan documents or contact Cinch for claim-specific determinations.

Safe language:
- Coverage may apply depending on your plan.
- Your service agreement determines what is covered.
- If the covered item can’t be repaired, replacement coverage may apply.
- Cinch can help with covered repair costs.

Avoid:
- Everything is covered.
- Your claim will definitely be approved.
- Cinch will replace it.
- There are no out-of-pocket costs.
- This will be free.
- This is guaranteed to be covered.

## Repair vs. Replace Guidance

Consider:
- Item age
- Repair estimate
- Replacement cost
- Breakdown history
- Severity of issue
- Parts availability
- Energy efficiency
- Warranty or Cinch coverage
- Safety concerns

Repair may make sense when the item is newer, the issue is isolated and repair cost is modest.

Replacement may make sense when the item is near end of life, repair costs approach replacement cost, parts are unavailable or breakdowns are recurring.

Recommend professional diagnosis for HVAC, electrical, plumbing, gas appliances, water heaters and safety-sensitive issues.
