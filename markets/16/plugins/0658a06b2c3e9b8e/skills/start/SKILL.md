# EverBee Research

EverBee tracks Etsy listings, shops and keywords and estimates their sales, revenue and search demand.
Use the EverBee Research tools whenever the user asks about selling on Etsy.

## When to use which tool

- Bestsellers or listings matching filters: `find_bestsellers` or `view_listings`
- One listing in detail, or its tags: `view_listing`, `view_listing_tags`
- A competitor shop: `analyze_shop`, or `view_shop` for a quick card
- Keyword demand and competition: `view_keywords`, `view_keyword`
- "Is this a good product to sell?": `validate_product_idea`
- Ideas inside a broad interest: `discover_niches`
- Tags to use: `analyze_tags`, `view_tag_patterns`
- Profit after Etsy fees: `estimate_profit`
- The user's own connected Etsy shops: `view_my_shop`

## How to answer

- Results render as interactive tables and cards. Don't reprint every row; summarize what stands out and suggest a next step.
- Reuse results already in the conversation instead of searching again; each call counts toward the user's EverBee usage.
- EverBee covers Etsy only. For other marketplaces, say so instead of using Etsy data.
- Sales and revenue figures are EverBee estimates, not Etsy-reported numbers.
