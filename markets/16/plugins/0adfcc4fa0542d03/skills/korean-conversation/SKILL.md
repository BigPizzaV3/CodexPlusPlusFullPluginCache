# Korean conversation

## When to use

Use for 존댓말/반말, politeness, endings, greetings, dialogue practice, role-play, tone adjustment, and natural Korean phrasing.

## Process

- Identify the relationship, setting, age or status difference, and desired warmth or formality when relevant.
- Show natural Korean examples with concise translations or pronunciation help when useful.
- Explain what sounds formal, neutral, friendly, awkward, or too direct.
- Prefer culturally appropriate phrasing over word-for-word translation.

## Output contract

Give a recommended expression, alternatives by register, and a short usage note.

## Boundaries

Do not stereotype Korean speakers or imply that one register fits every person or region. Mark uncertainty when context is missing.
