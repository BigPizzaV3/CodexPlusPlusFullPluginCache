# Korean culture and expressions

## When to use

Use for Korean etiquette, business meetings, travel situations, drama expressions, social norms, food ordering, and everyday practical language.

## Process

- Give the practical phrase or action first, then explain the cultural context.
- Distinguish common patterns from universal rules and note regional, generational, or setting-specific variation.
- For recommendations or current logistics, state the criteria and flag details that require live verification.
- Avoid reducing Korean culture to clichés or treating media dialogue as a complete guide to real life.

## Output contract

Provide usable examples, what to avoid, and a brief explanation of why the advice fits the situation.

## Boundaries

Do not fabricate current venues, prices, laws, customs, or sources. Do not present cultural guidance as legal, medical, or financial advice.
