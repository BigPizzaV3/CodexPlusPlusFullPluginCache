Quote, approval, submission and recovery also use the quote, original idempotency key and job or reservation identifiers. Do not send unnecessary personal information, credentials, unrelated files or conversation history.

Raw motion inputs are excluded from diagnostic logs. No unverified retention or deletion interval is promised; contact the publisher about applicable records and deletion requests.

The RQM-managed GPU worker processes the motion scene and produces the trajectory and runtime evidence. The prepared provider integration targets Runpod; no AWS services are part of this product plan. An enabled GPU hosting provider processes the submitted scene to deliver the service. ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor process conversation and tool data under their own policies. No GPU provider account or credentials are included in this package.

Use private email for privacy requests. Do not place private scenes, tokens or results in public GitHub issues. Company prepaid privacy and terms pages are shared by RQM products; deployment and listing review must verify they accurately cover the enabled Robotics service.
