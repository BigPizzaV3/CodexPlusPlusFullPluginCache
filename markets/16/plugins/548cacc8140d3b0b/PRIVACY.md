# Olympic Weightlifting Program Builder: privacy notice

Effective for plugin version 0.2.0, prepared 3 October 2026.

## Who provides this plugin

Weightlifting Analytics provides the plugin instructions. Contact: yegeunji@outlook.com. Website: https://www.weightliftinganalytics.com. This notice supplements the website policy at https://www.weightliftinganalytics.com/privacy and describes this plugin version.

## What happens to your information

The basic skill generates a plan using training details you choose to share in chat, without a website account or account access. It does not automatically send that conversation to the website.

The optional account skill instructs your AI assistant to use the Weightlifting Analytics website through browser-control tools. You sign in on the website using your existing account. The package has no separate MCP service, OAuth connection, credential store, analytics endpoint, or database.

At your request, the assistant may view relevant account information, personal bests, training history, goals, exercise rules, and programs that your website account can access. Relevant page content may be processed in your AI host's conversation context. The workflow does not request Apple Health, sleep, hydration, medication, or unrelated administrative data. Avoid sharing unrelated sensitive information in program notes.

When you request a save or edit, the assistant enters the program into the website. Saved programs use the website's existing database, account permissions, sharing rules, and retention. An athlete assignment is separate from saving a template. Coaches must have the appropriate relationship and editing permissions.

Passwords and verification codes are entered by you into the website or its normal identity provider, not into chat. The plugin does not extract or retain session tokens. Browser sessions and credentials remain managed by the browser and authentication provider.

## Optional program import links

The basic skill can encode a generic program in a website link. Anyone holding that link can read the program. Do not include names, contact details, medical information or private notes. Opening the link sends its program to Weightlifting Analytics for validation and preview, but does not create an account or save a program by itself.

Choosing the website's save/signup action stores the pending program in this browser's local storage for up to 24 hours, to resume after authentication and profile setup. This copy is removed on successful import, cancellation, or the next access after expiry. You can also clear it through browser site-data settings. Different browsers do not share this pending state; reopen the original link if you switch devices. Saved programs and an account-linked fingerprint used to prevent duplicate imports remain in the website database under its existing account and deletion policies. Links and copies in chat remain with their holders.

## Your AI host

The platform running the assistant processes prompts, responses, files, and any browser content according to its own privacy policy, account settings, and retention rules. The plugin developer cannot promise that the host never stores or uses conversations. For OpenAI products, see https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/ and your account's data controls. Other hosts have their own policies.

## Storage, deletion, and control

This package does not add a separate developer-operated data store or advertising service. Deleting a chat or exported file is handled by the host or device holding it. Contact the AI host for its data-access, export, deletion, and retention controls.

You can stop browser actions, sign out of the website in the browser being used, or disable the plugin. This stops that workflow but does not erase programs already saved or copies already present in the conversation. Website account and training-data requests can be sent to yegeunji@outlook.com; the website policy explains account deletion. Your browser session may remain active until you sign out, even after removing the plugin.

Contact yegeunji@outlook.com about the plugin or website data. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data, or object to or request restriction of processing. You may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Requests about the AI host's copies must be directed to that provider.

## Changes

Changes to data access, storage, or authentication require an updated notice and plugin release. This notice describes the current instruction-based plugin; it does not describe a future hosted API integration.
