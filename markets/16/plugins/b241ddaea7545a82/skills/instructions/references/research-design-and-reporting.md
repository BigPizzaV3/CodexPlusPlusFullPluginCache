# Research design and transparent reporting

## Design map
- Determine whether experiment/RCT, quasi-experiment, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, case series, survey, diagnostic accuracy, prediction model, or secondary analysis. Identify sampling/selection and time sequence before selecting statistics.
- Specify PICO/PECO/PIRD (or appropriate question structure), primary outcome and estimand, population, follow-up, intervention/exposure and relevant comparator. If exploratory, say so.
- Clinical trial method discussions are **educational**. Do not generate patient-specific treatment advice or use data to decide real clinical interventions.

## Reporting checklists are not validation of research quality
- RCT report: CONSORT 2025; protocol: SPIRIT 2025. Both must reflect actual conduct; compliance with checklist does not prove low bias.
- Observational epidemiology: STROBE, choose cohort/case-control/cross-sectional variant. Distinguish manuscript reporting from risk-of-bias analysis.
- Prediction model: verify relevant TRIPOD+AI guidance edition and extension by current official source at time of use; avoid asserting that checklist alone proves model quality.
- Diagnostic accuracy: refer to STARD reporting framework; verify applicable version/current updates in live sources.
- QI studies: SQUIRE 2.0; align only if truly healthcare improvement and not an RCT mislabeled as QI.
- Apply a specific extension only if it matches design and population. Cite current guideline versions when the user requests web updates.

## Audit and traceability
- Keep a decision log: variable dictionary, pre-specified versus post-hoc analyses, exclusions, missing data, transformations, assumptions, model specification, multiplicity, sensitivity analyses, software and package version (if actually executed).
- Check consistency: sample size in abstract/methods/tables, group denominators, sums/percentages, numerator/denominator labels, CI/p-value against effect estimate where recomputable.
- Avoid statements like “statistically proven” or “significant clinical benefit” solely from p<0.05. Discuss effect size, imprecision, bias, residual confounding, reproducibility and applicability.
- If only a PDF abstract or summary table is available, do not claim independent patient-level analysis.

## Verified source starting points (checked by direct open at development time 2026-10-28; re-verify before use)
- CONSORT 2025 original statement: https://www.bmj.com/content/389/bmj-2024-081123
- STROBE directory: https://equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/strobe/
- SPIRIT/CONSORT official project: https://www.consort-spirit.org/published-statements
These point to reporting frameworks rather than prescribing a statistical test. Verify each live source and specific version when using it for a user.
