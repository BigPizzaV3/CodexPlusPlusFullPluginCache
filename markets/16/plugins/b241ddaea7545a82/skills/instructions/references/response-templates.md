# Output templates for Data Analyst 4HP

## 1. Plan from research question (no dataset)
**Study design / units:** [known / missing]
**Objective / estimand:** [target parameter]
**Variable map:** [exposure, outcome, confounders and coding]
**Analysis plan:** [descriptive, main contrasts, assumptions, sensitivity analyses]
**Required source data:** [minimum rows/columns or summaries]
**Warnings:** [no invented result]

## 2. Dataset audit
**Observed data:** source/format, analyzed vs displayed rows; unit of analysis.
**Quality checks performed:** show exact count and checks actually run; distinguish possible vs proven anomalies.
**Data dictionary:** name, type, unit, missing codes, group coding.
**Issues and next steps:** rank by their impact on primary outcome; never silently remove observations.

## 3. Result interpretation
| Quantity | Value and unit | Calculation/source | Interpretation | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sample size | n=... only if observed | [file/table] | [unit] | [missing exclusions] |
| Effect measure | [not computed/observed value] | [formula/tool] | [magnitude and direction] | [bias/confounding] |
| Precision | [CI or not available] | [method] | [uncertainty] | [assumptions] |

**Conclusion:** what is supported; do not claim causality or generalizability without reason.
**What remains unknown:** list material information needed.

## 4. Brief teaching explanation
- Definition in ordinary terms.
- Transparent formula and small **SIMULATED** example.
- One interpretation trap and how to avoid it.
- Optional one follow-up exercise, with answers only if requested.

## 5. Update by Bing
### Bản cập nhật
Rewritten response with verified citations at claims.
### Những gì đã thay đổi
New/corrected/retracted points; indicate unresolved items.
### Đã kiểm tra nguồn
Only links directly opened and actually used; note source-access level and relevant date.
