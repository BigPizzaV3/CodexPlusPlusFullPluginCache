# Dreamteam CRM for ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT about your Dreamteam CRM in plain words: your day, your pipeline, your next meeting, or one deal, contact or company. ChatGPT answers from your Dreamteam workspace, drafts follow-up emails, and adds or updates records after you confirm.

Dreamteam CRM connects only to Dreamteam, through its Dreamteam app (`https://mcp.dreamteamcrm.ai/mcp`), and sees only what your Dreamteam role allows. It stores nothing, changes records only after you reply **yes**, never deletes records and never sends email.

## What you can ask

| Skill | Try asking | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Daily brief | "Brief me on my day", "What's overdue?" | Reads only |
| Pipeline health | "How's my pipeline?" | Reads only |
| Meeting prep | "Prep me for my next meeting" | Reads only |
| View a record | "Show me my largest open deal", "Who is Priya Shah?" | Reads only |
| Draft an email | "Draft a follow-up email for my biggest deal" | Reads only; drafts, never sends |
| Update a record | "Move the close date of my largest open deal to the last day of next month" | Writes only after you confirm |
| Add a record | "Add Priya Shah from Acme as a contact", or a photo of a business card | Writes only after you confirm |
| Dreamteam help | "What can Dreamteam do?" | Reads only |

Most answers end with short next steps you can type, such as *draft reply to Northwind*.

## Get started

1. Install Dreamteam CRM from the directory: click **Plugins** in the ChatGPT sidebar, search for **Dreamteam CRM**, then **Install plugin**.
2. Choose **Connect** and sign in with your Dreamteam account. There's no API key to set up. On Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces, an admin may need to make the plugin available first.
3. Start a new chat, type **@Dreamteam CRM**, then "Brief me on my day". Start each new chat with **@Dreamteam CRM**, or ChatGPT may answer without the plugin's skills.

We recommend the permission **Allow read-only tools** under **Settings → Plugins → Dreamteam CRM**, on its Dreamteam app: ChatGPT reads without asking and asks before any change. To revoke access, disconnect your account there.

## If something doesn't work

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT answers without using the plugin | Start a new chat with **@Dreamteam CRM** |
| ChatGPT can't reach Dreamteam | Choose **Reconnect** under **Settings → Plugins → Dreamteam CRM** |
| Times look off | Tell ChatGPT your time zone, for example "I'm in Pacific time" |
