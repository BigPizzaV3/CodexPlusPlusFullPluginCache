--- PAGE 13 ---
Your Ropes and Anchors:
Radical Specification

If you don’t write the spec, a quality
problem is your fault.

What to Specify:

- Materials (#E95A27): What alloy? What
type of cloth?

- Measurements (#E95A27): Weight,
thickness, gauge, density, stitches per inch.

- Objective Tests (#E95A27): Refer to specific
lab tests (e.g., ASTM F963 for toys).

The Principle: “Trust, but verify.”
