## 1. Budget commentary

Request: "How are we doing against budget this year?" Files: `budget_fy26_v3.csv`, `actuals_jan_aug.csv`.

**Draft (151 words)**

> Great question! Looking at the data, overall performance this year has been somewhat mixed relative to the budget. Revenue came in slightly below expectations at approximately $35.6M, which represents a variance of around -1.7% compared to the budgeted figure of $36.2M.
>
> On the cost side, there were some favorable trends, particularly in cost of revenue, which helped offset some of the revenue shortfall. However, it's worth noting that Sales & Marketing spending was notably elevated compared to plan, coming in at roughly $8.1M versus $7.8M budgeted, while R&D was broadly in line and G&A was a little higher than expected.
>
> Taken together, these factors meant that EBITDA was impacted, landing at around $4.6M versus a budget of $5.26M. This is a meaningful shortfall that may warrant further investigation, especially given the trends in Sales & Marketing. Let me know if you'd like me to dig deeper into any of these areas!

**Rewrite (72 words and a table)**

> EBITDA is $4.62M for Jan–Aug 2026. This is $0.64M (12%) below budget v3.
>
> Two causes explain most of the gap:
>
> - Revenue is $35.59M. This is $0.61M (1.7%) below budget. Lower cost of revenue recovers $0.29M of it.
> - Sales and marketing is $8.09M. This is $0.29M (3.7%) above budget.
>
> R&D is $0.04M below budget. G&A is $0.08M above budget.
>
> | Jan–Aug 2026, $K | Actual | Budget v3 | Better / (worse) |
> | :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
> | Revenue | 35,590 | 36,200 | (610) |
> | Cost of revenue | 13,829 | 14,120 | 291 |
> | Sales and marketing | 8,090 | 7,800 | (290) |
> | R&D | 5,560 | 5,600 | 40 |
> | G&A | 3,495 | 3,420 | (75) |
> | EBITDA | 4,616 | 5,260 | (644) |
>
> Next step: ask the sales lead which S&M costs are above plan.

**Changes**

- The answer moved from the last paragraph to the first sentence (rule 1).
- "Sales & Marketing" and "S&M" now name one metric. "Came in" and "landing at" became "is" (rule 3).
- Each figure has its period and its base. The rounded "approximately $35.6M" became the exact $35.59M (rule 4).
- "Favorable trends", "notably elevated" and "a little higher" became amounts above or below budget (rule 5).
- Six lines of three figures each moved into a table, with adverse figures in parentheses (rule 7).
- The greeting, the hedges and the closing offer are gone. One next step is left (rule 8).

## 2. Close summary

Request: "Write the September close summary for the CFO." Files: `tb_sep.csv`, `tb_aug.csv`.

**Draft**

> September was another solid month for the business, with revenue coming in at around $4.95M, which is a bit higher than August. Margins held up reasonably well at roughly 61.6%, although they did dip slightly by about 0.2%. EBITDA looks healthy at $0.79M and cash remains strong at $2.98M, which is down a little, mainly due to receivables.

**Rewrite**

> September revenue is $4.95M, up 1.6% on August. EBITDA is $0.79M, a 16.0% margin.
>
> | September 2026 | Sep | Change on Aug |
> | :--- | ---: | ---: |
> | Revenue | $4.95M | +1.6% |
> | Gross margin | 61.6% | −0.2 pts |
> | EBITDA | $0.79M | |
> | Cash at month end | $2.98M | −$70K |
>
> Cash fell $70K on August, mostly from higher receivables.

**Changes**

- The draft's "dip slightly by about 0.2%" was wrong. Gross margin went from 61.8% to 61.6%, which is 0.2 pts (rule 6).
- "A bit higher than August" became "up 1.6% on August". "Down a little" became "−$70K" (rule 4).
- "Solid", "reasonably well", "healthy" and "strong" judged without figures. The figures now carry the meaning (rule 8).

## 3. Board pack paragraph

**Draft**

> Gross margin decreased by 2% to 59.8% in Q3 compared to 61.8% in Q2, mainly driven by headwinds in freight and some pricing pressure in the EU, which we expect to ease going forward.

**Rewrite**

> Gross margin is 59.8% for Q3 2026, down 2.0 pts on Q2 (61.8%). Higher freight costs and lower EU prices caused most of the fall. Management expects both to ease in Q4.

**Changes**

- "Decreased by 2%" became "down 2.0 pts". A 2.0 pt fall from 61.8% is a 3.2% fall, so the draft understated it (rule 6).
- One sentence of 34 words became three sentences of 13, 12 and 7 words (rule 2).
- "Headwinds" and "pricing pressure" became the named causes. "We expect" now says who expects it, and "going forward" became Q4 (replacements).

## 4. Email

**Draft**

> Hi team, hope you're all well! Just wanted to give a quick heads up that it looks like we might be trending a little over on travel this quarter. We're at about $182K so far vs the $150K we had planned, so roughly 21% over. It would be great if everyone could try to keep an eye on this going forward. Let me know if you have any questions!

**Rewrite**

> Subject: Q3 travel is $32K (21%) over budget
>
> Hi team,
>
> Travel spend is $182K for Q3 2026, against a budget of $150K. This is $32K (21%) over budget.
>
> From October, book new trips only after your budget owner approves them.

**Changes**

- The subject line and the first sentence carry the answer (rule 1).
- "This quarter" became Q3 2026, and "planned" became budget (rule 4).
- The hedging ("it looks like we might be trending") and the closing offer are gone. The request became one dated action (rule 8).

## Checklist before handing back a rewrite

- [ ] Every figure in the rewrite is in the draft or was computed with the calculator, with the same rounding.
- [ ] The first sentence answers the question on its own.
- [ ] No sentence has more than 20 words.
- [ ] Every change in a rate is in points.
- [ ] Three or more related figures are in a table.
