# Meaning-preserving compression checklist

Before returning a compressed version, compare it with the source for:

- Negation, prohibitions, exceptions, scope, and qualifiers.
- Names, roles, dates, quantities, units, thresholds, and sequence.
- Requirements versus suggestions; facts versus hypotheses; confidence and attribution.
- Code, commands, paths, URLs, schemas, identifiers, quoted strings, and errors.
- Preconditions, safety warnings, consent, reversibility, and rollback instructions.
- Audience, requested format, and the actual decision or action the text supports.

Prefer deleting repetition, throat-clearing, redundant transitions, and irrelevant background. Preserve headings and structure when they help retrieval. Do not silently invent abbreviations. Keep the original available and state when compression is approximate. If a reduction would alter meaning, stop and explain the tradeoff.
