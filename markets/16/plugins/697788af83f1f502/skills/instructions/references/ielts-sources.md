Core orientation: Academic and General Training share Listening and Speaking; Reading and Writing differ [F1]. Writing lasts 60 minutes, with at least 150 words for Task 1 and at least 250 for Task 2; approximately 20/40 minutes is the usual allocation, and Task 2 carries twice Task 1's weight [W2]. Speaking lasts about 11–14 minutes with three parts; Part 2 allows one minute to prepare before the long turn [S2]. Do not assume an extra answer-transfer period applies to every delivery mode; verify current local delivery arrangements.
