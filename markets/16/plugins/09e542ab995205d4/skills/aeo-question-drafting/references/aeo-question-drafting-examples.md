# AEO question drafting examples


These examples come from Frontmost AI's own project (an AI visibility tracker, Nov 2026). Use them as patterns for any business by swapping the category, rivals and qualifiers. Do not copy the wording into another project unchanged.

### Good unbranded questions

| Question | Format | Stage | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|
| What are the best AI visibility tools right now? | List, head term | Comparing | The plain category query most cited list articles target. No qualifier, no year. |
| What are the best AEO tools for getting my brand recommended by ChatGPT? | List | Comparing | Uses the category's own vocabulary, which the earlier set never did. |
| How do I find out if ChatGPT recommends my company when people ask about my category? | Problem-first | Early research | Names the problem, not the tool category, the way someone new to the space would ask. |
| My clients keep asking if they show up in ChatGPT. What tool should I use to check and report on it? | Persona | Comparing | Lifted almost word for word from a Reddit thread that AI apps cite. A clear buyer and a clear job. |
| What are the best Peec AI alternatives? | Alternatives-to | Comparing | One named rival. Winnable, because the answer is an open list. |
| What's the best AI visibility tracker under $50 a month? | List with one qualifier | Ready to buy | A single budget qualifier that matches a verified strength. |
| Which AI visibility tools have an MCP server or work with Claude and ChatGPT agents? | Differentiator | Comparing | Built on a verified feature few list pages cover. |

### Good branded questions

| Question | What it tests | What it revealed on day one |
|---|---|---|
| What is Frontmost AI and who is it for? | Whether AI describes the business correctly | Two Google surfaces described Front, a different company. A fixable error the visibility score could not show. |
| Frontmost AI vs Profound: which should a startup pick? | A comparison with a top rival that had no question yet | Named first in 6 of 6 answers, with a clear positioning line. |
| Is Frontmost AI legit? Are there reviews from real users? | The trust gap | Named in 4 of 6. Exposes missing independent reviews. |
| What are the best alternatives to Frontmost AI? | Who AI positions the business against | Named in 4 of 6, with no position in the list. |

### Bad questions and how to fix them

| Bad question | What is wrong | Evidence or reason | Better version |
|---|---|---|---|
| For daily scans across several AI apps, what platforms do agencies recommend? | Ambiguous category | Answers recommended developer monitoring tools (LangSmith, Datadog) and security tools (CrowdStrike) | Which AI search visibility tools do agencies use to track client brand mentions every day? |
| Which would marketers recommend among Peec AI, Profound, and Otterly.AI for tracking AI search? | Unwinnable closed set | The AI is asked to choose among three named rivals, so the brand cannot be named. Six guaranteed zeros a day | Keep as rival intelligence outside the unbranded score, or replace with "What are the best Peec AI alternatives?" |
| Best AI visibility tools 2026 | Year and keyword fragment | Dates the question and reads like a search query, not a person | What are the best AI visibility tools right now? |
| What's the cheapest AI visibility tool with an MCP server for agencies in Texas? | Too many qualifiers | Price, feature, buyer and location are four intents in one. A miss tells you nothing | Split into one question per qualifier |
| Is Frontmost AI or Peec AI better for small teams? (filed as unbranded) | Brand name in an unbranded question | It measures recognition, not discovery, and inflates the unbranded score | File it as branded, or drop the brand: "What is a good Peec AI alternative for a small team?" |
| Why is Frontmost AI the best budget AI visibility tracker? | Leading claim | Presumes the answer, and "best budget" was never verified | How much does Frontmost AI cost, and is it worth it for a small software company? |
| What AI visibility platforms do marketers recommend? / What AI visibility tools do marketers suggest? | Accidental near-duplicate | Same meaning and same format, so the second adds cost and no information | Keep one. Make the variant a different format, such as the list-style head term |

### A good cluster

One intent (a small agency choosing a tracker for several clients), three formats:

- List: What are the best AI visibility tools for agencies?
- "Recommend": Recommend an AI visibility tool for a small agency tracking several clients.
- Persona: My clients keep asking if they show up in ChatGPT. What tool should I use to check and report on it?

Read the three together. If one wording names the brand and the others do not, that is noise until it repeats.

### A good approval row

| Question | Scope | Topic | Stage | Format | Cluster | Based on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| What are the best Peec AI alternatives? | Unbranded | Competitor comparisons | Comparing | Alternatives-to | Rival alternatives | Peec AI is among the most-named rivals; cited "Peec alternatives" threads and articles |

### A good first-results readout

"Total 21% (41 of 191). Branded 85% (41 of 48). Unbranded 0% (0 of 143). The 12 new unbranded questions got 0 of 72 and the original 12 got 0 of 71. Branded looks lower than yesterday only because the four new branded questions scored 18 of 24 while the original four held at 23 of 24. One answer did not come back. This is one scan; treat it as a first read."

It gives all three scopes with counts, separates old from new, explains the mix effect, states what is missing, and does not overclaim.
