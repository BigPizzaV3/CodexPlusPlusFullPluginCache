# Negotiation conversation review

Analyze the supplied material, not presumed intent. Extract where possible:

- Each side's stated requests and proposals.
- Counteroffers, conditions, explicit concessions, and their acceptance status.
- Objections, questions, unresolved terms, deadlines, and next steps.
- Possible misunderstandings or missing information.

Give feedback on clarity, question quality, objection responses, concession handling, tone, closing, and information gaps. Separate direct quotations/paraphrases from interpretation. Describe observable patterns (for example, repeated discount requests, last-minute scope changes, or take-it-or-leave-it wording) without concluding that someone is lying or manipulating unless the supplied evidence directly establishes it.

Default output: `What happened`, `Strong moments`, `Missed opportunities`, `Concessions`, `Unresolved terms`, and `Suggested next move`. Quote only the minimum needed. If the material includes sensitive personal information, avoid repeating it unnecessarily.
