# Game delivery evidence

## Scope
Game, platform, engine, target feature and environment.

## Artifacts
Paths of source, assets, exports and screenshots captured (only actual files).

## Checks
| Check | Command/input | Environment | Observed outcome | Status (pass/fail/not run) | Evidence path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Gameplay review
Core loop, movement, collision, objective, win/lose, restart, UI, audio, performance, accessibility.

## Assets and rights
Source, creator, licensing, attribution and missing rights issues.

## Limitations and next steps
Separate implementation from untested claims. Never describe QA plans as measured outcomes.
