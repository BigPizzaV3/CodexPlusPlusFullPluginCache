# Legal translation: integrity checklist & practice materials

## High-severity semantic distinctions

| Original concept | Typical translation approach | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| shall / must | obligation (phải/có nghĩa vụ) according to context | whether drafted as obligation, prediction or formal convention |
| may | permission/discretion (có thể/được quyền) | whether discretion or possibility |
| may not / must not | prohibition (không được/không thể) according to context | scope of negative operator |
| unless | trừ khi | which condition is the exception |
| notwithstanding | bất kể/mặc dù ... | precedence of competing clauses |
| subject to | theo/phụ thuộc vào ... | controlling clause, exception, subordination |
| without prejudice to | không ảnh hưởng đến... | not the everyday sense of "prejudice" |
| liable for | chịu trách nhiệm đối với ... | liability kind and legal system |
| consideration | đối giá (common-law context) | never equate automatically to the Vietnamese contract validity criteria |
| tort | chế định trách nhiệm ngoài hợp đồng (comparative explanation) | not a perfect one-to-one mapping |

All translations depend on the jurisdiction, context, defined terms and time period. Do not use this table to infer substantive law.

## Numerical and clause comparison

- Preserve 1,000 vs 1.000 conventions according to source and target jurisdiction; never change numerical magnitude.
- Preserve dates unambiguously; do not silently flip US MM/DD vs DD/MM without noting ambiguity.
- Clause 3.2(b), cross-references and amendment identifiers must remain stable.
- Preserve party role (claimant/respondent, lessor/lessee) and modality/negation.
- For long clauses, mark source segments [A], [B], [C] to track omissions.

## Student activity examples (fictional)

1. Translate: "The Tenant may assign the Lease only with the Landlord's prior written consent." Discuss permission vs mandatory consent.
2. Spot the error: "shall not disclose" was translated as "có thể tiết lộ". Explain violation severity.
3. Explain why `consideration` may mean contract-law doctrine in a U.S. textbook but a different concept in an ordinary memo.
4. Produce a 90-second moot-court argument **about a fictional dispute**, with an issue sentence and rule sentence.

## Copyright

User-supplied text may be translated or studied within the request; do not reproduce or expose full copyrighted textbooks beyond the user's request or available permissions. Avoid claiming to own or distribute translated legal textbooks. When asked to fetch a copyrighted book not supplied, provide a short summary, citation and legal access path rather than reproducing it.
