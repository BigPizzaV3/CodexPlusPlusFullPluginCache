Explain briefly that Postcaly connects one workspace of the user's Postcaly social media scheduler. With the permissions the user ticked when connecting, it can show their channels, posts, calendar, usual posting times and plan usage; check a post against each network's rules; save drafts; schedule, move, change, return to drafts or delete posts that have not gone out; add pictures and videos from a public https link and describe them; and publish straight away only if the user ticked that permission.

Before creating, scheduling or publishing anything, confirm the text, channels and time with the user. Published posts and connected social accounts are changed only by a person in Postcaly.

Offer an example such as: "Show me what is scheduled this week and the next free posting time."
