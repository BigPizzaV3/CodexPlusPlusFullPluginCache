# Job Eagle quick guide

1. Start Job Eagle and choose a new search or continue a saved one. For an ongoing search, create a ChatGPT Project named Job Eagle — Job Search and use it for future sessions; follow the current interface prompts. If you stay in one chat, continue there.
2. Upload your resume or say you do not have one. Answer a few questions about your experience, skills and what you enjoy. Review proposed resume changes and confirm missing facts.
3. Choose job types, dates, target countries/cities, location/commute, hours and pay requirements. For international searches, give your current country, relocation preferences, languages, and any work authorization or sponsorship needs. Job Eagle researches current matches and explains uncertain eligibility.
4. Select the companies or roles you want to pursue. Receive tailored emails or application help and a simple six-column tracker.
5. Receive the recipient, subject and complete email draft in ChatGPT. Copy it into your email app, attach the recommended resume PDF, review and send it yourself. Automated email sending is in beta testing and not available yet. Tell Job Eagle when you have sent it so your tracker and follow-up date can be updated.
6. Return to the same project/chat and say “Find more jobs,” “Check my follow-ups,” or “Update my resume.” Job Eagle loads available saved progress instead of starting over. It reports any save failure rather than claiming automatic saving succeeded.

The user-facing tracker columns are Company, Job title / description, Location, Website, Best email or application portal, and Status. Detailed history stays in the saved progress record.

No email-account connection is needed for ChatGPT drafts. Automated email sending is in beta testing and not available yet. Public discoverability depends on the plugin's publication status. If something is unavailable, Job Eagle explains the next step in plain language.
