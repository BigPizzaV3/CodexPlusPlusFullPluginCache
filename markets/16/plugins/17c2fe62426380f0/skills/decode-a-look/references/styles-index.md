# Style library

30 looks. Pick from these names; do not invent new ones.

- **Five Stars** — Five-star hotel finish — veined stone, walnut, brass, velvet and a statement chandelier. Mood: luxurious, serene, refined, elegant.
- **Mid-Century Modern** — Timeless 1950s-60s aesthetic with organic curves, tapered legs, and warm wood tones. Mood: warm, inviting, sophisticated, organic.
- **Scandinavian Modern** — Clean lines, natural materials, and cozy minimalism. Mood: serene, inviting, cozy, functional.
- **Hollywood Psycho** — Late-80s Manhattan power minimalism — gallery white, matte black, steel, glass and one big artwork. Mood: controlled, clinical, aspirational, unsettling.
- **LA Luxe Material Maximalism** — LA luxe — veined stone, warm brass, jewel velvet and oversized art. Mood: bold, luxurious, confident, dramatic.
- **Air** — California lightness — warm white, pale travertine, linen and one small sharp accent. Mood: airy, serene, sophisticated, bright.
- **The Queen of Color** — Candy-bright colour, curved velvet and playful Parisian chic. Mood: playful, sophisticated, joyful, luxurious.
- **Amalfi Coast** — Italian coastal luxury — crisp white walls, cobalt blue, lemon yellow, terracotta, majolica tile and linen. Mood: sun-drenched, sophisticated, elegant, relaxed.
- **Coastal Calm** — Relaxed beachy elegance with natural textures and ocean-inspired palette. Mood: relaxed, airy, fresh, natural.
- **Monastic Minimalism** — Architectural silence — pale plaster, honed stone, oak and almost nothing else. Mood: serene, austere, contemplative, precise.
- **Quiet Luxury** — Understated elegance with premium materials and refined simplicity. Mood: refined, understated, sophisticated, premium.
- **Desert Oasis** — Southwestern warmth with earthy tones, cacti, and artisanal crafts. Mood: warm, earthy, artisanal, serene.
- **Mediterranean Villa** — Sun-drenched warmth with terracotta, natural stone, and lush greenery. Mood: warm, tranquil, organic, inviting.
- **Joyful Collector Modernism** — Art, colour, pattern and mid-century collector finds. Mood: vibrant, confident, art-first, tactile.
- **70s glam penthouse** — Jewel velvet, shag, chrome and smoked glass — a 1970s penthouse at full glamour. Mood: saturated, sensual, sociable, glamorous.
- **Cabin In The Sky** — Warm redwood and stainless steel, with one glossy egg-yolk move. Mood: tailored, precise, ordered, playful.
- **Hill House II** — Pale limewash, tomato-red drapes, a chartreuse rug and a green-backed library. Mood: tailored, modern, ordered, serene.
- **Modern Baroque** — Checkerboard, cabbage roses, oversized scrolls and fearless colour. Mood: theatrical, cheerful, grand, exuberant.
- **Grand Eclectic Luxury** — Gilt, crimson velvet, damask and palace symmetry. Mood: majestic, sumptuous, formal, elegant.
- **Cinematic Deco Warmth** — Burl wood, onyx, brass arcs and amber light. Mood: opulent, nostalgic, warm, sensuous.
- **Sculptural California Glamour** — Carved relief, raw stone, boucle and brass. Mood: moody, glamorous, theatrical, luxurious.
- **Soft Scandinavian Minimalism** — Pale oak, lime plaster, linen and diffused light. Mood: serene, tactile, warm, quiet.
- **Haute Couture Modernism** — Plump boucle, blond oak, lime plaster and a witty colour pop. Mood: refined, warm, playful, serene.
- **Whimsical Storybook Eclecticism** — Peacock blue, butter yellow, scallops and wit. Mood: joyful, witty, charming, inviting.
- **Sculptural Primitive Calm** — Chalk plaster, cast concrete, rotund forms and antique Windsors. Mood: hushed, elemental, contemplative, sculptural.
- **Curated Warm Modern** — Ivory plaster, veined marble, oxblood accents and European antiques. Mood: serene, intimate, quietly luxurious, polished.
- **Florentine Mid-Century Glamour** — One deep wall colour, white upholstery, dark glossy wood and brass. Mood: polished, inviting, sophisticated, classic.
- **Soft Material Modernism** — Travertine, fluted oak, glass-globe lights and rounded upholstery. Mood: calm, tactile, unshowy, inviting.
- **Tailored Milanese Modernism** — Book-matched marble, bronze, tan leather and saffron. Mood: hushed, controlled, luxurious, precise.
- **Raw-Shell Hotel Noir** — Exposed brick, fluted brass, velvet in green, orange and mustard. Mood: dim, urban, sophisticated, intimate.
