# Staging tiers

For homes being sold, rented out, listed for guests or photographed. Staging is not about making a showroom; it is about letting a stranger imagine living there. Most of the high-impact moves cost little or nothing. Work down the tiers in order and stop where the time or money runs out.

## Tier 1 — 5 to 30 minutes, free

Highest return. Always do these first.

- Declutter hard: if in doubt, box it.
- Clear every countertop completely.
- Depersonalise: family photos away for now.
- Straighten everything: books, cushions, frames.
- Remove half the decor.
- Hide all visible cables.
- Take out surplus furniture so the room flows.
- Clear the walking paths.
- Give every room one obvious purpose.
- Open all blinds and curtains.
- Air the rooms 30 to 60 minutes before a viewing. No perfume, just fresh air.
- Hide the dish rack and sponges; clear the fridge door.
- One bin sweep before each showing.
- Noisy appliances off; temperature slightly cool.

## Tier 2 — 30 to 120 minutes, very low cost

Cleaning and light. These make a home feel cared for.

- Deep clean surfaces, sinks and toilets.
- Clean the windows inside.
- Remove wall scuffs; touch up chipped paint.
- Fresh sealant on visible gaps; clean the grout where it shows.
- Clean the blinds.
- Align cabinet doors with a quick hinge adjustment.
- Replace dead bulbs and match the bulb warmth everywhere (2700K).
- Brighter bulbs in the living room and kitchen.
- Fresh towels and a new bath mat.
- At most three toiletries on show.
- Make the bed tight, hotel style.
- Cushions and a throw from what is already in the house.
- Pet things out of sight.
- Wardrobes about 30 percent empty.

## Tier 3 — 2 to 6 hours, small spend

- Matching containers in the kitchen and bathroom.
- Matching hangers.
- A runner in a long hallway.
- A bigger rug, properly anchored under the furniture.
- A lamp on each side of the bed.
- Floor lamps in dark corners.
- LED strips under the kitchen cabinets.
- Kitchen: a bowl of citrus and one neutral tray.
- Bathroom: a tray with rolled towels.
- One or two good plants or fresh flowers.
- One large artwork at eye level.
- Scale check: swap many tiny things for a few large ones.

## Tier 4 — half a day to a weekend, moderate cost

- Curtains hung higher and wider, just touching the floor.
- One metal finish across the room.
- New cabinet hardware.
- New switch plates.
- A new toilet seat.
- A full-length mirror.
- A headboard.
- An entry console or bench with a mirror.
- A fresh doormat and a clean entrance.
- Trim the plants outside and clear visible clutter.

## Tier 5 — a weekend or more, higher cost

These go wrong when rushed. Only with time to do them properly.

- Mirrors placed opposite windows.
- Peel-and-stick splashback, only if it can be done cleanly.
- Pressure-wash the drive, patio or garage floor.
- New curtains throughout.
- Furniture swaps or rented staging furniture.
- Any repainting beyond touch-ups.

## Photographing the result

- Shoot in daylight with the lights on as well.
- Keep the verticals straight.
- Shoot from the corners to show the room's size.
- Style before shooting: a book, a plant, a throw.
- Edit every photo to the same warmth and brightness.
