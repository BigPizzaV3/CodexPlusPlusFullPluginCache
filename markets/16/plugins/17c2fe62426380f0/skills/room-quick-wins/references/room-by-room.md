# Room by room

Moves that reliably improve each kind of room. Pick the ones the room in front of you actually needs.

## Living room

- Pull the sofa off the wall if there is space.
- Angle the chairs inward so the seating forms a triangle for conversation.
- One hero piece: a large plant or a statement lamp.
- Three kinds of light: general, task and accent.
- Books: fewer, some stacked flat.
- One large artwork instead of several small ones.

## Bedroom

- White bedding gives a hotel feel at once.
- Two pillows, two shams, one lumbar cushion.
- Nightstands that roughly match; they do not need to be identical.
- A lamp on each side, warm bulbs (2700K).
- A full-length mirror makes the room feel larger.

## Kitchen

- Clear the counters completely, then put one or two things back.
- Decant into matching containers.
- A flat-weave rug that is easy to clean.
- Clean the extractor hood.
- Nothing on top of the fridge.

## Bathroom

- Neutral shower curtain, thick white towels.
- A tray with rolled towels and a candle.
- At most three toiletries on show.
- A large mirror in a small bathroom.
- Towel bars look cleaner than hooks.

## Entry and hallway

- A console or bench with a mirror above.
- A runner in a long hallway.
- One place for keys, one for shoes.

## Small rooms of any kind

- Walkways of at least 30 in (75 cm). Measure first.
- Furniture on visible legs.
- Pieces that do two jobs: storage ottoman, sleeper sofa.
- A light, limited palette.
- Curtains hung high and wide.
- A mirror opposite the window.
- One focal point only.

## Large rooms of any kind

- Fewer, longer pieces rather than more furniture.
- One rug that all the seating sits on.
- Furniture floated off the walls into zones.
- Bare wall is allowed: leave a third or more of it empty.
- One oversized artwork rather than a scatter of small frames.

## Lighting everywhere

- Match the bulb warmth across the room.
- Add a lamp before adding anything else to a dark corner.
- Overhead light alone flattens a room; put light at two or three heights.

## Curtains everywhere

- Rod high and wide: close to the ceiling, past the window frame on both sides.
- Long enough to touch the floor.
