# Room purpose

A style is a look: palette, materials, era, mood and styling. It is not a list of furniture to place. Most looks are described through a living room, with sofas and coffee tables and never a bed. Applied literally, a bedroom comes back as a lounge and a hallway as a waiting room.

So fix the purpose first, then apply the look to it. Where the style sheet names a piece that belongs in another kind of room, translate it into the nearest thing this room would have, in the same materials and spirit. The finished room must read unmistakably as what it is.

## The anchor of each room

- **Bedroom** — the bed is the anchor, with a nightstand beside it. A sofa or lounge chairs in the look become a bench at the foot of the bed or a single reading chair.
- **Living room** — a sofa or a seating group is the anchor.
- **Kitchen** — it stays a working kitchen. Counters, storage and appliances are the anchor; the look arrives through stools, lighting, textiles, objects and, where allowed, removable wraps on the fronts.
- **Dining room** — a table with chairs around it is the anchor.
- **Home office** — a desk and a proper task chair are the anchor.
- **Bathroom** — every fixture stays exactly as it is. Only textiles, accessories, mirrors and lighting change.
- **Hallway or entry** — it stays easy to walk through: a slim console or bench, a mirror, good lighting.
- **Balcony** — it stays open to the outside and takes outdoor furniture only.
- **Dressing room** — clothes storage and a mirror are the anchor.
- **Anything else** — the furniture the room is actually used for is the anchor.

## Translating a piece

| The look names | In a bedroom | In an office | In a hallway |
|---|---|---|---|
| A long low sofa | An upholstered headboard and a bench at the foot | A daybed or one lounge chair | A bench |
| A statement coffee table | A pair of nightstands in that material | The desk itself | A console |
| A bar cart or console vignette | A dresser top styled the same way | A credenza | The console |
| A seating group around a rug | The bed centred on the rug | Desk and chair on the rug | A runner |

If the room has two uses (a guest room that is also an office), ask which one leads, and let the other fold away.
