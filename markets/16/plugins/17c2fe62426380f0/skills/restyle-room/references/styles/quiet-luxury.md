# Quiet Luxury

Understated elegance with premium materials and refined simplicity

**Mood:** refined, understated, sophisticated, premium

## Essence
A serene and elevated atmosphere achieved through a minimalist approach, emphasizing the inherent quality and subtle textures of fine materials over overt ornamentation.

## Palette
- Base: ivory, greige, warm taupe, rich chocolate
- Accent: muted bronze, brushed nickel
- Balance: Predominantly warm, low-saturation neutrals across large surfaces, grounded by deeper tones. Metallic accents provide subtle points of reflection and luster.

## Materials
- matte solid wood
- polished or honed marble
- textured natural textiles (bouclé, wool)
- soft cashmere
- brushed metal (nickel, bronze)

## Shapes
Dominantly rectilinear forms with clean lines, softened by occasional organic or gently curved volumes. Vertical articulation or reeding provides subtle textural interest.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of varied natural textures—soft textiles, smooth stone, subtly reeded surfaces, and brushed metals—with a complete absence of busy or complex patterns.

## Light
Warm (around 3000K), ambient light that is often pooled and focused, creating a sophisticated glow rather than harsh, even illumination.

## Composition
Arrangements are balanced and uncluttered, with a focus on clear sightlines and the deliberate placement of a few significant forms that draw the eye.

## Contrast
Subtle contrast between varying warm neutral tones, light against deep, and matte finishes against polished or brushed sheens.

## Negative space
Ample negative space is paramount; surfaces are kept clear with minimal, deliberately chosen objects to allow forms and materials to be appreciated.

## Signature moves
- Vertically reeded or fluted surfaces on freestanding elements.
- Substantial, cool-toned stone objects with polished or honed finishes.

## Avoid
- Overt branding or logos.
- Ephemeral, mass-produced designs.
- Synthetic materials.
- Cluttered or busy displays of objects.
- Bright, high-saturation colors.
- Exaggerated or ostentatious forms.

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- cashmere throw
- marble accessories
- sculptural vase
- quality hardcover books
- **Sofa:** bouclé or high-quality wool in neutral tones
- **Table:** solid wood or marble with clean lines
- **Storage:** fluted wood cabinet, freestanding fluted sideboard

### Lighting pieces
- **Style:** sculptural pendants, linear fixtures, architectural lighting
- **Temperature:** warm neutral 3000K

### Never
- logos or branding
- trendy fast furniture
- synthetic materials
- cluttered displays
