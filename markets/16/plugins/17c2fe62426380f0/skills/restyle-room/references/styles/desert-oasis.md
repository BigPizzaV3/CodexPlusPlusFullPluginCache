# Desert Oasis

Southwestern warmth with earthy tones, cacti, and artisanal crafts

**Mood:** warm, earthy, artisanal, serene, grounded

## Essence
A feeling of grounded warmth and tranquility, achieved through a sun-baked, natural color scheme and handcrafted, textural elements.

## Palette
- Base: Cream (#F5F5DC), Sand (#C2B280), Warm Terracotta (#E2725B), Rust (#B7410E)
- Accent: Turquoise (#40E0D0), Sunset Orange (#FD5E53)
- Balance: A dominant warm, muted base of creams, sands, and terra cottas covers large surfaces and textiles, with rust offering a deeper tone. Small, vibrant pops of turquoise and sunset orange appear as specific focal points. Overall, the palette is warm and moderately saturated.

## Materials
- Woven natural fibers (matte, soft, nubby)
- Unglazed ceramic (matte, porous)
- Aged leather (matte, supple)
- Light-toned wood (matte, smooth grain)
- Smooth stone (matte, cool)

## Shapes
Predominantly organic and rounded forms, often with a hand-hewn appearance. Lower profiles are common for massing, balanced by occasional tall, slender vertical elements.

## Texture and pattern
Rich in varied textures, combining rough weaves, smooth ceramics, and soft leather. Patterns are minimal and abstract, often geometric or derived from natural forms, appearing as subtle repeats or textural variations rather than bold graphics.

## Light
Warm and diffused, often mimicking natural desert light at sunrise or sunset. It creates soft shadows and highlights the texture of materials, avoiding harshness.

## Composition
Asymmetrical arrangements that feel balanced and organic. Elements are layered by texture and height to create visual interest without overwhelming. Focal points are established through distinct shapes or accent colors.

## Contrast
Moderate contrast, primarily between soft, matte textures and smooth, sometimes slightly glossy surfaces. Subtle variations in hue and value within the earthy palette also provide gentle contrast.

## Negative space
Ample open space, with objects placed thoughtfully to avoid clutter, allowing individual pieces to be appreciated. Surfaces are generally uncluttered, promoting a sense of calm.

## Signature moves
- The juxtaposition of sun-baked, matte earthen tones with singular, vibrant accents of turquoise or orange.
- The presence of tall, sculptural plant forms in organic vessels.

## Avoid
- Cold metals
- Bright plastics
- Busy patterns
- Stark white
- Shiny chrome finishes
- Overly symmetrical arrangements
