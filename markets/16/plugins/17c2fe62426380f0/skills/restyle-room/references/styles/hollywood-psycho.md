# Hollywood Psycho

Late-80s Manhattan power minimalism — gallery white, matte black, steel, glass and one big artwork

**Mood:** controlled, clinical, aspirational, unsettling

## Essence
Cinematic minimalism that feels cool, hyper-clean, and deliberately unlived-in, defined by a gallery-white envelope, matte-black edges, and the gleam of steel and glass, often with one oversized contemporary art statement.

## Palette
- Base: Bright White, Matte Black, Cool Silver-Grey
- Accent: Saturated Primary Color
- Balance: The overall temperature is cool and desaturated. Bright white dominates large vertical and horizontal surfaces. Matte black defines edges and selected rectilinear forms. Cool silver-grey provides reflective accents and structural elements. If present, one highly saturated color is an isolated focal point.

## Materials
- Matte paint
- Smooth leather
- Polished stainless steel
- Clear glass
- Matte lacquer
- High-gloss laminate
- Tight-weave fabric

## Shapes
Predominantly low, rectilinear, and modular forms with strong right angles. Silhouettes are often thin-profiled or slab-like, creating crisp lines. Features large-scale geometric objects.

## Texture and pattern
A mix of smooth, matte, and reflective finishes. Textures are minimal, confined to tight-weave fabrics and low-pile rugs. Patterns are largely absent, limited to large-scale, abstract photographic or graphic art if present.

## Light
High-key, evenly diffused, neutral-to-cool white light. Directional light creates a museum-style accent on a single artwork and produces crisp specular highlights on reflective surfaces.

## Composition
Orthogonal, axial, and near-symmetrical arrangements with clear circulation paths and generous voids. Objects are layered minimally and distinctly. A single, oversized artwork or object serves as the primary focal point.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between bright white and matte black. Sharp distinction between opaque surfaces and clear or reflective elements. A mix of smooth matte finishes with crisp specular highlights from polished metals and glass.

## Negative space
Generous. Large vertical surfaces and floors are mostly empty. Horizontal surfaces are kept clear, holding very few, deliberately placed objects.

## Signature moves
- A single oversized, frameless monochrome artwork or mirror, leaned or removably mounted as a primary focal point.
- Prominent matte black linear elements, often in thin profiles, providing crisp definition against expansive bright white surfaces.

## Avoid
- Warm or cosy lighting
- Rustic or distressed materials
- Wicker, rattan, or boho textures
- Brass, bronze, or patinated metals
- Plush, tufted, or overstuffed forms
- Clutter, small personal objects, or gallery walls

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Low, rectilinear and modular: squared arms, tight upholstery, thin slabs, strong right angles.
- **Pieces:** a sleek white or black leather sofa with squared arms; chrome-framed leather lounge chairs; a thin glass-and-chrome coffee table; freestanding low matte-black credenzas and media units with concealed pulls and hidden cables; minimal linear shelving on removable brackets, or a freestanding shelving unit; a gallery plinth carrying one oversized white vessel or geometric sculpture
- **Rule:** Freestanding and reversible over wall-mounted; storage concealed; horizontal surfaces kept clear.

### Art, objects and plants
- one dominant oversized monochrome photograph, frameless mirror or backlit acrylic panel, leaned or removably mounted
- one scaled-up geometric object in white, chrome or matte black
- chrome or black trays and a restrained stack of art or architecture books
- no labels, cables or small knick-knacks

### Textiles and pattern
- smooth leather and tight-weave monochrome fabrics
- flat-weave or low-pile rugs in white, grey or black
- sheer white roller or panel shades
- few cushions, squared edges; no tufting, fringe or plush textures

### Lighting pieces
- **Quality:** High-key, evenly diffused, neutral-to-cool white; a museum-style accent on the one artwork; crisp specular highlights on steel and glass.
- **Fixtures:** plug-in track bars on adapter rails; clamp spots; slim plug-in panel or batten lights; adhesive linear LED backlight with cords hidden in surface raceways
- **Rule:** Plug-in only: no hardwiring, no ceiling cutting, no coves or built niches.

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- handleless gloss-white cabinetry -> high-gloss white wraps on the existing cabinet fronts, or freestanding gloss-white units
- concealed or cove lighting -> plug-in track bars, clamp spots and adhesive LED strips with cords in raceways
- concrete or stone surfacing -> a large grey flat-weave rug over the existing floor
- wall-mounted storage -> freestanding low credenzas
