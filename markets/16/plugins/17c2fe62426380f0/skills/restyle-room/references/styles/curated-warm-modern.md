# Curated Warm Modern

Ivory plaster, veined marble, oxblood accents and European antiques

**Mood:** serene, intimate, quietly luxurious, polished, personal

## Essence
A polished, emotionally resonant room that balances refined antique forms and sculptural, often vintage, elements with modern restraint. Every object feels deliberately chosen, storied, and precisely placed, creating a serene, gallery-like atmosphere.

## Palette
- Base: warm ivory (#F1EAE0), greige plaster (#CFC5B8), taupe brown (#8A7A6A), soft black (#26231F)
- Accent: oxblood burgundy (#5E2A28), antique brass (#A98C5B), muted rose (#D9A9A0)
- Balance: Predominantly warm neutrals (ivory, greige, taupe) on large surfaces like walls and major textiles. Soft black is used to ground compositions, often through larger items or frames. Accent colors (oxblood, brass, muted rose) are used sparingly as concentrated, jewel-like touches through small objects, a single prominent textile, or metallic finishes.

## Materials
- honed marble with dramatic veining
- aged European oak, matte
- unlacquered brass, brushed or slightly patinated
- vintage glass, often with organic or sculptural forms
- linen, natural and textured
- wool bouclé, nubby
- antiqued mirror

## Shapes
A deliberate mix of low, generous, and often rounded volumes, contrasted with fine, delicate linear elements. Silhouettes range from refined, rectilinear forms to sculptural, organic shapes. Individual pieces often have a strong, singular presence.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of textures: the smoothness of honed marble, the grain of aged oak, the nubby feel of wool bouclé, the soft sheen of unlacquered brass, and the subtle reflections of antiqued mirror. Patterns are used sparingly and tend to be tonal, like subtle stripes or small-scale checks, or large-scale and singular, such as a black-and-white checkerboard rug or a dramatic scenic wallpaper on one wall.

## Light
Soft, diffused, and candle-warm light, often pooled in specific areas and generally low, creating intimacy rather than even, bright illumination. Light sources are often sculptural or antique in appearance.

## Composition
Often symmetrical or balanced asymmetrical compositions with ample breathing room. Each primary object is given gallery-like prominence, with sightlines often directed to a single sculptural form or carefully placed antique piece. Elements are layered thoughtfully to create depth without busyness.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between light and dark, especially with soft black against warm ivory. A mix of matte surfaces (plaster-like paint, honed stone, bouclé) against polished elements (glass, antiqued mirror, unlacquered brass). Subtle textural variations add quiet depth, rather than bold pattern mixing.

## Negative space
Generous, breathing-room negative space around primary objects, giving them a gallery-like prominence. Surfaces hold a few chosen, storied items, avoiding either clutter or stark emptiness.

## Signature moves
- The deliberate pairing of low-slung, modern forms with fine-legged antique pieces.
- The grounding presence of a black-and-white checkerboard rug contrasted with warm, muted tones.

## Avoid
- Bright, saturated color
- Matching furniture sets
- Glossy contemporary finishes
- Busy pattern mixing
- Cluttered surfaces
- Cool gray minimalism

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Low, generous modern sofas mixed with fine-legged antique French and Italian case pieces, curved barrel chairs and sculptural one-off vintage seats.
- **Signature pieces:** antique painted armoire; vintage Italian glass pendant or sconce; curved bouclé chair; marble-topped console; French upholstered bed with corner screen
- **Arrangement:** Symmetrical, breathing-room layouts where each antique is given gallery-like space and sightlines end in a single sculptural object.

### Art, objects and plants
- stacked art and design books
- stone and ceramic vessels
- framed black-and-white photography
- antique busts and figures
- single-stem florals in aged pots
- woven baskets

### Textiles and pattern
- scenic chinoiserie wallpaper
- tonal stripes
- solid velvets
- hand-loomed Moroccan-style rugs

### Lighting pieces
- Antique lanterns, cloud-like bubble chandeliers and vintage Italian glass pendants deliver soft, candle-warm light; sconces in brass and plaster flank mirrors and beds.

### Staging
- checkerboard stone floor
- antique-meets-modern pairing
- dramatic marble slab
- storied objects on open shelves
- tonal room with one dark accent

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- black-and-white checkerboard stone floor -> a large black-and-white checkerboard rug over the existing floor
- limewashed plaster walls -> limewash paint in warm ivory or greige
- dramatic veined marble slab on walls or counters -> a marble-topped console or a freestanding marble table
- subtle beams and crown moulding -> keep the existing ceiling exactly as it is
- black-grounded doorways -> leave the doors; the soft black comes through furniture, frames and lamp bases
- storied objects on open shelves -> a freestanding etagere or open shelving unit, not fixed shelving
- scenic chinoiserie wallpaper -> removable scenic wallpaper on one wall
