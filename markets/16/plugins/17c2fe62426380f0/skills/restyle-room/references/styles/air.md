# Air

California lightness — warm white, pale travertine, linen and one small sharp accent

**Mood:** airy, serene, sophisticated, bright, calm

## Essence
A bright, serene, and sophisticated atmosphere created by a dominant palette of warm pale tones and natural textures, punctuated by a single, sharp color accent.

## Palette
- Base: Warm White, Chalk Off-white, Soft Sand, Pale Greige
- Accent: Tomato Red
- Balance: Predominantly warm and desaturated base colors cover large surfaces. A single, highly saturated accent is used in small, sharp doses.

## Materials
- Linen, light matte
- Boucle, nubby
- Leather, smooth matte or satin
- Travertine, pale matte porous
- Terrazzo, light polished smooth
- Chrome, soft reflective
- Nickel, brushed matte

## Shapes
Clean, understated lines dominate, with occasional soft curves. Forms are generally moderate in scale and proportion, avoiding heavy or overly delicate silhouettes.

## Texture and pattern
A mix of smooth and tactile textures, such as nubby boucle, porous stone, and soft metallic sheens. Patterns are minimal and subtle, if present at all, to maintain a clean aesthetic.

## Light
Bright, natural, and diffused, creating an even, warm glow that enhances the pale palette and airy feel.

## Composition
A foundation of tailored order and thoughtful arrangement, often balanced or subtly asymmetrical, with one element serving as a deliberate, unexpected focal point.

## Contrast
Low to medium contrast, primarily between subtle variations of pale tones and textures, with one sharp color contrast from the accent.

## Negative space
Ample open space, with objects arranged deliberately, allowing for visual breathing room and an uncluttered feel.

## Signature moves
- The extensive use of warm pale tones (whites, greys, sands) combined with natural, varied textures like linen and travertine.
- A single, small, highly saturated color accent placed precisely within the serene backdrop.

## Avoid
- Excessive opulence or high-shine finishes
- Dark, moody lighting or heavy shadows
- Large, ornate, centrally dominant lighting fixtures
- Oversized or generic wall art
- Voluminous, plush, overtly luxurious seating
- Heavy or elaborate window treatments

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Staging
- **Accent policy:** examples: chartreuse glass object; tomato red lacquer micro; ultramarine detail; max accents: 2; accent scale: small and sharp
- **Upholstery bias:** light linen; boucle light; tobacco leather small dose
- **Envelope palette:** warm white; chalk offwhite; soft sand; pale greige
- **Reflectance bias:** pale travertine; light terrazzo; soft chrome; brushed nickel
- **Window treatment:** linen sheers or light drapery no heavy swags

### Never
- hotel lobby glam
- night scene
- oversized brass chandelier as main event
- giant generic abstract mural
- curved velvet megasofa
- heavy drapery theater swags
- high contrast everywhere
