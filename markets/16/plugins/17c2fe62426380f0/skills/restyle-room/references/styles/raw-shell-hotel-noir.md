# Raw-Shell Hotel Noir

Exposed brick, fluted brass, velvet in green, orange and mustard

**Mood:** dim, urban, sophisticated, intimate, moody

## Essence
A dim, urban, and sophisticated atmosphere created by a deliberate contrast of raw, industrial-inspired textures with plush, deeply colored materials and low, intimate lighting.

## Palette
- Base: soot black (#1B1A19), bottle green (#1F4034), stained ash brown (#3B2A22), raw concrete grey (#8C8A84)
- Accent: burnt orange (#C8622A), mustard (#C98A1E), dusty rose (#8E5A56)
- Balance: Dominant dark and muted tones of black, dark green, and brown cover large surfaces, with lighter grey serving as a background. Sparingly applied, saturated accents of orange, mustard, and rose appear on textiles, alongside metallic glints. The overall palette is warm and desaturated with pops of jewel tones.

## Materials
- dark-stained ash with a smooth finish
- brushed brass with an aged patina
- green marble with subtle veining
- deeply buttoned leather
- plush cotton velvet
- antique mirror
- reeded glass

## Shapes
Predominantly low-slung, rounded, and plump volumes, often with fluted or channel-stitched details. Some pieces feature slim, turned supports or delicate three-legged structures.

## Texture and pattern
A rich mix of tactile textures, including plush velvet, deeply buttoned and woven leather, smooth polished marble, and aged brushed metal. Patterns are either large-scale and graphic (like on a rug or wall hanging) or subtle through textural detailing like fluting and channel stitching.

## Light
Dim and warm, primarily low-level and directional. Light is pooled in intimate zones, often originating from sources close to surfaces, creating strong shadows and a moody ambiance.

## Composition
Asymmetrical arrangements create intimate zones within a larger space. A prominent, low-slung piece often anchors each area, with visual interest drawn by the contrast of rich materials against a darker background and the layering of varied textures.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between dark, deep-toned, plush textiles and dark wood against lighter, raw-textured surfaces. Also, a contrast between matte textures and occasional metallic sheen.

## Negative space
Deliberately composed zones feature substantial, low-slung pieces. Surfaces are styled with a few carefully chosen objects, rather than being empty or cluttered.

## Signature moves
- The contrast of a dark, textured background (paint or wallcovering) with plush, deeply colored, low-slung, rounded forms.
- Low, directional lighting that creates dramatic pools of light and shadow.

## Avoid
- Bright uniform overhead lighting
- Pale all-neutral color schemes
- Overly symmetrical arrangements
- Polished chrome and sleek glass
- Minimalist or sterile aesthetics
- Floral or frilled textiles

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Low, rounded and channel-stitched: scalloped velvet bed frames, curved sofas with fringe, bolster-armed club chairs, fluted drum tables and slim three-legged turned-timber stools.
- **Signature pieces:** curved bar counter with fluted timber or brass front; long chesterfield banquette in green leather; scalloped channel-tufted velvet bed; fluted brass drum side table with marble top; round artist-designed rug; tile-topped dining tables in dark timber frames; metal-mesh bed base and steel pipe wardrobe rail
- **Arrangement:** Social spaces are zoned by archways, columns and level changes rather than walls, with banquettes along the perimeter and the bar as the fixed centre; guest rooms place a small sofa at the foot or side of the bed.

### Art, objects and plants
- commissioned murals and calligraphy by local artists
- shelves of old books, shells and found objects
- palms and trailing plants in glazed pots
- mushroom-dome brass table lamps
- framed drawings in deep timber frames
- dried grasses in ceramic vases

### Textiles and pattern
- plain cotton velvet upholstery
- channel-stitched and deep-buttoned backs
- bullion fringe on sofa bases
- washed linen bedcovers
- full-height dark wool curtains
- graphic hand-painted wall murals
- woven-leather headboards

### Lighting pieces
- Low-level and directional: articulated chrome and black task lamps mounted on walls and ceilings, ribbed-glass brass pendants over the bar, paired glass wall lights on brick piers, LED strips under stair treads and behind panelling.

### Staging
- black-lined archway cut through exposed brick
- fluted bar front with brass edge
- pink-painted pipework on a dark green ceiling
- articulated task lamps as wall and ceiling lights
- green chesterfield under a wall of bookcases
- round pictorial rug in a guest room
- marble shelf line running through slatted panelling

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- exposed brick walls and vaults, board-marked concrete beams and columns -> only if they already exist in the photo; never invent them. Otherwise dark textured paint in black or bottle green
- black-lined archways cut through brick -> a tall arched black-framed floor mirror; never add openings
- dark ceilings with visible ducts and pink-painted pipework -> keep the existing ceiling exactly as it is; never add ducts or pipes
- fluted joinery walls and ebonised slatted oak wall panelling with a marble shelf line -> a freestanding fluted dark-wood cabinet or headboard and a marble-topped console
- curved bar counter with a fluted brass front -> a fluted brass drum side table or a brass bar cart
- chesterfield banquette under a wall of bookcases, corner banquettes -> a freestanding deep-buttoned green leather or velvet sofa and a freestanding bookcase that stands clear of the ceiling
- herringbone timber or brick-paved floors -> a round pictorial or artist-designed rug over the existing floor
- commissioned wall mural -> one very large framed print or canvas leaning against the wall
- task lamps mounted on walls and ceilings, and LED strips under treads or behind panelling -> articulated black or chrome task lamps on stands or clipped to furniture, and a ribbed-glass brass table lamp; no light strips
