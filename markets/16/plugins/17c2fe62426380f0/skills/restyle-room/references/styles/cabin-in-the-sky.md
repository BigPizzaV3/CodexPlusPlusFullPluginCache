# Cabin In The Sky

Warm redwood and stainless steel, with one glossy egg-yolk move

**Mood:** tailored, precise, ordered, playful, refined

## Essence
A precisely ordered and tailored space, defined by a deliberate tension between warm woods and cool metals, punctuated by a singular, unexpected element of high-gloss color, all staged with cinematic precision.

## Palette
- Base: Chalk white, Warm red-browns and honey tones, Glossy white, Brushed silver
- Accent: Egg-yolk yellow, Dusty blue
- Balance: The majority of the space is calm, dominated by chalk white surfaces, warm wood forms, and glossy white elements. Brushed silver appears in one prominent, freestanding form. Egg-yolk yellow is reserved for one singular, high-gloss element, while dusty blue is used sparingly in one small textile or patterned zone. The overall palette is warm and quiet, with sharp, saturated, and cool interventions.

## Materials
- Wood (redwood, teak, beech) with a satin grain
- Stainless steel (brushed)
- Lacquered wood (high gloss)
- Wool (nubby texture)
- Mohair velvet (soft sheen)
- Linen (sheer, open weave)
- Chrome (mirror polished)
- Ceramics (matte or glazed)
- Perforated metal (matte white)

## Shapes
Dominantly clean, rectilinear forms create a crisp geometry. Silhouettes are tailored and precise, often featuring thin lines against solid volumes, exemplified by modular systems with clean uprights. Occasional, soft curves are introduced deliberately, but never as the primary design element.

## Texture and pattern
A considered mix of finishes, including matte paint, satin wood grain, high-gloss lacquer, brushed metal, soft mohair velvet, sheer linen, and nubby wool. Patterns are graphic but not busy, limited to one area rug that can introduce complementary colors, and one small 'tile-look' or textile zone.

## Light
Bright, high-key natural light is prioritized, creating an even wash during the day. Artificial lighting is warm (2700K-3000K) and typically pooled in specific areas by edited fixtures, rather than providing uniform ambient light.

## Composition
A clean, ordered envelope forms a quiet backdrop. Asymmetrical arrangements maintain balance through precise placement and proportion. A singular, high-gloss element acts as the primary focal point. Objects are layered with intentionality, often juxtaposing soft textiles with crisp forms, and are placed with 'library logic' rather than clutter.

## Contrast
Low to medium global contrast, with pronounced local highlights. Strong material contrast between warm, satin-finished woods and cool, brushed or polished metals. Visual tension between matte surfaces (like paint or wool) and highly reflective ones (like lacquer or polished chrome). Plain surfaces are balanced by specific, edited graphic patterns.

## Negative space
The room features significant empty floor and wall space, forming an 'ordered envelope'. Objects are placed with edited, medium-low density on surfaces. Any plants serve as sculptural volumes, adding softness without creating a dense, jungle-like feel.

## Signature moves
- A singular, high-gloss, egg-yolk yellow element, providing a deliberate and unexpected pop of saturated color within a generally subdued palette.
- A material tension created by juxtaposing the warmth and grain of satin-finished woods with the coolness and reflectivity of brushed or polished metal forms.

## Avoid
- Any building work such as panelling, mouldings, or built-ins
- Oversized, curved, or puffy upholstered forms
- Generic abstract murals or art
- Hotel-lobby glamour or excessive opulence
- More than one hero or statement piece
- Over-smoothed or artificial textures

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Vibe:** tailored silhouettes + iconic modernist references + one surprising object
- **Avoid shapes:** oversized curved megasofa; puffy hotel sectional; blob everywhere
- **Preferred shapes:** clean rectilinear; occasional soft curve if not the hero
- **Iconic cues allowed:** paper lantern over table; italian postwar forms; precise side tables in stone or glass; occasional_statement_sofa_or_chrome_piece (only if it’s the ONE hero)

### Art, objects and plants
- **Rule:** Specificity over scale. Avoid giant generic abstract murals.
- **Objects:** ceramics (edited); books (library logic, not clutter); one oddball found object (intentional placement)
- **Surface density:** edited medium low
- **Preferred art types:** one_pedigree_feeling_piece (photography, graphic print, or sculptural wall work); ceramic relief / textured piece (if it reads crafted and specific)
- **Bias:** monstera; split leaf philodendron; sculptural potted tree
- **Rule:** Plants as volume + softness, not jungle.
- **Allowed:** architectural greenery; one or two large plants

### Textiles and pattern
- **Rugs:** wool_rug_with_deliberate_complementary_colors (e.g., chartreuse + aubergine); graphic_but_not_busy (one rug drives palette; no multiple competing patterns)
- **Upholstery:** tobacco_or_cognac_mohair_velvet (controlled); one_goldenrod_or_egg_yolk_mohair_accent (small)
- **Window treatments:** linen sheers; open weave drapery in a warm natural tone

### Lighting pieces
- **Avoid:** oversized scalloped brass chandelier as the main event; nightclub spotlighting; too many statement fixtures
- **Temperature:** 2700K to 3000K warm
- **Daylight first:** yes
- **Fixture archetypes:** paper_lantern; italian modernist task or floor lamp; edited_pendant_cluster (only if not turning into hotel glam)

### Staging
- **Priority order:** snap the walls into focus with soft white limewash and crisp continuity; add one freestanding ordering system (modular shelving with clean uprights and library order); add one softening layer (sheers, open-weave drapery, mohair, wool rug)
- **Shelving signatures:** freestanding modular shelving (clean uprights, adjustable bays); one monolithic freestanding cabinet with crisp edges that hides a bar or media

### Rules of the look
- **One sentence:** An ordered, historically aware envelope snapped into focus, then punctured by 1–2 discordant color notes and 1 industrial-vs-handmade material tension—staged like a film still.
- **Nonnegotiables:** Order before ornament; One weird move beats three loud moves; Specific materials over generic ‘luxury’
- **Rule:** Only one hero gesture: the egg-yolk lacquered piece. No giant mural, mega-sofa or statement chandelier on top of it.
- **Max accent colors:** 2
- **Max pattern zones:** 1
- **Max statement pieces:** 1
- **Max high gloss surfaces:** 2

### Never
- **Hard avoid:** hotel lobby luxury; moody night render by default; generic giant abstract mural; too many curves; velvet everywhere; over-smoothed textures; random art deco glam; color everywhere instead of one or two notes

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- a high-gloss single-colour painted floor -> one glossy egg-yolk-yellow lacquered furniture piece; the floor itself stays as it is
- a stainless platform or counter wrap -> one freestanding stainless or perforated-metal cabinet, bench or table
- built-in shelving and a millwork volume -> freestanding modular shelving and one freestanding cabinet
- a dusty blue tile zone -> tile-look objects, a tiled tray or table top, or a dusty blue textile
