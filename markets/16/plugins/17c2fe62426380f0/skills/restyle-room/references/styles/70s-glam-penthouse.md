# 70s glam penthouse

Jewel velvet, shag, chrome and smoked glass — a 1970s penthouse at full glamour

**Mood:** saturated, sensual, sociable, glamorous, inviting

## Essence
Saturated, sensual, and sociable, this style evokes a glamorous 1970s high-rise through jewel-toned, plush textures, reflective surfaces catching warm light, and low, deep, modular seating.

## Palette
- Base: Burnt Orange, Harvest Gold, Chocolate Brown, Avocado Green
- Accent: Emerald, Deep Teal, Magenta
- Balance: The palette is warm and highly saturated. Base colors cover expansive rugs and walls, while vivid jewel-toned accents are reserved for plush textiles. Lighter neutrals are used sparingly.

## Materials
- Crushed velvet, plush
- Shag textile, deep pile
- Chrome, polished
- Glass, smoked and tinted
- Lacquer, high-gloss
- Travertine, matte
- Acrylic, polished

## Shapes
Dominant shapes are low, deep, and modular, featuring rounded volumes and plump forms. These are often contrasted with slender, rectilinear elements or crisp, geometric profiles in reflective materials.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of textures: deep, shaggy piles meet smooth, crushed velvets and sheer, flowing textiles. Reflective polished metals and glossy lacquered surfaces provide a hard counterpoint. Patterns are bold and graphic, often large-scale op-art or supergraphic motifs on a single surface.

## Light
Warm and inviting, emanating an amber evening glow. Light is pooled and directed, often low-level, creating dramatic pockets and bouncing off highly reflective surfaces, distributing a soft shimmer throughout the room.

## Composition
Asymmetrical yet balanced, with a low-slung, inward-facing seating group serving as the central anchor. A single surface with a bold graphic pattern creates a focal point, complemented by strategically placed reflective elements that capture and distribute light, fostering a cinematic and inviting ambiance.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between highly reflective, polished surfaces and deep, plush matte textures. Graphic patterns starkly juxtapose solid, saturated color fields.

## Negative space
The room feels generous and layered, with a central, low-slung seating arrangement forming a dense core. Surfaces hold a moderate number of objects, allowing visual breathing room without feeling sparse.

## Signature moves
- A low, deep, modular seating arrangement in varied jewel tones
- A single wall featuring a bold, large-scale graphic or op-art pattern

## Avoid
- Pale, minimal color palettes
- Sparse arrangements or empty surfaces
- Monochromatic beige or muted tones
- Natural wood finishes dominating
- Small-scale or fussy patterns
- Anything overtly rustic or farmhouse-inspired

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Seating:** a freestanding modular velvet sofa system in mixed jewel tones, arranged as a low sunken-lounge conversation group; a chrome-framed lounge chair or a chunky sculptural armchair; floor cushions or poufs
- **Tables:** a lucite or smoked-glass-and-chrome coffee table; a travertine or chrome side table
- **Storage:** a freestanding mirrored or smoked-glass cabinet; a lacquered sideboard
- **Hero statement:** one mirrored disco ball or a cluster of globe pendants catching the light; a large leaning smoked mirror
- **Object styling:** macrame plant hanger with a trailing plant; chrome and glass decanters; a stack of oversized art books; a vintage record player or globe bar cart

### Lighting pieces
- **Fixtures:** colourful glass globe pendants or a swag pendant; a chrome arc floor lamp; a mushroom table lamp
- **Mood:** warm amber evening glow with reflections bouncing off chrome, glass and mirror
- **Color temperature:** 2400K-2700K warm
