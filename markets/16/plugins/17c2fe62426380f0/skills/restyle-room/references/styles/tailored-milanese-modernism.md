# Tailored Milanese Modernism

Book-matched marble, bronze, tan leather and saffron

**Mood:** hushed, controlled, luxurious, precise, austere

## Essence
A sense of hushed, controlled luxury created through strict, rectilinear geometry, high-quality natural materials, and deliberately sparse arrangements.

## Palette
- Base: black stone (#1A1A1A), ivory (#EFE6D6), bleached oak (#D8C4A6), dark hardwood (#8A4B2A)
- Accent: saffron (#E0A320), Bulgari orange (#C8561E), emerald (#1E8A4C), tan leather (#C27B3A)
- Balance: Predominantly dark base colors with warm timber tones for public-style areas, shifting to ivory and pale oak for private spaces. Accent colors are introduced sparingly as singular, strong statements, often through a single material, lending an overall warm and moderately saturated feel.

## Materials
- black stone (polished, honed)
- burnished bronze (subtle sheen)
- saddle leather (smooth, matte)
- highly veined coloured marble (polished)
- bleached oak (smooth, matte)
- dark hardwood (smooth, matte)
- gold woven metal mesh (lustrous)

## Shapes
Dominantly low, square, and rectilinear forms with precise proportions. Silhouettes are often boxy, extended linearly, or feature thin metal or dark wood bases. Oval forms appear sparingly for specific elements.

## Texture and pattern
A mix of smooth, refined surfaces with occasional subtle textures like woven wool, chenille, or velvet. Patterns are geometric (circles, squares, diamonds, triangles), small-scale or sparse, and never layered.

## Light
Dim, warm, and often low-level, creating distinct pools of illumination to highlight specific areas and objects rather than providing overall even light.

## Composition
Objects are aligned on a strict grid, arranged axially, and widely spaced, creating large empty areas. Layering is minimal, focusing on the strong presence of individual volumes and surfaces.

## Contrast
High contrast between dark and light masses, between smooth matte surfaces and polished or burnished metals, and between plain expanses and subtle geometric patterns.

## Negative space
Ample negative space with widely spaced, deliberately arranged objects, leaving significant floor and surface areas empty to emphasize proportion and material quality.

## Signature moves
- A single vibrant color (like saffron or emerald) used as a strong, solid block of material or textile.
- Thin dark lines, often metallic or dark wood, outlining or supporting pale, substantial volumes.

## Avoid
- Ornate or period decoration
- Layering multiple patterns
- Curvy or whimsical shapes
- Shiny chrome finishes
- Cool pastel color schemes
- Cluttered surfaces

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Low, square and precisely proportioned: cube armchairs, long linear sofas, timber-framed chairs with leather slings, box-shade lamps and oval bar counters, all on thin metal or dark wood bases.
- **Signature pieces:** black leather cube armchairs; black-framed four-poster bed; oval bar counter with glossy black top; timber-framed armchairs with leather seats; Atollo brass dome table lamp; long low sofa in pale velvet; tan leather tub chair and pouf
- **Arrangement:** Furniture is aligned on a strict grid parallel to the walls, in widely spaced groups along a long axis, leaving large areas of floor empty.

### Art, objects and plants
- tight bunches of green anthurium or hydrangea
- framed vintage jewellery drawings
- art and fashion books on open shelves
- large branch arrangements in dark vases
- pierced stone and bronze sculpture
- backgammon board on an ottoman

### Textiles and pattern
- full-height dark wool curtains
- plain ivory fabric wall panels
- saffron jacquard with circle and square motifs
- pale chenille and velvet upholstery
- rust-orange woven banquette fabric
- diamond-grid carpets
- small triangle-pattern cushions

### Lighting pieces
- Recessed linear slots and lit ceiling coves, box-shaped fabric lamps on stands, brass dome lamps on bars and tables and backlit stone or glass bar fronts; public rooms are kept dim with warm pools of light.

### Staging
- bathroom lined in one coloured marble
- black stone floor with black fireplace wall
- bronze-clad columns
- backlit glass or onyx bar
- gold mesh folding screens
- pivoting mesh screens between bed and bath
- mosaic-lined pool with stone columns
- circular lit ceiling recess over the bed

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- rooms and bathrooms lined on every face in one book-matched marble -> a marble-topped table or console, marble accessories and one large stone-toned artwork; never re-clad walls or bathrooms
- black stone, teak plank or inlaid terrazzo floors -> a large flat-woven rug in black, sand or saffron over the existing floor
- bronze-clad columns and gold mesh pivoting screens -> a freestanding bronze- or brass-mesh folding screen; never add columns
- mosaic-lined pool with stone columns -> not a room feature to build; a small mosaic tray, green glass and gold-toned objects carry the colour
- backlit onyx or glass bar front -> a freestanding bar cabinet or cart with a glass front and a brass dome table lamp on top
- circular lit ceiling recess and recessed linear light slots -> keep the existing ceiling; plug-in disc-shaped wall lights, box-shaped fabric floor lamps and a brass dome lamp
- bleached oak panelling, fabric-panelled walls and a black fireplace wall -> a tall upholstered headboard or fabric folding screen behind the bed; a fireplace only if it already exists in the photo
