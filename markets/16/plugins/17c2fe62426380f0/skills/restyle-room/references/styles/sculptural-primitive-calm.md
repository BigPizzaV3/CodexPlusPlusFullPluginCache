# Sculptural Primitive Calm

Chalk plaster, cast concrete, rotund forms and antique Windsors

**Mood:** hushed, elemental, contemplative, sculptural, serene

## Essence
A serene, gallery-like atmosphere created by raw, tactile surfaces and primitive, sculptural forms. It blends substantial, rounded volumes with lighter, more linear elements, evoking a quiet contemplation.

## Palette
- Base: bone white (#EDEAE3), chalk grey (#B8B6B2), putty pink (#D9BBAC)
- Accent: clay terracotta (#A9705A), slate blue-green (#5D7268), burnt sienna (#C05A28)
- Balance: A mostly monochromatic scheme of cool greys and warm putty tones covers large surfaces and textiles. Muted mineral accents appear sparingly as single blocks of color in smaller objects. The overall palette is desaturated and warm-leaning.

## Materials
- cast concrete (matte, raw)
- translucent ribbed resin (smooth, subtly glossy)
- dark stained wood (matte)
- hand-dyed wool (nubby, soft)
- unlacquered metal (matte, raw)
- ceramic (smooth, matte)
- linen (textured, matte)

## Shapes
Dominantly rounded, monolithic, and blocky elemental forms, often low-slung and substantial. These are contrasted with thin, linear, skeletal frames. Overall, shapes are sculptural and large-scale.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of tactile textures: rough and chalky, smooth and translucent, nubby and soft, with occasional deep pile. Patterns are minimal and subtle, featuring organic mottling, subtle woven checks, or solid blocks of color.

## Light
Soft, diffuse, and atmospheric. It provides an even, misty glow rather than sparkle, often emanating from low sources or through translucent elements, creating a hushed quality.

## Composition
Highly asymmetric and sparse, with single, impactful objects often positioned centrally or in deliberate isolation, creating visual pauses. Elements are layered simply, with a strong emphasis on individual forms.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between weighty, sculptural forms and delicate, linear silhouettes. Matte, raw surfaces juxtaposed with subtle translucence and soft sheens. Muted, desaturated color fields against occasional, rich blocks of color.

## Negative space
Significant negative space, with a generous amount of empty floor and wall. Objects are placed sparingly, often centrally, allowing each piece to stand out. Surfaces hold minimal, intentional items.

## Signature moves
- A prominent, rounded, sculptural volume placed as a central focal point.
- A freestanding translucent ribbed screen or panel, often subtly lit.

## Avoid
- glossy polished luxury surfaces
- bright saturated colour schemes
- ornate pattern and florals
- matching showroom furniture
- chrome-and-glass modern glamour
- dense decorative clutter

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Rounded primitive monoliths and blocky elemental forms — furniture that reads as abstract sculpture — mixed with skeletal antique wood chairs.
- **Signature pieces:** Roly-Poly style rotund chair; antique spindle-back Windsor chair; monolithic concrete or plaster display drum; chunky slab bench on pedestal feet; low tufted daybed or bateau sofa; pebble-form side tables in resin or clay
- **Arrangement:** Sparse museum-like placement: single objects centred in space or held in niches, with wide empty floor between them.

### Art, objects and plants
- primitive earthenware jugs and vessels
- hand-carved wooden bowls
- bronze and pewter objects on staggered shelves
- abstract seascape or tonal paintings
- plaster maquettes and sculptural fragments
- single dried branch in a stone vase

### Textiles and pattern
- woven checked headboard canvas
- mottled hand-dyed carpet
- raw fringed linen
- sheepskin throws
- solid velvet in earth tones
- undyed calico

### Lighting pieces
- Diffuse and atmospheric: backlit translucent panels, milky glass globes and skylight-like ceiling coffers give an even, misty glow rather than sparkle.

### Staging
- rotund sculptural chair as room centrepiece
- antique Windsor chair against raw concrete
- staggered display shelves cut into a curved monolith
- grey-on-grey chalky monochrome rooms
- translucent ribbed screen walls
- objects displayed like archaeological finds

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- raw chalky plaster and clay-pigmented render walls -> chalky limewash or clay-toned matte paint
- translucent ribbed resin screen walls and backlit panels -> a freestanding translucent ribbed folding screen, softly lit from behind by a lamp
- staggered display shelves cut into a curved monolith, and niches -> a freestanding curved plaster or concrete display drum with open shelves
- skylight-like ceiling coffers -> keep the existing ceiling; the misty glow comes from milky glass globe lamps
- painted white floorboards, sealed concrete and green tile borders on floors -> a large cloudy hand-dyed wool rug over the existing floor, and a green-tiled freestanding plinth
- cast concrete and limestone -> freestanding cast concrete and limestone pieces such as a bench, drum or side table
