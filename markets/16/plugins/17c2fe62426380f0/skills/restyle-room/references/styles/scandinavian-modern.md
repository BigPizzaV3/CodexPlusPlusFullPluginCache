# Scandinavian Modern

Clean lines, natural materials, and cozy minimalism

**Mood:** serene, inviting, cozy, functional, calm

## Essence
A serene and inviting atmosphere achieved through unadorned forms, soft textures, and abundant, gentle light.

## Palette
- Base: Warm White, Pale Grey, Light Sand
- Accent: Muted Sage Green, Dusty Blue
- Balance: Large surfaces are warm white, pale grey, or light sand, complemented by light natural wood tones. One desaturated accent color is used sparingly in textiles or small decorative items. The overall impression is warm and muted.

## Materials
- Light-toned wood (pale oak, birch) with a matte or natural finish
- Soft natural textiles (linen, wool, cotton, sheepskin) with a matte, nubby, or plush texture
- Unglazed ceramic with a matte finish
- Frosted glass
- Brushed or matte metals

## Shapes
Predominantly low and rectilinear, with occasional soft, rounded or oval forms. Silhouettes are simple and unadorned, often with thin supports.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of natural textures provides depth: smooth wood, soft plush sheepskin, coarse woven textiles, and matte ceramics. Patterns are absent or extremely subtle, relying on textural variations for visual interest.

## Light
Abundant, soft, and warm light (2700K-3000K). It is often diffused through linen shades, creating gentle pools rather than harsh, even illumination. Natural light is prioritized.

## Composition
An uncluttered, balanced arrangement with ample negative space around each element. Objects are typically arranged with a sense of quietude, often asymmetrically, to create visual interest without busyness.

## Contrast
Subtle contrast is created between smooth, light-toned surfaces and soft, textured fabrics or natural wood grain. Forms often contrast thin lines with solid masses.

## Negative space
Ample empty space around objects and on surfaces is crucial, allowing each element to stand out. Surfaces are mostly clear.

## Signature moves
- The combination of low-profile, clean-lined forms in pale wood or light fabric with soft, natural textural accents like a plush sheepskin
- A single, organically shaped light fixture as a focal point

## Avoid
- Ornate details
- Heavy or dark-toned forms
- Cluttered surfaces
- Bold or high-contrast patterns
- Shiny metallics
- Highly saturated colors

- **Density:** minimal, breathing room between pieces

- dark heavy furniture
- cluttered surfaces
- bold patterns
- gilded or shiny metallics
