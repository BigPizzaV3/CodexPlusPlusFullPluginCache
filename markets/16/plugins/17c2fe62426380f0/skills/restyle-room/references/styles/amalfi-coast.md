# Amalfi Coast

Italian coastal luxury — crisp white walls, cobalt blue, lemon yellow, terracotta, majolica tile and linen

**Mood:** sun-drenched, sophisticated, elegant, relaxed, uplifting

## Essence
This style evokes the feeling of a sun-drenched Italian retreat, balancing refined simplicity with vibrant Mediterranean warmth. It achieves an effortless elegance through crisp, bright foundations punctuated by confident bursts of color and natural textures.

## Palette
- Base: Pure White, Pale Linen, Bleached Oak, Terracotta
- Accent: Cobalt Blue, Lemon Yellow
- Balance: Pure white covers approximately 90% of large surfaces, providing a bright and airy backdrop. Pale linen and bleached oak tones introduce subtle warmth through textiles and medium-scale objects. Terracotta adds earthy depth via rugs and larger decorative pieces. Cobalt blue and lemon yellow are used sparingly as vibrant, intentional focal points in small objects and patterned details. The overall impression is warm, bright, and highly saturated in its accent points.

## Materials
- Natural linen, crisp
- Bleached oak, matte
- Olive wood, smooth
- White stone, honed
- Wrought iron, matte dark
- Ceramic, hand-painted glossy
- Jute/Sisal, nubby

## Shapes
Predominantly clean-lined and low-profile. Rectilinear forms are softened by the occasional organic shape, particularly in horizontal planes and decorative objects. Silhouettes are generally grounded, with a balance of substantial forms and lighter, delicate elements.

## Texture and pattern
A blend of smooth, crisp, and nubby textures. Large surfaces are smooth and often reflective. Textiles are crisp and clean. Rugs introduce coarse, natural fibers. Patterns are used sparingly and are primarily traditional tile motifs, often in blue and white, with a hand-painted quality. No busy or overwhelming patterns.

## Light
Bright, maximized natural light fills the space, often diffused by sheer textiles and amplified by reflective white surfaces. Artificial light is warm (2700-3000K), creating a romantic glow in the evenings, typically pooled from freestanding sources at varying heights.

## Composition
Open, airy, and inviting, with clear sightlines. Objects are arranged to encourage flow and often to frame views. The composition features a relaxed symmetry, creating a balanced but not rigid feel. Items are layered with an emphasis on clarity and purpose rather than density.

## Contrast
Strong contrast exists between the dominant pure white surfaces and the vibrant, saturated cobalt blue and lemon yellow accents. A subtle textural contrast is achieved between smooth, reflective white and the crispness of linen, the grain of bleached wood, and the nubby quality of natural fiber rugs. Clean rectilinear forms contrast with organic shapes.

## Negative space
Generous and open. Large expanses of walls and floor remain empty, allowing the space to breathe. Surfaces are minimally appointed with curated, purposeful objects, avoiding clutter.

## Signature moves
- Expansive pure white surfaces punctuated by bold, clear cobalt blue accents.
- Low, clean-lined forms juxtaposed with the artisanal texture and patterns of hand-painted ceramics.

## Avoid
- Dark or moody color schemes
- Heavy ornate forms
- Synthetic materials
- Cluttered surfaces or excessive accessories
- Cool grey tones
- Busy floral patterns
- Black accents, except for very sparingly used matte wrought iron

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Density:** minimal but curated—each piece should feel collected and purposeful, never cluttered
- **Ceramics:** hand-painted Italian majolica vases, decorative plates in blue and yellow patterns, ceramic lemon arrangements
- **Lighting:** wrought iron pendant lights or chandeliers, white ceramic table lamps, brass wall sconces
- **Textiles:** crisp white linen everywhere, blue and white striped cushions, natural jute or sisal rugs
- **Furniture:** clean-lined sofas in natural white linen, statement cobalt blue accent chair, bleached oak or olive wood coffee tables
- **Accessories:** fresh lemons in ceramic bowls, olive branches in terracotta pots, vintage Italian tiles as art
- **Sofa:** low-profile, clean lines in natural white or cream linen; no busy patterns
- **Tables:** olive wood, bleached oak, or white stone; organic shapes preferred
- **Seating:** one statement accent chair in cobalt blue velvet or natural rattan with blue cushion
- **Storage:** white-washed wood cabinets, open shelving displaying ceramics, minimal and functional
- **Outdoor blur:** seamless indoor-outdoor flow with terrace furniture visible or integrated

### Lighting pieces
- **Mood:** bright and uplifting by day, warm and romantic by night
- **Layers:** statement wrought iron or ceramic pendants, brass or iron wall sconces, candles in the evening
- **Natural:** maximize—sheer curtains, reflective white surfaces, mirrors to bounce light
- **Color temperature:** warm white 2700-3000K for evening glow

### Staging
- **Flow:** open and airy with clear sightlines; furniture arranged to frame views
- **Symmetry:** relaxed symmetry; balanced but not rigid
- **Negative space:** generous—let the architecture breathe

### Never
- NO dark or moody color schemes
- NO heavy ornate furniture or baroque details
- NO synthetic materials or plastic
- NO cluttered surfaces or excessive accessories
- NO cool grey tones—keep warm and sunny
- NO industrial or modern minimalist aesthetics—this is Mediterranean warmth, not Scandinavian restraint
- NO wall-to-wall carpet
- NO busy floral patterns—keep patterns to traditional tile motifs only
- NO black accents—use wrought iron sparingly and in traditional forms

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- terracotta or tiled floors -> a terracotta-toned rug and ceramics over the existing floor
- arches, columns and vaulted ceilings -> only where they already exist; never invented
