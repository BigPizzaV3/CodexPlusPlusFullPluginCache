# Cinematic Deco Warmth

Burl wood, onyx, brass arcs and amber light

**Mood:** opulent, nostalgic, warm, sensuous, cinematic

## Essence
An opulent, nostalgic, and cinematic warmth is created by a dominant palette of ambered woods and stones, layered lighting, and a blend of curvilinear forms with distinct Art Deco geometry.

## Palette
- Base: amber cognac (#A86B3E), burl chestnut (#6E4A2E), travertine cream (#E9DFC9), oxblood red (#7E3B34), smoked bronze (#5A4A3A)
- Accent: teal petrol (#31626B), polished brass (#C9A45C), rose terracotta (#C57F5F)
- Balance: Warm woods and stone hues cover large surfaces (paint, rugs, large pieces), while polished brass provides consistent glinting details. Teal, oxblood red, and rose terracotta are sparingly used for upholstery and small objects. The overall palette is warm and moderately saturated.

## Materials
- Burl wood (rich, polished, veined)
- Walnut wood (deep, polished)
- Onyx (translucent, veined, polished)
- Marble (veined, polished)
- Travertine (honed, matte)
- Brushed brass (subtle sheen)
- Polished brass (high sheen)
- Cane (woven, natural texture)
- Mohair velvet (plush, soft sheen)
- Jacquard (woven, patterned texture)
- Smoked glass (translucent, tinted)
- Wool (plush, for rugs)

## Shapes
A dominant interplay of plump, rounded volumes, monolithic and weighty forms, and long, low rectilinear shapes. These are complemented by sinuous curves and strong Art Deco elements like arches and stepped profiles in objects and details.

## Texture and pattern
A rich mix of plush (velvet), smooth (polished woods and stones), and varied woven textures (cane, jacquard). Patterns include geometric motifs, wavy designs, flame-stitch, subtle animalier, and botanical weaves, often with channel tufting adding linear texture.

## Light
Golden, dim, and warm light, creating a flattering and atmospheric glow. It is layered and pooled, creating intimate zones and cinematic 'scenes'. Light sources are often diffused by fabric or parchment domes, and positioned at low to mid-levels.

## Composition
Overall symmetry guides the layout, but within this, distinct, often asymmetrical groupings create intimate zones. Objects and lighting are layered to create depth and visual interest, arranging the space to guide the eye and movement like a choreographed film scene.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between polished and matte surfaces (e.g., brass against honed travertine). Moderate contrast between darker woods and lighter creams. Patterned textiles provide a counterpoint to plainer surfaces, adding visual depth and interest.

## Negative space
Significant empty floor and wall space is balanced by distinct, rich clusters of objects and seating, creating intimate zones within a grander spatial feeling. Surfaces are well-appointed but not cluttered.

## Signature moves
- The prominent use of arched and stepped forms in objects, mirrors, and lighting elements.
- The distinctive, rich grain of burl wood featured as a primary decorative surface, often with a polished finish.

## Avoid
- Cool white light
- Flat, unadorned minimalist planes
- Matte grey palettes
- Spindly, lightweight forms or pale, unstained woods
- Raw, unfinished surfaces lacking warmth
- Random, unstructured arrangements that lack a clear visual narrative

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Rounded club chairs and swivel tubs, monolithic stone-topped tables, long channel-tufted banquettes and sinuous bars with sculpted fronts.
- **Signature pieces:** arched brass floor lamps with fabric domes; cane-backed dining armchairs; onyx or marble bistro tables; curved velvet swivel chairs; monumental carved bar counter; tiered brass chandeliers
- **Arrangement:** Grand symmetrical rooms broken into intimate lamp-lit islands of seating, choreographed so guests move through the space like a camera through a film scene.

### Art, objects and plants
- potted palms and large ficus trees
- sculptural ceramics
- vintage barware and silver trays
- stacked art and travel books
- bronze objets and small sculptures
- oversized vases with branches

### Textiles and pattern
- wavy and flame-stitch jacquards
- channel tufting
- geometric carpet motifs
- silk-look drapery
- leather with contrast piping
- subtle animalier and botanical weaves

### Lighting pieces
- Layered and theatrical: parchment domes on brass arcs, sconces washing brick and stone, candle-like table lamps — always golden, dim and flattering.

### Staging
- Art Deco arches and stepped profiles
- burl-wood grain as ornament
- palm-court greenery under high ceilings
- amber cinematic light
- Riviera nostalgia in grand shells

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- burl and walnut wall panelling -> a freestanding burl cabinet, a burl folding screen and burl-veneer furniture
- stone mosaic and terrazzo floors -> a graphic wool rug with Deco motifs over the existing floor
- exposed brick, gothic arches and coffered ceilings -> only if they already exist in the photo; never invent them
- monumental carved bar counter -> a freestanding bar cabinet or brass bar cart
- Art Deco arches and stepped profiles -> arched mirrors, arched-back chairs and stepped lamp bases
