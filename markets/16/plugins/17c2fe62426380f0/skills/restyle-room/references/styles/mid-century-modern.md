# Mid-Century Modern

Timeless 1950s-60s aesthetic with organic curves, tapered legs, and warm wood tones

**Mood:** warm, inviting, sophisticated, organic, understated

## Essence
A feeling of approachable warmth and sophisticated mid-century comfort, achieved through organic shapes, rich natural woods, and clean, unembellished forms.

## Palette
- Base: Rich Reddish-Brown, Creamy Off-White, Soft Caramel Brown
- Accent: Mustard Yellow, Olive Green, Burnt Orange
- Balance: Predominantly warm, muted tones. Rich brown wood colors form the largest surfaces of freestanding furniture. Cream and warm white define the background (paint or large textiles). Caramel appears in smaller, often textured textiles. Accent colors are used sparingly in small objects or single textile elements. Overall, moderately saturated.

## Materials
- Walnut (matte/satin)
- Teak (matte/satin)
- Wool (nubby/textured)
- Linen (matte/soft)
- Brushed metal (satin/matte)
- Clear glass (smooth/glossy)

## Shapes
Predominantly soft, organic curves and flowing, often asymmetrical shapes for primary volumes. These are frequently supported by lean, often tapered or hairpin-like, rectilinear or slightly angled elements. Overall silhouettes are low and horizontal.

## Texture and pattern
A mix of smooth, often polished surfaces with subtle, tactile textures from natural fibers. Patterns are minimal, often abstract, geometric or organic, and typically of a medium scale, used as an isolated accent.

## Light
Warm and inviting (around 2700K), emanating from multiple sources at varying heights to create soft pools of light rather than harsh, even illumination. Light sources often feature spherical or radiating forms.

## Composition
Often asymmetrical, with a focus on individual, sculptural forms that create visual interest. Objects are arranged to allow ample negative space, encouraging the eye to appreciate each piece. Layering is subtle, building depth with varied heights of low-profile elements.

## Contrast
Moderate contrast between warm, rich wood tones and lighter, creamy backgrounds. Smooth, polished surfaces are balanced by subtle fabric textures. Thin, dark lines of metal or wood often provide a crisp accent against broader, softer forms.

## Negative space
Generous. Surfaces tend to have few, carefully placed objects. Floors remain largely clear, emphasizing the sculptural forms of freestanding elements.

## Signature moves
- Low, horizontal volumes often featuring organic, curvilinear shapes.
- Slender, tapered or hairpin-like supports lifting forms off the floor.

## Avoid
- Ornate details
- Heavy textiles that obscure light
- Overly matched sets of furniture
- Excessively high contrast patterns
- Cold or stark lighting

## The full-build version
