# Five Stars

Five-star hotel finish — veined stone, walnut, brass, velvet and a statement chandelier

**Mood:** luxurious, serene, refined, elegant, composed

## Essence
An atmosphere of luxurious serenity, achieved through flawless surfaces, reflective accents, sumptuous fabrics, and thoughtfully chosen sculptural forms. The overall impression is opulent and composed.

## Palette
- Base: warm white, soft greige, mushroom taupe, smoky beige
- Accent: emerald, brushed brass, polished nickel
- Balance: Expansive warm neutrals form the primary backdrop. Deep brown-black and dark wood tones provide grounding depth. A single jewel tone is used sparingly for a focused impact. Metallic accents, both warm and cool, are distributed as small, bright highlights. The overall saturation is moderate with a leaning towards warmth.

## Materials
- Walnut veneer, smooth satin
- Veined stone, polished
- Brushed brass, matte
- Polished nickel, glossy
- Velvet, plush
- Boucle, nubby
- Smoked glass, translucent

## Shapes
Predominantly clean, tailored rectilinear forms, often low-profile and substantial in volume, sometimes featuring recessed bases for a 'floating' appearance. These are balanced by occasional soft curves and plump, sculptural volumes. Ribbed or fluted details add subtle texture to cabinet fronts.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of textures: smooth and matte plaster-effect surfaces contrast with the deep pile of rugs and the plushness of velvet and boucle. Polished stone and glossy metals introduce reflective qualities, while subtle fluting or ribbing adds refined tactile detail. Patterns are minimal and primarily found in natural stone veining or the subtle weave variations of textiles, avoiding overt graphic designs.

## Light
Warm (2700K) and soft, emanating from multiple sources to create distinct pools of light. Light is frequently low-level, often positioned behind elements to create a glow, or directed to highlight artwork and textured surfaces. It emphasizes reflections on glossy and metallic finishes rather than providing uniform illumination.

## Composition
A balanced and often symmetrical arrangement around a central axis, with occasional, deliberately placed asymmetrical elements. The eye is drawn to a single large focal point, such as an oversized artwork or mirror, complemented by layered lighting that defines zones. Objects are few and strategically placed on surfaces, creating vignettes with varied heights and materials.

## Contrast
Strong but refined contrast between expansive light neutral surfaces and deep, dark elements. This is heightened by the interplay of matte finishes with high-gloss and metallic sheens, and the detailed veining of stone against smooth surfaces.

## Negative space
Ample empty space is maintained on both walls and floor, emphasizing the generous proportions. Objects are few and meticulously arranged, allowing each piece to stand out with clear separation. Surfaces are largely clear, with minimal, sculptural adornment.

## Signature moves
- Expansive low-profile rectilinear forms, frequently featuring veined stone surfaces and a floating appearance.
- Dramatic, multi-layered warm lighting that creates intimate pools and highlights reflective materials and textures.

## Avoid
- Clutter and busy surfaces
- Flat, uniform lighting
- Generic or mass-produced appearance
- Cheap-looking textures
- Excessive or conflicting patterns
- Bright, primary colors

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Rule:** Everything should look custom or designer-grade; no flat-pack vibes.
- **Sofas:** tailored low-profile sofa; curved sectional (only if room supports it)
- **Chairs:** sculptural lounge chairs; boucle swivel chair; leather club chair
- **Tables:** stone plinth side table; glass + brass coffee table; marble-topped console
- **Overall:** designer-lounge comfort with clean silhouettes and premium finishes
- **Casegoods:** floating-look low credenza on a recessed plinth; ribbed-front sideboard; a long low media console

### By room
- **Dining:** stone or lacquer dining table; sculptural dining chairs; statement pendant centered over table; large art or mirror on main wall
- **Office:** executive desk with leather inlay; ribbed credenza; gallery wall in consistent frames; statement desk lamp in brass
- **Bedroom:** a tall freestanding upholstered headboard; hotel bedding: crisp white + layered throws; two matching bedside lamps; bench at foot of bed in leather or velvet
- **Bathroom:** spa-like stone trays and accessories; a large mirror flanked by plug-in lamps; brass accessories; hotel towel stack and tray styling
- **Living room:** add a statement rug; add a chandelier; upgrade to velvet or leather seating; create a bar vignette on a console

### Art, objects and plants
- **Art:** one oversized gallery artwork with a luxe frame; or a curated pair/triptych in consistent framing
- **Rule:** Sparse but deliberate; every object looks costly.
- **Decor:** large mirror with thin metal frame; minimal but expensive-looking ceramics; fresh florals or branch arrangement
- **Coffee table styling:** designer books stack; stone tray; small sculptural object (brass/ceramic); crystal decanter set (optional)

### Lighting pieces
- **Layers:** statement pendant or chandelier; a plug-in picture light or uplight washing a textured surface; table lamps with linen shades; floor lamp with metal shade; a warm glow from lamps set low behind furniture
- **Effects:** soft pools of light; highlight veining and texture; subtle reflections on metal and lacquer
- **Temperature:** 2700K warm

### Staging
- **Hotel signifiers:** layered lighting (statement pendant + floor and table lamps + accent); tailored full-height curtains; oversized art; statement chandelier or sculptural pendant; perfectly styled coffee table tray; fresh florals or sculptural branch arrangement; spa-like accessories (if bathroom) or concierge-bar vignette on a console (if living space)
- **Material cost signals:** large stone-topped tables and consoles; bookmatched marble or dramatic veining on furniture; fluted or ribbed fronts on freestanding cabinets; high-gloss lacquer accents; brushed brass and warm metals; custom-looking freestanding casegoods with shadow gaps; premium textiles (velvet, boucle, cashmere, silk)

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- cove lighting, toe-kick glow and wall washers -> lamps set low behind furniture and a plug-in picture light
- fluted, ribbed or upholstered wall panels and a stone slab feature wall -> a freestanding fluted screen or upholstered headboard, and stone on tabletops
- walnut millwork and a media wall -> freestanding walnut casegoods and a long low media console
- large-format tile or new wood floors -> the existing floor under a plush silk-wool rug
