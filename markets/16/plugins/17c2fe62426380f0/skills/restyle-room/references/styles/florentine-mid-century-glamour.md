# Florentine Mid-Century Glamour

One deep wall colour, white upholstery, dark glossy wood and brass

**Mood:** polished, inviting, sophisticated, classic, cinematic

## Essence
A sophisticated blend of tailored Mid-Century Modern forms and classical references, rendered in a palette of deep, saturated color, crisp white, dark polished wood, and warm metallics, creating a polished and inviting ambiance.

## Palette
- Base: chalk white (#F2EFE8), ink black (#1B1B1D), Capri navy (#1C2340), dark polished brown (#3A2219)
- Accent: polished brass (#B8923F), warm amber (#E08A1E), signal red (#C8322B)
- Balance: Crisp white dominates large textile surfaces and negative space. A single deep, saturated color (navy, emerald, or chocolate brown) covers significant wall areas. Ink black provides strong graphic lines and grounding. Dark polished wood tones add depth. Brass, amber, and small touches of signal red or pink provide warm, sparkling accents. The overall palette is balanced between cool and warm, with high saturation on the accent wall and low saturation on white.

## Materials
- dark polished wood
- polished brass
- crisp white cotton
- soft pale grey velvet
- honed marble
- translucent amber glass

## Shapes
A mix of elegant mid-century silhouettes: plump, rounded volumes with sinuous curves, often supported by thin, tapered legs. These are contrasted with strong rectilinear forms, particularly in dark lacquered finishes, and occasional circular elements. Proportions lean towards comfortable and substantial seating.

## Texture and pattern
Smooth and polished surfaces (glossy wood, brass, glass) are balanced by the natural textures of crisp cotton and linen. Softness comes from pale velvet. Patterns are scarce and graphic: often a large-scale black-and-white geometric, a bold stripe, or a subtle fretwork.

## Light
Warm and low-set, largely provided by numerous freestanding lamps with opaque or metal shades, creating pools of light rather than even illumination. Accent lighting from glowing amber glass or alabaster.

## Composition
Often symmetrical arrangements around a central feature or focal point. Objects are layered on surfaces and walls, creating a sense of history and collection. The eye is drawn to strong, saturated blocks of color and carefully placed art.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between deep, saturated wall colors and crisp white upholstery. Sharp graphic contrast of ink black lines and patterns against pale surfaces. Rich sheen of polished wood and brass against matte white textiles and surfaces.

## Negative space
Surfaces are thoughtfully populated with collected objects, books, and framed art. Walls are significant visual elements with a dominant color or a grid of framed pieces. The room feels full and lived-in, but not cluttered, maintaining clear pathways and visual breathing room.

## Signature moves
- A single deep, saturated wall color providing a dramatic backdrop for crisp white upholstery and dark, glossy wood.
- Strong graphic elements: either a black-and-white geometric pattern on a rug, or a grid of closely hung black-framed photographs.

## Avoid
- gilded baroque ornamentation
- busy floral patterns
- overhead downlight glare
- rustic distressed finishes
- minimalist empty rooms
- beige-dominated schemes

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Mid-century Italian and American shapes on tapered legs - button-backed armchairs, wing chairs, curved sofas, round pedestal tables - mixed with campaign-style four-posters and black lacquered consoles.
- **Signature pieces:** white wing chairs with contrast piping; round centre table stacked with art books; dark-wood four-poster hung with white cotton; brass-legged bar stools with leather seats; 1950s armchairs with exposed timber frames; black console on a brass Greek-key base; velvet dining chairs on brass legs
- **Arrangement:** Seating is grouped as in a private drawing room, symmetrical around a fireplace or centre table, with books, framed photographs and sculpture placed as if collected by an owner rather than styled by a hotel.

### Art, objects and plants
- black-framed black-and-white photographs
- plaster casts of classical statues and torsos
- stacks of art and fashion books
- white orchids in tall glass cylinders
- bronze heads and figures on stands
- framed prints on easels
- glass hurricane lamps

### Textiles and pattern
- plain white cotton and linen
- red, white and navy striped rugs
- geometric fret-pattern carpets
- full-length cream curtains on metal poles
- herringbone sisal stair runners
- monogrammed bed linen
- small graphic cushions in red or pink

### Lighting pieces
- Table lamps with dark or metal shades on every surface, brass-and-glass hall lanterns, mid-century brass sconces and brass picture lights; evening light is low and warm, with backlit onyx in the bar.

### Staging
- navy wall panels framed in white
- black and white chequerboard hall floor
- brass lattice screen
- round porthole windows and mirrors
- centre table of books under a lantern
- white louvred doors
- book-matched stone chimney breast

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- white-painted panelling, walls divided into panels by white mouldings, navy wall panels framed in white, reeded plaster or dark reeded wood -> flat painted walls in one deep colour (emerald, navy or chocolate) with NO mouldings, panel frames, dado rail or wainscoting on them; the white comes from upholstery, lampshades and picture mounts, the grid from black-framed photographs hung tightly together, and the ribbing from a reeded-front cabinet
- black stone, chequerboard or ebonised floors -> a black-and-white geometric rug over the existing floor
- dark glossy wood arches, white louvred doors and porthole windows -> a round porthole mirror, an arched floor mirror and a dark lacquered freestanding cabinet; never add arches, doors or windows
- brass lattice screens -> a freestanding brass or black-metal lattice folding screen
- backlit onyx bar and book-matched stone chimney breast -> an amber glass or alabaster table lamp and a stone-topped side table; a fireplace only if it already exists in the photo
- white beamed or coved ceilings -> keep the existing ceiling; one brass-and-glass lantern hung from the ceiling light point already in the photo
- brass wall sconces and picture lights -> plug-in brass sconces and table lamps with dark or metal shades on every surface
