# Modern Baroque

Checkerboard, cabbage roses, oversized scrolls and fearless colour

**Mood:** theatrical, cheerful, grand, exuberant, luxurious

## Essence
Theatrical and high-contrast cheerfulness, achieved through oversized baroque shapes, bold saturated colors, and dense floral patterns.

## Palette
- Base: crisp white (#F4F1E9), glossy black (#141414), bright turquoise (#5BB7DB), emerald green (#1F5C3F)
- Accent: scarlet red (#C2242C), rose pink (#D9527E), gilt gold (#C79A3B)
- Balance: Large surfaces are covered in crisp white, black, or one saturated, cool color. Small accents and patterned textiles introduce warmer tones and other cool hues. The overall saturation is high.

## Materials
- polished marble (black-and-white veined or checkerboard pattern)
- glazed cotton (smooth, with sheen, patterned)
- high-gloss lacquer (smooth, reflective)
- ebonized wood (smooth, often glossy)
- cut velvet (soft, plush, with sheen)
- gilt metal (smooth, reflective)
- matte plaster (textured, chalky)

## Shapes
Dominant shapes are generous, soft curves, scrolls, and robust, often plump volumes. Leg structures are substantial or curvaceous. Overall scale is bold and oversized.

## Texture and pattern
Textures are varied: smooth glazed cotton, plush velvet, high-gloss lacquer, polished wood and metal, contrasting with matte plaster. Patterns are bold and large-scale, dominated by lush florals, geometric checkerboards, and sometimes broad stripes.

## Light
Light is often pooled and dramatic, emanating from both high and mid-level sources. It is warm and ambient, reflecting off glossy and metallic surfaces to create a glamorous glow.

## Composition
Symmetrical compositions are favored, with a strong central anchor. Objects are arranged with a sense of grandeur and theatricality, often with layering of textures and patterns.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between highly saturated colors and brilliant white, black and white geometric patterns, and matte surfaces against high-gloss finishes.

## Negative space
The room is full and richly appointed, with surfaces frequently occupied by objects or layered with textiles. Walls, while often boldly colored, carry significant decorative elements.

## Signature moves
- A prominent black-and-white checkerboard pattern on a rug.
- Large-scale, curvaceous decorative elements in crisp white, contrasting with saturated walls.

## Avoid
- minimalist forms
- muted color palettes
- industrial finishes
- small-scale decoration
- sparse arrangements
- understated elegance

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Generous neo-baroque and Georgian shapes with cabriole legs, deep tufting and bold scale, mixed with sweeping curved banquettes.
- **Signature pieces:** chintz-upholstered wing chairs and stools; curved scarlet velvet banquettes; carved dark-wood console and center tables; four-poster beds with fabric canopies; tall lacquered screens; Chinese garden-seat side tables
- **Arrangement:** Grand symmetrical compositions around archways and fireplaces, with a dramatic center table anchoring big rooms.

### Art, objects and plants
- gilt eagle convex mirrors
- huge white urns overflowing with flowers
- blue-and-white porcelain lamps
- oil portraits in gold frames
- Staffordshire figures on mantels
- banana palms and tall tropical plants
- black grandfather clocks

### Textiles and pattern
- oversized cabbage-rose chintz
- rhododendron florals
- rose-swirl moire carpet
- bold stripes with banded borders
- damask screens
- solid scarlet and emerald velvets
- swagged and pelmeted curtains

### Lighting pieces
- Chandeliers, gilt sconces and black-shaded classical lamps; light bounces off polished marble and lacquer for a glossy, glamorous glow.

### Staging
- black-and-white checkerboard marble floor
- cabbage-rose chintz against saturated walls
- oversized baroque white plasterwork
- scarlet-and-emerald color blocking
- glossy black doors with white architraves
- swagged floral curtains at arched windows

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- black-and-white checkerboard marble floor -> a large black-and-white checkerboard area rug laid over the existing floor
- oversized white plaster mouldings, cornices and columns -> an oversized white scroll-framed baroque mirror, white plaster-look urns and brackets hung or standing, never new plasterwork
- high-gloss lacquered doors -> leave the doors as they are; put the high gloss on freestanding lacquered furniture and a tall lacquered screen
- wall-to-wall patterned carpet -> a large patterned area rug
- saturated walls -> paint or removable wallcovering is fine
