# The Queen of Color

Candy-bright colour, curved velvet and playful Parisian chic

**Mood:** playful, sophisticated, joyful, luxurious, feminine

## Essence
A feeling of joyful sophistication, achieved through unexpected, bold color combinations, plump rounded forms, and a confident mix of luxurious textures.

## Palette
- Base: Dusty Rose Pink (#E8B4B8), Warm Cream, Soft Terracotta, Mint Green
- Accent: Burnt Orange, Saffron Yellow, Deep Burgundy, Deep Teal
- Balance: Bold, saturated colors dominate, often layered for depth. A signature dusty rose pink or coral typically covers large surfaces or a significant textile element, balanced by warm cream and soft terracotta on walls or foundational pieces. Mint green or seafoam provide a cool counterpoint on medium-sized textiles or objects. Burnt orange, saffron yellow, deep burgundy, or deep teal are used sparingly as concentrated pops. Overall, the palette is warm and highly saturated.

## Materials
- Velvet, plush and saturated
- Bouclé, tactile and soft
- Lacquered wood, high-gloss
- Brass, polished
- Marble, bold-veined
- Ceramic, often glazed and colorful
- Silk, smooth

## Shapes
Dominantly curved, plump, and organic volumes with generous proportions, often set on recessed or subtly defined bases. These are frequently contrasted with some round or rectilinear forms in secondary elements. Sculptural and often oversized forms are prominent.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of soft and hard textures, with a strong emphasis on sumptuous, saturated velvets and tactile bouclé. Polished metals and lacquered surfaces provide sleek, reflective contrast. Patterns are generally subtle within materials (e.g., veined marble) or found in art, rather than geometric patterns on textiles or rugs. If patterns appear, they are often organic, abstract, or in large scale, avoiding small, fussy designs.

## Light
Warm (2700K-3000K) and intimate, creating a golden glow. Lighting is layered, with sculptural, often oversized pendant forms acting as art pieces, and decorative table lamps providing ambient pools of light. A subtle, low glow emanates from lamps placed behind elements, creating depth and softness.

## Composition
Asymmetrical arrangements create dynamic focal points, often centered around a significant 'color moment' on a large upholstered piece or a large-scale artwork. Furniture is angled into intimate groupings, promoting conversation and flowing circulation. Layered objects and diverse forms create visual interest without clutter, with each piece holding its own presence.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between soft, plush textures and hard, polished surfaces. High contrast between bold, saturated colors and softer neutrals. Matte finishes are juxtaposed with high-gloss sheen.

## Negative space
Spaces feel collected and layered with a curated abundance, yet maintain generous pathways and an airy quality. Surfaces hold a considered number of objects, each a statement piece, avoiding clutter. Objects float in the space rather than being pushed against walls.

## Signature moves
- Plump, curved upholstered forms in saturated, unexpected colors.
- A confident use of a signature pink or coral (#E8B4B8 or #FF6B6B) as a prominent color moment.

## Avoid
- Minimalist gray palettes
- Industrial or raw finishes
- Sharp, angular forms
- Monochromatic neutral schemes
- Sparse or empty spaces
- Harsh overhead lighting

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Art:** contemporary works with color relationships to the space. Large-scale pieces or curated gallery walls. Abstract or figurative with bold palettes
- **Density:** curated abundance - spaces should feel collected and layered but never cluttered. Each piece is a statement
- **Lighting:** statement pendant lights with sculptural forms - oversized globes, geometric clusters, or custom art pieces. Table lamps with ceramic bases in bold colors
- **Furniture:** curved, sculptural sofas in plush velvet. Rounded armchairs with generous proportions. Organic-shaped coffee tables in lacquered finishes or brass
- **Sofa:** deep, curved velvet sofa in coral, blush, or emerald. Generous cushions, rounded arms, low-slung silhouette. Consider channel tufting or button details
- **Table:** round or organic-shaped dining and coffee tables. Lacquered tops in bold colors, brass or gold bases. Marble with interesting veining
- **Chairs:** sculptural occasional chairs with organic shapes - egg chairs, bubble chairs, or custom curved designs. Upholstered in contrasting velvet or boucle
- **Storage:** lacquered cabinets in jewel tones, vintage buffets with brass hardware, freestanding shelving painted in accent colours

### Textiles and pattern
- **Rule:** layer soft and hard textures, matte and shiny finishes
- **Accents:** polished brass, lacquered surfaces, terrazzo, marble with bold veining
- **Primary:** sumptuous velvet in rich saturated colors
- **Secondary:** bouclé, mohair, silk for tactile variety

### Lighting pieces
- **Layers:** sculptural pendants as art pieces, decorative table lamps, a warm glow from lamps set low behind furniture, candles for ambiance
- **Statement pieces:** oversized pendants, custom chandeliers, neon art, illuminated objects
- **Color temperature:** warm 2700K-3000K for intimate, golden glow

### Staging
- **Circulation:** generous pathways, furniture floats in space rather than pushed against walls
- **Focal point:** create color moments - a bold sofa, a statement art piece, or an architectural color detail
- **Conversation areas:** intimate groupings with furniture angled toward each other, not walls

### Never
- minimalist gray palettes
- industrial or raw finishes
- sharp angular furniture
- monochromatic neutral schemes
- exposed concrete or brick
- sparse or empty spaces
- flat white walls without color
- harsh overhead lighting
- masculine or severe aesthetics

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- colour-block walls -> paint or removable wallcovering
- concealed cove lighting -> lamps set low behind furniture
- built-in shelving -> freestanding shelving painted in accent colours
- terrazzo and bold-veined marble -> on tabletops and objects, not the floor
