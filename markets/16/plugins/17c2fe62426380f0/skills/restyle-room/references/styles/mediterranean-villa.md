# Mediterranean Villa

Sun-drenched warmth with terracotta, natural stone, and lush greenery

**Mood:** warm, tranquil, organic, inviting, relaxed

## Essence
A sense of sun-drenched calm and natural warmth, achieved through earthy hues, organic textures, and ample diffused light.

## Palette
- Base: Earthy Terracotta Red, Creamy Off-White, Pale Sandy Beige, Warm Light Grey
- Accent: Deep Olive Green, Muted Ocean Blue
- Balance: Predominantly warm and earthy neutrals for large surfaces, complemented by the singular use of terracotta as a rich, grounding element. Accent colors are used sparingly for small details and natural elements, maintaining a low to medium saturation overall.

## Materials
- Unglazed terracotta with a matte finish
- Natural stone (honed or slightly textured)
- Wrought iron with a matte black finish
- Light-toned untreated wood (matte)
- Woven natural fibers (nubby)

## Shapes
Predominantly soft, rounded forms and gentle curves, often appearing in plump volumes or as graceful arching lines, balanced with occasional simple, robust rectilinear elements.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of varied natural textures: the subtle grit of unglazed terracotta, the smoothness of honed stone, the slight unevenness of hand-woven textiles. Patterns are minimal, usually organic and subtle, or expressed through the inherent variation of natural materials.

## Light
Abundant, soft, and diffused, often appearing as warm sunlight filtering into the space, creating gentle highlights and shadows rather than harsh contrasts.

## Composition
A relaxed, inviting arrangement, often featuring a prominent central element and a sense of natural flow. Elements are layered simply, focusing on inherent beauty rather than complex arrangements.

## Contrast
Gentle contrast between smooth and subtly textured surfaces, and between warm earthy tones and soft, muted blues or greens.

## Negative space
Ample, open areas that allow light to permeate, with objects arranged thoughtfully rather than densely, creating an uncrowded and airy feel.

## Signature moves
- The presence of rich, earthy terracotta red in rounded, substantial forms.
- Thin, dark, curvilinear lines accenting the space.

## Avoid
- Sleek, reflective metals
- Cool or stark dark colors (e.g., charcoal, black not as a fine line)
- Sharply angular or sparse, minimalist silhouettes
- Highly saturated or artificial hues
- Glossy synthetic materials
