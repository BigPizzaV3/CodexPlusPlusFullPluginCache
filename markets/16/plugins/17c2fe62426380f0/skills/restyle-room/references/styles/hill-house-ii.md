# Hill House II

Pale limewash, tomato-red drapes, a chartreuse rug and a green-backed library

**Mood:** tailored, modern, ordered, serene, sophisticated

## Essence
A tailored, historically literate modernist look that balances a calm, pale envelope with precise, high-character interventions, designed to snap into sharp focus.

## Palette
- Base: chalk_white, near_black, putty_neutral
- Accent: tomato_red, blackish_green, cognac, chartreuse, aubergine
- Balance: Large surfaces are chalk white, grounded by near-black for rugs and dark freestanding pieces. Putty neutral provides supporting warmth on textiles. Saturated accents of tomato red, blackish green, cognac, chartreuse, and aubergine are used sparingly on a single large textile plane, specific interiors of freestanding storage, and a rug. Overall, the palette is warm, with precise, bold color interventions.

## Materials
- matte limewash paint
- near-black wool (nubby)
- cognac mohair velvet (plush)
- mirror-polished chrome (glossy)
- dark veined marble (polished)
- cerused oak (textured matte)
- brushed cotton (soft matte)

## Shapes
Dominantly crisp, clear rectilinear geometry. Silhouettes are sculptural and edited, often low-slung, avoiding overstuffed forms. Vertical elements, such as freestanding storage, are scaled to human height, stopping short of the ceiling, creating open space above.

## Texture and pattern
A rich mix of textures including soft limewash paint, nubby wool, plush mohair velvet, soft brushed cotton, and open-weave sheer textiles. These are contrasted by glossy chrome and polished veined marble. Patterns are minimal and graphic, such as a subtle open weave or the natural veining of marble, with any bold color appearing as a solid field.

## Light
Natural daylight is prioritized, bright but soft. Evening light creates warm pools without an orange cast, cast from sculptural overhead and refined task lamps.

## Composition
A clear axial layout with calm geometry. A single large textile field (full-height drapery) serves as the primary visual anchor. Objects and freestanding pieces are carefully placed to create designed moments and define human scale within the volume, with a rug providing disciplined color intelligence.

## Contrast
Strong contrast between matte, pale walls and dark, grounded elements. Matte textiles and rougher textures are juxtaposed against mirror-polished chrome and glossy stone. Plain surfaces are highlighted by a single, bold graphic color field.

## Negative space
Edited medium-low. Surfaces are sparse and intentional, with objects placed to preserve composed negative space and allow individual elements to stand out.

## Signature moves
- A single, full-height plane of tomato-red brushed cotton drapery layered over cream fishnet sheers, reading as a bold graphic statement.
- A freestanding bookcase with fine-grid fronts and blackish-green interiors, positioned clear of the ceiling, establishing human scale.

## Avoid
- Clutter or excessive ornamentation
- Overstuffed or shapeless forms
- Overtly rustic or distressed finishes
- Warm light with an orange cast
- Uncontrolled or random color usage
- Architectural elements requiring building work

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Tables:** Coffee table in dark veined marble with crisp geometry.; One freestanding cerused oak console with a refined 1930s French sensibility.
- **Seating:** Cognac mohair velvet seating as the warm counterpoint to plaster + black floor.; Late-20th-century silhouettes with sculptural clarity (edited, not overstuffed).
- **Hero statement:** One mirror-polished chrome statement seating piece OR one mirror-polished chrome furniture accent that reads unmistakably high-character.
- **Object styling:** Books and objects are sparse and intentional.; A few ceramics and a few art pieces; everything placed with negative space.
- **Dining cameo optional:** Italian Chiavari chairs paired with a minimalist table.; paper lantern as the clean sculptural overhead.

### Lighting pieces
- **Fixtures:** one chandelier or sculptural overhead at room scale; one paper lantern option (dining); one slim brass piano/task lamp cue
- **Night mood:** warm pools of light without orange cast
- **Daylight bias:** natural daylight first  bright but soft

### Staging
- **Library move:** A freestanding chicken-wire-fronted bookcase (fine grid) with blackish-green interiors, clear of the ceiling.; Shelving rhythm gives an oversized room human scale.
- **Textile move:** Tomato-red brushed cotton drapery layered over cream fishnet sheers.; Drapery is tailored, full-height, and reads as a bold graphic plane.
- **Floor and plaster:** A near-black rug and dark wood pieces that visually ground the room over the existing floor.; Soft whitewashed limewash walls that keep the envelope calm and bright.

### Rules of the look
- **Tagline:** Snap the room into focus with an ordered, pale limewash envelope and near-black grounding; then add a few precise, high-character interventions: tomato-red drapery, a freestanding chicken-wire bookcase with blackish-green interiors, cognac mohair velvet, a chartreuse and aubergine rug, and edited mirror-polished chrome.
- **Principles:** Snap-into-focus: strengthen the room's underlying order first (pale walls, trim continuity, a dark grounding rug).; Human-scale the volume: one freestanding library bookcase, clear of the ceiling, becomes the calibrated gesture.; One big textile field: tomato-red drapery becomes the signature graphic move.; Complementary color intelligence: chartreuse + aubergine live as disciplined rug energy.; Material contrast: matte limewash and wool against mirror-polished chrome and dark wood.

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- ebonized or stained near-black floors -> the existing floor under a near-black wool rug border
- a built-in library wall or niche -> a freestanding chicken-wire bookcase clear of the ceiling, blackish-green inside
- a wall-hung console -> a freestanding console
- whitewashed plaster -> limewash paint
