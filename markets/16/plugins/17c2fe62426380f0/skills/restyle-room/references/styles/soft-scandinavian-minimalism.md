# Soft Scandinavian Minimalism

Pale oak, lime plaster, linen and diffused light

**Mood:** serene, tactile, warm, quiet, restorative

## Essence
A serene and tactile environment created by a restrained palette of warm, muted tones, diffused light, and a sparse arrangement of sculptural forms and natural materials.

## Palette
- Base: warm white plaster (#EDE7DB), oatmeal beige (#D6C9B4), pale oak (#C8A87C), stone grey (#9B958A)
- Accent: caramel sheepskin (#B67C42), olive drab (#6B6A4F), charcoal (#2B2B28), smoked brown-black (#3E362E)
- Balance: Predominantly warm and low saturation. Base colours cover large surfaces, such as walls and major textiles. Accents, including dark tones, are used sparingly for small objects or fine details.

## Materials
- White-oiled oak, matte
- Smoked oak, matte
- Linen, sheer and textured
- Bouclé wool, nubby
- Leather, matte with patina
- Travertine, matte
- Unglazed ceramic, matte
- Paper, diffused

## Shapes
Predominantly low and grounded. Forms are softly geometric, combining rectilinear elements with rounded, plump volumes, and occasional thin, linear details.

## Texture and pattern
Rich interplay of natural textures: smooth, coarse, soft, and nubby. Patterns are absent, with visual interest derived solely from material texture and subtle variations in weave.

## Light
Diffused and soft, warm-toned, creating an even glow. After dark, light sources are often low-level.

## Composition
Sparse and axial, with an emphasis on negative space. Objects are few and deliberately placed, creating a calm rhythm and directing the eye towards sculptural elements or framed views.

## Contrast
Gentle, primarily between light and dark tones, and between varied material textures. Contrast in saturation or pattern is minimal.

## Negative space
Significant empty floor and wall space. Surfaces hold few, carefully chosen, sculptural objects.

## Signature moves
- Low, softly geometric forms in warm, muted tones, placed sparsely to emphasize negative space.
- The pervasive diffusion of natural light through sheer textiles, complemented by soft, low-level artificial light.

## Avoid
- Bright saturated colours
- Printed patterns
- Glossy or reflective finishes
- Clutter
- Gallery walls
- Cool-toned lighting

## The full-build version
The concrete pieces and moves of the look as a designer would build it with no constraints. Use it to choose actual pieces. Anything fixed to the building goes through the renter swaps below, and furniture that belongs to another kind of room is translated to this room's purpose.

### Key pieces
- **Silhouettes:** Low, grounded and softly geometric — plank benches, rounded bouclé sofas, spindle-back oak chairs and monolithic stone plinths, all with quiet Japanese-Danish craft joints.
- **Signature pieces:** oak dining chairs with woven or pale upholstered seats; boucle sofa in off-white; solid-wood bench with leather cushion; stone or travertine pedestal side table; paper or fabric cone pendant; built-in oak wall of storage
- **Arrangement:** Sparse and axial: a few carefully placed pieces breathe inside empty space, aligned to window light and framed views of nature.

### Art, objects and plants
- single sculptural stone or ceramic vessel
- ikebana-like branch arrangements
- stacked art books
- hand-thrown bowls
- abstract line-drawing artwork leaning on sideboards
- organic wabi-sabi objects on plinths

### Textiles and pattern
- undyed linen curtains that filter light
- boucle and nubby wool
- flatweave wool rugs in oat tones
- sheepskin throws
- no printed pattern — texture is the pattern

### Lighting pieces
- Daylight diffused through floor-length linen sheers is the primary fixture; after dark, paper lanterns, small wall spots and hidden coves give low, even warmth.

### Staging
- niches with a single lit object
- framed views of sea or meadow
- curtain-diffused light
- Japanese-Danish craft fusion
- material honesty over ornament

## Renter-friendly swaps
What the look asks for -> what to do without construction.

- built-in oak wall of storage and flush oak joinery -> a long freestanding oak sideboard or low open oak shelving
- niches with a single lit object -> a stone or travertine plinth carrying one sculptural vessel, lit by a small lamp
- microcement or wide-plank floors -> an oat-toned flatweave wool rug over the existing floor
- lime-plaster, clay-render or dark-stained wood panelled walls -> limewash paint, with dark stained wood arriving through freestanding pieces
- hidden cove lighting and wall spots -> paper lanterns, a paper cone pendant and low table lamps
