# Coastal Calm

Relaxed beachy elegance with natural textures and ocean-inspired palette

**Mood:** relaxed, airy, fresh, natural, inviting

## Essence
A serene and refreshing atmosphere, achieved through a light-filled palette, natural textures, and organic forms that evoke a coastal environment.

## Palette
- Base: Soft White, Sandy Beige, Driftwood Gray, Natural Linen
- Accent: Ocean Blue, Seafoam Green, Coral
- Balance: Base colors dominate large surfaces like paint and major textiles, providing a light and airy foundation. Accent colors are used sparingly in small objects and occasional soft furnishings, offering subtle pops of medium saturation. The overall balance is warm-neutral with cool, desaturated accents.

## Materials
- Bleached wood (matte, smooth)
- Natural linen (nubby, soft)
- Rattan (woven, textured)
- Jute (coarsely woven, textured)
- Shell (smooth, lustrous)
- Rope (braided, textured)

## Shapes
Predominantly soft, rounded, and organic forms, often appearing substantial yet relaxed, occasionally contrasted with slender, open frameworks of natural materials.

## Texture and pattern
A rich interplay of varied natural textures—smooth, nubby, coarse, woven—creates depth and interest. Patterns are minimal, appearing as organic weaves or subtle, tonal variations within textiles.

## Light
Bright and even, maximizing natural daylight. Artificial light is diffused and warm, mimicking daylight at a moderate color temperature, creating a soft glow from varied heights.

## Composition
Asymmetrical and relaxed, with a focus on comfort and flow. Elements are layered to create depth through varied textures and forms, drawing the eye to natural focal points without strict formality.

## Contrast
Soft contrast is present between varied natural textures, light tonal differences in the base palette, and subtle, desaturated pops of accent color.

## Negative space
Ample negative space is maintained to provide an airy and open feel, with surfaces holding a few carefully placed objects rather than being heavily decorated.

## Signature moves
- The prominent use of textured natural fibers in substantial forms, such as coarsely woven rugs or large, open-weave textile coverings.
- The sporadic placement of small, organically shaped objects with a natural origin, such as polished shells or branching coral forms.

## Avoid
- Strong, high contrast color palettes
- Rigid geometric shapes
- Highly polished or shiny surfaces
- Synthetic textures
- Dark, heavy massing in objects
- Overly formal or symmetrical compositions
