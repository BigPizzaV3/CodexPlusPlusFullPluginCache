# Bheka for ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT about sightings, camera event flow, activity and your Plate Alerts matches in Bheka workspaces you approve. Bheka uses your existing account and returns read-only answers with links to supporting records.

Bheka is owned by Tech Local, which publishes this ChatGPT connection. For support, [contact Tech Local](https://www.techlocal.co.za/contact).

## Connect Bheka

1. Choose **Connect** for Bheka in ChatGPT.
2. Sign in with your existing Bheka account.
3. Select the workspaces ChatGPT may access, review the access summary and approve.
4. Ask about one of the workspaces you approved.

For help, see the [Bheka connection guide](https://bheka.systems/account/connected-apps/chatgpt) or [contact Bheka support](https://www.techlocal.co.za/contact).

## Try these prompts

- “Find sightings for ABC123 in my North Region workspace today.”
- “Summarize yesterday’s activity by camera in North Region.”
- “Show my recent Plate Alerts matches in North Region.”

Name the workspace and time period when you can. Bheka uses South African time for relative dates and limits each search to 31 days. For a regional summary, use the approved super-organisation workspace when it provides the authoritative regional view. If you ask about separate workspaces, compare them over the same period and keep results separate; their source records can overlap.

## Read the evidence carefully

- A plate value is an OCR reading, not confirmed vehicle identity.
- Separate sightings do not prove a continuous journey.
- Stale event flow does not by itself confirm a camera outage.
- A recorded notification status does not prove that someone received or viewed it.
- Results may be incomplete because of coverage or retention limits. Ask ChatGPT what period, workspace and filters it used, then open the Bheka links to check the underlying records.

This connection is read-only. It cannot change alerts or settings, export data, retrieve photos, or start investigations.

## Disconnect

In Bheka, open [Connected apps](https://bheka.systems/account/connected-apps) and disconnect ChatGPT. Reconnect to choose a different set of workspaces. Changes to your Bheka access or workspace permissions take effect on the next request.
