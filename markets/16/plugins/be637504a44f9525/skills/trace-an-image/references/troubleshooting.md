# Troubleshooting

Read this when the user reports that the camera, the placement or the overlay
is not working.

## The camera does not start

- **Opened inside another app.** Instagram, Facebook and the Google app open
  links in their own built-in browser, which usually cannot reach the camera.
  Copy the link and paste it into Safari or Chrome.
- **iPhone.** The camera needs a tap every time. Tap Start camera, then Allow.
- **Android, Chrome.** Chrome asks once per site. Choose "While visiting this
  site".
- **Samsung Internet.** Tap the lock or tune icon in the address bar, then
  Permissions, Camera, Allow, and reload the page.
- **Permission was denied earlier.** The browser may not ask again. Allow the
  camera for the site in the browser's site settings, then reload.
- **The address does not start with https.** The camera is blocked on insecure
  pages.

## The reference is hard to see

- Raise Opacity.
- Low Power Mode or battery saver dims the screen. Turn it off or raise
  Opacity.
- Add light on the paper, not on the phone screen.

## Detect cannot find the surface

- Step back until all four edges of the paper or canvas are in view.
- Put the paper on a surface of a different color so the edges stand out.
- Otherwise drag the four Corners onto the surface by hand.

## The reference drifts while drawing

- Without markers, the reference is fixed to the screen, not to the paper. The
  phone has to stay still; use a stand.
- With Anchor, at least one marker must stay in view. If the tool asks to show
  a marker, move the phone until it sees one.
- Print markers on matte paper. Glossy paper catches the light and hides them.
- Stopping the camera or switching between front and back cameras loses the
  anchoring. Sweep the markers again.

## The overlay looks stretched on a mug or jar

- Check that Surface is set to Cup, Roller or Ball and not Flat.
- Adjust Curve until the sides of the reference follow the object.
