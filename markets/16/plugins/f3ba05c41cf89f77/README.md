# Bible Study Companion

A Ukrainian-first Bible study skill for exegesis, theological analysis, sermon and teaching preparation, and source research. Its Protestant theological approach is compatible with the beliefs of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. This independent project does not claim official Church endorsement or authorship.

The default Ukrainian Bible text is the modern translation by Rafael Turconiak, published by the Ukrainian Bible Society in 2020 and reissued in 2023. The plugin has no separate external-service connection in this version.
