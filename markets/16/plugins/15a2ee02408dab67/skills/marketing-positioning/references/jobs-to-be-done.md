# Jobs-to-be-Done

Adapted into English from the supplied DELUCKS knowledge module.

Focus on the progress customers seek in a particular situation, not only product features. A customer uses an offer to accomplish something or overcome a barrier.

Ask:
- What task or responsibility does the customer need to handle?
- In what situation does the need arise?
- What outcome or progress matters?
- What prevents that progress today?
- What alternative, including doing nothing, is currently used?

A practical formulation: "When [situation], I want to [progress], so I can [desired outcome]." Treat it as a hypothesis until supported by customer evidence.

Typical B2B jobs include resolving a bottleneck, improving a process, reducing reputational or operational risk, building a competitive capability, saving time and being effective in a role. These are examples, not facts about an unresearched audience.

For leadership coaching, "buy coaching" describes the transaction. A possible job is "handle conflict confidently and keep the team effective without becoming overwhelmed." Verify that this reflects actual customer needs.

Use the questions "What does your customer want to achieve but currently struggles to do?" and "After using the offer, what should the customer experience differently?" Connect the resulting jobs to benefits, evidence, buying criteria and validation questions.
