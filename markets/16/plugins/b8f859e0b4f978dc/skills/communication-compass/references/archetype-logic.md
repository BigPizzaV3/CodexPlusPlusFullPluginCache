# Original TRX archetype tagging logic — qualitative reference, not a diagnostic

TRX Communication Compass™ – Full GPT Tagging Logic

This logic set enables GPT to automatically assign archetypes across all four Communication Compass™ modules by analyzing candidate responses.

### Synthesizer (Thinking & Learning Module)
- Trigger Indicators: connect ideas, see the big picture, synthesize, across domains, pattern recognition, zoom out
- Disqualifiers: need structure, step-by-step, linear process, manuals, prefer predefined paths
- Summary Output: You exhibit the Synthesizer archetype—a big-picture thinker who quickly connects ideas across domains to create insight and solve complex problems conceptually.

### Builder (Thinking & Learning Module)
- Trigger Indicators: follow structure, frameworks, systems, documentation, process improvement
- Disqualifiers: improvised, unstructured, spur-of-the-moment
- Summary Output: You align with the Builder archetype—someone who learns and executes best within structured frameworks and scalable systems, driving reliable improvement.

### Improviser (Thinking & Learning Module)
- Trigger Indicators: figure it out as I go, hands-on, pivot quickly, real-time learning, move fast
- Disqualifiers: need instructions, prefer consistency, hate uncertainty
- Summary Output: You reflect the Improviser archetype—a fast-moving learner who adapts in real time, solving problems by doing rather than planning.

### Analyst (Thinking & Learning Module)
- Trigger Indicators: analyze deeply, need clarity, logic-driven, research, prefer precision
- Disqualifiers: intuitive, feel it out, move without data
- Summary Output: You match the Analyst archetype—someone who learns through structure, data, and logic, and prefers understanding before acting.

### Connector (Thinking & Learning Module)
