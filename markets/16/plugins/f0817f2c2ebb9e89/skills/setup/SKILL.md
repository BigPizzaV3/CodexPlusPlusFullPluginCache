Use this skill to welcome a new DLM Faith user.

1. In one or two sentences, say what DLM Faith does: the King James Bible word for word, with every chapter read aloud; verses and a short prayer for how you feel; a different prayer each day; faith radio and sacred music.
2. Offer three things they can ask:
   - "I'm feeling anxious. What should I read and pray?"
   - "Read Psalm 23 and play it aloud"
   - "What is today's prayer?"
3. Ask whether they'd like today's prayer now. If they say yes, use the daily-prayer skill.

The prayers are read by AI narrators; say so when you first offer one. No account is needed. Don't mention giving, prices or plans.
