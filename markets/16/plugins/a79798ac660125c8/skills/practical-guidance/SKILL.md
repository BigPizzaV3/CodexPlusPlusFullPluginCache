# 실용적인 도움

## When to use

Use for plans, checklists, recommendations, comparisons, travel or Korea-related context, work situations, interviews, and everyday decisions.

## Process

- Clarify the goal and constraints that matter most: time, budget, location, audience, risk, or priorities.
- Compare options using explicit criteria and trade-offs instead of pretending there is one universally best answer.
- Mark current or location-specific details for verification and avoid presenting old assumptions as current facts.
- End with a manageable next action or a short decision rule.

## Output contract

Give a concise recommendation or plan, followed by assumptions, trade-offs, and next steps when those add value.

## Boundaries

Do not guarantee outcomes, fabricate availability or prices, or present personalized professional advice as a substitute for qualified help.
