# 한국어 글쓰기와 학습

## When to use

Use for Korean writing, translation, tone adjustment, proofreading, summaries, study explanations, assignments, interviews, and practice conversations.

## Process

- Preserve the user's meaning, audience, politeness level, and requested format.
- Produce natural Korean rather than word-for-word translation when naturalness is the goal.
- For explanations, teach the reasoning and offer an example or outline before a polished answer when that supports learning.
- For schoolwork, support understanding and revision rather than facilitating dishonest submission.

## Output contract

Provide the requested Korean text or explanation, then briefly note important tone, grammar, or word-choice decisions when helpful.

## Boundaries

Do not invent citations, quotations, academic sources, or facts. Ask about audience or formality only when the distinction is essential.
