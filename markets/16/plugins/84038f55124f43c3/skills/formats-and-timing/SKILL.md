## Platform guide (general guidance; specs change often, so check each platform's current help pages before a big launch)
| Platform | Main ratio | Common UGC sweet spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 9:16 | 15-45s | trend-native, captions in safe zone |
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 | 15-45s | cover frame matters |
| Stories (IG/FB) | 9:16 | 5-15s per card | fast hook, sticker-style text |
| Facebook / Instagram feed | 4:5 or 1:1 | 15-30s | many watch sound-off; captions essential |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 | 20-50s | loop-friendly ending, searchable title |
| YouTube in-stream ads | 16:9 | 15-30s | hook before the skip button |
| Snapchat | 9:16 | 5-10s | very fast, casual |
| Pinterest | 9:16 or 2:3 | 6-30s | how-to and inspiration, keyword titles |
| LinkedIn | 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16 | 30-60s | B2B, founder and expert content |

**Safe zones:** keep faces, product and text central; leave space at the top, right side and bottom of vertical videos. Film vertical with room around the subject so it can be cropped to 4:5 or 1:1.

## Words-per-second guide
| Pace | Words/sec | 15s | 30s | 45s | 60s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calm / premium | ~2.0 | ~30 | ~60 | ~90 | ~120 |
| Natural UGC | ~2.5 | ~35-38 | ~70-75 | ~110 | ~150 |
| Fast / energetic | ~3.0 | ~45 | ~90 | ~135 | ~180 |
Leave 1 to 2 seconds for silent product shots or the CTA card.

## Expert workflow
1. Count words and estimate length.
2. **Shorten:** cut setup first, merge benefits, remove filler; keep hook + one proof + CTA.
3. **Lengthen:** add a proof moment, demo step, objection answer or second benefit; never filler.
4. **Cutdown ladder:** 60s > 30s > 15s > 6s (hook + product + CTA).
5. Adjust hook text and CTA wording to each platform's culture.
