# Bản đồ chủ đề Tiêu hóa Nhi
Không cung cấp câu trả lời lâm sàng cố định; mỗi chủ đề phải cập nhật guideline theo thời điểm.

| Nhóm | Lớp phân tích học thuật | Điểm chống ngoại suy |
|--|--|--|
| GER/GERD, EoE | physiology/age-related symptoms; diagnostic criteria; RCT antacid/alginate/PPI | nhũ nhi ≠ thiếu niên; pH-impedance outcome ≠ symptom response |
| Abdominal pain/IBS/constipation | Rome criteria version; brain–gut axis; RCT psychological/diet/pharmacology | tránh gộp functional vs organic; tuổi mắc |
| H. pylori | diagnostic purpose, eradication trials, resistance | không thay adult test-and-treat cho trẻ |
| IBD | phenotype, treatment efficacy, PROs, mucosal healing | pediatric growth and development, TDM; trial mixed ages |
| Acute/chronic pancreatitis | standardized definitions, etiology, genetics, outcomes | pediatric causes and outcomes may differ adult |
| Cholestasis/biliary atresia | timing, workup evidence, screening and outcomes | newborn definitions, exact day-of-life |
| Wilson/metabolic liver | copper trafficking, genetics, clinical spectrum, case series | penetrance, pediatric variants, non-RCT evidence |
| Nutrition/malabsorption | growth indices, formula/interventions, RCT | age/gestation, feeding context, anthropometry |
| Diarrhea/intestinal disorders | infectious/noninfectious differential, oral interventions | geographic etiology & age |
| Endoscopy/ERCP | indication evidence, safety/quality, procedural studies | pediatric center experience confounding |

Nguồn chuyên môn đầu mối:
- NASPGHAN Clinical Guidelines & Position Statements — https://naspghan.org/professional-resources/clinical-guidelines/
- ESPGHAN Publications — https://www.espghan.org/our-organisation/Publications
- JPGN / professional society official pages; kiểm DOI của guideline/trials trước khi trích.

ESPGHAN tách Clinical Guideline chính thức khỏi Position/Committee Paper; không đánh đồng độ mạnh khuyến cáo.
