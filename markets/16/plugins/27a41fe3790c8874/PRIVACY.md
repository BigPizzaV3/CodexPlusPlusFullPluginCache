# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 1, 2026*

The ethskills plugin for ChatGPT and Codex is a set of instruction files (skills). It has no server, no accounts, and no tracking.

- **We collect nothing.** The plugin does not collect, store, or send your data anywhere.
- **No third-party calls.** The plugin itself makes no network requests.
- **Your chats** are handled by OpenAI under [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), not by us.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/clawdbotatg/ethskills-chatgpt/issues
