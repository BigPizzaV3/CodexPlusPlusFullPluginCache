HDI Overview

Explore guided capabilities across clinical workflow intelligence, cohorting and analytics, ontology mapping, care gap identification, care coordination, patient-level insight generation, and research.

Capabilities

Semantic Intelligence

Use ontology-aware data services to resolve clinical terms, codes, and concepts across connected health data sources.

Cohorting & Analytics

Ask population-level clinical questions in natural language, such as identifying uncontrolled diabetes cohorts or surfacing population trends.

Care Gap & Quality Workflows

Identify missing therapies, overdue labs, open care gaps, and quality opportunities for human review before downstream action.

Care Coordination

Assemble timelines, medication history, open referrals, active tasks, interaction risks, and care gaps for care teams.

Patient-Level Insight

Generate structured, traceable patient-level insights from connected clinical data and domain-specific agents.

Ontology Mapping

Map FHIR, ICD-10, LOINC, SNOMED CT, RxNorm, and related source data into canonical ontology contexts.

Research

Explore connected clinical data for research discovery, cohort feasibility, study planning, and evidence generation workflows.
