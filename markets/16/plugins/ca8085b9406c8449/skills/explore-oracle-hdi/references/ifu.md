# Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector — IFU

## Intended Use

Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector enables authorized users to access Oracle Health agents, tools, and data through permitted third-party AI agent clients using the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector is intended to support retrospective operational, clinical research, policy, population-health, and life-sciences research and analytics workflows, including cohort creation, care-gap analysis, patient summarization, patient prioritization, research exploration, and related analytic tasks.

Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector is an integration layer intended to facilitate adjunctive analytic support. Oracle Health agents accessed through Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector are not intended to replace clinical judgment, provide treatment or diagnostic recommendations, inform real-time clinical care, or support clinical decision-making for an individual patient.

## Indications for Use

Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector is indicated for use by authorized analytics, research, informatics, population health, care management, and operational users who are entitled to Oracle Health Data Intelligence capabilities and who access those capabilities through permitted third-party AI client interfaces.

Users may request analytic assistance, cohort or concept construction, data exploration, subject to customer entitlements, data rights, access controls, and jurisdiction-specific approvals.
