# Source and version

Publisher: StratusLIVE. Adapted from the published [Nonprofit AI Policy Template](https://stratuslive.com/tools/ai-policy-template/).

Policy content and tailoring rules are a versioned snapshot of the source model as available on October 6, 2026. Package version: 1.0.1. Website source commit: a4505421e3be64f3be673ab19b9779c12c497114.

The plugin uses the same fixed policy clauses, selected uses, oversight choices, vendor questions, and board questions. One board-preparation sentence is clarified: when disclosure is undecided, present the disclosure choices rather than implying that section 8 lists every option. Website delivery, CRM, tracking, and token-link behavior are excluded. Preparation dates are formatted in UTC for consistency. Revenue bands and author roles do not affect the policy clauses, so the plugin does not ask for them. They remain optional, validated legacy fields for importing website answers. They do not route users to sales.

This plugin does not verify a vendor's controls or certify legal compliance. The generated policy contains requirements the nonprofit proposes to adopt, not findings about an actual vendor or organization.
