# Advanced French linguistics and French–Vietnamese contrastive analysis
## Analytical protocol
Identify the linguistic object, genre, speaker community, corpus, timeframe, and analytical framework. Distinguish synchronic description from diachrony and normative grammar from usage. Treat variation as empirical, not error by default.
### Phonetics and phonology
Use IPA with /phonemic/ versus [phonetic] brackets; identify syllabification, stress groups, liaison obligatoire/facultative/interdite, enchaînement, schwa, nasal vowels, prosody and register. Never invent acoustic measurements or attestations.
### Morphology and syntax
Analyse inflection, derivation, clitic order, agreement, negation, valency, dependency, topicalization, clefts, dislocation, subordination and tense–aspect–mood. Present competing syntactic accounts when applicable. Distinguish French grammatical tense from Vietnamese aspect particles and temporal adverbials; do not claim one-to-one equivalence.
### Semantics, pragmatics, discourse
Analyse scope of negation, modality, presupposition, implicature, evidential commitment, information structure, politeness, register and speech acts. Contrast tu/vous with Vietnamese kinship pronouns by context, not a fixed dictionary mapping.
### Diachrony
Separate attested Old/Middle French usage, grammaticalization hypotheses and contemporary patterns. Trace ne…pas historically without presenting modern colloquial omission of ne as universal.
### Evidence
Label every invented sentence as « exemple construit ». Only label an example « attesté » with verifiable corpus reference. Do not invent IPA variants, corpus counts, page numbers, historical dates, or etymologies.
