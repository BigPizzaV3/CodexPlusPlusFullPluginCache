# Response templates (modify to suit user's question)

## A. Disease profile
**Tên bệnh / tên khác** (only verified codes if found)
**Tóm tắt (3–5 dòng)** including strength of genetic association
**Cơ chế bệnh & di truyền** with what is established/uncertain
**Dịch tễ** (population and denominator or `chưa có ước tính tin cậy`)
**Biểu hiện cốt lõi & bệnh học liên quan tuổi**
**Khung chẩn đoán phân biệt** (educational)
**Tiếp cận xét nghiệm** (sensitivity/limitations if verified)
**Quản lý và theo dõi (tổng quan)** with regulatory/age/evidence distinctions
**Tiên lượng & khoảng trống kiến thức**
**Nguồn** with named specific document, date, URL and access status

## B. Variant education (no patient specific verdict)
- Gene–disease pair and validation tier (if verified)
- Input variant exactly as provided; reference transcript/build if available
- Variant class, expected mechanism, zygosity/phase, phenotype congruence
- ClinVar classification(s), review status, last evaluation, conflicts (if checked)
- ACMG/AMP evidence examples (if evidence supplied; do not award formal codes without full supporting record)
- Interpretation limit: pathogenicity is NOT equivalently a diagnosis; VUS is inconclusive
- Reanalysis options discussed as learning concepts and specialist oversight
- References

## C. Short EBM update
| Domain | Confirmed new information | Source and date | Evidence strength | Implication/uncertainty |
Provide only verified entries; if search unavailable, do not fill with guessed findings. End with what was searched and what wasn't accessible.

## D. Teaching MCQ
- Fictional vignette, 1 best answer, 4–5 plausible options with distinct choices.
- On self-test mode, do NOT reveal correct answer until user responds.
- Then explain why right option is right, why alternatives are less appropriate, quality of supporting evidence, and what was not checked.
- Never solicit real patient identifiers; educational scenarios are invented.
