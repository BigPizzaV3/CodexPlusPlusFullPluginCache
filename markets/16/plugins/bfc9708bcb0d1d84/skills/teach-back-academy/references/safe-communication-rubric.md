# Safe communication educational rubric /24 — **not a WHO-validated tool**
Independent formative observational instrument. Not a clinical competence certification. One 0/1/2 score per observed criterion; N/O when no opportunity to observe. Report `a/b` with b=2×number observed, and quote real dialogue. 0 = absent/misleading if observed; 1=partial; 2=consistently clear/context-sensitive when opportunity exists. No forced pass score.

| # | Item | 2-point behavior anchor |
|---|---|---|
|1|Establish audience and purpose|States what will be learned and why relevant, checks context|
|2|Respectful approach|Nonjudgmental language, respects autonomy and emotions|
|3|Active listening|Open questions, acknowledges or paraphrases concerns|
|4|Plain language|Explains jargon, accessible word choice and examples|
|5|Chunking and pacing|Focuses 1–3 key messages, checks along the way|
|6|Accuracy and credibility|Indicates verified source and uncertainty appropriately|
|7|Relevance and inclusiveness|Adapts to audience/language/access without stereotyping|
|8|Safe action focus|Defines a realistic next step appropriate to educational scenario|
|9|Teach-back or alternate comprehension check|Non-shaming open prompt and hears reply rather than yes/no only|
|10|Correction and closure|Corrects misunderstanding without blame, rechecks if needed|
|11|Partnership and agency|Invites questions, preference, concerns, avoids coercion|
|12|Closes loop/follow-up|Summarizes plan, checks where to get verified information or questions|

**N/O examples:** #10 if no misunderstanding/occasion to correct; #9 if learner only sent opening line; #6 if scenario contains no sources and source evaluation is not possible. Stop at evidence; no inferred safety outcomes. Applicable to roleplay only and transcript-only audits; for brochures use six-principle checklist instead.

**During OSCE:** Do not reveal hidden key; only reply one turn as actor. On finish: 1) what happened, 2) `a/b observed`, 3) quote 2 strengths and 2 targeted fixes, 4) a rewritten sentence and opportunity to redo.
