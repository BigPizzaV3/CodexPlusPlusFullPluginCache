# WHO primary references — Học Giao tiếp an toàn
Trang HTML gốc WHO được đối chiếu ngày 2026-10-25 cho các URL đã mở trực tiếp; các liên kết còn lại phải được kiểm tra tại lúc sử dụng. **Do not assert full-text PDF reviewed** merely because the landing page was read. Hyperlinks may later change. These are WHO frameworks and tools **adapted for teaching**, not a WHO-endorsed plugin, WHO-approved rubric, or a clinical SOP.

| ID | WHO document | Official link | What can legitimately be attributed |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | **Communicating for health — WHO Strategic Communications Framework** (WHO) | https://www.who.int/about/communications/principles | SIX general effective communication principles: accessible, actionable, credible, relevant, timely, understandable; framework for WHO communications. **Do not call this a WHO 6-point OSCE scale or clinical guideline**. |
| W2 | **Health literacy** fact sheet, 22 Dec 2025 | https://www.who.int/vietnam/news/fact-sheets/detail/health-literacy | Access, understand, appraise and use information/services; both personal and organizational responsibility; not just reading skill. |
| W3 | **Health promotion / health literacy**, WHO discussion | https://www.who.int/teams/health-promotion/enhanced-wellbeing/seventh-global-conference/health-literacy | Health education broader than one-way information; interactive, participatory, critical appraisal and understanding determinants. |
| W4 | **Global Patient Safety Action Plan 2021–2030**, published 2021 | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240032705 | Strategic role of engaging patients and families and supporting information/education/self-care; avoid presenting as patient-specific treatment protocol. |
| W5 | **Patients for patient safety** | https://www.who.int/initiatives/patients-for-patient-safety | Patient/family/community engagement, partnership and being heard. |
| W6 | **Patient safety curriculum guide: multi-professional edition**, 6 July 2011 | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241501958 | Educational resource for patient safety; multi-professional training including patient/carer engagement and teamwork. |
| W7 | **5 moments for medication safety**, 1 July 2019 | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-HIS-SDS-2019.4 | Patient engagement tool about starting, taking, adding, reviewing and stopping medicines; **not a dosing algorithm**. |
| W8 | **WHO competency framework, risk communication and community engagement**, 2 May 2024 | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240092501 | Competency framework for RCCE **in public health emergencies**; avoid applying its status to ordinary individual consultations. |
| W9 | **WHO policy brief: Building trust through risk communication, community engagement and infodemic management**, 10 Dec 2024 | https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/who-policy-brief-building-trust-through-risk-communication-and-community-engagement-and-infodemic-management | Evidence-informed emergency/public-health risk communication and trust, community feedback and misinformation. |
| W10 | **Health promotion** WHO EMRO | https://www.emro.who.int/health-topics/health-promotion/introduction.html | Health education as communication, motivational and skill-building approach, part of broader health promotion. |
