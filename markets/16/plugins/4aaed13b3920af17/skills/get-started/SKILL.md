---
name: get-started
description: Explains what the Nivro connector can answer about a store (sales, orders, customers, stock, receivables, cash flow) and how to ask.
---

Nivro is a retail management system. This connector reads the user's own store data in Nivro, read-only.

When the user asks about their store, use the Nivro tools to answer from the data: sales summaries by period, orders, customers and what they owe, products and stock by size or color, overdue installments, cash flow and best-selling products. Answer in the user's language. Never claim to create, change or delete anything: the connector only reads. If the user has not connected Nivro yet, ask them to connect it and sign in with their Nivro account.
