# Rapid EBM appraisal map (use study-design-appropriate methods)

- Intervention RCT → Cochrane RoB 2; effect size, 95% CI, applicability, treatment adherence, trial registration. Reporting: CONSORT (verify current version; CONSORT 2025 available).
- Observational non-randomized intervention → ROBINS-I when appropriate (do not equate with JBI checklist).
- Diagnostic test accuracy → QUADAS family with version and applicability checked, reference standard, thresholds, spectrum/verification bias; reporting STARD. Check current version before specifying QUADAS-3.
- Prognosis prediction models → PROBAST/PROBAST+AI as applicable, discrimination/calibration, validation; reporting TRIPOD/TRIPOD+AI.
- Systematic review → ROBIS/AMSTAR 2 depending purpose; PRISMA is a reporting checklist not risk-of-bias instrument; inspect search currency, duplicates, publication bias.
- Certainty across outcome-level evidence → GRADE (not inferred solely from RCT vs cohort), separate certainty of evidence from recommendation strength.
- Clinical practice guidelines → AGREE II or AGREE-REX depending question; organization, year, update/version, country, and conflicts.

Default extraction: Population | intervention/exposure/test | comparator/reference | outcome | design | N | effect + CI | harms | bias | indirectness | clinical relevance | source status.
When full text is unavailable label "abstract-only appraisal" and do not invent methods/results. Never convert a source's absolute effects to NNT without a valid baseline and period.
