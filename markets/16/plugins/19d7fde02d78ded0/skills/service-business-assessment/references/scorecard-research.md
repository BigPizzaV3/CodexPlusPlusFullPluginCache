# Format references

Reviewed September 9, 2026. Recheck live documentation before relying on current product features.

- [BrightLocal Local Search Audit](https://help.brightlocal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025565753-How-can-I-create-a-Local-Search-Audit-Report): business category and search location inform local comparisons; supports PDF, external report and page-level evidence. Adopt paired brief/detail delivery and explicit geographic context.
- [Vendasta Snapshot Report](https://support.vendasta.com/snapshot-report/): channel summaries and shareable assessments improve scanning. Its [historical 2019 sample](https://support.vendasta.com/assets/files/Snapshot_Report_Sample-0db7c22a46bb13f887da1c6188e1d88d.pdf) is a layout example, not current benchmark data. Use transparent checks rather than adopting proprietary grades.
- [LinkedIn Ad Library](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1632229): understand retention, advertiser filters and country-specific fields before historical claims.
- [Ahrefs traffic estimates](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/944144-how-can-i-check-organic-search-traffic-of-sites-i-don-t-own): estimates are distinct from actual owner traffic and other channels.

Additional value in this workflow: independent inbox verification; advertiser identity before count attribution; null-safe measurement fields; agent-readable source provenance; and an editable opportunity model with assumptions visible.

## Editorial design references

Reviewed September 9, 2026 for the Wunderland format exploration:

- [Pentagram: Silver Linings](https://www.pentagram.com/work/silver-linings): an editorial research report with consistent chart treatment and an interactive assumptions tool. Useful for relating the concise narrative to deeper evidence.
- [Pentagram: Lloyd’s of London](https://prod.origin.pentagram.com/work/lloyds-of-london): coordinated print and digital reporting, restrained typography and selective color. Useful for a shared PDF/web system.
- [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Benchmarks Report](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf): findings beside supporting charts. Use its pacing as a layout reference, without importing its statistics or agency sales framing.
- [Stripe 2024 annual letter](https://stripe.com/en-ca/annual-updates/2024): connected prose with charts introduced to develop the argument. Useful for keeping an owner’s business question ahead of the tool output.

Skye selected “Project field guide” for Wunderland: warm paper, serif headings, Overtime’s restrained blue accent, actual company photography and concise evidence tables. The renderer supports this through `brief.theme: field-guide` and an optional supplied `heading_font`. Three generated concept boards remain separate design samples; generated projects are never evidence of the business’s work. This is a selected treatment for the pilot, not a mandatory format for every industry.
