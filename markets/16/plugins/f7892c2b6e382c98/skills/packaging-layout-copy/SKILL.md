# Panel Layout and Packaging Copy

Use for label content, front/back/side/top/bottom panel layouts, packaging copy, and mapping user-supplied content to an attached dieline.

## Process

1. Separate supplied product facts, approved brand language, requested creative copy, and unknown or legally required text.
2. Build a panel-by-panel content map for the actual format. Respect dieline orientation, folds, seams, glue areas, windows, and user-supplied production zones.
3. Establish a readable hierarchy: product/brand, product type, variant, verified benefit if any, quantity when supplied/required, and secondary information. Adjust for category and current requirements when verified.
4. Draft optional marketing copy only from verified facts. Label creative, unverified suggestions so they cannot be mistaken for approved factual claims.
5. Use explicit placeholders for unknown content, e.g. `[manufacturer address required]`, `[verified net quantity]`, or `[nutrition panel from approved source]`.
6. Return unresolved content as a checklist. Do not claim mandatory panels are complete based on a visual layout.

## Output

Prefer a table with panel, purpose, supplied content, proposed placement, and missing/verify notes. For copy drafts, separate front-panel copy, supporting copy, and placeholder/legal fields. Use [copy and claims rules](../packaging-design-assistant/references/copy-and-claims.md) and [format guidance](../packaging-design-assistant/references/packaging-types.md).

## Checks and boundaries

Never fabricate ingredients, nutrition values, quantity, product efficacy, medical claims, certifications, warnings, manufacturer details, barcodes, QR destinations, recycling instructions, or regulatory text. Do not convert a suggested phrase into a verified claim. If jurisdiction-specific label rules are requested, route to `packaging-compliance-research` and use current authoritative sources.
