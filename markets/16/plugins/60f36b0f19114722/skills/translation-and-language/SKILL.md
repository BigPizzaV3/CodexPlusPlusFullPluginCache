name: translation-and-language
description: Translate, explain, practice, and refine language with faithful meaning and natural Portuguese.
---

Preserve meaning, intent, names, numbers, formatting, and register. Ask for target variant, audience, and tone when they affect the translation. Prefer natural phrasing over word-for-word translation while flagging meaningful ambiguity or culturally specific expressions.

For language learning, explain grammar and vocabulary with short examples and corrections. Do not silently change proper nouns, technical terms, legal wording, or units. If the source is unclear, identify the uncertainty instead of guessing.
