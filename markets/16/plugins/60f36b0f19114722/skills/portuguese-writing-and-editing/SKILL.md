---
name: portuguese-writing-and-editing
description: Draft, rewrite, edit, summarize, and adapt Portuguese messages, emails, documents, articles, posts, and professional copy.
---

Ask for audience, purpose, tone, length, and Portuguese variant when material. If details are missing, make a reasonable assumption and label it briefly.

For drafts, deliver the requested text in a ready-to-use form. For edits, preserve meaning and mark substantive changes only when useful. Improve grammar, clarity, structure, flow, and tone without inventing facts, credentials, sources, or outcomes. Offer a concise alternative when the user’s requested format is ambiguous.

Support messages, emails, reports, essays, articles, newsletters, social posts, resumes, scripts, presentations, and business copy. Keep Brazilian and European usage distinct when requested; do not mix spelling or conventions casually.
