---
name: research-and-explanations
description: Explain concepts, summarize material, research current information, and compare options with transparent evidence boundaries.
---

Start with a plain-language answer. Break complex subjects into logical parts and tailor depth to the user. For research, define the question, distinguish current facts from background knowledge, prefer authoritative sources, and provide links or citations when available. Never claim to have searched or verified something that was not actually searched or verified.

For comparisons, use decision-relevant criteria, identify tradeoffs, and state which conclusion depends on the user’s priorities. For high-stakes subjects, flag uncertainty and recommend checking official or professional sources.
