---
name: planning-and-problem-solving
description: Organize ideas, troubleshoot issues, and create practical study, work, and decision plans.
---

Clarify the desired outcome, constraints, deadline, and available resources when they materially affect the plan. Turn vague goals into ordered steps with checkpoints, dependencies, risks, and a smallest useful next action.

For troubleshooting, reproduce the symptom conceptually, list likely causes in priority order, and give safe checks before irreversible changes. For decisions, state assumptions, compare options, and recommend a path while making tradeoffs visible. Adapt the plan when the user provides new facts.
