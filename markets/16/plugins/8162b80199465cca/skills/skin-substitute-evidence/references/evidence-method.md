# Assess assertions, study quality, and applicability

## What is being disputed?

| Assertion | Investigation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| A Q code alone makes the product experimental | Exact cited coding/coverage authority and service-period code identity | Code assignment alone does not answer efficacy, regulatory status, or coverage. Do not claim all Q-coded products are covered. |
| Regulatory status or intended function is inadequate | Actual product/version, primary regulatory documents, label/IFU, and documented function | Do not rename a use to fit an argument. Regulatory classification and reasonable/necessary coverage are separate inquiries. |
| No adequate product-specific efficacy evidence | Original direct clinical studies and gaps | Related-product studies support only a stated bridge, not automatic equivalence. |
| Evidence does not apply to this wound/population | Inclusion/exclusion, wound type, care context, comparator, outcomes | DFU findings do not automatically establish pressure, venous, traumatic, or surgical-wound effects. |
| Case-specific medical necessity is unsupported | Contemporaneous course, alternatives, serial response, relevant clinical factors, provider explanation | Literature does not repair absent historical facts or establish the individual's benefit. |
| Policy excludes or restricts use | Applicable public service-date/jurisdiction policy, its scope and legal weight | A later policy, another jurisdiction's LCD, or a favorable decision does not silently displace the governing authority. |

The reviewer's statement is evidence of the rationale, not proof of the rule. Present the verified authority and the response argument separately. For applicable Medicare authority, use the shared [source register](../../../references/source-register.md).

## Two axes, not one score

**Quality:** randomized allocation and concealment; actual comparator; prospective registration/protocol; blinding or independent assessment; sample and analysis denominators; exclusions after randomization; missingness/attrition; duration; outcome definition; analysis method and uncertainty; funding and conflicts. Randomized origin does not eliminate bias in a selected interim analysis.

**Applicability:** exact product/version and formulation; tissue composition/layers; preservation and sterilization; dose/application regimen; intended and documented function; wound category; relevant clinical eligibility; standard care and co-interventions; setting; endpoint and measurement method. Higher design quality can coexist with lower relevance to the actual product or wound.

| Evidence type | What it may support | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Direct product RCT | Comparative effects in the studied population, subject to methods | Effects in every wound, guaranteed individual benefit, or coverage |
| Related-product RCT | Biological/clinical context and an explicit comparability argument | Automatic equivalence or transfer of that product's numerical effect |
| Comparative observational RWE | Real-world associations under disclosed adjustment/design | Elimination of unmeasured confounding |
| Consecutive/all-eligible uncontrolled series | Observed outcomes and feasibility in a defined cohort | Attribution of improvement exclusively to the graft |
| Selected success cases or individual reports | Documented examples, trajectory, hypotheses | Cohort success rate, counterfactual hospitalization avoidance, or causal effect |
| Systematic review/meta-analysis | Synthesis under disclosed selection and compatible methods | More certainty than its underlying studies or relevance to an unstated product |
| Laboratory/in-vitro study | Measured structure/function and biological plausibility | Human healing rate or clinical comparative effectiveness |

Peer review is a publication property, not a guarantee of design quality. "Real-world evidence" describes a broad evidence family, not an automatic quality level. A narrative aggregation of cases is a case-series synthesis unless the methods actually establish a systematic review/meta-analysis. Conflicts must be disclosed, not used alone to accept or dismiss a finding.

## Bridging table

| Dimension | Target product | Studied product | Primary source for each | Similarity/difference/unknown | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Consider layers, structural properties, preservation, processing/sterilization, composition, clinical role, application schedule, and wound population. A shared manufacturer or process name is a hypothesis to investigate. Marketing descriptions may document a manufacturer claim but do not independently verify equivalence. Explain material differences and unresolved details; never fill them with plausible assumptions. Do not convert a bridge into the target product's healing percentage.

## RWE summary review

Ask whether the series includes all eligible/consecutive cases or selected successes; whether the denominator and attrition are known; whether baseline, follow-up, complete closure, and recurrence are consistently defined; and whether independent assessment or comparison exists. Report treatment changes, standard care, and confounding where available. "Improved after treatment" is an observed association, not proof that the graft caused closure or avoided hospitalization.

Check measurement compatibility. Length × width planimetric estimates differ from expressions incorporating depth or side-wall area. Percent reduction requires a consistent baseline and follow-up method; do not compare incompatible metrics with trial area-reduction results. Near closure is not complete epithelialization. Missing data is not zero response. Do not repair a summary by inventing measurements, eligibility, clinical testimony, or missing follow-up.

## Response positions

For each proposed argument, give the exact assertion, authority/source, narrow supporting observation, applicability limits, contrary evidence, and further confirmation needed. A useful provisional position might be: "The cited report provides product-named randomized-study data in DFU; it does not resolve applicability to the wound category described here." Do not claim that this refutes all experimental/investigational findings.

If browsing/full text is unavailable, use accessible verified material with its access limits, list unresolved checks, and continue with the framework. Never invent a study, DOI, quotation, page, completed search, or verification date.
