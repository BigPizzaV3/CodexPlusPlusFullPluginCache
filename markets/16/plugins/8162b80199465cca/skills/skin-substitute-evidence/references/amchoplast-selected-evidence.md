These are manufacturer statements. They do not establish clinical equivalence to other products or a coverage determination.

## Clinical randomized-study report

Hall HJ, Keenan A, Massey K, D'Costa WF, Moyer P. *Bridging the Wound Gap: Interim Results From Randomized Trials Evaluating Dehydrated Human Amnion-Intermediate Layer-Chorion Membrane for the Treatment of Non-healing Diabetic Foot Ulcers.* Cureus. 2025;17(11):e97875. DOI: [10.7759/cureus.97875](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.97875). [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41458871/); [full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12743229/).

The original full text was reviewed for this snapshot. The authors pooled interim data from ELITE (reported ongoing) and CAMPLIFE (reported terminated), comparing AmchoPlast plus standard care with standard care in non-healing DFUs. They reported favorable wound outcomes in the analyzed sample. Randomized origin distinguishes this from uncontrolled case reports, but the pooled interim analysis needs its own bias assessment.

The report describes 64 randomized participants and 53 in the analysis (28 product-plus-care, 25 standard-care); only 29 completed the protocol. Eligibility for the analysis depended on follow-up completeness, and some outcomes used the most recent visit rather than a common completed endpoint. The two protocols differ. Assessment was open-label without independent closure verification for this interim analysis, recurrence follow-up was short, and attrition was material. The studies were sponsored by Cellution, with financial relationships disclosed. Consult methods/results and limitations/disclosures in the full text before quoting an effect size.

**Permitted inference:** there is a published product-named randomized-study report in the studied DFU context; it warrants consideration with its limitations. **Limits:** this is not a completed final RCT across wound types, a pure all-randomized analysis, proof of effects in pressure/surgical/traumatic wounds, a success probability for an appeal, or a determination of Medicare coverage. Recheck trial status rather than describing the 2025 study status as current.

## Structural/functional study

Muthuchamy M et al. *Structural and functional validation of dehydrated human amnion/chorion allograft: A biological wound cover.* Molecular and Cellular Endocrinology. 2026;621:112869. DOI: [10.1016/j.mce.2026.112869](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mce.2026.112869); [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42462898/). The supplied paper records online availability July 16, 2026; the indexed issue is November 2026.

The paper examines membrane structure and in-vitro function. It supplies laboratory context, not comparative human healing results. Authors disclose LifeCell employment and funding. Do not treat structural preservation or cellular assay results as a clinical healing rate. Product/version and processing correspondence to the actual coded item still require confirmation.

## What to add for a particular appeal

Identify the exact denial theory and applicable policy; verify product identity; determine whether the wound resembles the clinical study population; map relevant contemporaneous standard-care and response facts through the evidence-map skill; evaluate any other direct/related studies with the same method. RWE may contribute observations in the actual product/wound context, but selection and confounding must remain explicit. The [decision example](decision-example.md) illustrates adjudicator consideration, not proof of product efficacy or broad precedent.

No client case reports, patient facts, product brochures, copyrighted article PDFs, or detailed RWE production procedures are bundled. Missing additional studies are **not** a finding that none exist. For another product, retrieve its own sources rather than reuse these conclusions.
