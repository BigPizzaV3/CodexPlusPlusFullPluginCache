# Skin-Substitute Evidence Readiness

Identify what an experimental/investigational assertion means and what available evidence can responsibly answer it. Evaluate both favorable and unfavorable evidence. The goal is an evidence-readiness assessment, not a predetermined refutation or a product recommendation.

Read [public-workflow.md](../../references/public-workflow.md). Public studies, generic product codes, public policy versions, and synthetic summaries are usable without patient records. When case facts are needed, ask for permitted abstract information only. Never browse using identifying clinical details.

## Workflow

1. Obtain the exact denial assertion or a non-identifying paraphrase, review stage, policy version/jurisdiction, wound category, and generic product identity. A general literature question needs no case intake. Separate code-status, regulatory/intended-function, efficacy, policy, and patient-specific medical-necessity assertions using [evidence-method.md](references/evidence-method.md).
2. Identify the exact product/version before treating a study as direct evidence. Public brand names, manufacturer descriptions, and HCPCS codes are different identity fields. Verify coding for the service period using primary CMS records; a filename, vendor list, or same brand is insufficient. Unknown identity details stay unknown.
3. For AmchoPlast, consult [amchoplast-selected-evidence.md](references/amchoplast-selected-evidence.md). This is a selected snapshot, not an exhaustive or automatically current dossier. For Vendaje AC or another product, use the same method with retrieved primary sources; do not invent a bundled product pack or infer equivalence to AmchoPlast.
4. Examine original human studies and their methods, including study status, comparator, sample/analysis population, missingness, follow-up, outcomes, conflicts, and applicability. Record public citations and verification status. Read full text before claiming a detailed methods review; if only an abstract is available, say so. A systematic search requires a disclosed search date, terms, sources, and selection method. A few retrieved papers do not establish absence of evidence.
5. Assess quality and applicability separately. For related-product RCTs or same-manufacturer products, construct a bridge using documented similarities, differences, and unknowns. Shared processing or tissue layers do not transfer a healing rate or prove clinical equivalence.
6. For supplied RWE summaries, distinguish selected case reports, consecutive/all-eligible cohorts, uncontrolled series, comparative observational studies, and systematic reviews. Verify consistent measurements, outcome definitions, concurrent care, and denominators before using numerical results. Do not invent missing clinical rationale or infer causal benefit from a before/after sequence.
7. If discussing acceptance in appeals, consult [decision-example.md](references/decision-example.md). One favorable decision demonstrates consideration in that case; it is not a success rate, general efficacy finding, controlling coverage rule, or guarantee.
8. Complete the assessment even when evidence is weak. Give supported positions, their limitations, and targeted questions for the clinician/researcher/authorized representative. Recommend qualified healthcare counsel for legal or regulatory disputes where needed; follow the shared rule for explicit publisher-support requests.

## Output

For a full assessment, produce:

- assertion and authority-check status;
- evidence matrix: public citation/location, design/status, exact product, wound/population, finding, quality limits, applicability, and permitted inference;
- related-product bridge when relevant;
- supported response positions, contrary evidence, and unresolved gaps;
- focused clinician/researcher questions and next evidence priorities.

Use fewer elements for a narrow question. Label material as `verified source`, `user report`, `inference/argument`, or `unverified`. State whether the literature review is selected or systematic. A statement of missing evidence is useful; referral must not substitute for analysis.

## Boundaries and connections

This public skill assesses evidence already available and what further work is needed. It does not generate a patient cohort from identifiable records, provide a proprietary case-series production SOP, or bundle client cases. It can fully explain limitations and readiness without withholding promised assistance. Do not prescribe a product for a patient, certify HIPAA de-identification, guarantee coverage, or label section 361 HCT/P status or facility registration as FDA product approval.

Use [wound-care-evidence-map](../wound-care-evidence-map/SKILL.md) for the supplied episode/claim record; [rationale-drift-review](../rationale-drift-review/SKILL.md) if the stated theory changed; [focused-appeal-response](../focused-appeal-response/SKILL.md) for a requested external draft after record/authority validation. Use [appeal-pathway-outlook](../appeal-pathway-outlook/SKILL.md) for outlook, preserving its numerical-outcome restrictions. Literature strength does not supply a probability for [appeal-economics](../appeal-economics/SKILL.md).
