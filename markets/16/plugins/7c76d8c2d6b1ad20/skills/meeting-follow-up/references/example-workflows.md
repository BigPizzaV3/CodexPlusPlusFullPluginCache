# Example workflows

## Project meeting with a revision

**Source:** “We can launch Tuesday. The analytics aren't ready. Later: let's move launch to Thursday; everyone agrees. Sam, can you confirm the dashboard by Wednesday? Sam: yes.”

- Decision: launch Thursday (`CONFIRMED`); note that it replaced Tuesday.
- Action: confirm the dashboard; owner Sam; deadline phrase “by Wednesday.” Resolve the date only if the meeting date is known and unambiguous.
- Blocker: analytics readiness, if the source indicates it still blocks launch.
- Do not retain Tuesday as the current plan or invent who will finish analytics.

## Brainstorm with an unassigned task

**Source:** “Maybe we should update the onboarding guide. We need to check analytics before launch.”

- The guide change is a proposal, not a decision or assigned task.
- The analytics statement may be an open issue; create an action only if context clearly makes it an agreed task. If treated as a task without an owner, mark `UNASSIGNED`.
- No deadline is specified.

## Sales call

Summarize the prospect's goals, problems, requirements, objections, buying criteria, stakeholders, and timeline only where explicitly discussed. Keep salesperson interpretation separate. Do not infer budget or decision authority. Capture promises as commitments and preserve the wording of any follow-up dates.

## Client project review

List requested work separately from approved scope. Capture responsibilities, deliverables, approval state, dates, dependencies, and blockers. Do not turn a client's request into approved work unless the meeting clearly confirms it.

## Standup

For each participant, group supported updates into completed, working on, blockers, and needs help. Then summarize shared blockers, dependencies, and urgent items. Use the supplied speaker label if the person's identity is unavailable.

## One-on-one

Summarize the requested topics and follow-ups. Keep performance, personal, and career details to the minimum needed. Do not repeat sensitive personal information simply because it appears in the source.

## Client-safe email

Draft from confirmed information only. Exclude internal negotiation comments. If a commitment or date is uncertain, mark it for the sender to verify instead of presenting it as agreed.
