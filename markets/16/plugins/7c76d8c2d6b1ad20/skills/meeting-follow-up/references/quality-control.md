# Quality-control checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- The full available source was considered; later revisions were not missed.
- Each decision is separated from proposals, questions, and general discussion.
- Each action is a supported task, not an inferred next step.
- Every named owner is supported by an assignment or acceptance; otherwise it is `UNASSIGNED`.
- No deadline, priority, status, dependency, or approval was invented.
- Relative dates were resolved only with an unambiguous meeting date/reference; original wording remains visible.
- Conflicts and reversals are represented accurately.
- Source locations and excerpts are real and accurately attributed.
- The client-facing version omits internal-only material unless specifically requested and appropriate.
- Personal or confidential information is minimized and relevant.
- The output follows the requested format and has no unsupported claims.
- Drafted messages are clearly drafts; none is described as sent or posted.
- No claim is made that this skill stores, deletes, or synchronizes meeting data.
