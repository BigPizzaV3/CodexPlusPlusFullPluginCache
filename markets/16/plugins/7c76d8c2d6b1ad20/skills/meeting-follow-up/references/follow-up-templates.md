# Follow-up templates

Use these as structures, not as a reason to add content. Fill only fields supported by the meeting. Use neutral placeholders or omit a section when information is missing.

## Follow-up email

**Subject:** [Meeting recap / agreed next steps — topic]

Hi [recipient/team],

Thanks for the discussion. We covered [brief factual recap].

**Agreed decisions**
- [Confirmed decision]

**Next steps**
- [Task] — [Owner or “Owner unassigned”] — [Original deadline phrase or “No deadline specified”]

**Open questions**
- [Unresolved question, if any]

Best,
[Sender name, only if supplied]

Avoid implying approval, agreement, a delivery date, or an obligation beyond what participants stated. If the transcript contains a conflict or unclear owner, make the draft conditional or flag it for review before sending.

## Slack or Teams recap

**Quick recap:** [One or two factual sentences]

**Decisions:** [Confirmed only]

**Actions:** [Task — owner — deadline phrase]

**Open:** [Question/blocker]

Keep it concise, preserve uncertainty, and do not claim the message was posted.

## Client-facing recap

Thank the client, summarize the relevant discussion, list confirmed decisions and agreed responsibilities, and state supported dates. Exclude internal-only comments, negotiation strategy, private personnel matters, or unrelated details. If scope was requested but not approved, call it “requested” or “under review,” not “agreed.”

## Internal recap

Include relevant context, decisions, tasks, risks/blockers, dependencies, and open issues. Sensitive internal notes should be included only when relevant to the requested purpose; keep them concise and attributed. Clearly label opinion as opinion rather than meeting fact.
