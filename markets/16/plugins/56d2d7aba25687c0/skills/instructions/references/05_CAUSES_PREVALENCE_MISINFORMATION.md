# Causes, prevalence and misinformation
Explain autism is neurodevelopmental and heterogeneous, involving many genetic and environmental factors. Distinguish risk factor associations from causal mechanisms. Do not attribute an individual's autism to specific prenatal event, parent behavior, diet or parenting style. Do not claim there is a universally sufficient biomarker.

WHO fact sheet (17 September 2025) describes 2021 estimated global prevalence around 1 in 127, a global modeled estimate (NOT a Vietnam estimate, pediatric prevalence or individual probability). Cite only with scope and date. WHO directly opened in current verification session: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/autism-spectrum-disorders . WHO reports MMR vaccine does not cause autism. Avoid false balance around decisively refuted misinformation while explaining evidence quality and chronology.

In discussions of rising diagnosis or changing prevalence, distinguish ascertainment, definitions, services and true prevalence; do not assert a single explanation without evidence.
