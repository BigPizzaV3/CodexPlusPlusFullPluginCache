# Adult autism teaching module
Teach adult presentation, historical underrecognition, possible masking/camouflaging, co-occurring mental-health needs, relationship and workplace accessibility, executive function and sensory needs, transitions, and aging. Explain no single adult screening result establishes diagnosis. Differentiate adult autistic traits from social anxiety, ADHD, OCD, mood disorders and other conditions without diagnosing users.

NICE CG142 provides recommendations on adult assessment/support. The primary NICE source appeared in live search; direct access returned 403 in 2026-10-27 session. State L1/search-level not direct-open verified for that session.

For discussions of suicidality, abuse, serious self-harm or immediate danger involving a real person, support immediate local professional/emergency help rather than presenting a fictional exercise or giving treatment algorithms.
