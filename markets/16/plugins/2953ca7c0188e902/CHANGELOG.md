# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-11 — OpenAI ChatGPT Directory Compliance Update)
- **OpenAI Directory Manifest & Licensing:** Added root `LICENSE` (MIT) and `"license": "MIT"` in `plugin.json` and `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`; updated `shortDescription`, `longDescription`, `commerce_description`, and `release_notes` to cover both the 48 artist skills and the 42-role Label Team (264 playbooks) with explicit non-endorsement and general-audience (13+) disclosures.
- **MCP Tool Independence, Schemas & Annotations (`mcp.json` & `plugin.json`):** Added explicit OpenAI tool schemas and boolean annotations (`readOnlyHint: true`, `destructiveHint: false`, `openWorldHint: false`) and response-minimization declarations for `plan_release`, `check_release_readiness`, and `get_epk_outline`. Replaced the legacy `find_skills, get_skill` dynamic discovery test case in `plugin.json` with a direct skill test case (`register-tracks-with-pro`) to comply with OpenAI's Tool Independence and Exposure requirement.
- **Commerce, Pricing & Promotional Copy Removal:** Removed the `/pricing` link and promotional `"free to download and use"` / `"Free Royalty Health Check"` phrasing from `README.md` and `get-started`, removed `"or sell"` digital playbook language from `build-a-skill`, removed `"for free"` from model-readable skill descriptions (`get-ein`, `claim-tracks-with-pro`), and removed the `"Cost"` column and competitor `https://claude.ai` rows from all 42 `skills/label-*/references/tools.md` files.
- **Privacy, Restricted Data & Location Boundaries:** Updated `artist-profile`, `get-ein`, `create-llc`, `register-with-pro`, `claim-tracks-with-pro`, `create-split-sheet-agreement`, and `directory-search` to use local placeholders (`[LEGAL_NAME]`, `[BUSINESS_ADDRESS_ON_FORM]`, `[DOB_ON_PORTAL]`) instead of soliciting legal names, dates of birth, or street addresses in chat. Expanded universal Guardrails across all 95 skills (including the 4 helper skills) to prohibit all four OpenAI Restricted Data categories (PCI DSS, PHI, Government IDs, Credentials/Secrets), precise location data (street addresses and GPS coordinates), and full conversation transcripts.
- **Label Team Reference Consistency:** Completed the `"agent"` -> `"role"` and third-person table header cleanup across all 42 `skills/label-*/references/tools.md` and `skills/label-*/references/sources.md` files.
- **Core v2.0.0 Content:** Bundles all 48 Artist Shop skills, 42 Label Team roles (264 playbooks), the `label-team` router, and 4 live-tool helpers (`get-started`, `plan-release`, `check-release-readiness`, `draft-epk`), with corrected royalty routing (SoundExchange digital performance vs. PRO composition performance vs. The MLC US streaming mechanicals).
