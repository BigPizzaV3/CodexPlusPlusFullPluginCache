# Live & Touring Manager · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Bandsintown for Artists | Show listings and fan alerts | https://artists.bandsintown.com |
| DICE / Eventbrite (ticketing) | Ticketing | https://dice.fm |
| AtVenu (tour merch inventory & settlements) | Tour merch inventory and settlements | https://www.atvenu.com |
| Square POS | Merch POS | https://squareup.com |
| Google Sheets | Budgets and settlements | https://sheets.google.com |
| Chartmetric | Audience by city | https://chartmetric.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Artist availability and audience data
- Tour budget
- Agent and venue contacts

## Outputs produced

- Tour plan and budgets
- Offer comparisons
- Advance and day sheets
- Settlements and tour summaries

## Starter stack

Bandsintown for Artists, a Google Sheets budget and settlement template, Square for merch, and Chartmetric free for city data.
