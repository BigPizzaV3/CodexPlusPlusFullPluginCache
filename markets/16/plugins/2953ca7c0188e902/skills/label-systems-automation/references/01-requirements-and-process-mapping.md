# Label Systems & AI Automation Lead · Playbook 1: Requirements and process mapping

Understanding the job before building.

## Steps

1. Interview the people or roles doing the work
2. Map the current steps
3. Find the bottlenecks
4. Write simple requirements

## Deliverable

Process map and requirements

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
