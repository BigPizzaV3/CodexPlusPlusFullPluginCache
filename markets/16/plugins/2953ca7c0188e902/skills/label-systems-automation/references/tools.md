- Current processes and pain points
- Tool list and logins (stored securely)

- Process maps
- Systems and automations
- Integration docs
- Training guides
- Governance checklist

## Starter stack

Airtable, Notion, Zapier or Make starter tiers (or self-hosted n8n), and an AI assistant. This folder of roles is your starting system.
