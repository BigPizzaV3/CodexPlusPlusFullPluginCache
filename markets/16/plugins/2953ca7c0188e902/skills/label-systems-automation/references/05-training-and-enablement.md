# Label Systems & AI Automation Lead · Playbook 5: Training and enablement

Helping people (and roles) use the systems.

## Steps

1. Run short training sessions
2. Write how-to guides
3. Hold office hours
4. Collect feedback and improve

## Deliverable

Training guides

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
