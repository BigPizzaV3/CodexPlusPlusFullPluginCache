# Label Manager / GM · Playbook 5: Performance and risk reporting

Telling the owner what's working, what isn't, and what could go wrong.

## Steps

1. Pull streams, revenue and campaign results from Data and Finance
2. Compare against plan
3. List the top three risks with likelihood and impact
4. Recommend one action per risk

## Deliverable

Weekly owner report (one page)

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
