# Label Manager / GM · Playbook 7: Hiring the right role (or human)

Deciding which roles to automate, outsource, or hire.

## Steps

1. Map the workload against this role roster
2. Identify gaps that need a human
3. Write a short brief or job post for the gap
4. Recommend contractor vs. hire based on budget

## Deliverable

Team plan and role briefs

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
