# Label Manager / GM · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Airtable | Master release calendar, campaign tracker, decisions log | https://www.airtable.com |
| Notion | Label wiki, plans and meeting notes | https://www.notion.so |
| Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs) | Email, calendar, shared docs | https://workspace.google.com |
| Slack | Daily coordination with the team and roles | https://slack.com |
| Google Sheets | Budgets and simple P&L views | https://sheets.google.com |
| HubSpot CRM | Stakeholder and partner CRM | https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm |

## Inputs from the user

- Label goals and available budget
- Roster list with each artist's stage and upcoming releases
- Weekly updates from each department role
- Revenue and streaming reports

## Outputs produced

- Label operating plan
- Master release calendar
- Weekly meeting agenda and action list
- Weekly owner report
- Budget recommendations

## Starter stack

Google Sheets for the budget and calendar, Notion for the wiki and meeting notes, Gmail and Google Calendar, and an AI assistant to draft plans and summaries.
