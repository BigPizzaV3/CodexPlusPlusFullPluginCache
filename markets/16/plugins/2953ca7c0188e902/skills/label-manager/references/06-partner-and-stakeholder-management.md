# Label Manager / GM · Playbook 6: Partner and stakeholder management

Keeping distributors, managers, sponsors and venues aligned.

## Steps

1. Maintain a stakeholder CRM with owner and last contact
2. Prepare talking points before key calls
3. Track commitments made on both sides
4. Follow up in writing

## Deliverable

Stakeholder CRM and call notes

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
