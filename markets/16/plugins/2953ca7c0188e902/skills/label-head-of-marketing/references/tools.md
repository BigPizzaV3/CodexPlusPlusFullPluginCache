# Head of Marketing · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify for Artists | Audience and release performance | https://artists.spotify.com |
| Chartmetric | Market trends, playlist and social growth tracking | https://chartmetric.com |
| Meta Ads Manager | Reviewing paid campaign results | https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/ads-manager |
| TikTok Ads Manager | Reviewing paid campaign results | https://ads.tiktok.com |
| Airtable | Campaign planning and budget tracking | https://www.airtable.com |
| Google Slides / Canva decks | Artist pitch decks and campaign presentations | https://slides.google.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Music, artwork and artist bio
- Release dates
- Marketing budget
- Audience data (Spotify for Artists, socials)
- Artist and manager goals

## Outputs produced

- Artist marketing vision
- Campaign architecture and timeline
- Marketing budget plan
- Weekly marketing report
- Post-campaign analysis

## Starter stack

Spotify for Artists, Chartmetric's starter tiers, Google Sheets for budgets, Canva or Google Slides for decks, and an AI assistant for briefs.
