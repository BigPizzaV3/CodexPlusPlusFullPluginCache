# Label Assistant · Playbook 5: Meeting prep and follow-up

Agendas, materials and action items.

## Steps

1. Draft agenda from open items
2. Gather materials
3. Take notes and record decisions
4. Send actions with owners and dates

## Deliverable

Agenda and action list

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
