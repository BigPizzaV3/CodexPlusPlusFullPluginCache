# Content Calendar

> A posting plan you can actually keep up with.
> *Content desk · Content & marketing · about 20 minutes*

## When to use

- The artist posts randomly or not at all.
- A release or event needs a content run-up.
- The artist wants a sustainable schedule.

## Ask first

Read the artist profile first (`artist-profile.md`, or the profile pasted in chat) and only ask for what is missing. Ask in one short batch.

- Platforms the artist will actually use (pick one to three).
- Hours per week available for content, and who helps.
- Upcoming dates: release, shows, video drops.
- Voice and what the artist is comfortable showing (face, studio, personal life).
- Best-performing past posts, if known.

## Workflow

1. Cap the plan to the artist's real hours. A smaller plan that is kept beats a big plan that is dropped.
2. Define 3 to 5 content pillars (for example: the music, process and studio, the story, community, and the offer).
3. Choose cadence per platform from available hours; do not exceed what can be produced.
4. Lay out 30 days: date, platform, pillar, format, idea, call to action. Tie the run-up and follow-up to release dates.
5. Create a batching plan: one or two sessions to shoot or record a week of content at once.
6. Add a weekly review question and the single metric to watch.
7. Reserve flex slots for reacting to real moments.

## Deliverables

- 30-day calendar table.
- Pillar definitions with example ideas.
- Batching schedule.
- Weekly review checklist.

Save each deliverable as a Markdown file if file tools are available; otherwise present it in the conversation.

## Output template

| Date | Platform | Pillar | Format | Idea / hook | Call to action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Quality checks (run before handing over)

- [ ] Total posts per week fit the hours the artist stated.
- [ ] Every post has a pillar and a call to action.
- [ ] Release dates are reflected before and after.
- [ ] At least 20 percent of slots are easy to produce.

## Guardrails

- Do not recommend buying followers, engagement pods, or other manipulation.
- Do not fabricate trends or statistics about the best time to post.
- Never invent facts, numbers, credits, quotes, placements, or contacts. If the artist has not supplied something, ask or mark it `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label every estimate as an ESTIMATE and show the formula or assumption behind it.
- Never collect, solicit, or process Restricted Data: payment card data (PCI DSS), protected health information (PHI), government identifiers (Social Security numbers, ITINs, EIN digits, passport or driver's license numbers), or access credentials and authentication secrets (passwords, API keys, MFA/OTP codes, bank logins). The artist enters sensitive information directly on official sites.
- Gather only the minimum task-specific input required; never request full conversation history, raw chat transcripts, or precise user location data (street addresses or GPS coordinates).
- Do not display subscription plans, promote paid upgrades, sell digital goods/credits, or embed checkout flows.
- Draft first. Never claim anything was filed, registered, sent, posted, uploaded, or paid. The artist takes every outside action.

## Hand-offs

- `generate-social-media-content`: write the posts
- `release-music-with-dsp`: align with the release plan
- `analyze-social-media-engagement`: review what worked

## Start with

> Plan 30 days of content for me. I have this many hours a week and these platforms.
