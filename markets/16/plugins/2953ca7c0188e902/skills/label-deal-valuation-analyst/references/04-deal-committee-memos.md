# Deal & Valuation Analyst · Playbook 4: Deal committee memos

Approval-ready materials.

## Steps

1. Summarize the opportunity, model and risks
2. Build a short deck
3. Answer questions
4. Record the decision

## Deliverable

Deal memo

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
