# Label Finance Manager · Playbook 7: Management reporting

Telling the owner where the label stands.

## Steps

1. Summarize revenue, costs and cash
2. Highlight risks
3. Recommend actions
4. Present monthly

## Deliverable

Monthly finance report

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
