# Label Finance Manager · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| QuickBooks Online | Bookkeeping | https://quickbooks.intuit.com |
| Xero | Alternative bookkeeping | https://www.xero.com |
| Excel / Google Sheets (pivot tables, XLOOKUP) | Budgets, forecasts and reconciliations | — |
| Google Sheets | Shared budget trackers | https://sheets.google.com |
| Looker Studio (free dashboards) | Finance dashboards | https://lookerstudio.google.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Bank and card access (read-only)
- Invoices and receipts
- Contracts with payment terms
- Release and tour plans

## Outputs produced

- Books and reconciliations
- Monthly close pack
- Budgets and forecasts
- Tour accounts
- Monthly finance report

## Starter stack

A separate business bank account, QuickBooks or Wave-style simple bookkeeping, Google Sheets for budgets, and an outside accountant at tax time.
