# Rights & Catalog Administrator · Playbook 4: Conflict and claim resolution

Fixing duplicate and disputed claims.

## Steps

1. Research ownership documents
2. Contact the other claimant or society
3. Submit evidence
4. Track to resolution

## Deliverable

Conflict file

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
