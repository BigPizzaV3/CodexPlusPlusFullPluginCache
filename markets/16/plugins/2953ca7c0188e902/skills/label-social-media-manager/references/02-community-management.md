# Social Media & Community Manager · Playbook 2: Community management

Moderating and growing fan communities.

## Steps

1. Reply to comments and DMs
2. Moderate Discord and fan groups
3. Spotlight fan content
4. Escalate issues

## Deliverable

Community log

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
