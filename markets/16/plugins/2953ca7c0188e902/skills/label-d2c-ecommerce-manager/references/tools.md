# D2C & E-Commerce Manager · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify | Main store | https://www.shopify.com |
| Bandcamp | Music and merch store for core fans | https://bandcamp.com |
| Klaviyo (email & SMS) | Store email/SMS | https://www.klaviyo.com |
| Laylo (drops & fan alerts) | Drop alerts | https://laylo.com |
| Google Analytics 4 | Store analytics | https://analytics.google.com |
| Printful (print-on-demand merch) | No-inventory merch | https://www.printful.com |
| Canva | Store banners | https://www.canva.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Product list and inventory
- Release and tour calendar
- Store budget

## Outputs produced

- Store and product pages
- D2C campaign calendar
- Conversion and weekly reports

## Starter stack

Bandcamp (no monthly fee) for music and merch, Printful for print-on-demand, and Laylo free for drop alerts. Move to Shopify when volume grows.
