# Get Started with Artispreneur

Artispreneur gives independent artists 48 step-by-step skills for the business side of music, plus a 42-role Label Team (264 playbooks) for anyone running a label, plus four live-tool helpers. Everything is **draft-first**: the skills produce checklists, documents, and plans in this conversation. They do not access accounts, file, send, post, register, pay, or release anything. The artist takes every outside action.

## How to use this skill
1. If the user has no `artist-profile.md`, offer to run `artist-profile` first (about 10 minutes). Every other skill reads it, so facts are asked for only once.
2. Match the user's goal to a skill from the catalog below and run it.
3. If the user is unsure, ask one question: "What is the next thing you want to finish: a release, a business setup, a press kit, bookings, royalties, or content?" Then pick.
4. Quick routes: running or starting a label -> `label-team`; release date given -> `release-music-with-dsp`; "am I ready" -> `distribution-readiness`; "get paid" -> `royalty-readiness`; "pitch" -> `directory-search`; "what now" -> `weekly-win-plan`.
5. Never invent facts, shows, numbers, or clearances. Mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`. Legal and tax content is general information, not professional advice.

## Optional live tools
Where the Artispreneur MCP server is connected (`https://www.artispreneur.com/mcp`, no sign-in), these helpers compute checklists: `plan-release`, `check-release-readiness`, `draft-epk`. Without it, the matching full skills do the same job.

## Full catalog (48 artist skills)
### Career: Know where you're going.
- `artist-profile`: Your artist record, set up once. Every other skill reads from it.
- `artist-goals`: Turn 'I want to blow up' into 1 to 3 dated goals you can actually hit.
- `weekly-win-plan`: Your top 3 moves this week, ranked by what actually moves the needle.

### Studio: Look and sound like a pro.
- `create-an-epk`: A press kit built for the person reading it: booker, press, brand, or festival.
- `create-an-artist-bio`: One story, told at every length you'll ever need.
- `create-brand-logo`: A logo brief a designer (or Canva) can execute on day one.
- `write-brand-guidelines`: Your brand bible: voice, visuals, and rules everyone follows.
- `brand-positioning`: The one true thing about you that no other artist in your genre can say.

### Legal entity: Make it a real business.
- `get-ein`: Your federal tax ID directly from the IRS, done step by step.
- `create-llc`: Separate your music money from your personal money.
- `create-c-corp`: Incorporate for investors, a label, or scale, with eyes open.
- `legal-business-checklist`: The business basics (entity, copyright, licensing, PRO) in the right order.

### Contracts: Put it in writing.
- `create-split-sheet-agreement`: Everyone's share, in writing, before the song blows up.
- `license-music`: Pitch-ready sync briefs and licensing basics, in plain English.
- `contract-intake`: Understand any music contract in plain English before you sign.

### Finance: Know where the money goes.
- `open-business-bank-account`: Walk into the bank (or app) with everything they'll ask for.
- `manage-business-expenses`: Know where every dollar goes, and keep the receipts that save you taxes.
- `file-business-taxes`: Get organized for tax season instead of panicking through it.
- `track-royalties-on-dsps`: Every royalty stream in one tracker, so nothing slips through.
- `write-a-business-plan`: A real plan with real numbers, for you, a partner, or a lender.
- `budget-planning`: A plain-language budget and runway for your release, tour, or year.

### Publishing & PRO: Get paid when your songs play.
- `register-with-pro`: Join ASCAP, BMI, or SESAC the right way, as writer and publisher.
- `register-tracks-with-pro`: Register every song so royalties can actually find you.
- `royalty-readiness`: Make sure every royalty stream you're owed has somewhere to land.
- `claim-tracks-with-pro`: Find and claim royalties already owed to you.

### Release & DSP: Ship music the right way.
- `distribution-readiness`: Everything checked before you upload, and the right distributor for you.
- `release-music-with-dsp`: A release plan that works backward from drop day.
- `set-up-dsp`: Claim and polish your artist profiles on every streaming service.

### Catalogue: Keep every track organized.
- `upload-track-to-catalogue`: Every track logged once, reused by every other tool.
- `extract-metadata-from-track`: Clean, complete metadata for every track in your catalog.
- `transcribe-lyrics`: Clean, formatted lyrics ready for DSPs, videos, and liner notes.

### Content & marketing: Show up consistently.
- `create-content-calendar`: A posting plan you can actually keep up with.
- `generate-social-media-content`: Platform-native posts in your voice, ready to publish.
- `create-lyric-video`: A kinetic lyric video, ready to render, no editor skills needed.
- `write-music-video-treatment`: A shoot-ready treatment a director or friend-with-a-camera can run with.
- `upload-music-video-to-youtube`: Everything ready to upload your video to YouTube.
- `analyze-social-media-engagement`: See what's working, what's not, and what to post next.

### Merch: Sell something fans can wear.
- `create-merch-designs`: Merch your fans actually want to wear.
- `set-up-merch-drop-shipping`: Print-on-demand merch with zero inventory risk.

### Outreach: Reach the people who can help.
- `directory-search`: Find the right blogs, playlists, radio, podcasts, press and venues for your genre.
- `booking-outreach`: Venue and promoter outreach for the cities and dates you want.
- `pr-campaign`: A 5-email press sequence built around one real, newsworthy hook.
- `media-outreach`: Personal pitch emails for every journalist and blog on your list.
- `add-music-to-collaborative-playlist`: Real playlist curators, honest pitches, no pay-for-play.
- `playlist-outreach`: Short, curator-friendly pitches matched to playlists in your genre.

### Build your own: Make the toolkit yours.
- `knowledge-ingestion`: Teach your agent: turn your bios, contracts and past EPKs into facts it remembers.
- `build-an-agent`: Spin up your own sub-agent for a project, with its own tools and memory.
- `build-a-skill`: Turn your workflow into a skill you can reuse or share with your team.

## Guardrails

- Never collect, solicit, or process Restricted Data: payment card data (PCI DSS), protected health information (PHI), government identifiers (SSN, ITIN, EIN digits, passport or driver's license numbers), or access credentials and authentication secrets (passwords, API keys, MFA/OTP codes, bank logins).
- Gather only the minimum task-specific input required; never request full conversation history, raw chat transcripts, or precise location data (street addresses or GPS coordinates).
- Do not display subscription plans, promote upgrades, sell digital goods, or link to checkout/pricing pages.
- Never invent facts, numbers, credits, quotes, placements, or contacts. Mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Draft first. Never claim anything was filed, registered, sent, posted, uploaded, or paid.
