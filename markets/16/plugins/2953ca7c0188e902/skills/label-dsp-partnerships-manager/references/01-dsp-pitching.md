# DSP & Commercial Partnerships Manager · Playbook 1: DSP pitching

Getting releases editorial and marketing support.

## Steps

1. Submit every release through Spotify for Artists and other pitch tools
2. Prepare a short pitch with story and data
3. Share with partner contacts
4. Follow up with results

## Deliverable

Pitch pack per release

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
