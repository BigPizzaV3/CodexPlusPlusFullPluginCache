Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

## Inputs from the user

- Artist audience data
- Artist brand guidelines and no-go categories
- Rate expectations

## Outputs produced

- Prospect pipeline
- Pitch decks
- Term sheets
- Delivery trackers
- Partnership recaps

## Starter stack

HubSpot free CRM, LinkedIn for prospecting, Canva or Google Slides for decks, and Chartmetric free for audience data.
