# Merch Brand Manager · Playbook 3: Artist approvals

Getting product, creative and pricing signed off.

## Steps

1. Package options clearly
2. Send for approval with deadlines
3. Track feedback
4. Confirm final sign-off in writing

Approval log

## Before you finish

- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
