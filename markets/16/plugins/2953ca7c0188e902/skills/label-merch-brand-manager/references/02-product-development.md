# Merch Brand Manager · Playbook 2: Product development

Designing products fans want.

## Steps

1. Brief concepts and mood boards
2. Pick blanks, print methods and price points
3. Sample and approve
4. Finalize the line

## Deliverable

Product line sheet

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
