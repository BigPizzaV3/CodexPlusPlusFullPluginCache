# Recovery estimates

Every figure is an ESTIMATE built from numbers the artist supplies, with the formula shown. Never present an estimate as money found or owed.

- **MLC mechanicals:** US interactive streams x the current statutory mechanical rate range. Look up the published rate for the years in question and cite the source and date. Streams from before the MLC's launch may sit in its historical unmatched pool.
- **SoundExchange:** non-interactive spins x the published statutory rates for the service type. If the artist has no non-interactive plays, the estimate is zero, and that is a fine answer.
- **PRO performance:** too variable for a per-song formula because PROs pay from pooled revenue on their own schedules. Estimate only at catalog level from prior PRO statements the artist shares.

Rank each gap by (estimated dollars x confidence) / effort. Direct registrations on official portals usually outrank disputes. Registries pay on their own cycles, so set expectations for timing.
