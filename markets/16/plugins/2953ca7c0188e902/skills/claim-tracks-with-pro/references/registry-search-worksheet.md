# Registry search worksheet

The artist runs each search by hand on the official sites. Registries rate-limit and may prohibit automated queries, so never script them.

| Registry | What it shows | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Songview | Combined ASCAP and BMI composition lookup: writers, publishers, shares, ISWC | songview.com |
| ASCAP Repertory | ASCAP works in detail | ascap.com/repertory |
| BMI Repertoire | BMI works in detail | repertoire.bmi.com |
| The MLC | Works and recordings matched for US streaming mechanicals | portal.themlc.com/search |
| SoundExchange | Recordings by ISRC for digital performance | isrc.soundexchange.com |
| PPL / PRS (UK) | UK recordings and compositions | ppluk.com, prsformusic.com |
| AFM and SAG-AFTRA Fund | Non-featured performer royalties | afmsagaftrafund.org |

Comparison table (one per song):

| Source | Writers | Shares | IPI/CAE per writer | ISWC | ISRC | Query used | Date checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Flag a lead when: a writer appears in one registry but not another; shares differ or do not total 100%; the same recording shows different ISRCs; names are spelled differently across sources (record both spellings). Public search can lag registrations by weeks, so note the date and recheck before filing a dispute.
