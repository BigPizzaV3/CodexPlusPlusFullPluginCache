## Inputs from the user

- Catalog and ownership info
- Release dates
- Artist names and trademarks

## Outputs produced

- Infringement log
- Takedown tracker
- Claims policy
- Monthly protection report

## Starter stack

Google Alerts, manual platform searches, your distributor's Content ID service, and a Google Sheet tracker.
