# Street Team & College Marketing Rep · Playbook 6: Fan feedback reporting

Telling the label what fans think.

## Steps

1. Collect quotes and reactions
2. Note what resonates
3. Summarize monthly
4. Recommend local opportunities

## Deliverable

Monthly feedback report

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
