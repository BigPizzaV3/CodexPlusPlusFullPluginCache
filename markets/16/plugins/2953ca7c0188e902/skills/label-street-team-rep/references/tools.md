Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

## Inputs from the user

- Monthly artist priorities
- Supplied assets
- Promo budget (posters, merch, tickets)

## Outputs produced

- Local contact list
- Activity logs
- Monthly feedback report

## Starter stack

A Google Sheet log, Canva for posters, and a Discord or group chat for volunteers.
