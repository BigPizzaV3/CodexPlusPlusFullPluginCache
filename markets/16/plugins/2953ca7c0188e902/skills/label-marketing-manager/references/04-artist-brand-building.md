# Marketing Manager (Product / Campaign Manager) · Playbook 4: Artist brand building

Creating and evolving the artist brand with artist and management.

## Steps

1. Agree brand pillars and visual rules
2. Brief creative and content teams
3. Review every asset against the brand
4. Refresh as the artist evolves

## Deliverable

Artist brand guide (one page)

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
