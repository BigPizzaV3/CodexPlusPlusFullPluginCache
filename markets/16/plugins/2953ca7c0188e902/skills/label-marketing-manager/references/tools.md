# Marketing Manager (Product / Campaign Manager) · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Airtable | Campaign timelines and asset tracking | https://www.airtable.com |
| Spotify for Artists | Release performance | https://artists.spotify.com |
| Chartmetric | Campaign tracking | https://chartmetric.com |
| Google Slides / Canva decks | One-sheets and decks | https://slides.google.com |
| Canva | Quick marketing assets | https://www.canva.com |
| Feature.fm (smart links & pre-saves) | Pre-saves and smart links | https://www.feature.fm |

## Inputs from the user

- Music, artwork and release date
- Campaign budget
- Artist and manager goals
- Department contacts

## Outputs produced

- Release marketing plan
- Campaign timeline
- Weekly reports
- Asset kits
- Post-campaign recap

## Starter stack

Google Sheets or a free Airtable base for the timeline, Canva for one-sheets, Feature.fm free for pre-saves, and Spotify for Artists for results.
