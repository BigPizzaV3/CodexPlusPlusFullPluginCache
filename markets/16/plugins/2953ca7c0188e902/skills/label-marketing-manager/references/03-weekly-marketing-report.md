# Marketing Manager (Product / Campaign Manager) · Playbook 3: Weekly marketing report

Keeping everyone briefed on progress.

## Steps

1. Collect results from each channel
2. Compare to goals
3. Highlight wins, misses and next steps
4. Send to artist team and label

## Deliverable

Weekly marketing report

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
