# Social Content Creator · Playbook 2: Content ideation

Pitching ideas from briefs and trends.

## Steps

1. Read the brief and the artist brand
2. Study what's trending
3. Pitch 5–10 concepts with references
4. Refine with feedback

## Deliverable

Concept list

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
