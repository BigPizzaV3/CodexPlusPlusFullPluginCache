# Social Content Creator · Playbook 3: Reactive content

Jumping on trends, news and cultural moments.

## Steps

1. Monitor trends daily
2. Match to roster songs
3. Produce within hours
4. Post and track

## Deliverable

Reactive post

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
