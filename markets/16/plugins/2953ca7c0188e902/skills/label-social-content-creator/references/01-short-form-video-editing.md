# Social Content Creator · Playbook 1: Short-form video editing

Editing TikToks, Reels and Shorts.

## Steps

1. Pick the strongest song moment
2. Cut with a hook in second one
3. Add captions and safe-zone text
4. Export in correct specs

## Deliverable

Ready-to-post videos

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
