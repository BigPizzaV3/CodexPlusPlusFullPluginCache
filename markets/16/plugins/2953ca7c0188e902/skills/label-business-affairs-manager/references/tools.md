# Business Affairs & Contracts Manager · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| DocuSign / Adobe Sign | E-signatures | https://www.docusign.com |
| Airtable | Rights, obligations and clearance tracking | https://www.airtable.com |
| Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs) | Drafting and templates | https://workspace.google.com |
| Dropbox / Google Drive asset library | Contract archive | https://www.dropbox.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Deal terms
- Our templates
- Other side's drafts
- Release clearance needs

## Outputs produced

- Draft agreements
- Redline summaries
- Clearance tracker
- Obligations calendar
- Compliance checklist

## Starter stack

A folder of reviewed templates, a standard e-signature tool, and an Airtable or Google Sheet obligations tracker. The `contract-intake` skill can draft starting points.
