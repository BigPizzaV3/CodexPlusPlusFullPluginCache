# Business Affairs & Contracts Manager · Playbook 1: Contract drafting from templates

Standard agreements done fast and right.

## Steps

1. Pick the right template
2. Fill in deal terms
3. Highlight non-standard terms
4. Send for review

## Deliverable

Draft agreement

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
