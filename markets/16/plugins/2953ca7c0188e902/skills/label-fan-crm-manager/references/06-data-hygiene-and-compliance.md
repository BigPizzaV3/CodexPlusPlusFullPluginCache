# Fan CRM & Audience Development Manager · Playbook 6: Data hygiene and compliance

Keeping the list clean and legal.

## Steps

1. Remove bounces and inactive contacts
2. Honor unsubscribes and deletion requests
3. Document consent sources
4. Review with Business Affairs

## Deliverable

Clean list and compliance log

- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
