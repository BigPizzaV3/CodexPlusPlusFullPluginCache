- Existing fan lists and consent sources
- Release and tour calendar
- Store data
- Brand voice

## Outputs produced

- Lifecycle flows
- Campaign sends
- Segment maps
- Monthly CRM report

## Starter stack

Laylo or Mailchimp free, Feature.fm free pre-saves with email capture, and a Google Sheet to track list growth.
