# A&R Scout & Analyst · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Chartmetric | Discovery and growth tracking | https://chartmetric.com |
| Songstats | Quick cross-platform stats | https://songstats.com |
| TikTok Creative Center (trending sounds & ads) | Trending sounds | https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter |
| Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X, Snapchat (native apps) | Scene and artist research | — |
| Airtable | Lead tracker | https://www.airtable.com |
| Google Sheets | Data comparisons | https://sheets.google.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Label genre focus
- Demo inbox access
- Watch list from the A&R Manager

## Outputs produced

- Weekly discovery report
- Artist research profiles
- Demo shortlist
- Monthly trend brief

## Starter stack

Chartmetric and Songstats starter tiers, native social apps, and a Google Sheet as the lead tracker.
