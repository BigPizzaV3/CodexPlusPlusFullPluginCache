# A&R Scout & Analyst · Playbook 2: Music and social data analysis

Reading streaming and social data for real momentum.

## Steps

1. Pull streams, listeners, saves and follower growth
2. Compare growth rate against peers
3. Check engagement quality and audience geography
4. Write a plain-English takeaway

## Deliverable

Data snapshot with takeaway

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
