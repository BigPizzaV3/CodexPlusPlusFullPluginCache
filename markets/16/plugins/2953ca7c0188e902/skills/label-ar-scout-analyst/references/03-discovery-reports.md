# A&R Scout & Analyst · Playbook 3: Discovery reports

Turning research into a weekly ranked list.

## Steps

1. Collect the week's top signals
2. Rank by fit and momentum
3. Summarize each in three lines
4. Recommend next steps

## Deliverable

Weekly discovery report

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
