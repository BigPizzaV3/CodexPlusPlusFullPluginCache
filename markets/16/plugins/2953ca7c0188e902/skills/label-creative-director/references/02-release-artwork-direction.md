# Creative Director · Playbook 2: Release artwork direction

Cover art and campaign visuals.

## Steps

1. Brief the designer or photographer
2. Review concepts
3. Approve finals
4. Export to DSP and print specs

## Deliverable

Approved artwork in all formats

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
