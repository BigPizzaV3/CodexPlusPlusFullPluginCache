# Creative Director · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Adobe Photoshop / Illustrator | Artwork and retouching | https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop.html |
| Figma | Storefront and layout design | https://www.figma.com |
| Adobe After Effects | Motion | https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html |
| Blender (3D) | 3D | https://www.blender.org |
| Canva | Templates for the team | https://www.canva.com |
| Frame.io (video review) | Video review | https://frame.io |
| Dropbox / Google Drive asset library | Final asset library | https://www.dropbox.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Music and artist story
- Campaign plan
- Creative budget

## Outputs produced

- Brand guidelines
- Artwork
- Treatments
- Motion assets
- Page designs

## Starter stack

Canva and Figma starter tiers, Blender for 3D, CapCut for motion, and Pinterest or Are.na for mood boards.
