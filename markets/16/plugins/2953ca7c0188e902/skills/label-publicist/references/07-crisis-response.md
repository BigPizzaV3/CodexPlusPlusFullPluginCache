# Publicist · Playbook 7: Crisis response

Handling negative stories or mistakes.

## Steps

1. Gather the facts
2. Draft a holding statement
3. Get legal and owner sign-off
4. Monitor and update

## Deliverable

Crisis statement and monitoring log

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
