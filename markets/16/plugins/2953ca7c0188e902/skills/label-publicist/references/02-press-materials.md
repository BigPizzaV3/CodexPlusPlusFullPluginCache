# Publicist · Playbook 2: Press materials

Bios, press releases, quote sheets and clips.

## Steps

1. Interview the artist for story details
2. Write short, medium and long bios
3. Draft the press release
4. Assemble the press kit

## Deliverable

Press kit

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
