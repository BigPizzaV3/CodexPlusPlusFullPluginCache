# Sync Licensing Manager · Playbook 1: Sync catalog management

Keeping the catalog ready to license.

## Steps

1. Upload tracks with stems, instrumentals and metadata
2. Tag by mood, genre and tempo
3. Confirm rights info
4. Update searches and playlists

## Deliverable

Sync-ready catalog

## Before you finish

- Use only facts the user supplied; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.
- Label estimates and show the formula.
- End with one next action, an owner, and a date.
- Hand off per the Hand-offs list in `../SKILL.md` when the work belongs to another role.
