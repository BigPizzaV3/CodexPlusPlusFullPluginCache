# Physical Supply Chain & Fulfillment Manager · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Vinyl/CD pressing plant or broker portal | Quotes and test pressings | — |
| ShipStation | Shipping labels and tracking | https://www.shipstation.com |
| Shopify | Order data | https://www.shopify.com |
| Google Sheets | Inventory and reorders | https://sheets.google.com |
| QuickBooks Online | Inventory valuation | https://quickbooks.intuit.com |
| AtVenu (tour merch inventory & settlements) | Tour stock | https://www.atvenu.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Release dates and quantities
- Sales data
- Warehouse or packing space details

## Outputs produced

- Manufacturing timelines
- Inventory reports
- Reorder recommendations
- Fulfillment SOPs

## Starter stack

A Google Sheet inventory, your store's built-in shipping labels (or ShipStation's starter plan), and a local or online pressing broker.
