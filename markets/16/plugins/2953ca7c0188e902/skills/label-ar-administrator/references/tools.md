# A&R Administrator · Tools

Software commonly used by this role in industry job postings, along with standard starter options for independent labels.

| Tool | Typical use in this role | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Airtable | Budgets, POs and deliverables | https://www.airtable.com |
| Google Sheets | Budget calculations and reconciliations | https://sheets.google.com |
| QuickBooks Online | Vendors and payments | https://quickbooks.intuit.com |
| DocuSign / Adobe Sign | Contract signatures | https://www.docusign.com |
| Dropbox / Google Drive asset library | Document and asset storage | https://www.dropbox.com |

## Inputs from the user

- Signed deal terms and project budget
- Producer and vendor details
- Invoices
- Session schedule

## Outputs produced

- Budget trackers
- PO and invoice logs
- Vendor files
- Executed contract archive
- Delivery packages

## Starter stack

Google Sheets for budgets and PO logs, a free DocuSign or Dropbox Sign plan, and Google Drive folders per project.
