# Structure, Performance Max and new campaigns

## Separation

- Brand, non-brand and Performance Max are separate campaigns with separate budgets and bid strategies, and are reported separately.
- Competitor campaigns, if any, are separate from generic non-brand.
- Multi-product accounts separate by product when products differ in price, margin or CPA target.

## Performance Max

- Turn on brand exclusions and run brand in a separate Search campaign. Without exclusions, PMax takes brand searches and reports them as its own results. Brand exclusions cover Search, Shopping and YouTube search inventory.
- One theme per asset group: a product line, audience or value proposition. Never mix brand and non-brand, or prospecting and retargeting, in one asset group.
- Read the channel report: how much of PMax is Shopping and brand search versus genuine prospecting.
- Mine the PMax search terms report and add campaign-level negatives, as for Search.
- PMax needs weeks to settle. Avoid structural changes in the first 4 to 6 weeks.
- Search themes and audience signals guide PMax; they do not restrict it.

## Demand Gen and video

- Demand Gen replaced Video action campaigns. Provide both video and image assets.
- Choose channels deliberately (YouTube, Discover, Gmail, Display) instead of leaving all on by default when the goal is narrow.
- Last-click conversions understate upper-funnel campaigns. Judge them by a lift study or a geo test, not by last-click CPA alone.

## AI Max for Search

- AI Max adds query expansion beyond keywords, text customization and URL expansion to Search campaigns.
- Test it side by side on campaigns that are strong but stalled, with 30 or more conversions a month and negatives in place. Judge by CPA or ROAS over 4 or more weeks.
- Avoid it on small budgets, in regulated verticals, or without someone reviewing search terms weekly.
- When it expands to URLs that should not receive traffic, exclude them with URL exclusions at campaign level.

## Creating a new campaign

Copy the full configuration of a working reference campaign of the same type and product. Then change only what the new campaign is meant to change. Forgetting one setting causes silent drift.

Settings to copy and verify:

1. Bid strategy, target and portfolio link.
2. Budget.
3. Networks: Google Search, search partners, Display expansion.
4. Locations, positive and negative.
5. Location options: Presence, or Presence or interest, for both targeting and exclusion.
6. Languages.
7. Ad schedule.
8. Device settings.
9. Conversion goals at campaign level.
10. Audiences (observation or targeting), and audience signals in PMax.
11. Brand exclusions in PMax.
12. Tracking template, custom parameters and final URL suffix at every level.
13. Final URLs: use the landing pages that already work for that theme.
14. Linked negative keyword lists and placement exclusions.
15. Content exclusions and brand safety settings.

Create it paused, compare it setting by setting with the reference, then enable.

A new campaign does not bring new demand. Moving keywords into a new campaign resets their history; adding them to an existing well-performing campaign usually costs less.

## Naming

Follow the account's existing naming pattern exactly: order of tokens, separator, language, case. If the pattern is inconsistent, ask before choosing.
