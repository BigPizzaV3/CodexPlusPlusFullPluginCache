# Common mistakes and what to say instead

Each line: the claim people make, why it is wrong or incomplete, and the correct move. Use these to correct the user directly, with the reason.

| Claim | Problem | Correct move |
|---|---|---|
| "Ad Strength is Poor, rewrite the ad" | Google states Ad Strength is not used for Ad Rank, Quality Score or auction wins | Judge ads on conversions and cost; use Ad Strength only as a hint about asset variety |
| "Raise mobile bids 20%" on a Smart Bidding campaign | Smart Bidding does not use manual bid adjustments (device -100% and tCPA device target adjustments aside) | Change the target, the budget, or the conversion data |
| "Brand is our most efficient campaign, scale it" | Brand captures existing demand; more budget does not create searches | Separate brand from the rest; measure brand's defensive value with a test |
| "CPA went up, the landing page must be broken" | Most CPA rises are CPC rises with conversion rate flat | Split CPA into CPC and CVR before naming a cause |
| "Conversions dropped, we lost impression share" | Often eligible impressions (demand) fell and share held | Compare eligible impressions with impression share |
| "Add budget, we are losing impression share" | Share lost to rank is not recovered by budget | Check lost IS (budget) versus lost IS (rank) |
| "The last 3 days are terrible" | Conversion lag: recent days have not matured | Compare at the same age, or use traffic metrics for fresh periods |
| "Move these keywords into a new campaign to give them room" | A new campaign brings no new demand and resets history | Add them to the existing campaign or ad group that already performs |
| "This keyword had a bad week, pause it" | One week of a low-volume keyword is noise | Decide on 90 to 180 days, with conversion volume stated |
| "Exact negatives on every bad query" | Hundreds of exact negatives miss the pattern | Use n-grams and phrase negatives, checked against converting terms |
| "This list name says it covers the campaign" | A shared list only blocks campaigns it is linked to | Check the campaign's linked lists |
| "PMax is our best campaign" | Without brand exclusions PMax takes brand searches | Turn on brand exclusions and read the channel report |
| "Set tROAS to last quarter's ROAS" | Copying realized ROAS as target chokes volume | Set the target from margin and LTV |
| "Let's test 5 things at once this week" | Overlapping changes cannot be read | One change, one intent; experiments with a control for big bets |
| "Our attribution says Meta drove 40% of sales" | Attribution assigns credit, it does not prove cause | Run a lift study or geo test before moving budget on it |
| "Use first-click attribution to value upper funnel" | Rules-based models are gone from Google Ads since 2023 | Data-driven or last click, and an incrementality test for the real answer |
| "Add misspellings as negatives" | Negative keywords block misspellings since June 2024 | Add plurals and synonyms instead, which negatives do not match |
| "Pin headline 1 to control the message" | Pinning cuts the combinations Google can test | Write every asset to work in any position; pin only legal text |
| "AI Max will fix our stalled campaign" | Results vary widely; it needs volume and negatives in place | Test side by side for 4+ weeks on a campaign with 30+ conversions a month |
| "Turn on all recommendations, Optimization Score goes up" | Optimization Score is built from Google's recommendations, not from your results | Review each recommendation against the account's goal; turn off auto-apply for structural ones |
