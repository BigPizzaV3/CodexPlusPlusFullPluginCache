# Quick account audit checklist

Ordered by money at stake. For each item, ask for the screenshot or export that answers it, record what was found, and give one action.

## 1. Measurement (everything else depends on it)

- Which conversion actions are primary, and do they match the business goal? Micro-conversions as primary pull bidding toward cheap actions.
- Conversion goals set per campaign where actions differ by campaign.
- Values: present, not constant, updated after the fact if the sale happens later.
- Enhanced conversions on; offline conversions imported for lead generation.
- Consent signals for EEA traffic.
- Recent tracking breaks: days with clicks and near-zero conversions.

## 2. Bidding

- Bid strategy per campaign and whether it is a portfolio (and who owns it).
- Targets set from unit economics, not copied from past results.
- Recent target or strategy changes, and whether the campaign is still calibrating.
- Manual bid adjustments left on Smart Bidding campaigns (they do nothing except device -100% and tCPA device target adjustments).

## 3. Structure and settings

- Brand, non-brand, PMax and competitor campaigns separated.
- PMax brand exclusions on, brand Search campaign present.
- Location options: Presence where the business only serves the targeted area.
- Search partners and Display expansion on Search campaigns: on by choice, not by default.
- Auto-applied recommendations: which types are on.
- Campaigns limited by budget while losing share to budget, and campaigns with spare budget.

## 4. Queries

- Share of spend on search terms with zero conversions in the last 30 days.
- N-gram review of non-brand search terms (Search and PMax).
- Negative lists: what they contain and which campaigns they are linked to.
- Negatives that block converting queries.

## 5. Ads and landing pages

- Each ad group has at least one responsive search ad with distinct headlines.
- Final URLs that work, load fast and match the ad group.
- Claims in ads exist on the landing page.
- Assets (sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, images) present at the right level and not pointing to paused or wrong pages.

## 6. Spend efficiency

- CPA or ROAS by campaign against target, separated by segment.
- Campaigns or ad groups with spend and no conversions over 90 days.
- Impression share lost to budget on campaigns below target CPA (room to grow) and lost to rank on campaigns above it.

## Output format

- Top 3 findings by money at stake, each with its number and one action.
- Then the rest as a checklist: pass, fail, unknown (what is needed to check).
- No finding without its evidence. "Unknown" is better than a guess.
