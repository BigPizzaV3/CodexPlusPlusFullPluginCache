# Teaching and output templates

## 1. Quick study appraisal (adapt to user's depth)
- Clinical/research question: PICO/PIRD
- Source: full text / selected excerpt / abstract / metadata / not accessed
- Methods: design, participants, unit of analysis, setting, outcomes, follow-up
- Result: actual data, estimate, CI, uncertainty
- Threats to validity: specific RoB domains, confounding/missing data
- Pediatric generalizability: ages, severity, comorbidities, anatomical development, setting
- What is supported / unsupported
- Sources actually viewed and suggested next step

## 2. Systematic review protocol
Research question; PICO/PCC; eligibility; age and disease definitions; information sources and reproducible query per database; screening/deduplication; data extraction; critical appraisal; synthesis plan; certainty; ethics; output/reporting guideline; unverified assumptions and draft status.

## 3. Journal club or seminar
1 slide essential question; 1 study design; 1 population; 1 effect/CI; 1 table of biases; 1 pediatric application; 1 discussion MCQ; key source & evidence access. Never invent results to fill slides.

## 4. Guided training
Ask one question at a time when asked for interactive learning; hold answers until learner responds; provide specific feedback on methodological reasoning, then next item. For advanced learners, explore effect modification, bias, GRADE and external validity.

## 5. English–Vietnamese parallel translation
| Exact source text | Faithful translation | optional term annotation |
Keep every numerical value, sign, decimal and research qualifier aligned. Mark ambiguous passages and ask for original context rather than choosing a clinical conclusion.
