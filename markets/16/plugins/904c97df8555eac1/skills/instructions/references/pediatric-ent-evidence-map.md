# Pediatric ENT evidence map (research education, not care protocol)

A review topic should specify the phenotype, developmental stage, setting, intervention/test and clinically meaningful outcome. Choose appropriate reporting and bias tools before interpreting results.

| Domain | Research questions / heterogeneity to assess | Typical outcomes / risks of conflation |
|---|---|---|
| Acute otitis media (AOM) | Diagnostic criteria, antibiotic versus observation trials, age/severity, unilateral vs bilateral, recurrent episodes | Pain course, adverse events, recurrence, tympanic findings; don't confuse AOM with OME |
| Otitis media with effusion (OME) / tympanostomy | Duration of effusion, hearing status, developmental risk, intervention eligibility and comparison | Hearing tests, quality of life, complications, speech-language outcomes; ears vs children clustering |
| Pediatric hearing loss | Hearing screening vs diagnosis; congenital vs acquired; accuracy of tests | Reference standard, audiological thresholds, age-specific assessment, timely interventions |
| Obstructive sleep apnea / adenotonsillectomy | PSG vs symptoms, severity, age, obesity/comorbidities | AHI, sleep symptoms, quality of life, perioperative events, persistent OSA |
| Pediatric chronic rhinosinusitis / allergic rhinitis | Diagnostic criteria, age, infectious vs allergic causes | Disease-specific symptom scores, recurrence, treatment harms |
| Airway anomalies / stridor | Congenital vs acquired, diagnostic methods and referral selection | Airway outcomes and urgent red flags; avoid remote individualized management |
| Infections and complications | Mastoiditis, peritonsillar/retropharyngeal infection, epiglottic disease, foreign body | Serious complications, hospitalization, age, local resistance; urgent signs require in-person evaluation |
| Clinical procedures | Surgical indication criteria, baseline disease, selection bias, duration of follow-up | Comparative outcomes and harms; confounding by indication |

**Example verified specialty source (checked 2026-10-28):**
- AAO-HNSF 2022 update, *Clinical Practice Guideline: Tympanostomy Tubes in Children (Update)*; DOI 10.1177/01945998211065662; https://aao-hnsfjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1177/01945998211065662 . Population: children aged 6 months to 12 years. Use as an example of evidence-based guidance for this specific topic and age scope; check newer or local guidance at use time.
- Avoid claiming that this 2022 guideline covers other ENT conditions.

Do not add clinical dosing schedules, operative steps or unverified institutional protocols.
