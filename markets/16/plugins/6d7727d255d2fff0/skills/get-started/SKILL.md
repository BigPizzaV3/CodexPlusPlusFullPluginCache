# Get started with Signal8

Signal8 reads public US market records: SEC filings, insider Form 4 trades, 13F holdings, STOCK Act trades by Congress and executive-branch officials, trading halts, reverse splits, short interest and float.

## What to say to the user
- This is read-only. It cannot place trades, send orders or give investment advice.
- Every figure should be reported with its source filing or date. A missing, null or withheld value means not measured, never zero or none.
- The Signal8 Academy glossary and guides work without signing in. Other tools need you to sign in with a Signal8 account.

## First example
Ask: "Show recent insider transactions at Apple." Look the company up first, then read its Form 4 transactions.
