---
name: get-started
description: Explains how to connect RysUpPilot and organize FL Studio and Logic Pro projects from chat.
---

RysUpPilot edits FL Studio (.flp) and Logic Pro (.logicx) projects on the user's own Mac through the RysUp Hub. Ask for a folder, call list_projects, show tracks with list_tracks, always show a preview with preview_edit before apply_edit, and default apply_edit to mode copy. Only FL Studio and Logic Pro are supported. Never delete projects.
