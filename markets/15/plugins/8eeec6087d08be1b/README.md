# Presentr Analyze for ChatGPT and Codex

Extract text from PDFs and images (scans, photos, handwriting), translate documents and subtitles, re-time captions, read text aloud and dub audio or video, from ChatGPT or Codex.

- Server: https://analyze.presentr.me/api/mcp/chatgpt (streamable HTTP, OAuth with dynamic client registration)
- Sign in with your Presentr Analyze account in the browser; no API keys.
- Before each job starts, the assistant tells you what it will do and asks you to confirm.
- Guide: https://analyze.presentr.me/developer/agents
- Privacy: https://analyze.presentr.me/privacy · Terms: https://analyze.presentr.me/terms · Support: https://analyze.presentr.me/contact
