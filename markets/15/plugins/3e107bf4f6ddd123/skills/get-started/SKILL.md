# Get started with BestSaaSWebDesigns

BestSaaSWebDesigns is a catalog of 400+ real SaaS marketing websites. Use it to find design references: real
pricing pages, heroes, footers, palettes and fonts from shipped products.

## Which tool to use

- **`list_filters`**: see the valid categories, page sections, styles, stacks and the most used fonts, with counts.
  Call it first when you're unsure which filter values exist.
- **`search_sites`**: find sites by any mix of `query`, `category`, `section`, `style`, `stack`, `font` and `color`
  (a hex value). Each result has the website link, palette, fonts, stack and captured sections. Up to 20 per call.
- **`get_site`**: everything about one site by its slug (from `search_sites`), plus links to similar sites.
- **`list_site_links`**: just names and homepage links for the same filters, up to 50. Use it when you want to open the
  live sites and look at them yourself.

## Tips

- Combine filters: `style: "dark"` with `section: "pricing"` gives dark sites that have a pricing page.
- `color` matches palettes near that hex colour, nearest first.
- Results are links and design data, not images. To see a design, open the website link.
- The catalog is read-only and only covers its own sites; for anything else, say so instead of guessing.

## Good first prompts

- "Find 5 dark SaaS pricing pages and compare their colour palettes."
- "Which SaaS sites use Inter as their display font?"
- "Give me 10 homepage links of AI product websites."
