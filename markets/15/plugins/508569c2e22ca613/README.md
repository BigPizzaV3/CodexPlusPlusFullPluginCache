## Directory description
Access public market data with VT Markets. Discover supported trading instruments and check available price quotes across forex, gold, indices, commodities, stocks and cryptocurrencies. Use ChatGPT to look up market symbols and retrieve market information supported by the service.

No account login is required. This plugin provides read-only market data and does not access personal trading accounts, execute trades, manage positions or provide personalized investment advice.

Market data availability and update frequency depend on the connected service. Prices may be delayed and should be independently verified before making financial decisions.
