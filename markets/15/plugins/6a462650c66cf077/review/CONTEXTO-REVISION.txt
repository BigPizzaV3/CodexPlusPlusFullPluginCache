The dictionary includes anatomical terms, sexual vocabulary, slang and derogatory expressions as linguistic records. Their presence should be assessed in the context of factual definitions and language education.

Version 1.2.51 adds instructions to keep educational explanations neutral, provide synonyms only on explicit request, preserve documented usage labels, and avoid gratuitous sexual examples. It distinguishes explaining an offensive term from helping use it to harass or sexually humiliate someone.
