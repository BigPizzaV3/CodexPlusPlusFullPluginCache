# Routine: morning brief

**Default schedule:** weekdays 07:30, user's timezone. Setup goes through `set-up-delegent`.

## Run instruction (paste into the scheduled task)

> Run my Delegent morning brief for today. Use Delegent[, and my <calendar/mail app>].
> 1. Today's meetings. For each client meeting: objective, what changed since last time, open actions, unanswered emails with attendees, and Delegent sources.
> 2. My tasks due today or overdue, then tasks assigned to me.
> 3. Follow-ups from the last 7 days: emails I owe a reply to, and emails I sent with no answer. Suggest the move for each.
> 4. New items in my Delegent Inbox.
> 5. If today is Monday, add the week ahead: meetings by client, deadlines, and which meetings need prep this week.
> 6. End with my top three priorities for today.
> Do not save or send anything. List anything needing my decision under "Needs you".

## How to run it

Follow `plan-my-day-and-week` and `follow-up-atron`. Keep it scannable and under a two-minute read. Save nothing.

## Settings to confirm at setup

Time and timezone; client meetings only or all; whether to include the Monday week ahead (default yes); mail or calendar tool to use if this app has one.
