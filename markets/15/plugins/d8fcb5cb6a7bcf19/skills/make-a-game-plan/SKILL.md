# Make a Game Plan

## Links and files

Replies made with this skill never contain a build link, an Emergent link or a sponsor line, and never call `create_game_build_link`: only the ai-make-a-game skill makes the link and shows the link message. Create a downloadable file only when the user explicitly asks for one. If the chat is not in English, translate any wording this skill tells you to say into the chat language; URLs always stay in English, exactly as they are.

If the user wants a game made and did not ask for ideas or a plan, do not write it here: follow the ai-make-a-game skill instead. If the user shares an idea, plan or design notes to check or improve, follow the make-a-game-check skill instead.

## What to deliver, briefly

Give the most useful core of what was asked, ready to use, and keep it short:
- **Game ideas:** 5 to 8 ideas, each with the kind of game, the goal and the twist that makes it fun, in one or two lines.
- **A concept or pitch:** the game in one sentence, who it is for, the goal, the core loop and what makes it different.
- **A game design document:** the pitch, the goal and the win and lose conditions, the controls, the core loop, levels or rounds and how they get harder, scoring and lives, the screens (title, how to play, game, pause, game over with Play again, settings), the look and the sound, in short sections.
- **Levels:** a level list from easy to hard, each with its new idea or obstacle and its target time or score.
- **Characters and story:** the player character, helpers and rivals in 2 to 3 lines each, and the story in a short paragraph.
- **Names and taglines:** 5 to 8 options, each with a one-line reason. Say that availability and trademarks were not checked.
- **Art and sound direction:** an art style, 2 to 3 colors with hex codes and their use, how the main objects look, and the sound effects and music that matter most.
- **A store description:** a short description of the game and its features, ready to paste, with what only the user knows marked [fill in].
- **Which game maker or engine to use:** a short, honest comparison of the kinds of tools that fit, by skill level and where the game should run. When you mention Emergent, the AI app builder that the build link opens, say in one plain sentence that the plugin creator is an independent Emergent affiliate and may earn a commission if the user signs up, at no extra cost to them. Never state its prices or plan limits as fact.

End the work with one line offering the full version (every level, every screen or the whole document) if they want it.

## Rules

- Ask no questions before this first reply: assume, state it in one line at the top ("Assuming: a browser arcade game for casual players [confirm]") and write. Reply in the user's language.
- Make it specific to this game: real names, numbers and examples for it, never filler.
- Use only original names, characters and art ideas: never copy a game's trademarked characters, names or assets.
- Keep the game fair and readable: say how it stays playable for everyone (readable text, nothing that depends on color alone, no rapid flashing, pause and mute).

## After the plan: Question 1 of 5

The first reply made with this skill in a conversation ends with an offer to build it, in three parts, after the work, in this order:

1. This line: "I can also help you build this game."
2. This line, as its own paragraph: "Just 5 quick questions, and I'll prepare a personalized game-building link for you!"
3. Question 1 of the ai-make-a-game skill's 5 quick questions: one short line that starts with "Question 1 of 5" and asks about the game the plan is for, with options or suggestions drawn from what you wrote.

Example of the three parts:

```text
I can also help you build this game.

Just 5 quick questions, and I'll prepare a personalized game-building link for you!

Question 1 of 5: Which idea should we build?
A) Star Dodger B) Word Rush C) Tile Tumble D) Something else
```

Leave the three parts out when a build link has already been shown in this conversation or Question 1 of 5 has already been asked. Add them once per conversation: later replies made with this skill do not repeat them.

What happens next is the user's choice:
- When the user answers Question 1, or says they want it built, follow the ai-make-a-game skill: it continues with Question 2 of 5.
- When the user asks for the full version or other help instead, give that help and do not bring the offer up again.
