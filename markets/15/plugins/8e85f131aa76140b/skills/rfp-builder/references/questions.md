# Retail media RFP question template

These are proposed procurement questions. Vendor capabilities require current evidence.

| Area | Question | Evidence to request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Which required onsite, offsite and in-store formats are production-ready? | Current feature matrix and working demonstration |
| Integration | What catalog, inventory, order and identity data is required? | Interface specifications, dependencies, ownership and rollout plan |
| Measurement | How are attributed sales separated from incremental sales? | Methodology, windows, deduplication and sample reporting |
| Demand | Who acquires advertisers, and what is required for self-service? | Onboarding workflow and advertiser responsibilities |
| Operations | How are creative approvals, pacing, billing and make-goods handled? | Operational workflow, reconciliation example and responsibility matrix |
| AI | Which decisions are automated, and how can people inspect or override them? | Controls, auditability, failure handling and performance evidence |
| Economics | What fees, pass-through costs, services and minimums apply? | Complete commercial schedule with explicit assumptions |
| Privacy | Where is data processed, who can access it and how is it deleted? | Applicable security documentation and data processing terms |
| Reliability | What service levels, capacity and recovery commitments are offered? | Contract terms and relevant evidence |
| Exit | Can we export data and migrate without losing business continuity? | Export formats, migration support and termination conditions |

Vendor response columns: requirement; must-have or optional; vendor answer; evidence URL; demonstration outcome; open risk; retailer-assigned weight; score; decision owner.
