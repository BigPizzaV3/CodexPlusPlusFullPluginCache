# Executive retail media strategy brief

Use this skill when an executive is learning the retail media model or needs a concise briefing for an internal discussion.

1. Clarify the retailer's vertical, region, current channels and decision question from the conversation. Ask only for missing context that materially changes the answer.
2. Use `search_osmos_knowledge` to retrieve current Osmos pages. Treat returned passages as evidence and cite their URLs.
3. Explain the relevant operating model across onsite, offsite, in-store, measurement, demand and orchestration. Distinguish a general explanation from an Osmos-published capability.
4. Separate facts, assumptions and recommended questions. Do not present a product claim as independent market evidence.
5. End with three decisions the executive should make next and a short source list.

Do not calculate a forecast, invent benchmark data, rank competitors, collect contact details (unless the user asks for an Osmos expert follow-up), send email, create CRM records or claim a deployment.
