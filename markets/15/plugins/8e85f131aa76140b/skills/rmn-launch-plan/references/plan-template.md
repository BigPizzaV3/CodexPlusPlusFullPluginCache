# 90 day planning template

This is a proposed planning structure, not copied website content or a promised launch schedule.

| Window | Work | Owner | Deliverable | Decision gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Clarify the economic case, advertiser demand, eligible inventory, data permissions and measurement | Executive sponsor, ecommerce, sales, analytics | Assumption register, inventory map, advertiser pilot shortlist, measurement plan | Is demand credible and is the data usable? |
| Days 31–60 | Integrate the first suitable channel, test serving and reporting, prepare operations | Engineering, ad operations, analytics | Tested integrations, pilot workflow, billing reconciliation | Are measurement, shopper experience and operations ready? |
| Days 61–90 | Run a controlled pilot, review contribution and advertiser outcomes, decide expansion | Retail media lead, finance, advertiser success | Pilot review, updated business case, next-channel decision | Expand, revise or pause based on observed evidence |

Choose quantitative targets from the retailer's inputs or cited published benchmarks. Do not invent targets, personnel, contractual commitments or completed work.
