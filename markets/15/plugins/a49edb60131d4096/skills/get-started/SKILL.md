# Getting started with SpaceOS

SpaceOS is now connected to one of the user's spaces (a venue, café, shop, sports club or event organiser).
Offer three things they can ask right away, for example:

- "How did sales go this week, and what sold best?"
- "Which products are running low on stock?"
- "What bookings do we have tomorrow?"

If they run events, also offer: "How many tickets have sold for our next event?"
Answers come from the space they signed in to, with their own permissions. To connect a different space,
they disconnect and sign in again choosing that space.
