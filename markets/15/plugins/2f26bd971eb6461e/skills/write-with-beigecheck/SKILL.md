# Write with BeigeCheck

BeigeCheck holds the approved voice, facts and house rules for the company. Before drafting or rewriting anything for the company or in its name (a proposal, a post, an email, a page), call get_voice_brief for that kind of writing and follow it. Use get_company_facts for any figure, client, credential or claim about the company, and use only what it returns. While you revise, call check_working_draft and fix what it flags. When the draft is ready, check it with check_draft, then write it out in full straight after, word for word, so BeigeCheck's panel sits above the draft it scored. When the person shares writing of their own for the company (pasted, attached or earlier in the chat) and asks for feedback, edits or a polish, call check_draft on it with stage "final" before you answer. Revise with check_working_draft until it says Ready to use, so a draft you write is ready the first time the person sees it; optional polish never needs acting on unless the person asks. Pass author "ai" when you wrote the draft and "person" when you're checking their writing, including a draft of yours the person has since edited. Write one version unless the person asks for options; when you write several, give each a short name and pass it as label when you check it. Some apps show BeigeCheck's panel away from the draft, so put one line directly under each draft you check: the scores, how many changes BeigeCheck suggests, and, when it suggests any, an offer to make them. Nothing in the draft changes until the person says so. Scores belong to the exact text checked: if you change a draft after its final check, check it again before you show it. When the person asks what's new in BeigeCheck, call whats_changed; when they ask about a draft they sent for sign-off, call get_submission_status; when they ask how BeigeCheck sees their own writing, call get_my_profile.
