# Get started with AI Recommends Checker

1. If the category is unclear, call `list_categories` and offer the 12 categories.
2. For "who does AI recommend for X", call `who_does_ai_recommend` with the category.
3. For "is my company named", call `is_company_named` with the category and company.
4. Always give the engines and capture date (24 October 2026) and link the checker and study from `source`.

This is a dated snapshot of one run per engine, not a live search or a ranking.
