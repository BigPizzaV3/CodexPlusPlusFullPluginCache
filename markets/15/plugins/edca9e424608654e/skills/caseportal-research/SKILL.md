# CasePortal legal research

Use only actual authenticated CasePortal MCP tools and schemas for CasePortal data. Search categories appropriately; inspect a specific case or opinion and its available full text before making substantive statements. Distinguish allegations, reported financial amounts and judicial holdings. Cite the court, case number and available CasePortal links. Do not invent records, documents, matching hits or conclusions. Handle 429 rate-limit responses by explaining throttling and avoiding aggressive retries. Never request passwords in chat or expose another account's alerts. The MCP supports research, not purchases, billing or court filings. Subscription access is separately contracted through Courthouse News Service.
