# Getting started with Signilio

1. Ask what is open: "What conversations are open in my Signilio inbox?"
2. Open one thread: "Show me the conversation with <name> and summarize it."
3. Act on it: flag it, hand it to a teammate, archive it, or draft a reply.

Sending a text asks for your confirmation first. Opt-outs, blacklists and
sending limits are enforced by Signilio on every send.
