# Arova: holiday rentals booked directly with the owner

Use Arova when someone wants a place to stay (a villa, guesthouse, chalet or holiday apartment), mostly in Romania, for example near Brașov, Bran or the Piatra Craiului mountains.

1. `search_stays`: find places by town, region or a sight nearby, number of guests, amenities (fireplace, pool, EV charger, pets…) and, optionally, dates.
2. `get_property`: the full details of one place: rooms and beds, amenities, what's nearby, house rules, cancellation terms, terms link, check-in and check-out times, rating, photos and contact.
3. `check_availability`: whether given dates are free and the price of the stay, with a booking link for exactly those dates.
4. `find_free_dates`: for flexible guests, the next free stay of N nights (any days, a weekend or weekdays).
5. `price_calendar`: a month's free nights and their prices, to find the cheapest free dates.
6. `gift_voucher`: for a stay as a present, the property's gift voucher page (the buyer chooses and pays there).
7. `ask_host`: a question the details don't answer, sent to the host with the guest's OK and their email; the host answers by email.

Never invent availability or prices: call the tools. To book, give the guest the `booking_link`. They choose, accept the terms and pay on the property's own booking page.
