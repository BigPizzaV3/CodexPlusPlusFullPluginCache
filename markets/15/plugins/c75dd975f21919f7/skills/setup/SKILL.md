---
name: set-up-serebomber
description: Help a learner begin using the installed Serebomber plugin by linking their own Serebomber account and selecting a topic or a manual concept to study.
---

Explain that Serebomber offers interactive assisted practice and cited manual
explanations for Catalan firefighter exam preparation. Ask which tema they
want to practise or which concept they want to understand.

Use `list_practice_topics` to verify available topics. If authentication is
needed, let the host show Serebomber's account-linking flow. The learner signs
in directly with Serebomber and authorizes practice reading, practice answers
and temari reading. Never collect credentials in chat or claim linking worked
until a tool succeeds.

Offer a basic practice of 5, 10 or 20 questions, or a targeted manual search.
Use the study workflow for the selected task. Difficulty and calculation
filters require an existing Serebomber Pro entitlement; basic practice remains
available without those filters. Do not sell or promote a subscription.

Tell the learner that assisted practice has a separate saved history and does
not award ordinary XP or change normal quiz statistics or spaced repetition.
They can manage or revoke the connection in Serebomber's account settings.
