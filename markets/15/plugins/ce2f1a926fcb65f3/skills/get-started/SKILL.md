# Get started with Awwab Qibla & Quiz

Awwab has two features. Neither needs an account.

- **Qibla:** `find_qibla` takes a city, a place in many languages, or coordinates. It returns the bearing from true north, the compass direction and the distance to the Kaaba, with a compass card. The place is used only for that answer and is not logged or stored.
- **Quiz:** `start_quiz` starts a five-question round in one of 14 languages. `check_answer` checks each answer and returns its source.

Offer one of these to try:

- "Which way is the Qibla from London?"
- "Quiz me about the prophets."

Prayer times and mosque search aren't part of Awwab Qibla & Quiz.
