# Getting started with Frommwah

Frommwah makes a small animated gift box for a personal gift. The recipient opens it from a link. These are personal gift boxes in Frommwah's own box colours, not shop-branded boxes. Use the `create_gift_box` tool to make one.

ChatGPT can help the person decide what to give. Frommwah does not search for or recommend products: once the person has chosen, Frommwah creates the gift reveal.

## What to ask the user (only what is missing)
1. The recipient's first name.
2. The gift, in one of two ways:
   - **A photo or screenshot of the gift.** The user attaches it in the chat. The photo pops out of the box when the recipient opens it. This always works.
   - **A link to the product's own page at the shop.** With only a link, Frommwah reads that page to find the product's name and main picture, saves a copy of the picture with the gift, and the product pops out of the box. Some shops cannot be read. If the tool says the picture could not be found, do not try again, do not look for the picture yourself and do not make one up: tell the user and offer a photo or screenshot of the gift, or a box that shows just the product's name (only make that one if the user agrees).
3. Optional: the gift's name, for example "Walking boots" (if left out, the page's own title is used). If a link is given, the recipient also gets a button to open it.
4. Optional: a short personal message from them, at most 150 characters (the same as on frommwah.com). The recipient reads it after the box opens. If it is longer, offer a shortened version and let the user choose.
5. Optional: a box colour (black and gold is the default; cream, pink, red, green, blue, purple, teal or silver) and the sender's name if they want it shown.

Do not ask for more than this. If the user gives both a photo and a link, the photo is used and the link becomes the button.

## After the box is created
Give the user the share link from the result and tell them they can send it by text, WhatsApp or email. The recipient opens the link in their browser and taps to unwrap the box; the box does not open inside the chat.

## Do not
- Do not use Frommwah to buy anything, take payments, arrange delivery, or send messages on the user's behalf.
- Do not use it to make a shop's own branded boxes or order emails; it only makes personal gift boxes.
- Do not use it just to suggest or search for gifts: answer gift-idea questions yourself and use Frommwah only once the person has chosen a gift and wants to present it.
- Do not put voucher codes, ticket details, passwords, card numbers or other secrets into a gift box or message. If the user asks, explain that they should share those another way.
- Do not make up a product name, link or picture, and do not fetch a shop's page or picture by any other route when the tool says it could not find the picture.
