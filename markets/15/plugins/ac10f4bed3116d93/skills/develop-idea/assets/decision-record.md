# Concept brief

Coverage guide: combine related fields for a small task, omit empty sections, and avoid repeating context already stated. A self-contained brief can serve as its own continuation record.

**Revision:** Use a local version label only when supported by this conversation.

## Outcome and audience
State the original idea, the problem it addresses, intended users and the observable improvement sought.

## Constraints and basis
List hard constraints separately from preferences. Distinguish user-supplied facts, checked sources, assumptions and unknowns. Attach available source references to the claims they support.

## Review and alternatives
Summarize the useful contributions from the selected perspectives and the principal disagreement. Compare viable alternatives against the same constraints; include the trade-off that drives the recommendation.

## Decision record
| Choice | Status | Rationale and basis | Alternative or unresolved objection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use proposed, user-selected, deferred or rejected status. Add only substantive decisions; do not invent an approval.

## First useful scope
Describe what is included, what is excluded and what observable checks would show the concept is useful. Keep proposed success thresholds labelled as proposals.

## Unknowns and next actions
For each material unknown, explain what observation or decision would resolve it. Give a short sequence of next actions; owner and dates remain unassigned unless supplied or explicitly proposed.

## Changes and continuation
If revising, state the changed constraint or choice, displaced scope and resulting changes. End with a compact portable summary: objective; hard constraints; selected/proposed decisions; unresolved questions; next step. Include only context relevant to continuing this work.
