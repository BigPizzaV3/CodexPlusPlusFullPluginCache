# Proposal review

Coverage guide: combine related fields for a small task, omit empty sections, and avoid repeating context already stated. A self-contained brief can serve as its own continuation record.

## Decision and constraints
State what the user is deciding, the desired outcome and the constraints the review must preserve.

## What is worth preserving
Identify the proposal's strongest relevant feature or argument and its evidence basis.

## Findings
| Priority and reason | Claim or choice under review | Evidence status | Consequence | Test or bounded correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Evidence status distinguishes supplied evidence, unverified user claim, assumption and unknown. Priorities describe effects on the decision; they are not risk scores.

## Main trade-off
State the principal objection, the strongest defense and any remaining disagreement. Compare the proposed approach with a viable alternative using the same constraints.

## Recommendation and changes
Recommend proceeding, revising a named part or resolving a specific uncertainty. Distinguish recommendations from user decisions. On revision, identify what changed and which issues remain open.

## Next validation and continuation
Name the smallest useful test, the observation sought and what result would change the recommendation. Any threshold not supplied by the user is proposed. Close with constraints, unresolved findings, proposed changes and the next decision for reuse in another conversation.
