# Get started

Tools: `list_projects`, `create_project`, `open_editor`.

1. Call `list_projects`. If the person is asked to sign in to Simple House Planner, explain in one sentence that their plans are stored in that account, and that signing in the first time creates it.
2. Explain the plugin in three short sentences: you design and change plans in the chat, the editor in the chat shows each change as you make it, and the person can change anything by hand at any time.
3. If they have plans, name them and offer to open one with `open_editor`.
4. If they have none, ask what they want to plan: a house from a short description, one room to furnish, or a plan from a sketch or photo.
5. Follow the `design-house`, `furnish-room` or `plan-from-sketch` skill for what they choose.

Do not ask for an address or other personal facts. The town is enough to set the sun and the climate.
