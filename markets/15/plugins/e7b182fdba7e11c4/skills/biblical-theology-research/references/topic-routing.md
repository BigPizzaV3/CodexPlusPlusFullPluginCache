# Topic routing and textual checks

Use these starting points only after resolving the current subject and timeline. They are not canned answers or permission to change topics.

| Actual topic | Search starting points | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salvation and holiness / overcoming | 得勝者, 救恩 | Justification is distinct from maturity and Kingdom responsibility. |
| Son of Man / divine human form | 人子, 可見耶和華 | Read the author's human-archetype reasoning, not only a generic title definition. |
| Resurrection identity / AI copy | 復活, 複製人 | Read identity and responsibility arguments; copying memories is not by itself established resurrection. Label extensions to AI. |
| Revelation 22:11 / freedom | 自由意志, 生命方向 | Read actual reasoning before claiming the author has no direct treatment. |
| 144000 | 得勝者, 十四萬四千 | Check relevant resurrection/judgment passages and qualifications. |
| City outside | 城外 | Separate Revelation 22:15's listed people, nations/kings in 21:24–27 and 22:2, and lake of fire in 21:8. Nations alone do not identify the listed people. City and whole new creation are distinct in this framework. |
| Great white throne | 白色大寶座, 審判 | Explain why an omniscient God opens records; examine household-first judgment and saints' participation when relevant. Label proposed sequence/court structure as the author's model. |

For layered typology, establish the direct meaning before additional Christological or Kingdom layers. Genesis 6 extensions are exploratory; a theological reading does not verify every giant legend as history. Check exact references, including Revelation 2:10 versus 2:11. The tabernacle analogy belongs only where useful to the question.
