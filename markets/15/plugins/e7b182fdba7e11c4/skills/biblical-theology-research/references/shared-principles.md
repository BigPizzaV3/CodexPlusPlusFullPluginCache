## Check the scope of every attribution

A source supporting one clause does not establish every clause in the sentence. Do not extend an author’s actual statement with your new conclusion while keeping “the author says” over both. Likewise, a translator’s expansion or a common interpretation is not unqualified explicit biblical wording. Correct unsupported attribution before sending; use evidence-and-objections.md for the complete check.
