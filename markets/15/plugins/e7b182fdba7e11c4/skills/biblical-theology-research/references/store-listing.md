XIAO YANG · LIVE OUT GOD'S GLORY

## Chinese description

以整本《聖經》為最高經典依據、以耶穌基督為絕對中心的聖經神學研究助手。深入探索絕對基督論、得救與得勝、至聖至愛、舊約中的基督、人子與神顯、聖靈、《啟示錄》、被提、頭一次復活、千禧年、新天新地，以及從《創世記》到《啟示錄》的整體聖經脈絡。不是只問傳統怎樣解釋，而是回到整本聖經重新查考。

## English description

A Christ-centered biblical theology research assistant that reads Scripture as one connected story from Genesis to Revelation. Explore Absolute Christology, salvation and overcoming, holiness and love, Christ in the Old Testament, the Son of Man, the Holy Spirit, Revelation, resurrection, the Millennium, the New Creation, and the wider biblical storyline. Traditional interpretations may be compared, but Scripture and the person of Jesus Christ remain central.

## Six preferred starter questions

1. 從《創世記》到《啟示錄》，整本聖經其實在講一條怎樣的主線？
2. 舊約哪些地方可以看見耶穌的顯現、預表或彌賽亞線索？
3. 得救和得勝有什麼不同？因信稱義之後為什麼還要談得勝？
4. 被提、頭一次復活、千禧年和耶穌再來之間是什麼關係？
5. 為什麼耶穌基督是理解整本聖經最重要的鑰匙？
6. 如果我是猶太讀者，應怎樣從舊約開始理解耶穌和新約？

Note: the Agent Plugins 1.0 manifest currently allows up to three plugin-level default prompts, so the manifest uses three representative prompts. Preserve all six here for any UI surface that supports a larger starter-question set.
