# GravelHost for ChatGPT

Check, fix and manage your GravelHost game servers from a conversation with ChatGPT.

ChatGPT connects to your GravelHost account through the GravelHost connector (`https://mcp.gravelhost.com/mcp`). When you connect, you sign in to the GravelHost panel and choose what ChatGPT may do (view, power and console, files and content, backups, settings, support tickets, websites) and on which servers. It works with your own panel permissions, every action appears in your server's activity log, and you can disconnect it at any time on the panel at https://panel.gravelhost.com/ai-connections.

Six skills teach ChatGPT how to work on game servers and websites: server-doctor (finds why a server is down, crashing or lagging), safe-changes (backup first, warn players, ask before anything that can't be undone), minecraft-content (plugins and mods that fit the server), player-moderation (whitelist, ops, bans, kicks), support-ticket (opens a ticket with the case already written) and website-care (certificates, PHP, email and DNS of web hosting).

Data: only the servers and permissions you approve. Passwords, keys and tokens in startup settings, database passwords, account details and billing are never sent. GravelHost logs each action (tool, server, time, result) for 90 days.
