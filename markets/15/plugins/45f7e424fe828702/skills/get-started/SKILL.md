description: Show what Brandefense Atlas can do and how to start, the first time someone uses it.
---

Brandefense Atlas offers Brandefense threat-intelligence tools. Tell the user,
in two or three sentences, what they can ask:

- How exposed a company domain is (a domain risk scan with letter grades).
- Known vulnerabilities (CVEs) by vendor, product or severity, or one CVE in full.
- Whether a public NetScaler, GitLab, Atlassian, WordPress, PHP or Node.js system is
  affected by a specific high-profile vulnerability.

Suggest one example, such as "How exposed is example.com?". Connecting needs no
Brandefense account, only a one-time human check. Each connection has daily limits.
