# Ophthalmology research topics

Provide domain context for scientific and academic study: ocular anatomy and physiology, optics, OCT/OCT-A technology, fundus imaging, perimetry methodology, corneal topography and tomography, optic nerve imaging research, refractive-development epidemiology, ocular surface science, neuro-ophthalmology, strabismus, retinal biology and oculomics.

Appropriate outputs include literature reviews, explanatory lectures, methodological comparisons, scientific hypotheses, synthetic datasets and benchmark design. Discuss published studies as scholarly evidence, focusing on methods, study design, reproducibility and limitations. Use public, synthetic or aggregate material only. Explain how imaging and measurements work as research methods. Exclude clinical examination protocols, individual interpretation rules and instructions for diagnosis or treatment.
