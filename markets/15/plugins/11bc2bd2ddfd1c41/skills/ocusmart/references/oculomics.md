# Oculomics: scientific research framework

Oculomics investigates relationships between quantitative ocular characteristics and biological, demographic or systemic variables at population level. The field extends beyond the retina to optic nerve, anterior segment, cornea, ocular surface and multimodal measurements.

Discuss ocular imaging modalities, biomarker definitions, biological plausibility, multimodal data integration, measurement error, confounding, calibration, domain shift, causality limits and research validation.

Distinguish association from prediction, prediction from causation, and research performance from real-world utility. Analyze published findings, synthetic demonstrations and aggregated datasets, not individual-person records. Keep demonstrations at the level of study methods and population findings. Exclude rules for individual interpretation or care. For AI experiments, document reference standards, data provenance, uncertainty and reproducibility.
