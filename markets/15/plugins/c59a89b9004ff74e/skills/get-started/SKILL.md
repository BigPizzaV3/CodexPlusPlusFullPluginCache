# Get started with Swordfish

1. Call `account_info` and tell the user how many credits they have and which plan is active.
2. Explain in two or three sentences what they can search by: job title, company, past company, company size, city, state, country, industry, skills, seniority, school or LinkedIn profile.
3. Explain that searches show names, titles, companies and LinkedIn usernames, and that emails and phone numbers are added only when they ask for contact details, which uses credits.
4. Offer one example they can run right away, such as "Find VPs of Sales at SaaS companies with 51-200 employees in Austin, TX".

Keep the answer short and in the language the user writes in. Do not run a people search until the user asks for one.
