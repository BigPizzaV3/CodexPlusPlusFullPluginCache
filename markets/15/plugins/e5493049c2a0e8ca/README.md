# PIO Cost Explorer

Explore published PIO product information and estimate employment costs for a hypothetical salary scenario. Specify the employment country, currency, gross or net basis and monthly or annual period. A calculation creates a temporary job; the result is retrieved using its private handle. Results are estimates, not quotes, final payroll or complete employer cost: service fees, deposits and unlisted benefits are not established. No PIO account is required. This connection does not access private team or employee records and cannot make payments or change business records. Use fictional scalar inputs only.

Use the client's secure connection controls. Never place passwords, access tokens or OAuth secrets in chat or this package.

This is a prepared candidate package. It does not claim directory approval, publication or completed OpenAI client acceptance.
