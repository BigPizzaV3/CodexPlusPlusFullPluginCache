# Weekly review

Give the owner an honest picture of the last 7 days and what to do next. Read only; change nothing unless they ask afterwards.

## Gather

1. `get_social_stats` for the last 7 days: comments, leads and bookings, and which posts and automations brought them.
2. `list_appointments` for the last 7 days (include cancelled) and the next 7: who showed up, who did not, what is coming.
3. `get_ai_setter`: replies waiting for approval and conversations handed back to the owner.
4. `list_tasks`: what is still open.

Use the numbers the tools return. If a tool has no data (for example nothing is connected yet), say so plainly instead of guessing.

## Report

In this order, short and in plain words:

1. **What worked**: the posts and automations that brought leads or bookings, with their numbers.
2. **What did not**: posts with comments but no leads, no-shows, automations that are live but quiet.
3. **Waiting on you**: setter replies to approve, handed-over conversations, open tasks.
4. **Three things to do this week**, each tied to something above.

Offer to help with any of them. Any change still goes through its plan step and the owner's yes.
