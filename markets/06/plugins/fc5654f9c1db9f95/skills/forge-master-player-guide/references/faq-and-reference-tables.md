# ForgeMaster FAQ, Myths and Open Questions

Status: **Player-facing draft v0.1**  
Evidence cutoff: **11 September 2026**

This chapter gives short answers to recurring player questions. Exact tables and fuller explanations remain in the dedicated chapters.

## Character statistics and Power

### How do I check my complete statistics?

Tap the small **i** beside the equipment area on the main game screen. You can also tap your player icon from the PvP League screen. Tapping the displayed Power number does not open the statistics.

### If a modifier is missing from the complete statistics screen, is it unknown?

No. If the full screen is visible and a supported modifier is absent, its value is **zero**. If the screenshot is cropped or incomplete, the missing value is unknown.

### Does higher Power guarantee a win?

No. Power measures Base Damage and Base Health. It does not directly value most modifiers, active Skill timing, Fairy effects or melee/ranged matchup behaviour.

### Did the game make a mistake if a lower-Power player wins?

Not necessarily. Crit, Attack Speed, Double Chance, Lifesteal, Block, Regen, Skills and weapon matchup can legitimately overcome a Power deficit.

### Does Critical Damage start from zero?

No. A critical hit has a built-in **1.20×** multiplier before the displayed Critical Damage modifier is added.

### Does Double Chance just double the damage number?

It creates a follow-up weapon attack. That attack can independently Crit, be blocked and generate Lifesteal.

### Are melee and ranged weapons interchangeable?

No. Ranged weapons can normally attack during the approach, while melee weapons must reach contact. Melee Damage benefits melee weapons and Ranged Damage benefits ranged weapons.

## Campaign and daily activity

### What does a Campaign number such as 4-15 mean?

It means battle 15 of stage group 4. Group 1 contains ten battles; configured groups 2–11 contain twenty each.

### Do individual enemies become stronger at every battle within a stage group?

No. Within a group, the battle number changes waves, enemy counts and composition. The per-enemy base Health and Damage change with the stage group and difficulty.

### How much stronger is Hard than Normal?

The current configuration reuses the same formations and multiplies each enemy's Health and base Damage by **6,000,000**.

### Does advancing the Campaign continually increase offline income?

No. Campaign progress unlocks offline income at 1-9, but the current base offline rates are not stage-scaled.

### Should I save Dungeon Keys for Clan War scoring days?

No. Each Dungeon has two daily uses and they do not carry over. Use them every day; on Days 1 and 4 the same uses also score Clan War points.

### Should I save today's League tickets for tomorrow?

No. The five daily Challenge Tickets do not carry over.

### Does losing a League match preserve the ticket?

No. Both wins and losses consume a ticket. A loss gives no points but does not remove League points already earned.

### Are Dungeons and Clan Missions linked?

No. They are separate systems with independent progression, entries and rewards.

### Does failing a Clan Mission consume a Mission Ticket?

No. The ticket remains, but the failed mission is removed from that day's offer list.

### Does a ten-minute mission rally provide a larger automatic boost?

No. It gives other clan members more time to notice and join. Up to three helpers provide the actual assistance.

### Do mission assistants receive the reward?

No. Only the player who started the mission receives it. Assisting is unlimited and does not use the assistant's Mission Tickets.

## Equipment and the Forge

### Is a higher-era or higher-level item always better?

No. Check Damage, Health, star count, modifiers and the complete build. A lower item with the right modifiers may be more useful.

### Does an Auto Forge modifier filter mean every retained item has only those modifiers?

No. The filter uses **at least one selected modifier** as a match. A retained item can also contain an unwanted modifier.

### Does ordinary Auto Forge keep running after it finds a match?

Normally it stops. The later Continue Forging option allows matched items to accumulate in a review stack until its capacity is reached.

### Does Forge level alone determine the level of the next equipment item?

Not completely. Current evidence suggests each equipment slot progresses around that slot's highest level already seen, while Personal Tech can raise its obtainable level ceiling. The exact random range still needs direct confirmation, so this should not yet be treated as a fully decoded rule.

### Should I save Eggshells before ascending my Forge?

No. That mixes two different pillars. Forge ascension uses Coins and post-ascension forging uses Hammers. Eggshells are relevant to Pet progression and Pet ascension.

### Does the 3,000,000-Coin Forge ascension payment score Forge-upgrade Clan War points?

No.

### Does Forge ascension instantly return equipment to its former strength?

No. The Forge returns to Level 1 and the normal era-probability sequence repeats. Newly forged items carry the star, but rebuilding still takes forging and Forge-upgrade time.

## Skills

### What happens when I summon a duplicate Skill?

The duplicate fills that Skill's own progress bar. When the required number of copies is reached, the Skill can advance a level.

### Does every Skill level require the same number of copies?

No. Early levels require fewer copies; later progress bars rise through larger requirements.

### What is the base Skill summon price?

Five summons cost **200 Skill Tickets**, or 40 tickets per summon before Personal Tech discounts.

### Are Skill summons passive Clan War scoring?

No. They consume a scarce stored resource and are active scoring on Skill days.

### Is 49,600 Skill Tickets a universal ascension requirement?

No. It is a community first-ascension rebuilding estimate aimed at reaching a practical ascended Legendary probability with no discount. It is guidance, not a guarantee or an activation cost.

## Pets and eggs

### What are Pets for?

Three equipped Pets contribute Damage, Health and modifiers. Pet archetypes are biased toward Damage, Health or a balanced mixture.

### Should ordinary Eggshells be converted into eggs immediately?

Usually yes, so the time-limited hatcheries remain occupied. The exception is Eggshells reserved for Pet ascension: eggs are removed by ascension while Eggshells survive.

### Does buying or summoning an egg score Clan War points?

No. The completed hatch scores; buying the egg scores zero.

### Is carefully timing an egg to hatch on Day 3 or Day 5 active scoring?

No. Hatching remains passive because it uses the time-gated pipeline rather than consuming a scarce reserve at the scoring moment.

### Does Extra Egg Chance mean a hatched Pet can duplicate?

No. It gives a chance of receiving an additional egg when Eggshells are converted. Hatching is a later, separate step.

### How many hatcheries can I have?

Players start with two and can buy two additional hatcheries, giving four parallel slots.

### Are all Pets basically identical apart from graphics?

No. Every rarity contains Damage-, Health- and Balanced-archetype Pets. Individual modifier rolls also matter.

## Mounts and merging

### What are Mounts for?

One equipped Mount contributes Damage, Health and modifiers. Unlike Pets, Mounts have no Damage/Health/Balanced archetypes.

### Do Mounts hatch?

No. A Mount appears immediately when summoned.

### Are Mount summons passive Clan War scoring?

No. Summoning directly consumes Clock Winders and scores immediately, so it is active scoring.

### What does merging do?

Pets and Mounts act as XP buckets. One donor egg, Pet or Mount is consumed and its XP is added to the retained recipient. Enough XP raises the recipient's level and therefore its Damage and Health contribution.

### Does a developed donor lose the XP invested in it?

No. Its accumulated XP transfers to the recipient. The donor itself is permanently consumed.

### Do the donor's modifiers transfer?

No. The recipient keeps its own modifiers; the donor's modifiers disappear.

### Does a high-level donor give more Clan War merge points?

No. Every donor counts as one merge regardless of rarity, level or accumulated XP.

### Are merges reversible?

No. Lock valuable Pets and Mounts to prevent accidental selection.

### Does Extra Mount Summon Chance guarantee a second Mount?

No. It increases the expected yield. It is a probability, not a promise on each summon.

### Is 30,000 Clock Winders the only safe Mount-ascension number?

No. It is the community guide's no-discount estimate for reaching the first-ascension Level-31 Epic target. Personal Tech discounts and Extra Mount Summon Chance reduce the expected resource requirement, while modifier luck can still alter the practical result.

## Ascension

### What is ascension?

Ascension resets one progression pillar—Forge/equipment, Skills, Pets or Mounts—so it can produce starred assets with much larger applicable base values. The player then rebuilds that pillar.

### Does everything reset when one pillar ascends?

No. Only the selected pillar resets. Its assets are removed while its raw rebuilding resource is retained:

| Pillar | Assets removed | Retained rebuilding resource |
|---|---|---|
| Forge | Equipment | Coins and Hammers |
| Skills | Skills | Skill Tickets |
| Pets | Eggs and Pets | Eggshells |
| Mounts | Mounts | Clock Winders |

### Does one star mean exactly 50 times the complete character strength?

No. It gives approximately ×50 to the applicable base values of the ascended assets. The complete character also contains the other pillars, modifiers and different rarities/levels.

### Is every new one-star asset immediately better than every old unstarred asset?

No. There is an overlap period. A mature high-rarity unstarred asset can remain stronger than an early low-rarity starred replacement.

### Should I ascend immediately when the button appears?

No. Check what will be removed, the surviving stock, the rebuilding target, Personal Tech and Clan War timing first.

### Should I ascend Pets during a savings week?

Usually no. Eggs and Pets will be destroyed, but each is also a stored Clan War merge. If you ascend without merging them, you forfeit that scoring potential. If you merge them all first, you may spend hundreds of thousands of active points in a week where they do not help secure a win.

Since building a substantial Eggshell reserve—often 20,000+ for the selected rebuild route—takes weeks, normally wait for a hard or competitive war. On a Pet-scoring day, merge only enough old stock to help the clan win. When you then ascend, every remaining egg and Pet is destroyed; only the Eggshell reserve survives. The exact reserve is target-specific, so 20,000 is not a universal minimum.

### Does the same savings-week problem apply to Skills and Mounts?

Yes. All Skills and copy progress disappear while Skill Tickets survive; all Mounts disappear while Clock Winders survive. Available Skill upgrades or Mount merges can score before ascension, but spending them all in an easy war may be wasteful and leaving them unused means the potential is lost at reset.

### Why is Forge ascension different?

All equipment and Forge progress are lost, while Coins and Hammers survive. However, the Forge cannot be restored immediately because every level has an upgrade timer and later timers last many days. Retained currency prepares the rebuild but does not remove the time gate.

### Should two pillars be ascended together?

Usually not unless each has a separately protected rebuild reserve and the combined temporary loss is acceptable. Staggering them makes it easier to recover one source of strength at a time.

### Do ascension stars have any reward benefit beyond stronger assets?

Yes. Every accumulated character ascension star currently adds **+5% Clan Tech Potion rewards from all sources**. The bonuses appear additive, but combined rounding should still be checked against live rewards.

## Personal Tech and Clan Tech

### Must a parent Personal Tech node reach 5/5 before its child opens?

No. Each required parent normally needs at least one completed sublevel.

### Does +40% research speed remove 40% from the original timer?

No. Speed bonuses normally divide the base time by `1 + speed bonus`. A +40% speed gives about 71.4% of the base duration, not 60%.

### When does a Personal Tech upgrade score Clan War points?

When the completed node's **Complete** button is pressed, not when the research is started.

### Does Personal Tech increase the Clan War points awarded by each summon or merge?

No. It can reduce costs, add expected summons or improve assets, but shared Clan Tech controls the action-specific Clan War scoring bonuses.

### Are red and green Tech Potions interchangeable?

No. Red potions start Personal Tech. Green potions are donated to the shared Clan Tech tree.

### Are Clan Tech Race score and Clan Tech Race rewards the same bonus?

No. One node increases the race score credited for donations. Another increases the green-potion placement reward.

### Does donating more always improve the Clan's long-term position?

Donations always advance Clan Tech and add race score, but timing and reserves still matter. The race is a daily elimination competition, so clans should coordinate rather than empty every member's stock without a plan.

## Clan War

### What is passive scoring?

Passive scoring comes from normal or expiring activity that does not irreversibly consume a scarce reserve at the scoring moment—for example daily Dungeon uses or completing a timed egg hatch.

### What is active scoring?

Active scoring consumes a scarce stored resource or irreversible asset specifically to generate points—for example Skill summons, Mount summons, Pet/Mount merges or discretionary green-potion donations.

### Does scheduling an action for the correct day automatically make it active?

No. Timing and resource consumption are separate questions. An egg deliberately timed to hatch on Day 3 is still passive.

### What does a “save” instruction mean?

Use passive and expiring opportunities, then spend active-scoring resources only if needed to win. It does not normally mean skipping Dungeons, League tickets, ordinary income or productive timers.

### Should players spend the allowed maximum active resource every week?

No. A spending ceiling is not a target. The correct amount is the minimum needed to win, which may be zero.

### Does receiving a Progress Pass or weekly reward itself score Clan War points?

No. Receiving stock is not one of the scoring actions. Later spending or use may score on the appropriate day.

## Fairies

### Do Fairies create a bonus from nothing?

No. Each Fairy converts complete steps of an existing source modifier into another capped combat effect.

### Should I equip poor equipment merely to activate a Fairy?

Usually no. The source modifier should normally be useful in its own right; judge the complete build rather than chasing the Fairy line alone.

### Does Reflect prevent the incoming hit?

No. The defender still takes the resolved weapon hit while the attacker also receives reflected damage.

### Can Block and Reflect trigger together?

**Observed in the 16 September 2026 correction:** yes, for the same incoming direct weapon hit. Block protects the defender, while Reflect returns the pre-Block weapon damage that would otherwise have been inflicted. Skill damage is not reflected in the tested recordings. Ordinary Lifesteal remains unchanged; exact simultaneous ordering and whether reflected retaliation can itself be Blocked remain **Pending**.

### Is Reflect confirmed for Skill damage and Lifesteal interactions?

Skill-hit Reflect is not supported by the tested evidence. Ordinary Lifesteal still applies to qualifying weapon hits, but the exact same-frame ordering with Reflect remains **Pending**.

## Leagues

### How are my five League opponents chosen?

Normally they are the three players immediately above and two immediately below you in the current table. League points determine the ranking first, with Power breaking equal-point ties. The shortlist therefore uses the Power-resolved order when scores are tied. It changes when scoring changes the rankings. Table-edge handling remains unconfirmed.

### Is League ranking based on Power?

Primarily no. It is based on accumulated League points, but Power breaks ties between players with the same points.

### Are League and Clan War battle tickets the same?

No. They are separate allowances for separate competitions.

## Stepping Stones

### What is Stepping Stones?

It is a recurring chance-based minigame. One token buys one play, and every jump is presented as a 50/50 choice between one safe stone and one breaking stone.

### Do the eight free daily tokens carry over?

The unclaimed daily shop allowance does not. Claim the tokens first: tokens already in your personal balance can accumulate.

### When can I use Auto ×20?

When you have at least 20 tokens. It spends 20 tokens to automate 20 games and saves time; there is no evidence that it improves the odds.

### Can Stepping Stones skins improve my character?

Yes. Weapon, helmet and armour skins can add Damage or Health. Random tile-8 skins display a 2%–12% range, and a 10.5% skin has been directly observed.

### Can a Stepping Stones skin exceed 10%?

Yes. A 10.5% skin has been won directly. The game displays a maximum of 12%, although an awarded 12% skin has not yet been directly captured.

### Do skins have set bonuses?

Yes. Equipping three matching skins completes the set and activates an additional bonus. The pictured Knight set gives **+10% Damage and Health** when its three-piece set is complete.

## Open questions the guide must not pretend to answer

The following remain under investigation:

- the player-facing name of Campaign stage group 11;
- exact campaign defeat/retry behaviour between waves;
- failed-Dungeon use consumption;
- Ghost Town and Tech-bonus reward rounding;
- precise Mission success and assistant-Power calculations;
- League table-edge opponent selection and time-limit resolution;
- Fairy interaction with Skill damage, Lifesteal and some other modes;
- Pet/Mount Quick Select rules;
- exact equipment-level random-range behaviour;
- some live ascension activation requirements and probability assumptions;
- stacking and rounding of multiple Clan Tech Potion reward bonuses;
- Stepping Stones reward settlement, exact cadence, evolution and skin stacking/duplicates, plus direct confirmation of the displayed 12% maximum.

When one of these affects an answer, the correct response is to identify the uncertainty and say what observation would settle it—not to invent a plausible rule.

## How to read the tables

- `Points/level` is the number of donation points required for one level.
- One ordinary point costs two green Clan Tech Potions.
- `Potions/level` therefore equals `Points/level × 2`.
- `Total potions` assumes starting at Level 0 and completing all ordinary levels.
- Partial progress reduces the remaining cost; it is not refunded.
- Infinite levels begin only after the ordinary maximum.

## Reading the table

- Level 1 is free and has no upgrade timer.
- **Total coins is already the whole cost of that target level.** Node count divides that total into equal payments; never multiply Total coins by Nodes.
- A level’s coin nodes must all be filled before its timer begins.
- Partial node funding remains paid, but gives no Forge-level benefit until every node for that level is complete.
- Timer expiry makes the upgrade ready; the Forge level changes only when **Complete** is tapped.
- Only one Forge upgrade can run at a time, and its timer runs once per level—not once per node.
- Personal Tech can reduce ordinary Forge-upgrade coin cost and duration.
- Forge ascension at Level 35 is separate: it costs 3,000,000 coins, is instantaneous, resets the Forge to Level 1 and awards no Clan War points.
- Cumulative times use 30-day months. The Level-34 value above normalises the workbook's equivalent `3mo 21d 29h` display to `3mo 22d 5h`.
- From post-ascension Level 1, exact base totals are: Level 27 = 60d 14h and 20,971,100 ordinary upgrade Coins; Level 28 = 66d 22h and 23,201,100; Level 35 = 120d 23h 20m and 41,891,100. Add the fixed 3,000,000-Coin ascension payment once when a full ascension-plus-rebuild total is requested.

## Interpretation rules

- A probability is the chance for each individual forge, egg purchase, Skill summon or Mount summon at that pillar level.
- It is not a guarantee that the rarity will appear within a particular number of attempts.
- The summons and cost on a Pet or Mount row are what the player spends **while at that level** to advance to the next level.
- To calculate the base cost of arriving at target Level N from Level 1, use the cumulative figure on the **Level N−1** row. For example, Pet Level 15 costs 7,100 base Eggshells to reach, and Mount Level 31 costs 30,000 base Clock Winders to reach.
- Skill purchases contain five summons. The Skill table therefore rounds cumulative base cost up to a whole 200-ticket purchase; the player's Personal Tech discount changes the displayed purchase price.
- “Cumulative cost after this level” is before relevant Personal Tech.
- Extra Egg and Extra Mount chances change expected asset yield, not the table’s base rarity percentages.
- A cost discount changes the resource spent per attempt, not the rarity roll.
