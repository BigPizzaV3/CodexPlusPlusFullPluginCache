# ATS Compact Resume Template

Use for technical candidates, extensive relevant experience, or a credible one-page fit. Build a single-column document with selectable text and no tables, text boxes, icons, sidebars, or header/footer contact information.

## Layout contract

- Page: US Letter
- Margins: 0.65 in on all sides
- Typeface: Arial
- Name: 17 pt bold, left aligned
- Headline: 10 pt
- Contact line: 9 pt
- Section heading: 10 pt bold uppercase; 8 pt before and 2 pt after
- Role and body: 10 pt; single line spacing
- Final bullet in an entry: 4 pt after
- Bullets: 0.17 in left indent with 0.17 in hanging indent
- Role header: employer bold and location regular on line one; title italic and dates regular on line two, with dates at a right tab stop
