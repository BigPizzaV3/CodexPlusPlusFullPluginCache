# Privacy Notes

Version 2.1.1

This plugin contains research instructions, reference maps, and image assets.
It has no plugin-operated backend, account integration, analytics, telemetry,
or credential collection. It does not independently receive or store records.

The host assistant processes the conversation and any files under the host's
own privacy, retention, and data-use settings. This package cannot promise zero
retention, encryption arrangements, or regulatory compliance on the host's behalf.

When live research is used, public search and website services may receive the
queries and requests made through the host. The skill instructs the assistant
to use de-identified queries and not send private claim text or identifiers to
public search. Only provide records necessary for your question; omit passwords,
MFA codes, SSNs, and unnecessary account or claim identifiers.

The optional Ko-fi support page is an external service with its own policies.
Donations are not required and do not affect assistance or benefit outcomes.

These package-level notes are not a substitute for any publisher-specific
privacy disclosures required by the chosen publishing platform.
