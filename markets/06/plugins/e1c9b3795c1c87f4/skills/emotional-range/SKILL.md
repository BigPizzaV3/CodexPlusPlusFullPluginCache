## Build range into the clone
Record samples with the range you will need (warm, excited, serious, calm), in the user's natural way of expressing them, while keeping the voice consistent.

## Direct emotion at generation time
1. Audio tags on expressive models: short bracketed directions such as [excited], [whispers], [laughs], [sighs], [serious].
2. Writing cues: punctuation, short sentences for impact, ellipses for hesitation.
3. Emotion in the words themselves works best.

## Rules
Tag only where emotion changes; keep tags the model does not support out of the text; test the most emotional lines first.

## Output
A directed script with emotion placed, the model note, and a plain version without tags.
