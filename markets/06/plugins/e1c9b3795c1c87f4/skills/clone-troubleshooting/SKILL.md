## Diagnose the cause
- Doesn't sound like the user: samples too noisy, mixed styles or too little clean audio; re-record cleaner and more consistent samples, or move to a professional clone.
- Wrong or drifting accent: samples with mixed accents, or model mismatch; use consistent samples and a model that supports the language well.
- Noise, hum or echo in output: it was in the samples; clean or re-record, and lower similarity slightly.
- Robotic or flat: stability too high, script too formal; lower stability a little, write more conversationally.
- Too dramatic or unstable: lower style, raise stability, shorten the text chunk.
- Odd breaths, clicks, stutters: remove them from samples; regenerate the problem line only.
- Reads symbols or tags aloud: remove unsupported markup.
