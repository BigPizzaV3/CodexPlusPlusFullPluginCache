1. Hook in the first 5 to 10 seconds.
2. Structure for retention: short segments, pattern breaks, open loops.
3. Write in the user's style (see script-in-your-style), one block per scene with timings.
4. One saved clone preset for the whole channel so every video sounds the same.

## Short videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
- Open with the hook; no intro.
- About 2.5 to 3 words per second: 15s about 40 words, 30s about 80, 60s about 150.
- One idea, short lines, strong ending or loop back; lines that also work as captions.
