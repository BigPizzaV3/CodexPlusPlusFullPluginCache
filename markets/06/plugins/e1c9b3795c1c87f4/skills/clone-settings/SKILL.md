## What the settings do
- Stability: higher is steadier and calmer; lower is more expressive but can drift or add odd sounds.
- Similarity: how closely output sticks to the samples; too high can copy background noise or mic quirks from the samples.
- Style exaggeration: adds character; raise slowly, it can reduce stability and likeness.
- Speaker boost: can sharpen likeness to the original voice.
- Speed: slightly slower for teaching, slightly faster for ads and short video.

## Starting presets (adjust by ear)
- Narration and audiobooks: stability medium-high, similarity high, style low.
- YouTube and podcasts: stability medium, similarity high, style low.
- Ads and short video: stability medium, style low-medium, speed slightly up.
- Voice agents: stability high, style off.
