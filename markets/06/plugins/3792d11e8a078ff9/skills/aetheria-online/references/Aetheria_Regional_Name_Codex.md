# Aetheria Online — Regional Name Codex

**Purpose:** a large, culturally grounded name bank for the Aetheria Online GPT. Use this codex before improvising a new Aetherian NPC name.

## Sacred anti-Edric-Vale rule

Do **not** default to generic AI-fantasy names. In particular, avoid stock LLM-fantasy forms such as **Edric Vale, Mara, Haldren, Kael, Verren/Varren**, and close variants unless the user explicitly requests them.

Use rules:
- Prefer an unused entry from the correct regional/cultural bank.
- Reuse a name only when realistic coincidence, family tradition, dynastic repetition, or deliberate namesaking justifies it.
- A second name may be a family, house, tribe, clan, brood, workshop, registry, or byname depending on culture; never force every culture into modern Western first-name/surname structure.
- Canonical rulers and established individuals are included as anchors only. Do not assign an established full name to a new NPC.
- Ordinary people should often have ordinary-sounding names for their culture. Not everyone needs a protagonist name.
- This file is a **direct selection bank**, not merely a phonotactic seed list. The GPT may choose from it directly.

# Generic-AI naming blacklist and smell test

Do not generate a name merely by combining one dramatic noun with another. Avoid habitual overuse of **Vale, Varren/Verren, Raven, Shadow, Thorn(e), Night, Storm, Ash, Frost, Black, Silver, Blood, Moon, Star** as surnames or compounds unless the relevant culture actually supports the construction and the individual has a causal reason to bear it.

Especially avoid the recurring LLM-fantasy cadence of names such as **Edric Vale, Mara Veyne, Haldren Voss, Kael Varren, Lyra Thorne, Seraphina Nightshade**. These are examples of the *problem*, not names to use.

When uncertain, choose something from the bank that sounds like a person could have inherited it from boring grandparents. Aetheria needs bakers, clerks, shepherds, washerwomen, teachers, dockworkers, petty nobles and accountants—not forty million protagonists.
