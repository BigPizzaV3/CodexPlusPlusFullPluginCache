MedAcad Writer Support

Academic writing support for medical and health-research education.

Designed for manuscripts, theses, dissertations, literature reviews, systematic/scoping reviews, meta-analysis reporting, and reporting guidelines.

What it helps with

  • Outline and manuscript structure
  • Academic language, clarity, and coherence
  • Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion writing support
  • CONSORT, PRISMA, PRISMA-ScR, STROBE, CARE, and related reporting guidance
  • Research-literature summarization and academic correspondence

Educational boundary

This plugin is for academic writing and research education. It does not diagnose, treat, triage, prescribe, provide personalized healthcare recommendations, process real patient records, or support clinical-care workflows.
