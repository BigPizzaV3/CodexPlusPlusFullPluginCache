MedAcad Writer Support v0.6.1 — Netlify update

1. Unzip this archive. It contains index.html, privacy.html, terms.html, support.html, style.css at its root.
2. Open the EXISTING site medacad-writer-hlp in your Netlify account.
3. Choose Deploys; drag the extracted folder containing these HTML files into the manual deploy dropzone. Do not use Add new site / generic Netlify Drop (that creates another site).
4. Confirm the existing domain https://medacad-writer-hlp.netlify.app/ and check the four pages and the mailto email link.
