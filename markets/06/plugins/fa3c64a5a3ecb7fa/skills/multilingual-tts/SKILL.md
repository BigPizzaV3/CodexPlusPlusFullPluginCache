## Method
1. Translate for speaking, not word for word: natural phrasing, local idioms, correct formality.
2. Plan for length: many languages run longer than English; trim to fit timed video.
3. Choose a voice that sounds native for the target language and region (for example Latin American vs European Spanish, Brazilian vs European Portuguese).
4. Localize numbers, dates, money and units.
5. Mixed-language text: mark foreign words for pronunciation or keep them in the voice's language.
6. Have a native speaker review important audio.

## Dubbing
For existing videos, AI dubbing can translate and re-voice speech while keeping the speaker's voice style; check lip-sync and timing after.
