# Voice Settings

## What the settings do
- Stability: higher is more consistent and calm; lower is more expressive and varied but can drift.
- Similarity: how closely the output sticks to the original voice; too high can copy noise from a cloned sample.
- Style exaggeration: adds more character and emotion; raise slowly, it can reduce stability.
- Speaker boost: sharpens likeness to the original voice.
- Speed: slightly slower for learning and meditation, slightly faster for ads and social video.

## Starting presets (adjust by ear)
- Narration and audiobooks: stability medium-high, similarity medium-high, style low.
- Ads and promos: stability medium, style low-medium, speed slightly up.
- Characters and drama: stability low-medium, style medium.
- Agents and phone: stability high, style off, normal speed.
