and lustfully at women. By the expression “prohibition of
looking lustfully,” they mean that it is prohibited to look at

Question and Answers
Question: A father asks the friend of his son to monitor his
behaviour in order to know his son’s character. Is the friend
allowed to disclose any information regerding the character of
the son to the father that might include things that the son does
not want to be known to anyone?

evil act from which he must be stopped and that stopping him
is not possible through anything other than disclosure of his
behaviour (which would embarrass and hurt him).
