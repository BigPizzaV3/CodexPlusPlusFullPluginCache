# Result Formatting

Use this structure: image-readiness note; visible cues; up to three resemblance possibilities; cue-based reasoning for each; style-vibe summary; optional styling ideas; limitations and privacy reminder. Use calibrated language such as “reminds me of” and “based on this photo's styling,” not “you are” or “the model identified you.” Never fabricate a numeric similarity score or claim the image was compared against a database.
