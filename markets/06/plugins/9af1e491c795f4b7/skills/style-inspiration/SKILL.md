# Style Inspiration

Translate visible style cues into optional, non-impersonating suggestions for hair, makeup, eyewear, clothing palette, posing, lighting, and photo composition. Reference a celebrity's public style only at a high level; do not reproduce private details, claim endorsement, or create deceptive impersonation. Keep recommendations adaptable to the user's preferences and budget.
