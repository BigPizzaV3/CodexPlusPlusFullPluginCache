# Healthcare Professional Headshot

## Purpose
Patients choose on trust and calm. The photo has to look clinical enough to be credible and human enough to be reassuring.

## The spec
- **Wardrobe**: white coat over a solid shirt or blouse reads as clinical authority; scrubs read as hands-on and accessible; a plain shirt without either reads as therapy, counselling and allied health. Pick one and use it across the practice.
- **Background**: soft neutral grey or warm off-white for clinical trust. Blurred clinic interiors work if no equipment, signage, patient areas or other people are readable.
- **Lighting**: soft, even, minimal shadow. Hard shadows read as stress.
- **Expression**: warm closed-lip or small open smile. Patients rate approachability and competence together; calm eyes do more than a big smile.
- **Crop**: head and upper chest so the coat collar or scrub neckline is visible. 1:1 for directories, 4:5 for the practice site.

## Compliance details worth checking
- **Credentials**: many directories display name and credentials separately, so keep badges and embroidered names legible or remove them entirely rather than half-visible.
- **Patient privacy**: never include any readable patient information, charts, screens or people in the background.
- **Hospital and health system rules**: many mandate a specific backdrop and a coat; check before generating.
- **Accuracy**: do not generate a coat, stethoscope or scrubs for someone not entitled to present that way. Match the actual role.

## Specialty tuning
Surgery and specialist roles lean slightly more formal and cooler; paediatrics, midwifery, GP and mental health lean warmer, lighter, softer. Same crop, different colour temperature and expression.

## Quality standard
Work like a senior portrait photographer and art director: give exact crops, pixel sizes, hex values, light angles and named garments instead of vague advice. Put likeness above flattery; a photo that is not recognisably the person has failed. Never alter body shape, age, skin tone or facial structure, and never remove permanent features unless the person asks for their own. Only work from photos of people who have consented. Say plainly when AI images are not acceptable, such as passports and other official identity documents, or restricted, such as casting and press.
