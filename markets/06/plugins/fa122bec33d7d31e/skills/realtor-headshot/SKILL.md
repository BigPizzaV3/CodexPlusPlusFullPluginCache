# Real Estate Agent Headshot

## Purpose
A realtor photo is used at two extremes: a 40 px portal thumbnail and a 1.2 m yard sign. It must be built for both.

## The spec
- **Crop**: slightly wider than a corporate headshot, showing the top of the jacket and a bit of shoulder, so the sign layout has room.
- **Background**: plain, high contrast against hair and clothing. Signage often uses a cutout on brand colour, so shoot or generate against a clean, evenly lit background that keys out cleanly.
- **Wardrobe**: business casual to business. Jacket reads as "handles a transaction". Brokerage brand colour works as an accent (tie, blouse, pocket square), not head to toe.
- **Expression**: warm, open, teeth showing. Property is a trust and approachability sale.
- **Variants to deliver**: 1:1 portal thumbnail, 4:5 brochure, 3:2 web banner crop, and a transparent PNG cutout for signage and flyers.

## Print and signage specs
- Signage art at 150 DPI at final size for large format (a 24x36 in panel = 3600x5400 px), CMYK for offset, with 3 mm bleed.
- Test the face at 10% zoom: on a sign seen from 20 m, only head shape, hair and contrast register.
- Avoid thin patterns and fine stripes: they moiré badly in print and on screens.

## Brokerage rules
Many brokerages mandate a background colour, a jacket, a logo lockup and a specific crop. Get that spec first; regenerating later costs more than asking once.

## Refresh cadence
Update every 2-3 years, or immediately after a significant look change. An outdated photo is the most common complaint clients raise at a first meeting.

## Quality standard
Work like a senior portrait photographer and art director: give exact crops, pixel sizes, hex values, light angles and named garments instead of vague advice. Put likeness above flattery; a photo that is not recognisably the person has failed. Never alter body shape, age, skin tone or facial structure, and never remove permanent features unless the person asks for their own. Only work from photos of people who have consented. Say plainly when AI images are not acceptable, such as passports and other official identity documents, or restricted, such as casting and press. Never state pricing, plan contents, turnaround times or results as guaranteed facts, and never claim an official relationship with any platform. Follow OpenAI usage policies.
