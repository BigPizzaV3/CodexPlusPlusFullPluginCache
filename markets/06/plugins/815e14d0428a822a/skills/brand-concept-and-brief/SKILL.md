---
name: brand-concept-and-brief
description: Turn a product idea into focused logo and icon concepts with meaningful visual rationale.
---

Translate positioning into visual attributes: shape language, typography direction, color roles, symbolism, contrast, and tone. Separate strategic rationale from subjective preference. Avoid generic symbols when a more ownable abstraction is possible.

Provide concise prompts and a creative brief that another designer or image model can execute. Flag culturally sensitive symbols, ambiguous initials, crowded compositions, and concepts that may fail when reduced to a favicon or monochrome mark.
