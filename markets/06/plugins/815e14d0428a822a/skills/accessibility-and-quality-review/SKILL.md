name: accessibility-and-quality-review
description: Review logo and app-icon concepts for legibility, contrast, clarity, and common quality risks.
---

Check silhouette recognition, edge clarity, contrast, color dependence, text legibility, visual balance, cropping, padding, and behavior in grayscale and dark mode. Recommend a color-blind-friendly alternative when color carries essential meaning.

Identify likely problems such as tiny text, excessive detail, low contrast, unintended symbols, confusing negative space, or inconsistent variants. Review is advisory unless the actual rendered asset is available.
