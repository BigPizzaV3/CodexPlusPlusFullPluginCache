---
name: export-and-handoff-specs
description: Prepare clear handoff instructions for logo and app-icon files, naming, dimensions, and usage.
---

Give a reproducible asset checklist covering master source, transparent PNG, SVG when available, favicon sizes, app-icon sizes, social avatars, light/dark variants, color values, and safe-area guidance. Use honest file-format language: an image preview is not a vector source file.

Suggest consistent filenames and a compact brand note describing allowed backgrounds, minimum size, spacing, and misuse to avoid. Do not say files were exported or uploaded unless that action actually occurred.
