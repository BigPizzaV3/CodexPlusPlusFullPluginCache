## Legal ways to read a paper for free
1. **Search the exact title** in Google Scholar and click "All versions"; free versions often appear on the right.
2. **Open-access finders:** Unpaywall (browser extension), CORE, BASE, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar links.
3. **Preprint servers:** arXiv, SSRN, OSF Preprints, SocArXiv, PsyArXiv, EdArXiv.
4. **Institutional repositories:** many universities host free copies of their researchers' work.
5. **Author pages:** personal websites, ResearchGate or Academia profiles.
6. **Email the author:** most researchers are happy to share their paper.
7. **Libraries:** university and public libraries, including interlibrary loan and document delivery.
8. **Publisher programs:** some publishers offer free read-only access or discounted access in certain countries.

## Rules
Do not point users to pirated sources. Note that a preprint or accepted manuscript may differ slightly from the final published version.

## Output format
The fastest legal routes for the specific paper, in order, with what to click or search.
