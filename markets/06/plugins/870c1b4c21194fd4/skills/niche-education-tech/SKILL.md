## What the viewer needs to see
The tool or the outcome, instantly. Tech and tutorial viewers are searching with intent - ambiguity loses them to the next result.

## Layouts
- **Tool + outcome:** app or device on one side, the finished result on the other, an arrow between
- **Screen hero:** a clean, zoomed UI region (never a full shrunk screenshot) with one element circled
- **Comparison:** two devices or two tools, VS mark, contrasting halves
- **Number:** "7 SETTINGS", "IN 3 MIN" as the graphic element
- **Face + screen:** face reacting, screen behind, one benefit word

## Craft notes
- Zoom into the part of the UI the video is about; full screenshots are unreadable at 320x180.
- Use the product's real look but keep logos small and legal (see policy-safety).
- Clean, cool palettes with one bright accent read as credible; neon overload reads as clickbait and costs trust in this niche.
- Device mockups: consistent angle, soft shadow, dark gradient background.

## Text
Concrete, not hyped: "NO CODE", "FIXED IT", "3 MIN SETUP", "FREE". Version numbers and years help searchers ("2026") but date the video.

## Quality standard
Work like a senior thumbnail designer and packaging strategist: specific, practical and original. Give exact pixel sizes, HEX codes, crops, placements and the actual words, never vague advice. Always give at least two testable directions. Keep every thumbnail honest: it may create curiosity, never a false belief about what is in the video. Respect copyright, trademarks and likeness rights: never copy another creator's artwork, use characters, logos, film stills or a real person's face without rights, and never fabricate screenshots, statistics or results. Do not promise specific CTR or view outcomes. Follow OpenAI usage policies and each platform's rules.
