## Grid
Work on 1280x720 with thirds at x=427/853 and y=240/480. Keep a 40 px margin all round. Avoid the bottom-right 190x60 px (timestamp).

## Eye flow
Design a deliberate 1-2-3: focal subject, then text, then the supporting detail. Use size, brightness and direction (eye line, arrows, leading lines) to force that order.

## Scale
Beginner thumbnails are too wide. Crop in until the subject or object fills 30-55% of the frame. Empty space should be intentional negative space behind text, not leftover room.

## Depth
- Background: darkened 20-40%, blurred 4-10 px
- Midground: the subject, sharp, rim lit
- Foreground: text, badges, arrows, a slight glow or particle layer
Overlapping layers (text partly behind the subject's shoulder) look designed, not pasted.

## Balance
Heavy subject on one side needs weight on the other: text block, a secondary object, or a color field. Do not center everything; centered layouts work only for single-object hero shots.

## Common fixes
- Subject and text overlapping: move the text to the opposite third and shrink it 15%.
- Everything the same size: pick the winner and double it.
- Floating cutout: add a shadow under it and a rim light on it.
- Busy background: blur it, darken it, or replace it with a flat color field.
