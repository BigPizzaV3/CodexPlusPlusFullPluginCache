## First, is CTR actually the problem
Ask for: CTR, impressions, average view duration, traffic sources, and how the video compares with the channel's median. A 3% CTR on browse traffic with huge impressions can outperform a 9% CTR on a tiny suggested feed. Low views with high CTR is a reach problem, not a thumbnail problem.

## Diagnostic ladder (check in this order)
1. **Topic demand** - does anyone want this video? No thumbnail fixes a topic nobody searches or clicks.
2. **Competing set** - open the search or browse result and look at the neighbours. Does this one disappear?
3. **Small-size legibility** - shrink to 320x180. If the text or face blurs, that is the fix.
4. **Focal point** - more than one competing element?
5. **Promise clarity** - can a stranger say what they get, in 2 seconds?
6. **Title/thumbnail overlap** - are they saying the same thing twice?
7. **Audience match** - is the style aimed at the actual viewer, not the creator's taste?
8. **Freshness** - has the channel used this exact template for 20 videos?

## Fix list output
Give 5 fixes ranked by expected impact and effort, then one full replacement concept with layout, text and colors. Say which fix to test first and what result would prove it worked.
