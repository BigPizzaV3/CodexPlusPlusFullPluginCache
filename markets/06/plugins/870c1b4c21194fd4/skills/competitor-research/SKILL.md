## Method
1. **Gather the set.** The top 10-20 results for the main search term, plus the browse feed for the niche, plus the creator's own best and worst performers.
2. **Tally the pattern.** For each: face or no face, expression, text word count, dominant colors, crop tightness, layout type, style (photo, 3D, illustrated).
3. **Find the convention.** What does almost every winner do? Keep that - it signals the viewer is in the right category.
4. **Find the saturation.** What do they *all* do the same way? That is the axis to break: color, framing, style or emotion.
5. **Find the gap.** An emotion or angle nobody in the set uses.

## Output
- A pattern table of the set
- The 2-3 conventions to keep
- The 1 axis to break, and how
- 3 concepts built on that break, with layouts, text and palettes

## Cautions
- Study the pattern; do not copy a specific thumbnail's artwork, characters or photography.
- Outliers with huge subscriber counts are not proof - a big channel wins clicks on its name.
- Patterns shift; re-run this every few months.
