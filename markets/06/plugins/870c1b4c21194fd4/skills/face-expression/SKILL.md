## Why faces work
Human faces are found first by the eye and carry emotion instantly. In most niches a clear, emotional face raises click-through. Faces hurt when the expression is neutral, the crop is wide, or the face has nothing to do with the promise.

## Expression menu (match it to the video's emotion)
Shock (wide eyes, open mouth), concern, disbelief, delight, smugness, exhaustion, determination, curiosity (head tilt, raised brow), pain, pride. Overacted reads better at 320x180 than subtle - but keep it true to the content and the creator's persona.

## Cropping
- Head fills 35-55% of the frame height for a single-subject thumbnail.
- Chin to top of head inside the frame; a slight crop of the hair is fine, never crop the chin.
- Eyes on the upper third line. Eye contact with the camera for direct address; eyes looking at the object when the object is the story.
- Place the face on the left third for text-right layouts, and flip it if the face naturally looks toward the center.

## Lighting and separation
Key light from 45 degrees, rim or hair light in the accent color behind the subject, background darker or blurrier than the face. A clean cutout with a 4-10 px stroke or glow separates the subject when the background is busy.

## Multiple people
Two faces maximum, different sizes so one leads. Angle them toward each other or toward the object.

## Rights
Use the creator's own face, or a face they have written permission to use. Never build a thumbnail on a real person's likeness without their consent, and never imply someone appears in a video they are not in - see policy-safety.
