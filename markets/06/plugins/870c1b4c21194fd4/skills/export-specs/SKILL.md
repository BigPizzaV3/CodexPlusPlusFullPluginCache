## Core specs (YouTube long-form)
- 1280x720 px, 16:9, under 2 MB, JPG/PNG/WebP (JPG is safest for size).
- Design for the small size first: most impressions are 320x180 or smaller on mobile.
- Avoid the bottom-right ~15% (timestamp) and the bottom strip (progress bar on suggested views).
- Leave a small margin: keep faces and text at least 40 px from every edge.

## Export settings
- JPG quality 80-90 is the sweet spot: sharp text, small file.
- Export at 1280x720 exactly; do not upload a 4K image expecting better quality, YouTube re-encodes it.
- sRGB color profile. Wide-gamut exports shift color in browsers.
- Sharpen slightly after downscaling (0.3-0.5 px radius), not before.
- If a PNG exceeds 2 MB, switch to JPG rather than shrinking dimensions.

## Common problems
- Blurry thumbnail: the source was upscaled, or it was exported below 1280 px wide.
- Color shift after upload: wrong color profile.
- Text soft: text was rasterized at a small size then scaled up. Always set type at final size.
