## Shoot once, use for months
Plan a 20-minute session that produces a library: 10 expressions x 3 angles, plus hands (pointing, holding, palms up), plus full-body and half-body.

## Setup
- Plain wall in a color you will never use in the thumbnail (grey or green), 1.5-2 m behind you so you are not casting shadows on it
- One key light 45 degrees, at head height or slightly above, soft (softbox, window, diffused)
- Optional rim light behind and to the side, gelled to your accent color
- Camera at eye level, 50-85 mm equivalent, f/2.8-f/4, subject filling the frame from chest up
- Shoot RAW or high-quality JPG, well exposed on the face

## Expressions to capture
Shock, laughing, worried, disgusted, confused, pointing, thumbs up, arms crossed confident, hands on head, whispering. Exaggerate more than feels natural.

## Cutting out
1. Use an automatic subject-select or background-remove tool, then fix by hand.
2. Refine hair with an edge/refine brush; avoid a hard halo.
3. Remove colored fringe from a green screen with a defringe or decontaminate edges step.
4. Add a 3-6 px inner dark edge or a subtle outer stroke so the cutout sits in the scene.
5. Match color temperature and contrast to the new background before adding a rim light.

## File hygiene
Save cutouts as transparent PNG at full resolution, named by expression, so a series can be assembled in minutes.
