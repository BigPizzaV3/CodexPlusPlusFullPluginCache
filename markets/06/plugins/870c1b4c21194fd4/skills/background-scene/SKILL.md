## Options ranked by reliability
1. **Flat or two-tone field** with a radial glow behind the subject - always readable
2. **Heavily blurred location** (aperture look, 6-12 px blur) with a darkening layer
3. **Studio or set** shot wide and clean, subject lit separately
4. **Composited scene** built from stock or generated elements
5. **Split screen** for comparisons, with a hard divider or a torn-paper edge
6. **Collage** only when the video is a list; keep it to 3 tiles

## Treatments that make any background work
- Darken the edges (vignette 15-25%) and brighten behind the head
- Push the background 1-2 stops darker than the subject
- Desaturate the background slightly so the subject's colors lead
- Add a large soft shape (circle, arc, brush stroke) behind the subject in the accent color

## Replacing a background
Cut out the subject with a hair-safe mask, add a 3-8 px dark edge fringe removal, then match the light direction of the new background or the composite will look fake. If the original light comes from the left, the new rim light goes on the left.

## Avoid
Full-frame busy photos behind text, stock images with watermarks, generic gradient meshes with nothing on them, and anything with small detail that vanishes at 320x180.
