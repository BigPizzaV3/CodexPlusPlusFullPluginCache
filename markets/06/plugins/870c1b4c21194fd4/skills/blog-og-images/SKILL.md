## Sizes
- Open Graph / link preview: 1200x630 (also works for X summary cards)
- Blog featured / hero: 1600x900 or 1920x1080, with a centered safe area for responsive crops
- Pinterest-friendly variant: 1000x1500
- Newsletter header: 600x200 to 600x300 at 2x for retina

## Rules
- The share card is a thumbnail: 4-8 words maximum, huge, high contrast.
- Keep text in the central 1000x450 of a 1200x630 card; platforms crop the edges differently.
- Include the site logo small and consistent, so shares build recognition.
- Compress hard: WebP or JPG at quality 75-85, under 150-250 KB. Page speed is part of the job.
- Build one template and change only the headline and accent color per post.

## Accessibility
Real text in the page headline, not only in the image, and write alt text describing the purpose of the image.
