## Concept engines (run the video through each)
- **Transformation:** before to after, the gap is the hook
- **Stakes:** money, time, risk, a countdown, a consequence
- **Contrast:** cheap vs expensive, old vs new, expected vs real
- **Conflict:** a person against a thing, a VS setup
- **Curiosity gap:** hide the key detail with a blur, a hand, a covered object
- **Extreme:** the biggest, smallest, first, last, only
- **Relatability:** the exact moment the viewer has lived
- **Authority or surprise source:** the unexpected person or place
- **Negative frame:** the mistake, the warning, what not to do
- **Reveal:** the finished thing, shot to look better than the viewer expected

## Output
8-10 concepts, each in this shape: **Name** - visual in one line - text (0-4 words) - why it earns a click - difficulty to produce (easy / needs a shoot / needs AI or editing). Rank them and recommend two to test against each other.

Never propose a concept the video cannot deliver.
