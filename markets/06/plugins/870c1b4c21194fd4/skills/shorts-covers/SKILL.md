## Sizes and crops
- Vertical canvas 1080x1920 (9:16).
- **The grid crop is the real target.** YouTube Shorts shelf and the Instagram profile grid crop to roughly the central portrait area - design the key content inside the middle 1080x1350, and put the essential text inside the central 1080x1080.
- UI overlays eat the bottom ~340 px (caption, handle, buttons) and the top ~250 px. Keep text out of both.
- Right edge: the action buttons column covers roughly the right 200 px on TikTok and Reels.

## What works vertically
- Text at the top third, subject at the center, nothing at the bottom
- 2-5 words, large: 90-160 px on a 1080-wide canvas
- The cover frame should be the most dramatic frame, not the first frame
- On Shorts, the cover mostly matters on your channel grid, not in the feed - so design it for the grid

## Series grids
Plan covers three at a time: the profile grid is read in rows of three. Alternating colors or a continuing element across a row makes a profile look designed.
