## Word count
0-4 words. 3 is the sweet spot. Every extra word shrinks the others.

## Choosing the words
- Say what the title does not. Title: "I tried the 5am club for 30 days" - thumbnail: "IT BROKE ME".
- Use the emotional or numeric hit: "$0", "DAY 30", "IT WORKED", "NEVER DO THIS", "BEFORE / AFTER".
- Cut every filler word: articles, prepositions, "how to", "my".
- One word can be enough when the image is strong.

## Sizing on a 1280x720 canvas
- Primary word: 140-260 px cap height for 1-2 words, 90-140 px for 3-4 words.
- Secondary line: 50-70% of the primary size.
- Text block should occupy 25-40% of the frame width.
- Test: at 320x180 the primary word is still 35-65 px tall. If it is under 25 px, it is too small.

## Fonts
Heavy geometric or grotesque sans (extra bold, black, condensed for long words). Script and thin fonts fail at small size. Two weights of one family beats two families. Slight rotation (2-5 degrees) adds energy; more looks amateur.

## Legibility treatment (use one or two, not all)
- Outline: 8-14 px, in the background's dark tone or pure black
- Drop shadow: offset 4-8 px, 40-60% opacity, soft
- Highlight box or brush stroke behind the text
- Outer glow in the accent color, 15-25 px, low opacity

## Color
White or near-white text with a black outline is the reliable default. Use one accent color (yellow #FFD400, red #FF2D2D, lime #B6FF00) on the single most important word only. Never put saturated text on a saturated background.

## Placement
Opposite the face. Keep 40 px clear of every edge, and out of the bottom-right corner. Align the text block to a third line, not the exact center, unless the subject is centered.
