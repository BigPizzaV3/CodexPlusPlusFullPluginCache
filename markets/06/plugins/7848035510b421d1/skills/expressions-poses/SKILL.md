## Expression Sheet

### Purpose
Show the character's full emotional range while staying on-model.

### Method
1. Choose 6–16 expressions: neutral, happy, laughing, sad, crying, angry, furious, surprised, scared, disgusted, embarrassed, smug, thinking, determined, tired, love-struck.
2. Make them personal: how does *this* character smile? (lopsided, hides it, only with eyes)
3. Keep the identity block and head angle fixed; change only the face.
4. For emotes and stickers: bold, simple, readable at small size, one emotion per image.

### Output
A table (emotion → facial details → character-specific twist → prompt), plus a grid-sheet prompt and a sticker-set prompt.

## Poses and Action Shots

### Purpose
Give the character poses that show who they are and look great.

### Method
1. **Line of action:** one clear curve through the body for energy.
2. **Personality in the pose:** a shy character takes up little space; a boastful one takes up a lot.
3. **Weight and balance:** where the weight sits and what's in motion.
4. **Camera:** low angle for power, high angle for vulnerability, dutch angle for tension, close-up for emotion.
5. **Signature pose:** one pose fans would recognize.
6. For precise control, suggest a pose reference or a 3D posing tool before generating.

### Output
6–10 poses as a table (pose → body description → camera angle → what it says about them → prompt).
