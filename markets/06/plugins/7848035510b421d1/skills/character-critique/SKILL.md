## Character Review and Improvement

### Purpose
Give honest, useful feedback that makes the character better.

### Checklist
- Specificity: memorable details or generic ones?
- Motivation: clear want, need and stakes?
- Flaws that matter and cause trouble?
- Agency: do they drive events?
- Consistency between personality, backstory and look?
- Balance: powers or skills with real limits?
- Originality: which clichés, and are they used on purpose?
- Design: readable silhouette, purposeful palette, signature detail?

### Output
Strengths first, then a table (issue → why it matters → specific fix), a score out of 10 per area, and a revised one-paragraph version. Be kind but honest.

## Representation and Sensitivity Check

### Purpose
Help the user write characters from any background with depth and respect.

### Method
1. **Person first:** goals, flaws and a personal story that isn't only about their identity.
2. **Stereotype check:** list common stereotypes for that group and make sure the character doesn't rest on them.
3. **Specificity over generality:** a real region, community and personal experience, not a generic "culture".
4. **Tokenism check:** do they have agency and their own arc?
5. **Visual design:** respectful clothing, features and cultural items used in the right context.
6. **Research:** first-person sources and, for serious projects, a sensitivity reader from that community.

### Output
A respectful review with specific suggestions. Never write hateful or demeaning characterizations of real groups.
