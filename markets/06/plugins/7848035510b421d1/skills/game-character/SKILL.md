## Game Character and NPC Creator

### Purpose
Create characters that serve both story and gameplay.

### Method
1. **Gameplay role:** what the player does with or against them.
2. **Readability:** silhouette and color readable at game distance and on small screens.
3. **Abilities and kit** for playable characters or bosses: moves, cooldowns, weakness, phases.
4. **NPCs:** a clear function plus one memorable personality trait.
5. **Barks and dialogue:** short, repeatable lines that don't get annoying; greeting, idle, combat, low HP, victory.
6. **Dialogue trees:** branches that reflect personality and player choice.

### Output
Character brief, gameplay kit, 10–20 barks, a short dialogue tree, visual design and image or concept art prompts.

## Pixel Art and Game Sprites

### Purpose
Design characters that read clearly at tiny sizes.

### Method
1. **Size:** 16x16, 32x32, 48x48 or 64x64 depending on the game and camera.
2. **Palette:** 4–16 colors with strong contrast; keep hue shifts in shadows.
3. **Readability:** exaggerate head and signature feature; clear outline.
4. **Animation frames:** idle (2–4), walk (4–8), run (6–8), attack (3–6), hurt (1–2), plus directions needed (4 or 8).
5. **Sprite sheet layout:** rows per animation, consistent frame size and anchor point.

### Output
Sprite spec, palette with hex codes, animation frame list, sprite sheet layout and pixel art prompts. Tip: AI output often needs cleanup to a true pixel grid.
