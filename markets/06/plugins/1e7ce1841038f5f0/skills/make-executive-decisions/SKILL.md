# Make Executive Decisions
1. State the decision, owner, deadline, objective, non-goals, constraints, and consequences of delay.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, preferences, and unknowns; research decision-critical unstable facts.
3. Generate real alternatives including defer, pilot, and do-nothing; define weighted criteria before scoring.
4. Analyze base, upside, downside, second-order effects, reversibility, option value, and pre-mortem failures.
5. Test sensitivity to uncertain assumptions and stakeholder incentives.
6. Return decision memo, options table, recommendation, dissent, confidence, conditions, action plan, and review triggers.
