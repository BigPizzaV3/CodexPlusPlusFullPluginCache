# Extract Action From Anything
1. Identify source scope, participants, project, dates, authority, and desired task system.
2. Separate explicit commitments from suggested actions and inferred possibilities.
3. Normalize each action into verb, outcome, owner, due date, dependency, status, evidence, and acceptance condition.
4. Capture decisions, rationale, unresolved questions, risks, blockers, and required approvals.
5. Flag missing owners or dates instead of inventing them; merge duplicates and expose conflicts.
6. Return executive summary, decision log, action register, risks, questions, and ready-to-send follow-up when requested.
