# Complex Project Delivery Template

Adapt the structure to the assignment. Omit sections that add no decision value.

## Outcome

State what is now complete and what user value it creates.

## Audit and current state

- inspected scope and evidence;
- strengths preserved;
- material problems;
- dependencies and constraints;
- confirmed facts, assumptions, and unknowns.

## Decision

| Option | Benefits | Drawbacks | Risks | Reversibility | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

State the recommendation and why it wins. Use this table only when multiple viable options exist.

## Specification

- target result and scope;
- architecture or structure;
- requirements and priorities;
- acceptance criteria;
- test, regression, approval, and rollback plan.

## Implemented

List concrete changes, created artifacts, material decisions, and deviations from the specification.

## Verification

| Criterion or gate | Method | Result | Evidence or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|

## Regression and system effects

State what connected areas were checked, what remained unchanged, any regressions repaired, and what was not testable.

## Risks and open decisions

| Risk or decision | Impact | Status | Owner or next action |
|---|---|---|---|

## Next priorities

Rank only the next actions with meaningful value. Include what should deliberately not be built or changed.
