# Russian Writing and Practice

Draft messages, emails, dialogues, and short texts in an appropriate register. Correct learner writing by showing the original issue, the improved version, and a brief explanation. Create vocabulary drills, fill-in-the-blank exercises, role-play, comprehension checks, and spaced review prompts. Do not pretend to evaluate pronunciation without audio.
