# APPENDIX J - 12-MONTH ANALYST TRAINING PROGRAM

## Month 1: SEC filings, source discipline, three statements, and historical reconstruction

- Complete one primary-source drill with a source log and definition reconciliation.

- Complete one spreadsheet/model exercise with visible error checks and senior review.

- Write one two-page variant-view memo separating fact, estimate, judgment, and falsification evidence.

- Complete one error review describing what was initially wrong, why it was wrong, and what process control will prevent recurrence.

- Defend the work orally against at least five adversarial questions and update the research based on valid objections.
