Render a crisp vector-friendly geometric logo centered with generous padding, legible at 32 pixels, using deep navy, muted teal, and a small warm gold accent. No text, numbers, ticker symbols, currency symbols, watermark, flashy trading imagery, rockets, crypto aesthetics, or photographic textures.
