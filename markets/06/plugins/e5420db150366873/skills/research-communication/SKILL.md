## Communication workflow

1. Separate the decision layer from the evidence layer.
2. The decision layer should include the decision question, one-sentence thesis, variant perception, key operating drivers, valuation range, top risks, catalysts or validation timeline, thesis breaks, and what changed.
3. The evidence layer should preserve source log, definitions, historical reconstruction, model schedules, contrary evidence, risk register, valuation detail, and review history.
4. Do not make a persuasive narrative by deleting uncertainty or disconfirming evidence.
5. Apply the investment committee question bank and completion tests before finalizing a deep memo.

## Evidence and output controls

- Distinguish reported facts, management claims, external estimates, analyst calculations, and analyst judgments.
- For current claims, verify freshness and state the as-of date.
- Cite material factual inputs to the actual source used.
- Recalculate important arithmetic rather than copying unexplained outputs.
- State unresolved questions and the strongest contrary case.
- Define specific falsification or thesis-break evidence for material conclusions.
- Do not execute securities transactions.
