## Training workflow

1. Diagnose the learner's current level from demonstrated work, not tenure or self-rating.
2. Assign progressive filing, accounting, modeling, valuation, industry, forensic, risk, and communication exercises from the bundled references.
3. Require reproducible work products, closed-book drills where specified, reviewer challenge, and documented error reduction.
4. Advance responsibility only after the pass standard is demonstrated.
5. Use the final self-audit and mastery rubric for certification, while preserving the learner's error log and repeated-error rate.

## Evidence and output controls

- Distinguish reported facts, management claims, external estimates, analyst calculations, and analyst judgments.
- For current claims, verify freshness and state the as-of date.
- Cite material factual inputs to the actual source used.
- Recalculate important arithmetic rather than copying unexplained outputs.
- State unresolved questions and the strongest contrary case.
- Define specific falsification or thesis-break evidence for material conclusions.
- Do not execute securities transactions.
