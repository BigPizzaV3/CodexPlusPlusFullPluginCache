# Release Notes

## 1.0.0

Initial skills-only release of Institutional Public Equity Analyst.

- Preserves the complete Version 3.0 analyst manual as a canonical bundled source.
- Adds 15 focused Skills for full-company analysis, filings, accounting, forensics, business quality, industry, governance, modeling, valuation, events, alternative data, risk, communication, sector analysis, and analyst training.
- Adds all 106 modules, Appendices A-M, 14 Master Labs, and the sector common protocol as retrieval-oriented Markdown mirrors.
- Adds deterministic finance calculation helpers and research-state validation.
- Adds source, assumption, model-change, decision-journal, risk, and research-state schemas.
- Adds marketplace starter prompts and positive/negative review tests.
- Adds build-time content manifests and integrity checks designed to detect omitted manual sections.
