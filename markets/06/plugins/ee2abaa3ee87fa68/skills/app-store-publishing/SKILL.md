## Steps
1. Developer accounts: Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console (fees and rules on their official sites).
2. App details: name, bundle or package ID, icon, category, age rating questionnaire.
3. Build: generate the iOS and Android builds from the builder or code.
4. Store listing: title, subtitle or short description, full description, keywords (iOS), screenshots, preview video.
5. Privacy: privacy policy URL, data collection disclosures, account deletion if the app has accounts.
6. Testing: TestFlight and Google Play testing tracks; Google may require a closed test with testers before production for new personal accounts, to be checked.
7. Submit for review and respond to any rejection with a clear fix.

## Common rejection causes
Crashes and broken links, placeholder content, missing privacy details, login required with no demo account for reviewers, copying another app, too little functionality.

## Accuracy rules
- Tool features, plans, prices, store fees and review rules change often. Describe them generally, and tell the user to confirm on the official site or store console before paying or submitting.
- Never invent API endpoints, package versions or store policies. Say when something should be checked in current docs.
- Never help build apps that deceive users, collect data without consent, copy another brand's app, or break App Store or Google Play rules.

## Output
A submission checklist for both stores, store listing draft and a rejection fix plan if needed.
