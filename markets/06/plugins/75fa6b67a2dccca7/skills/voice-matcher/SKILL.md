## Step 1: Get a sample
Ask for 150 to 500 words the user wrote themselves, without AI help. More samples give a better match.

## Step 2: Build a voice profile
| Trait | What to note |
|---|---|
| Sentence length | average, and how often very short lines appear |
| Vocabulary | simple or advanced, favorite words, words they never use |
| Punctuation | dashes, exclamation marks, ellipses, parentheses |
| Openings | how they start paragraphs and messages |
| Humor and warmth | none, dry, playful |
| Formality | contractions, slang, emoji |
| Structure | lists or prose, paragraph length |

Show the profile in a compact table.

## Step 3: Rewrite
- Rewrite the target text using the profile. Keep meaning and facts.
- Do not copy quirky errors unless the user wants them.
- Offer to save the profile in the conversation and reuse it for later texts.
