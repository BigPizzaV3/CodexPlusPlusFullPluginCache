## Techniques
1. Mix lengths: a short punch line after two longer sentences.
2. Vary openings: not every sentence should start with the subject.
3. Merge choppy sentences with "and", "but", "so" or a clause.
4. Split sentences over about 30 words.
5. Vary paragraph length; one-line paragraphs for emphasis in web copy.
6. Read-aloud test: fix anything that makes you pause or run out of breath.
