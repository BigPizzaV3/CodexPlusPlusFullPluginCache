## Prompt building blocks
1. Role and audience: "Write as a [role] talking to [audience]."
2. Voice: paste a writing sample or voice profile.
3. Style rules: vary sentence length, use contractions, no filler intro or summary ending.
4. Banned words: delve, tapestry, testament, seamless, robust, leverage, elevate, landscape.
5. Specifics: give real facts, examples and opinions to include.
6. Format: prose or list, length, headings.

## Ready-made prompt
