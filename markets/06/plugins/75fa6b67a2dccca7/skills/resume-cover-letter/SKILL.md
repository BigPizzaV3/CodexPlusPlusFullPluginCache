## Cover letters
1. Open with why this company and role, specifically, not "I am writing to express my interest".
2. Two short stories or achievements that match the job needs.
3. Close with a confident, simple ask.
4. 250 to 350 words.

## Resume bullets
- Formula: action verb + what you did + result (number if the user has one).
- Cut "responsible for", "passionate", "results-driven", "dynamic team player".
- Never invent achievements, titles or metrics; mark gaps as [add a number if you have one].

## LinkedIn About
First person, 3 to 5 short paragraphs: what you do, how, proof, what you want next.
