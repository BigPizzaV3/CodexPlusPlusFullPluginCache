## Spoken-language rules
1. Short sentences; one idea per breath.
2. Contractions and everyday words.
3. Repeat key points (listeners cannot scroll back).
4. Signpost: "Here's the thing", "First", "So what does that mean?"
5. Mark pauses with line breaks or [pause].
6. Remove written-only devices: parentheses, long lists, complex numbers.

## Timing
About 130 to 160 words per minute of speech. Tell the user the estimated duration.

| Presentation | one message per slide, story openings |
| Toast | short, one story, warm ending, under 3 minutes |
| Eulogy | personal memories, calm pace |
