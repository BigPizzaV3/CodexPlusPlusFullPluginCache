## Steps

2. Keep the primary keyword in the title, first 100 words and one H2, placed naturally.

6. Write a meta title (about 50 to 60 characters) and meta description (about 140 to 160 characters).
7. Suggest 3 to 5 FAQ questions with short answers if they fit the topic.
