## Step 1: Read the text and the goal
- Identify the text type (essay, email, blog, post, ad, story, report, message) and the audience.
- If the user gave no instructions, use standard mode and the tone the text already has. Do not ask questions before a first version unless the text is missing.
- Note everything that must stay fixed: facts, numbers, names, quotes, citations, links, keywords, headings and required terms.

## Step 2: Choose the mode
| Mode | When | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Text is mostly fine, user wants a polish | Swap AI vocabulary, fix stiff transitions, keep 80–90% of wording |
| Standard (default) | Typical ChatGPT output | Rebuild sentences, vary rhythm, cut filler, add specific wording |
| Deep | Very robotic, list-heavy or generic text | Restructure paragraphs, change openings, turn lists into prose where suitable, add a clear point of view |

## Step 3: Humanize with these techniques
1. **Vary rhythm.** Mix short sentences with longer ones. Break three-sentence patterns of equal length.
2. **Cut AI vocabulary.** Replace words like delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, realm, seamless, robust, leverage, unlock, elevate, navigate the landscape, in today's fast-paced world, it's worth noting, furthermore, moreover.
3. **Remove filler openers and closers.** No "In conclusion", "Overall", "Ultimately" summaries that repeat the text. End on a real point.
4. **Break the rule of three.** AI loves three-item lists and three adjectives. Use one, two or four when natural.
5. **Prefer concrete over abstract.** Replace "various benefits" with the actual benefit.
6. **Use natural connectors.** "But", "so", "still", "that said", or no connector at all, instead of stacked formal transitions.
7. **Active voice and direct verbs.** "The team fixed it", not "It was fixed by the team".
8. **Contractions where the tone allows** (don't, it's, we're). Keep them out of formal academic text.
9. **Remove hedging stacks.** One hedge at most ("may", "often"), never "may potentially possibly".
10. **Reduce em dashes and colons** used as dramatic pauses; use a comma or a new sentence.
11. **Limit bold, emoji and headings** unless the format needs them.
12. **Keep the meaning.** Never add false facts, invented quotes, fake experiences or fake statistics.
