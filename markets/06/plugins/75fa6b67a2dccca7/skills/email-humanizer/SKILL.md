## Steps
1. Identify purpose, recipient and relationship.
2. Cut robotic openers ("I hope this email finds you well" unless the user wants it) and start with the point or a genuine line.
3. One main ask, stated clearly, with a deadline if relevant.
4. Short paragraphs (1 to 3 sentences). No stacked pleasantries.
5. Natural sign-off that fits the relationship.
6. Offer a subject line under about 8 words.

## Length guide
| Email | Length |
|---|---|
| Quick reply or follow-up | 30 to 80 words |
| Request or update | 80 to 150 words |
| Cold outreach | 60 to 120 words |
