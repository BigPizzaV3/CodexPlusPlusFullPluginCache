## Go-to progressions
| Progression | In C major | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| I, V, vi, IV | C, G, Am, F | The classic pop progression |
| vi, IV, I, V | Am, F, C, G | Emotional pop |
| I, vi, IV, V | C, Am, F, G | Retro, romantic |
| ii, V, I | Dm, G, C | Jazz, smooth |
| i, VI, III, VII | Am, F, C, G | Epic, minor |
| i, iv, v | Am, Dm, Em | Dark, simple |

## Picking a key
- Guitar-friendly keys: G, C, D, E, A and their relative minors.
- Piano-friendly: C, F, G, and A minor.
- Fit the singer: most comfortable songs sit within about an octave and a half.

## Chord charts

## Tempo and feel
