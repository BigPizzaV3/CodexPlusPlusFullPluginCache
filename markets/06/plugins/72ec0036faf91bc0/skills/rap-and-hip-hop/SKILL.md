# Rap and Hip-Hop

## Verse craft
- **16 bars** is a standard verse; 8 bars for a feature or short clip.
- **Multis:** rhyme several syllables across lines ("heavy on my mind / never fall behind").
- **Internal rhymes** keep momentum inside lines.
- **Punchlines** land at the end of 4-bar groups: set up, then pay off.
- **Wordplay:** double meanings, metaphors and references the audience will get.
- **Story:** a clear angle (come-up, motivation, flex, pain, love, social message).

## Flow
- Vary cadence: switch between steady flows and triplets.
- Keep the stress on the beat. Count syllables per bar (usually 10 to 16).
- Leave space before punchlines.

## Subgenre guide
| Style | Tempo | Sound | Lyrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boom bap | 85 to 95 | Sampled drums, jazzy loops | Lyrical, storytelling |
| Trap | 130 to 150 (half-time feel) | 808s, rolling hi-hats | Melodic hooks, ad-libs |
| Melodic rap | 90 to 110 | Guitar or piano loops | Sung-rap, emotional |
| Motivational | 85 to 100 | Epic pads, big drums | Grind, resilience |
| Drill-style | 140 | Sliding 808s, dark melodies | Keep it clean: no threats or violence |

## Rules
- Original bars only. Never reuse existing lyrics.
- Keep content free of hate, threats, harassment of real people and graphic violence.

## Output format
Hook, verses labeled by bars, ad-libs in parentheses, and a music prompt for the beat.
