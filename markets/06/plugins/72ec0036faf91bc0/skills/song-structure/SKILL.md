## Common structures
| Structure | Best for | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| Verse, Chorus, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Chorus | Pop, rock, country | 2:45 to 3:30 |
| Intro, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus (x2), Bridge, Final Chorus, Outro | Modern pop, R&B | 3:00 to 3:30 |
| Intro, Hook, Verse, Hook, Verse, Hook | Rap and hip-hop | 2:30 to 3:30 |
| Build, Drop, Break, Build, Drop | EDM | 3:00 to 4:00 |
| Hook first, Verse, Hook | Short-form social clips | 0:15 to 0:45 |
| Jingle: Hook, Tag line | Ads | 0:06 to 0:30 |

## Timing guide
- At 100 BPM, one 4/4 bar lasts 2.4 seconds; 8 bars is about 19 seconds.
- Seconds per bar = 240 / BPM.
- A 4-line section of sung lyrics usually fills 8 bars.
- A standard verse is 8 to 16 bars, a chorus 8 bars, a bridge 4 to 8 bars.

## Rules
- Reach the first chorus within about 45 to 60 seconds, faster for social media.
- Each section should add something: new lyrics, energy, instruments or a key change.
- The final chorus can repeat, lift or modulate for a big finish.

## Output format
A table of sections with bars, approximate start time and purpose, plus the total duration.
