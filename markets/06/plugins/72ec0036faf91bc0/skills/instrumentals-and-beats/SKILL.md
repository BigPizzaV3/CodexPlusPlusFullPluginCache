## Beat prompts
Name: genre, BPM, key or mood, drums, bass, melody instrument, texture, structure and length.

Example: "Instrumental trap beat, 140 BPM half-time, dark minor key. Hard 808 bass with slides, crisp rolling hi-hats, snappy snare on 3. Haunting bell melody with a soft choir pad. 8-bar intro, drop at 0:14, beat switch at 1:30, 2:30 total. No vocals."

## Calm and Lo-fi Music
### Principles
- Slow tempo (55 to 80 BPM) or no drums at all.
- No sudden hits, loud drops or sharp high sounds.
- Simple, repeating harmony that does not demand attention.
- Long, smooth fades at the start and end.

### Styles
| Style | Description |
|---|---|
| Lo-fi | Dusty drums, jazzy chords, vinyl warmth, 75 to 85 BPM |
| Ambient | Evolving pads, nature textures, no beat |
| Soft piano | Gentle, sparse, lots of space |
| Lullaby | Soft voice or music box, 3/4 time, simple melody |
| Nature blend | Rain, ocean or forest sounds under soft music |
| Focus | Steady, minimal, loopable, no vocals |

### Lullaby lyrics
Short lines, soft consonants, repetition, and comforting images (stars, moon, home). Keep the melody within a small, easy range.

### Output format
Music prompt with length and "no sudden changes" instructions, and lyrics if the track has vocals.
