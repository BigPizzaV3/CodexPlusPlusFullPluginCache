## Quick reference
| Genre | Tempo (BPM) | Signature sound | Lyric style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pop | 95 to 125 | Big hooks, synths, clean vocals | Simple, relatable, repetitive chorus |
| Hip-hop / rap | 80 to 100 (trap 130 to 150 half-time) | 808s, hi-hats, samples | Dense rhymes, wordplay, storytelling |
| R&B | 60 to 100 | Smooth keys, groove, vocal runs | Romantic, sensual, emotional |
| Rock | 110 to 140 | Electric guitars, live drums | Direct, energetic, rebellious |
| Country | 80 to 120 | Acoustic guitar, fiddle, steel guitar | Storytelling, place, family, heartbreak |
| EDM / house | 120 to 128 | Build-ups, drops, four-on-the-floor kick | Short phrases, euphoric hooks |
| Afrobeats | 100 to 115 | Syncopated percussion, log drums, warm guitars | Joyful, danceable, call-and-response |
| Reggaeton | 88 to 100 | Dembow rhythm | Party, romance, Spanish lyrics |
| Lo-fi | 70 to 90 | Dusty drums, jazzy chords, vinyl crackle | Mostly instrumental, calm |
| Folk / acoustic | 70 to 110 | Acoustic guitar, natural voice | Honest, poetic storytelling |
| Metal | 100 to 200 | Heavy distorted guitars, double kick | Intense, dramatic themes |
| Gospel | 60 to 120 | Choir, organ, piano | Faith, hope, uplift |
| Cinematic | Varies | Orchestra, big percussion | Usually instrumental |
| South Asian pop / film style | 80 to 120 | Tabla, dholak, sitar, strings with modern beats | Romantic, poetic, often Urdu or Hindi |

## How to use
- Match the genre to the use: gift songs work well as acoustic pop, ads as upbeat pop, study music as lo-fi.
- For a genre blend, name a primary genre and one element from the second ("pop song with afrobeats percussion").
- Adapt the lyric style to the genre, not just the music prompt.
