## Emotional arc
A song feels powerful when the emotion moves. Plan where it starts, how it builds and where it lands:
- Verse 1: set the scene quietly.
- Chorus: state the feeling clearly.
- Bridge: the biggest shift, a realization or a change of heart.
- Final chorus: the payoff, often bigger or reframed.

## Output format
A short arc plan, then the lyrics or prompt changes that create it.
