---
name: terminology-and-formatting
description: Maintain glossaries, terminology consistency, document structure, and translation-ready formatting.
---

Extract repeated terms and maintain consistent translations across a document or project. Preserve variables, tags, code, filenames, URLs, product names, and placeholders exactly unless the user authorizes changes. Identify terms that require an approved glossary or human review.

For bilingual output, label source and target clearly. For tables and structured content, preserve column meaning and order. Do not claim terminology was checked against an external glossary unless one was actually provided or consulted.
