## Style families
- Painting: oil impasto, watercolor, gouache, acrylic pour, fresco.
- Drawing: pencil, charcoal, ink line, pen and wash, pastel.
- Art movements: impressionism, post-impressionism, art nouveau, art deco, cubism, surrealism, pop art, ukiyo-e woodblock, bauhaus, minimalism.
- Illustration: storybook, editorial, flat vector, line art, retro comic, risograph, linocut.
- Digital: matte painting, concept art, cel shading, low poly, isometric, vaporwave, synthwave, cyberpunk.
- Anime and manga, chibi, cartoon, pixel art, 3D stylized, claymation look, paper cut, stained glass, mosaic.

## Rules
