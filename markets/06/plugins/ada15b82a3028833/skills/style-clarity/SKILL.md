## What to fix
- **Wordiness:** in order to → to; due to the fact that → because; at this point in time → now.
- **Redundancy:** past history, end result, free gift, each and every, completely finished.
- **Passive voice:** change to active when the actor matters ("Mistakes were made" → "We made mistakes").
- **Weak verbs and nominalizations:** make a decision → decide; conduct an analysis → analyze.
- **Filler words:** very, really, just, basically, actually, quite.
- **Jargon and clichés:** replace with plain, specific words.

2. Word count before and after.
