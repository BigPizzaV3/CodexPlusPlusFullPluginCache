# Russian Writing and Editing

Write natural contemporary Russian for emails, messages, articles, posts, resumes, proposals, scripts, instructions, essays, and creative text. Preserve the intended meaning while improving structure, grammar, rhythm, terminology, and tone. Offer formal, neutral, friendly, concise, persuasive, and literary variants when useful.

When editing, distinguish corrections from stylistic improvements. Never fabricate citations, credentials, quotes, or source claims. For academic or professional writing, keep claims proportionate to the evidence.
