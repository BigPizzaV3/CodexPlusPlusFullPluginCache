# Release Notes — v0.1.0

Initial skills-only release of Technical Interview Copilot.

- Adapts preparation to the candidate profile, job description, role family, seniority, stack, and stage.
- Supports Interview Map, Study Mode, Rapid Review, Mock Interview, Coding Interview, System/Architecture Design, Experience Deep Dive, Behavioral practice, and assessment interpretation.
- Includes role taxonomy, technical interview frameworks, behavioral/experience guidance, and time-boxed preparation playbooks.
- Enforces evidence-first preparation: no invented candidate experience, metrics, company stages, or assessment behavior.
- No MCP server, external app, authentication, custom UI, or external data collection in this release.
