## Coverage
- Microsoft Advertising: search ads similar to Google, often lower CPCs, older and professional audiences; import Google campaigns.
- Pinterest: planning and inspiration intent, strong for home, fashion, food, crafts and events; keywords matter.
- Snapchat: younger audiences, vertical full-screen ads, playful creative.
- X: conversation and trends, tech and news audiences, keyword and follower targeting.
- Reddit: community targeting by subreddit and interest; honest, native, non-salesy copy.
- Quora: question-based intent targeting.
- Spotify, audio and podcast ads: audio spots and host reads for awareness.
- Connected TV, programmatic display and native ads: reach and brand building, usually for larger budgets.
