## Definitions
- **TAM** (total addressable market): total yearly revenue if every possible customer bought.
- **SAM** (serviceable available market): the part of TAM the business can reach with its product, channels and geography.
- **SOM** (serviceable obtainable market): the realistic share of SAM it can win in a set period (often 3–5 years).

## Step 1: Pick methods (use at least two and compare)
1. **Bottom-up (preferred)**: number of target customers × purchase frequency × average price. Most credible for investors.
2. **Top-down**: total industry size from published sources × share relevant to the offer. Quick, but easy to overstate.
3. **Value theory**: value created for the customer × share the business can capture. Useful for new categories.
