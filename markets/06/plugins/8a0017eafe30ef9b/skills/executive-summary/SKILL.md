## Executive summary formula
1. **Situation**: the context in one or two sentences.
2. **Question**: what needed answering.
3. **Answer**: the main conclusion first.
4. **Key findings**: three to five, each with one supporting number or fact.
5. **Recommendation**: what to do and the expected result.
6. **Next steps and owners**.

## Presentation outline (8–12 slides)
1. Title with the main finding as the headline
2. Executive summary
3. Objectives and method (one slide)
4. Market size and growth
5. Customer insights
6. Competitive landscape
7. Demand and trends
8. Opportunities
9. Recommendations
10. Next steps
Each slide: an action headline, one chart or table, and two to three supporting points.

## Talking points
Give a short script for the presenter and likely questions from leadership with strong answers.

The summary or slide outline in clean format, plus speaker notes on request.
