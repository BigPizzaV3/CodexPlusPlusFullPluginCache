## Step 1: Define what is tested
Concepts, features, names, packaging, prices, ads, landing pages or messages. Set the decision rule before testing (for example "launch the concept with the highest top-two-box purchase intent").

## Step 2: Choose the design
| Design | When to use |
|---|---|
| Monadic (each person sees one option) | Cleanest read, needs larger samples |
| Sequential monadic (each person sees several, rotated) | Smaller samples, watch order bias |
| MaxDiff | Ranking many features or claims |
| A/B test (live) | Ads, pages and emails with real behavior |

## Step 3: Concept board
Headline, the problem, the solution, key benefits, a reason to believe, price (if tested) and an image description. Keep all concepts at equal quality so the test is fair.

## Step 4: Measures
Purchase intent, uniqueness, relevance, believability, likes and dislikes (open), and value for money.

## Step 5: Output
Test plan, concept boards, questionnaire, a results template and a decision summary with the winner and what to improve.
