## SWOT and TOWS
- Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external.
- Make each point specific and evidence-based ("fastest delivery in the region" not "good service").
- Turn SWOT into TOWS actions:
| | Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|---|
| Strengths | SO: use strengths to win opportunities | ST: use strengths to reduce threats |
| Weaknesses | WO: fix weaknesses to capture opportunities | WT: defend against the worst case |
