## What it is
Keyword Insights is a keyword research and topical authority platform. For market research it turns live search and community data into clear demand signals.

## Features that fit market research
- **Keyword Discovery**: thousands of keyword ideas from a seed term, drawn from sources such as autocomplete, "people also ask", forums and Q&A sites, with search volume, keyword difficulty and CPC. Useful for sizing online demand and seeing commercial value.
- **Trending questions**: what people are asking right now, a direct window into customer needs.
- **Keyword Clustering**: groups keywords by search result similarity, showing distinct sub-needs and demand themes.
- **Search Intent**: shows whether results are informational, commercial or transactional, revealing buyer readiness.
- **Competitor Visibility**: benchmarks a site against competitors for a topic and shows content gaps.
- **Content Briefs and AI Writer**: turn research into content plans and articles.
- **Mentions**: finds forum, Q&A and video threads where a brand can join relevant conversations.

## Workflow with this plugin
1. Plan the research here: market definition, personas and seed keywords.
2. Run the seeds through Keyword Discovery and export the results.
3. Cluster the keywords and turn on search intent.
4. Paste or upload the export here to size demand by theme, score opportunities and write the report.
5. Use Competitor Visibility to find gaps, then plan content and positioning here.

## Honest notes
