## Step 1: Foundations from research
Target segment and persona, core problem, positioning statement, competitive alternatives and proof points.

## Step 2: GTM building blocks
1. **Offer**: product, packages, guarantee, onboarding.
2. **Pricing**: model and entry price (link to pricing research).
3. **Channels**: search and content, marketplaces, paid social, partnerships, community, outbound sales, retail, events. Pick two or three primary channels where the target customer already is.
4. **Sales motion**: self-serve, sales-assisted, enterprise sales.
5. **Content and search plan**: topics mapped to buyer questions and search intent across awareness, comparison and purchase stages.
6. **Launch plan**: pre-launch, launch week, post-launch.
7. **Budget split** across channels with test and scale phases.

## Step 3: KPIs
| Stage | KPI examples |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach, impressions, search visibility |
| Interest | Site visits, sign-ups, demo requests |
| Conversion | Conversion rate, CAC |
| Retention | Churn, repeat purchase, LTV |

## Step 4: 30-60-90 day plan
Actions, owners and targets per period.

## Output
A one-page GTM summary, channel plan table, KPI table and the 30-60-90 plan. Keep all marketing claims truthful and within advertising rules.
