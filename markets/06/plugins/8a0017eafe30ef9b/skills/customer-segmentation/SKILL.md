## Step 1: Choose segmentation bases
| Base | Examples |
|---|---|
| Demographic | Age band, income band, household type |
| Geographic | Country, city, urban vs rural, climate |
| Behavioral | Usage level, purchase frequency, loyalty, channel |
| Needs-based | Main problem to solve, desired outcome |
| Psychographic | Values, lifestyle, attitudes |
| Firmographic (B2B) | Industry, company size, revenue band, tech stack, region |

Needs-based and behavioral segments usually predict buying better than demographics alone.

## Step 2: Build segments
Define three to six segments that are distinct, measurable, reachable and large enough to matter.

## Step 4: Select targets
