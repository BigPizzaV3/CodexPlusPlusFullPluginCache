## Why it matters
Forums, Q&A sites, video comments and reviews show unfiltered customer language, real pains and the questions people ask before buying. They also shape what AI assistants and search engines say about a category.

## Step 1: Where to look
Discussion forums and communities, Q&A sites, video comments, product review sites, app store reviews, social media, niche groups and industry communities.

## Step 2: Search method
Search for problem phrases, "best [category]", "[competitor] alternative", "[category] vs", "is [product] worth it", and complaint phrases.

## Step 3: Code the findings
| Theme | Example (paraphrased) | Frequency | Sentiment | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Themes: pains, desired outcomes, objections, feature requests, competitor complaints, price concerns, trust questions.

## Step 4: Turn insight into action
- Product ideas and fixes.
- Content topics that answer frequent questions.
- Messaging using customer language.
- Threads where the brand can genuinely help.

## Rules
Respect community rules. Be transparent when representing a brand, never post fake reviews or pose as a customer, and do not collect personal data about individual users.

## Output
A theme table, top questions list, content and product ideas, and a listening plan with keywords and frequency.
