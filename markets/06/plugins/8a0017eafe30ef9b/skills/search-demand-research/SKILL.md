## Why it matters
Search data shows what people actually want, in their own words, at scale. It is one of the fastest ways to validate demand, spot sub-niches and size online opportunity.

## Step 1: Seed keywords
Build seeds from four angles:
- **Problem**: what the customer struggles with.
- **Solution**: product category names.
- **Brand and competitor** terms.
- **Question** terms (how, what, best, vs, cost, near me).

## Step 2: Expand and measure
Expand seeds into long-tail keywords from autocomplete, "people also ask", forums and Q&A sites. For each keyword capture search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC and trend. CPC is a strong signal of commercial value: advertisers pay more where buyers spend.

## Step 3: Classify intent
| Intent | Signals | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | how, what, guide | Awareness and education demand |
| Commercial | best, review, vs, top | People comparing options |
| Transactional | buy, price, discount | Ready-to-buy demand |
| Navigational | brand names | Brand strength |

## Step 4: Cluster into demand themes
Group keywords that share the same search results into clusters. Each cluster is a sub-need or page opportunity. Sum volume per cluster to see which sub-niches have the most demand.

## Step 5: Read the signals
- Rising trends and seasonality peaks.
- High volume with weak competitor content = opportunity.
- High CPC with transactional intent = strong buyer demand.
- Many questions = education gap to own.

## Step 6: Output
A demand summary, a cluster table (theme, example keywords, estimated volume range, intent, opportunity score), and a verdict on demand strength. If live data is not available in the chat, give the keyword list and method, and label volumes as estimates to confirm with a keyword research tool.
