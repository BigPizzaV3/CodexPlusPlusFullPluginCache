## Step 1: Pick the criteria
Choose 8–15 criteria that buyers actually use to decide: price, core features, ease of use, support, integrations, speed, quality, brand trust, guarantees, delivery.

## Step 2: Weight the criteria
Give each a weight (totaling 100%) based on customer importance, from research or stated assumptions.

## Step 3: Score
| Criteria | Weight | You | Competitor A | Competitor B | Competitor C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Score 1–5 with a short reason for each score. Show weighted totals.

## Step 4: Feature matrix
Use ✓, ✗ and "partial" for a clear side-by-side table of features.

## Step 5: Battlecards (for sales and marketing)
For each competitor:
- When we win, when we lose
- Their main claims and our honest response
- Landmines to set (questions that highlight our strengths)
- Proof points to use

## Step 6: Output
Weighted scorecard, feature matrix, a summary of where the user leads, lags and ties, and battlecards on request. Keep claims factual and fair.
