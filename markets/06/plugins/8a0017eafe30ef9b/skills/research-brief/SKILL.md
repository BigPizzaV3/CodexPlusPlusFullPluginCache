## Step 1: Start from the decision
Write the business decision the research will support in one sentence. Research without a decision becomes a report nobody uses.

## Step 2: Brief structure
1. **Background**: company, product, market and what prompted the research.
2. **Business decision**: what will be decided and by whom.
3. **Research objectives**: three to six clear objectives.
4. **Key questions**: the specific questions under each objective.
5. **Hypotheses**: what the team currently believes, so research can confirm or reject it.
6. **Target audience**: who must be studied, with inclusion and exclusion criteria.
7. **Methods**: secondary research, search demand analysis, surveys, interviews, focus groups, competitor audits, usage data, tests.
8. **Sample**: size and quotas with a reason.
9. **Timeline and budget level**.
10. **Deliverables**: report, dashboard, personas, presentation.
11. **Success criteria**: how the team will know the research was useful.

## Step 3: Choose methods wisely
| Need | Best method |
|---|---|
| How big is the market | Secondary data plus a sizing model |
| What people search for and ask | Search demand and keyword research |
| Why people behave a certain way | Interviews, focus groups |
| How many people think X | Survey |
| What competitors do | Competitor audit and benchmarking |
| Which option wins | Concept or price testing |

## Step 4: Output
Deliver a clean, copy-ready brief with headings and a method table. Offer to write the survey or interview guide next.
