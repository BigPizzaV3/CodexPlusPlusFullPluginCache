## Step 1: Scan for signals
Look across technology, consumer behavior, economy, regulation, demographics, sustainability and culture. Useful signal types: rising search interest, new questions people ask, startup and funding activity, product launches, policy changes and changes in retail shelves.

## Step 2: Test each trend
| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| Driver | What deeper force causes it? |
| Breadth | Is it spreading across segments and regions? |
| Durability | Does it solve a real need, or is it novelty? |
| Adoption stage | Innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority? |
| Evidence | Which signals support it? |

Classify as fad, trend or long-term shift.

## Step 3: Impact
For each trend: impact on demand, customers, competitors and pricing, and the time horizon (now, 1–3 years, 3–5+ years).

## Step 4: Output
A trend radar table (trend, stage, impact, horizon, confidence), a short story for the top three trends, and specific actions: what to build, stop, test or watch. Label forecasts as judgment, not certainty.
