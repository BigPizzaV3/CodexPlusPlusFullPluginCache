## Step 1: Map discovery and buying channels
Where the target customer learns about products (search, social, video, communities, word of mouth, events, publications) and where they buy (own website, marketplaces, retail, wholesale, distributors, direct sales, partners).

## Step 2: Compare channels
| Channel | Reach of target | Cost to enter | Margin impact | CAC estimate | Control | Speed | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Step 3: Channel economics
Show margin after channel fees and estimated CAC per channel, and compare with customer lifetime value. A channel works when LTV is comfortably higher than CAC (a common target is around 3:1).

## Step 4: Prioritize
Pick one or two channels to test first, with budget, success metric and time box, then scale what works.

## Output
Channel map, comparison table, economics summary and a test plan.
