## Step 1: Pick competitors and topics
Choose three to five search competitors (they may differ from business competitors) and the main topics of the market.

## Step 2: Visibility comparison
| Topic cluster | Your coverage | Competitor A | Competitor B | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Mark covered, weak or missing for each.

## Step 3: Content gap analysis
- Topic clusters competitors rank for that the user does not.
- Questions nobody answers well.
- Intent gaps: competitors have guides but no comparisons, or product pages but no education.
- Cannibalization: several of the user's pages competing for the same topic.

## Step 4: Topical authority
Search engines and AI assistants tend to favor brands that cover a topic deeply. Map the full topic into clusters and plan coverage cluster by cluster.

## Step 5: Beyond search
Note where competitors are mentioned in forums, Q&A sites and videos, since these mentions influence AI answers.

## Output
Visibility table, prioritized gap list (topic, intent, demand level, difficulty, priority) and a content plan. Label estimates when live data is not available.
