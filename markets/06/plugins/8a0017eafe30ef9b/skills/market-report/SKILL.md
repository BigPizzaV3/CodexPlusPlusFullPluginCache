## Report structure
1. **Title page and one-line key finding**.
2. **Executive summary**: the question, the answer, three to five key findings, top recommendations.
3. **Objectives and methodology**: sources, methods, sample, dates, limitations.
4. **Market overview**: definition, size, growth, drivers.
5. **Demand analysis**: search demand, trends, seasonality.
6. **Customer analysis**: segments, personas, needs, buying process.
7. **Competitive landscape**: players, positioning, pricing, gaps.
8. **Trends and outlook**: PESTLE forces and scenarios.
9. **SWOT and opportunities**.
10. **Recommendations**: prioritized, with expected impact and next steps.
11. **Appendix**: tables, assumptions, sources, questionnaire.

## Writing rules
- Lead every section with the insight, then the evidence (pyramid principle).
- Use action titles ("Mid-market buyers are underserved on price" not "Pricing").
- Use tables and simple charts; one message per chart.
- Separate fact, estimate and assumption; cite source types for all data.
- Keep the executive summary to one page.
