## Purpose
Turn what the literature leaves unanswered into a clear contribution.

## Types of gaps
- **Evidence gap:** findings conflict.
- **Knowledge gap:** the question has not been studied.
- **Population or context gap:** groups, regions or settings not covered.
- **Methodological gap:** only one design, small samples, self-report, no longitudinal data.
- **Theoretical gap:** findings without explanation, or a theory never tested here.
- **Practical gap:** research not yet applied.
- **Temporal gap:** old data in a changing area.

## Output
| Gap | Type | Evidence (source, page) | Research question | Suggested design |
