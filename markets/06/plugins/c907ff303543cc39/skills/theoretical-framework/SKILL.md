## Purpose
Give the study a solid theoretical backbone.

## Method
1. From the research question, identify the key concepts and the relationships being studied.
2. Suggest 2–4 established theories or models used in that field, each with: core idea, key constructs, typical use, strengths and limits. Name originators only when confident; otherwise mark *verify*.
3. Recommend one (or a justified combination) and show how its constructs map to the study's variables or themes.
4. Draw the conceptual framework as a text diagram: concepts → relationships → outcomes, with moderators and mediators.

## Output
A comparison table of candidate theories, the recommendation, a text diagram, and a draft paragraph justifying the framework for the paper.
