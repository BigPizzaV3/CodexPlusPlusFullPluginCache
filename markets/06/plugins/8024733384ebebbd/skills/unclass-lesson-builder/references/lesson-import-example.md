```page
title: Plan your visit
description: Talk about a day out with a friend.
```

```text
content: |-
  ## Before you go

  Would you like to visit a zoo? Which animal would you most like to see?

  1. Name three animals you know.
  2. Choose one animal and describe it.
  3. Tell your partner what you would take for a day out.

  > Give a reason for each choice. There is more than one good answer.
```

```text
content: |-
  ### Maya and Leo's plan

  Maya and Leo are visiting the zoo on **Saturday**. They will meet at the entrance at **ten o'clock**.

  Maya wants to see the elephants because they have *long trunks*. Leo wants to watch the birds because he enjoys their songs.

  They will bring water and some fruit. At noon, they will take a break at the café.

  | Time | Activity |
  | --- | --- |
  | 10:00 | Meet at the entrance |
  | 10:15 | Visit the elephants |
  | 11:00 | Watch the birds |
  | 12:00 | Take a break at the café |

  Their original meeting place was ~~the café~~ **the entrance**.

  Their meeting-point label is `Zoo entrance`.

  ---

  **Your turn:** Which activity would you change? Explain why.
```
