EM2 Statistics Assignment (Presentation) – Instructions 
You are expected to perform a hypothesis test to investigate a claim (or statement) 
about a population mean found in published media. First, identify the claim, gather 
relevant data, and then carry out a hypothesis test to determine whether there is 
sufficient or insufficient evidence to reject the claim. 
• Respond to all the parts of this instruction sheet. Present all your work on a 
spreadsheet application (using Microsoft Excel – Refer to blank template in LMS). 
• Create a video presentation of up to 4 minutes, explaining all parts of your work 
in the spreadsheet. Contents beyond 4 minutes will not be graded. 
• Record your video presentation in your own voice and upload it to YouTube, 
ensuring that you minimally appear with your face speaking into the video at the 
beginning, and, at the end of the video. 
• Ensure that your YouTube video is unlisted (not private!) and available minimally 
until the end of the current semester. 
 
→ This Statistics Assignment (Presentation) consists of 2 components:  
• Interim Progress Check (4%) 
• Video Presentation (8%) 
i.e. The Statistics Assignment (Presentation) constitutes 12% of your overall EM2 grade

You are expected to:

• State both the null hypothesis (H₀) and alternative hypothesis (H₁). 
• Describe your proposed plan for data collection (and if you have already started, 
share your progress). 
• Must show evidence of how you have made use of the (AI chatbot??) to support

• Be ready to explain and justify your choices / decisions when asked by your tutor. 
This interim check is worth 4% of the module grade. It ensures that you are on the right 
track before completing your final video presentation.

Your video presentation is expected to cover the following: 
Part 1. Identify a Claim 
Select a real-life scenario from an online news portal (e.g. Straits Times, CNA, Today 
Online), online publication (e.g. Mothership, 8days), or any other credible source. 
• The claim must mention a population mean (a numerical value).  
This reported value will serve as the basis for your assignment. 
• Show the source of the claim at the beginning of your video presentation. 
(e.g. screenshot or actual view of the article or webpage, briefly <5 seconds)

Part 2. Define your Hypotheses 
• Clearly state both the null and alternative hypotheses. 
• Explain your reasoning for stating the alternative hypothesis. 
• For this assignment, use a two-tailed test.  
 
Part 3. Collect your Data 
• Obtain a random sample of at least 30 data points (or more). 
• Briefly explain why your sample is suitable for testing the claim. 
• You must collect data yourself. 
(e.g. oral survey, paper-and-pen survey, Google / MS Forms, etc.) 
 
Part 4. Using a spreadsheet application (Microsoft Excel) 
You must show and briefly explain the formulas used for: 
• sample mean and sample standard deviation 
• population standard deviation  
(applicable if your source provides this information) 
• test statistic and p-value 
• margin of error and confidence interval

Part 5. Carry out the Hypothesis test (with conclusion) 
• State whether you are using a t-test or z-test, and explain why. 
• Use your Excel results to conclude if there is sufficient evidence or insufficient 
evidence to reject the claim at a 5% significance level.  
• State your conclusion in a clear and logical manner. 
 
Part 6. Construct and interpret the 95% Confidence Interval 
• Use your earlier calculations to construct the interval. 
• Interpret what this interval says about the claim in a clear and logical manner 
such that someone unfamiliar with statistics would understand.
