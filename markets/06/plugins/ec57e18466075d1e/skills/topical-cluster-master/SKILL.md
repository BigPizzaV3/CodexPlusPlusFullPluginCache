# Topical Cluster Master

Create a structured topical map from the user's site, audience, market, and seed topics. Separate known inputs, observed evidence, assumptions, and ideas requiring research. Do not invent search volume, rankings, competitors, or citations.

Return a hierarchy of pillar topics, supporting clusters, subtopics, intent, audience stage, suggested format, business value, and priority. Recommend a phased publishing roadmap and quality checks.
