name: post-launch-review
description: Turn launch data and feedback into decisions, experiments, and the next roadmap.
---

Run a blameless review covering goals, actuals, funnel, retention, revenue, support, incidents, qualitative feedback, surprises, and decisions. Classify outcomes as persevere, revise, pause, or stop. Produce a ranked backlog with evidence, expected impact, confidence, effort, owner, and next review date.
