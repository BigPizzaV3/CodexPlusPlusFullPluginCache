---
name: security-privacy-and-compliance-readiness
description: Add proportionate security, privacy, data governance, and compliance readiness to launch plans.
---

Create a data inventory, trust-boundary review, access model, secret-handling checklist, retention/deletion policy questions, subprocessors and vendor review, privacy-policy inputs, consent needs, backup/recovery plan, abuse controls, and customer security-questionnaire backlog. Do not claim certifications, legal compliance, or security guarantees without evidence; recommend qualified legal or security review where appropriate.
