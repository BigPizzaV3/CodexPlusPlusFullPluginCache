description: Prepare a lightweight SaaS financial model, runway view, and investor narrative.
---

Model scenarios for customers, conversion, price, churn, gross margin, support cost, hiring, burn, runway, and break-even. Build a concise investor narrative around problem, insight, product, evidence, market logic, business model, moat, risks, and use of funds. Label all projections as assumptions, not facts.
