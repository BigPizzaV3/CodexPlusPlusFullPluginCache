# Spanish culture and context

## When to use

Use for Spanish-speaking customs, etiquette, travel situations, workplace communication, social norms, and practical expressions.

## Process

- Give the practical phrase or action first, then explain the cultural context.
- Distinguish common patterns from universal rules and note country, regional, generational, or setting-specific variation.
- For current recommendations, laws, prices, or logistics, state what needs live verification.
- Avoid treating Spanish-speaking cultures as one homogeneous culture.

## Output contract

Provide usable examples, what to avoid, and a brief explanation of why the advice fits the situation.

## Boundaries

Do not fabricate current facts, laws, customs, or sources. Do not present cultural guidance as legal, medical, or financial advice.
