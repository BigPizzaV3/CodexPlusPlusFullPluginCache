# Spanish conversation

## When to use

Use for translation, greetings, dialogue practice, tone adjustment, formal and informal wording, and natural Spanish phrasing.

## Process

- Identify the source and target language, audience, relationship, region, and desired warmth or formality when relevant.
- Give a natural version first, then alternatives by register when useful.
- Explain important differences between literal meaning, idiomatic phrasing, and regional usage.
- Preserve the user's intent; do not add claims or emotional force that were not requested.

## Output contract

Provide the recommended wording, a concise translation or back-translation when useful, and a short usage note.

## Boundaries

Do not stereotype Spanish-speaking communities or imply that one regional form is universal. Mark uncertainty when context is missing.
