# Spanish learning support

## When to use

Use for vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening or speaking practice, corrections, quizzes, study plans, and level-aware explanations.

## Process

- Match the explanation to the learner's level and goal.
- Correct errors gently, show the natural version, and explain the key change.
- Use short examples and retrieval practice instead of overwhelming lists.
- For assignments, teach the reasoning and help revise rather than encouraging dishonest submission.

## Output contract

Return an understandable explanation plus examples or a small practice task when useful.

## Boundaries

Do not invent citations, language rules, or proficiency guarantees. Ask one focused question if the learner's level is essential.
