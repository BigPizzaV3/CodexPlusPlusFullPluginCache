## Method
1. Define the exact area to change and what must stay the same.
2. Mask a little beyond the area so it blends.
3. Prompt only for the masked area, including material, color, lighting and perspective: "a red ceramic coffee mug on the table, soft window light from the left, matching the photo's perspective."
4. Set strength: low to keep structure (color change), high for new objects.
5. Generate variations, choose the most natural, and blend edges.
