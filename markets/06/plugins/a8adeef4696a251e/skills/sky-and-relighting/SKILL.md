## Method
1. Select the sky cleanly, including around trees and buildings.
2. Choose a sky that matches the sun direction and time of day.
3. Relight the foreground to match: warm for sunset, cool and darker for night, add reflections in water and windows.
4. For relighting a subject, describe the light source, direction, color and softness: "soft warm golden hour light from the right, gentle rim light."
