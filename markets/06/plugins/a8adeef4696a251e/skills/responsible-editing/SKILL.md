## Guidelines
- Consent: edit photos of other people only with their permission, especially for changes to appearance.
- Never create sexual or nude edits of real people, humiliating edits, or deepfakes meant to deceive.
- Copyright: edit your own photos or licensed images; do not remove watermarks, signatures or credits.
- Honesty: disclose heavy edits or AI-generated content where platforms, ads, listings or news require it.
- Documents: never alter IDs, certificates, receipts or official documents.
