## Correction first
1. White balance: set with a neutral gray or white point; skin should look natural, not orange or pink.
2. Exposure and contrast: full tonal range without clipped highlights.
3. HSL: fix problem colors (oversaturated reds, green casts, dull skies).

## Then grading
Choose a look and apply with color grading or split toning: warm highlights and cool shadows (cinematic teal and orange), soft pastel, moody dark, clean bright, film look.
