## Method
1. Check the source: size, compression artifacts, noise, faces.
2. Clean first: reduce JPEG artifacts and noise before upscaling.
3. Choose the upscale: 2x for moderate needs, 4x for print or large displays; use a face-aware or creative upscaler for faces and AI art, a faithful upscaler for photos and products.
4. Keep creativity low for real photos so details are not invented; higher only for art.
5. Final light sharpening for the output size.

## Print guide
Pixels needed = inches x 300 for high-quality print (about 150 to 200 for large posters viewed from a distance).
