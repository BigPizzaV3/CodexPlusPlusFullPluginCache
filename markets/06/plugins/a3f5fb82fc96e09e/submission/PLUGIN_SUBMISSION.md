## Plugin type

## Plugin name
Ohana

## Short description
Turns classroom observations into developmental classifications and warm, family-ready documentation using the Experience Early Learning Continuum and the Our Ohana Village voice.

## Long description
Ohana helps early-childhood educators turn classroom observations, photos, and screenshots into concise developmental documentation. It grounds classification in the Experience Early Learning Continuum and rewrites the result in the warm, direct, family-friendly Our Ohana Village voice. The skill is designed to avoid inventing observations, to verify classifications against the reference continuum, and to keep family-facing notes clear and concise.
