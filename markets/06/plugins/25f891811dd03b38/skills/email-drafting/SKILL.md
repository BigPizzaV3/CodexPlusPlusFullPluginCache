# B2B email drafting

Apply when the user wants a new one-to-one professional email or a revision.

## Inputs

Gather the sender's actual offer, recipient context, reason for contact, credible benefit/evidence, preferred tone, and next step. Ask before making a material claim if these are missing.

## Process

1. Lead with a truthful, relevant reason for this recipient.
2. Explain one concrete value proposition in plain language; avoid unsupported ROI, superlatives, fake urgency, or false scarcity.
3. Keep the message brief, readable, and focused on one low-friction next step.
4. Make subject lines accurate and non-deceptive. Do not disguise advertising as an existing thread or personal relationship.
5. Follow-ups must respect elapsed context and stop if the recipient objects, opts out, or asks not to be contacted.

## Output

Offer a concise subject and body; include optional variants only when useful. Preserve a natural human voice, not formulaic “growth-hack” language.

## Boundaries

No sending or automation. Do not generate deceptive claims, impersonation, coercive pressure, harassment sequences, or language designed to conceal commercial purpose where disclosure is required.
