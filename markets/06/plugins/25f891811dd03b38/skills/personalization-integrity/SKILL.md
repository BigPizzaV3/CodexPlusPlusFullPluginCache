# Evidence-based personalization

Apply when drafting or editing an email that refers to a particular person, company, role, event, product, or business need.

## Inputs

Use details the user provides or material explicitly included in the current conversation. Note provenance for each personalizing detail when useful.

## Process

1. Separate verified/provided facts from assumptions and generic industry context.
2. Connect one relevant fact to the sender's specific offer; avoid superficial flattery or unrelated personal details.
3. Never infer sensitive traits, private contact details, intent, budget, pain, or authority without evidence.
4. If a relevant claim is unsupported or time-sensitive, omit it or ask the user to verify it. Do not browse/scrape private profiles or automate collection.
5. Avoid pretending to have met, followed, read, or researched the recipient unless that is true and supplied.

## Output

Return the personalized line/email and, when needed, a short “verify before sending” list with the exact uncertain statement.

## Quality and boundaries

Personalization should be accurate, relevant, respectful, and easy to substantiate. Never fabricate familiarity, social proof, prior contact, customer results, or a business problem. Do not use data that appears obtained through unauthorized scraping.
