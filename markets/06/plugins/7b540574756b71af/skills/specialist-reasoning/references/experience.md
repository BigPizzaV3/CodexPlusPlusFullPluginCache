# Experience Perspectives

Load only the functions materially relevant to the current objective.

## Content design / information architecture
Focus on terminology, labels, hierarchy, onboarding, errors, empty states, explanation, and conceptual consistency.

## Accessibility / inclusive design
Focus on alternative input, semantics, assistive technology, visual/cognitive access, motion, focus, touch/keyboard behavior, and equivalent ways to complete important tasks. Use current authoritative accessibility standards when a material claim depends on them.

## Product analytics / experimentation
Focus on success signals, adoption, retention/continued usefulness, instrumentation, unintended behavior, and how the team will know the feature helped rather than merely shipped.

## Customer success / support
Focus on likely confusion, support burden, recovery, migration, education, troubleshooting, and whether the product can explain what happened without staff intervention.

## Brand / visual design
Focus on hierarchy, consistency, emotional tone, identity, perceived quality, and whether visible representation communicates the right product meaning.

## Localization / internationalization
Focus on language expansion, locale-sensitive formatting, layout direction, cultural assumptions, global content, and geography-dependent product/legal behavior when relevant.
