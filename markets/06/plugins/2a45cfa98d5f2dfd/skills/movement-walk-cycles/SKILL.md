## Steps
1. Identify the action and the character's weight, mood and personality (a proud walk differs from a sneaky walk).
2. Give key poses (contact, down, passing, up for walks), frame counts (for example a 12 or 16 frame walk cycle at 24fps), and spacing notes.
3. Add personality: arm swing, head bob, hip and shoulder rotation, overlap on hair and clothes.
4. For AI video, write movement wording that keeps motion natural and describe one clear action per shot to reduce warping.
5. Suggest reference to film yourself or study.
