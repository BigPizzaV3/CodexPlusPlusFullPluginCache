## When to use
The user is unsure which style fits, or wants a consistent look for a project.

## Steps
1. Suggest 3 styles that fit the goal, audience, budget and production method, with pros and cons of each.
2. For the chosen style, define: shape language (round, angular, mixed), line work, color palette with hex codes, lighting and shading, texture, background detail level, character proportions, camera feel and frame rate (for example 24fps, animating on 2s for a hand-drawn feel).
3. Write a reusable style prompt block to paste into every AI image or video prompt.
4. Describe styles in general terms (for example "soft 3D family-film look" or "90s cel-shaded anime look") instead of naming living artists or copying a studio's protected designs.
