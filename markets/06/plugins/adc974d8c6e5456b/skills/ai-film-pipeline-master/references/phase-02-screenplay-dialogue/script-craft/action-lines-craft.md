# Action Lines

4 cards. Writing what is seen without deciding how it is shot. The boundary between the script and the shot plan lives here.

Every card carries what it is, what it does to the audience, where it works best, and what to avoid.

---

---
### Observable Only

**Also called:** the camera test, no unfilmable interiority  
**What it is:** An action line describes only what a camera could record. What a character thinks, remembers or feels is written as what they do.  
**Effect on the audience:** A generative model, and a crew, can only render what is described. An interior state written as interior state returns either nothing or an invention.  
**Used for and where it works best:** Every action line in every script type, and absolutely in generative work, where there is no actor to translate an interior note into a performance.  
**Best in:** formats: Feature Film, Drama Series, Vertical Short-Form (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), Documentary Feature, Heritage / Historical Documentary | genres: Drama, Historical / Biopic, Heritage (civilisation-focused), Action, Documentary  
**Avoid when:** Never in the script. Interior state belongs in the story document, where it drives the choice of what is shown.  
**Example:** > ❌ "He remembers his father."
> ✅ "He stops. His hand stays on the stone longer than it needs to."  
`act.observable_only`

### No Camera in the Script

**Also called:** the director's boundary  
**What it is:** The action line says what happens; it does not say how it is shot. No shot sizes, no angles, no lenses, no transitions.  
**Effect on the audience:** Two documents deciding the same thing means one of them is wrong and nobody knows which. The shot plan owns the frame, and it needs the action to be free.  
**Used for and where it works best:** Every script. The discipline also improves the writing: forced to describe the action rather than the coverage, the writer finds what actually matters in the moment.  
**Best in:** formats: Feature Film, Drama Series, Vertical Short-Form (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), Documentary Feature, Heritage / Historical Documentary | genres: Drama, Historical / Biopic, Heritage (civilisation-focused), Action, Documentary  
**Avoid when:** The camera move is the event itself and the story does not exist without it, which is rare and always worth arguing about.  
**Example:** > ❌ "CLOSE ON the hand, low angle, 50mm."
> ✅ "His hand finds the carved line and follows it."  
`act.no_camera_in_script`

### One Step Per Line

**Also called:** the advancing line  
**What it is:** Each action line moves the scene exactly one step. A line that carries three events is three lines.  
**Effect on the audience:** A packed line is read at the speed of one line and shot as one shot, and two of its three events disappear.  
**Used for and where it works best:** All script types, most strictly in short form where every line will become a separate generated clip.  
**Best in:** formats: Feature Film, Drama Series, Vertical Short-Form (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), Documentary Feature, Heritage / Historical Documentary | genres: Drama, Historical / Biopic, Heritage (civilisation-focused), Action, Documentary  
**Avoid when:** The three events are genuinely simultaneous and the simultaneity is the point.  
**Example:**
> ❌ "He kicks the bronze door open, draws his iron blade and rushes straight into the dark corridor."
> ✅ "He kicks the bronze door open.
> It slams against stone.
> He draws his iron blade.
> The dark corridor waits."  
`act.one_step`

### Present Tense, Active

**Also called:** the screenplay present  
**What it is:** Action is written in present tense, active voice, as it happens.  
**Effect on the audience:** Present tense puts the reader in the moment rather than in a report of it, which is what the finished piece will do to the viewer.  
**Used for and where it works best:** Every script type.  
**Best in:** formats: Feature Film, Drama Series, Vertical Short-Form (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), Documentary Feature, Heritage / Historical Documentary | genres: Drama, Historical / Biopic, Heritage (civilisation-focused), Action, Documentary  
**Avoid when:** Never in a script. Past tense belongs to the treatment and the story document.  
**Example:**
> ❌ "The chariot was being driven recklessly by the warrior while arrows were flying around him."
> ✅ "The warrior lashes the four horses. Chariot wheels tear through mud. Arrows hiss past his bronze helmet."  
`act.present_active`
