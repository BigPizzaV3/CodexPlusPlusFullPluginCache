## Part III. Babylon

_The dress of the cities of the middle land, from Old Babylonian Hammurabi through the Kassite and Neo-Babylonian ages. Its signature is the long, smoothly draped robe of wool or fine linen, often pleated, fringed and bound with a tasseled cord, worn with the rounded brimmed cap of the king and a long combed beard. Two hundred and fifty garments follow._


### Babylon: Infants and Young Children, Birth to Four (Nos. 1 to 15)

**1. Newborn's Wool Swaddle.** _(Common.)_ An undyed sheep-wool band wound in a spiral so only the face is free, tucked and tied with a wool cord. The common newborn wrap of the Babylonian towns.

**2. Wealthy-Household Linen Swaddle.** _(Wealthy.)_ A soft bleached linen band with a thin fringed edge, a flat carnelian bead at the throat, for the child of a prosperous Babylonian house.

**3. Poor Riverside Reed Swaddle.** _(Poor.)_ Coarse undyed wool and reused cloth bound with twisted reed cord, bare-headed, the wrap of the poor families by the Euphrates.

**4. Lambswool Winter Bundle.** _(Cold season.)_ Carded lambswool over an inner linen band, bound with a felted wool sash in cream and brown, warming the swaddled infant through the cold.

**5. Lamashtu-Guarded Swaddle.** _(Protective.)_ A plain wool swaddle with a small clay or bronze amulet of Pazuzu knotted into the chest cord, set to turn away Lamashtu, the demoness who steals newborns.

**6. Crawling Infant's Half-Swaddle.** _(Six to twelve months.)_ The arms freed while the lower body stays wrapped, a short undyed wool wrap crossing the chest and tied, for the crawling child.

**7. Infant's Linen Shift.** _(Everyday.)_ A tiny sleeveless linen shift to the knees, undyed with a faint edge-stripe, gathered at the neck with a flax drawstring.

**8. Fringe-Edged Infant Wrap.** _(Modest means.)_ A small wrap finished with a thin fringe along the edge, in the Babylonian love of the fringed border, soft and warm.

**9. Felt Bonnet and Body Wrap.** _(Winter infant.)_ A rounded wool-felt bonnet tied under the chin with a layered wool body wrap in grey-brown tones, for the cold months.

**10. Toddler's Short Tunic.** _(Everyday toddler.)_ A plain knee-length sleeveless wool tunic belted with a woven cord, the child barefoot. The everyday dress of the small Babylonian child.

**11. Toddler's Pleated Linen Tunic.** _(Well-off child.)_ A small linen tunic with soft pleats and a fine edge, in the pleated Babylonian fashion, a carnelian bead at the neck.

**12. Toddler's Bordered Wrap.** _(Comfortable means.)_ A wool wrap with a woven colored border and a thin cord belt, the head shaved with a single side-lock.

**13. Little Girl's Beaded Wrap.** _(Girl, festival.)_ A soft wool wrap pinned over one shoulder with a short string of faience and carnelian beads, for a small girl at a feast.

**14. Little Boy's Fringed Kilt.** _(Boy, everyday.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a fringed edge and a cord belt, the chest bare, the head shaved with a side-lock.

**15. Marduk-Festival Child's Robe.** _(Wealthy child, Akitu.)_ A soft pale linen tunic with a woven saffron and blue border and a beaded carnelian collar, a fillet, for a wealthy child in the New Year procession of Marduk.

### Babylon: Children and Youth, Four to Eighteen (Nos. 16 to 35)

**16. Boy's Everyday Kilt.** _(Boy, 4 to 7.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a cord belt, the chest and feet bare, the head shaved with a side-lock. The small Babylonian boy at play.

**17. Girl's Wrapped Pinafore.** _(Girl, 4 to 7.)_ A length of wool wrapped and pinned over one shoulder, cream with a thin stripe, simple clay beads at the neck.

**18. Boy's Fringed Tunic.** _(Boy, 7 to 10.)_ A knee tunic with a fringed hem and a woven belt, running the lanes and learning the trade, plaited sandals.

**19. Girl's Beaded Festival Tunic.** _(Girl, 7 to 10.)_ A linen tunic dyed pale saffron, a collar of faience and carnelian, a beaded fillet, for a feast day.

**20. Herder-Boy's Fleece Wrap.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A coarse wool tunic under a small fleece wrap pinned at one shoulder, a cord belt and a staff, the son of a herding family.

**21. Scribe-Student Boy's Tunic.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A clean linen tunic, a tablet and stylus, the hair cropped, copying the laws and the omens in the tablet house of Babylon.

**22. Girl's Wool Day Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A simple ankle gown wrapped and belted, the hair bound back, helping the women at loom and hearth.

**23. Weaver-Girl's Practical Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, learning the craft of cloth.

**24. Youth's First Draped Robe.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A fringed wool robe taken up over a tunic and draped about the body, the young man assuming the adult Babylonian style, a first short beard.

**25. Young Woman's Draped Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ A softly draped ankle gown with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, a beaded necklace, the hair dressed, near the age of marriage.

**26. Noble Boy's Pleated Tunic.** _(Boy, wealthy, 7 to 10.)_ A fine pleated linen tunic with a woven border and a fringed shawl, a carnelian bead and a fillet, the son of a great house.

**27. Noble Girl's Fringed Gown.** _(Girl, wealthy, 10 to 15.)_ A fine fringed gown with a draped shawl, gold ear-studs and a lapis necklace, a daughter of standing.

**28. Poor Boy's Patched Kilt.** _(Boy, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A worn and patched wool kilt with a cord belt, the feet bare, carrying water and minding the goats by the river.

**29. Poor Girl's Worn Shift.** _(Girl, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A thin much-washed wool shift, no jewelry but a single clay bead, helping her mother at the well.

**30. Temple-Novice Youth's Robe.** _(Youth, 15 to 18.)_ A plain undyed linen robe, the head shaven, learning the washings and the rites in the great temple of Marduk, the Esagila.

**31. Young Hunter's Tucked Kilt.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A kilt hitched and tucked high for the chase, a sling and a short spear, the fit young man at the river thickets.

**32. Bride-to-Be's Adorned Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ A dyed draped gown with a beaded carnelian collar and a fillet, the hair oiled and scented, prepared for her betrothal.

**33. Apprentice Scribe at the Tablet House.** _(Boy, 12 to 16.)_ A clean linen tunic, a satchel of styluses and a damp tablet, learning contracts, letters and law in the busy city of clerks.

**34. Girl Musician's Festival Dress.** _(Girl, 12 to 16.)_ A decorated tunic for the temple song, a small lyre, a fillet and beads, singing at the feast of the goddess.

**35. Merchant's-Son Youth's Robe.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A clean draped robe with a thin red border and a cylinder seal at the neck, learning the accounts and the trade of a Babylonian merchant house.

### Babylon: Men by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 36 to 80)

**36. Young Man's Draped Fringed Robe.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ A wool robe wound smoothly about the body with the fringed edge spiraling across it, a trimmed beard. The everyday dress of a young Babylonian townsman.

**37. Young Man's Plain Wrapped Kilt.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain knee kilt and a cord belt, the chest bare, hitched for movement at the work of the yard and the field.

**38. Bridegroom's Pleated Linen Robe.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A fresh pleated linen robe with a fringed shawl, a fillet on oiled hair, new sandals and a ring, for the marriage feast.

**39. Young Field-Worker's Short Kilt.** _(18 to 25, labor.)_ A short coarse wool kilt, a twisted-cord belt and bare feet, working the canals and the barley under the sun.

**40. Summer Linen Wrap.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light undyed linen wrap knotted at the waist, the shoulders bare and oiled, cool for the long Babylonian summer.

**41. Young Man's Akitu Festival Robe.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A draped robe dyed and bordered with a tasseled edge and a bronze armlet, the beard curled, bright in the New Year procession of Marduk.

**42. Young Soldier's Belted Tunic.** _(18 to 25, military.)_ A knee tunic and a wide leather belt, a helmet and a spear, a young man of the Babylonian levy.

**43. Young Merchant's Travel Robe.** _(18 to 25, trade.)_ A neat belted robe and a dust-cloak, a seal and a tablet-pouch, learning the long Euphrates trade of the merchant city.

**44. Prime Man's Long Draped Robe.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A full robe to the calf, the fringed edge wound across the body and over one shoulder, soberly belted, the beard grown full.

**45. Spiral-Fringed Wrapped Robe.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A long robe whose deep fringe spirals from hem to shoulder as the cloth is wound around the body, the formal Babylonian dress of standing.

**46. Father's Work Kilt and Apron-Sash.** _(25 to 35, labor.)_ A sturdy kilt with a thick folded apron-sash for tools and grain, the sleeves of an under-shirt pushed back.

**47. Winter Wool Mantle and Cap.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A heavy fulled-wool mantle over a long tunic, a rounded felt cap, the folds deep against the cold river wind.

**48. Festival Bordered Robe.** _(25 to 35, festival.)_ A robe with a woven bordered hem and a tasseled sash, an armlet and a cup, bright among the men at the feast.

**49. Temple Offering Robe.** _(25 to 35, devotional.)_ A clean fringed robe and a folded shawl, the beard combed, bringing an offering to Marduk in the Esagila.

**50. Soldier's Campaign Dress.** _(25 to 35, military.)_ A belted war tunic, a helmet and a shield, a spear and a dagger, marching in the army of the Babylonian king.

**51. Caravan Merchant's Robe.** _(25 to 35, trade.)_ A long belted robe under a dust-cloak, a seal and a heavy purse, a tablet of accounts, trading up and down the great river.

**52. Mature Man's Heavy Draped Robe.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full heavy draped robe with a fringed edge, soberly belted, the beard streaked and squared. The dress of a settled head of household.

**53. Full Spiral-Fringed Robe, Brimmed Cap.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ The deep-fringed robe wound full about the body and a rounded brimmed cap, in the manner of the image of Hammurabi the law-giver.

**54. Master Craftsman's Worn Kilt.** _(35 to 45, work.)_ A practical kilt softened by years, a thick belt holding a knife and a seal, a felt skullcap. An experienced man at his bench.

**55. Banquet Pleated Robe.** _(35 to 45, feast.)_ A robe of fine pleated linen with a fringed shawl, a cup in hand, reclining among the men at the banquet.

**56. Summer Linen Robe.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose light linen robe to the calf, open at the throat and undyed, plain sandals, cool for middle life in the heat.

**57. Winter Layered Wool.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A long wool tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a felt cap and wrapped hands, fastened with a bronze pin.

**58. Officer's Distinguished Robe.** _(35 to 45, military.)_ A finer fringed robe with a marked belt under light armor, a crested helmet and a sword of office, commanding men in the field.

**59. Mourning Sack-Wool Wrap.** _(35 to 45, grief.)_ A coarse undyed sack-wool wrap, the chest bare and dusted with ash, the beard untrimmed, mourning a wife or a child.

**60. Senior Man's Sober Robe.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A long draped robe in muted tones, a plain belt, the beard mostly grey and combed long, the bearing settled.

**61. Elder's Fringed Robe at the Assembly.** _(45 to 55, standing.)_ A full fringed robe and a smooth staff, sitting among the older men who judge the city's disputes at the gate.

**62. Old Master's Hearthside Robe.** _(45 to 55, work.)_ A softened robe and a shawl about the shoulders, a felt cap, teaching the craft now more by word than by hand.

**63. Banquet Robe of an Elder of Means.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A fine bordered robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet and a cup, reclining at the feast, the hair and beard silvered.

**64. Senior Traveler's Heavy Robe.** _(45 to 55, road.)_ A long robe under a thick hooded cloak, a strong staff, wrapped shins and stout boots, moving slower now between the cities.

**65. Winter Fleece Robe and Cap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A long tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a deep felt cap over the ears, the hands tucked by the hearth.

**66. Devotional Robe, Grey Beard.** _(45 to 55, temple.)_ A clean fringed robe and a folded shawl, the grey beard combed, standing long in prayer before Marduk.

**67. White-Bearded Elder's Robe.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A long warm draped robe, a soft belt, a smooth staff, the beard long and white. The daily dress of a venerable old man.

**68. Counselor's Fringed Robe at the Gate.** _(55 to 65, standing.)_ A long fringed robe over a full tunic, seated among the city elders who advise and judge, the staff across the knees.

**69. Honored Banquet Seat Robe.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A fine soft robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet, a cushioned seat served by the young, a thin fillet on the silvered head.

**70. Elder's Staff-and-Shawl Dress.** _(55 to 65, walking.)_ A warm wrapped robe and a shawl over the shoulders, leaning on a smooth staff, moving slowly through the lanes.

**71. Winter Heavy Fleece Robe.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A thick fleece-lined robe and a deep felt cap, a wrap over the knees by the hearth, the hands tucked.

**72. Devotional Robe of the Aged.** _(55 to 65, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated long before the god, the prayers worn smooth by a lifetime of feast days.

**73. Grief Wrap of an Elder.** _(55 to 65, mourning.)_ A coarse wrap over thin shoulders, ash on the white head, the fine robe set aside, mourning those gone before.

**74. Frail Elder's Warm Robe.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm robe worn loose, a shawl always over the thin shoulders, a strong staff and a steadying hand.

**75. Sage's Robe in the Place of Honor.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft robe and a long fringed shawl, seated in highest honor among the elders, the white beard reaching the chest.

**76. Patriarch's Banquet Robe, Supported.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm robe and a folded shawl, cushioned and supported at the feast, a cup held for him by a grandchild, a thin fillet on the white head.

**77. Hearthside Frail Robe.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft robe easy to put on, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide shoes, seated by the hearth and tended by the family.

**78. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece robe and a thick blanket-mantle, a deep felt cap and covered hands, close by the fire.

**79. Devotional Robe of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated and supported before the god, prayers murmured from long habit and deep peace.

**80. Mourning of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse wrap drawn over the bowed white head, ash and dust, mourning the last of his own generation, upheld by the young.

### Babylon: Women by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 81 to 125)

**81. Young Woman's Draped Wool Gown.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ A softly draped ankle gown with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, belted, the hair bound back. The daily dress of a young Babylonian woman.

**82. Fringed Shawl over Pleated Gown.** _(18 to 25, formal.)_ A pleated linen gown beneath a fringed wool shawl drawn over the shoulders, hoop earrings and a strung necklace, for the temple and the feast.

**83. Bride's Adorned Gown.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A dyed draped gown with a beaded collar of carnelian and lapis, a thin fillet and heavy earrings, the hair oiled and scented, for the marriage feast.

**84. Young Wife's Nursing Gown.** _(18 to 25, motherhood.)_ A loose draped gown of soft wool, easily opened at the breast, belted high, a Pazuzu amulet at the shoulder to guard the infant.

**85. Summer Linen Sheath.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light sleeveless linen sheath to the ankle, undyed and cool, a thin beaded cord at the neck, the hair tied up against the heat.

**86. Festival Adornment.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A brightly dyed gown, a tasseled shawl and the full jewelry of faience, carnelian and gold, the eyes kohl-lined, anklets ringing in the Akitu.

**87. Temple Worshipper's Gown.** _(18 to 25, devotional.)_ A modest gown of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, simple beads and clasped hands, before Marduk in the Esagila.

**88. Young Weaver-Woman's Gown.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, the fingers stained with dye at the loom.

**89. Matron's Long Draped Gown.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A full ankle gown softly draped and belted, a fringed shawl over one shoulder, the hair under a plain fillet. The dress of a settled wife and mother.

**90. Fringed Shawl and Bead Collar.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A pleated gown under a fringed shawl, a broad collar of carnelian and lapis, hoop earrings, for the feast and the rite.

**91. Household Manager's Gown with Seal.** _(25 to 35, authority.)_ A belted gown of good wool from whose waist hang a pouch and a cylinder seal, the woman who keeps the household's accounts in this city of clerks.

**92. Mother's Gown with Carrying Sling.** _(25 to 35, motherhood.)_ A practical wool gown belted high, a wide woven sling for a small child, a protective amulet pinned at the shoulder.

**93. Festival Matron's Adornment.** _(25 to 35, festival.)_ A richly dyed gown layered with necklaces of carnelian, faience and gold, heavy earrings and a thin diadem, kohl-lined eyes, a tasseled shawl.

**94. Summer Linen Matron's Sheath.** _(25 to 35, hot season.)_ A straight sleeveless linen gown to the ankle, undyed and cool, a folded shawl for the temple, a single beaded necklace.

**95. Winter Wool-and-Felt Layers.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A long wool gown under a thick felted mantle drawn over head and shoulders, a felt-lined wrap at the throat, in deep browns.

**96. Mourning Gown of Coarse Wool.** _(25 to 35, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, the jewelry set aside, the hair loosed and dusted with ash, the cloth torn at the breast.

**97. Senior Matron's Full Gown.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full, modestly cut gown of good wool to the floor, softly belted, the greying hair under a fillet and a light shawl.

**98. Large Fringed Shawl of Standing.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ A long pleated gown under a great fringed shawl over head and shoulders, several heavy necklaces and broad earrings, a matron of substance.

**99. Keeper of the Stores Gown.** _(35 to 45, authority.)_ A practical good-wool gown with a heavy belt bearing tools, a seal and a pouch, the sleeves bound for oversight of a large house.

**100. Grandmother's Practical Gown.** _(35 to 45, daily care.)_ A soft, easy gown belted high, a shawl always at the shoulder for a grandchild, an old amulet at the breast.

**101. Festival Display of a Matron of Means.** _(35 to 45, festival.)_ A deeply dyed gown layered with gold and carnelian, a broad collar, a diadem over a fringed shawl, scented oils and kohl.

**102. Summer Linen Gown of Middle Life.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose ankle-length linen gown, undyed and cool, modest at the neck, a single strand of beads, the hair bound up.

**103. Winter Layered Wool and Felt.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle drawn close over head and shoulders, fleece-lined slippers, a bronze pin at the throat.

**104. Temple Votary's Fine Robe.** _(35 to 45, devotional.)_ A robe of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, modest jewelry and clasped hands, long practiced in the temple's prayers.

**105. Elder Wife's Full Modest Gown.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A full floor-length gown of soft good wool, soberly belted, the greying hair bound and a light shawl over it.

**106. Matriarch's Fringed Mantle.** _(45 to 55, formal.)_ A long gown beneath a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, heavy old necklaces from her youth, broad earrings.

**107. Mistress of the Household Gown.** _(45 to 55, authority.)_ A good-wool gown with a heavy belt of keys, seal and pouch, commanding the stores and the younger women of the house.

**108. Banquet Adornment of an Elder Lady.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A deep-dyed gown with layered gold and carnelian, a diadem over a fine shawl, scented and kohl-lined, honored at the feast.

**109. Summer Elder Gown.** _(45 to 55, hot season.)_ A loose undyed linen gown, modest and cool, a thin shawl for the temple, the hair bound up off the neck.

**110. Winter Wool-Felt Elder Wrap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle close about head and shoulders, fleece slippers, a pin at the throat, by the hearth.

**111. Mourning Gown of an Elder Woman.** _(45 to 55, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, jewelry removed, the white hair loosed and ashed, the cloth rent. Grave and dignified grief.

**112. Venerable Grandmother's Gown.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A full soft floor-length gown, soberly belted, a shawl over the white hair, a slow and gentle bearing.

**113. Matriarch's Heavy Mantle.** _(55 to 65, formal.)_ A long gown under a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, the old gold of a lifetime at her ears and throat.

**114. Eldest Woman of the House.** _(55 to 65, authority.)_ A good-wool gown, the keys now passed on yet still consulted, a shawl and a staff, seated where the family gathers.

**115. Banquet Robe of an Aged Lady.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A deep-dyed soft gown, the heirloom gold and carnelian, a thin diadem over a fine shawl, seated in honor at the feast.

**116. Grandmother by the Hearth.** _(55 to 65, daily care.)_ A soft warm gown belted high, a child often in her lap, a shawl and an old amulet, slippers of fleece.

**117. Winter Fleece-Wrapped Elder.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A heavy gown under a felted mantle, a fleece wrap over the knees, slippers and a pin at the throat, by the fire.

**118. Aged Votary's Quiet Robe.** _(55 to 65, devotional.)_ A fine pale robe and a soft shawl, seated long in the temple's calm, a lifetime of prayer behind her.

**119. Frail Grandmother's Warm Gown.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm gown worn loose, a shawl ever over the thin shoulders and white hair, a staff and a steadying arm.

**120. Most-Honored Matriarch's Mantle.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft gown under a great soft mantle over head and shoulders, the heirloom gold light on thin skin, seated in highest honor.

**121. Banquet Honor-Seat Gown.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm gown, the family's oldest gold and a thin diadem over a soft shawl, cushioned and tended at the feast.

**122. Hearthside Frail Gown.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft gown easy to wear, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide slippers, seated by the fire with a great-grandchild near.

**123. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder Woman.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece gown and a thick blanket-mantle, a felt hood and covered hands, close by the hearth.

**124. Mourning of the Very Old Woman.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse gown, all gold set aside, the white hair loosed and ashed, mourning the last companions of her years.

**125. Last Votary's Gown.** _(65 to 80, devotional.)_ A fine soft pale gown and a gentle shawl, seated and supported in the temple's calm, a long life of prayer at its close.
