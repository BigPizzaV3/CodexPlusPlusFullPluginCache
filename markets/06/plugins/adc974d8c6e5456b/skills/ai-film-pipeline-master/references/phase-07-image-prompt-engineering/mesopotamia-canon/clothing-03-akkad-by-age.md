## Part II. Akkad

_The dress of the first empire, the Akkadians of Sargon and Naram-Sin, who ruled from Akkad over Sumer and the lands beyond. Its signature is the heavy woven mantle wrapped about the body and drawn over the left shoulder, baring the right arm and chest, its edge weighted with deep fringe, as carved on the victory steles, worn with a full oiled beard and a confident, muscular bearing. Two hundred and fifty garments follow._


### Akkad: Infants and Young Children, Birth to Four (Nos. 1 to 15)

**1. Newborn's Wool Swaddle.** _(Common, Sargonic.)_ An undyed sheep-wool band wound in a spiral so only the face is free, tucked and tied with a wool cord. The common newborn wrap of the Akkadian towns.

**2. Officer-Family Linen Swaddle.** _(Wealthy, military house.)_ A soft bleached linen band with a thin fringed edge, a flat carnelian bead at the throat, for the child of an officer of the imperial army.

**3. Pasture-Family Reed Swaddle.** _(Poor, herders.)_ Coarse undyed wool and reused cloth bound with twisted reed cord, bare-headed, the wrap of the poor herding families of the open country.

**4. Lambswool Winter Bundle.** _(Cold season.)_ Carded lambswool over an inner linen band, bound with a felted wool sash in cream and brown, warming the swaddled infant through the cold.

**5. Bead-Guarded Newborn Wrap.** _(Protective.)_ A plain wool swaddle with a string of faience and cowrie beads knotted into the chest cord to guard the newborn against the night demons.

**6. Crawling Infant's Half-Swaddle.** _(Six to twelve months.)_ The arms freed while the lower body stays wrapped, a short undyed wool wrap crossing the chest and tied, for the crawling child.

**7. Infant's Linen Shift.** _(Everyday.)_ A tiny sleeveless linen shift to the knees, undyed with a faint edge-stripe, gathered at the neck with a flax drawstring.

**8. Fringe-Edged Infant Wrap.** _(Modest means.)_ A small wrap finished with a thin fringe along the edge, marking even the infant with the Akkadian love of the fringed border.

**9. Felt Bonnet and Body Wrap.** _(Winter infant.)_ A rounded wool-felt bonnet tied under the chin with a layered wool body wrap in grey-brown tones, for the cold months.

**10. Toddler's Short Tunic.** _(Everyday toddler.)_ A plain knee-length sleeveless wool tunic belted with a woven cord, the child barefoot. The everyday dress of the small Akkadian child.

**11. Toddler's Fringed Bare-Shoulder Wrap.** _(Well-off child.)_ A small fringed wrap drawn over one shoulder leaving the other bare, in the adult Akkadian manner, a carnelian bead at the neck.

**12. Toddler's Bordered Linen Tunic.** _(Comfortable means.)_ A linen tunic with a woven colored border and a thin cord belt, the head shaved with a single side-lock.

**13. Little Girl's Beaded Wrap.** _(Girl, festival.)_ A soft wool wrap pinned over one shoulder with a short string of faience and carnelian beads, for a small Akkadian girl at a feast.

**14. Little Boy's Fringed Kilt.** _(Boy, everyday.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a fringed edge and a cord belt, the chest bare, the head shaved with a side-lock.

**15. Imperial-City Child's Festival Robe.** _(Wealthy child, Agade.)_ A soft pale linen tunic with a woven saffron and red border and a beaded carnelian collar, a thin fillet, for a wealthy child in the capital's procession.

### Akkad: Children and Youth, Four to Eighteen (Nos. 16 to 35)

**16. Boy's Everyday Kilt.** _(Boy, 4 to 7.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a cord belt, the chest and feet bare, the head shaved with a side-lock. The small Akkadian boy at play.

**17. Girl's Wrapped Pinafore.** _(Girl, 4 to 7.)_ A length of wool wrapped and pinned over one shoulder, cream with a thin stripe, simple clay beads at the neck.

**18. Boy's Fringed Tunic.** _(Boy, 7 to 10.)_ A knee tunic with a fringed hem and a woven belt, learning the herd or the workshop, plaited sandals.

**19. Girl's Beaded Festival Tunic.** _(Girl, 7 to 10.)_ A linen tunic dyed pale saffron, a collar of faience and carnelian, a beaded fillet, for a feast day.

**20. Herder-Boy's Fleece Wrap.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A coarse wool tunic under a small fleece wrap pinned at one shoulder, a cord belt and a staff, the son of a herding family.

**21. Scribe-Student Boy's Tunic.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A clean linen tunic, a tablet and stylus, the hair cropped, learning to write the Akkadian tongue in the wedge-signs.

**22. Girl's Wool Day Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A simple ankle gown wrapped and belted, the hair bound back, helping the women at loom and hearth.

**23. Weaver-Girl's Practical Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, learning the craft of cloth.

**24. Youth's First Fringed Mantle.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A fringed wool mantle taken up over a tunic and drawn across one shoulder, the young man assuming the adult Akkadian drape, a first short beard.

**25. Young Woman's Wrapped Fringed Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ An ankle gown of fringed wool drawn over one shoulder and belted, a beaded necklace, the hair dressed, near the age of marriage.

**26. Noble Boy's Bordered Tunic.** _(Boy, wealthy, 7 to 10.)_ A fine tunic with a woven border and a fringed shawl, a carnelian bead and a fillet, the son of a great house of the empire.

**27. Noble Girl's Fringed Gown.** _(Girl, wealthy, 10 to 15.)_ A fine fringed gown leaving one shoulder draped, gold ear-studs and a lapis necklace, a daughter of standing.

**28. Poor Boy's Patched Kilt.** _(Boy, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A worn and patched wool kilt with a cord belt, the feet bare, carrying water and minding the goats.

**29. Poor Girl's Worn Shift.** _(Girl, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A thin much-washed wool shift, no jewelry but a single clay bead, helping her mother at the well and the hearth.

**30. Young Soldier-Recruit's Kilt.** _(Youth, 15 to 18.)_ A belted wool tunic and a leather cap, a wooden practice-spear, a boy drilling for the famed army of the Akkadian kings.

**31. Young Hunter's Tucked Kilt.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A kilt hitched and tucked high for the chase, a sling and a short spear, the fit young man at the river thickets.

**32. Bride-to-Be's Adorned Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ A dyed fringed gown with a beaded carnelian collar and a fillet, the hair oiled and scented, prepared for her betrothal.

**33. Apprentice Smith-Boy's Apron.** _(Boy, 12 to 16.)_ A short tunic under a scorched-leather apron, the forearms wrapped, learning to cast and hammer bronze beside the master.

**34. Girl Musician's Festival Dress.** _(Girl, 12 to 16.)_ A decorated tunic for the temple song, a small lyre, a fillet and beads, singing at the feast of the goddess.

**35. Youth at the Garrison.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A plain belted tunic and a leather cap, learning the spear and the march among the soldiers of the imperial garrison.

### Akkad: Men by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 36 to 80)

**36. Young Man's Fringed Mantle.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ A fringed wool mantle wound about the body and drawn over the left shoulder, the right arm and chest bare, the Akkadian everyday dress of a young townsman.

**37. Young Man's Plain Wrapped Kilt.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain knee kilt and a cord belt, the chest bare, hitched for movement at the work of the field and the yard.

**38. Bridegroom's Bordered Mantle.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A fresh fringed mantle with a woven border over a white tunic, a fillet on oiled hair, new sandals and a ring, for the marriage feast.

**39. Young Field-Worker's Short Kilt.** _(18 to 25, labor.)_ A short coarse wool kilt, a twisted-cord belt and bare feet, following the plow and the harvest under the sun.

**40. Summer Light Linen Wrap.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light undyed linen wrap knotted at the waist, the shoulders bare and oiled, cool for the long heat of the plain.

**41. Young Man's Festival Mantle.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A fringed mantle dyed and bordered, a tasseled edge and a bronze armlet, the beard curled and scented, bright at the feast.

**42. Young Soldier's Belted Tunic.** _(18 to 25, military.)_ A knee tunic and a wide leather belt, a copper helmet and a spear, a young man of the conquering Akkadian host.

**43. Young Herder's Fleece Wrap.** _(18 to 25, pasture.)_ A coarse tunic under a fleece wrap pinned at one shoulder, a crook and a sling, watching the flocks of the open country.

**44. Prime Man's Long Fringed Mantle.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A full fringed mantle to the calf drawn over one shoulder, soberly belted, the beard grown full. The settled dress of a man in his prime.

**45. Draped Mantle with Curled Beard.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A large fringed mantle wound in deep folds over one shoulder, the beard and hair carefully curled, in the manner of the Akkadian bronze heads.

**46. Father's Work Kilt and Apron-Sash.** _(25 to 35, labor.)_ A sturdy kilt with a thick folded apron-sash for tools and grain, the sleeves of an under-shirt pushed back.

**47. Winter Fleece Mantle and Cap.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A heavy fleece mantle over a long wool tunic, a rounded felt cap, the folds deep against the cold wind.

**48. Festival Bordered Robe.** _(25 to 35, festival.)_ A robe with a woven bordered hem and a tasseled sash, an armlet and a cup, bright among the men at the feast.

**49. Temple Offering Robe.** _(25 to 35, devotional.)_ A clean fringed robe and a folded shawl, the beard combed, bringing an offering up to the god of the city.

**50. Soldier's Campaign Dress.** _(25 to 35, military.)_ A belted war tunic, a copper helmet and a wide shield, a spear and an axe, marching in the long campaigns of the empire.

**51. Traveler-Merchant's Robe.** _(25 to 35, road.)_ A long belted robe under a dust-cloak, a seal and a tablet-pouch, a money-belt, trading along the roads the empire had opened.

**52. Mature Man's Heavy Fringed Mantle.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full heavy fringed mantle drawn over one shoulder, soberly belted, the beard streaked and squared. The dress of a settled head of household.

**53. Full Draped Mantle, Elaborate Beard.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ The fringed mantle in its fullest fall over one shoulder, the beard long and elaborately curled, the formal dress of a man of weight.

**54. Master Craftsman's Worn Kilt.** _(35 to 45, work.)_ A practical kilt softened by years, a thick belt holding a knife and a seal, a felt skullcap. An experienced man at his bench.

**55. Banquet Bordered Robe.** _(35 to 45, feast.)_ A robe with a woven bordered hem and a fringed shawl, a cup in hand, reclining among the men at the banquet.

**56. Summer Linen Mantle.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose light-wool or linen mantle over a thin tunic, open and airy, plain sandals, cool for middle life in the heat.

**57. Winter Layered Wool and Fleece.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A long wool tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a felt cap and wrapped hands, fastened with a bronze pin.

**58. Officer's Distinguished Mantle.** _(35 to 45, military rank.)_ A finer fringed mantle with a marked belt, a crested helmet and a sword of office, commanding men in the field.

**59. Mourning Sack-Wool Wrap.** _(35 to 45, grief.)_ A coarse undyed sack-wool wrap, the chest bare and dusted with ash, the beard untrimmed, mourning a wife or a child.

**60. Senior Man's Sober Mantle.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A long fringed mantle in muted tones over one shoulder, a plain belt, the beard mostly grey and combed long, the bearing settled.

**61. Elder's Fringed Mantle at the Assembly.** _(45 to 55, standing.)_ A full fringed mantle wound high and a smooth staff in hand, sitting among the older men who judge at the gate of the city.

**62. Old Master's Hearthside Robe.** _(45 to 55, work.)_ A softened robe and a shawl about the shoulders, a felt cap, teaching the craft now more by word than by hand.

**63. Banquet Robe of an Elder of Means.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A fine bordered robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet and a cup, reclining at the feast, the hair and beard silvered and curled.

**64. Senior Traveler's Heavy Mantle.** _(45 to 55, road.)_ A long robe under a thick hooded cloak, a strong staff, wrapped shins and stout boots, moving slower now between the cities.

**65. Winter Fleece Robe and Cap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A long tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a deep felt cap over the ears, the hands tucked by the hearth.

**66. Devotional Robe, Grey Beard.** _(45 to 55, temple.)_ A clean fringed robe and a folded shawl, the grey beard combed, standing long in prayer before the god.

**67. White-Bearded Elder's Mantle.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A long warm fringed mantle, a soft belt, a smooth staff, the beard long and white. The daily dress of a venerable old man.

**68. Counselor's Fringed Robe.** _(55 to 65, standing.)_ A long fringed mantle over a full robe, seated among the elders who advise the city and the governor, the staff across the knees.

**69. Honored Banquet Seat Robe.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A fine soft robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet, a cushioned seat served by the young, a thin fillet on the silvered head.

**70. Elder's Staff-and-Shawl Dress.** _(55 to 65, walking.)_ A warm wrapped robe and a shawl over the shoulders, leaning on a smooth staff, moving slowly through the lanes.

**71. Winter Heavy Fleece Robe.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A thick fleece-lined robe and a deep felt cap, a wrap over the knees by the hearth, the hands tucked.

**72. Devotional Robe of the Aged.** _(55 to 65, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated long before the god, the prayers worn smooth by a lifetime.

**73. Veteran's Honored Mantle.** _(55 to 65, old soldier.)_ A fringed mantle over a scarred frame, an old campaign-belt kept with pride, telling the young of the wars of the empire.

**74. Frail Elder's Warm Mantle.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm mantle worn loose, a shawl always over the thin shoulders, a strong staff and a steadying hand.

**75. Sage's Robe in the Place of Honor.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft robe and a long fringed shawl, seated in highest honor among the elders, the white beard reaching the chest.

**76. Patriarch's Banquet Robe, Supported.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm robe and a folded shawl, cushioned and supported at the feast, a cup held for him by a grandchild, a thin fillet on the white head.

**77. Hearthside Frail Robe.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft robe easy to put on, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide shoes, seated by the hearth and tended by the family.

**78. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece robe and a thick blanket-mantle, a deep felt cap and covered hands, close by the fire.

**79. Devotional Robe of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated and supported before the god, prayers murmured from long habit and deep peace.

**80. Mourning of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse wrap drawn over the bowed white head, ash and dust, mourning the last of his own generation, upheld by the young.

### Akkad: Women by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 81 to 125)

**81. Young Woman's Wrapped Fringed Gown.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ An ankle gown of fringed wool drawn over one shoulder and belted, the hair bound back simply. The daily dress of a young Akkadian woman.

**82. Fringed Mantle over Gown.** _(18 to 25, formal.)_ A long gown beneath a fringed wool mantle drawn over the shoulders, hoop earrings and a strung necklace, for the temple and the feast.

**83. Bride's Adorned Gown.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A dyed fringed gown with a beaded collar of carnelian and lapis, a thin fillet and heavy earrings, the hair oiled and scented, for the marriage feast.

**84. Young Wife's Nursing Gown.** _(18 to 25, motherhood.)_ A loose wrapped gown of soft wool, easily opened at the breast, belted high, a protective bead-string for the infant at the shoulder.

**85. Summer Linen Sheath.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light sleeveless linen sheath to the ankle, undyed and cool, a thin beaded cord at the neck, the hair tied up against the heat.

**86. Festival Adornment.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A brightly dyed fringed gown, a tasseled shawl and the full jewelry of faience, carnelian and gold, the eyes kohl-lined, anklets ringing.

**87. Temple Worshipper's Gown.** _(18 to 25, devotional.)_ A modest gown of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, simple beads and clasped hands, standing before the god.

**88. Young Weaver-Woman's Gown.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, the fingers stained with dye at the loom.

**89. Matron's Long Fringed Gown.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A full ankle gown of fringed wool drawn over one shoulder and belted, the hair under a plain fillet. The dress of a settled wife and mother.

**90. Fringed Mantle and Bead Collar.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A long gown under a fringed mantle, a broad collar of carnelian and lapis, hoop earrings, for the feast and the rite.

**91. Household Manager's Gown with Seal.** _(25 to 35, authority.)_ A belted gown of good wool from whose waist hang a pouch and a cylinder seal, the woman who keeps the household's stores.

**92. Mother's Gown with Carrying Sling.** _(25 to 35, motherhood.)_ A practical wool gown belted high, a wide woven sling for a small child, a protective amulet pinned at the shoulder.

**93. Festival Matron's Adornment.** _(25 to 35, festival.)_ A richly dyed gown layered with necklaces of carnelian, faience and gold, heavy earrings and a thin diadem, kohl-lined eyes, a tasseled shawl.

**94. Summer Linen Matron's Sheath.** _(25 to 35, hot season.)_ A straight sleeveless linen gown to the ankle, undyed and cool, a folded shawl for the temple, a single beaded necklace.

**95. Winter Wool-and-Felt Layers.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A long wool gown under a thick felted mantle drawn over head and shoulders, a felt-lined wrap at the throat, in deep browns.

**96. Mourning Gown of Coarse Wool.** _(25 to 35, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, the jewelry set aside, the hair loosed and dusted with ash, the cloth torn at the breast.

**97. Senior Matron's Full Gown.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full, modestly cut gown of good wool to the floor, softly belted, the greying hair under a fillet and a light shawl.

**98. Large Fringed Mantle of Standing.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ A long gown under a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, several heavy necklaces and broad earrings, a matron of substance.

**99. Keeper of the Stores Gown.** _(35 to 45, authority.)_ A practical good-wool gown with a heavy belt bearing tools, a seal and a pouch, the sleeves bound for oversight of a large house.

**100. Grandmother's Practical Gown.** _(35 to 45, daily care.)_ A soft, easy gown belted high, a shawl always at the shoulder for a grandchild, an old amulet at the breast.

**101. Festival Display of a Matron of Means.** _(35 to 45, festival.)_ A deeply dyed gown layered with gold and carnelian, a broad collar, a diadem over a fringed shawl, scented oils and kohl.

**102. Summer Linen Gown of Middle Life.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose ankle-length linen gown, undyed and cool, modest at the neck, a single strand of beads, the hair bound up.

**103. Winter Layered Wool and Felt.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle drawn close over head and shoulders, fleece-lined slippers, a bronze pin at the throat.

**104. Temple Votary's Fine Robe.** _(35 to 45, devotional.)_ A robe of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, modest jewelry and clasped hands, long practiced in the temple's prayers.

**105. Elder Wife's Full Modest Gown.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A full floor-length gown of soft good wool, soberly belted, the greying hair bound and a light shawl over it.

**106. Matriarch's Fringed Mantle.** _(45 to 55, formal.)_ A long gown beneath a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, heavy old necklaces from her youth, broad earrings.

**107. Mistress of the Household Gown.** _(45 to 55, authority.)_ A good-wool gown with a heavy belt of keys, seal and pouch, commanding the stores and the younger women of the house.

**108. Banquet Adornment of an Elder Lady.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A deep-dyed gown with layered gold and carnelian, a diadem over a fine shawl, scented and kohl-lined, honored at the feast.

**109. Summer Elder Gown.** _(45 to 55, hot season.)_ A loose undyed linen gown, modest and cool, a thin shawl for the temple, the hair bound up off the neck.

**110. Winter Wool-Felt Elder Wrap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle close about head and shoulders, fleece slippers, a pin at the throat, by the hearth.

**111. Mourning Gown of an Elder Woman.** _(45 to 55, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, jewelry removed, the white hair loosed and ashed, the cloth rent. Grave and dignified grief.

**112. Venerable Grandmother's Gown.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A full soft floor-length gown, soberly belted, a shawl over the white hair, a slow and gentle bearing.

**113. Matriarch's Heavy Mantle.** _(55 to 65, formal.)_ A long gown under a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, the old gold of a lifetime at her ears and throat.

**114. Eldest Woman of the House.** _(55 to 65, authority.)_ A good-wool gown, the keys now passed on yet still consulted, a shawl and a staff, seated where the family gathers.

**115. Banquet Robe of an Aged Lady.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A deep-dyed soft gown, the heirloom gold and carnelian, a thin diadem over a fine shawl, seated in honor at the feast.

**116. Grandmother by the Hearth.** _(55 to 65, daily care.)_ A soft warm gown belted high, a child often in her lap, a shawl and an old amulet, slippers of fleece.

**117. Winter Fleece-Wrapped Elder.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A heavy gown under a felted mantle, a fleece wrap over the knees, slippers and a pin at the throat, by the fire.

**118. Aged Votary's Quiet Robe.** _(55 to 65, devotional.)_ A fine pale robe and a soft shawl, seated long in the temple's calm, a lifetime of prayer behind her.

**119. Frail Grandmother's Warm Gown.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm gown worn loose, a shawl ever over the thin shoulders and white hair, a staff and a steadying arm.

**120. Most-Honored Matriarch's Mantle.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft gown under a great soft mantle over head and shoulders, the heirloom gold light on thin skin, seated in highest honor.

**121. Banquet Honor-Seat Gown.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm gown, the family's oldest gold and a thin diadem over a soft shawl, cushioned and tended at the feast.

**122. Hearthside Frail Gown.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft gown easy to wear, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide slippers, seated by the fire with a great-grandchild near.

**123. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder Woman.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece gown and a thick blanket-mantle, a felt hood and covered hands, close by the hearth.

**124. Mourning of the Very Old Woman.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse gown, all gold set aside, the white hair loosed and ashed, mourning the last companions of her years.

**125. Last Votary's Gown.** _(65 to 80, devotional.)_ A fine soft pale gown and a gentle shawl, seated and supported in the temple's calm, a long life of prayer at its close.
