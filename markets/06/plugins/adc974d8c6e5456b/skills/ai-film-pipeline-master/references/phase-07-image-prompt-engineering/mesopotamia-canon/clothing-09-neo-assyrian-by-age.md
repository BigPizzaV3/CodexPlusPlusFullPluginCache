## Part VI. Assyria. The Neo-Assyrian Empire

_The dress of the Assyrian empire in its third and greatest age, approximately 911 to 609 BC — the span of the empire rather than a single peak — ruled from a capital that **moved**: Ashur first, then Kalhu (Nimrud) from Ashurnasirpal II, briefly Dur-Sharrukin (Khorsabad) under Sargon II, then Nineveh from Sennacherib onward. They were successive, never simultaneous, and a piece should name which one it is in. It is the most fully documented dress of all Mesopotamia, carved in vast detail across the palace reliefs: the long fringed robe embroidered with rosettes and the sacred tree, the diagonally wound shawl, the squared and curled beard, the royal **polos** — a tall truncated cone topped with a smaller cone and wrapped in a diadem, not a pointed crown, per `grimoire-16-neo-assyrian.md` — the mural-crowned queen, and for war the coat of bronze scale and the pointed helmet. Two hundred and fifty garments follow._


### Neo-Assyrian: Infants and Young Children, Birth to Four (Nos. 1 to 15)

**1. Newborn's Wool Swaddle.** _(Common.)_ An undyed sheep-wool band wound in a spiral so only the face is free, tucked and tied with a wool cord. The common newborn wrap of the Assyrian towns.

**2. Palace-Household Linen Swaddle.** _(Wealthy, court.)_ A soft bleached linen band with a thin fringed and rosette-stamped edge, a flat carnelian bead at the throat, for the child of a household of the royal court.

**3. Poor Quarter Reed Swaddle.** _(Poor.)_ Coarse undyed wool and reused cloth bound with twisted reed cord, bare-headed, the wrap of the poor families of the city's lower quarters.

**4. Lambswool Winter Bundle.** _(Cold season.)_ Carded lambswool over an inner linen band, bound with a felted wool sash in cream and brown, warming the swaddled infant through the cold northern winter.

**5. Pazuzu-Amulet Swaddle.** _(Protective.)_ A plain wool swaddle with a bronze Pazuzu head knotted into the chest cord, the most trusted Assyrian guard against Lamashtu, who threatens mother and newborn.

**6. Crawling Infant's Half-Swaddle.** _(Six to twelve months.)_ The arms freed while the lower body stays wrapped, a short undyed wool wrap crossing the chest and tied, for the crawling child.

**7. Infant's Linen Shift.** _(Everyday.)_ A tiny sleeveless linen shift to the knees, undyed with a faint edge-stripe, gathered at the neck with a flax drawstring.

**8. Fringe-and-Rosette Infant Wrap.** _(Modest means.)_ A small wrap finished with a thin fringe and a row of stamped rosettes, the Assyrian ornament carried even onto the infant, soft and warm.

**9. Felt Bonnet and Body Wrap.** _(Winter infant.)_ A rounded wool-felt bonnet tied under the chin with a layered wool body wrap in grey-brown tones, for the cold months.

**10. Toddler's Short Tunic.** _(Everyday toddler.)_ A plain knee-length sleeveless wool tunic belted with a woven cord, the child barefoot. The everyday dress of the small Assyrian child.

**11. Toddler's Fringed and Tasseled Tunic.** _(Well-off child.)_ A small wool tunic with a fringed hem and a little tassel at the belt, in the Assyrian love of fringe and tassel, a carnelian bead at the neck.

**12. Toddler's Bordered Wrap.** _(Comfortable means.)_ A wool wrap with a woven colored border and a thin cord belt, the head shaved with a single side-lock.

**13. Little Girl's Beaded Wrap.** _(Girl, festival.)_ A soft wool wrap pinned over one shoulder with a short string of faience and carnelian beads, for a small girl at a feast.

**14. Little Boy's Fringed Kilt.** _(Boy, everyday.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a fringed edge and a cord belt, the chest bare, the head shaved with a side-lock.

**15. Ashur-Festival Child's Robe.** _(Wealthy child, Akitu.)_ A soft pale linen tunic with a woven rosette border and a beaded carnelian collar, a fillet, for a wealthy child in the New Year procession of the god Ashur.

### Neo-Assyrian: Children and Youth, Four to Eighteen (Nos. 16 to 35)

**16. Boy's Everyday Kilt.** _(Boy, 4 to 7.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a cord belt, the chest and feet bare, the head shaved with a side-lock. The small Assyrian boy at play.

**17. Girl's Wrapped Pinafore.** _(Girl, 4 to 7.)_ A length of wool wrapped and pinned over one shoulder, cream with a thin stripe, simple clay beads at the neck.

**18. Boy's Fringed Tunic.** _(Boy, 7 to 10.)_ A knee tunic with a fringed hem and a woven belt, running the lanes and learning a trade, plaited sandals.

**19. Girl's Beaded Festival Tunic.** _(Girl, 7 to 10.)_ A linen tunic dyed pale saffron, a collar of faience and carnelian, a beaded fillet, for a feast day.

**20. Herder-Boy's Fleece Wrap.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A coarse wool tunic under a small fleece wrap pinned at one shoulder, a cord belt and a staff, the son of a herding family of the hills.

**21. Scribe-Student Boy's Tunic.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A clean linen tunic, a tablet and stylus, the hair cropped, learning the signs and the omens for the great libraries of Nineveh.

**22. Girl's Wool Day Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A simple ankle gown wrapped and belted, the hair bound back, helping the women at loom and hearth.

**23. Weaver-Girl's Practical Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, learning the craft of cloth and the fine embroidery the Assyrians prized.

**24. Youth's Fringed Tunic and Shawl.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A knee tunic under a fringed shawl drawn diagonally across the body, the young man taking on the adult Assyrian dress, a first short squared beard.

**25. Young Woman's Gown with Shawl.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ A long belted gown under a fringed shawl over the hair and one shoulder, a beaded necklace, the hair dressed, near the age of marriage.

**26. Noble Boy's Rosette-Bordered Tunic.** _(Boy, wealthy, 7 to 10.)_ A fine tunic with a woven rosette border and a fringed shawl, a carnelian bead and a fillet, the son of a great house of the empire.

**27. Noble Girl's Fringed Gown.** _(Girl, wealthy, 10 to 15.)_ A fine fringed gown with a draped shawl, gold ear-studs and a lapis necklace, a daughter of standing.

**28. Poor Boy's Patched Kilt.** _(Boy, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A worn and patched wool kilt with a cord belt, the feet bare, carrying water and minding the goats.

**29. Poor Girl's Worn Shift.** _(Girl, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A thin much-washed wool shift, no jewelry but a single clay bead, helping her mother at the well and the hearth.

**30. Young Soldier-Recruit's Tunic.** _(Youth, 15 to 18.)_ A belted wool tunic and a leather cap, a wooden practice-spear, a boy drilling for the famed and feared Assyrian army.

**31. Royal Page Boy's Tunic.** _(Youth, 15 to 18, palace.)_ A clean fine tunic with a fringed edge and a beaded belt, the hair dressed, a young attendant serving in the halls of the king.

**32. Bride-to-Be's Adorned Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ A dyed gown with a beaded carnelian collar and a fillet, the hair oiled and scented, prepared for her betrothal.

**33. Apprentice Relief-Carver's Tunic.** _(Boy, 12 to 16.)_ A tunic grey with stone dust, the sleeves bound, a small chisel and mallet, learning to carve the palace reliefs beside the master sculptor.

**34. Girl Musician's Festival Dress.** _(Girl, 12 to 16.)_ A decorated tunic for the temple song, a small lyre, a fillet and beads, singing at the feast of the goddess.

**35. Young Charioteer-Trainee's Dress.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A fitted tunic with crossed straps and a leather cap, learning the reins and the bow on the practice ground of the chariot corps.

### Neo-Assyrian: Men by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 36 to 80)

**36. Young Man's Fringed Tunic and Diagonal Shawl.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ A knee tunic under a fringed shawl drawn diagonally across the body and over one shoulder, a wide belt, a short squared beard. The everyday dress of a young Assyrian townsman.

**37. Young Man's Plain Belted Tunic.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain knee tunic and a wide belt, the sleeves pushed back, practical for the work of the yard and the field.

**38. Bridegroom's Embroidered Robe.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A fine tunic embroidered with rosettes under a fringed shawl, a fillet on oiled and curled hair, new sandals and a ring, for the marriage feast.

**39. Young Field-Worker's Short Kilt.** _(18 to 25, labor.)_ A short coarse wool kilt, a twisted-cord belt and bare feet, working the fields and the irrigation of the northern plain.

**40. Summer Linen Tunic.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light undyed linen knee tunic, short-sleeved and loose, plain sandals, cool for the heat of the northern summer.

**41. Young Man's Akitu Festival Robe.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A tunic and shawl dyed and bordered with rosettes and a tasseled edge, a bronze armlet, the beard curled, bright in the procession of Ashur.

**42. Young Soldier's Armored Tunic.** _(18 to 25, military.)_ A belted tunic sewn with bronze scales, a pointed helmet, a composite bow and a short sword, a young man of the dreaded Assyrian host.

**43. Young Royal Attendant's Tunic.** _(18 to 25, palace.)_ A clean fringed tunic and a beaded belt, the hair and beard carefully dressed, serving in the great halls of the palace.

**44. Prime Man's Long Fringed Robe.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A long fringed robe to the calf with a diagonal shawl, soberly belted, the beard grown full and squared. The settled dress of a man in his prime.

**45. Rosette-Embroidered Robe and Shawl.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A long robe embroidered with rosettes and a sacred-tree border under a deeply fringed shawl wound across the body, the formal Assyrian dress of standing.

**46. Father's Work Tunic and Belt.** _(25 to 35, labor.)_ A sturdy knee tunic and a wide belt holding tools, the sleeves bound, practical for the day's trade and the raising of a family.

**47. Winter Wool Mantle and Cap.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A heavy fulled-wool mantle over a long tunic, a rounded felt cap, the folds deep against the cold of the northern hills.

**48. Festival Tasseled Robe.** _(25 to 35, festival.)_ A robe with a rosette border and a tasseled shawl, an armlet and a cup, the curled beard scented, bright among the men at the feast.

**49. Temple Offering Robe.** _(25 to 35, devotional.)_ A clean fringed robe and a folded shawl, the beard combed, bringing an offering before the god Ashur in the temple.

**50. Soldier's Scale-Armor Dress.** _(25 to 35, military.)_ A war tunic covered in overlapping bronze scales, a tall pointed helmet, a long shield and a spear, the armored shock troop of the empire.

**51. Cavalry Rider's Dress.** _(25 to 35, military.)_ A fitted tunic with wrapped and laced leggings for the horse, a bow and a short spear, a helmet and riding boots, the mounted Assyrian soldier.

**52. Mature Man's Heavy Fringed Robe.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full heavy fringed robe with a diagonal shawl, soberly belted, the squared beard streaked. The dress of a settled head of household.

**53. Full Rosette Robe, Squared Beard.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ A long robe densely worked with rosettes and a fringed shawl wound twice across the body, the beard long, squared and elaborately curled, a man of weight.

**54. Master Craftsman's Worn Tunic.** _(35 to 45, work.)_ A practical knee tunic softened by years, a wide belt holding a knife and a seal, a felt skullcap. An experienced man at his bench.

**55. Banquet Embroidered Robe.** _(35 to 45, feast.)_ A robe embroidered at hem and sleeve, a fringed shawl and a cup in hand, reclining in the garden or the painted hall at the banquet.

**56. Summer Linen Robe.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose light linen robe to the calf, open at the throat and undyed, plain sandals, cool for middle life in the heat.

**57. Winter Layered Wool.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A long wool tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a felt cap and wrapped hands, fastened with a bronze pin.

**58. Officer's Distinguished Armor.** _(35 to 45, military.)_ A fine fringed tunic under a scale corselet, a banded helmet with a plume, a sword of office and a decorated belt, commanding the troops.

**59. Mourning Sack-Wool Wrap.** _(35 to 45, grief.)_ A coarse undyed sack-wool wrap, the chest bare and dusted with ash, the beard untrimmed, mourning a wife or a child.

**60. Senior Man's Sober Fringed Robe.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A long fringed robe in muted tones with a diagonal shawl, a plain belt, the squared beard mostly grey, the bearing settled.

**61. Elder's Fringed Robe at the Gate.** _(45 to 55, standing.)_ A full fringed robe and a smooth staff, sitting among the older men who judge the city's disputes at the gate.

**62. Old Master's Hearthside Robe.** _(45 to 55, work.)_ A softened robe and a shawl about the shoulders, a felt cap, teaching the craft now more by word than by hand.

**63. Banquet Robe of an Elder of Means.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A fine embroidered robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet and a cup, reclining at the feast, the hair and squared beard silvered.

**64. Senior Traveler's Heavy Robe.** _(45 to 55, road.)_ A long robe under a thick hooded cloak, a strong staff, wrapped shins and stout boots, moving slower now along the royal roads.

**65. Winter Fleece Robe and Cap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A long tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a deep felt cap over the ears, the hands tucked by the hearth.

**66. Devotional Robe, Grey Squared Beard.** _(45 to 55, temple.)_ A clean fringed robe and a folded shawl, the grey squared beard combed, standing long in prayer before Ashur.

**67. White-Bearded Elder's Robe.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A long warm fringed robe, a soft belt, a smooth staff, the squared beard long and white. The daily dress of a venerable old man.

**68. Counselor's Fringed Robe.** _(55 to 65, standing.)_ A long fringed robe and a diagonal shawl, seated among the elders who advise the governor and the city, the staff across the knees.

**69. Honored Banquet Seat Robe.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A fine soft robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet, a cushioned seat served by the young, a thin fillet on the silvered head.

**70. Elder's Staff-and-Shawl Dress.** _(55 to 65, walking.)_ A warm wrapped robe and a shawl over the shoulders, leaning on a smooth staff, moving slowly through the lanes.

**71. Winter Heavy Fleece Robe.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A thick fleece-lined robe and a deep felt cap, a wrap over the knees by the hearth, the hands tucked.

**72. Devotional Robe of the Aged.** _(55 to 65, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated long before the god, the prayers worn smooth by a lifetime of feast days.

**73. Veteran Officer's Honored Robe.** _(55 to 65, old soldier.)_ A fringed robe over a scarred frame, an old decorated campaign-belt kept with pride, telling the young of the wars of the empire.

**74. Frail Elder's Warm Robe.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm robe worn loose, a shawl always over the thin shoulders, a strong staff and a steadying hand.

**75. Sage's Robe in the Place of Honor.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft robe and a long fringed shawl, seated in highest honor among the elders, the white squared beard reaching the chest.

**76. Patriarch's Banquet Robe, Supported.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm robe and a folded shawl, cushioned and supported at the feast, a cup held for him by a grandchild, a thin fillet on the white head.

**77. Hearthside Frail Robe.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft robe easy to put on, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide shoes, seated by the hearth and tended by the family.

**78. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece robe and a thick blanket-mantle, a deep felt cap and covered hands, close by the fire.

**79. Devotional Robe of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated and supported before the god, prayers murmured from long habit and deep peace.

**80. Mourning of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse wrap drawn over the bowed white head, ash and dust, mourning the last of his own generation, upheld by the young.

### Neo-Assyrian: Women by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 81 to 125)

**81. Young Woman's Fringed Wool Gown.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ A long belted gown of fringed wool with a shawl over one shoulder, the hair bound back. The daily dress of a young Assyrian woman.

**82. Gown with Shawl over the Hair.** _(18 to 25, formal.)_ A long gown beneath a fringed wool shawl drawn over the back of the hair and across one shoulder, hoop earrings and a strung necklace, worn as a formal covering. No Neo-Assyrian legal text is known to govern this covering; the legal restriction on the same shawl shape is Middle Assyrian only, see Part V No. 38. In the surviving Neo-Assyrian art the shawl is documented as a status marker only on royal women (Naqi'a's bronze relief, the Libbali-sharrat stele, the Garden Scene queen); ordinary Assyrian women of status are almost entirely absent from the surviving pictorial record, so a specific temple or feast occasion is a reading, not an established fact, when applied to this garment. (Source: N. N. May, "Neo-Assyrian Women, Their Visibility, and Their Representation in Written and Pictorial Sources," in Studying Gender in the Ancient Near East, ed. S. Svärd and A. Garcia-Ventura (University Park, PA: Eisenbrauns/Penn State University Press, 2018), 260-261, 280-281; open-access PDF via Zenodo, record 4445957, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4445957, checked 2026-09-23.)

**83. Bride's Adorned Gown.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A dyed gown with a rosette border and a beaded collar of carnelian and lapis, a thin fillet and heavy earrings, the hair oiled and scented, for the marriage feast.

**84. Young Wife's Nursing Gown.** _(18 to 25, motherhood.)_ A loose gown of soft wool, easily opened at the breast, belted high, a Pazuzu amulet at the shoulder to guard the infant from Lamashtu.

**85. Summer Linen Sheath.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light sleeveless linen sheath to the ankle, undyed and cool, a thin beaded cord at the neck, the hair tied up against the heat.

**86. Festival Adornment.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A brightly dyed gown with a rosette border, a tasseled shawl and the full jewelry of faience, carnelian and gold, the eyes kohl-lined, anklets ringing.

**87. Temple Worshipper's Gown.** _(18 to 25, devotional.)_ A modest gown of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over hair and shoulder, simple beads and clasped hands, before the god.

**88. Young Weaver-Woman's Gown.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, working the fine embroidery for which Assyrian cloth was prized.

**89. Matron's Long Fringed Gown.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A full ankle gown of fringed wool with a shawl over hair and shoulder, belted, the dress of a settled Assyrian wife and mother.

**90. Fringed Shawl and Bead Collar.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A long gown under a fringed shawl over the hair, a broad collar of carnelian and lapis, hoop earrings, for the feast and the rite.

**91. Household Manager's Gown with Seal.** _(25 to 35, authority.)_ A belted gown of good wool from whose waist hang a pouch and a cylinder seal, the woman who keeps the household's stores.

**92. Mother's Gown with Carrying Sling.** _(25 to 35, motherhood.)_ A practical wool gown belted high, a wide woven sling for a small child, a protective amulet pinned at the shoulder.

**93. Festival Matron's Adornment.** _(25 to 35, festival.)_ A richly dyed gown layered with necklaces of carnelian, faience and gold, heavy earrings and a thin diadem, kohl-lined eyes, a tasseled shawl over the hair.

**94. Summer Linen Matron's Sheath.** _(25 to 35, hot season.)_ A straight sleeveless linen gown to the ankle, undyed and cool, a folded shawl for the temple, a single beaded necklace.

**95. Winter Wool-and-Felt Layers.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A long wool gown under a thick felted mantle drawn over head and shoulders, a felt-lined wrap at the throat, in deep browns.

**96. Mourning Gown of Coarse Wool.** _(25 to 35, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, the jewelry set aside, the hair loosed and dusted with ash, the cloth torn at the breast.

**97. Senior Matron's Full Gown.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full, modestly cut gown of good wool to the floor, softly belted, the greying hair under a shawl.

**98. Large Fringed Shawl of Standing.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ A long gown under a great fringed shawl over head and shoulders, several heavy necklaces and broad earrings, a matron of substance.

**99. Keeper of the Stores Gown.** _(35 to 45, authority.)_ A practical good-wool gown with a heavy belt bearing tools, a seal and a pouch, the sleeves bound for oversight of a large house.

**100. Grandmother's Practical Gown.** _(35 to 45, daily care.)_ A soft, easy gown belted high, a shawl always at the shoulder for a grandchild, an old amulet at the breast.

**101. Festival Display of a Matron of Means.** _(35 to 45, festival.)_ A deeply dyed gown with a rosette border, layered gold and carnelian, a diadem over a fringed shawl, scented oils and kohl.

**102. Summer Linen Gown of Middle Life.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose ankle-length linen gown, undyed and cool, modest at the neck, a single strand of beads, the hair bound up.

**103. Winter Layered Wool and Felt.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle drawn close over head and shoulders, fleece-lined slippers, a bronze pin at the throat.

**104. Temple Votary's Fine Robe.** _(35 to 45, devotional.)_ A robe of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over hair and shoulder, modest jewelry and clasped hands, long practiced in the temple's prayers.

**105. Elder Wife's Full Modest Gown.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A full floor-length gown of soft good wool, soberly belted, the greying hair bound and a shawl over it.

**106. Matriarch's Fringed Mantle.** _(45 to 55, formal.)_ A long gown beneath a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, heavy old necklaces from her youth, broad earrings.

**107. Mistress of the Household Gown.** _(45 to 55, authority.)_ A good-wool gown with a heavy belt of keys, seal and pouch, commanding the stores and the younger women of the house.

**108. Banquet Adornment of an Elder Lady.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A deep-dyed gown with layered gold and carnelian, a diadem over a fine shawl, scented and kohl-lined, honored at the feast.

**109. Summer Elder Gown.** _(45 to 55, hot season.)_ A loose undyed linen gown, modest and cool, a thin shawl for the temple, the hair bound up off the neck.

**110. Winter Wool-Felt Elder Wrap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle close about head and shoulders, fleece slippers, a pin at the throat, by the hearth.

**111. Mourning Gown of an Elder Woman.** _(45 to 55, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, jewelry removed, the white hair loosed and ashed, the cloth rent. Grave and dignified grief.

**112. Venerable Grandmother's Gown.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A full soft floor-length gown, soberly belted, a shawl over the white hair, a slow and gentle bearing.

**113. Matriarch's Heavy Mantle.** _(55 to 65, formal.)_ A long gown under a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, the old gold of a lifetime at her ears and throat.

**114. Eldest Woman of the House.** _(55 to 65, authority.)_ A good-wool gown, the keys now passed on yet still consulted, a shawl and a staff, seated where the family gathers.

**115. Banquet Robe of an Aged Lady.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A deep-dyed soft gown, the heirloom gold and carnelian, a thin diadem over a fine shawl, seated in honor at the feast.

**116. Grandmother by the Hearth.** _(55 to 65, daily care.)_ A soft warm gown belted high, a child often in her lap, a shawl and an old amulet, slippers of fleece.

**117. Winter Fleece-Wrapped Elder.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A heavy gown under a felted mantle, a fleece wrap over the knees, slippers and a pin at the throat, by the fire.

**118. Aged Votary's Quiet Robe.** _(55 to 65, devotional.)_ A fine pale robe and a soft shawl, seated long in the temple's calm, a lifetime of prayer behind her.

**119. Frail Grandmother's Warm Gown.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm gown worn loose, a shawl ever over the thin shoulders and white hair, a staff and a steadying arm.

**120. Most-Honored Matriarch's Mantle.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft gown under a great soft mantle over head and shoulders, the heirloom gold light on thin skin, seated in highest honor.

**121. Banquet Honor-Seat Gown.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm gown, the family's oldest gold and a thin diadem over a soft shawl, cushioned and tended at the feast.

**122. Hearthside Frail Gown.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft gown easy to wear, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide slippers, seated by the fire with a great-grandchild near.

**123. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder Woman.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece gown and a thick blanket-mantle, a felt hood and covered hands, close by the hearth.

**124. Mourning of the Very Old Woman.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse gown, all gold set aside, the white hair loosed and ashed, mourning the last companions of her years.

**125. Last Votary's Gown.** _(65 to 80, devotional.)_ A fine soft pale gown and a gentle shawl, seated and supported in the temple's calm, a long life of prayer at its close.
