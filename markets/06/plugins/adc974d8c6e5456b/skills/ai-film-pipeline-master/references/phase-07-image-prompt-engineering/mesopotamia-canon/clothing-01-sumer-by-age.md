## Part I. Sumer

_The oldest dress of the Two Rivers, from the cities of the southern plain: Ur, Uruk, Lagash, Eridu and Nippur, across the Early Dynastic period and the Third Dynasty of Ur. Its signature is the kaunakes, wool worked into rows of hanging fleece tufts, worn as tufted skirts and tiered robes, often with a bare chest for men and one shoulder bare for women, as seen in the votive statues of clasped-handed worshippers. Wool and linen, undyed or in soft natural tones, fringed hems, wide belts and bronze pins. Two hundred and fifty garments follow._


### Sumer: Infants and Young Children, Birth to Four (Nos. 1 to 15)

**1. Newborn's Plain Wool Swaddle.** _(Common, river town.)_ An undyed sheep-wool band wound in a spiral around the newborn so that only the face is free, tucked and tied with a wool cord. The common Sumerian newborn wrap of the southern canals.

**2. Temple-Household Linen Swaddle.** _(Wealthy, temple-serving.)_ A band of soft bleached linen edged with a thin natural stripe, a flat carnelian bead on a flax cord at the throat, for a child of a family that serves the temple at Ur or Uruk.

**3. Marsh-Family Reed-and-Wool Swaddle.** _(Poor, southern marshes.)_ Coarse undyed wool and strips of reused cloth bound with twisted reed-fiber cord, the head bare, the wrap of the poorest reed-cutting families of the south.

**4. Lambswool Winter Bundle.** _(Cold season.)_ Carded lambswool laid over an inner linen band and bound with a felted wool sash in cream and natural brown, warming the swaddled infant through the cold southern night.

**5. Bead-Guarded Newborn Wrap.** _(Protective.)_ A plain wool swaddle with a small string of faience and cowrie beads knotted into the chest cord to guard the newborn against the demons of the night. Humble cloth, watchful charm.

**6. Crawling Infant's Half-Swaddle.** _(Six to twelve months.)_ The arms freed while the lower body stays wrapped, a short undyed wool wrap crossing the chest and tied with cord, for the Sumerian infant beginning to crawl.

**7. Infant's First Linen Shift.** _(Everyday.)_ A tiny sleeveless linen shift slipped over the head to the knees, undyed with a faint stripe at the edge, gathered at the neck with a flax drawstring.

**8. Tufted Fleece Infant Wrap.** _(Modest means.)_ A small wrap edged with soft fleece tufts in the kaunakes manner, cream and warm, marking even the infant with the signature look of Sumer.

**9. Felt Bonnet and Body Wrap.** _(Winter infant.)_ A rounded wool-felt bonnet tied under the chin, paired with a layered wool body wrap in grey-brown fleece tones, for the cold months.

**10. Toddler's Short Wool Tunic.** _(Everyday toddler.)_ A plain knee-length sleeveless wool tunic belted with a woven cord, the child barefoot. The everyday dress of the small Sumerian child.

**11. Toddler's Tiny Kaunakes Skirt.** _(Well-off child.)_ A small tufted fleece skirt with the rows of wool tufts in miniature, a bare chest and a carnelian bead at the neck, for a toddler of a well-off family.

**12. Toddler's Fringed Linen Tunic.** _(Comfortable means.)_ A linen tunic with a fringed hem and a thin red cord belt, the head shaved with a single long side-lock, for a child of comfortable means.

**13. Little Girl's Beaded Wrap.** _(Girl, festival.)_ A soft wool wrap drawn over one shoulder and pinned, with a short string of faience and carnelian beads at the neck, the dress of a small Sumerian girl at a feast.

**14. Little Boy's Plain Kilt.** _(Boy, everyday.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a cord belt, the chest bare, the head shaved with a side-lock. The dress of a small Sumerian boy.

**15. Temple-Festival Child's Robe.** _(Wealthy child, procession.)_ A soft pale linen tunic with a woven saffron border and a beaded carnelian collar, the hair bound with a thin fillet, for a wealthy child in the procession at the city temple.

### Sumer: Children and Youth, Four to Eighteen (Nos. 16 to 35)

**16. Boy's Everyday Wool Kilt.** _(Boy, 4 to 7.)_ A short wrapped wool kilt with a cord belt, the chest bare and the feet bare, the head shaved with a side-lock. The small Sumerian boy at play in the lanes.

**17. Girl's Wrapped Wool Pinafore.** _(Girl, 4 to 7.)_ A length of wool wrapped about the body and pinned over one shoulder, cream with a thin woven stripe, simple clay beads at the neck.

**18. Boy's Fringed Field Tunic.** _(Boy, 7 to 10.)_ A knee tunic with a fringed hem and a woven belt, learning the herd and the field, the feet in plaited sandals.

**19. Girl's Beaded Festival Tunic.** _(Girl, 7 to 10.)_ A linen tunic dyed pale saffron, a collar of faience and carnelian, a beaded fillet in the hair, for a feast day.

**20. Shepherd-Boy's Fleece Wrap.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A coarse wool tunic under a small tufted fleece wrap pinned at one shoulder, a twisted-cord belt and a staff, the son of a herding family of the steppe edge.

**21. Scribe-Student Boy's Tunic.** _(Boy, 10 to 15.)_ A clean linen tunic, a fresh clay tablet and a reed stylus, the hair cropped, the boy of the edubba copying his signs over and over.

**22. Girl's Wool Day Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A simple ankle gown wrapped and belted, the hair bound back, helping the women at the loom and the hearth.

**23. Weaver-Girl's Practical Gown.** _(Girl, 10 to 15.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, thread and spindle to hand, learning the great Sumerian craft of cloth.

**24. Youth's Belted Tunic.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A knee tunic gathered by a woven belt, a first short beard trimmed, the feet in plaited sandals, a young man nearing his full strength.

**25. Young Woman's Wrapped Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ An ankle gown drawn over one shoulder and belted, a beaded necklace and the hair freshly dressed, near the age of her marriage.

**26. Noble Boy's Tufted Skirt.** _(Boy, wealthy, 7 to 10.)_ A fine tufted kaunakes skirt, the chest bare with a single carnelian bead, a thin fillet at the brow, the son of a great house.

**27. Noble Girl's Flounced Gown.** _(Girl, wealthy, 10 to 15.)_ A small flounced tiered gown leaving one shoulder bare, gold ear-studs and a lapis necklace, a daughter of standing.

**28. Poor Boy's Patched Kilt.** _(Boy, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A worn and patched wool kilt with a cord belt, the feet bare, carrying water and driving the birds from the ripening fields.

**29. Poor Girl's Worn Shift.** _(Girl, poor, 7 to 12.)_ A thin, much-washed wool shift, no jewelry but a single clay bead, helping her mother beat the cloth clean at the canal.

**30. Temple-Novice Youth's Robe.** _(Youth, 15 to 18.)_ A plain undyed linen robe, the head shaven smooth, learning the washings and the rites at the temple of the city god.

**31. Young Hunter's Tucked Kilt.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A kilt hitched and tucked high to free the legs for the chase, a sling and a short spear, the fit young man at the marsh edge.

**32. Bride-to-Be's Adorned Gown.** _(Young woman, 15 to 18.)_ A dyed gown with a beaded carnelian collar, a fillet, the hair oiled and scented, prepared for her betrothal in the southern city.

**33. Apprentice Potter-Boy's Tunic.** _(Boy, 12 to 16.)_ A clay-dusted tunic with the sleeves rolled, the hands wet at the wheel beside the master, learning to raise the jar.

**34. Girl Musician's Festival Dress.** _(Girl, 12 to 16.)_ A decorated tunic for the temple song, a small lyre in her arms, a fillet and beads, singing at the feast of the goddess.

**35. Youth at the Edge of the Assembly.** _(Young man, 15 to 18.)_ A clean belted tunic and a folded shawl, standing at the edge of the men's assembly to learn the ways and the speech of the city.

### Sumer: Men by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 36 to 80)

**36. Young Man's Wrapped Wool Kilt.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ A plain knee-length wool kilt wound at the hip and tucked at a rolled waistband, the chest bare and sun-darkened. The daily dress of a young Sumerian townsman at the canals.

**37. Young Man's Tufted Kaunakes Skirt.** _(18 to 25, assembly.)_ The fleece-tufted skirt to the knee, its rows of wool tufts standing like petals, a broad belt and a bare chest, worn by a young man of standing before the temple.

**38. Bridegroom's White Linen Wrap.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A fresh white linen wrap to the calf with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, a woven fillet on oiled hair, new sandals and a single bronze ring, for his marriage feast.

**39. Young Field-Worker's Short Kilt.** _(18 to 25, labor.)_ A short coarse wool kilt hitched for movement, a twisted-cord belt and bare feet, following the seeder-plow behind the oxen.

**40. Summer Bare-Chest Wrap.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light undyed linen wrap knotted at the waist to mid-thigh, the shoulders bare and oiled against the sun, cool for the long southern summer.

**41. Young Man's Akitu Festival Tunic.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A tunic dyed madder red with a woven border, a tasseled shawl and a bronze armlet, the hair oiled, for the New Year procession at the city temple.

**42. Young Marsh-Man's Reed-Edge Wrap.** _(18 to 25, marshes.)_ A minimal wool wrap with a reed-fiber cord, the legs bare for the boat and the water, a net over the shoulder among the southern reeds.

**43. Young Man's Fringed Shoulder-Wrap.** _(18 to 25, formal visit.)_ A fringed wool wrap drawn over one shoulder above a plain kilt, the bare chest beneath, worn to call upon an elder or a master.

**44. Prime Man's Long Wrapped Kilt.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A longer wool kilt to the lower calf with a wide rolled waistband, the chest bare, the beard full. The settled daily dress of a man in his prime.

**45. Kaunakes Skirt with Wide Belt.** _(25 to 35, standing.)_ A dense tufted kaunakes skirt bound by a broad studded belt, worn at the assembly and the law-court of the city in full dignity.

**46. Fringed Wrap over Bare Chest.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A large fringed wool wrap wound about the body and over one shoulder, the right arm and chest free, in the manner of the seated figures of Lagash.

**47. Father's Work Kilt and Apron-Sash.** _(25 to 35, labor.)_ A sturdy kilt with a thick folded sash that doubles as a carrying apron for tools and grain, the sleeves of an under-shirt pushed back.

**48. Winter Fleece Wrap and Felt Cap.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A heavy fleece wrap over a long wool tunic, a rounded felt cap pulled low, the folds deep against the cold canal wind.

**49. Festival Dyed Tunic.** _(25 to 35, Akitu.)_ A knee tunic dyed and bordered, a tasseled sash and an armlet, the beard curled and scented, bright among the men at the New Year.

**50. Temple Offering Wrap.** _(25 to 35, devotional.)_ A clean undyed wrap with a folded shawl over one shoulder, the head bared and the beard combed, bringing a kid or a measure of grain to the god.

**51. Traveler's Kilt and Hide Cloak.** _(25 to 35, road.)_ A short belted kilt, a hide cloak across the back, a shoulder bag and water-skin, stout sandals and a staff, going between the southern cities.

**52. Mature Man's Long Fringed Wrap.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full wool wrap to the ankle with a fringed edge, soberly belted, the beard streaked and squared. The dress of a settled head of household.

**53. Full Kaunakes Robe over One Shoulder.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ The tufted fleece extended to a long robe drawn over the left shoulder, the right bare, the formal dress of a man of weight in middle life.

**54. Master Craftsman's Worn Kilt.** _(35 to 45, work.)_ A practical kilt softened by years, a thick belt holding a knife and a cylinder seal, a felt skullcap over thinning hair. An experienced man at his bench.

**55. Banquet Tunic with Bordered Hem.** _(35 to 45, feast.)_ A knee tunic with a woven bordered hem, a tasseled sash and a cup in hand, reclining at the banquet to the sound of the lyre.

**56. Mature Man's Summer Linen Wrap.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose linen wrap to the calf, open at the throat and undyed, plain sandals, cool and modest for the established man in the heat.

**57. Winter Layered Wool and Fleece.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A long wool tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a felt cap and wrapped hands, fastened with a bronze pin. Built for the cold householder's winter.

**58. Temple Devotional Robe.** _(35 to 45, worship.)_ A clean undyed robe and a single folded shawl, the beard combed, standing with clasped hands in the manner of the votive worshippers of Sumer.

**59. Mourning Sack-Wool Wrap.** _(35 to 45, grief.)_ A coarse undyed sack-wool wrap about the waist, the chest bare and smeared with dust, the beard untrimmed, mourning a wife or a child.

**60. Senior Man's Long Sober Wrap.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A long wool wrap in muted tones, a plain belt, the beard mostly grey and combed long, the bearing settled. An elder still active in his house.

**61. Elder's Fringed Shawl at the Assembly.** _(45 to 55, standing.)_ A long tunic under a deep-fringed shawl wound full and pinned high, a smooth staff in hand, sitting among the older men who judge at the gate.

**62. Old Master's Hearthside Kilt.** _(45 to 55, work.)_ A softened kilt and a shawl about the shoulders, a felt cap over thinning hair, teaching the craft now more by word than by hand.

**63. Banquet Robe of an Elder of Means.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A fine bordered robe and a folded shawl, a gold armlet and a cup, reclining at the feast, the hair and beard silvered and curled.

**64. Senior Traveler's Heavy Wrap.** _(45 to 55, road.)_ A long robe under a thick hooded cloak, a strong staff, wrapped shins and stout boots, moving slower now between the cities.

**65. Winter Fleece Robe and Cap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A long tunic under a fleece-lined mantle, a deep felt cap over the ears, the hands tucked in a fold of cloth by the hearth.

**66. Devotional Robe, Combed Grey Beard.** _(45 to 55, temple.)_ A clean undyed robe and folded shawl, the grey beard combed, standing long in prayer with a practiced calm before the god.

**67. White-Bearded Elder's Wrap.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A long plain wrap of warm wool, a soft belt, a smooth staff in hand, the beard long and white. The daily dress of a venerable old man.

**68. Counselor's Fringed Robe at the Gate.** _(55 to 65, standing.)_ A long fringed shawl over a full robe, seated among the city elders who advise and judge, the staff across the knees, the bearing weighty.

**69. Honored Banquet Seat Robe.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A fine soft robe and a folded shawl, a single gold armlet, a cushioned seat served by the young, a thin fillet on the silvered head.

**70. Elder's Staff-and-Shawl Dress.** _(55 to 65, walking.)_ A warm wrapped robe and a shawl over the shoulders, leaning on a smooth staff, moving slowly through the lanes of the old city.

**71. Winter Heavy Fleece Robe.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A thick fleece-lined robe and a deep felt cap, a wrap over the knees by the hearth, the hands tucked. Guarding the old body from cold.

**72. Devotional Robe of the Aged.** _(55 to 65, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated long before the god, the prayers worn smooth by a lifetime of feast days.

**73. Grief Wrap of an Elder.** _(55 to 65, mourning.)_ A coarse wrap over thin shoulders, ash on the white head, the fine robe set aside, mourning those gone before, leaning on the staff.

**74. Frail Elder's Warm Wrap.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft robe of warm wool worn loose, a shawl always over the thin shoulders, a strong staff and a steadying hand. The gentle dress of the very old.

**75. Sage's Robe in the Place of Honor.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft robe and a long fringed shawl, seated in the highest honor among the elders, the white beard reaching the chest, words few and weighty.

**76. Patriarch's Banquet Robe, Supported.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm robe and a folded shawl, cushioned and supported at the feast, a cup held for him by a grandchild, a thin fillet on the white head.

**77. Hearthside Frail Robe.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft robe easy to put on, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide shoes, seated by the hearth and tended by the family.

**78. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece robe and a thick blanket-mantle, a deep felt cap and covered hands, close by the fire, the cold kept far off.

**79. Devotional Robe of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, temple.)_ A clean soft robe and shawl, seated and supported before the god, prayers murmured from long habit and deep peace.

**80. Mourning of the Very Old.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse wrap drawn over the bowed white head, ash and dust, mourning the last of his own generation, upheld by the young.

### Sumer: Women by Age, Eighteen to Eighty (Nos. 81 to 125)

**81. Young Woman's Wrapped Wool Gown.** _(18 to 25, everyday.)_ An ankle-length wool gown wound about the body and drawn over the left shoulder, belted with a woven band, the hair bound back simply. The daily dress of a young Sumerian woman.

**82. Flounced Kaunakes Gown.** _(18 to 25, formal.)_ A tiered fleece gown of hanging tufts falling to the ankle and leaving one shoulder bare, worn by a young woman of standing at temple and feast.

**83. Bride's Adorned Gown.** _(18 to 25, wedding.)_ A dyed gown with a beaded collar of carnelian and lapis, a thin fillet, heavy earrings, the hair oiled and scented, for the marriage feast of the southern city.

**84. Young Wife's Nursing Gown.** _(18 to 25, motherhood.)_ A loose wrapped gown of soft wool, easily opened at the breast, belted high, plain and undyed, a protective bead-string for the infant pinned at the shoulder.

**85. Summer Linen Sheath.** _(18 to 25, hot season.)_ A light sleeveless linen sheath falling straight to the ankle, undyed and cool, a thin beaded cord at the neck, the hair tied up against the heat.

**86. Akitu Festival Adornment.** _(18 to 25, festival.)_ A brightly dyed gown, a tasseled shawl and the full jewelry of faience, carnelian and gold, the eyes kohl-lined, anklets ringing in the New Year procession.

**87. Temple Worshipper's Gown.** _(18 to 25, devotional.)_ A modest gown of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, simple beads and clasped hands, standing before the god as the votive women of Sumer.

**88. Young Weaver-Woman's Gown.** _(18 to 25, work.)_ A plain belted gown with the sleeves bound, spindle and thread at hand, the fingers stained with dye, at the loom that clothed the city.

**89. Matron's Long Wrapped Gown.** _(25 to 35, everyday.)_ A full ankle gown wound and drawn over one shoulder, belted, the hair under a plain woven fillet. The modest daily dress of a settled wife and mother.

**90. Flounced Tiered Gown with Bead Collar.** _(25 to 35, formal.)_ A deep tiered fleece gown with a broad collar of carnelian and lapis, hoop earrings, worn by a woman of standing at the feast.

**91. Household Manager's Gown with Seal.** _(25 to 35, authority.)_ A belted gown of good wool from whose waist hang a pouch and a cylinder seal on a cord, marking the woman who keeps the household's stores.

**92. Mother's Gown with Carrying Sling.** _(25 to 35, motherhood.)_ A practical wool gown belted high, a wide woven sling across the body for a small child, a protective amulet pinned at the shoulder.

**93. Festival Matron's Adornment.** _(25 to 35, Akitu.)_ A richly dyed gown layered with necklaces of carnelian, faience and gold, heavy earrings and a thin diadem, kohl-lined eyes, a tasseled shawl.

**94. Summer Linen Matron's Sheath.** _(25 to 35, hot season.)_ A straight sleeveless linen gown to the ankle, undyed and cool, a folded shawl kept for the temple, a single beaded necklace.

**95. Winter Wool-and-Felt Layers.** _(25 to 35, cold season.)_ A long wool gown under a thick felted mantle drawn over head and shoulders, a felt-lined wrap at the throat, in deep browns.

**96. Mourning Gown of Coarse Wool.** _(25 to 35, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, the jewelry set aside, the hair loosed and dusted with ash, the cloth torn at the breast.

**97. Senior Matron's Full Gown.** _(35 to 45, everyday.)_ A full, modestly cut gown of good wool to the floor, softly belted, the greying hair under a fillet and a light shawl.

**98. Large Flounced Gown of Standing.** _(35 to 45, formal.)_ A long tiered gown with a great bead collar and broad earrings, the dress of a matron of substance and respect.

**99. Keeper of the Stores Gown.** _(35 to 45, authority.)_ A practical good-wool gown with a heavy belt bearing a ring of household tools, a seal and a pouch, the sleeves bound for oversight.

**100. Grandmother's Practical Gown.** _(35 to 45, daily care.)_ A soft, easy gown belted high, a shawl always at the shoulder for a grandchild, an old amulet at the breast.

**101. Festival Display of a Matron of Means.** _(35 to 45, Akitu.)_ A deeply dyed gown layered with gold and carnelian, a broad collar, a diadem over a fringed shawl, scented oils and kohl.

**102. Summer Linen Gown of Middle Life.** _(35 to 45, hot season.)_ A loose ankle-length linen gown, undyed and cool, modest at the neck, a single strand of beads, the hair bound up.

**103. Winter Layered Wool and Felt.** _(35 to 45, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle drawn close over head and shoulders, fleece-lined slippers, a bronze pin at the throat.

**104. Temple Votary's Fine Robe.** _(35 to 45, devotional.)_ A robe of fine pale linen with a fringed shawl over one shoulder, modest jewelry and clasped hands, long practiced in the temple's prayers.

**105. Elder Wife's Full Modest Gown.** _(45 to 55, everyday.)_ A full floor-length gown of soft good wool, soberly belted, the greying hair bound and a light shawl over it.

**106. Matriarch's Flounced Mantle-Gown.** _(45 to 55, formal.)_ A long gown beneath a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, heavy old necklaces kept from her youth, broad earrings.

**107. Mistress of the Household Gown.** _(45 to 55, authority.)_ A good-wool gown with a heavy belt of keys, seal and pouch, commanding the stores and the younger women of the house.

**108. Banquet Adornment of an Elder Lady.** _(45 to 55, feast.)_ A deep-dyed gown with layered gold and carnelian, a diadem over a fine shawl, scented and kohl-lined, honored at the feast.

**109. Summer Elder Gown.** _(45 to 55, hot season.)_ A loose undyed linen gown, modest and cool, a thin shawl for the temple, the hair bound up off the neck.

**110. Winter Wool-Felt Elder Wrap.** _(45 to 55, cold season.)_ A heavy wool gown under a felted over-mantle close about head and shoulders, fleece slippers, a pin at the throat, by the hearth.

**111. Mourning Gown of an Elder Woman.** _(45 to 55, grief.)_ A coarse undyed gown, jewelry removed, the white hair loosed and ashed, the cloth rent. Grave and dignified grief.

**112. Venerable Grandmother's Gown.** _(55 to 65, everyday.)_ A full soft floor-length gown, soberly belted, a shawl over the white hair, a slow and gentle bearing.

**113. Matriarch's Heavy Mantle.** _(55 to 65, formal.)_ A long gown under a great fringed mantle over head and shoulders, the old gold of a lifetime at her ears and throat.

**114. Eldest Woman of the House.** _(55 to 65, authority.)_ A good-wool gown, the keys now passed on yet still consulted, a shawl and a staff, seated where the family gathers.

**115. Banquet Robe of an Aged Lady.** _(55 to 65, feast.)_ A deep-dyed soft gown, the heirloom gold and carnelian, a thin diadem over a fine shawl, seated in honor at the feast.

**116. Grandmother by the Hearth.** _(55 to 65, daily care.)_ A soft warm gown belted high, a child often in her lap, a shawl and an old amulet, slippers of fleece.

**117. Winter Fleece-Wrapped Elder.** _(55 to 65, cold season.)_ A heavy gown under a felted mantle, a fleece wrap over the knees, slippers and a pin at the throat, by the fire.

**118. Aged Votary's Quiet Robe.** _(55 to 65, devotional.)_ A fine pale robe and a soft shawl, seated long in the temple's calm, a lifetime of prayer behind her.

**119. Frail Grandmother's Warm Gown.** _(65 to 80, everyday.)_ A long soft warm gown worn loose, a shawl ever over the thin shoulders and white hair, a staff and a steadying arm.

**120. Most-Honored Matriarch's Mantle.** _(65 to 80, formal.)_ A full soft gown under a great soft mantle over head and shoulders, the heirloom gold light on thin skin, seated in highest honor.

**121. Banquet Honor-Seat Gown.** _(65 to 80, feast.)_ A fine warm gown, the family's oldest gold and a thin diadem over a soft shawl, cushioned and tended at the feast.

**122. Hearthside Frail Gown.** _(65 to 80, frail daily.)_ A loose soft gown easy to wear, a fleece wrap over the lap, soft hide slippers, seated by the fire with a great-grandchild near.

**123. Winter Blanket-Wrapped Elder Woman.** _(65 to 80, cold season.)_ Wrapped in a fleece gown and a thick blanket-mantle, a felt hood and covered hands, close by the hearth.

**124. Mourning of the Very Old Woman.** _(65 to 80, grief.)_ A coarse gown, all gold set aside, the white hair loosed and ashed, mourning the last companions of her years, upheld by daughters.

**125. Last Votary's Gown.** _(65 to 80, devotional.)_ A fine soft pale gown and a gentle shawl, seated and supported in the temple's calm, a long life of prayer at its close.
