## 3 — Angle, Height and Azimuth


These are **three independent dials**, not one. Most people conflate them and lose two-thirds of
their power.

| Dial | What changes | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | camera above or below | who is physically larger |
| **Angle** | camera tilted up or down | power and judgement |
| **Azimuth** | camera around the subject | openness and honesty |

You can shoot an **eye-level angle from a low height** — camera on the floor but not tilted up.
That is a completely different shot from a low angle.
