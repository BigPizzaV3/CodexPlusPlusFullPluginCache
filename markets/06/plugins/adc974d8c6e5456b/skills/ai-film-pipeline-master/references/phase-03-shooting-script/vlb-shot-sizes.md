### Narrated / Voice-Over Shot
**What it is:** an image with no dialogue in it; the voice comes from outside the scene.
**What it does to the viewer:** detaches sound from place — which reads as memory, reflection or commentary.
**Where it fits:** documentary, heritage, film openings, premium advertising.
**Prompt:** `no dialogue, contemplative composition, room for narration`
