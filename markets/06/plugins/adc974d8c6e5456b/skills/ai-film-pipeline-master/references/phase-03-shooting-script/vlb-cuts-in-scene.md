## 10 — Cuts and In-Scene Transitions


### The Governing Law
A cut is not the end of a shot — a cut is **a sentence**. Every cut says something: *and then*,
*meanwhile*, *notice this*, or *something just broke*.

**Murch's hierarchy** — when a cut cannot satisfy everything, sacrifice from the bottom up:

1. Emotion (51% — never sacrifice this)
2. Story
3. Rhythm
4. Eye-trace
5. Two-dimensional plane of screen (the 180-degree line)
6. Three-dimensional continuity of action

Which means: **a cut that is continuity-perfect and emotionally dead is a failed cut.** And the
reverse also holds.
