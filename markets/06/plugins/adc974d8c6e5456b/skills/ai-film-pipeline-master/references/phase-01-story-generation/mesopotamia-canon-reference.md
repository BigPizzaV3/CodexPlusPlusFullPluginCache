# Mesopotamia Canon & Heritage Worldbuilding Reference

Department Owner: **Art & Visual Look Bible (Phase 7: Image Prompt Engineering)**  
Canonical Repository: [`../phase-07-image-prompt-engineering/mesopotamia-canon/`](../phase-07-image-prompt-engineering/mesopotamia-canon/)

This reference connects Phase 1 story architects, narrative worldbuilders, and screenwriters to the master archaeological, historical, and visual canon for ancient Mesopotamian civilizations (Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, Assyria) and modern Assyrian/Syriac heritage.

1. **Canon Rules & Anachronism Guardrails:**

   Rules on historical accuracy, chronological hygiene, divine hierarchies, and cultural integrity.

2. **Clothing & Regalia Library:**  
   [`../phase-07-image-prompt-engineering/mesopotamia-canon/clothing-00-about.md`](../phase-07-image-prompt-engineering/mesopotamia-canon/clothing-00-about.md)

3. **Heritage & Archeological Rules:**  
   [`../phase-07-image-prompt-engineering/mesopotamia-canon/heritage-rules.md`](../phase-07-image-prompt-engineering/mesopotamia-canon/heritage-rules.md)

4. **World Grimoire & Architectural Catalog:**

5. **Canon Router:**
