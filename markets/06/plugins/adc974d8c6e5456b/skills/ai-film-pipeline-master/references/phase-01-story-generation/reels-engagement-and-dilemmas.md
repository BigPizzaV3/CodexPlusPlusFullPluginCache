# Interactive and Choice-Based Fiction on a Feed

**Moved.** The `inter.*` cards that were here are now in
[`interactive-and-branching.md`](interactive-and-branching.md), with the rest of the family. Every interactive
card is in one file.

**Why they moved.** A filename is a routing signal. These cards - second person and its
alternatives, choice framing and option order, timed choices, string-length budgets for localised
interface text, visual-novel skip layering, the meaningful-choice test, ARG trailheads, chat-thread
and community fiction - are the craft of a piece the viewer navigates. Filed under a name about
reels, they were reachable only through the short-form route, so a branching piece that was not
social never found them. A test run built a branching museum film and reached 57 of the 73.

**If you came here for a reel hook**, you want [`short-form-craft.md`](short-form-craft.md) - the
`sf.*` cards are the hook and retention material, and were never in this file.

This file is kept as a pointer rather than removed, because routers outside this package may still
aim at it.
