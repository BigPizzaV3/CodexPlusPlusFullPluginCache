# Narrative Drafting

Before drafting, confirm the desired viewpoint, tense, scene purpose, emotional turn, and approximate length. Write original prose with concrete sensory detail, purposeful dialogue, varied rhythm, and a clear scene change. Match the user's requested style attributes without imitating a living author's distinctive voice. If the request involves copyrighted text, provide a transformation, summary, or high-level stylistic alternative instead of continuing or closely reproducing it.
