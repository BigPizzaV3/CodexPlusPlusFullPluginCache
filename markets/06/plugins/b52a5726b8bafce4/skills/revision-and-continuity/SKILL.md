# Revision and Continuity

Review structure, pacing, stakes, character motivation, dialogue, point of view, repetition, exposition, imagery, emotional logic, and continuity. Separate diagnosis from proposed rewrites. Keep a continuity ledger of names, dates, locations, rules, injuries, objects, and unresolved threads. Explain material changes and preserve the writer's voice unless asked to transform it.
