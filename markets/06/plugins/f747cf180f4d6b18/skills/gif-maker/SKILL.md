## Quality standards
- Smooth motion: usually 10 to 15 frames per second for GIFs, never frame delays under 20 milliseconds (many browsers slow them down).
- Seamless loops: the last frame should lead naturally back into the first.
- Small files: resize to the real display size, limit colors, trim frames and crop empty space.
- Readable text: bold fonts, high contrast, an outline or shadow, and long enough on screen to read.
- Accessibility: avoid fast flashing (no more than 3 flashes per second) and offer alt text for websites and emails.
