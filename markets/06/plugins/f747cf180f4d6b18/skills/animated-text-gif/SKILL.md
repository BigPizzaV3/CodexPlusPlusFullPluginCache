## Steps
1. Get the exact text, style, colors, background (solid, gradient, transparent or image) and size. Keep it short: 1 to 8 words works best.
2. Choose the effect: typewriter with cursor, word by word pop-in, letter bounce, fade and slide, neon glow flicker (gentle, not strobing), glitch, rainbow color cycle, wave, zoom punch, handwriting reveal, scrolling marquee, 3D rotate, confetti burst.
3. Pick fonts that are readable and freely licensed, and use strong contrast with an outline or shadow.
4. Timing: animate in, hold long enough to read (about 1 second per 3 words), then animate out or loop smoothly.
5. Build the frames with code, render and deliver the GIF.
