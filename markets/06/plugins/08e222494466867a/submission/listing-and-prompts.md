# Public Listing

## Name
Atelier Pottery Mentor

## Subtitle
Pottery & ceramics mentor

## Category
Education & Research

## Description
Atelier Pottery Mentor helps ceramic artists learn techniques, critique work, troubleshoot clay, glaze, and firing problems, visualize ideas, and build safer studio practice. It can review uploaded pottery photos, generate ceramics-focused concept images and educational visuals, preview targeted changes to a supplied piece, diagnose issues such as cracking, warping, pinholing, crawling, crazing, shivering, and blistering, explain wheel throwing and handbuilding, reason about clay bodies, pyrometric cones, firing schedules, and kiln behavior, and design controlled glaze tests and firing logs. It separates visible evidence from inference and avoids guessing firing temperatures, hidden properties, or food safety.

## Capabilities
- Critique pottery and ceramic work from photos with specific, constructive next steps
- Generate pottery concepts, form studies, glaze visualizations, and ceramics-focused teaching images
- Edit supplied pottery photos to visualize requested form, glaze, handle, or surface changes
- Teach wheel throwing, handbuilding, trimming, joining, handles, drying, and surface techniques
- Troubleshoot cracking, warping, pinholing, crawling, crazing, shivering, blistering, and other defects
- Reason about clay bodies, shrinkage, absorption, glaze fit, firing atmosphere, and heatwork
- Explain pyrometric cones and firing schedules without treating cone numbers as fixed temperatures
- Plan test tiles, line blends, glaze tests, and firing logs that change one variable at a time
- Review kiln-loading, ventilation, clearance, and firing practices against manufacturer guidance
- Improve studio safety around silica dust, dry materials, glaze chemicals, cleanup, and PPE
- Discuss functional ware cautiously without inventing food-safety or glaze-safety claims
- Use current primary sources for kiln, cone, glaze, material, and studio-safety questions

## Starter prompts
1. Critique this pottery piece and tell me what to improve next.
2. Visualize this piece with the glaze, handle, or form change I describe.
3. Help me diagnose this glaze or firing defect from the photo and firing details.
