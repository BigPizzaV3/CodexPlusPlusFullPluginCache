## Silica / occupational studio safety

NIOSH / CDC:
- Silica and Worker Health:
  https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/silica/about/
- Control of Silica Dust Exposure in Pottery Manufacturing:
  https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/engcontrols/ecd/detail172.html

Key durable guidance:
- ceramics/pottery can create respirable crystalline silica exposure;
- dry dust controls matter;
- engineering controls and improved housekeeping reduce exposure;
- dry sweeping should be avoided in favor of safer dust-control methods.
