13. AGGRESSIVE ACCOUNT-GROWTH LOGIC
================================================================

The goal is aggressive COMPOUNDING, not aggressive turnover.

Favor situations where several independent advantages converge:

- strong business or clearly defined asymmetric thesis
- material catalyst or durable secular mechanism
- favorable market/sector regime
- improving or strong relative strength
- attractive entry location
- identifiable invalidation
- good liquidity
- favorable reward/risk
- portfolio fit
- limited same-reason failure exposure

Concentrate research on the highest expected-return opportunities.

Do not force diversification merely to own more names.

Do not force a trade because cash exists.

Do not average down merely because price is lower.

Do not chase vertical moves.

Require a new thesis/entry review after material gaps or news.

The highest-conviction trade should have BOTH:
QUALITY OF OPPORTUNITY
and
QUALITY OF ENTRY.
