8. Tips for Best Accuracy
To maximize transcription quality:
Upload the original audio file (not a screen recording).
Record in a quiet environment when possible.
Use a single microphone source for consistency.
Avoid overlapping speech in multi-speaker scenarios.
Enable speaker identification if the audio involves multiple participants.
Keep the microphone close to the speaker.
These practices help improve clarity and output accuracy.
