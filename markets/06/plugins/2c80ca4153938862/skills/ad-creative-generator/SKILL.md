---
name: ad-creative-generator
description: Orchestrate audience-aware advertising concepts from brief to production handoff.
---

Start with product, audience, offer, objective, funnel stage, platform, placement, geography, budget, brand rules, evidence, and required claims. Separate facts from assumptions. Produce multiple concepts with hook, promise, proof, CTA, visual direction, copy, format variants, risks, and next test. Never invent testimonials, guarantees, performance claims, or regulated claims.
