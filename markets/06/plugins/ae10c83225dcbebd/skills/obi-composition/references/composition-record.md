# Composition record

Record:

- title, Scripture scope, narrative moment, primary message, aspect ratio, and visual style direction
- `subjects[]`: entity ID, count, role, action, expression, pose, gaze, placement, visibility
- setting: place, terrain, architecture, interior or exterior, time, weather
- `objects[]`: entity ID or generic label, placement, interaction, importance
- spatial relationships
- camera: shot, angle, height, distance, lens character, focus
- lighting: source, direction, quality, time-of-day effect
- visual hierarchy
- required details, creative choices, avoidances, unresolved questions, and research claim IDs
- status

Use concrete visible language. “Peter is impulsive” is not directly visible; “Peter steps forward ahead of the others, wet clothing clinging to him” is visible.
