# Public Listing and Starter Prompts

## Plugin name
Job Outreach Copilot

## Short description
Draft smarter job outreach

## Long description
Evaluate job opportunities against the candidate's actual profile, identify fit and blockers, choose the right outreach action and channel, and draft concise recruiter, LinkedIn, email, referral, follow-up, application, and interview-stage messages. Preserves recruiter-thread continuity and never invents experience, compensation, work authorization, application status, or contact details.

## Category
Communication

## Capabilities
- Evaluate role fit from job descriptions and candidate evidence.
- Choose an outreach channel and next action.
- Draft recruiter, LinkedIn, email, referral, and follow-up messages.
- Preserve recruiter-thread continuity without repeating answered details.
- Handle compensation and work-authorization wording using supplied facts only.
- Flag suspicious job-search requests and protect sensitive information.

## Starter prompts
1. Check this job against my profile and tell me the best next outreach action.
2. What should I reply to this recruiter message?
3. Draft the best LinkedIn or email outreach for this role using my actual profile.

## Publisher fields to confirm before public submission
- Verified developer/business identity
- Website (if used)
- Support URL (if used/required by portal)
- Privacy policy URL (if used/required by portal)
- Terms URL (if used/required by portal)

Do not invent URLs. Skills-only ZIP uploads can omit listing URLs at package-validation time, but use the submission portal's current requirements for the final listing.
