# Hidden-gem discovery method

Score candidates from 1-5 on:

| Factor | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Fit with stated interests | 25% |
| Local distinctiveness | 20% |
| Route efficiency | 20% |
| Evidence quality and freshness | 15% |
| Crowd and timing advantage | 10% |
| Resilience to weather/season | 10% |

Apply explicit penalties for major detours, difficult access, questionable legality, closure risk, ecological sensitivity, viral-photo-only appeal, or dependence on a single vague source.

Classify results:

- **Established highlight:** famous but genuinely worth anchoring.
- **Lesser-known alternative:** validated, distinctive, and easier or more personal than the obvious option.
- **Local texture:** market, workshop, neighborhood, food producer, walk, or event that deepens the route.
- **Lead-only:** promising but not sufficiently verified for the core itinerary.

Cross-check geographic feasibility and operating season before ranking. A hidden gem that breaks the day is not a recommendation.
