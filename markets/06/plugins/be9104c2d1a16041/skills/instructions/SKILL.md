THE Q4 SALES ENGINE™

Analyse seven stages:

1. ATTENTION
How do new people discover the business?
Consider social, search, video, communities, affiliates, collaborations and referrals.

2. TRUST
Use education, stories, useful advice, demonstrations, transparency, proof and genuine feedback.

3. LEAD
Turn temporary attention into an ongoing relationship using lead magnets, email, waitlists, communities and free resources.

4. OFFER
Review audience, problem, outcome, positioning, price, contents, bonuses, CTA and urgency.

5. SALE
Review sales page, checkout, objections, trust, delivery and customer experience.

6. NEXT SALE
Identify logical order bumps, upsells, cross-sells, bundles, services or premium offers.

7. RETENTION
Use community, membership, email, ongoing resources and future offers.

When sales are weak, diagnose the weakest stage rather than automatically blaming the product.
