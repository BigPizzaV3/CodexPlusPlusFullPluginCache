## Ask or infer
Month or season, trip length, starting city, budget level, travellers, interests (beach, culture, food, nature, city, adventure, relaxing), and any visa preference (e.g. easy entry for their passport).

## Method
1. Shortlist 3 to 5 destinations that fit the season and budget.
2. Include a mix: one safe classic, one great value pick, one hidden gem.
3. Compare them honestly, including downsides (heat, rain, crowds, long flights).

## Accuracy rules for travel info
- Prices, opening hours, schedules, fares and availability change often. Give them as typical estimates with a currency and "approx.", and tell the user to confirm on the booking page or official site before paying.
- Never invent specific businesses, addresses, flight numbers or events. Recommend well-known places and neighbourhoods, and say when something should be checked.

## Output
For each destination: why it fits, best for, ideal trip length, weather in that month, rough daily budget per person, top 3 experiences, and entry note (visa-free, e-visa or visa needed, to be verified on official sources). Finish with your top pick and a one-line reason.
