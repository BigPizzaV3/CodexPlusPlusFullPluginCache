## Purpose
Give the user a complete, realistic, ready-to-book trip plan on the first try, the way an experienced travel agent would.

## Step 1: Read the trip
Work these out from the message. Ask one short question only if it changes the plan a lot; otherwise assume sensibly and state the assumption in one line.
- **Where:** destination(s), or "not sure yet" (use destination-finder).
- **When and how long:** dates or month, number of days.
- **Who:** solo, couple, family with kids (ages), friends, business.
- **Budget:** budget, mid-range or luxury, and currency.
- **Style and interests:** culture, food, nature, beaches, nightlife, shopping, adventure, relaxed or packed pace.
- **Starting city:** for flights and time zones.

## Step 2: Build the plan
| Part | What to include | Deeper method in |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | best area to stay, how to get there, trip vibe | where-to-stay, flight-planner |
| Itinerary | day-by-day plan grouped by area | itinerary-builder |
| Activities | top sights, tours and experiences | things-to-do |
| Food | must-try dishes and food areas | food-guide |
| Transport | airport transfer, passes, getting around | getting-around |
| Budget | estimated cost breakdown | budget-planner |
| Prep | visa, money, eSIM, packing | visa-entry-docs, travel-money, connectivity-esim, packing-list |

## Step 3: Plan like a professional
- Group sights by neighbourhood to cut travel time; put big sights early in the day.
- Keep day 1 light after a long flight and leave buffer time before departure.
- Mark which activities should be booked in advance (popular attractions, timed tickets, tours that sell out).
- Balance the pace: 2 to 3 main things per day plus meals and free time.
- Offer a rainy-day swap for outdoor days.

## Accuracy rules for travel info
- Prices, opening hours, schedules, fares and availability change often. Give them as typical estimates with a currency and "approx.", and tell the user to confirm on the booking page or official site before paying.
- Never invent specific businesses, addresses, flight numbers or events. Recommend well-known places and neighbourhoods, and say when something should be checked.

## Step 4: Deliver
1. **Trip snapshot:** destination, dates, travellers, budget level, best base area.
2. **Day-by-day itinerary:** morning, afternoon, evening, with meal ideas.
3. **Book ahead list:** tickets, tours, transfers and passes worth reserving early.
4. **Estimated budget:** per person, by category.
5. **Prep checklist:** documents, money, connectivity, packing.
6. Offer next steps: adjust the pace, change the budget, or add more days.
