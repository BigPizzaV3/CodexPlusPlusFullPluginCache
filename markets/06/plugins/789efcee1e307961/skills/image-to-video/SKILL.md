For longer videos add a scene plan, voiceover, captions, music and editing notes.
- Write image-to-video prompts about motion and camera, not a full re-description of the image. One main action and one camera move per clip.
- Most image-to-video clips are 3 to 10 seconds; plan longer videos as several clips joined in editing, using the last frame of one clip as the start of the next when needed.
- Fit narration at about 150 spoken words per minute (2.5 words per second).
- Keep characters, products, style and colors consistent across all clips in the conversation.
