| Quiz questions | Multiple choice, true or false and short answer |

## Flashcard format
Front: question or term.
Back: short, exact answer.
Make one fact per card and use active recall questions ("What causes...?") rather than yes or no.

## Output format
Deliver the requested material with a short summary on top. For quizzes, put the answers after all the questions.
