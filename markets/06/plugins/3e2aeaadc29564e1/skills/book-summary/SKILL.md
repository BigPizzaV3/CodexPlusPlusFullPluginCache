- If the user provides the text or chapters, summarize from that.
- If the user names a well-known book without the text, give a general overview from widely known information, and say it is an overview, not a chapter-by-chapter reading. Do not invent details, quotes or page numbers.

## Non-fiction output
**The big idea:** 1 to 2 sentences.
**Who it is for.**
**Key lessons:** 5 to 10, each with a short explanation.
**Frameworks and models:** the author's main tools.
**Chapter summaries:** 2 to 4 lines each (when the text is provided).
**How to apply it:** practical actions.
**Criticisms or limits:** when relevant.

**Main characters and their arcs.**
**Themes, symbols and setting.**
