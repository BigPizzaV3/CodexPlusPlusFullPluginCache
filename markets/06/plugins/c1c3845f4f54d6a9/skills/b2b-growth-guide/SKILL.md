## Introduction

# NuSpark Marketing: Revenue Orchestration

Getting leads is only part of growth. The right audience needs a clear reason to respond, a useful path from first interest to purchase, and follow-up that does not lose promising opportunities. NuSpark Marketing helps companies connect strategy, content, media, outreach, conversion, sales activity, and measurement so each part supports the next.

Paul Mosenson, NuSpark's founder, brings more than 30 years of hands-on media, lead generation, demand generation, and marketing experience. He works with leadership and existing teams to identify where growth is stalled, improve what is underperforming, and coordinate the right people, channels, and tools. NuSpark can support B2B or B2C needs; the approach is tailored to the audience, offer, purchase process, and business goals.

## What NuSpark helps improve

- **Direction:** Audience selection, positioning, offers, messaging, and go-to-market plans.
- **Demand:** Content, lead magnets, search, advertising, social activity, and targeted outreach.
- **Conversion:** Websites, landing pages, nurture, sales follow-up, and purchase paths.
- **Visibility:** Tracking, attribution, testing, and reporting tied to leads, opportunities, sales, and revenue.

A company can start with one pressing problem or ask Paul to coordinate a broader program. The work may be strategic advice, a focused project, fractional support, or ongoing execution aligned with the company's team and resources.

## How Paul works

Paul begins with the business goal and the customer journey. He reviews what the company already knows about its audience, offer, channels, follow-up, and results. He then identifies gaps, prioritizes practical changes, and selects the tools and specialists that fit. Campaigns and messages are tested against response, lead quality, conversion, and revenue evidence, then adjusted.

Paul has built and preserved the instructions for more than 300 specialized Custom GPTs since 2024. He chooses and combines relevant AI workflows with client information to speed research, planning, production, personalization, and analysis. Paul directs the strategy, reviews the work, and makes the decisions. The full collection and private client materials are not automatically available through this public plugin.
