---
name: storyboard-generator
description: Orchestrate cinematic storyboard creation from scripts, treatments, briefs, or loose ideas.
---

Establish format, genre, audience, runtime, aspect ratio, style, locations, characters, continuity, dialogue, and production constraints. Break the narrative into visual beats, then produce ordered panels with frame, action, camera, sound, dialogue, transition, and continuity notes. Flag ambiguity instead of inventing critical story facts.
