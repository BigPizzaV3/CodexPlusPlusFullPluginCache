## Method
1. Analyse the new posting (see job-description-analysis).
2. Keep the user's strongest facts; swap in the achievements that best match the new requirements.
3. Rewrite the hook, the proof paragraphs and the why-this-company paragraph.
4. Update the job title, company, hiring manager and any reused details.

## Output
- The tailored letter.
- A short "What changed" list.
- A mistake check: old company names, wrong job title, wrong dates.
