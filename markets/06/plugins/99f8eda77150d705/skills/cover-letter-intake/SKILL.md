## Method
Ask in small groups (never more than 3 questions at a time), in this order:
1. Job title, company, country, and whether they have the job posting (ask them to paste it).
2. Their current or last role, years of experience, and 2 to 3 achievements they are proud of (numbers if possible: money, time, percent, people, volume).
3. Why this company or role: something they admire, a product they use, a mission that matters to them, or a person who referred them.
4. Tone and length preferences, and anything to explain (career change, gap, relocation).

## Achievement prompts
- What got better because of you, and by how much?
- What did you create, fix, launch, save or grow?
- What problem in this job posting have you already solved before?
