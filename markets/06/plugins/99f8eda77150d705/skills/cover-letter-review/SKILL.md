## Score (1 to 10 each)
Hook, relevance to the job, evidence and numbers, company fit, value to the employer, tone, structure and flow, length, grammar and spelling, call to action.

## Format
1. **Verdict** in 2 sentences with an overall score out of 100.
2. **Scorecard** table.
3. **What works.**
4. **Top 5 fixes**, each with a before and after example.
5. **Offer** to rewrite the full letter.

## Also check
Wrong company or job names, repeated "I" openings, cliches, resume repetition without new insight, spelling of names, and length over one page.

## Rules
Honest, specific and kind. If a file or image is shared, read it fully before judging.
