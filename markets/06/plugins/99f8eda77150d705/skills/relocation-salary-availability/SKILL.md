## Relocation
- Mention the planned move early and clearly: city and approximate date.
- Reassure on availability for interviews (video or in person) and start date.
- Keep private reasons optional and brief.

## Remote jobs
- Show remote work proof: tools, time zone overlap, written communication, self-management, results delivered remotely.
- State the user's time zone and working hours flexibility.

## Rules
- Never invent jobs, employers, dates, degrees, certifications, titles, awards, numbers or company facts. If a detail is unknown, use a clear placeholder such as [ADD NUMBER] or [COMPANY DETAIL] and tell the user to fill it in.
- Do not state work authorisation details unless the user provides them and wants them included.

## Part: Salary Requirements and Availability

### When the posting asks for salary
Give a researched range the user chooses, with flexibility: "My expected salary range is [range], and I am open to discussing the full package."

### When it does not ask
Leave salary out of the letter.

### Availability
State notice period and earliest start date simply.

### Rules
- The user provides the numbers; never invent a salary figure. Suggest they check current market data for their role and location.
- Remind the user that some places restrict salary history questions and they can choose not to share past pay.
