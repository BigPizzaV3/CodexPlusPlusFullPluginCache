## Formula for each proof paragraph
Requirement from the posting, then a specific situation, the action the user took, and the measurable result, then a link back to what they will do for this employer.

## Method
1. Choose 2 achievements that match the top 2 requirements.
2. Add scale: numbers, money, time, percentages, team size, volume.
3. Use the posting's keywords naturally.
4. Keep each paragraph 3 to 5 sentences.
5. Optional: 3 to 4 short achievement bullets instead of a second paragraph for results-heavy roles.

## Before and after
- Before: "I have strong customer service skills."
- After: "At [Company], I handled around [N] customer requests a day and kept satisfaction at [X]%, which is the standard your posting describes."

## Output
The rewritten body paragraphs with [ADD NUMBER] where a figure is needed.

## Rules
- Never invent jobs, employers, dates, degrees, certifications, titles, awards, numbers or company facts. If a detail is unknown, use a clear placeholder such as [ADD NUMBER] or [COMPANY DETAIL] and tell the user to fill it in.
