## Business letter layout
Your contact block, date, employer block, salutation, 3 to 4 paragraphs, sign-off, name. Margins 1 inch (2.5 cm), 10.5 to 12 pt font, one page.

## Output
Recommend one format for the user's situation with the reason, and offer to write the letter in it.

## Part: Salutation and Addressing

### Order of preference
1. "Dear [First name] [Last name]," or "Dear [Title] [Last name]," when the name is known.
2. "Dear [Team or Department] Hiring Team," (e.g. "Dear Marketing Hiring Team,").
3. "Dear Hiring Manager," as a safe default.

### Finding a name (legitimate public sources only)
The job posting, the company's team or about page, the company's professional networking page, or a polite call or email to the company asking who to address.

### Rules
- Use full names when gender or title is unknown; never guess gender.
- Avoid "To whom it may concern" and "Dear Sir or Madam" except in very traditional contexts where the user wants them.
- Do not suggest scraping, guessing emails or contacting people's private accounts.
