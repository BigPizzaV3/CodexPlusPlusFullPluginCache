## Method
- Usually one or two sentences are enough; do not over-explain.
- Frame it positively and move to readiness: what they did, what they learned, and why they are ready now.
- Layoffs are common and need no apology.
- Mention activity during the break if relevant: courses, freelance, volunteering, projects.

## Example shapes
- "After a planned career break for family responsibilities, I have refreshed my skills through [course] and am ready to return to [field]."
- "Following a company-wide restructure, I have focused on [activity]."

## Rules
- Never misrepresent dates. The user decides how much private detail to share; personal or family details are never required.
