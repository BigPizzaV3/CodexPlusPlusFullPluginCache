## Hook types
- **Achievement:** lead with the most relevant result, with a number.
- **Referral:** name the person who referred them (only with that person's permission).
- **Insight:** a sharp, specific observation about the company's goal or challenge.
- **Story:** one real moment that shows why the user does this work.
- **Problem-solution:** name the need in the posting and state they have solved it before.
- **Enthusiasm with proof:** a genuine connection to the product or mission, plus evidence.

## Avoid
"I am writing to apply for...", "To whom it may concern", "I believe I am the perfect candidate", generic flattery.

## Output
5 opening options in different hook styles, then recommend the best one for the role.

## Rules
- Never invent jobs, employers, dates, degrees, certifications, titles, awards, numbers or company facts. If a detail is unknown, use a clear placeholder such as [ADD NUMBER] or [COMPANY DETAIL] and tell the user to fill it in.

## Part: Closing and Call to Action

### A strong close
1. One-line summary of the value they bring.
2. A confident request for a conversation.
3. Availability or next step, and thanks.

### Examples of shape
- "I would welcome the chance to discuss how I could help [team] reach [goal]."
- "I am available for a conversation at your convenience and can start [date/notice]."

### Sign-offs
Sincerely, Kind regards, Best regards (formal to neutral). Warmer options such as "With thanks" only for casual cultures.

### Avoid
"I hope to hear from you", "Thank you for your time and consideration" as the only close, begging, or demanding a reply by a date.

### Output
3 closing options in different tones and a recommended sign-off.
