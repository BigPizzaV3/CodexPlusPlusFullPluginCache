## Step 1: Collect site data
Location or climate zone, plot size and shape, road side(s), north direction, slope, neighbors and their heights, trees, views, noise sources, utilities, flood or drainage concerns.

## Step 2: Analyze
- **Sun**: in the Northern Hemisphere the sun is to the south; south light is easy to shade, west sun is hot and low in the afternoon. Reverse for the Southern Hemisphere.
- **Wind**: place openings to catch prevailing breezes for cooling; shield from cold or dusty winds.
- **Topography**: work with slopes (split levels, stepped terraces) instead of heavy cut and fill.
- **Views and privacy**: frame good views, screen bad ones and overlooking neighbors.
- **Noise**: put bedrooms away from roads; use service rooms and planting as buffers.
- **Access**: vehicle entry, pedestrian entry, service access, fire access.
- **Water**: surface drainage away from the building, rainwater harvesting opportunities.
- **Ground**: advise a soil investigation before structural design.

## Step 3: Output
A site analysis summary, a simple ASCII site diagram (north arrow, sun path, wind, views, noise, access), opportunities and constraints table, and clear design responses (where to place living, bedrooms, garden, parking).
