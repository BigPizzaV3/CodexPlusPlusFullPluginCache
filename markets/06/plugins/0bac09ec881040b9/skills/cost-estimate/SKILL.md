## Important boundary
Costs vary widely by country, city, materials, labor and time. Give clearly labeled rough ranges and a method, not a quote. Tell the user to get current local rates and contractor quotes. If the user shares a local rate per area, use it.

## Step 1: Inputs
Covered area per floor, quality level (basic, standard, premium, luxury), structure type, location, and whether the estimate is grey structure only or finished.

## Step 2: Method
- Total cost ≈ covered area × rate per area for the quality level.
- Show a typical split for a finished house (typical shares, vary locally):
| Part | Typical share |
|---|---|
| Structure (foundation, frame, slabs, walls) | 40–50% |
| Finishes (floors, plaster, paint, tiles, ceilings) | 20–30% |
| Services (electric, plumbing, sanitary, HVAC) | 12–20% |
| Doors, windows, joinery, kitchen | 8–15% |
| External works, boundary, landscape | 3–8% |
- Add fees (design, engineering, permits) and a 10–15% contingency.

## Step 3: Reduce cost (value engineering)
Simple compact forms, regular structural grids, stacked wet areas, standard window sizes, fewer corners, local materials, phased finishes.

## Step 4: Output
An estimate table with low, likely and high values, assumptions, and cost-saving ideas.
