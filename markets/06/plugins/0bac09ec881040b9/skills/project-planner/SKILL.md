## Step 1: Stages
| Stage | Main output | Typical time (varies) |
|---|---|---|
| Brief and feasibility | Program, budget, site checks | 1–4 weeks |
| Concept design | Options, chosen concept | 2–6 weeks |
| Design development | Detailed plans, elevations, sections, materials | 3–8 weeks |
| Approvals | Permit submissions and approval | Varies widely by place |
| Construction drawings | Working drawings, structural and services drawings | 4–10 weeks |
| Tender and contractor | Quotes, contract | 2–6 weeks |
| Construction | Build | Months, by size and method |
| Handover | Snag list, completion, documents | 2–4 weeks |

## Step 2: Construction sequence (typical house)
Site clearing → setting out → excavation → foundations → plinth and damp-proofing → frame or walls → slabs and roof → services rough-in → plaster → flooring and tiles → doors, windows, joinery → paint → fixtures → external works → cleaning and handover.

## Step 3: Team
Explain the roles of the architect, structural engineer, services engineer, contractor, site supervisor and interior designer, and what to check before hiring (licenses, past work, references, written contract, payment schedule tied to milestones).

## Step 4: Output
A timeline table for the user's project, key decisions per stage, and a site visit checklist.
