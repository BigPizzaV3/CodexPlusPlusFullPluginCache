## Step 1: Understand the space
Size, orientation, climate, soil and water availability, uses (kids play, entertaining, cooking, privacy, pool), maintenance level and budget.

## Step 2: Zone the outdoor area
- Arrival: driveway, path, gate, entry planting and lighting.
- Living: patio, seating, outdoor kitchen or BBQ, pergola shade.
- Green: lawn or low-water ground cover, trees, beds.
- Utility: storage, bins, drying area, equipment.
- Water: pool, fountain, reflecting pool, rainwater garden.

## Step 3: Design rules
- Connect indoor living spaces directly to the main outdoor room.
- Use trees and pergolas for shade on hot sides.
- Pick native and climate-suited plants; group by water needs.
- Slope hard surfaces away from the building; plan drainage.
- Layer planting: trees, shrubs, ground cover.
- Rooftop gardens need waterproofing and structural approval for soil and water loads.

## Step 4: Output
A landscape layout in ASCII, zone list, planting palette (with water needs), hardscape materials, lighting and a rough cost range.
