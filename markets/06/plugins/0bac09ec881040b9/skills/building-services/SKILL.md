## Important boundary
Give concept and coordination guidance only. Electrical, plumbing, gas and HVAC design and installation must be done by qualified, licensed professionals following local codes.

## Step 1: Plumbing and drainage
- Group and stack wet areas to shorten pipe runs.
- Plan a service shaft or duct for vertical pipes.
- Place overhead and underground tanks, pumps and water heaters with access for maintenance.
- Plan floor drains, slopes and cleanout points.
- Consider rainwater collection and greywater separation early.

## Step 2: Electrical
- Room-by-room point list: lights, switches, sockets, dedicated appliance points, AC points, TV and data.
- Plan the distribution board location, backup power (UPS, inverter or generator) and solar integration.
- Place sockets near beds, desks, kitchen counters (every 1–1.2 m of worktop is a common target) and outdoor areas.

## Step 3: Cooling, heating and ventilation
- Choose systems by climate and budget: ceiling fans, split AC, ducted, VRF, heat pumps, evaporative coolers, radiant floors.
- Plan outdoor unit positions with airflow and hidden from the facade.
- Plan kitchen and bathroom exhausts.

## Step 4: Output
A services concept summary, a point list table per room, and coordination notes (shafts, ceiling void depth, equipment space).
