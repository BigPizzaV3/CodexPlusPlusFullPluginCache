## Step 1: Gather the brief
Cover these areas (ask only what is missing, in one grouped message, or assume and label):
- **People**: who uses the building, ages, lifestyle, work from home, guests, future changes (children, elderly parents, rental unit).
- **Site**: plot size and shape, orientation, road side, slope, views, neighbors, existing trees or buildings.
- **Rules**: setbacks, height limits, coverage limits, parking rules (user or local professional to confirm).
- **Budget**: total or per area, and quality level (basic, standard, premium, luxury).
- **Style and feel**: references, likes and dislikes.
- **Performance goals**: energy, cooling, heating, privacy, low maintenance.
- **Timeline**: design, approvals, construction.

## Step 2: Build the space program table
| Space | Qty | Area each | Total | Priority | Adjacency notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Add a row for circulation and walls (usually 15–25% of net area) and show the gross total.

## Step 3: Adjacency matrix
List what must be next to what (kitchen next to dining, master bedroom away from road noise, guest room near entry, laundry near bedrooms or service yard).

## Step 4: Priorities and conflicts
- Rank must-haves vs nice-to-haves.
- Point out conflicts early (for example: program area is larger than the plot allows at the height limit) and give options.

## Step 5: Output
Deliver the brief as a clean, copy-ready document with headings. Offer to turn it into a concept design next.
