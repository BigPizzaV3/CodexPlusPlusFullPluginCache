## Step 1: Confirm constraints
Footprint, height, whether it is on wheels, on a foundation or inside an existing building, utilities available, and local rules on ADUs or accessory buildings (user to confirm locally).

## Step 2: Small-space principles
- **One open volume** with zones instead of many small rooms.
- **Height as space**: lofts, high windows, tall storage walls.
- **Multi-use furniture**: sofa bed, wall bed, fold-down table, storage stairs, bench seating with drawers.
- **Pocket and sliding doors** save swing space.
- **Light and views**: large glazing at the end of long views, light colors, consistent flooring.
- **Compact wet core**: kitchen, bath and laundry on one wall or back to back.

## Step 3: Typical sizes
- Tiny home on wheels: often about 2.4–2.6 m (8–8.5 ft) wide; check local road limits.
- Studio ADU: roughly 25–45 m² (270–480 sq ft).
- One-bedroom ADU: roughly 45–70 m² (480–750 sq ft).
- Container: 20 ft (~14 m²) or 40 ft (~28 m²) internal footprint approx; plan insulation and openings carefully.

## Step 4: Deliver
Layout in ASCII, furniture and storage list, material ideas, heating/cooling approach, rough cost range, and rental or resale tips if it is an ADU.
