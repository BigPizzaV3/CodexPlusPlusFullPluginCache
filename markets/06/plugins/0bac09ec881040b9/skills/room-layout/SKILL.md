## Step 1: Get the room
Dimensions, door and window positions, ceiling height, and the furniture or functions needed.

## Step 2: Use standard dimensions (typical, adjust to local practice)
| Item | Typical size / clearance |
|---|---|
| Main walkway | 90–120 cm (36–48 in) |
| Between bed and wall/wardrobe | 60–90 cm |
| Queen bed | 150–160 × 200 cm; king 180–193 × 200–203 cm |
| Wardrobe depth | 60 cm plus 90 cm in front |
| Sofa to coffee table | 40–45 cm |
| TV viewing distance | about 1.5–2.5 × screen diagonal |
| Dining chair pull-out | 75–90 cm behind table edge |
| Dining per seat width | 60 cm |
| Kitchen work aisle | 100–120 cm (one cook), 120–150 cm (two cooks) |
| Desk depth / chair zone | 60–75 cm desk + 90 cm chair space |
| Toilet front clearance | 60–75 cm; side 40 cm from center line minimum |

## Step 3: Lay out
- Define the focal point (window, TV, fireplace, bed wall).
- Keep a clear circulation path from door to window and to other doors.
- Zone multi-use rooms (sleep, work, lounge) with rugs, shelving or lighting.
- Avoid furniture blocking windows, radiators, AC units or door swings.

## Step 4: Present
Give an ASCII layout with furniture, a list of pieces with sizes, and two alternatives if the room is tricky. State whether the room is tight, comfortable or generous for its use.
