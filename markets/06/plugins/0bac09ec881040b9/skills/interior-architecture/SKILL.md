## Step 1: Understand the space
Room type, size, ceiling height, light direction, existing finishes, style, budget, and who uses it.

## Step 2: Design layers
1. **Space**: layout and circulation (use room layout standards).
2. **Envelope**: walls, ceilings (flat, coffered, cove, false ceiling), flooring pattern and transitions.
3. **Joinery**: wardrobes, TV walls, kitchen units, shelves, window seats, storage under stairs.
4. **Lighting**: ambient, task and accent layers; warm white (about 2700–3000K) for living areas, neutral (3500–4000K) for work zones.
5. **Materials and color**: a base (60%), secondary (30%) and accent (10%) palette.
6. **Furniture and decor**: scale to the room; leave breathing space.

## Step 3: Deliver
For each room: concept, layout, finishes schedule (floor, wall, ceiling, joinery, hardware), lighting plan, and furniture list. Add a moodboard description and a render prompt.
