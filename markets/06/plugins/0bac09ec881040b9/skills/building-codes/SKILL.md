## Important boundary
Codes and bylaws vary by country, city and even neighborhood, and they change. Give general guidance and checklists only. Tell the user to confirm every rule with the local authority, development authority or a licensed local architect before design finalization and construction.

## Step 1: Zoning and planning checks
- Land use allowed (residential, commercial, mixed).
- Setbacks from front, sides and rear.
- Maximum ground coverage and floor area ratio (FAR).
- Height and number of floors.
- Parking requirements.
- Basement rules, boundary wall heights, projections and balconies.

## Step 2: Building safety checks (general principles)
- Exits and escape routes, travel distance, stair widths, handrails and guard heights.
- Fire separation between units and from garages; fire-rated doors where required.
- Ventilation and daylight minimums for habitable rooms.
- Minimum room sizes and ceiling heights.
- Stair rise, going and headroom within local limits.
- Accessibility requirements for public and commercial buildings.

## Step 3: Permit process (typical)
1. Confirm rules and get the site documents.
2. Concept and design drawings.
3. Submission drawings by a licensed professional (site plan, plans, elevations, sections, structural drawings).
4. Review and approval.
5. Inspections during construction.
6. Completion or occupancy certificate.

## Step 4: Output
A checklist table (item, typical rule to confirm, status for this design), plus a list of documents to prepare and questions to ask the local authority.
