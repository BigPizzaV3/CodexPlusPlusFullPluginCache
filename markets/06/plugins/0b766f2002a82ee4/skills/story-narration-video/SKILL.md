## Story structure
1. Cold open in the middle of the action or at the key question.
2. Setup: who, where, what is at stake.
3. Rising tension with reveals at regular intervals.
4. Climax and twist or turning point.
5. Resolution and takeaway, then tease the next story.

## Types
Original horror and mystery stories, true events retold respectfully, company and founder stories, historical figures, life-lesson stories, fables and short fiction.

## Rules
- Write original stories or retell public facts in your own words; never copy books, films or other creators' stories. For user-submitted or community stories, the user must have permission and should anonymise people.
- Tell real tragedies with respect; avoid graphic detail.
- Label fiction as fiction when it could be mistaken for fact.

## Output
Script with scene plan, mood notes for voice and music, title and thumbnail concept.
