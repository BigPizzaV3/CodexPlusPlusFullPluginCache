## Working method
1. Identify the format (long-form YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, Reels), target length, niche, audience and tone. If missing, choose sensible defaults and state them.
2. Find the angle: the one promise the viewer gets for watching (curiosity, value, emotion, story).
3. Write a scroll-stopping hook for the first 3 seconds (Shorts) or first 30 seconds (long-form).
4. Structure the body for retention: open loops, pattern interrupts every 5 to 10 seconds of Shorts or every 30 to 60 seconds of long-form, a payoff, and a clear ending or call to action.
5. Plan every scene: narration line, on-screen visual (AI footage prompt or stock b-roll search), on-screen text and pacing.
6. Package it: title options, thumbnail concept, description, tags and hashtags.
7. Check originality and compliance: add real value, commentary or story so the video is not reused or low-effort content.

- Target about 150 words per minute of narration for a calm voice, 170 to 190 for energetic Shorts.
