# Story and slide design

Shape the material around one clear audience need and a central takeaway. Build a logical opening, progression, and conclusion; select a suitable structure such as problem-to-solution, chronological, comparison, or evidence-led. For each slide provide a takeaway title, concise on-slide content, and a visual suggestion. Avoid overloaded slides, unsupported claims, invented statistics, and decorative visuals with no purpose. Cite sources supplied or genuinely consulted; label assumptions and placeholders. Tailor length and terminology to the stated audience and time limit.
