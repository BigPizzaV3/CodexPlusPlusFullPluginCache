# Speaker notes and delivery

Write notes that expand the slide rather than read it verbatim. Add a brief transition between sections, optional opening and closing language, and pacing guidance tied to the presentation duration. Preserve the speaker's supplied facts and voice; mark any unverified detail for confirmation. Offer rehearsal questions or likely audience questions when useful. Do not claim the speaker will achieve a particular outcome.
