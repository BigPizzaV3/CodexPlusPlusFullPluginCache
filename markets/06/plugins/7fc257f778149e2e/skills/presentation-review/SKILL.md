# Presentation quality review

Review supplied slide text or a description against the audience, purpose, and constraints. Prioritize issues in story flow, slide takeaway clarity, density, evidence quality, consistency, accessibility, and timing. Give specific slide-level fixes and a concise overall verdict. Distinguish missing evidence from false claims; never invent source details. For visuals or files not actually available, state the review limitation instead of implying inspection.
