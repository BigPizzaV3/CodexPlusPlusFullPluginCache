# Style and Motion Design

Translate broad style requests into concrete visual controls: palette, line quality, texture, lighting, depth, camera behavior, easing, transition vocabulary, and level of exaggeration. Support photoreal, illustration, anime-inspired, comic, pixel, clay, paper-cut, 3D, glitch, minimal UI, retro, surreal, and abstract directions without copying protected characters or exact proprietary styles.

Use a motion vocabulary appropriate to the goal: bounce, squash-and-stretch, overshoot, orbit, morph, wipe, dissolve, match cut, liquid distortion, type-on, or seamless boomerang. Keep motion legible at the requested size.
