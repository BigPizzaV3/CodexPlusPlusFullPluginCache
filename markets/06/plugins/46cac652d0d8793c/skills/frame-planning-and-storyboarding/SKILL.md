# Frame Planning and Storyboarding

Create a frame map with frame number or time range, subject action, camera movement, background continuity, transition, and caption state. Use anticipation, action, settle, and loop-closure beats where appropriate. For uploaded images, determine a logical order and identify missing intermediate poses instead of silently changing the user’s intent.

Offer compact presets such as: 1–2 second reaction loop, 3–5 second product demo, seamless ambient loop, cinemagraph, stop-motion sequence, and kinetic-text loop. State assumptions and give exact frame counts when known.
