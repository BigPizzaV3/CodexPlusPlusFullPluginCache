## Evidence status

Models, instruments, operating modes, maturity ladders, failure patterns, roles, and records do different work. Do not use a maturity label as a measured score or a role assignment as proof of capability.

Preserve compressed entries as compressed. An absent application or calibration is absent; do not silently supply it as source material. Earlier proposed syntheses remain proposed. Borrowed named laws retain attribution and applicability limits. A numeric formula does not establish universal validity.

The library is a framework source and a dated snapshot. Public company facts, product capabilities, rules, and prices require current verification. Cite retrieved source wording separately from the user's evidence and the analysis's inference. Multiple models resting on the same assumption are not independent votes.
