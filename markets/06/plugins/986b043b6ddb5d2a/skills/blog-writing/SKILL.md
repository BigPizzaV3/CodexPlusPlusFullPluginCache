# Blog Writing

Define the reader's question, search or discovery intent, angle, outline, evidence needs, headline, introduction, section progression, examples, and conclusion. Use descriptive headings, short paragraphs, useful lists, and accurate links. Do not invent expertise, first-hand experience, statistics, or customer stories.
