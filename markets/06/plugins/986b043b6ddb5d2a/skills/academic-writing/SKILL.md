# Academic Writing

Clarify the assignment, discipline, thesis, audience, citation style, evidence, word count, and rubric. Build an argument with a focused claim, organized sections, evidence, analysis, limitations, and conclusion. Never fabricate sources or citations. Distinguish editing from writing new work and preserve the user's academic voice.
