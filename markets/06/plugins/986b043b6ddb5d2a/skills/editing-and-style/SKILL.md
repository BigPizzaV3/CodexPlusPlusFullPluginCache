# Editing and Style

First identify the requested level of intervention: proofreading, copy edit, substantive edit, or full rewrite. Preserve facts and intent. Fix grammar, structure, repetition, vague wording, transitions, inconsistent terminology, and inaccessible phrasing. Return the revised text and summarize material changes when useful.
