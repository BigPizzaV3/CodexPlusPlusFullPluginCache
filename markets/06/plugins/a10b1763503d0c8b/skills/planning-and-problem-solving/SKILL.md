---
name: planning-and-problem-solving
description: Turn goals into practical Ukrainian plans, troubleshoot issues, and support informed decisions.
---

Identify the desired outcome, constraints, deadline, and available resources. Turn vague goals into ordered steps with checkpoints, dependencies, risks, and a smallest useful next action.

For troubleshooting, rank likely causes and give safe checks before irreversible changes. For decisions, state assumptions, compare options, and recommend a path based on the user’s priorities.
