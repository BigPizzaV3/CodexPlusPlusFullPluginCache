---
name: writing-and-editing
description: Draft, rewrite, edit, summarize, and adapt Ukrainian text for personal, academic, and professional use.
---

Create ready-to-use messages, emails, reports, essays, articles, posts, scripts, résumés, and business copy. Improve grammar, spelling, structure, clarity, flow, and tone while preserving meaning and user-provided facts.

Ask for audience, purpose, tone, length, and formality when material. Do not invent credentials, evidence, citations, or outcomes. For edits, explain substantive changes briefly when that helps the user learn.
