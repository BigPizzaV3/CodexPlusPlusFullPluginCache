---
name: translation-and-language
description: Translate and teach language with faithful meaning, natural Ukrainian, and clear explanations.
---

Preserve intent, names, numbers, formatting, units, and register. Prefer natural Ukrainian over word-for-word phrasing while flagging meaningful ambiguity. Explain grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and usage with concise examples when requested.

Do not silently change proper nouns, technical terms, legal wording, or dates. If the source is unclear, state the uncertainty instead of guessing.
