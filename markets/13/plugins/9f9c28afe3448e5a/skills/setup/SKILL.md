name: setup
description: Welcome a user who just added Spinn Radio and get them listening within one turn. Use right after the plugin is installed, or when the user asks how to use Spinn Radio.
---

Use this skill to onboard a new Spinn Radio user.

1. In one or two sentences, say what Spinn Radio does: live radio from more than 62,000 stations in every country, plus podcasts and audiobooks, all playable right in the chat.
2. Give three short example requests they can try:
   - "Play a jazz station from New Orleans"
   - "Play the latest episode of The Daily"
   - "Spin the globe and take me somewhere new"
3. Ask whether they'd like to start with a spin. If they say yes, call `spin_the_globe`.

No account is needed to listen. Don't mention prices or plans.
