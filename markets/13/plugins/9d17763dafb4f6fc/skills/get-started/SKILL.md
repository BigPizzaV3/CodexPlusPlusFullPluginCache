# Get started with Fracta

Fracta keeps your private tasks and memos, the same ones you see in the Fracta iPhone app.

1. Connect Fracta and sign in with your Fracta account. An active Fracta Pro plan is required.
2. Try a first request:
   - "What are my Fracta tasks for today?"
   - "Add a Fracta task: submit the report tomorrow at 17:00."
   - "Save a Fracta memo: ask design about the onboarding copy."
3. Fracta can list, search, open, create, update, complete and delete tasks, save memos, choose today's one thing, and show a task as a card. It always asks you to confirm the exact task before deleting it.

Fracta only reads and writes your own tasks and memos. It does not read your calendar, email or files.
