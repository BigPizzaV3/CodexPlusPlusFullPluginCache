# Biology 101 notes: Photosynthesis

- Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.
- It takes place in the chloroplasts of plant cells.
- Overall equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2.
- The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes and produce ATP, NADPH, and O2.
- The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma and uses ATP and NADPH to fix CO2 into sugar.
- Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green light.
