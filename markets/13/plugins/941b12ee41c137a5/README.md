# OpenMail

Search, read and reply to your email on any IMAP provider. Read-only by default; sending is opt-in per mailbox and every email needs your OK. Never deletes or marks mail as read.

Remote MCP server: https://mcp.openmail.tools/mcp (OAuth 2.1, scopes mail.read and opt-in mail.send).

Source: https://github.com/webumer/openmail
