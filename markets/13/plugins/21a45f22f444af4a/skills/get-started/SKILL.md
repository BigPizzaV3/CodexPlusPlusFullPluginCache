# Get started with Hachimo

Hachimo keeps your private English-learning memory: daily plans, lesson records, review cards and progress, the same ones you see in the Hachimo iPhone app.

1. Connect Hachimo and sign in with your Hachimo account.
2. Try a first request:
   - "Show my Hachimo plan for today."
   - "Give me one Hachimo review card. Don't show the answer until I reply."
   - "Save this lesson to Hachimo as today's materials."
3. Hachimo previews everything before saving a lesson, and never reveals a review answer before you try it.

Hachimo only reads and writes your own learning data. It does not read your email, calendar or files.
