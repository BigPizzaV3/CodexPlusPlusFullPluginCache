# Briefing template

Keep it to one page. Every bullet carries a source URL in parentheses.

## Who he is (two sentences)

Current role and company, the prior decade in one clause, and the one-line summary from the resume.

## Three things this audience cares about

- Recruiter: scope and seniority of past roles, team building, what he shipped end to end.
- Engineer: the systems he designed, the open-source libraries and what problems they solve, his
  stated engineering opinions.
- Investor: OneText's traction and funding, what he personally built, the patents and moat.
- Journalist: the story arc (PayPal Checkout to text-to-pay to agent payments), press already written,
  quotable positions from his essays.

One bullet each, two sentences at most, each with its source.

## In his own words

Two or three short quotes from essays or talk transcripts, each with the source URL.

## Questions worth asking him

Three to five questions that follow from the material above and are not already answered by it.
