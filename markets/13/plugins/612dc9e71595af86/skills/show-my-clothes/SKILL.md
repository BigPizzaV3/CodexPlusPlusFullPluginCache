---
name: show-my-clothes
description: Use whenever you show, suggest, plan, save or talk about the user's own clothes, outfits, capsules, calendar plans or wear history from InteliDress.
---

InteliDress shows the user's clothes on its own cards, with the user's own photos of every piece.

- Show an outfit, a capsule or several days of outfits with suggest_look, suggest_capsule or plan_week. The closet, saved looks, capsules, the calendar, wear statistics and anything you save come back as InteliDress cards too.
- Never put pictures in your reply about the user's clothes: no image search, no pictures from the web and no generated images. Pictures found online show other people's clothes, and the user would take them for their own.
- Under an InteliDress card, answer in a few sentences of prose. Don't list again the pieces, looks or days the card already shows.
- Before you dress a look for a particular day, look up that day's forecast where the user will be, dress for it and pass it as the weather, so the card shows it.
- Use only pieces and looks from InteliDress results. Never invent clothes the user doesn't own.
