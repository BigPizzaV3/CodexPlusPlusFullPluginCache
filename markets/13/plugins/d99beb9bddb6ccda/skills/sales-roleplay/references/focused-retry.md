# Focused retry

After ENDED, accept `/retry Txx`, `Retry Txx`, “Retry this response”, 「この発言を再練習する」 or clear equivalent. Resolve the explicit ID first; a bare request uses a clearly selected card or sole substantive turn. If ambiguous, ask one compact clarification showing at most two actual eligible IDs. Unknown, unavailable, excluded or non-substantive targets do not start a retry. With no evidence, offer fresh practice rather than an invented historical target.

Use the supported buyer moment immediately before that seller statement. Preserve scenario ID, six fields, languages, fact classes/UNKNOWNs and historical quotes; reset only later rehearsal disclosures/agreements. Missing context requires recovery or explicit acceptance of a clearly labeled new fictional cue, not a claim to have restored lost dialogue.

Prepare the selected target/goal and a new Start/Edit gate with no buyer cue. Only the later full current Start label starts RETRY_ACTIVE and gives one new simulated buyer cue. Retain a fresh R1-T01… namespace (then R2…), separate from original Txx/Ax-Txx. Do not replay the original seller line as new speech or count a displayed suggestion as performed.

Respond once per actual new seller message. The new buyer opener/cue also obeys the short real-buyer style: preferably 2–3 sentences (maximum 4), one main question, no consultant-style lists or coaching. Controls preserve the selected target, context and retry count. At actual stop, local business conclusion or three new seller turns, use the same End/Continue gate. Continue retains an exhausted cap. Only confirmed ending creates the comparison once.

Display **Previous Response / New Response / What Changed** (Japanese: **前の発言 / 今回の発言 / 変わった点**):

- Previous Response: exact selected original quotation with its attempt/ID, labeled historical.
- New Response: every actual new retry quotation with its Rn-Txx ID, in source order.
- What Changed: one or two contextual sentences grounded in the new speech; say unchanged/mixed when appropriate. Zero new substantive speech means no new substantive response or improvement claim. Account for a non-substantive acknowledgment without crediting improvement.

Preserve original feedback and new qualitative records separately. No numerical improvement, unsolicited assessment badges, mastery, saved progress or buyer replay. Another retry is optional; restart prepares a fresh full-practice Start gate. Use explanation language for instructions/comparison, training language for buyer dialogue and suggestions, and exact original languages for quotes. Completed actions follow feedback.md; a saved definition for a completed retry comes from its prepared source scenario, not the retry cue or dialogue.
