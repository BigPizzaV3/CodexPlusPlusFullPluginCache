# Fina support

For help connecting Fina, using the plugin, correcting your financial records, or managing privacy requests, contact **support@finamoney.co**.

[Email Fina support](mailto:support@finamoney.co)

Describe the assistant you are using, the action that failed and the error message. Do not post passwords, authentication codes, complete account/card numbers, financial statements or private financial records in public repository issues.

For account access, deletion or privacy requests, use the private support email above. Fina verifies ownership before acting on an account request.

Fina is a service operated by GOPIN SAS. See the published [privacy policy](https://finamoney.co/privacy-policy) and [terms and conditions](https://finamoney.co/terms-conditions).
