Use this skill when the poster is a present for someone else. The user's explicit instructions always come first.

## Flow

1. Ask for the runner's name as it should appear on the poster, the race, the year and their finish time. The user gives these; Routeframe has no race results.
2. `find_race` confirms the race and year; `preview_poster` draws the poster with the runner's details and an optional short note, such as "First marathon" or the date.
3. The gift is the poster's design and its preview in the card; there is no file to download.

## Scope

- Keep the note short and in the user's words.
- The tool results say what this user can do with the poster.
