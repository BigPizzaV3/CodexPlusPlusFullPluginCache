# Choose a work base

1. Establish duration or dates, budget/currency, team hours, workspace and connectivity requirements, and non-negotiable constraints. Reuse the setup brief. If the user supplies candidates, compare those; otherwise select at most three candidates and explain why they fit the supplied criteria.
2. Before recommending current locations, research with available browsing. Read `../setup/references/planning-contract.md` for source standards. If research is unavailable, produce a provisional framework using user facts and labeled assumptions, with no confident current location recommendation.
3. Build a comparison table covering housing for the actual stay length and season, expected total spending, team overlap, evidence for workspaces/connectivity, local transport, and user-specific needs. For each material entry show a source or evidence label. Use `plan-budget` and `coordinate-hours` for numerical claims. Never substitute annual averages or citywide broadband claims for the proposed accommodation.
4. Gate hard constraints before ranking. A candidate that fails a required overlap or budget limit cannot win on lifestyle. Mark legal permission or employer approval unknown until verified rather than treating it as a scoring preference. Exclude blocked candidates from the recommendation, or clearly make it conditional on resolving the blocker.
5. Recommend the strongest supported option, a runner-up, and the one fact most likely to reverse the decision. If evidence is too thin, give a research shortlist instead of a winner. Prefer a transparent qualitative decision over a fabricated weighted score. Use weights only if the user requests them, show the formula, and keep unknowns visible.
6. Add copy-ready accommodation questions: measured download/upload and latency at the desk, video-call reliability, backup connection/power, desk/chair details, quiet hours, cancellation terms, and extra fees. Draft only.

Completion: an evidence-labeled comparison, justified or conditional recommendation, and a short verification queue. Do not book or assert visa/tax eligibility.
