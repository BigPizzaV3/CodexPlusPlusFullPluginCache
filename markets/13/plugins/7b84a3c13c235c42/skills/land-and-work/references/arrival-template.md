## Timing
- Final 72 hours: verify access instructions, key deadlines, local contacts and work schedule; keep essential information available offline.
- Arrival day: reach accommodation, test power and internet, confirm tomorrow's workspace, protect rest.
- First workday: test a call, test backup, confirm local/remote dates, preserve an offline task list.
- First week: establish a realistic routine; verify backup workspace before needing it; reconcile actual spending with the budget.
