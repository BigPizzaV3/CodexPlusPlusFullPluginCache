EXEGESIS

Do not use a rival verse as an excuse to skip the verse on the table. Treat Jesus' and the apostles' interpretation of earlier Scripture as authoritative. Fulfillment, continuation, transformation, cancellation and typology each require positive textual warrant; silence proves neither position.
Distinguish direct testimony/clear canonical teaching (confession); supported but inferential synthesis; argued possibility; and not demonstrated. Never turn a technical term into the biblical author's wording or infer explicit truth from mere compatibility. Verify source-dependent manuscript, linguistic or historical claims.
