# Labels and catalogue series

Labels are context for evidence, not recommendations. A label tells you who recorded,
mastered and pressed a record and when; the score still comes from the credits,
runouts, plant and community data on the actual release. Use this page to read a
catalogue number, to know which label names signal a careful reissue, and to place a
record in its scene. Never suggest a record from here that the user did not ask about:
WOIII.me's suggestions come only from the user's own collection and its own canons.

## Jazz

- **Blue Note** (peak 1955–1970). Alfred Lion's sessions, Rudy Van Gelder's recording
  and cutting (an "RVG" stamp in the runout), Reid Miles' covers. The BLP 1500 series
  is the 1950s (12" from BLP 1501), the BLP 4000 series runs from 1958 (4001) through
  the mid-1960s; "BST 84xxx" is the stereo numbering. Lexington Ave and W. 63rd St
  label addresses and a deep groove mark the earliest pressings; Liberty-era (1966 on)
  and United Artists pressings are later. Japanese Toshiba/King pressings from the
  1970s–80s are well made.
- **Prestige** (1949–1972). One-take sessions, Van Gelder on most 1950s dates; PRLP
  7xxx numbers run roughly in chronological order. **New Jazz** is its sibling.
- **Impulse!** (1961–1977). Orange-and-black spines, gatefolds; A- / AS- numbers (mono
  / stereo) are the ABC-Paramount originals, later "AS-" reissues by ABC and MCA.
  Coltrane, Pharoah Sanders, Alice Coltrane.
- **ECM** (1969 on). Manfred Eicher; the catalogue is numbered in order and German
  pressings are the reference.
- **Strata-East** (1971 on). Artist-owned; small runs, Charles Tolliver, Stanley Cowell.
- **Black Jazz** (1971–1975). About twenty releases, West Coast spiritual and modal jazz.
- **Tribe** (Detroit, 1972–1977), **Muse**, **Theresa**, **SteepleChase** (Denmark,
  clean pressings), **DIW** and **Three Blind Mice** (Japan, close-miked trio dates).

## Soul, funk and R&B

- **Motown** (Tamla, Gordy, Soul). Detroit pressings before the 1972 move to Los
  Angeles; UK Tamla Motown pressings are well made.
- **Stax / Volt**. The yellow Stax label is the classic era; after 1968 the label
  went independent and the catalogue deepens.
- **Philadelphia International** (1971–1984). Gamble and Huff; orchestral soul,
  plentiful originals.
- **Hi Records**. Willie Mitchell's Royal Studios sound; Al Green, Ann Peebles.
- **Curtom**. Curtis Mayfield's label, soundtracks included.
- Reissues: **Numero Group** (deep research, compilations).

## Electronic and dance

- **Warp** (1989 on). WAP numbers; early catalogue originals are sought after.
- **Tresor**, **Ostgut Ton** (Berlin techno), **R&S** (Belgium, early 1990s),
  **Hyperdub**, **Clone** (electro, Clone Classic Cuts reissues), **Planet Mu**.
- Smaller: **Night Slugs**, **Hessle Audio**, **Livity Sound**, **L.I.E.S.**,
  **Music From Memory** (ambient and Balearic reissues).

## Hip hop

- **Def Jam** (Rick Rubin era mid-1980s), **Tommy Boy** (12" singles),
  **Stones Throw**, **Rawkus** (Soundbombing compilations), **Fondle 'Em**,
  **Rhymesayers**, **Definitive Jux**.

## Rock, indie and alternative

- **4AD** (Vaughan Oliver sleeves), **Factory** (FAC numbers, Peter Saville; the
  label closed in 1992, so its pressings are finite), **Sub Pop** (SP numbers below
  about 100 are the early singles-club era), **SST**, **Creation** (CRE numbers),
  **Touch and Go**, **Dischord**, **Rough Trade**.

## Reggae and dub

- **Studio One** (Coxsone Dodd; Jamaican originals are scarce, Soul Jazz reissues
  careful), **Trojan** (UK licensing, compilations), **Pressure Sounds** and
  **Blood & Fire** (UK reissues), **Wackies** (Bronx), **On-U Sound**.

## African and global

- Reissue labels with research and good pressings: **Soundway**, **Analog Africa**,
  **Strut**, **Awesome Tapes From Africa**, **Mr Bongo**.
- Originals: Nigerian (Tabansi, EMI Nigeria, Decca West Africa), Ghanaian
  (Essiebons), Ethiopian (Amha, Kaifa, Philips Ethiopia).

## Library and soundtrack

- **De Wolfe**, **KPM**, **Bruton** (UK library music: never sold in shops, so most
  copies are first pressings); **CAM**, **Deneb**, **Beat Records** (Italian);
  reissues from **Cinedelic**, **Schema**, **Light in the Attic**, **Now-Again**.

## Classical

- **Deutsche Grammophon** (tulip-rim label = early), **Decca** (grooved labels =
  first pressings; wide-stage recordings), **Columbia Masterworks** (six-eye label
  = early US), **Harmonia Mundi**, **Nonesuch**, **Erato**.

## Reissue labels, and what they are known for

| Label | Known for |
|---|---|
| Analogue Productions | All-analogue chains, QRP pressings, UHQR editions |
| Music Matters (closed in 2020) | Blue Note at 33 and 45 RPM, Kevin Gray cuts |
| Speakers Corner | Pallas pressings in Germany, analogue sources |
| Pure Pleasure | UK cuts and pressings of jazz and soul |
| Be With Records | Soul, jazz, library and boogie, careful curation |
| Mr Bongo | Brazilian, Latin and African titles |
| Numero Group | Soul, gospel and funk compilations with deep research |
| Light in the Attic | Eclectic and global, strong packaging |
| Craft / Concord | Prestige, Fantasy and Stax catalogues; quality varies by title |

When a reissue from one of these exists, name it as a candidate the survey can
check; the score decides, not the label name.
