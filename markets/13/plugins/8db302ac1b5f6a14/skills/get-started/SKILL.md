# Generate routes with Remap

Ask for a route from somewhere, or let the map use your location, with a distance or a time, and
Remap generates several routes that start and finish there. Name a destination instead and it generates a
route there; on a bike tour it splits the trip into days, with places to sleep along the way. They appear on a map in the conversation; tap Ride, Run, Walk or Hike on one
to open it in the Remap app.

Try:

- "A 40 km ride from the Ferry Building in San Francisco, as flat as possible."
- "A 45 minute trail run from where I am."
- "A hilly 15 km hike from Ambleside in the Lake District."
- "A bike tour from Amsterdam to Brussels, about 100 km a day."
- "A one hour walk from my hotel near Kings Cross, through parks."

Say what you want more or less of (shorter, longer, flatter, hillier, quieter roads) and Remap generates new routes.

Connect your Remap account and it also remembers you:

- "Remember that I mostly ride a road bike, about 40 km, on quiet roads."
- "Save the second route to my Remap account."
- "What were my last three rides?"
