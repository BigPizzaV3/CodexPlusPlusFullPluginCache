For an unresolved interpretation, follow official links to primary legislation
on wetten.overheid.nl or zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl. Separate enacted law
from a consultation or proposal. A newer publication is not necessarily an
effective rule. If the official guidance cannot resolve the particular fact
pattern, leave it open for qualified review instead of constructing a new rule.

Use **Nederlandse Autoriteit Uitleenmarkt (NAU)** for the admission authority.
Do not confuse it with the **Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie**, the enforcement
authority; their English translations can look similar.
