# Making a Say It Again video

Say It Again makes AI UGC videos of a store's products: a generated presenter films themselves with the product. A video is made in steps: read_product with the product's link, which also lists the angles a video of it can argue; then optionally write_scripts and get_scripts to read three scripts first; then quote_video for the price; then make_video, then get_video until its status is ready or failed. A video can be built like an ad the store holds, which list_ads lists, and said by a presenter of the team's own, drawn with describe_presenter, shown with get_described and kept with keep_presenter, whose voice set_presenter_voice says when an Arabic video asks for it. A ready video lists in after_steps what can be done with it next and what each costs: change_video, make_longer, another_language and other_sizes. Say It Again also makes product videos, the product alone on camera with a voiceover or music and no presenter: quote_product_video for the price, then make_product_video, then get_product_video until its status is ready or failed, a ready one listing its available remakes, another_language and other_sizes in after_steps; and image ads of a product, each a picture drawn whole with its own words: quote_image_ads for the price of a set, then make_image_ads, then get_image_ads until its status is ready or failed. make_video, change_video, make_longer, another_language, remake_product_video, make_product_video and make_image_ads spend the team's credits, at the price quote_video, after_steps, quote_product_video or quote_image_ads gives, refunded if the video or the set fails. Every other tool is free. In ChatGPT, Say It Again makes no script, no video and no image ad for these kinds of product, promotional or not, and a tool asked for one refuses it: adult and sexual products and services, among them sex toys; real-money gambling; marijuana, THC and psilocybin products, illegal drugs, and CBD products whose THC is over the legal limit or not stated; drug paraphernalia; prescription and age-restricted medicines; counterfeit, replica, stolen or contraband goods, fraud tools and pirated software; malware, spyware and covert surveillance devices; tobacco and nicotine products, among them vapes and e-liquids; weapons, among them firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks and self-defense weapons, and extremist merchandise; and fraudulent or high-risk financial, identity, document and travel services. The pictures of the team's products and of the presenters these tools list are the ones Say It Again's display shows with the answers of read_product, list_presenters, get_video and list_videos; a picture from another source shows another product or another person. Those answers are given in words, with what matters in each and the next step, and cards or images of what the display shows would only repeat it, unless the person asks for a list or a table.

## What the tools take

- Every video starts from a product's link, or the id read_product gave for it.
- Every choice has a default, used when it is left out.
- make_video refuses a video the team's balance does not cover, which quote_video says before it.
- make_video takes an idempotency_key: the same key sent again for the same video answers as the first call did and spends nothing more.

## What the answers hold

- What is known of a product, a script, a price or a video is what the tools answered, and the product's facts are the ones read_product gave.
- A ready video's download link is the one in the latest answer, and get_video makes a new one for a later download.
- A video that fails says why in get_video's answer, and its credits are refunded.

## Lines for the presenter

Lines sent in the lines argument, and words a description gives someone to say, are said word for word as sent, not translated, and read by no check of ours. They come from what the person asks for and what read_product gave, and this rule holds for every one of them.

Every person in the ads we make, on camera or heard as a voice, is made by AI and is not a customer. Nothing in an ad may present them as someone who has used the product, because that is a fake testimonial and the law forbids it. So they have no past with it: no time spent with it, no result it has had on their own body, home, pet or life, no before and after of their own, no having bought it, and no job, family role or other identity that makes them sound like a real customer. What they have is the moment: showing the product, using it on camera and reacting to what it does there, saying what it is and what it does, and the facts the product page gives.
