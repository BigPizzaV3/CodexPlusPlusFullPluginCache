# Original Sort Generator Tool Example
This reference preserves the structure and behavior of the original Sort Generator.

## Example input
Grade level: 6th grade
Topic: Figurative language

## Menu pattern
### Knowledge Building
Sequential Sort; Classification/Category Sort; Examples vs. Non-Examples; True/False; Matching Sort; Prior Knowledge vs. New Learning.
### Critical Thinking
Cause-and-Effect; Compare-and-Contrast; Fact vs. Opinion; Evidence-Based; Multiple Perspectives; Analogical; Error Analysis; Value/Opinion.
### Application
Problem-Solution; Prediction; Priority/Ranking; Hypothesis Testing.

## Fully developed example
The teacher selected Classification/Category Sort, "Figure It Out." The original tool produced an objective, 15–20 minute time, pairs or groups of 3–4, 25 cards with five examples per category, teacher setup, learner directions, discussion questions, sample key, supports, formative checks, misconceptions, digital and physical options, transfer ideas, and an optional four-level rubric.

Representative cards:
Simile: "She was as busy as a bee."
Metaphor: "The classroom was a zoo."
Personification: "The wind whispered through the trees."
Hyperbole: "I've told you a million times."
Idiom: "It's raining cats and dogs."

## What to preserve
The three-category framework; a menu covering all Sort types before selection; concrete samples; a classroom-ready build after selection; and reasoning/justification as part of the activity.
