# Get started with Hearth

Hearth keeps your diet goal and your meal, weight and workout logs, the same ones you see in the Hearth iPhone app.

1. Connect Hearth and sign in with your Hearth account. An active Hearth Pro plan is required.
2. Try a first request:
   - "Show my Hearth meals for this week."
   - "Log my weight in Hearth: 68.4 kg today."
   - "How is my Hearth progress toward my goal?"
3. Hearth can read your goal, pacing and progress, show your meals as a card, log meals (including a meal photo you share), weight and workouts, save a daily plan and coaching notes, and propose goal changes that you approve in the app.

Hearth only reads and writes your own Hearth data. It does not read your calendar, email or files, and it does not give medical diagnoses.
