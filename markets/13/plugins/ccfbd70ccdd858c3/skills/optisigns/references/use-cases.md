# Common use cases

## Who uses this

- Office, plant and warehouse managers posting messages for employees.
- Small restaurant, coffee shop, ice cream, gym, yoga studio and retail owners running promotions.
- Back-of-house teams at larger restaurant, hotel and retail chains.
- Clinic, dental, pharmacy and veterinary front desks.
- Property managers: commercial buildings, apartments, senior living, coworking, museums, galleries.
- Nonprofit, church, school and university administrators; government, military and library clerks.

### What they need from the assistant

- **Time:** 5 to 10 minutes per sign, often while doing something else.
- **Skills:** comfortable with Word and PowerPoint, not a designer.
- **Main question:** "Does it look professional?" **Main fear:** "Will people understand it?"
- **Success:** "I didn't have to ask IT."
- They may only have a PNG logo and brand colors. They update content daily or weekly and value speed over design options.

So: short copy readable from across the room, one message per slide, high contrast, their logo and colors, and a ready-to-publish result instead of a list of options.

## Patterns

| Pattern | Typical content | Usual shape |
|---|---|---|
| Time-sensitive | wait times, daily specials, schedule changes, weather alerts, emergency notices | one slide, short schedule or takeover |
| Promotion | sale, featured product, drink of the week, seasonal offer | slide in a playlist, start and end dates |
| Reminder | policy change, safety notice, deadline, maintenance window, compliance notice | slide in a playlist until the date passes |
| Recognition | employee of the month, birthdays, anniversaries, team wins | photo slide, weekly or monthly |
| Wayfinding | room assignments, tonight's events by room, directions, capacity | slide or split screen, by day |
| Menu | food menu, daily specials, availability, prices | menu design, dayparted schedule, often live data |
| Recurring digest | weekly update from a calendar, chat channel, email or social accounts | one or a few slides rebuilt on a cadence |
| Kiosk / dashboard | training reference with quiz, interactive directory, live KPI board | web app or dashboard on a screen |

Educational content (how-to guides, FAQs, best practices) fits the reminder or kiosk shapes.

## Workflow examples

These combine OptiSigns with other sources the user has connected to their AI assistant.

1. **Calendar to weekly slide.** Read the team, school, church or holiday calendar, build one signage-friendly slide per week (or per day if busy), publish it and send it to the chosen screens.
2. **Chat channel digest.** Each week, read a releases or announcements channel, summarize what is notable, and publish one slide.
3. **Topic slides from instructions.** "Make slides about our new recycling program; here are the instructions." Build a short set and put them in a playlist.
4. **Brand guide extraction.** Read the user's slides or PDFs, pull out logo, colors and fonts, and use them as the default style for future signs.
5. **Social digest.** Each week, read the organization's social accounts and turn the highlights into a few signage-ready slides.
6. **Training-manual kiosk with quiz.** Turn a training manual into a kiosk app staff can browse as a reference, with a short quiz.
7. **Dashboard.** Build a dashboard on a topic (sales, safety days, queue status) and push it to the screens.

For recurring workflows, confirm the cadence, which screens, and whether each new slide replaces the last one or is added to the playlist.

## Industry background

- A signage system has three parts: **content**, a **player** device driving each screen, and a **CMS** where content, schedules and devices are managed remotely. The value over a poster or a USB stick in a TV is remote, scheduled, multi-location control: change a price in 50 stores from one browser, run a promo from 4 to 7 PM, know when a screen goes dark.
- It is not live presentation (that is casting) and not an ad network selling slots on other people's screens.
- OptiSigns is a cloud CMS: software-led, priced per screen per month, runs on many existing devices (Fire TV, Apple TV, Android, Windows, Linux, Raspberry Pi, ChromeOS, Samsung, LG, BrightSign) and on its own players. It scales from one café screen to large multi-location fleets.

### What each industry usually needs

| Industry | Needs |
|---|---|
| Restaurants | menu boards, dayparting (breakfast, lunch, dinner), price changes across locations, screens that stay on at rush hour |
| Retail | promotions, video walls, consistent branding across stores |
| Healthcare | waiting-room content, check-in and queues, quiet hours, reliability |
| Corporate | dashboards, KPIs, employee messaging, often behind sign-in |
| Education | campus wayfinding, event boards, emergency alerts, tight budgets |
| Hospitality | lobby and event signage, wayfinding, brand experience |
| Manufacturing / warehouse | safety and KPI boards, often on restricted networks |
| Worship, fitness, small business | simple, quick, price-conscious self-serve |

Ask about the setting rather than assuming; a clinic often just wants reliable waiting-room content.
