# About OptiSigns


## Summary

OptiSigns is a **HWSaaS** (hardware + software-as-a-service) digital signage CMS, with its own hardware offers. We exist to solve the **last-mile communication** between a business and the people it needs to reach — its customers (external) and its employees (workplace) — by making it easy and low-cost to display the right thing on a screen. Plug in an OptiStick (or use a device you already own that we support) and you're live in minutes. We are simple enough to start in minutes, yet sophisticated enough for large enterprise deployments.

## Who we are

OptiSigns is a modern, easy-to-use cloud digital signage platform with a hardware offer — a HWSaaS business. Customers manage screens, content, playlists, and schedules from one web portal.

## Why we exist — the last mile

Businesses struggle with the *last mile* of communication: actually reaching people at the moment and place that matters.

- **To customers (external)** — a nudge or promotion shown right at the location is proven to increase sales and customer satisfaction.
- **To employees (workplace)** — ~80% of the global workforce (~2.7B people) are deskless / frontline workers: restaurant, hospitality, logistics, manufacturing, healthcare. They often have no desk, no PC, and no corporate inbox (Emergence Capital, 2018; Microsoft Work Trend Index, 2022). For them, a screen on the wall may be the only reliable way to reach them.

Digital signage closes that gap with a low-cost, easy-to-run screen.

## Why signage works

Putting your message on a screen, in the right place, earns attention and sticks:

- High recall — Nielsen found 75–82% of viewers recalled a digital billboard (2015).
- Sales lift at the point of sale — a 2025 randomized field study (237 campaigns, 30M+ shopper receipts) found in-store digital signage raised purchase likelihood for featured products by ~8% on average, and ~25% for new product launches (Journal of Marketing, 2025).
- A large, growing category — ~$28.8B in 2024, projected ~$45.9B by 2030 (~8.1% CAGR, Grand View Research).
