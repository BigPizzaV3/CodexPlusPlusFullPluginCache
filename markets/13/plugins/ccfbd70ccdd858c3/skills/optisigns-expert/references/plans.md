# Plans and what each includes

Prices change and are not quoted here. For current prices and the full comparison, link
https://www.optisigns.com/pricing. Custom quotes and large deployments go to Sales from that page.

## How plans work

- **Per screen, per month.** Each paid screen is a license. Annual billing is cheaper than monthly.
- **One plan per account.** Every screen in an account shares the plan; you can't put some screens on
  Standard and others on Pro Plus. Different plans for different locations means separate accounts.
- **Add-ons are per screen.** They attach to chosen screens on top of the plan. The **Engage add-on**
  lets a Standard or Pro Plus account turn on Engage features for just a few screens, so "only three
  lobby screens need a kiosk" doesn't mean moving the whole account to Engage. Other add-ons include
  Video Wall (one player driving several screens), Background Music and Custom Domain. Add-ons aren't
  available on Free.
- **Plans build on each other.** Each tier includes everything in the one below.
- **Storage** is 10 GB per paid screen, pooled across the account. The Free plan has 1 GB.
- **On hold:** moving a screen to the OnHold folder stops it playing and frees its license slot, but
  doesn't lower the bill. Lowering the license count (or cancelling) is what stops billing.

## Tiers

| Plan | For | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Trying OptiSigns | Up to 3 screens, core playback, a set of popular apps. Supported devices are limited: Fire TV, Roku, Samsung and LG smart TVs, ChromeOS, Apple TV / iOS and BrightSign don't run on Free. |
| **Standard** | Production signage | Playlists, scheduling, the full app library (100+ apps), templates, the Website app, feeds. |
| **Pro Plus** | Teams, governance, integrations | Teams; custom roles and folder permissions; content approval workflow; proof-of-play reports; audit logs; SSO (SAML); API access; dashboards (Power BI and similar) and recorded-login web content; data sources (OptiSync); Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace apps; custom fonts; offline alerts; remote device management (reboot, screenshot, remote control, HDMI-CEC, RS-232); automatic TV power on/off; advanced playlist scheduling; white-label branding; external share links; priority support. |
| **Engage** | Interactive screens | Touch kiosks (Kiosk Lite, Kiosk Designer Pro), AI camera audience analytics, IoT sensors (Lift and Learn), visitor check-in with SMS, scan-a-QR-code interaction, Live TV with ads. Regional data residency can be enabled by support on Engage and above. |
| **Enterprise** | Large fleets | Mass provisioning, a dedicated account manager, management of conference-room systems (Zoom Rooms, Teams Rooms, Webex), custom contracts. Has a minimum screen count; see the pricing page. |

**Legacy Pro.** An older Pro plan is no longer sold. Accounts that have it keep it. Its governance
features (roles, folder security, approval, proof of play) all sit in Pro Plus now. Never suggest Pro
to a new customer.

## Answering plan questions

- **Signed in?** Check the account first (`optisigns_me`), then answer about *this* account.
- **Feature not on the plan:** "That isn't available on this account's plan." If useful, add where
  plans are compared (https://www.optisigns.com/pricing). One sentence, no pitch.
- **"Should I upgrade?" / "Is it worth it?":** one sentence: "Proof of play is included from Pro Plus;
  plans are compared at https://www.optisigns.com/pricing." Then stop. Don't weigh pros and cons, don't
  list what else the plan adds, and avoid the word "upgrade"; say "included from" or "available on".
- **Only a few screens need something interactive:** mention the Engage add-on before anything else.
- **Device won't come online on a Free account:** check whether that device type runs on Free (see
  the list above) before troubleshooting.
- **Billing, refunds, currency changes, invoices:** OptiSigns Support (support@optisigns.com).
