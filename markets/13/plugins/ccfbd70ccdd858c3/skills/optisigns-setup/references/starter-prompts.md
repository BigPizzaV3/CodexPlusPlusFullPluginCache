# Starter prompts

## Screen name → likely use case

| Name contains | Likely use | Good first sign |
|---|---|---|
| menu, kitchen, counter, drive-thru, cafe, bar | Menu board | daily special, new item, combo deal |
| lobby, reception, entrance, front desk | Welcome and announcements | welcome message, event today, hours |
| break room, warehouse, floor, plant, shop | Employee comms | safety reminder, recognition, shift notes |
| waiting room, clinic, patient | Waiting-room info | wait time, reminders, services |
| classroom, hall, library, campus | Schedules and events | exam schedule, extended hours, event |
| window, storefront, sale, retail | Promotion | flash sale, featured product |
| gym, studio, class | Class schedule | today's classes, spots left |
| conference, meeting, ballroom | Wayfinding and room status | today's events by room |

No match? Use the user's industry or role, else ask what the screens are for.

## Templates by industry (fill the brackets)

Restaurant / cafe
- "Design a [lunch special] slide for [Front Counter]: [4 items with prices], [warm colors], show it [11:00–14:00 daily]."
- "Every [Monday], turn our [specials sheet] into a fresh menu slide for [Front Counter]."

Retail
- "Make a [flash sale: 30% off summer dresses] sign for [Storefront], bold and readable from the street."
- "Create a [featured product of the week] slide for [Checkout] with photo, price and one line why."

Healthcare / clinic
- "Show [current wait time: 45 minutes] and a reminder to [update insurance cards] on [Waiting Room]."
- "Make a [flu shots available, no appointment needed] slide for [Waiting Room], calm colors."

Office / HR
- "Announce [Sarah Chen] as Employee of the Month on [Break Room] with her photo and achievement."
- "Every [Friday], summarize our [#announcements channel] into one slide for [Lobby]."

Manufacturing / warehouse
- "Put a [fire drill Friday at 2 PM] safety notice on [Warehouse Floor], high contrast, readable at 30 ft."
- "Create a [days without incident] board for [Plant Entrance] that we update daily."

Education
- "Post the [final exam schedule] and [extended library hours] on [Library Hall] for finals week."
- "Make a [Parent Night Out this Friday] promo for [Front Office], friendly and colorful."

Hospitality / venues
- "Show the [airport shuttle every 30 minutes] and [breakfast 7–10 AM] on [Lobby]."
- "List [tonight's events by room: wedding in Ballroom A, corporate event in Room B] on [Concourse]."

Fitness
- "Show that [today's yoga class is full] and [3 spots remain in 6 PM spin] on [Front Desk]."

Church / nonprofit
- "Announce [Easter service times] and [volunteer signup for the community lunch] on [Lobby]."

Interactive / recurring ideas (offer one when it fits)
- "Turn our [training manual] into a touch kiosk with a short quiz for [Training Room]."
- "Build a live [sales KPI] dashboard from our [sheet] for [Office TV]."
