# Getting started with Shoptegrity

Shoptegrity shows who owns the brands and stores you buy from, finds independent and local sellers, and checks sale prices against prices it has recorded daily. No account or sign-in is needed.

Try one of these:
- "Help me shop Black Friday with integrity. My list: cast iron skillet, wooden toys, board game. Budget $250."
- "Check my cart: The North Face jacket, Tide pods, a Walmart gift card. Also show approved alternatives for The North Face."
- "Find merino wool socks under $25 from independent sellers and check whether the prices are real deals."
- "Show verified price drops right now, verify the top deal with check_deal, and check 3-wick candles under $30 for prices that were raised before a discount."
- "Find local co-ops and farmers markets near Minneapolis, MN."

How to read results:
- A value comes with a source link, or it is withheld. "Withheld: no cited source yet" and "not in Shoptegrity yet" mean Shoptegrity has no cited record. They are not negative findings about a company.
- Grades and ratings are Shoptegrity's opinion under its published methodology (https://shopteg.com/methodology).
- Results are never ordered by commission.
- Price checks need at least 7 days of recorded prices for a product.
