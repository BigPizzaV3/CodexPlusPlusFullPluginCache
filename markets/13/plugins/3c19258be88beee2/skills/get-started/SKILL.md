Rablabla gives practice for the Duolingo English Test (DET) with real DET-format questions.

- Ask "Help me practice for the Duolingo English Test" to start with Read and Select.
- Name a question type to practice it, for example "Give me a DET Interactive Reading question".
- Answer in the card. Objective questions are checked instantly. Use "Explain my mistakes" or "Get feedback" to ask ChatGPT for help in your language.
- Test questions stay in English, like the real test. Results are practice results, not official DET scores.
- Rablabla is independent and not affiliated with Duolingo.
