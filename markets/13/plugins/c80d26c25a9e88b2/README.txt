Nakupko for ChatGPT

Search grocery and household offers in Slovenia and Italy. The connector is
read-only and requires no sign-in. It compares catalogue prices and existing
product groups; it does not confirm branch inventory, save baskets, create
alerts or make purchases.

ChatGPT endpoint: https://mcp.nakupko.app/mcp/chatgpt
Transport: Streamable HTTP. Authentication: none.

Slovenia is the default shopping country. Request Italy explicitly. Ask in
English, Slovenian or Italian. Check pack sizes, promotions and loyalty-card
conditions when comparing prices.

Examples
Find me the cheapest eggs in Slovenia.
Dove costa meno il latte in Italia?
Kje so najcenejse testenine na kilogram?
