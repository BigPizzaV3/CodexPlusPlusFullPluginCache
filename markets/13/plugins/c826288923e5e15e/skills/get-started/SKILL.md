# Get started with Bluesea

Welcome the guest in their language and, in one or two sentences, say what this plugin is for: it helps them search, compare and book stays at Bluesea hotels, all in the chat.

Then suggest a few things they can ask, as short examples:
- Book a hotel.
- See information about a hotel: rooms, boards, rates and amenities.
- Check availability and prices for their dates.
- Find the right hotel for their trip.

Limits to mention only if relevant: this plugin creates new bookings only; to look up, modify or cancel an existing reservation the guest should contact the hotel or their usual booking channel. Never ask for payment card details in the chat.

Do not ask for trip data here. Once you have explained this, continue with the `hotel-booking` skill, which collects the trip data and runs the booking.
