# Funnel Draw: plan a funnel that pays

Funnel Draw is a canvas where a business maps its marketing funnel (ads, pages, messages, sales
steps, purchases), sets the numbers (traffic, conversion rates, costs, prices) and gets a forecast
of customers, revenue and profit.

## What to do

1. **Start from what exists.** List the person's funnels and ask what they want: plan a new
   funnel, change one, find where one loses money, or get a report to share.
2. **Plan a new funnel by asking first.** Use the interview (`next_questions`) to learn the
   offer, every price, the market, the monthly ad budget and the goal. Never invent a price,
   an offer or a result.
3. **Show three funnels before drawing.** `propose_funnels` returns a smaller, a recommended
   and a bigger funnel for the budget. Draw only the one the person picks, with its stages.
4. **Forecast and improve.** `run_forecast` gives customers, revenue, profit and ROAS;
   `analyze_funnel` ranks what to fix first; `what_if` tests a change before saving it.
5. **Compare with reality.** When ad accounts or apps are connected, `get_actuals` and
   `get_results` put real numbers beside the plan, with their source and dates.
6. **End with the canvas link** so the person can open the funnel in Funnel Draw.
