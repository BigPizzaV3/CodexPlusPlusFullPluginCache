# Family Briefing

Read only the requested Home and date range. Use meal plan, planning context, saved content, and household operations only when each source is relevant.

Return a concise briefing with:

- the date range and source freshness;
- scheduled meals and missing meal rows;
- FamilyWise events and allowed busy windows;
- open routines, reminders, tasks, and relevant lists;
- information that is absent, stale, or unavailable.

Separate returned facts from suggestions. Never claim complete knowledge, infer medical or custody needs, expose private profile fields, or make a change from this skill.
