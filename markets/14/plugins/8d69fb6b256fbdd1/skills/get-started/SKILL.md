# Get started with BizGenius

BizGenius connects your AI assistant to your own BizGenius account, so you can ask
about your data in plain language instead of clicking through dashboards. The tools
available depend on the products your account has.

## Connecting

Sign in through BizGenius when prompted (OAuth). No API key or password is shared
with the assistant, and each connection only ever accesses that account's own data.

## What you can ask

- **Citations** — "How many papers cited our products in 2025, and which journals published them?"
- **Citation widget analytics** — "Show my citation-widget visitor stats for the last three months."
- **ChatGenius analytics** — "What are visitors asking my ChatGenius assistant this month?"
- **Deep Analysis** — "Run a deep analysis of single-cell RNA sequencing and show the results."
- **Academic leads** — "Preview academic leads for CRISPR gene editing we could contact about our kits."

Everything is read-only except exports, which are emailed to the account holder.
