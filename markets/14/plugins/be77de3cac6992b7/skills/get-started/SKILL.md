# Get started with buddybase

buddybase is your own small database that you fill from chat.

1. Connect buddybase and sign in with your ProtoductAI account.
2. Try a first request:
   - "Start a buddybase expense tracker."
   - "Save to buddybase: lunch 1200 yen today."
   - "How much did I spend this month in buddybase?"
3. buddybase can set up collections from templates (expenses, vocabulary, health log) or your own fields, save records, list collections, and show records with totals as a card.

buddybase only reads and writes your own collections and records. It does not read your calendar, email or files, and it does not delete records.
