# TalkTool

Prepare a translated phone call from ChatGPT. Connect your TalkTool account, select a destination and both languages, and receive a number to call from your verified phone.

Create an account, sign in, finish onboarding and verify your calling phone at https://www.usetalktool.com/chatgpt. Payment and verification codes stay on TalkTool.

Call setups last ten minutes. Preparing does not dial the recipient. When you dial the returned number from the verified phone with caller ID enabled, TalkTool checks your current calling access and attempts the translated outgoing call. Calling uses your TalkTool minutes and your carrier's usual charges. Destination restrictions apply.

Tools: `get_call_setup`, `prepare_translated_call`, `get_prepared_call`, `cancel_prepared_call`. The plugin does not buy numbers or change your organization's routing.

This is a remote MCP plugin package. Hosting, OAuth connection, live-call validation and OpenAI review are required before public publication. No secret or test account credentials are included.
