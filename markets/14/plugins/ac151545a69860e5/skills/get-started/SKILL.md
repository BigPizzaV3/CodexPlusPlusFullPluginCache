# Get started with SendNow

SendNow lets you share confidential documents as secure, trackable links and see exactly who opened them.

## Connect your account

Connect SendNow and sign in with your SendNow account. You only see and change documents in your own account.

## Things to try

- "List my recent documents"
- "Share my pitch deck with downloads turned off and email verification on"
- "Who viewed my pitch deck this week and how long did they spend?"
- "Create a Series A Diligence data room with email verification"
- "Revoke the link I sent to Acme"

## Good to know

- Before creating a link for anything confidential, confirm the security settings with the user.
- Some settings (NDA gate, allowed-viewer lists, watermarks, data rooms) need a paid SendNow plan. If one is refused, tell the user which setting was refused.
- Deleting a document, link or data room is permanent. Confirm exactly what will be deleted first. To just stop access, revoke the link instead.
