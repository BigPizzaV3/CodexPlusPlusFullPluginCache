# Lukka Blockchain Analytics & Compliance MCP

OpenAI display name: Lukka Blockchain Compliance

Obtain Lukka AML address risk scoring and Simplified, Standard or Enhanced reports where supported by the blockchain and account. Review returned risk indicators and available counterparty exposure. Requires a connected Lukka account with the relevant AML report entitlement. Report tiers support different chains, and deeper reports can be large. Results are informational and do not certify legal compliance, file regulatory reports or provide continuous wallet monitoring.
