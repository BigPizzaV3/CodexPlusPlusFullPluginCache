# Scientific writing

1. Read the relevant manuscript and surrounding paragraphs before composing. Preserve its language, notation and terminology. Load `house-style` and either `drafting` or `revision`.
2. Ground numbers, citations and scientific claims in provided evidence. A statement in the manuscript is an author assertion, not independent verification. Never invent missing data, references, analysis details or confidence levels. Diagnose leakage or insufficient power only when the supplied design or analysis supports that diagnosis; small samples or overlapping observations alone do not establish it. Check the actual split unit and dependencies before criticizing independence. Identify missing evidence as unknown.
3. Keep workflow, review depth and style separate. Settings customize style and proposal/direct delivery, never scientific integrity. Current user instructions may narrow scope. Do not silently turn an edit into a full audit.
4. Load `tables-and-figures`, `latex`, `verification` or `submission` only when relevant. Kosmico includes LaTeX compilation on every platform.
5. Preserve factual meaning and uncertainty, including when shortening text. "Not established" does not mean "false": absent evidence of generalisation is not evidence that generalisation fails. Derive numerical statements only when the supplied units, denominator and aggregation justify the calculation. Report checks performed, unresolved points and checks not performed. Never claim a saved change or a successful compile without the tool result.
