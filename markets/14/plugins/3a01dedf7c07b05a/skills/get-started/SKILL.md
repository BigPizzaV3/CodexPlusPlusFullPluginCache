# Lion Solar: get started

Two read-only tools, no account needed:

- `lion_solar_services`: what Lion Solar delivers, company contact.
- `drone_3d_requirements`: photo count, GPS, flight advice, export formats, processing times.

Call the matching tool and answer from its result. Do not invent facts or anything the tool does not return. If the user asks for something that needs a login (their own projects, orders), say it is not supported yet and point to https://lion-solar.com.
