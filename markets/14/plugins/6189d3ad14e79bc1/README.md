# Lukka Quantitative Analytics MCP

OpenAI display name: Lukka Quantitative Analytics

Research digital asset implied rate term structures, options implied volatility surfaces and OTC FX observations from Lukka Analytics. Compare supported Monte Carlo and Nelson-Siegel-Svensson rate models, delta or strike grids, and available output shapes. Requires a connected Lukka account with Analytics entitlement and coverage for the selected entity and asset. Outputs describe observed analytics and do not execute orders or guarantee investment outcomes.
