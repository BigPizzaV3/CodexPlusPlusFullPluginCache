# Lukka Pricing & Market Data MCP

OpenAI display name: Lukka Pricing & Market Data

Read digital asset spot and index prices, historical observations, OHLCV candles, VWAP, market capitalization, order books, and spot and derivative trades from Lukka. Compare observed prices and liquidity across available sources. A connected Lukka account with Pricing API entitlement is required. Coverage and timestamps depend on the source, instrument and account. Provides data for research; does not execute trades, transfers or recurring alerts. Source availability must be discovered before choosing venue-specific requests.
