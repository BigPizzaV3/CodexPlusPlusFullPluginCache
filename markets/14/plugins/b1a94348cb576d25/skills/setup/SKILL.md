Connect Stockly with your own Stockly account. After signing in, try:

- "Which SKUs are at risk of stocking out?"
- "Does a 5-day delay on SKU-1018 matter?"
- "What is waiting in the purchase queue?"

Actions such as approving a recommendation show a preview first and change nothing until you confirm.
Documentation: https://stockly.analytos.ai/docs
