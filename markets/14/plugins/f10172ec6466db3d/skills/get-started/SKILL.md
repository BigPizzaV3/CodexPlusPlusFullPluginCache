---
name: get-started
description: Help the user connect their Nida OS workspace and explore what they can do. Walk them through authorizing the plugin and show available capabilities.
---

When the user first connects or asks what Nida OS can do:

1. Confirm their workspace is connected by calling `workspace:state` to get a live summary of their CRM, websites, and configured modules.
2. Greet them with a brief overview of what is available in their workspace — lead count, active websites, pending bookings.
3. Ask what they would like to do: manage leads and contacts, work on websites, handle calendar bookings, or something else.
4. Route to the appropriate capability based on their answer.

Keep the introduction short. The user connected this plugin to get things done — show them the state of their workspace and ask what to tackle first.
