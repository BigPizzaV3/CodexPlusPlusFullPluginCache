# Step III · SCTs

| ID | Priority | Source | Title | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SCT-001 | A | Manageability Lever | Normative Management | Define the normative management framework that clarifies the organization’s purpose, values, principles, and long-term orientation. The task is to translate these guiding beliefs into practical decision criteria, leadership expectations, and boundaries for action, ensuring that strategy and operations remain aligned with the organization’s identity and intended contribution. |
| SCT-002 | A | Workshop Decision | Business Strategy | Develop the business strategy by defining where the organization will compete, how it will create value, and which priorities will guide future decisions. The task is to assess market opportunities, customer needs, competitive positioning, capabilities, and risks, then translate them into clear strategic choices, measurable objectives, and actionable fields of work. |
| SCT-003 | A | Workshop Decision | Manage Innovation | Establish a structured approach for managing innovation from idea generation to implementation. The task is to identify relevant opportunities, evaluate their strategic fit and feasibility, prioritize initiatives, and create the conditions for experimentation, learning, and scaling. Innovation activities should be connected to business goals, customer needs, available capabilities, and clear decision points. |
| SCT-004 | A | Workshop Decision | Operative Steering of Daily Business | Establish a structured approach for managing innovation from idea generation to implementation. The task is to identify relevant opportunities, evaluate their strategic fit and feasibility, prioritize initiatives, and create the conditions for experimentation, learning, and scaling. Innovation activities should be connected to business goals, customer needs, available capabilities, and clear decision points. |
| SCT-005 | C | Workshop Decision | Partner Management | Establish partner management to identify, select, coordinate, and develop external partners in line with business needs. The task is to clarify partner roles, expectations, interfaces, governance, and performance criteria, while ensuring that collaboration creates mutual value. Partner management should make dependencies transparent, reduce friction, and support reliable cooperation across organizational boundaries. |
| SCT-006 | B | Workshop Decision | Market Intelligence | Establish market intelligence to systematically observe, interpret, and share relevant market developments. The task is to gather insights on customers, competitors, technologies, regulations, and broader trends, assess their implications for the business, and translate them into actionable input for strategy, innovation, and decision-making. Market intelligence should create early awareness, reduce uncertainty, and support timely strategic responses. |
| SCT-007 | A | Manageability Lever | Competence Management | Establish competence management to identify, develop, and secure the capabilities needed for current and future business success. The task is to define required competencies, assess existing strengths and gaps, prioritize development needs, and connect learning activities with strategic and operational requirements. Competence management should ensure that the organization has the right skills, knowledge, and expertise available where they are needed. |
| SCT-008 | A | Manageability Lever | Internal Budget Allocation | Establish internal budget allocation to distribute financial resources in line with strategic priorities, operational needs, and expected value contribution. The task is to define allocation principles, assess competing demands, clarify decision rights, and ensure transparency on assumptions, trade-offs, and constraints. Internal budget allocation should enable informed prioritization, responsible resource use, and timely adjustments when conditions change. |
| SCT-009 | A | Workshop Decision | CIP, Self-Audit / Retrospectives and After Action Reviews as Practice | Establish continuous improvement as a regular management practice through CIP, self-audits, retrospectives, and after action reviews. The task is to systematically reflect on work results, identify deviations, extract lessons learned, and translate insights into concrete improvements. These practices should make learning explicit, strengthen accountability, and ensure that teams regularly adapt processes, behaviors, and collaboration based on real experience. |
| SCT-010 | B | Operation-Management | Align with Shareholders | Establish shareholder alignment to ensure that expectations, strategic priorities, and value creation logic are clearly understood and actively managed. The task is to clarify shareholder objectives, translate them into relevant governance and decision criteria, communicate strategic choices transparently, and manage trade-offs between short-term performance and long-term viability. Shareholder alignment should create trust, reduce ambiguity, and ensure that major decisions remain connected to ownership expectations. |
| SCT-012 | A | Operational Dependency | Variant Management | Establish variant management to control product, service, or process variety in line with customer value and operational feasibility. The task is to define variant logic, clarify configuration rules, assess complexity costs, and decide which variants should be created, maintained, bundled, or retired. Variant management should balance market responsiveness with internal efficiency, ensuring that variety remains intentional rather than accidental. |
| SCT-013 | A | Manageability Lever | Financial Controlling and Performance Management | Establish financial controlling and performance management to ensure that financial results, operational performance, and strategic objectives are consistently monitored and steered. The task is to define relevant KPIs, create transparent reporting structures, analyze deviations, and support decision-making with reliable financial and performance insights. This should enable early corrective action, disciplined resource use, and a clear understanding of whether the business is delivering against its intended goals. |
| SCT-014 | A | Workshop Decision | Promote offerings in markets | Establish a structured approach to promote offerings in target markets and create demand with the right customer groups. The task is to define positioning, key messages, channels, campaigns, and sales enablement activities, ensuring that offerings are clearly understood and differentiated. Market promotion should connect customer needs with value propositions, build visibility, and support measurable market traction. |
| SCT-015 | A | Workshop Decision | Communicate with Stakeholders, Public Affairs | Establish stakeholder communication and public affairs to actively manage relationships, expectations, and external perception. The task is to identify relevant stakeholders, clarify key messages, prepare communication formats, and ensure consistent dialogue with public, institutional, and societal actors. Public affairs should create trust, anticipate concerns, manage reputational risks, and support the organization’s ability to act within its broader environment. |
| SCT-016 | A | Manageability Lever | Transform the Organization | Establish organizational transformation to deliberately adapt structures, roles, processes, capabilities, and ways of working to future business needs. The task is to define the transformation ambition, identify required changes, mobilize stakeholders, manage dependencies, and translate the target picture into concrete implementation steps. Transformation should create orientation, reduce friction, and ensure that change becomes actionable rather than remaining an abstract intent. |
| SCT-017 | A | Workshop Decision | Create and keep customers | Establish a systematic approach to create and keep customers by identifying target groups, understanding their needs, winning their attention, and building lasting relationships. The task is to translate customer insights into compelling offerings, reliable delivery, and meaningful interaction across the customer journey. Customer creation and retention should generate trust, strengthen loyalty, and ensure that value is continuously created for both the customer and the organization. |
| SCT-018 | A | Workshop Decision | Manage Employee Experience | Establish employee experience management to shape how people encounter, understand, and contribute to the organization across their working life cycle. The task is to identify key employee needs, design meaningful touchpoints, improve leadership, collaboration, onboarding, development, and work conditions, and address friction that weakens engagement or performance. Employee experience should strengthen commitment, enable productive work, and ensure that people can contribute with clarity, energy, and a sense of belonging. |
| SCT-019 | B | Workshop Decision | Sponsoring & Social Engagement | Establish sponsoring and social engagement to create meaningful contributions beyond the core business while strengthening the organization’s visibility, credibility, and societal relevance. The task is to define focus areas, select suitable initiatives or partners, clarify objectives and responsibilities, and ensure that activities fit the organization’s values and strategic intent. Sponsoring and social engagement should create mutual benefit, support reputation, and make the organization’s contribution tangible. |
| SCT-020 | A | Workshop Decision | Develop Engineering Excellence | Establish engineering excellence to continuously improve the quality, reliability, efficiency, and innovation capacity of engineering work. The task is to define standards, methods, tools, competencies, and governance principles that enable robust technical solutions and consistent delivery. Engineering excellence should strengthen problem-solving capability, reduce rework, support knowledge sharing, and ensure that engineering output meets both customer expectations and business requirements. |
| SCT-021 | A | Workshop Decision | Manage Enterprise IT, Data & AI Infrastructure | Establish enterprise IT, data, and AI infrastructure to provide reliable, secure, and scalable digital foundations for the organization. The task is to define architecture principles, platforms, data governance, integration standards, security requirements, and operating responsibilities. This should enable efficient business processes, trusted data use, responsible AI application, and the continuous evolution of digital capabilities in line with strategic and operational needs. |
| SCT-022 | A | Workshop Decision | Integrated Planning Approach | Develop an integrated planning approach that aligns strategy, resources, timelines, dependencies, and decision points across all relevant functions. The task is to connect individual planning streams into one coherent view, clarify ownership, identify conflicts early, and ensure that planning assumptions are transparent, consistent, and actively managed. |
