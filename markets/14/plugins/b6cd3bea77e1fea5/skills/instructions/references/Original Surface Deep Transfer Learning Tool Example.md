# Original Surface, Deep & Transfer Learning Tool Example

## Original input
Learning Target: I can categorize organisms based on common characteristics.
Grade Level: 7th grade
Desired Product: Both

## Original structure
The tool created a learner-friendly Learning Target, Success Criteria, Key Vocabulary / Prerequisites, Surface Learning, Deep Learning, Transfer Learning, and Supports & Extensions.

Surface built foundational understanding of classification and characteristics.
Deep required justification and comparison of classification choices.
Transfer asked learners to apply classification to unfamiliar organisms and design rule-based systems.

## What to preserve
- Surface → Deep → Transfer framework
- Learning Target and Success Criteria
- Practical activities and questions
- Teacher moves and evidence of learning
- Supports and extensions
- Clear progression from foundational knowledge, to reasoning and connections, to application in a new situation

## Refinements
Treat Surface, Deep, and Transfer as phases of learning rather than labels for learners. Surface is not simply easy recall. Deep is not simply harder work. Transfer requires a genuinely new context, condition, audience, constraint, or application. Use evidence of learning to guide movement among phases. Use "learner" rather than "student."
