# Get started with Lore

Lore is your private knowledge inbox, the same one you read in the Lore iPhone app. ChatGPT researches and writes; Lore keeps and shows it, and quizzes you so it sticks.

1. Connect Lore and sign in with your Lore account. An active Lore Pro plan is required.
2. Try a first request:
   - "Show my latest Lore articles."
   - "Write today's Lore article about how vaccines train the immune system, with three quiz questions."
   - "Which Lore quiz topics am I weakest at?"
3. Lore can list, search and open your articles, write new articles with sources, add quiz questions, organize tags, archive articles and publish a day's batch to your iPhone.

Lore only reads and writes your own articles, quizzes and tags. It does not read your email, calendar or files.
