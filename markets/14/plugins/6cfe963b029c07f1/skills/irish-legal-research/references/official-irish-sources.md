# Official Irish legal sources

A map of official sources for the Republic of Ireland, by legal area. Use it to choose where to look and which body is responsible. All links were checked on 25 October 2026.

Statute names below are starting points. Before citing a provision, confirm the current revised text on the Revised Acts site or the Irish Statute Book, because Acts are amended and collective citations change.

## Legislation and the Constitution (all areas)

| Source | What it holds | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Irish Statute Book (eISB) | Acts as enacted, Statutory Instruments, commencement orders, legislation directory | https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/ |
| Constitution of Ireland | Current text of Bunreacht na hÉireann | https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/cons/en/html |
| Revised Acts (Law Reform Commission) | Acts with later amendments consolidated in one text | https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/ |
| Houses of the Oireachtas | Bills before the Oireachtas and recently passed Acts | https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/bills/ |
| Citizens Information | Plain-language official guidance across most everyday topics | https://www.citizensinformation.ie/ |
