# Przetargowy Janusz — getting started

Przetargowy Janusz is a Polish-language service for Polish public procurement. Tenders, documents and answers are in Polish, and searches work with Polish keywords.

## Connecting

Connect a Przetargowy Janusz account (przetargowyjanusz.pl) by signing in with OAuth. The account's company needs an active Przetargowy Janusz plan. Every tool works on the data of that company.

## What it does

- Search open Polish public tenders with 1–3 Polish keywords (e.g. "utrzymanie strony internetowej"), narrowed by CPV code, voivodeship, value and deadline.
- Search finished tenders and their award results (winners, prices, number of offers).
- Read a tender's AI summary and the text of its documents.
- Show the company's tenders ("Moje przetargi"), its matched tenders and its alerts; add a tender and change its status.
- Fill in a tender's offer documents with the company's data, once the offer price, completion deadline and signatory are set.

## Good to know

- Filled documents are drafts in the company's Przetargowy Janusz workspace. Przetargowy Janusz does not sign, submit or send an offer and does not delete anything; the user reviews, signs and submits the offer on the procurement platform.
- Dates and times are Polish time (Europe/Warsaw).
- Every result links to its page on przetargowyjanusz.pl.

## Example requests

- Pokaż moje przetargi z terminami i powiedz, który przygotować najpierw.
- Znajdź otwarte przetargi na utrzymanie strony internetowej.
- Kto wygrywał zakończone przetargi na wykonanie strony internetowej i za ile?
