# Understand Modern Greek

Start the mode named by the learner. For «Дай аудирование B1» or “Give me an A2 reading exercise”, choose an everyday situation and start without a menu. Keep the requested or current lesson level; use A2 if neither is set. If the learner asks for a general comprehension workout, suggest one mode that is available and let them change it. Respect preferences about language, topic, question format, count and when to review answers.

Use a passage the learner provides when available. Base questions and feedback on the material actually accessible in this chat. If only a transcript is accessible, say so: it supports reading or checking answers after the learner listens elsewhere. For practice without supplied material, use an original, fictional passage and label exam-style tasks as practice rather than official questions. Do not fetch personal vocabulary or account progress for ordinary comprehension training. If the learner asks to include personal words, use a small selection already available in the chat; if none is available, offer general comprehension or a personal-word exercise via [greek-practice](../greek-practice/SKILL.md#retrieve-real-words).

| Level | Passage | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A2 | A short message, announcement or everyday exchange with a clear purpose and common vocabulary. | Main idea and explicit details: who, where, when or what to do. |
| B1 | A short announcement, conversation, letter, account or opinion where a reason, change or decision matters. | Main idea, a significant detail and a reason or inference supported by the passage. |

## Listening

- When the chat can speak Greek, present a short passage aloud. Give a simple listening goal, then ask about the gist after the first listen. Offer a replay of the same passage for details; keep the wording and facts stable. If the interface also displays the utterance, suggest listening without looking or use the app route below if the learner wants the text hidden.
- If there's no suitable audio or voice in this chat, offer the [Vocabulum listening track](https://www.vocabulum.app/listening/) ([RU](https://www.vocabulum.app/ru/listening)) or offer a switch to reading. For the app route, suggest a stage at the chosen level and have the learner listen in the app. They can return with a transcript or a question they want to discuss. Don't claim to play or retrieve a lesson, see what they completed, or assess an unheard recording.
- Withhold the transcript, translations and answers for as long as the learner wants to attempt the exercise by ear. After their attempt, offer the relevant line or the transcript on request. If audio alone is understandable to the chat but the wording is uncertain, explain the evidence without inventing a quotation.

## Reading

- Show the passage without a translation or answer key. Ask one question about the gist, then a detail or a supported inference. With supplied text, preserve the wording; if the source is ambiguous, accept supported interpretations or reframe the question.
- After the answer, show the short Greek phrase that supports it. For B1 inference, explain how the phrase supports the conclusion. Offer a gloss or translation when it will help after the attempt.

## Feedback and next turn

Ask one question at a time by default. Check meaning before correcting the learner's Greek: accept paraphrases, equivalent details and an answer in the learner's own language unless they requested Greek-only responses. For a partially correct answer, acknowledge the part they understood and offer one clue or a replay before giving the solution. Honor requests to hold feedback to the end.

Finish after a short set on the passage or when the learner stops. Name one thing they understood and one detail or cue to listen or look for next time, based on their actual answers. Do not infer a certified level or claim that chat answers updated Vocabulum study progress.
