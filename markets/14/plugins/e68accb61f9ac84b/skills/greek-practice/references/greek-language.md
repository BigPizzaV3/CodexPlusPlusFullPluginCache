# Modern Greek and difficulty

Use standard Modern Greek by default. Introduce Cypriot colloquial expressions when requested or clearly appropriate to the scene, and label them; a setting in Cyprus alone does not require dialect.

## Forms and senses

Keep an entry's spelling, accents and article when quoting its dictionary display. In a sentence, inflect it normally: agreement, case, number, person, tense and aspect depend on the intended meaning. Do not require literal matching against the dictionary form. For example, τελειώνω can appear as τελειώνει or τελείωσα when the context calls for it.

Use the returned translation, example and `senses` as context, not as a complete list of all possible senses. A null translation or example is missing data, not a licence to invent a Vocabulum-authored meaning. If providing your own explanation, distinguish it from account content and avoid unsupported claims.

Use Vocabulum terminology consistently with MCP guidance: the form after θα / να / ας and in the imperative is “the future form without θα” / «форма будущего без θα»; reserve “aorist” for the simple past. Label persons in learner-friendly terms (я / ты / он / она, I / you / he / she). Do not replace a precise correction with terminology alone.

## A1–B2 calibration

A stated learner level takes priority. `learner.levels` describes the levels of studied vocabulary, not an assessed CEFR level of the person. If no level is known, use that distribution as an initial clue, start with a manageable task and adapt to actual responses. Do not claim to have measured proficiency from the word list.

| Level | Task calibration |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | Short, concrete situations, familiar vocabulary and one simple communicative goal. Provide support when requested. |
| A2 | Everyday exchanges, short messages and connected sentences about familiar events or plans. |
| B1 | Connected accounts, explanations and reasons, with more independence and fewer prompts. |
| B2 | Nuance, register, paraphrase and sustained opinions or argument, still grounded in natural use of the targets. |

These are tutoring heuristics, not official exam rubrics or claims that a particular tense belongs exclusively to a CEFR level. Vary sentence length, context, support and grammatical demands; do not pretend that MCP accepts a level parameter. An explicitly selected mock A2 exam stays at A2.

After recurring errors, simplify the next context or offer a focused retry within the chosen level. After comfortable answers, increase complexity gradually. Do not change the learner's declared level, practice mode or account data automatically.
