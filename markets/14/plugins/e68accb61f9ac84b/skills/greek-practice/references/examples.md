# Tutoring examples

These are illustrative language examples, not a real learner's dictionary or results. Use them to guide feedback; build live tasks from the connected learner's returned words.

## Valid word order

Task: translate «В прошлом году я изменил свой адрес».

Both answers preserve the meaning:

- Πέρυσι άλλαξα τη διεύθυνσή μου.
- Άλλαξα τη διεύθυνσή μου πέρυσι.

Accept either. If the learner challenges a rejection of the second answer: «Да, ваш вариант правильный. Здесь πέρυσι можно поставить в конце; я ошибочно отклонил допустимый порядок слов».

## A hint that preserves the retry

Task: translate «Вчера я пошёл в магазин».

Answer: «Χθες πηγαίνω στο μαγαζί.»

Feedback before a retry: «Смысл понятен. Χθες задаёт прошедшее время, а πηγαίνω стоит в настоящем. Попробуйте изменить глагол».

After the retry or a request for the solution: «Χθες πήγα στο μαγαζί». Briefly explain πηγαίνω → πήγα. Do not show πήγα in the initial hint if recalling that form is the task.

## Correct meaning without the target word

If the task targeted a particular lemma but a different expression is valid, accept the translation first: «Перевод верный. Целевое слово пока не использовано — попробуйте передать ту же мысль с ним». Supply the lemma only if the task permits a word bank or the learner asks for a hint.

## Ambiguous source sentence

Task: translate «Я живу здесь два года».

If the intended meaning is ongoing residence, «Μένω εδώ δύο χρόνια» is a valid answer. Do not impose a completed past event that the source did not specify. If a task genuinely needs an unresolved time distinction, clarify it before grading.

## Own sentences versus recall

For `compose`, a prompt such as «Составьте предложение с δουλειά и κουρασμένος; формы можно менять» may show Greek lemmas. For RU→EL recall, do not list those same lemmas under a “target words” heading before the answer.

## A partly correct answer

Task: translate «В прошлом году я изменил свой адрес».

Answer: «Άλλαξα τη διεύθυνσή μου.»

The action is correctly expressed, but the time is missing. Say «Само действие передано верно. Добавьте, когда это произошло» and wait. Do not mark the entire answer wrong or disclose πέρυσι before the retry.

## Several errors or a difficult task

If a draft has tense and agreement errors, start with the issue that blocks its meaning or the current task. Give one focused clue, wait for the retry and then review the remaining errors at the agreed time. Do not call the draft fully correct after only one issue has been repaired.

If the learner says «Слишком сложно», offer a shorter sentence or a simpler context in the same mode and agreed level. Do not automatically switch from free translation to multiple choice or lower their declared level.

## A fluent answer to a different question

If the role asks the learner to arrange a viewing with a landlord but the answer describes their weekend, acknowledge it and bring the conversation back to arranging the viewing. Explain that the communicative task is unfinished; do not invent a grammar error in an otherwise correct answer. Follow an explicit request to change the situation.

## Report with a retry

Suppose the learner answered three distinct tasks: two correctly on the first try, one correctly after a grammar hint and a retry. Report three tasks, two correct first attempts and one task corrected after a hint. Name only targets that were actually practised. Do not report four completed tasks or three error-free first attempts.

Close with a brief recommendation for the observed error and «Результаты этой практики не записаны в SRS Vocabulum». Do not reuse these illustrative counts in another lesson.
