# Connect Foodics
1. Select the Foodics plugin and connect your Foodics account using OAuth.
2. Sign in through Foodics Console as the business owner or a user with apps:manage; approve access.
3. Ask a specific question about the connected business, such as last week's branch sales or today's inventory levels.
4. For date-sensitive reports, confirm the date range and business timezone. Explain that Foodics tools cannot change data.
5. If the account cannot connect, explain that only owners or app managers can authorize access. Never request passwords or tokens in chat.
