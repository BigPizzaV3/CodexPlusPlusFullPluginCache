# Additional Closing Framework

Use this framework alongside the Closing Strategies reference.

A purposeful Closing should help accomplish three things:
1. Give learners an opportunity to summarize the learning and clarify what they now understand.
2. Praise pitfalls and progress by prompting reflection on the learning process, including productive struggle, revisions, misconceptions, and growth.
3. Provide formative evidence that can inform the teacher's next instructional steps.

Additional established formats may include Necessary Nine, Drawing the Journey, and Thought/Sought/Ought. Opening formats may also be adapted as Closings when the adapted structure directly elicits evidence connected to the learning target.

Keep the Closing brief, practical, and aligned to the intended learning. The Closing is not simply a fun ending or a generic reflection on whether learners liked the lesson.
