# Publishing approved offerings

ChatGPT Codex — CODEX-V2-009. Requires separately approved `catalogue_publish`.

Read the current approved business revision and `get_business_catalogue` first.
Prepare selected offerings with exact confirmed price fact keys. Whole INR prices
only; resolve tax/currency/range ambiguity with the owner. Do not ask them to
retype other facts already in their sources/draft.

Choose confirmed customer facts for descriptions. Confirmed internal floor facts
go only to the dedicated selling-floor field, never descriptions or documents.
Use explicit target IDs for intentional updates; do not overwrite by title.
Preserve separate packages/add-ons and show omitted details to the owner.

Show the exact before/after fields, prices, private floors, omitted facts and
active flags. Entries default inactive. Active entries become available to
existing AI sales/search; this is a real catalogue write. It does not activate
AI, send messages, make payments or establish actual stock/availability.

After exact owner approval, publish with the matching preview receipt. If the
response is lost, read the saved receipt; exact retries cannot duplicate products.
Changed source/target values require a fresh review and approval. Publication
history can be removed without deleting the actual products. Never expose private
floors or owner plans to customers.
