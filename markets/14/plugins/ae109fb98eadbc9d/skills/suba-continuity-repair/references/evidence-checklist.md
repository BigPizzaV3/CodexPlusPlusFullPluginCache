# Evidence for continuity corrections

Adapted from the user's subtitle-continuity-review method. This SubA variant saves supported edits; the original report-only skill remains unchanged.

## Source and version integrity

- Verify actual dialogue language rather than trusting filenames or language metadata. A Chinese-filled English template is not independent English. Never reconstruct an English sentence and cite it as source.
- Same-series reference identity requires series, season/episode and version evidence, not one matching title keyword or project ID. Prefer the latest user-approved completed draft; do not silently switch the target version.
- Pair by reliable unique IDs and verified timing/version. Equal counts or indices alone do not establish alignment. Ambiguous/multiple matches remain ambiguous; examine whole sentences across neighboring cues.
- Record reference versions, cue locations and available hashes. Old reports are leads: check whether their issue still exists in the current draft. Respect retracted conclusions and newer customer decisions.

## Full current-episode reading

Track scenes, relative dates, replay/flashback boundaries, what each speaker knows or believes, who addresses whom, and the objects/stages associated with money, institutions and legal/technical processes. Do not decide a contradiction before later dialogue can explain it. State current-episode coverage separately from the related passages actually read in prior episodes.

## Names and entities: mandatory

Build a compact table of identities, source spellings, full/short names, nicknames, surnames, titles, institutions, places and recurring technical terms. Include one-off unpunctuated names and parents' names, not just names containing a middle dot.

Check both directions: current forms against previous confirmed subtitles, and prior known forms/aliases against current dialogue. Read surrounding scene context. Same surname does not establish kinship; similar spelling does not prove identity. Preserve formal names, affection, roleplay and aliases when the distinction matters. Do not infer gender, marital status or family relationships from a name.

Current customer glossary/explicit decisions outrank older extracted tables. A new name without a baseline is unverified, not automatically wrong. Do not extend names, add real-world surnames or force a common online transliteration over project evidence.

## Semantic decisions

- Confirm the same person, object and source concept before unifying wording; different species, employees, legal documents or courses must remain distinct.
- Distinguish narrative time/stage: acquisition vs closing vs integration; valuation vs available cash; investigation vs charge vs conviction. A later change in knowledge is not automatically an inconsistency.
- Distinguish facts from lies, guesses, jokes, exaggeration, plans and self-justification. Preserve uncertainty and changes in perspective.
- Verify negation, agent/patient, cause/effect, scope, quantities and units against actual evidence. Missing context does not authorize adding 'only', 'all', motives or actions.
- Check currency and number scale against the same financial object and date. A funding gap is not total revenue, account balance is not net worth, and narrative years are not today's calendar.
- Replays of the same source should preserve the established meaning and strength. Different segmentation, shortened quotations and legitimate paraphrases can remain different.
- Technical/common-sense suspicions require source context; do not alter fictional species, diagnoses, dates, models, amounts or legal outcomes using outside assumptions.
- Follow the target-language customer guide rather than English punctuation or source wrapping. Respect reading duration when choosing a correction.

## Evidence levels and actions

- **Supported correction:** source/target mismatch is explicit, or actual Chinese text and confirmed identity/reference prove the error. Save the smallest correction with its evidence.
- **Optional expression:** existing meaning is defensible; retain it unless the user specifically requested polishing and the change preserves meaning and constraints.
- **Unresolved:** missing source, AV, speaker/object identity or terminology baseline. Retain existing text, cite cue/time and specify what evidence would resolve it. Continue other supported edits.

No evidence level exempts a patch from revision checks, layout rules or the write/read-back verification in the main skill.

## Target-language punctuation

Use the current project protocol and selected language/script guide, not a universal Chinese punctuation template. Traditional and Simplified Chinese have different comma, quotation and name conventions. Do not replace all spaces with commas or map every English comma literally: establish target-language clause boundaries from the source/context. A CPL override does not change punctuation policy. Correct verified punctuation errors on this job and record the evidence; unreadable or conflicting policy remains explicit review scope.
