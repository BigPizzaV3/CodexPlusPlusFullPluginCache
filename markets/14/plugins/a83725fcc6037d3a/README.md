# Octohog

Octohog's plugin connects Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code and Codex to your Octohog workspace through
Octohog's MCP server, `https://www.octohog.com/api/mcp`.

Find UGC creators who fit your brand, ask about your competitors' TikTok and Instagram posts (hooks,
captions, stored transcripts and views), and check your Creator Match and Creator Tracking campaigns,
your roster creators, and finished Video Blueprints and TikTok Deep-Dive reports.

- **Read-only.** The tools find and read. Saving shortlists, sending outreach, changing Radar and
  starting new research stay in the Octohog web app.
- **Your view.** The connection sees what your Octohog login sees. Creator contact details and
  payment records stay in Octohog.
- **You approve it.** Sign in to Octohog and approve the connection. Disconnect it anytime under
  Account, Connected apps.

Setup: add the server URL above as a custom connector or MCP server in your assistant, then sign in
to Octohog.
