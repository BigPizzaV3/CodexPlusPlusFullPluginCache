# AI search action plan
Diagnose first, then plan.
1. get_visibility_score and get_competitor_comparison: where the brand stands.
2. get_competitor_visibility_gap for the top 1–2 competitors: where the biggest gaps are.
3. get_citation_sources: which sources drive answers, and how often the own site is cited.
4. get_sentiment_breakdown: perception issues to address.
Output a prioritized plan in three horizons (next 30, 60 and 90 days). Each action names:
- the question or topic it targets;
- the evidence (numbers and links);
- the type of work (on-site content, earned coverage, messaging fix);
- a measurable signal to watch in Refora, such as visibility on that question or citations from a domain.
Keep it to 6–9 actions, and say which ones can't be measured with current monitoring.
