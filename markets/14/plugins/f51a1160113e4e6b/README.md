# Lukka Prediction Markets Data MCP

OpenAI display name: Lukka Prediction Markets Data

Research prediction market listings, outcomes, PMCS taxonomy, activity, open interest, price history and venue context through Lukka Predmar. Requires a connected Lukka account with Prediction Markets entitlement. Coverage varies by venue and market. Present observed market data with timestamps and sources, without treating market prices as guaranteed probabilities. The connector does not create markets, place wagers or execute trades.
