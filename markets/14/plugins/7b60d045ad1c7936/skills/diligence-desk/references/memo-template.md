# Diligence memo template

Use these headings in this order. Omit a section only when no source and no founder question applies to it, and say so in one line.

---

**Diligence Desk memo: {Legal name} ({jurisdiction} {company number})**
Prepared {date}. Sources: public registers and filings as listed at the end. Not legal or investment advice.

## 1. Snapshot

Three to five lines: what the records say the company is, where it is registered, when it was incorporated, its status, who controls it, and the single most important finding.

## 2. Corporate facts

## 3. People

Officers, directors and promoters named on the latest Form D (or in the latest 10-K and proxy for registrants) with roles. Changes across successive filings. Compare with the team in the pitch in one sentence.

## 4. Filings and financials

Form D history in date order: offering amount, amount sold, exemption, new notice or amendment. For SEC registrants: latest annual and quarterly reports, notable 8-Ks, and the key concepts for the last two fiscal years in a small table. SAM.gov and LEI registration status. Say explicitly that Form D does not disclose revenue or valuation.

## 5. Technical footprint

Domain registration date and registrar. GitHub organisation summary: repositories, stars, last activity, archived or forked flagships, licences. One sentence on whether the footprint matches the pitch.

## 6. Red flags

Numbered. Each: fact (source, date), why it matters, question for the founders. If none: "No red flags in the public record reviewed."

## 7. Open questions for the founders

Numbered, one sentence each, answerable in one sentence. Include the untestable claims from the consistency check.

## 8. Sources

Bulleted list of every record used, with the register or filing name, identifier and the date on the record. Example: *SEC EDGAR Form D, accession 0001234567-26-000012, filed 2026-03-02, retrieved 2026-10-23.* Also list the sources checked that returned no record.

---

Length: under 600 words for a first pass. Tables for facts, prose only in the snapshot and the flags.
