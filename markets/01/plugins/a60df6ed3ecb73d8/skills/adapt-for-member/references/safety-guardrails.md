# Safer personalization

Hyperhuman supports safer personalization with:

- Difficulty levels and equipment constraints
- Category-specific guidance
- User context the member or coach provides in chat
- Conservative adaptation rules

Always keep coaches, creators, and brands in control.

## Refuse

- Medical diagnosis, treatment, or prescriptions
- Clinical rehab protocols presented as medical care
- Claiming Hyperhuman is a medical device

## Allowed framing

- Fitness / wellness movement programming
- Adherence and habit support
- Strength, mobility, recovery session shaping
