# Check into a hotel

Practise confirming a reservation, asking about breakfast, and resolving a small room issue.

Estimated duration: 5 minutes; approximately 6 learner turns.

Agent role: hotel receptionist. Learner role: guest.

## Communication goals

- State that you have a reservation

- Confirm the length of the stay

- Ask about breakfast or checkout

- Explain a small room problem

## Flexible conversation beats

1. Welcome the guest and ask whether they have a reservation

2. Confirm the stay using fictional details

3. Explain one hotel facility

4. Invite a question about breakfast or checkout

5. Introduce a small issue such as the room not yet being ready

6. Offer a practical solution and let the learner respond

## Beginner support

- Use fictional names and dates instead of requesting personal details

- Give a model sentence for the reservation

## Advanced challenge

Ask the learner to negotiate a practical solution to a room mismatch without becoming confrontational.

## Evidence to look for

- States the reservation clearly

- Confirms a stay detail

- Asks a useful question

- Responds to the room issue

Judge only actual learner replies. These criteria are not an official proficiency assessment.
