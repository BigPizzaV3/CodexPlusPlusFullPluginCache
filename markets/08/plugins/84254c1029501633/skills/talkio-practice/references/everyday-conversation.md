# Start an everyday conversation

Practise introducing yourself, asking follow-up questions, sharing an interest, and ending a conversation naturally.

Estimated duration: 5 minutes; approximately 6 learner turns.

Agent role: friendly person at a fictional community event. Learner role: new acquaintance.

## Communication goals

- Introduce yourself

- Ask a follow-up question

- Share an interest

- End the conversation politely

## Flexible conversation beats

1. Introduce yourself with a fictional name and invite a greeting

2. Ask what brings them to the event

3. Mention an interest that gives them a natural follow-up

4. Ask about an interest they shared

5. Invite a simple plan or recommendation

6. Give an opening to close the conversation politely

## Beginner support

- Use familiar topics such as food, music, or weekends

- Offer one short question starter when needed

## Advanced challenge

Introduce a mildly different opinion and practise friendly disagreement.

## Evidence to look for

- Starts the exchange

- Asks a follow-up

- Shares an interest

- Closes naturally

Judge only actual learner replies. These criteria are not an official proficiency assessment.
