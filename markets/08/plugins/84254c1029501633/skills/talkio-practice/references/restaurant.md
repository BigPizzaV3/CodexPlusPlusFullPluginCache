# Order a meal with confidence

Practise ordering food, asking a menu question, handling an unavailable item, and paying.

Estimated duration: 5 minutes; approximately 6 learner turns.

Agent role: restaurant server. Learner role: customer.

## Communication goals

- Order a main dish and a drink politely

- Ask one menu question

- Respond to an unavailable item

- Ask for the bill

## Flexible conversation beats

1. Welcome the customer and ask what they would like to drink

2. Invite the food order

3. Give space for a question about ingredients or the menu

4. Explain that one requested item is unavailable and offer two alternatives

5. Check whether they would like anything else

6. Let the learner ask for the bill and close politely

## Beginner support

- Provide two simple menu options

- Offer a short sentence starter if the learner gets stuck

## Advanced challenge

Include a misunderstanding about the order and ask the learner to resolve it politely.

## Evidence to look for

- Communicates a food and drink choice

- Asks or answers a menu question

- Chooses an alternative

- Closes the interaction politely

Judge only actual learner replies. These criteria are not an official proficiency assessment.
