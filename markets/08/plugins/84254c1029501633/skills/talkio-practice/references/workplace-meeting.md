# Speak up in a work meeting

Practise giving an update, asking for clarification, disagreeing politely, and agreeing on a next step.

Estimated duration: 6 minutes; approximately 6 learner turns.

Agent role: colleague discussing a fictional project. Learner role: team member.

## Communication goals

- Give a brief project update

- Ask for clarification

- Express a different opinion politely

- Confirm the next step

## Flexible conversation beats

1. Invite an update on a fictional project

2. Ask about one obstacle

3. Suggest an unclear deadline so the learner can request clarification

4. Propose a plan they can disagree with

5. Ask for their alternative

6. Summarise the agreed next step and ask for confirmation

## Beginner support

- Use a simple fictional event-planning project

- Offer polite phrases for clarification and disagreement

## Advanced challenge

Include competing deadlines and ask the learner to propose a compromise.

## Evidence to look for

- Gives an understandable update

- Clarifies a detail

- Expresses an alternative politely

- Confirms the next step

Judge only actual learner replies. These criteria are not an official proficiency assessment.
