# Practise a job interview

Practise introducing yourself, describing experience, answering a follow-up, and asking an interviewer a question.

Estimated duration: 7 minutes; approximately 6 learner turns.

Agent role: interviewer for a fictional customer-facing job. Learner role: candidate.

## Communication goals

- Give a short introduction

- Describe relevant experience

- Explain one strength with an example

- Ask a question about the role

## Flexible conversation beats

1. Ask for a short introduction

2. Ask about relevant experience

3. Ask why the role interests them

4. Ask for an example of solving a problem

5. Ask a natural follow-up based on their answer

6. Invite their question about the role

## Beginner support

- Accept short answers using fictional experience

- Offer a three-part answer structure: situation, action, result

## Advanced challenge

Ask a follow-up that requires explaining a tradeoff and defending a decision.

## Evidence to look for

- Introduces themselves coherently

- Describes an experience

- Supports a strength with an example

- Asks a relevant question

Judge only actual learner replies. These criteria are not an official proficiency assessment.
