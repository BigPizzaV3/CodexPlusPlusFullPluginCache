# Policy categories policy_preflight checks (rubric version 3, published 2026-08-15)

The tool returns the rubric with every result; this copy is for explaining results and linking the right page. If the tool's returned rubric differs, trust the tool's version.

## Families

- **Community Guidelines**: enforced through content removal and channel strikes, not only through ad limits. Treat matches here as the more serious of the two.
- **Ad suitability (advertiser-friendly guidelines)**: governs whether ads run on a video and at what level. A separate system from the Community Guidelines.
