# TTSStudio product FAQ

## What can TTSStudio do in ChatGPT?

TTSStudio converts user-provided written text into MP3 speech. The interactive
workspace supports reviewing text, browsing and previewing voices, adjusting
delivery, generating audio, playing completed results and managing audio
history. It can help with narration, voiceovers and read-aloud audio.

Opening the workspace can prefill supplied text. The user chooses settings and
starts generation inside the widget. Opening it or previewing a catalog voice
does not create a new speech generation.

## How do I provide text?

Paste the text into the workspace, or supply the exact text in the conversation
and ask to open TTSStudio with it prefilled. A generation accepts up to 10,000
characters. Review longer material and split it into suitable passages before
generating. Do not assume that a document or recording attached to ChatGPT is
automatically imported into this workspace.

The plugin does not transcribe recordings, identify speakers, clone a voice
from an uploaded recording, or generate music and sound effects. Website
features outside the ChatGPT workspace do not establish plugin capabilities.

## How do I choose a voice and language?

Browse the current catalog and use its language, gender, age, timbre and
use-case filters or voice search. Preview a voice before selecting it. Choose
from the voices and language options currently shown; do not promise a fixed
voice count, a particular voice, or universal language support.

The widget supports emotion, speed, pitch and volume controls. These affect
delivery; the user should review the available settings before generating.
Keep a requested language or style separate from claims that a particular
catalog voice supports it.

## Does generation use my allowance?

Yes. A generation uses the connected account's applicable TTSStudio allowance
and saves its result to audio history. Opening the workspace does not spend
generation allowance. Do not promise free generations, remaining balance,
reset dates or subscription access without current account evidence.

If the workspace reports that the allowance has been reached, explain that
message and direct the user to the account or published pricing page. Do not
claim that an empty audio result is playable or that a purchase was completed.

## Where are previous results?

Open the workspace and review audio history. The widget can show completed and
failed generations and play available completed audio. Failed entries do not
contain successful audio; their playback and download actions show an error.
Unavailable or expired audio should not be described as a working download.

The user can select a history item for deletion and confirm it in the widget's
dialog. Canceling keeps the item. Confirming permanently deletes that audio
and its history record. This does not cancel a subscription or delete an account.

## How do downloads work?

Download access depends on the account and the selected result. For an eligible
paid result, MP3 downloads use its audio file and WAV opens that result on the
TTSStudio website. Free-plan download actions open the pricing page. The WAV
action does not mean a WAV file was returned by the MCP generation tool.

Use the actual controls shown for that result. A failed generation cannot
become a successful MP3 or WAV download by clicking its download action.
For current plan details, visit [pricing](https://ttsstudio.ai/pricing).

## How do I connect or recover the workspace?

Use the host's TTSStudio connection flow and sign in to the intended account.
If the workspace asks to reconnect, reconnect through the host before trying
again. Never ask the user to paste passwords, tokens or cookies into chat.

If opening or loading history fails, describe the failure and use the
workspace's available recovery controls. The user can also visit
[TTSStudio](https://ttsstudio.ai) or [support](https://ttsstudio.ai/contact-us).
If generation may already have completed, check history before submitting
again. Do not claim success from the opening of a panel alone.
