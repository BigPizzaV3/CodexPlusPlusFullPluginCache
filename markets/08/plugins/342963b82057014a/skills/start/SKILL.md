# Start with beckett

1. If beckett's tools aren't available, beckett isn't connected yet. Give the step and stop:
   - In ChatGPT, open **Plugins**, choose **beckett**, and connect a beckett account. Connecting creates one for anyone who doesn't have an account yet.
2. Start by finding out where beckett can help most. Ask one single-select question, using the interactive question tool if this surface has one, and otherwise offering the same four choices in a short message:

   **What should we set up first in beckett?**
   * **Suggest something for me (Recommended)**
   * **Stay on top of things:** tasks, reminders, calendar, projects
   * **Remember things:** notes, people, saved links, books and shows
   * **Health and food:** habits, lifting, recipes, meal plans

   Use these descriptions when working through what they choose:
   * **Tasks and to-do lists:** reminders in beckett when they want one
   * **Calendar:** appointments and plans on their beckett calendar
   * **Projects:** anything with several parts, such as a vacation, a renovation, or a hobby, as a beckett project with lists, tasks, tables, notes, and more
   * **Notes:** ideas and other things to remember, saved in beckett, automatically categorized and linked to the people, places, and things mentioned in them
   * **Personal CRM:** the people and places they mention, with linked notes, events, and tasks
   * **Saved links:** articles, videos, resources, and research to come back to
   * **Books, movies, and shows:** ratings and watch or read statuses
   * **Habits and trackers:** something they want to do regularly or keep an eye on, with a scheduled check-in from beckett if they'd like a nudge
   * **Lifting:** a beckett routine based on their goals and preferences, and workouts logged against it
   * **Cooking and meals:** finding or creating recipes, a meal plan based on their diet goals, or a grocery list based on recipes or the meal plan

   After they answer:
   * **If they pick a category:** immediately ask for one real example from their life, such as "What's one thing you need to get done this week?" or "Who's someone you always want to remember details about?" If the category covers several areas, let their example decide which one to use.
   * **If they type their own answer:** start with what they wrote.
   * **If they ask for a suggestion:** Offer one to three specific starting points that each name something real from their life and say what you'd set up, then ask which one to start with. Don't open with health, money, relationship trouble, or a hard time unless they raised it in this conversation.
   * **If you don't know enough to be specific:** say so and offer to make suggestions
3. Keep going. Save each thing in beckett as soon as you have the details, following the shared rules in the use-beckett skill, and confirm it in one short line. After each save, follow the thread: the next piece of what they described, such as the tasks and dates in a project or the people involved, or another area they brought up. Every question should come from something they said, and don't fill in anything they didn't say. The aim is several real things saved in this first conversation, not one. If they ask for something else instead, do that. Stop when they want to stop.
4. After the first save, say once where everything shows up: the beckett app on their phone and computer, and the web at app.yourbeckett.com, where they can open, change, or delete anything saved. If they set up a reminder or check-in, add that it arrives as a notification from the beckett app on their phone, not in ChatGPT, once they sign in there.
   - iPhone and iPad: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beckett-ai-life-assistant/id6761432798)
   - Android: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.beckettai.beckett)
