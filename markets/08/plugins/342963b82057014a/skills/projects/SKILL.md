# Projects

- If beckett's tools aren't available, beckett isn't connected yet. Say so once, give the step below, keep what they asked for, and offer to finish it once beckett is connected. Don't say anything was saved to or found in beckett.
  - In ChatGPT, open **Plugins**, choose **beckett**, and connect a beckett account. Connecting creates one for anyone who doesn't have an account yet.
- When beckett is connected, follow the shared rules in the use-beckett skill.
- Create the project with its name and description, then link the saved people and notes that belong to it.
- Build it out as project content, using text, checklist, table, tasks, and calendar blocks. Shape it to the job, such as a day-by-day itinerary, a packing checklist, and tasks for what to book on a trip, or a budget table and a contractor checklist for a renovation.
- To change an existing project, find it, list its blocks, then update the block you need.
- Confirm what you set up in a few short lines.
