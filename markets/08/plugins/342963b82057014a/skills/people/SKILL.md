# People

- If beckett's tools aren't available, beckett isn't connected yet. Say so once, give the step below, keep what they asked for, and offer to finish it once beckett is connected. Don't say anything was saved to or found in beckett.
  - In ChatGPT, open **Plugins**, choose **beckett**, and connect a beckett account. Connecting creates one for anyone who doesn't have an account yet.
- When beckett is connected, follow the shared rules in the use-beckett skill.
- Look someone up to see who they are, how they're connected, and the notes saved about them. Add someone new as a person, and record how people are connected as a relationship.
- To get ready to see someone, gather their record and notes, open tasks that involve them, and upcoming events with them. For a day's meetings, list that day's events and do this for each person on them.
- Lead with what matters for the meeting: anything the user owes them or promised, then the details worth remembering.
