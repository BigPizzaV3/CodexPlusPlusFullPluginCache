# Find Online businesses in CardShow

Use `cardshow_browse_online_businesses` with the customer's collecting interests and supported filters. Search, combined filters, and profiles are available on Free and Pro; use the full current directory returned by CardShow. The directory contains only businesses that are currently eligible, whose owners submitted and manage profiles through CardShow Pro in the app, and passed review. CardShow controls current visibility server-side; do not infer a complete directory, visibility, or listing eligibility from collector Pro status. A collector's Pro subscription does not establish ownership or permission to list a business. This MCP workflow only discovers and reads businesses; it cannot submit, manage, approve, or publish business profiles. No editorial seed businesses are included. The private saved shortlist is Pro-only. A saved-only access error means access was denied, not that the shortlist is empty. Never describe a partial or unavailable response as the full directory.

Use returned exact IDs with `cardshow_get_online_business` to inspect a currently visible profile, its reviewed selling links, and source dates. Respect blocked and unavailable responses; do not retry anonymously, use a stale profile as fallback, or substitute another account. A visible directory listing does not prove the collector owns or may publish that business. Online identities have no map location or directions. Seller links and reviewed sources are leads, not guarantees of stock, authenticity, ownership, price, or availability. Do not contact the seller or make a purchase.

For a shortlist change, read the exact profile first. If a `shortlistReview` is available, state the business name, whether it is currently saved, and the requested final saved state. Ask for confirmation of that exact change. A request to find or compare businesses does not authorize saving them. After confirmation, call `cardshow_set_online_business_saved` with the unchanged review, desired `saved` value, and `confirmed: true`. This changes only the signed-in customer's private shortlist. Never fabricate a review, account reference, or business ID.

If the account changes, the review expires, or the profile changes, read and confirm again. On an uncertain transport result, retry only the same confirmed desired state while the review is valid; never toggle or infer success from a failed call. Report success only from a successful tool result. Ownership claims, business edits, reporting, blocks, and publication remain in the existing CardShow app workflows.
