# La Pyme

Connect ChatGPT and Codex to your La Pyme account to inspect and manage sales, purchases, inventory, treasury, accounting, and reports.

The remote MCP server is `https://mcp.lapyme.com.ar/mcp`. Sign in with your La Pyme account through OAuth. The package has no local server, scripts, or lifecycle hooks.

The four bundled skills cover sales and ARCA invoicing, business reporting, inventory planning, and supplier cost updates. Tools enforce the connected organization and account permissions. Exact requests can authorize writes; an analysis request alone does not.

Government identifiers, including DNI, CUIL, and CUIT, cannot be entered or searched through MCP and are excluded from model-visible results. Complete fiscal setup in La Pyme. Raw document downloads are excluded; use the returned in-product review route. Some writes can issue fiscal documents or trigger configured synchronization with connected sales channels.
