# Translation and Licensing

The hosted MCP service serves the World English Bible from the existing Hedraios-controlled corpus.

- Translation label: World English Bible
- Code: WEB
- Rights status: public domain
- Source: https://ebible.org/web/
- Corpus source archive: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-web_usfx.zip

The plugin must identify WEB by name and must not present it as ESV, NIV, NLT, CSB, or another translation.

Do not add or redistribute copyrighted Bible text without a verified license that covers the intended storage, quotation, display, and marketplace use. A future provider adapter may add licensed translations, but absence of a license is a release blocker, not a feature gap to work around.

User-supplied text may be analyzed as user-supplied text. Do not identify its translation or certify its accuracy unless a licensed or public-domain source is available for comparison.
