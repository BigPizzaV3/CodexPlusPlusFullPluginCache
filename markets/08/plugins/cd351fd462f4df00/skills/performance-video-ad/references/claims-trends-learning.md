# Claims, trends, and learning

## Claims

- Preserve exact customer quotes when displayed as quotes.
- Keep rendered copy separate from source quotes.
- Mark hypotheses as hypotheses.
- Use stronger claims only with stronger proof.
- Do not invent metrics, rankings, ratings, testimonials, or guarantees.
- Use diagnostic order rather than comparative ranking when comparison evidence is absent.

## Trend policy

Trend is an optional format wrapper, not strategy or proof.

Trend may change opening composition, opening event, reveal order, pacing, first sound, and transition grammar. It may not silently change audience, moment, pain, insight, promise, proof, body, CTA, or destination.

Default `balanced_default` portfolio:

- grounded or evergreen ≥ 66%
- trend-adapted ≤ 20%
- experimental ≤ 20%

Require explicit user intent for `trend_exploration`.

## Three evidence layers

1. `Trend Observation`: a format appears to be spreading.
2. `Generation Learning`: a model followed or failed the requested shot.
3. `Performance Learning`: campaign Stop, Hold, CTR, CVR, CPA, or lead quality changed.

Never promote a trend observation or beautiful generation into a performance conclusion.
