# Creative QC checklist

Use this checklist before storyboard compilation and again after deterministic composition. Record each item as `PASS`, `REVISE`, `REJECT`, or `NOT_RUN`.

## Context and policy checks

- One `offerId` is locked and every canonical source belongs to that offer or is explicitly background only.
- The source precedence is user-designated canonical → approved offer decision → current offer LP/spec → offer VOC/research/interview → global strategy → general method/trend.
- Every required source was found, read, yielded evidence, and is used by at least one named insight.
- The derived Source Coverage Report matches the manifest and is `PASS`.
- Offer/global-strategy conflicts are explicit and resolved in favor of the higher-precedence offer source.
- The Insight Evidence Map reaches the offer's required depth without labeling synthesis as a direct quote.
- The Creative Premise is explicitly human-approved before any Hook exists.
- Pain, Stakes, failed approach, new belief, mechanism, payoff, and CTA deliverable are locked in the Message Inheritance Contract.
- Every viewer-facing Hook, Body, UI, narration, and CTA line has copy lineage and source refs.
- Audience temperature and awareness stage are classified separately.
- Trust, decision stakes, familiarity, sophistication, and evidence maturity cite canonical sources.
- Historical creative signals include scope, confidence, and source; they remain priors rather than universal laws.
- The resolved Expression Policy matches the context resolver output.
- Market type alone did not select the Expression Profile.
- Visual intensity and message strength are resolved independently; restrained visuals did not automatically weaken semantic tension.
- The claim ladder separates documented deliverables, mechanism-supported possibilities, and prohibited guarantees.
- The live destination was reviewed recently enough for the current phase.
- Every blocking funnel field was retrieved from an authoritative source or confirmed by the user; no blocking field relies only on inference or stale conversation memory.
- When blocking context remained missing or conflicting, the Skill asked no more than three decision-changing questions and stopped before Hook work.
- The Funnel Role Contract names one primary stage job, the next destination's job, a message budget, and the intentionally deferred questions.
- A cold click-through ad leading to a full-persuasion LP creates qualified curiosity instead of attempting to become a miniature LP.
- Core messages fit the stage budget; mechanism, proof, objection, deliverable, and price detail do not leak in from later stages.
- Every ad-scoped copy-lineage entry maps to one planned core message, and every planned core message is visibly rendered.
- Ad CTA and destination CTA request the same semantic action.
- Ad claims are equal to or narrower than the destination promise.
- The free consultation and paid deliverable remain visibly distinct.

## Per-Hook planning checks

- The WHO recognizes the work situation without explanatory setup.
- A work problem, anomaly, or social tension is explainable from the first 0.5 seconds; contradiction is not required for every mechanism.
- The work problem is understandable within two seconds.
- The opening contains an unresolved question that naturally connects to the offer.
- Pain is recognized before the visual technique.
- Removing decorative VFX does not remove the Hook's meaning.
- The opening from 0-2.5 seconds is a plausible PC, phone, document, chat, calendar, graph, meeting, or screen-recording surface.
- One scene contains one primary action; simultaneous rapid animation is absent.
- Exact work copy is deterministic rather than baked into generated footage.
- Sound intensity matches the expression profile and does not substitute for meaning.
- Brand treatment is mainly in the latter half, payoff, or End Card.
- The Hook inherits the approved Creative Premise instead of drifting to a globally convenient but different offer.
- Synthesized copy is labeled as synthesis and is not rendered as a customer quotation or performance fact.

For `restrained_b2b_social`, reject screen destruction or distortion, monitor impact, torn dashboards, falls into giant spaces, wall cracks, rescue light, strong flares, persistent camera warps, concurrent rapid animation, and full-screen gold light.

## Portfolio checks

- An initial portfolio contains at least six Hooks and targets eight.
- At least three psychological mechanisms are present; none occupies more than half.
- When the resolved default is restrained, at least six of eight are `restrained_b2b_social` and at most one is `balanced_social_brand`.
- In an eight-Hook portfolio, at most one is `spectacle_experiment`.
- Every `spectacle_experiment` has explicit user permission or a written experiment purpose and remains `experimental_only`.
- Recommendation reasons follow: WHO relevance, two-second problem understanding, unresolved question, offer connection, trust, compatible Body connection, production ease, visual novelty.
- Visual novelty or ease of AI generation is never the primary recommendation reason.
- Hypothesis-family, attention-mechanism, and visual-grammar diversity all pass.
- Freeze annotation, ranking, reply, and similar wrappers are stored as execution or trend wrappers rather than invented hypotheses.
- `testType`, fixed layers, variable layers, and causal claim scope agree.
- A package discovery is not described as a pure angle or execution test.
- Conditional Hooks state required data visibility and the risk of misreading a loss.
- A two- or three-Hook shortlist is explicitly non-gating exploration and does not replace the controlled batch.

## Body Contract and fit checks

- Every Hook has a Promise family and maps to exactly one compatible Body Contract.
- The rendered Body performs only the current funnel-stage job and leaves the declared destination questions unresolved.
- The Body remains fixed inside each controlled Hook batch.
- Mixed Body families remain consultation cohorts and are never presented as one controlled test.
- The Body names the belief to break, new belief, product-specific mechanism, visible before/after, payoff, and CTA deliverable.
- The Body Contract contains human Pain, failed response, Aha, calibrated mechanism, and concrete Payoff; the rendered Body includes only the layers assigned by the Funnel Role.
- The Aha explains why the existing workaround may fail and why the offer permits a different view.
- The main customer value is not reduced to checking, sorting, separating, holding, listing, or reviewing.
- Every critical insight has a named location in the Insight Render Map.
- A package-specific Handoff preserves Hook emotion when the common Body begins after the opening.
- A sequence of noun lists or service fields is rejected as a Body.
- The Body shows work-native documents or UI before abstract diagrams.
- Each scene has one primary action and a legible purpose.
- Each beat stays within `max_concepts_per_beat`.
- Exact promise, proof boundary, CTA, destination, logo, and safe zone remain deterministic.
- Heroic language or spectacle does not overstate the evidence.
- UI labels and spoken copy are separate. Compact internal nouns are not read aloud unchanged.
- Spoken Japanese passes the colleague, one-breath, noun-chain, concrete-action, duplication, and read-aloud checks in [japanese-naturalness-rules.md](japanese-naturalness-rules.md).
- A diagnosis demo shows observations and unknowns separately.
- The selected item is presented as the first check, not as the cause.
- A cause is asserted only when evidence in the artifact supports it.

## Generated-footage and finish checks

Leave this section `NOT_RUN` before video generation.

- The raw hero shot is reviewed before captions, sound design, shards, shake, or other deterministic polish.
- Central cause-and-effect, impact energy, identity fidelity, and natural-speed readability are all binary `PASS` items.
- Any intent-critical failure sets Creative QC to `FAIL`; `PASS_WITH_DEVIATIONS` is not used to excuse loss of the approved hero intent.
- Human raw-shot approval exists before the generated footage is integrated into Remotion.
- Requested subjects and actions match the manifest.
- Identity, anatomy, objects, continuity, and endpoint are stable.
- No unintended text, logo, endorsement, testimonial, result, or guarantee appears.
- Dimensions, duration, frame rate, codec, audio, loudness, crop, and safe zones pass technical QC.
- Provider-footage cadence is reviewed inside the final timeline; a source/timeline fps mismatch has been conformed or explicitly accepted, and fast motion has no uneven repeated-frame judder.
- Any post-approval speed ramp has explicit source/output boundaries and rates, avoids synthetic interpolation by default, preserves a readable landing, and has a fresh natural-speed human review.
- Concrete offer proof uses canonical anonymized actual deliverables when available; source, sanitization status, and hashes are recorded.
- Image-generated end-card art is treated as background/texture; exact logo, Japanese copy, offer terms, and CTA are rendered deterministically and remain inside the safe zone.
- Editorial tempo is reviewed separately from visual intensity: restrained B2B treatment may still use fast cuts, while every new claim retains a comprehension anchor.
- A quantity montage uses fast cuts only to communicate volume; at least one proof page before it and one payoff page after it remain large and readable.
- Generated-shot dead air is trimmed before retiming, and the readable endpoint remains intact.
- The final creative still makes sense without audio and after decorative VFX are muted.
- A `first_pass_85_baseline` Master passes the dedicated validator, remains explicitly pending human last-mile review, and does not convert its completion estimate into performance evidence or publication approval.

## Low-fidelity Animatic approval

Run this human review after Hook/Body approval and before executable shot routing. Require `PASS` for: work problem/anomaly/social tension by 0.5 seconds, WHO and situation by two seconds, VFX independence or explicit experimental status, Hook-to-Body Promise continuity, funnel-role and message-budget fit, the intended open question, mute legibility, cognitive-load policy, Aha clarity, semantic expectation without unsupported certainty, CTA/destination continuity, and preserved trust.

Machine validators enforce artifact presence and state transitions. Human reviewers decide whether meaning and trust actually pass. Leave `animatic-review.json` pending until static frames or a low-fidelity Animatic exist; never approve it from schema declarations alone.
