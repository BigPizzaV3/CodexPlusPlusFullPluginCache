# Attention source taxonomy

Keep three layers separate.

## Hook hypothesis

The business tension to test: work incident, Pain, contradiction when one exists, unresolved question, and product linkage. A hypothesis must remain meaningful without a rendering technique.

## Attention mechanism

Why the viewer continues:

- `workplace_contradiction`: two work facts conflict, such as more revisions and a flat result;
- `social_tension`: a client, colleague, meeting, or unanswered document creates accountability;
- `semantic_tension`: the visible facts imply an unanswered diagnosis;
- `imminent_consequence`: a credible deadline or decision approaches;
- `product_demonstration`: the product visibly performs useful work;
- `proof_demonstration`: source-backed evidence is shown;
- `native_format`: a platform-native interaction supports the message;
- `restrained_visual_change`: one bounded visual change clarifies meaning;
- `visual_novelty`, `spectacle`, `cinematic_vfx`, `destruction_metaphor`: expression-led sources that require policy permission and must not substitute for weak meaning.

## Execution grammar

How the mechanism is rendered: notification arrival, revision reveal, comment arrival, cursor pause, screen-recording freeze, product demo, hard cut, or cinematic transform.

A freeze-frame annotation, ranking layout, comment-reply layout, or platform wrapper is usually execution or `trendWrapper`, not a new hypothesis. Compare the same `hypothesisId` across executions when the learning question is about presentation.

Validate diversity across hypothesis family, attention mechanism, and execution grammar. “All PC screens” is not necessarily one grammar; “all notifications arriving” is.
