# First-pass 85 baseline

Use this contract after the Creative Premise, Territory, Animatic, and any paid Raw have received their required human approvals. Its purpose is to make the first deterministic review Master consistently reach a useful last-mile review state. It does not replace the human gates and it is not a performance claim.

## Definition

`first_pass_85_baseline` means:

- the approved Hook-to-Body arc is intact;
- the viewer can identify WHO and the work problem within two seconds;
- the selected emotional tension and Hope survive the edit;
- generated motion has passed Raw review and is integrated without cadence damage;
- exact Japanese, brand, offer terms, and CTA are deterministic;
- concrete proof is source-audited and shown with a readable-to-fast-to-readable rhythm;
- the full file decodes and plays across every splice boundary in the target player;
- the Master is ready for human last-mile notes, not approved for publication.

The number `85` is a human production-readiness target. Never report it as observed ad performance, conversion probability, or creative effectiveness.

## Default recipe

1. Lock the approved Territory, Hook-to-Body arc, funnel role, destination, and CTA. Do not rewrite strategy during finishing.
2. Select and justify a tempo profile independently from the Expression Profile. Use `anchor_compress_cascade_payoff` only when artifact breadth is part of the approved value demonstration.
3. Put the incident on screen by 0.5 seconds and WHO plus the problem by two seconds.
4. Alternate comprehension anchors with action compression. Give every cut one semantic job.
5. Review generated motion at natural speed. Remove unusable dead air before retiming; accelerate the causal action only as much as needed and preserve a readable endpoint.
6. When the offer has a concrete artifact, audit its source and choose either a readable single-artifact demonstration or `large readable proof -> rapid quantity cascade -> quantity wall -> large payoff proof`. Use the quantity pattern only when volume matters. The montage may prove volume; only the anchors may carry new claims.
7. Render exact Japanese, logo, price, legal copy, and CTA in Remotion or another deterministic layer.
8. Match delivery cadence to the approved Raw where practical. Require constant frame rate, a compatible timebase, `yuv420p`, start-at-zero timestamps, full decode, and target-player boundary playback.
9. Hold the End Card for at least 3.5 seconds.
10. Write `first-pass-baseline.json`, run `validate-first-pass-baseline.mjs`, then stop for last-mile human review.

## Proof modes

Use `offer_artifact` only when the offer promises a report, diagnosis, plan, design, or other concrete artifact. Require a source audit, source hashes, a claim boundary, at least one readable anchor, and a no-results-claim declaration unless results evidence exists.

Use `none` when the offer has no relevant artifact or no safe source exists. Do not invent fake proof to satisfy the baseline. Replace the proof sequence with another approved comprehension anchor and record why artifact proof is not applicable.

## The final 15 percent

Keep these decisions human-led:

- whether the emotional beat feels true;
- whether the edit feels urgent without becoming confusing;
- whether the copy sounds like the actual audience;
- whether the generated hero has the intended force and brand character;
- whether the proof creates desire rather than only demonstrating volume;
- whether the End Card creates the intended next action.

Do not automatically publish, activate ads, expand into a Hook batch, or buy another generation after the baseline passes. A passing baseline only unlocks human last-mile review.
