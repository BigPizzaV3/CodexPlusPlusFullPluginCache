# Destination and LP continuity

Review the live destination before Hook approval. Local canonical copy remains the planning source, but the live page is authoritative for what happens after the click.

Capture:

- URL, page title, and review timestamp;
- audience and entry promise;
- exact destination CTA copies and their semantic action;
- what the free consultation produces;
- what the paid offer produces, includes, and excludes;
- proof disclaimers and trust signals;
- claims the ad will make and any unsupported claims.

The ad CTA does not need to repeat the LP button verbatim. It must request the same semantic action. `案件を相談する` and `無料｜まずは相談してみる` can be continuous when both lead to the same case-consultation step.

The ad promise must be equal to or narrower than the destination. Never imply that detailed cross-channel analysis, a full strategy document, implementation, or results are included in a free call when the LP assigns them to a paid offer.

Copy continuity does not require copying every LP phrase. The ad may use plainer language when the problem, mechanism, outcome, proof boundary, and next action remain aligned.

Continuity also does not require the ad to complete the LP's persuasion job. Record the open question created by the ad and the answer supplied by the destination in the Funnel Role Contract. A correct curiosity handoff is continuous when the LP fulfills the expectation without the ad pre-explaining every mechanism, deliverable, objection, or price.

Set a reviewed contract stale after the configured age or when the LP changes materially. Recheck it before an Animatic is approved and again before any separately authorized publication.
