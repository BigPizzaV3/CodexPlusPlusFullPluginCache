# Semantic strength and claim calibration

## Independent axes

Resolve visual expression and message expression separately.

- `visual_intensity` controls spectacle, motion, camera behavior, and simultaneous visual information.
- `semantic_tension` controls how sharply the work contradiction is stated.
- `insight_strength` controls whether the ad contains a meaningful recognition shift.
- `emotional_specificity` controls whether the viewer's professional or social stake is concrete.
- `claim_certainty` controls what the evidence permits the ad to assert.

`restrained_b2b_social` normally lowers visual intensity and motion density. It does not require low semantic tension. A cold, high-trust B2B ad may be visually quiet and semantically sharp.

## Four-layer message

Build the Body in this order:

1. Expectation: state an evidence-calibrated possibility that makes the viewer want a different outcome.
2. Mechanism: show why the offer could reveal or enable that outcome.
3. Safety: label hypotheses and unknowns; do not turn them into proven causes.
4. Payoff: show the concrete progress the viewer can take back to work.

A diagnostic intermediate such as “find what is unverified” may support the mechanism. It must not replace the expectation or Payoff.

## Claim ladder

Record three levels:

- `proven_deliverables`: what the offer or consultation is documented to provide.
- `mechanism_supported_possibilities`: desired possibilities supported by the mechanism and written with calibrated language such as “may” or “could.”
- `prohibited_guarantees`: conclusions or outcomes the current evidence does not permit.

Do not weaken an allowed possibility into administrative copy merely because a guarantee is prohibited.

## Aha Gate

Require all four:

- why the current workaround may keep the viewer stuck;
- why the offer creates a different view;
- one concise “そうか” recognition line;
- an unresolved but honest reason to continue to the destination.

## Over-safety detection

Warn or fail when the Body's value is expressed only through operational actions such as checking, sorting, separating, holding, listing, or reviewing. Require at least one source-backed progress phrase such as leaving a loop, bringing a different next move, testing a new hypothesis, or looking for another breakthrough. Keep its certainty consistent with the claim ladder.

## Hook handoff and cognitive load

When Hook emotion disappears at the shared Body boundary, preserve it with a package-specific handoff. Carry one emotional state and one unresolved question into the common core.

Count concurrent reading layers, not only moving objects. A screen with a heading, several UI labels, explanatory subtitle, and changing diagram may be cognitively dense even with low motion. Prefer one new concept per beat in a comprehension-first Master.
