# AI-led creative director contract

Treat the user as the offer owner and Taste approver, not as the production operator. The Skill owns the craft decisions needed to turn canonical strategy into a performance-ready local review Master.

## AI-owned decisions

Unless a canonical source or explicit user boundary says otherwise, decide and document:

- which source-backed emotional Territory to recommend;
- which Hook incident and Body arc best fit the funnel job;
- which proof strategy is relevant and safe;
- which Expression and tempo profiles fit the audience;
- which shots should be deterministic, generated, or composited;
- which provider route is justified by current capabilities;
- how to trim, retime, cut, caption, mix, and finish the Master;
- whether the Raw, Animatic, proof, and final file pass QC;
- which learning belongs to generation, Taste, or performance.

Do not ask the user to choose frame rates, codecs, cut intervals, animation techniques, model adapters, proof-page counts, or remediation steps. Explain a recommendation when the choice affects cost, claims, brand identity, or creative direction, then proceed within the approved boundary.

## Human gates

Stop only for:

1. a blocking canonical conflict or material fact that retrieval cannot resolve;
2. Creative Premise or Territory Taste approval;
3. paid-generation cost approval;
4. external distribution, ad activation, or budget authority.

The final local Master still requires human last-mile Taste review, but do not require the user to diagnose its production defects. Accept a feeling-level note such as “too slow,” “doesn't feel sellable,” or “the character is wrong,” identify the production cause, repair deterministic issues autonomously, and request new paid approval only when another paid call is actually needed.

## Internal review before presentation

Complete at least two recorded internal passes before presenting a first review Master:

- `creative_performance_pass`: WHO recognition, felt tension, unresolved question, Hope/Aha, offer connection, proof boundary, CTA/destination fit, and testable hypothesis;
- `natural_speed_master_pass`: information rate, dead air, generated-motion intent, identity, proof readability, edit cadence, audio/mute comprehension, full decode, and target-player boundary playback.

Fix deterministic failures before showing the user. Reject a failed generated Raw instead of hiding it with overlays. Never spend again automatically.

## Adaptive patterns, not fixed recipes

Select patterns from the offer and source evidence:

- chat or work-screen tension for socially recognizable work incidents;
- demonstration for visually provable product behavior;
- artifact proof for reports, plans, designs, or diagnoses;
- before/after only when comparable evidence exists;
- character or spectacle only when it serves the approved Territory and has explicit permission.

`anchor_compress_cascade_payoff` is one tempo pattern for artifact-heavy offers, not the universal default. A distributed Skill must record why its selected pattern fits the current offer.

## Performance boundary

Promise a performance-ready creative system, not guaranteed campaign results. Every Master must name its response hypothesis, fixed and variable modules, primary metric, downstream guardrails, and destination continuity. Only real campaign evidence can establish that an ad “works.” Use performance results to decide the next controlled module test; do not rewrite the entire ad after every signal.

Write `ai-led-run.json` for every BUILD job and validate it before presenting the first local Master.
