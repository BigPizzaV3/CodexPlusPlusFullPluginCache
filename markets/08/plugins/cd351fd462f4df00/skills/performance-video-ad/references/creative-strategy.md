## Source and incident mining

Start with a moment the audience recognizes immediately. Prefer observable incidents over abstract pains.

Extract:

- who is present
- what just happened
- what deadline or consequence is approaching
- what the person tried already
- what changed or failed to change
- what exact sentence was said
- what emotional and business stakes coexist

Keep exact quotes exact. Do not convert community language into endorsements.

## Creative mechanisms

Internally compare at least three mechanisms before writing the portfolio:

- imminent consequence
- contradiction or failed effort
- reframe or hidden cause
- visual transformation
- diagnostic curiosity gap
- demonstration or proof
- POV recognition
- rescue or intervention

The initial portfolio must express at least three mechanisms. Do not turn the internal comparison into a mandatory three-route user gate. Three headline rewrites on the same storyboard do not count as breadth.

## Mechanism-diverse Hook portfolio

Default to eight packages and never use fewer than six for an initial test. A Hook Package contains:

- copy
- opening composition
- opening event
- first sound
- first cut timing
- optional format wrapper
- mechanism and incident reference
- source/evidence label
- learning rationale

No mechanism may occupy more than half of an initial portfolio. Preserve every package through consultation. A recommendation may rank packages, but it may not delete or hide them.

In package discovery, do not confuse different sentences with different visual packages. The initial portfolio should include distinct attention sources and recognizable opening surfaces where the hypotheses call for them. Reusing the same chat UI across multiple candidates is appropriate for a deliberate angle test with fixed execution; it is not the default for discovering whether chat contradiction, calendar pressure, revision fatigue, or a report anomaly earns attention.

When a recognizable workplace UI supplies the Hook, first-frame familiarity is part of performance. Preserve sender direction, message hierarchy, navigation, event labeling, and other structural conventions well enough that the viewer understands the work incident without first interpreting an invented app.

## Optional direction sprint

Use a direction sprint only when the user explicitly wants broad visual territories before Hook writing. Compare two or three nonbinding territories and record them under `explorations/`. Do not require the user to select one before creating Hooks. The final portfolio must still span at least three mechanisms.

## Hook portfolio and Body Question Gate

Hold the Body fixed while Hook Packages vary inside a controlled execution batch. Before that batch is locked, verify that every Hook promises the same downstream answer.

Make the first frame legible without audio. Place a visible event inside 0.2 seconds. Avoid beginning on a logo, a static product shot, or slow atmospheric establishment.

When the resolved policy prioritizes work-native attention, an event does not need to be a physical spectacle. Prefer a notification arriving, a cursor stopping, a blank document being shared, revision history increasing, a flat result line, a comment appearing, the same tab reopening, or a brief pause after a question. These are work incidents: their meaning supplies the tension.

For portfolio consultation, create eight persistent packages by default and assign each a `promiseFamily` and `matchedBodyFamilyId`. If all questions resolve through one Body, create one fixed execution batch. If questions split, show multiple Body Contract candidates, keep the test batch in draft, and prohibit cross-family comparison.

Each Body Contract must break one belief, supply one new explanation, show the product-specific mechanism, create a visible before/after, land a concrete payoff, and state what the CTA delivers. Reject Bodies that merely show successive lists of service fields.

Show the Hook portfolio and Body Contract candidates together. After human approval, split consultation cohorts into separate controlled jobs and expand each `initial_hook_test` cohort to at least six Hooks. `HOOK_PORTFOLIO_AND_BODY_FAMILY` approval then unlocks low-fidelity Animatic work for one Body family, not provider routing or generation.

When the user wants to think, stop with the full Hook portfolio, Body mapping, Body Contract candidates, and unresolved questions. Do not generate a storyboard merely to appear productive. Do not allow a generic artifact compiler to hide non-selected Hook candidates.

## Expression control and recommendation order

Choose an `expression_profile` from the source-backed Expression Policy, independently from market type and psychological mechanism. When `restrained_b2b_social` is resolved as the default, it must occupy at least six packages in an eight-package portfolio; `balanced_social_brand` and `spectacle_experiment` may occupy at most one each. Spectacle requires explicit permission or a defined experiment purpose.

For restrained work, test whether the Hook survives after every decorative VFX layer is removed. The problem must be understandable by two seconds, Pain must appear before visual technique, and the opening must look like a plausible work surface. Use brand treatment mainly in the Body payoff or End Card.

Recommend in this order: WHO relevance, two-second problem understanding, unresolved question, offer connection, trust, compatible Body connection, production ease, visual novelty. Never lead a recommendation with AI-generatability or novelty.

## Hypothesis, attention, and execution separation

Write the business hypothesis before visual treatment. Store the work incident, Pain, contradiction when one exists, unresolved question, and product linkage under a persistent hypothesis ID. Then attach an attention mechanism and execution grammar.

One hypothesis may have multiple executions. A screen-recording freeze, notification layout, ranking, or reply wrapper does not become a new hypothesis merely because it looks different. Conversely, two Hooks on the same PC screen may remain distinct grammars when one reveals revision history and another pauses a cursor.

Validate diversity across hypothesis family, attention mechanism, and visual grammar. At 0.5 seconds, require a legible work problem, anomaly, or social tension—not necessarily a contradiction.
