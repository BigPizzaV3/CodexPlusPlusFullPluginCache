# Expression delegation

Run approval may delegate copy, structure, design, implementation and quality_revision decisions within the objective, facts, offer and budget already fixed. Keep the existing six skills' evidence checks, comparisons, rendering/QA and completion criteria. A decision receipt records who chose what and why; it does not replace those checks.

Use `kind:'agent_delegation'` for local expression receipts, binding runId, scopeRevision, scopeHash, stepId, choice, agentId, reason, artifactHash and decidedAt. `scripts/run-contract.mjs::validateDelegationReceipt` accepts composition, visual_direction, copy_expression, candidate_selection, pacing and sound_expression only when listed in the caller's current allowedChoices. Map these to the server's scoped delegation area in the checkpoint's separate `delegated_ai_decision`.

Never put an AI choice in an existing `human`, `user`, conversation message, signed human receipt, or human approval field. The implemented child paths below accept a distinct AI receipt. Other human-only child gates retain their original requirement; never fabricate or silently skip a receipt. Standalone runs keep their original human choice behavior.

Pricing, terms, product changes, truth claims, source rights and formal strategy always require the appropriate human judgment; even a broad expression mandate cannot delegate them. An uncertain factual statement remains a hypothesis. Changes to offer, destination, permissions, money, source set or required outputs require a revised run scope and fresh conversation approval.

## LP and video child gates

LP structure expression can be delegated only with the structure area and separate human product, price, terms, truth and material_rights decisions. All original structure production/source/rights/price checks remain intact; a delegated receipt does not mark source facts as approved. `validate_direct_image_manifest.py` accepts `agent-delegation-record` only for lp-structure, lp-fv-candidate-selection, lp-fv-direction, lp-page-continuity, lp-direct-image-assembly and lp-runtime-assembly. Its strict fields are kind, schema_version (`1.0`), scope, decision (`selected`), subject_sha256 (the child's unchanged subject digest), agent_id, reason, decided_at, run_id, scope_revision, scope_hash, step_id and run_snapshot. The snapshot is the fresh `getMarketingRun` detail, with running status at the decision time, canonical scope hash, matching executor-reported approval, the delegated area and explicit human truth/material_rights decisions. The step must name lp-structure for structure, or lp-image-builder for the subsequent image/assembly gates. These records are host evidence, not server-verified human signatures.

FV `selection_context` becomes `{kind:'agent-delegation-context',selected_candidate_id,reason}`. Do not fabricate presentation/selection message IDs. Preserve the frozen pending comparison and all candidates, comparison observations, exact selected ID, source images, quality outcomes and transition hashes. Regeneration/batch revision gates remain separate cost/human gates.

Video Taste's optional `agentDelegation:{receipt,snapshot}` binds the canonical hash of the full taste-review with agentDelegation omitted, and requires the scope's design area plus human truth/material_rights decisions. Keep approvedBy and approvedAt null; the receipt's agentId and decidedAt identify the AI. Retain every territory, per-territory review and selected-territory criterion. Subjective ratings are the executor's creative judgment, not measured user reactions. These ratings still never prove performance. Explicit user-requested comparisons in other gates retain their user choice semantics.

Fetch the snapshot when making a new decision and checkpoint the corresponding delegated_ai_decision under the current server fence. Validate historical decidedAt within the recorded approval-to-lease interval. Such a past receipt remains valid after a lease refresh; never rewrite its snapshot/hash to make it current. New actions require a fresh current server fence and leaseToken, and the server rejects an expired or stale execution right. A local snapshot cannot prove the server has not changed since it was fetched; final mutations still require current server authorization.

## Final one-shot delivery

Final delivery uses `performance-video-production/scripts/validate-one-shot-review.mjs --require-reviewed --require-final-delivery --final-delivery final-delivery.json` with the existing upstream brief (or explicit standalone-review mode). This is a separate final record, not the earlier territory Taste gate. The [final-delivery schema](../../performance-video-production/assets/schemas/final-delivery.schema.json) binds the final media's assetRef/hash, the unchanged reviewed ACCEPT_PICTURE record, all final technical/creative/audio checks and their observed evidence. CLI validation also reads the final media and hashes its actual bytes. A repair/retry decision or failed check cannot pass this gate.

The final taste is either `{kind:'human_conversation',providedBy,recordedAt,statement}` from an actual user/controller decision, or `{kind:'agent_delegation',agentDelegation:{receipt,snapshot}}`. The agent path requires the approved design area and a performance-video-production step. Its artifactHash is the canonical hash of `{jobId,conceptId,assetSha256,reviewSha256,checks}`. No human fields appear in that branch. Record final observations as executor judgments, not measured audience reactions. Both paths keep publication/activation disabled and require the same QC. Checkpoint/finalize still requires the current server execution right separately from any historical Taste receipt.
