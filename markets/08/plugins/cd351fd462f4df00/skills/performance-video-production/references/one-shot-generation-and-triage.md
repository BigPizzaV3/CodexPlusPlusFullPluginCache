## Goal

Produce the approved campaign idea as one coherent generated film before introducing a multi-tool pipeline. The first Raw is evidence about both the concept and the provider. Review it before modifying it so failures remain attributable.

## Provider selection

Refresh current official capabilities and choose against the brief:

- supported vertical duration and resolution;
- text and Japanese behavior;
- reference-image and character consistency controls;
- ability to sustain several semantic stages inside one clip;
- camera and material quality;
- native audio support and control;
- edit/remix or inpainting support;
- cost per request and retry;
- commercial-use terms and privacy posture.

Do not claim one model is globally best. Record why the selected model fits this brief.

## Provider adaptation

Preserve the campaign idea, chronological semantic-motion arc, must-read copy and display order, identity and brand locks, claim and privacy no-gos, emotional ending, and delegated freedom.

Adapt only formatting, supported parameter syntax, duration wording, reference attachment method, and provider-specific negative prompting. A provider adaptation that changes the false response, Reframe, offer role, or CTA must return to planning.

## Natural-speed Raw review

Review the unmodified file in the target player with sound on and off.

Check in this order:

1. **Opening**: does the intended viewer understand the tension within two seconds?
2. **Causal event**: does each major change have a visible cause and effect?
3. **Semantic arc**: does the film move from Pain through Reframe to offer, or only look impressive?
4. **Offer binding**: does the offer resolve the film's tension rather than appear as an unrelated card?
5. **Identity**: are protagonist count, shape, colors, logo placement, and role stable enough?
6. **Critical copy**: which must-read units are correct, repairable, missing, or harmful?
7. **Typography integration**: do intended keywords belong to the generated world, and would post-added captions improve comprehension without cheapening the picture?
8. **Claims/privacy**: is any unsupported number, fake testimonial, real-looking client data, or guarantee present?
9. **Ending**: is there a stable, legible window for offer and CTA?
10. **Motion/audio**: are cadence, force, speech, and causal sound usable without hiding defects?
11. **Technical**: can the full file decode and fit delivery requirements?

Do not stabilize, retime, subtitle, or add SFX before this review.

## Decision matrix

### ACCEPT_PICTURE

Use when all concept checks pass, critical claims/privacy pass, and no material repair remains. Minor encoding normalization is allowed.

### SURGICAL_FINISH

Use when concept checks pass and all remaining issues are local:

- trim a bad tail;
- replace or add an End Card;
- repair one or two critical text surfaces;
- add exact brand/CTA;
- replace narration;
- align bounded SFX;
- normalize crop, color, loudness, or codec.

If more than roughly one quarter of the visible timeline must be rebuilt, reconsider the decision. This threshold is diagnostic, not a performance rule.

### REGENERATE_SAME_CONCEPT

Use when concept comprehension and prompt-to-concept fit pass, the provider missed a clearly requested event or identity, the failure is plausibly stochastic or addressable by one non-semantic clarification/reference, and the ending or motion is unusable in a way finishing cannot repair.

Name exactly one retry hypothesis. A new paid approval is mandatory.

### RETURN_TO_CONCEPT

Use when the opening does not communicate the Pain, the visual metaphor cannot be mapped to the offer, reading burden is the premise, the Reframe is absent or already answered in the Hook, the offer appears only as branding, fixing the ad would change the Sales Thesis or most of the primary picture, or two attempts repeat the same structural failure.

Return the earliest planning problem and the smallest new decision. Do not prescribe a full replacement concept from production.

## Surgical finish boundary

The generated picture must remain visually dominant. Use trim/stitch, provider edit/remix or inpainting, tracked/corner-pinned exact surfaces, final-frame repair, a native static End Card, captions, audio, and delivery encoding.

Avoid rebuilding cinematic camera motion in Remotion, covering failed action with large overlays, adding generic subtitle wallpaper, adding a second visual concept, using shake or SFX to fake missing physical force, or replacing long stretches while still calling the Raw accepted.

## Retry discipline

Every retry must record:

- previous request ID and Raw hash;
- unchanged concept ID;
- one named execution failure;
- one retry hypothesis;
- prompt/reference delta;
- new request count and maximum cost;
- fresh approval.

After two independent attempts repeat the same structural failure, default to RETURN_TO_CONCEPT or a named controlled route. More retries require a new evidence-based reason.
