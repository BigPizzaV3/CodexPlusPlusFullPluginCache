# Motion quality routing

Use this reference before treating an Animatic or deterministic preview as final picture. Route by the viewer job, not by tool preference.

## Route pressure

Mark whether the scene's primary motion requires:

- spatial depth;
- physical material interaction;
- organic camera motion;
- natural subject motion;
- environmental parallax.

Zero or one requirement usually permits deterministic primary motion when exact typography, UI, charts, proof, or editorial timing is the viewer job. Two or more requirements create `generated_primary_motion` pressure. Default to `directed_generation` when the acceptable choreography is broad and cinematic coherence is the value. Use `controlled_generation` only when a named identity, continuity, path, or endpoint risk needs extra anchors. Use deterministic primary motion only after a natural-speed human Taste override.

This score chooses the primary motion owner; it does not decide every layer. Keep Japanese, UI, logo, CTA, price, claims, proof, and exact timing deterministic even when the motion plate is generated.

## Preview semantics

Label each zero-cost preview as one of:

- `timing_only`: validate causality, duration, hierarchy, and boundaries; never present it as final-picture quality;
- `candidate_final`: the chosen route can plausibly ship if it passes human Taste review;
- `not_required`: no preview is needed because the scene is static or reuses an approved asset.

Do not promote a `timing_only` preview to `candidate_final` merely because technical QC passes.

## Adaptive control recovery

When route pressure is high:

1. preserve the approved Hook, Body, copy, and timing;
2. write one macro direction package and identify what the model may improvise;
3. choose `directed_generation` unless a named risk requires control;
4. add only the minimum anchors that solve that risk;
5. keep critical text out of free-running generated motion;
6. generate the fewest coherent motion units;
7. composite exact surfaces deterministically;
8. judge the Raw and first composition separately at natural speed.

Do not encode provider names, a fixed shot duration, or a permanent model preference into the route decision. Refresh current official capabilities before deciding whether one unit can contain multiple shots, native audio, reference inputs, or editing.
