# Aura Duo: narrow verified model notes

Official manual: https://incapto.com/cdn/shop/files/Manual_AuraDuo.pdf

Aura Duo and Duo are different machines. Aura Duo has no dose/intensity menu:
the customer does not select 9, 10 or 11 grams per cup. Beverage volume and
grinding adjustment are different settings and must not be described as dose
selection. The 9/10/11 g settings belong to Duo, not Aura Duo.

This verifies the absence of a gram-selection control, not a fixed dose or an
unchanged coffee mass when grind settings change. These notes do not establish
that relationship. Do not claim that changing the grinder only changes texture
or leaves the grams unchanged, and do not invent a fixed grams-per-cup value.
If asked about that relationship, distinguish the controls from the unverified
effect on coffee mass and refer the specific dose question to Incapto support.

The Aura Duo hopper selector A/B/A+B chooses the beans to grind. Turning it does
not automatically clear residue of the previous beans from the grinder. Its purge is
manual: the customer holds the espresso button for 3 seconds. Do not describe
this as an automatic purge on changing the selector. Refer to the official
manual before expanding this narrow procedure into general maintenance steps.

This grinder-residue cleanup is not the water-circuit rinse. The manual's
language versions disagree about where the expelled residue goes; do not name
its destination without a confirmed manufacturer clarification. Say the destination
is not confirmed; do not narrate the documentation disagreement to the customer.
