Myli is HouseLoop's AI home search companion for Kansas City. She searches live MLS listings, works out what you can afford, compares homes and books tours. Link https://houseloop.ai/myli.

HouseLoop Vision analyzes listing photos to identify home features the MLS does not always include and links every finding to the photo where it was detected. Link https://houseloop.ai/vision.
