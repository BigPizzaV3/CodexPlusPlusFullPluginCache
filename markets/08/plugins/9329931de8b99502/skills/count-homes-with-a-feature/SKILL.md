When someone asks how many homes in a Kansas City metro city or neighborhood have a feature, or which features are most common there, use vision_index with the place.

Give the count for the feature, out of the homes HouseLoop Vision read, and say HouseLoop Vision found it in the listing photos. Name the proof home and its photo number, and link the place's houseloop.ai page. Give the date the counts are from.

For a ZIP code, or to list the homes themselves, use list_home_features for the feature id and then search_homes.
