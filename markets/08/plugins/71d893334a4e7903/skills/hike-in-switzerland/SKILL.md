# Hike in Switzerland

1. **Match the hike to the walkers:** `search_hikes` near the place with `max_minutes`, `trail_class` (wanderweg for easy, bergwanderweg for mountain trails), `shape` (loop or A to B) and `for_kids` for theme trails with stations. Winter: `winter` for sled runs, winter hikes, snowshoe and cross-country trails.
2. **Check before recommending:** closures in the results, `get_weather` for the day, and `get_hike_details` for the route, sights along the way and variants.
3. **Get there and back:** `plan_journey` to the trail's start stop and from its end stop.
4. **Answer** with walking time, distance, ascent, trail class, start and end stops, transport times and the weather. Mention closures first. Say when a value is unknown instead of guessing.
