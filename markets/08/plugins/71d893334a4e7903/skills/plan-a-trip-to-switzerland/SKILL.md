# Plan a trip to Switzerland

1. **Frame it:** number of days, arrival airport or city, season, travellers (children and their ages), interests (mountains, lakes, cities, hiking, snow) and budget. Ask only for what is missing.
2. **Pick 2–3 bases** close together by train (e.g. Lucerne, Interlaken, Zermatt) instead of moving every night.
3. **Per base, collect options:** `search_activities` for excursions and scenic trains, `search_places` for sights and places with opening hours, `search_hikes` when hiking fits, `get_weather` for the dates within the next 7 days.
4. **Connect the bases** with `plan_journey` (real departures, transfers, platforms) and let `recommend_rail_pass` price the trains of the whole trip — dates, stops with nights, ages and country of residence — to name the pass (or single tickets) that pays off; excursion tickets come on top. For a named attraction, `get_point_of_interest` gives its hours and tickets.
5. **Answer day by day:** base, main activity, alternative for bad weather, travel times, prices from the tools and the booking links. Never invent prices, times or opening hours.
