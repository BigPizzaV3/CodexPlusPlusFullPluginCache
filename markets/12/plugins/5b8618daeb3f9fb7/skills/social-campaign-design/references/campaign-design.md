# Social campaign design

Adapted from Marketing Posters, Ad Creatives, Social Content for Brand Sellers, Knowledge Notes, Social Media Notes, Carousel Maker, Social Post Design, Local Business Visuals, Poster Makeover, Poster Recreation, and Poster Multi-size Expansion in the [Designkit Skills catalog](https://www.designkit.com/skills).

## Choose the deliverable

Identify whether the user wants a single poster, ad variants, an educational carousel, or a connected series of social posts. Preserve their chosen output; a single poster request must not expand into a campaign bundle. Reuse the campaign brief and existing materials before asking anything.

- **Poster or ad:** Capture the key message, exact headline/CTA, audience, requested placement/ratio, and supplied product or brand assets. Ask about missing dates, prices, discount conditions, or event details instead of inventing them. If the user requests a fictional example, label the fictional facts in the brief.
- **Carousel or series:** Capture the topic, approved source material, platform, copy language, number of images, and relationship between cover, content pages, and closing action. The website carousel form starts with six images, but do not impose six on every request. Keep one idea per panel and the requested order.
- **Makeover or recreation:** Use the uploaded original or a previously generated room. Preserve the requested information and assets while changing only the intended layout or style. Clarify an ambiguous source before modifying anything.

Use the user's supplied brand direction. Where the creative brief is already complete, proceed with generation instead of requiring another approval ceremony. Keep any proposed assumptions short and relevant.

## Create and adapt

Read [the MCP workflow](mcp-workflow.md). Submit a single cohesive task with the selected output, count/order, copy, audience, ratio, brand direction, and available references. Delegate professional visual execution to Designkit. If a returned design question needs the user's choice, ask that question rather than duplicating the full briefing process.

For a size variant, carry over the product, key message, and visual identity while allowing the composition to reflow. For revisions to generated work, use the existing room and clearly identify the image or panel to change. Do not create a fresh unrelated task or regenerate every panel merely to reopen a preview.

## Deliver

Show actual previews and original-image links. Report which requested assets are complete and provide the Designkit room link for further work. Publishing/scheduling posts, accessing social account analytics, buying ads, and reporting live trends are separate capabilities: do not claim those actions happened. Do not promise engagement or conversion gains from a design.
