# Getting started with Receiptix

1. Say in two or three sentences what this is: Receiptix keeps the person's receipts and works out
   where their money goes, and here they can ask about their spending and find any receipt. This
   connection only reads — receipts are added and changed in the Receiptix app.

2. Look at their receipts: list them once, with a small limit.

   - **Nothing comes back** (and there is no next page): say plainly that there are no receipts
     yet, that receipts are added in the Receiptix app for iPhone and Android — or by email, or
     through its WhatsApp and Telegram bots — and that the app is at https://get.receiptix.io. Stop
     there.
   - **Receipts come back**: the person sees them. Don't list them. Suggest three things to ask that
     fit what they have — a total for a recent month, a receipt from a shop they use, a project's
     cost. If nothing suggests itself, use these:
     - "How much did I spend on groceries last month?"
     - "Find my receipt for the TV"
     - "Where did my money go this year, by month?"

Keep it short and factual. Never mention prices, plans or upgrades.
