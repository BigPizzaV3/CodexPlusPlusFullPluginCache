# BookGuard age-band rubric (original teen thresholds preserved)

**Audience:** Any reader or family. Ask for age only if not provided, and make the question optional. All genres permitted. Ratings assess content, not literary quality or moral worth.

**Age bands:** Under 8: do not certify suitability without age-specific evidence and context; flag meaningful violence, sexual content, scary themes and strong language for caregiver review. Ages 8–12: more protective than ages 13–15; sexual 3+ or violence 4+ is Not Recommended by default, while sexual 2, violence 3, disturbing 3+, profanity 3+, or any self-harm/assault/abuse is Caution at minimum. Ages **13–15**: original age-13 thresholds below. Ages **16–17**: original age-17 thresholds below. Ages **18+**: content descriptions and preference-based cautions rather than parental age restrictions; prominently flag severe sensitive topics. New outer bands are conservative guidance, not universal developmental rules. Reader sensitivities and caregiver preferences override defaults.

If age is skipped, report evidence-based scores and warnings without an age-specific Appropriate verdict.

## Severity per category (0–5)
0 None evidenced; 1 fleeting/mild; 2 occasional/mild-to-moderate; 3 moderate or recurring (e.g. non-graphic sexual situations, ordinary combat, occasional strong profanity); 4 strong/explicit/graphic/frequent; 5 extreme/persistent/very graphic. Scores require cited support; no evidence => `?`, not 0.

Categories: **sexual content** (romance, nudity, explicitness), **violence** (physical harm and gore), **profanity**, **substances**, **disturbing themes** (trauma/horror/psychological intensity).

## Deterministic thresholds
| Trigger | Ages 13–15 | Ages 16–17 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sexual 3 | Caution | Normally Appropriate unless context changes |
| Sexual 4–5 | **Not Recommended** | **Caution**; parental review of explicitness and context, not automatic rejection |
| Violence 4 | Caution | Usually Appropriate unless other intense details warrant Caution |
| Violence 5 | **Not Recommended** | Caution |
| Profanity 4–5 | Caution | Usually Appropriate unless unusually extreme/contextually severe |
| Disturbing 4 | Caution | Consider context; may be Appropriate |
| Disturbing 5 | Caution | Caution |
| Substances 4–5 | Caution | Usually Appropriate unless other concerns |
| Self-harm or suicide | Caution / parental review | Caution when graphic or intense; otherwise describe and invite review |
| Sexual assault, sexual exploitation, child abuse | **Caution at minimum**, prominently flag for parental review | **Caution at minimum**, prominently flag for parental review |

Reader or caregiver can override any thresholds explicitly. For nuanced triggers, use cautious interpretation and explain evidence and context, never claim a hard-coded numeric label independently verifies the work. **Not Recommended** outweighs **Caution** for known evidence. **Insufficient Evidence** overrides positive certification when minimum evidence absent. Source confidence low/unspecified or any unknown category downgrades would-be Appropriate to Caution or Insufficient Evidence depending on significance. A 'Caution' can mean suitable with discussion, not an outright ban.

## Evidence thresholds
Require at least 3 reliably scored categories and at least one traceable relevant content source. More than one independent source preferable for strong claims; publisher metadata alone insufficient. Flag unresolved sensitive categories even with three known categories. Separate positive evidence from missing evidence. Never claim safety for an unassessed sequel. Source-specific evidence must support the rating it accompanies.
