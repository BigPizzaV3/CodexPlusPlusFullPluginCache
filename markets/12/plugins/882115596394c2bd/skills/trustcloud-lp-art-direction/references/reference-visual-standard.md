# Reference-relative visual standard

## Observed reference craft

The following pages were visually inspected on 2026-09-14. They are benchmarks
for design decisions, not sources of images, claims or copy to reuse.

- [Hinata event list](https://www.hinatahouse.jp/event/): 12 first-page campaign
  artworks use different combinations of vertical/horizontal lettering,
  handwritten headlines, photo strips, architectural cutouts and illustration.
  Quiet residences, family consultations and festivals have different voices.
- [Hinata 7266](https://www.hinatahouse.jp/event/event-7266/): complementary
  daylight/evening pictures with narrow copy rails, finely composed date type,
  numbered feature captions, plan/photo pairs, house data and a personal letter.
- [Daiwa Residence Designs](https://www.daiwahouse.co.jp/jutaku/visit/residence/):
  unequal image scales, precise architectural photography, detail inserts and
  chapter typography carry a visual narrative through the page.
- [SANU Weekday](https://www.sa-nu.com/weekday): preserve the view through the
  photograph; place the vertical idea and practical action in different quiet
  regions. Maps, large numerals and practical details support the experience.
- [Coffee City Festival](https://coffee-city-fes.com/): expressive lettering,
  strong rules, category colors and character imagery form a coherent event
  identity through the story and ticketing. Quiet beige is not universal quality.

Learn the relationship between content and form. Do not imitate a logo, quote,
character or distinctive campaign verbatim. Do not copy raster text, fake buttons
or inaccessible controls merely because a reference uses them.

## Concept before production assets

Apply the Build Web Apps concept-design principle: first design a complete visual
moment, then specify its implementable parts. A photographic asset brief is not
the same as a website concept brief.

For a concept image, specify the surface and viewport, event-specific idea,
evidence roles, focal point, exact copy, typographic relationships, photo sizes,
palette, transition to the next section and real action placement. Use readable
section-sized concepts; a miniature full-page picture cannot prove text quality.
Keep a shared brief for opening, proof, dense information and booking. Do not
generate disconnected styles section by section.

Carry the page art direction and crop needs into each asset brief. Decide
lettering from the asset role and keep interactive controls in the page. Preserve source-backed
property photographs, plans and price tables. Imagined architecture cannot replace
evidence of an actual property. A concept is never silently published as a real
property photograph or used as the entire interactive page.

If a concept requires unsupported multiple-image or typography arrangements,
record the missing capability. Do not quietly simplify it to a generic template
and then claim fidelity. Do not invent unsupported JSON fields or unsafe HTML.

## Acceptance, not a checklist beauty score

Compare actual renders against the relevant reference at matching viewport sizes.
Judge each separately: visual idea; photo quality/editing; typography and image
composition; full-page rhythm; coherent identity; attractive source evidence;
responsive composition/thumbnail; usable real booking.

Record `below-reference`, `reference-level`, `above-reference` or `not-evaluated`
with concrete screenshot evidence for each. Never average away a weak dimension.
"Surpasses" requires none below or unexamined, with demonstrable advantages in
visual idea, photo/type composition and full-page rhythm. This is a comparative
design judgment, not a guarantee of everyone's taste or conversion performance.

Reject generic centered text over a darkened stock image, oversized type without
composition, repeated equal cards, repeated image/text rows, incoherent image
subjects, tiny metadata used to avoid overflow and irrelevant decoration.

Re-typeset mobile instead of shrinking a desktop poster. Review the opening,
transitions, proof, dense facts, booking and actual list thumbnail. For each
iteration, write specific visual mismatches, change the general generation or
renderer behavior, and retain the failed run. Use bounded repairs; never loop
generation indefinitely. A mock, fixture or manually repaired page cannot certify
fresh normal-generation quality.
