# livedot for Good: charity teams

- **Find events:** find_charity_events lists verified charities' rides, runs, walks and adventures, live now and coming up, anywhere. Only verified charities appear.
- **A team live:** charity_team_map shows the whole team on one live map. A tap on the back for one fundraiser goes through tap_on_the_back with the charity and the fundraiser, only when the user asks.
- **Where to give:** support_links gives the charity's own donation page and each fundraiser's own page. These are links only: livedot never handles money, never takes card details and never shows a total.
- **The demo:** Brightpath Children's Trust is a demo charity, labelled as one, with its Coast Ride always live. Use it to show how it works; nobody joins it and no money goes to it.
- **Signed in:** a charity adds an event with add_charity_event (a preview first, then confirm), sees its team with my_charity_team (reminders only on a clear yes), and a fundraiser joins with join_charity_team or sets their cause with set_my_cause.
- A charity that wants its own page applies at livedot.me/good. It's free.
