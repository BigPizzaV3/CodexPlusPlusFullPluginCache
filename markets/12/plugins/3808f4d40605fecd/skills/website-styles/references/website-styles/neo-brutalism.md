## Purpose

Create a bold, direct, and structurally exposed website expression through emphatic hierarchy, visible boundaries, graphic contrast, and unapologetic component relationships without sacrificing clarity, accessibility, or task completion.

This profile is a contemporary digital synthesis. It draws selectively from architectural Brutalism's emphasis on materially legible and expressed construction, Web Brutalism's raw and direct use of the medium, and later graphic interface conventions. No single authoritative definition governs Neo Brutalism, so this profile defines a bounded Alpy interpretation rather than presenting current visual conventions as historical fact. The catalog name does not equate it with architectural New Brutalism or any later architectural Neo-Brutalism.

After the shared chat confirmation, use this profile only when Neo Brutalism was selected under confirmed delegation or preserved from the confirmed restatement. Apply it with relevant Design intent skills and the canonical Alpy package.

This profile describes semantic relationships and selection priorities. It does not define CSS, raw values, named typefaces, fixed colors, components, tokens, modes, breakpoints, imagery, motion, product behavior, or canonical interaction patterns.

## Classification

- **Kind:** Primary direction.
- **Default foundation:** A complete structurally explicit website foundation with forceful but ordered type hierarchy, visibly related semantic planes, purposeful boundaries, deliberate supported layering or displacement, unmistakable controls and states, and quieter functional regions. It requires no second style selection.
- **Best suited for:** Creative studios, cultural and editorial work, campaigns, portfolios, independent brands and products, events, selected commerce, and organizations that benefit from a candid, unconventional, or confrontational identity.
- **Use selectively for:** Broad public services, healthcare, finance, legal or policy content, sensitive subjects, long-form reading, and consequential transactions where visual force may undermine trust, comprehension, or calm task completion.
- **Compatible with:** None declared until adjacent profiles have been researched and cross-style validation confirms a coherent combination.
- **Conflicts with:** None declared until adjacent profiles have been researched and cross-style validation confirms a material conflict. Do not infer either relationship.

Treat Neo Brutalism as a complete preset. Do not require the user to choose an architectural era, raw-web subtype, border weight, shadow recipe, palette, typeface, degree of asymmetry, or second style after it is selected.

Use Neo Brutalism when exposed structure, emphatic hierarchy, direct controls, and graphic tension materially support the confirmed purpose and brand. A request for something bold, edgy, youthful, raw, or unconventional does not establish this direction by itself.

## Identity

### Principle

Expose structure and make hierarchy forceful; do not make usability brutal.

Communicate directly through visible construction, decisive contrast, and obvious relationships. Treat rawness as an expressive quality, not permission to weaken information architecture, interaction conventions, accessibility, content integrity, or finish.

### Lineage and scope

Keep these related ideas distinct:

- **Architectural Brutalism:** A broad, contested architectural lineage associated with expressed construction, material legibility, mass, texture, memorability, and forceful form. It is not simply a concrete appearance or anti-aesthetic functionalism.
- **Web Brutalism:** A varied reaction to polished, template-like websites that often favors direct content, visible web conventions, limited ornament, and an exposed sense of how the page is made.
- **Neo Brutalism:** A contemporary graphic and interface interpretation that commonly combines emphatic typography, visible boundaries, high contrast, direct controls, and deliberate offset or layered relationships.
- **Antidesign:** A strategy that may intentionally destabilize hierarchy, navigation, legibility, or comfort. It is outside this profile.
- **New Ugly:** A heterogeneous postmodern graphic tendency associated with tension, provocation, and unconventional form. It may overlap with web-brutalist discourse but is not a synonym or a requirement.

Do not present Neo Brutalism as a historically standardized movement or claim a direct formal equivalence between concrete architecture and interface styling. Architectural history provides lineage and judgment, not a digital component specification. Do not transfer historical political, social, economic, environmental, or ethical claims to a website.

### Character

Neo Brutalism should feel:

- Bold, blunt, and immediate.
- Raw in expression but resolved in execution.
- Energetic without becoming frantic.
- Confrontational without becoming hostile.
- Graphic without becoming decorative noise.
- Unconventional without becoming confusing.
- Tactile without imitating physical material.
- Direct without losing nuance or trust.

### Anti-goals

Do not turn Neo Brutalism into:

- A fixed dark-outline, bright-background, and offset-shadow formula.
- Architectural concrete, construction, or industrial imagery used as costume.
- Random misalignment, accidental overlap, or a deliberately broken grid.
- Universal uppercase, monospaced typography, or oversized text.
- Every element enclosed by a heavy box.
- Harsh color without semantic purpose.
- Weird cursors, hidden navigation, fake controls, or unfamiliar interaction for novelty.
- Constant animation, flashing, shaking, or aggressive hover behavior.
- Antidesign, intentional ugliness, or deliberate inaccessibility.
- An unfinished interface presented as authenticity.

### Signature traits

Build the direction through:

- A visible structural logic connecting major regions and repeated components.
- Decisive scale, weight, surface, and position relationships.
- Boundaries that clarify grouping, action, state, or hierarchy.
- Simple planes and controlled offset or layered relationships when canonically supported.
- Strong typography used as information architecture rather than spectacle.
- Modular repetition with purposeful changes in priority.
- High visual contrast resolved through semantic roles.
- Obvious interactive controls and unmistakable applicable states.
- Direct language and candid presentation of confirmed content.
- Selective asymmetry or displacement that remains visibly intentional.

The direction must remain recognizable without one palette, dark outlines, square corners, offset shadows, monospaced type, uppercase text, concrete texture, or animation.

## Expression Model

Infer the dominant emphasis internally from the confirmed website purpose, audience, content, layout, and direction. Do not open another subtype-selection loop after Neo Brutalism is selected.

Choose one dominant emphasis for each page family and use the others only where they support the same site-wide logic.

### Structural emphasis

Expose grouping, modules, shared edges, containment, and large-scale relationships. Favor this for information-rich websites, services, catalogs, and products where the direction must remain strong across several page types.

### Graphic emphasis

Use decisive type, shape, contrast, framing, and scale to create memorable focal regions. Favor this for campaigns, studios, events, portfolios, launches, and editorial work.

### Raw-web emphasis

Prioritize direct content, explicit labels, familiar web conventions, economical presentation, and limited ornament. Favor this when speed, candor, publishing, archives, or independent identity matter. Retain the minimum structural signature so this emphasis does not collapse into a separate Web Brutalism direction.

### Controlled expressive emphasis

Use canonically supported offsets, layered planes, unusual scale relationships, and selective asymmetry while preserving conventional task paths. Favor this when the experience needs more energy without becoming antidesign.

### Functional restraint

Reduce visual force around forms, transactions, authentication, account tasks, consent, legal content, recovery, and dense reading. Preserve the direction through strong grouping, direct language, clear hierarchy, and visible component anatomy.

### Website and Product Design boundary

Use this profile for public, evaluative, editorial, campaign, portfolio, documentation, support, commerce, and bounded transactional website surfaces.

When the primary request concerns authenticated workflows, administration, editable data, persistent operational context, professional tools, or dense application shells, use Product Design when available. If Product Design is unavailable, keep the work bounded to confirmed website responsibilities and report the unresolved product-design scope rather than inventing application architecture.

This profile may shape a confirmed website account entry or bounded account page. It does not define permissions, data behavior, persistent workspaces, or product workflow logic.

### Minimum structural signature

Establish all of the following when canonical support permits:

- One memorable, assertive page silhouette created through purposeful mass, repetition, alignment, contrast, or containment.
- One visible modular or alignment logic spanning major regions.
- One decisive relationship of scale, weight, surface, or controlled displacement at a focal moment.
- Repeated boundaries or planes that perform a clear grouping or hierarchy function.
- Canonical components, tokens, surfaces, and behavior presented directly rather than disguised by local styling.
- Strong type-role contrast within the canonical type system.
- Direct and consistently expressed controls and applicable states.

If canonical support cannot express the full signature, use the nearest supported relationships and report material gaps rather than creating local styling.

## Visual Expression

### Hierarchy

Lead with the confirmed subject, task, proposition, or action. Use clear differences in typography role, scale, weight, placement, surface, containment, and spacing to establish order.

Allow one region or message to carry conspicuous visual weight. Counter it with quieter supporting information so the page has force and legibility rather than continuous shouting.

Do not make every heading, action, card, or status equally loud. Do not use overlap, rotation, clipping, or interruption to hide essential meaning.

### Typography character

Use only typefaces, typography roles, complete tuples, and mappings available in `alpy/type-scale.md`. Resolve component-owned typography slots and bindings through `alpy/component-tokens.md`.

Build the character through blunt hierarchy, decisive display roles, compact labels, clear metadata, and strong contrast between primary and supporting text. Preserve readable body, control, helper, and error roles.

Monospaced, condensed, extended, uppercase, or unusually heavy text may support short roles only when available and appropriate. None is required. Do not manipulate tracking, line height, case, weight, or size outside canonical typography definitions.

### Color behavior

Resolve component-owned color through `alpy/component-tokens.md` and remaining intent through `alpy/semantic-tokens.md` and explicit mode mappings.

Use semantic contrast boldly. Distinguish planes, actions, sections, applicable states, and focus through supported role relationships. A restrained base with forceful accents or several clearly separated surfaces can both work when the confirmed direction and canonical mappings support them.

Do not require black, white, yellow, red, or another fixed palette. Do not repurpose functional colors for decoration, confuse decorative contrast with state meaning, or rely on color alone.

### Shape and surface

Favor simple, legible planes and visibly intentional boundaries. Use canonical surfaces, borders, radii, component anatomy, and supported depth relationships to make construction clear.

Square corners and heavy outlines are optional, not defining requirements. Do not override canonical radius or border mappings. Do not add notches, stickers, cutouts, tape, labels, or pseudo-physical attachments unless they are supplied, approved, and supported.

### Depth and offset

Prefer direct layering, supported surface contrast, boundaries, and deliberate plane relationships. When canonical component states or tokens support offset depth, use it consistently and only where it strengthens hierarchy, containment, or affordance.

Do not fabricate shadows, transforms, duplicate layers, or local depth values. A flat implementation can remain fully Neo Brutalist through structure, typography, boundaries, contrast, and scale.

### Texture and material

Use texture only through confirmed assets or canonical capabilities and only when it supports actual content or the confirmed direction. Do not simulate concrete, paper, ink, photocopying, halftone, noise, grain, tape, paint, or print misregistration through local effects.

Translate architectural material honesty into interface clarity and visible construction, not imitation of building surfaces.

### Imagery

Use only confirmed imagery and supported media behavior. Direct photography, documentary detail, isolated objects, hard crops, scans, or candid compositions may support the direction when the assets and purpose justify them.

Keep subjects identifiable and relationships understandable. Preserve captions, provenance, rights, focal points, and alternatives. Do not invent gritty, archival, political, protest, industrial, or underground imagery to manufacture authenticity.

### Iconography

Use only canonical icons and semantic mappings. Keep icon use sparse, direct, and consistent; pair symbols with labels whenever meaning is not self-evident.

Do not create hand-drawn icons, geometric substitutions, arrows, punctuation, emoji, or text glyphs as local replacements.

## Spatial Expression

### Composition

Preserve the composition and structural hierarchy confirmed by [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md) and the perceptual priority confirmed by [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md). Neo Brutalism intensifies relationships within that structure; it does not silently select, replace, or destabilize it.

Use controlled tension only when it preserves semantic sequence, reading order, focus order, and responsive behavior.

### Grid and alignment

Favor visible modular logic, shared edges, decisive spans, and clear changes of scale. The grid may feel exposed through alignment, containment, dividers, repeated modules, or contrasting planes without being drawn decoratively.

Use exact containers, columns, spans, gutters, and responsive behavior through Grid and Layout and canonical Alpy guidance and mappings. Report missing coverage instead of creating a local layout substitute. Do not impose a universal column count, fixed grid, decorative overlay, or arbitrary misalignment.

### Controlled displacement

Use stable alignment as the base. Introduce selective offsets, overlaps, or counterweights only when they clarify priority, create a purposeful focal relationship, and survive adaptation.

Keep headings, content, media, metadata, and actions visibly related. Remove displacement that appears accidental, separates content from meaning, obscures focus, or fails under content change.

### Density and rhythm

Choose density from confirmed content, audience, input conditions, and task. Concentrate visual force at important moments and provide enough negative space for comprehension. Brutality does not require crowding.

Build rhythm through repeated modules, firm intervals, compressed groups, decisive section changes, and occasional larger pauses using mapped spacing roles and component slots. Use spacing to expose structural units rather than add emptiness or crowding for effect.

### Content measure

Keep sustained reading within a comfortable canonical measure. Let short headings, labels, numbers, or calls to action take stronger spatial roles without forcing paragraphs into narrow, fragmented, overlapped, or excessively wide arrangements.

## Component Expression

Select only supported components, anatomy, variants, applicable states, content options, and behavior from `alpy/components.md`. Resolve styling through `alpy/component-tokens.md`; never restyle canonical components locally.

### Navigation

Keep primary destinations obvious, stable, and easy to scan. Use canonical selection and current-location states, strong grouping, and direct labels.

Do not hide navigation behind ambiguity, scatter destinations, replace labels with symbols, or make discovery depend on hover or experimentation.

### Actions and links

Use canonical action hierarchy and plain, specific language. Make buttons unmistakable through supported boundaries, surface contrast, placement, and state behavior. Keep text links visibly identifiable through canonical treatment.

Do not give every action primary emphasis, turn static labels into button-like shapes, or use visual novelty to pressure action.

### Controls and forms

Preserve documented component anatomy, labels, instructions, targets, applicable states, focus, validation, and recovery. Strong boundaries may reinforce control recognition when canonical components support them.

Do not remove labels, rebuild controls as generic boxes, or let decoration resemble inputs, toggles, handles, or selection.

### Containers and cards

Use containers to expose meaningful groups, modules, comparisons, or independent actions. Apply a coherent boundary and surface logic across page families.

Use cards only when items are genuinely repeatable or independently actionable. Do not box every paragraph, create nested outlines without purpose, or apply a unique treatment to every card.

### Data and metadata

Give labels, values, units, dates, identifiers, credits, and status clear canonical roles. Strong grid and boundary relationships may support scanning and comparison when they preserve data meaning.

Do not let graphic contrast alter scale integrity, imply false importance, or make status depend on color, shape, border, or position alone.

### Temporary surfaces

Use canonical dialogs, menus, popovers, tooltips, and other temporary surfaces only for their documented purpose. Preserve documented stacking, focus, dismissal, and background relationships.

Do not invent sticker-like notices, floating panels, custom overlays, or offset layers to make temporary context appear more Neo Brutalist.

## Page And Section Expression

### Header and navigation

Establish immediate identity and orientation through an explicit shell. Use a stable alignment or boundary relationship for identity, navigation, utilities, and current location while preserving canonical responsive behavior.

### Hero and opening region

Lead with one confirmed message, subject, action, or artifact. Use one decisive type, surface, image, scale, or supported displacement relationship and keep supporting copy visibly subordinate.

The opening must remain clear without decoration or motion. Do not default to a full-screen slogan, arbitrary collage, or enormous headline that delays the website's purpose.

### Section rhythm

Alternate forceful focal regions with calmer content and task regions. Reuse boundary, alignment, surface, and type relationships so the website feels intentionally constructed rather than independently styled section by section.

### Content and collection regions

Give each region one clear responsibility. For collections, portfolios, features, and editorial lists, use repeated component relationships, visible metadata, direct labels, and supported priority differences.

Let real content provide variation. Do not randomize spans, colors, offsets, or component anatomy to make every item unique.

### Evidence and trust

Present claims, sources, specifications, testimonials, provenance, pricing, terms, and limitations plainly. Strong visual expression must not exaggerate evidence, hide conditions, or imply credibility through style alone.

Do not invent claims, products, capabilities, metrics, reviews, customers, destinations, or interaction to make the website feel complete.

### Calls to action and footer

Place actions where content establishes their purpose and consequence. Use direct language and canonical hierarchy. Preserve nearby conditions, costs, consent, or supporting detail.

Use the footer as a durable, clearly bounded destination for supporting navigation, contact, resources, and confirmed policy links. Keep the site-wide construction logic at lower intensity.

## State Expression

Represent every applicable, confirmed, and canonically supported state through documented component anatomy, semantic roles, feedback, and behavior. Do not require every possible state on every component.

Keep hover, focus, selected, disabled, loading, empty, success, warning, error, and other confirmed states distinct where they apply. Use supported combinations of label, icon, boundary, surface, contrast, and motion so offset, border weight, color, shape, or movement is never the only state cue.

Keep the canonical focus indicator clearly visible against every border, plane, image, and high-contrast surface. Decorative boxes and shadows must never be mistaken for interaction or state.

Do not invent saved, synchronized, current, available, loading, progress, success, warning, or error truth. State expression may communicate only confirmed product behavior and content.

## Interaction Expression

### Affordance

Prefer familiar, explicit interaction. Links should look like links, buttons like buttons, inputs like inputs, and static surfaces like static surfaces. The direction may increase their visual force but must not redefine their meaning.

Only interactive elements should respond as interactive. Preserve back behavior, scrolling expectations, keyboard access, focus order, and input-method parity.

### [Motion character](../ui-design/motion-transition.md)

Motion is optional. Neo Brutalism must remain complete and recognizable without it.

When supported, use concise movement to reinforce direct manipulation, state change, layer relationship, or transition. Do not invent cursor effects, shaking, flashing, continuous loops, kinetic type, scroll hijacking, or aggressive transitions.

Honor supported reduced-motion preferences and documented applicable requirements. Keep content and state understandable without nonessential motion.

## Adaptation

### Responsive priorities

Preserve purpose, navigation, reading order, focus order, primary actions, component relationships, and state meaning as space changes. Retain structural logic before decorative positioning.

Reflow offset, layered, or asymmetric regions into a clear sequence. Reduce nonessential displacement, framing, and graphic contrast before allowing overlap, clipping, horizontal overflow, or disconnected labels.

Use only supported responsive behavior. Report missing layout coverage instead of creating custom breakpoints or local substitutes.

### Mode behavior

Resolve an explicit `light` or `dark` mode through canonical semantic mappings and component bindings. Preserve contrast, hierarchy, boundaries, focus, applicable states, and recognizable plane relationships in every supported mode.

Do not equate Neo Brutalism with light mode, dark mode, a bright background, or a black-and-white palette. Do not infer one mode from another.

### Content resilience

Review long and translated text, right-to-left reading, multiple scripts, real names, variable metadata, missing media, forms, errors, zoom, reflow, user spacing, keyboard access, reduced motion, and content extremes.

Let regions, boundaries, offsets, and type roles adapt. Do not truncate essential language, preserve dramatic overlap over readability, or force variable content into fixed graphic arrangements.

## Boundaries

### Must preserve

- Confirmed purpose, audience, content, hierarchy, task, and action priority.
- Strong but coherent relationships among structure, type, surfaces, boundaries, imagery, and behavior.
- Direct navigation, clear affordances, visible applicable states, and recoverable interaction.
- Semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, logical reading order, and usable targets.
- Canonical components, tokens, mappings, values, modes, applicable states, and behavior.
- Confirmed direction, User Experience, Grid and Layout, Visual Hierarchy, Balance, and Gestalt.

### Accessibility risks

Review for:

- Low contrast hidden inside a nominally high-contrast palette.
- Functional boundaries or focus indicators lost among decorative outlines.
- Meaning conveyed by color, border, offset, position, shape, or motion alone.
- Oversized, condensed, uppercase, monospaced, or display typography used beyond readable conditions.
- Asymmetric visual order that conflicts with semantic reading and focus order.
- Overlap, clipping, or fixed graphic relationships that fail under zoom, reflow, or localization.
- Static surfaces that look actionable or interactive elements that look decorative.
- Dense blocks, repeated boxes, or competing focal points that impair scanning.
- Motion, flashes, abrupt changes, or cursor behavior that distracts or disorients.
- Rawness used to excuse unclear language, missing states, broken navigation, or unfinished behavior.

User Experience identifies journey and inclusion risks. Accessibility Audit verifies implementation when that skill is available. Otherwise report required verification and untested risks without claiming that an audit occurred.

Canonical support, generated code, component names, visual inspection, keyboard checks, reflow checks, or assistive-technology checks do not independently establish accessibility conformance. Make claims only in proportion to documented requirements and actual evidence.

### Avoid

- Hardcoded CSS or raw design values.
- New colors, fonts, icons, imagery, illustrations, assets, shapes, effects, textures, tokens, components, variants, states, modes, motion, or breakpoints.
- Invented content, destinations, product capabilities, controls, data, or interaction added to make the website feel Neo Brutalist.
- A mandatory dark-outline, bright-fill, and offset-shadow recipe.
- Decorative use of functional color or state styling.
- Broken grids, arbitrary offsets, overlapping essential content, and accidental alignment.
- All-uppercase, monospaced, oversized, or aggressively compressed typography as a universal rule.
- Concrete, industrial, underground, protest, punk, photocopy, or print-distress shorthand without confirmed relevance.
- Fake imperfection, deliberately poor usability, or unfinished output presented as authenticity.
- Unstyled or browser-native output presented as inherently more honest when it abandons canonical Alpy expression or weakens usability.
- Antidesign techniques that obscure hierarchy, navigation, content, controls, or state.
- Exposed code, debug data, implementation labels, technical internals, or security-sensitive structure presented as material honesty.
- Unnecessary visual or asset weight, unsupported performance claims, or removal of necessary content and accessibility support to appear direct.
- Invented cultural, political, historical, ethical, authenticity, or product claims.

### Distinction from adjacent directions

- **Bauhaus:** Bauhaus joins function, construction, disciplines, and experiment through a historically grounded system. Neo Brutalism favors rawness, conspicuous boundaries, and more confrontational graphic weight.
- **Flat Design:** Flat Design reduces representation and ornamental depth. Neo Brutalism makes structure more visibly forceful and may use supported outlines, layering, or offset depth.
- **Bold Typography:** Bold Typography is a treatment centered on type-led hierarchy. Neo Brutalism is a complete direction governing structure, surfaces, components, and state as well as type.
- **Antidesign:** Antidesign may intentionally destabilize convention or comprehension. Neo Brutalism must preserve clear hierarchy, navigation, affordance, and task completion.

Do not infer compatibility or combine profiles until the registered catalog or selected profiles explicitly establishes that relationship.

## Success Criteria

The result should:

- Feel Neo Brutalist through visible structure, decisive hierarchy, direct controls, strong boundaries, and graphic confidence rather than a fixed visual recipe.
- Present one dominant focus with clearly ordered supporting content.
- Use contrast, containment, scale, and supported displacement to clarify relationships rather than create noise.
- Balance memorable expression with conventional, accessible task completion.
- Make pages, sections, components, applicable states, and responsive behavior feel part of one coherent construction.
- Derive recognizability from systemic relationships rather than a particular palette, typeface, outline, corner treatment, offset shadow, texture, or motion effect.
- Adapt safely across supported modes, viewport conditions, input methods, zoom, reflow, localization, reduced motion, and content extremes.
- Use only canonical Alpy components, tokens, roles, mappings, values, approved assets, applicable states, and behavior.

If the direction appears Neo Brutalist only because it uses thick outlines, bright blocks, oversized text, square corners, or offset shadows, it has not been applied deeply enough.

If novelty weakens navigation, hierarchy, legibility, affordance, trust, or task completion, the direction has failed.
