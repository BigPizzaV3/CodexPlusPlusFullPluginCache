# Grunge / Punk

## Mandatory Brief Flow

When the user selects Grunge / Punk, present every option in **Character Selection** and every material in **Textures and Material Sources**, with their concise selection cues. Do not filter, shorten, preselect, or silently combine the lists. Ask the user to choose what they want before composing the design.

Before building a homepage or landing page, read [Hero](../hero.md) and reproduce the complete Markdown table under **Choose the Structure** in the user-facing question, including both headers and every row with its full `Choose it when` guidance. Do not shorten, paraphrase, flatten it into a list, rename, filter, merge, preselect, or omit anything. Ask the user to choose exactly one structure or delegate the choice.

## Purpose

Make the design feel printed, handled, assembled, weathered, or imperfect while the underlying website remains clear and dependable. Grunge changes material, display typography, imagery, edges, and composition; it does not damage canonical components or product behavior.

A recognizable Grunge direction needs one coherent material or reproduction character. It does not require dirt on every surface, distressed body text, or a page-wide texture overlay.

## Character Selection

Choose one character from the actual brand, content, and audience. Do not mix several Grunge characters merely to make the page feel more expressive.

| Context and intended character | Select |
|---|---|
| Precise interface with restrained physical wear | Clean / Controlled Grunge |
| Music, fashion, events, activism, or independent publishing | Punk / Zine Grunge |
| Art, archives, craft, storytelling, or assembled evidence | Analog Collage Grunge |
| Automotive, logistics, architecture, tools, or physical production | Industrial / Distressed Grunge |
| Dark cultural, campaign, music, or photographic direction | Dark / Photocopy Grunge |
| Experimental digital media or intentionally damaged signal | Digital / Glitch Grunge |
| Editorial, portfolio, hospitality, or lifestyle with quiet tactility | Soft / Editorial Grunge |

## Style Characters

| Character | Practical visual guidance |
|---|---|
| **Clean / Controlled** | Start with a disciplined composition, then add one rough-framed focal image, restrained abrasion, and one repeated worn edge or rule. Keep task regions calm. |
| **Punk / Zine** | Use urgent live display type, one forceful cutout, high-contrast reproduction, cropped editorial fragments, and one cut, tape, staple, or registration language. Avoid generic conversion cards and invented underground claims. |
| **Analog Collage** | Build from one material backplate, one dominant cutout, two to four meaningful fragments, shallow overlap, and one fastening cue. Every fragment must carry content, evidence, origin, or story. |
| **Industrial / Distressed** | Pair structural display type with one product, vehicle, building, or process subject. Use scraped material fields, hard divisions, scored seams, serial marks, and measured crop lines without fake warnings or specifications. |
| **Dark / Photocopy** | Use a confirmed dark mode, one high-threshold subject, visible toner dropout or abrasion, oversized light type, and one sparse accent. Keep supporting copy cleaner than the reproduction field. |
| **Digital / Glitch** | Keep the live proposition intact beside one bounded raster tear, scan offset, compression, dithering, or registration treatment. Controls remain stable; avoid constant jitter, unsafe flicker, and generic neon effects. |
| **Soft / Editorial** | Combine spacious hierarchy with one assertive rough crop or display field, subtle material tactility, and sparse annotation. Use a repeated dry rule, frayed edge, crop bracket, or abraded frame rather than a generic beige moodboard. |

## Textures and Material Sources

Use a confirmed project scan or photograph first. When no suitable asset exists, use the following as reference sources for a material-matched local asset. Confirm the source page, current license, resolution, and attribution before implementation.

| Ink / Paint | [Peeling paint texture](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grunge_texture.jpg) — Georges Grondin, public domain |
| Concrete / Plaster | [Cracked concrete texture](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grey_speckled_moderately_cracked_grubby_grungy_seamless_concrete_building_wall_texture.jpg) — Sisters.seamless, CC0 1.0 |
| Metal / Oxidation | [Red rust texture](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RED_RUST_TEXTURE.jpg) — 1subwoofer, CC0 1.0 |
| Fabric / Stitch | [Wrinkled cotton texture](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Free_grungy_old_wrinkled_cotton_sheets_texture_for_layers_%283041538208%29.jpg) — D. Sharon Pruitt, CC BY 2.0 |

- Store an optimized local copy when implementation is requested; never hotlink the source.
- Record the creator, source page, license, material changes, and required attribution. Preserve CC BY credit in the shipped project.
- Match preparation to the material. Use crop, levels, threshold, inversion, color separation, or channel treatment to reveal physical marks before lowering opacity.
- Use varied crops or intensity on focal surfaces, media, seams, and transitions. Do not spread one equal-opacity texture across the page.
- Keep strong dirt away from sustained reading, prices, forms, controls, and status. CSS noise, grain, halftone, or filters may support a real source but must not replace it.

## Typography Recommendations

Use canonical typography by default. The following faces are exploration sources for a user-confirmed Option 2 headline typeface only; Grunge does not authorize a new family automatically.

| Character | Headline direction to explore |
|---|---|
| Clean / Controlled | [Rubik Distressed](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rubikdistressed) — restrained worn display |
| Punk / Zine | [Rubik Dirt](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rubikdirt) — heavy eroded block display |
| Analog Collage | [Special Elite](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/apache/specialelite) — worn typewriter display |
| Industrial / Distressed | [Stardos Stencil](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/stardosstencil) — structural stencil display |
| Dark / Photocopy | [Rubik Burned](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rubikburned) — abraded high-contrast display |
| Digital / Glitch | [Rubik Glitch](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rubikglitch) — damaged-signal display |
| Soft / Editorial | [IM Fell English](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfellenglish) — irregular editorial serif |

Keep the optional face in short display roles. Body copy, navigation, forms, controls, data, and sustained reading remain canonical. Test the final headline at wide and narrow widths without texture; reject a face that needs distress effects to become distinctive or readable.

## Composition

| Section need | Practical construction |
|---|---|
| Strong opening statement | One live display statement, one processed subject or rough crop, and one material field |
| Editorial story or evidence | One dominant cutout with two to four relevant fragments and shallow overlap |
| Services, projects, or categories | A ruled or rough-framed index with oversized names instead of unrelated cards |
| Product, venue, vehicle, or process | One clear subject crossing a single material boundary with specifications kept clean |
| Proof, metadata, or credits | A small repeated set of stamps, crop marks, serials, captions, or registration cues |
| Section transition | One torn, frayed, painted, scraped, misfed, or toner-dropped seam from the selected material family |
| Closing action | A clear canonical action attached to one final display statement, away from heavy texture |

Build each focal region from one dominant display or media element, one material field, and no more than two supporting fragments. Use no more than two overlap layers around essential media. Reuse the same material family, edge language, reproduction treatment, and small mark set throughout the page.

Compose with real content and approved assets. Do not add fake ephemera, warnings, serials, specifications, cultural claims, people, products, proof, or history merely to make the collage convincing.

## Mobile and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md)

On narrow screens, remove secondary fragments and overlaps first. Re-crop the material and focal media, preserve complete words and subjects, and retain one clear material cue and edge treatment. Do not preserve a desktop collage through clipping or horizontal overflow.

Motion is optional. Complete the static composition first, then use at most one restrained behavior such as a parallax material layer, registration shift, cutout reveal, or traveling credit strip. Remove parallax, glitch, and expensive effects for reduced motion while keeping the final content and Grunge character visible. Never use constant jitter, flicker, distortion, or motion as state feedback.

## Focused Visual Check

Inspect the rendered opening, one quiet section, the closing action, and a narrow layout at normal zoom.

- The selected character is recognizable without becoming a mixture of Grunge styles.
- Texture or reproduction is visibly material-specific, bounded, and more than a faint tint or repeating overlay.
- Display type is distinctive and readable; body and interface text remain clean.
- Media subjects, evidence, actions, controls, focus, status, and sustained reading remain clear.
- Quiet sections retain the same material, edge, or mark language without equal distress everywhere.
- Narrow and reduced-motion states preserve the character after decorative layers are removed.
- Removing the strongest optional effect still leaves a complete, usable composition.
