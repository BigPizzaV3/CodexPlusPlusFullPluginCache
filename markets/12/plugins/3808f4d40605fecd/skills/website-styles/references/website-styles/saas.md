## Creative Purpose

Turn invisible software into a recognizable visual world by treating one real product or domain artifact as the raw material for the page.

Repeat the selected method in the opening, middle, and close. A dashboard beneath a gradient is not a SaaS art direction.

## Method Specifications

Apply every field under the selected variant to the confirmed page. Do not add, remove, or reorder sections to satisfy the method.

### Product-led SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Stage the product interface as the brand image: one complete state, one focused crop, and one resolved state from the same frame family. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) to keep the proposition distinct from product labels. Reject dashboard wallpaper, disconnected floating panels, generic 3D objects, and decorative loops unrelated to product behavior.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Derive visual texture from approved product-native material such as a cursor path, selection field, timeline, node, canvas, or output. Carry it from large background gesture to small framing detail; resolve [canvas and accent color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [boundaries](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners.
- **Composition:** Inside the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), let one readable product state dominate and attach focused details to that same scene. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md), and [Scale and Proportion](../ui-design/scale-proportion.md) to form one composition rather than a pile of equal cards.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence a wide product tableau → open release → focused crop pair → larger state change → closing echo. Change crop, scale, and emphasis while preserving the frame language.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Reveal the product frame first, then one finite state change and its attached details. Hold on the useful result; the method must remain complete without motion.

### Enterprise Suite SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Build a system atlas: one parent visual language holds a family of real modules, roles, or platform views. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) to distinguish platform, module, and detail without local type roles. Reject separate art direction for every module, equal-weight dashboard mosaics, and generic corporate imagery.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Use a quiet structural field for the suite and reserve expressive domain media, illustration, or dimensional scenes for major chapter openings. Coordinate [surface color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [module boundaries](../ui-design/border-divider.md), [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md), and overall [visual weight](../ui-design/balance.md) through their owners.
- **Composition:** Make the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md) read as platform overview first and module family second. Align related modules inside one shared frame using [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Alignment](../ui-design/alignment.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), and [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md).
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence platform overview → quiet field → module cluster → connected system view → calm close. Repeat the module grammar; use one larger domain scene to punctuate it.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Assemble the platform overview before revealing module detail, or trace one real cross-suite relationship. Use novelty only at selected chapter changes and settle each view before the next.

### AI SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Build a transformation field around one approved input → transformation → output relationship. Repeat one product-native primitive across identity, diagram, interface, and output scales; use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) for prompts, labels, and generated material. Reject anonymous chat boxes, sparkles, neural meshes, floating orbs, and unexplained dimensional spectacle.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Create tension between rational product structure and the tactile, organic, or procedural character of the real transformation. Resolve [canvas and accents](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [edges](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners; AI implies no dark, purple, glass, glow, or 3D default.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), keep source, transformation, and output in one readable path. Move the same primitive between micro and macro scale through [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md), and [Scale and Proportion](../ui-design/scale-proportion.md).
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence concentrated transformation → quiet resolved output → interface close-up → expanded output field → still close. Alternate metaphor with real product material so the page never drifts into generic AI atmosphere.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Transform source into output only when the motion mirrors the real workflow. Show stable endpoints, expose meaningful intermediate states, and remove ambient computation loops from the static result.

### Fintech SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Treat one card, balance, transaction, invoice, portfolio, statement, or payment route as a recurring precision instrument. Use [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md) for monetary rank and [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) for numeral and text-role treatment. Reject fabricated balances, decorative cards, generic stock charts, crypto spectacle, and a blue or dark category default.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Combine measured tactility for physical instruments with a stable structural field for financial infrastructure. Resolve semantic [state color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), exact [rules and boundaries](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and localized [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), let one financial artifact dominate and make every supporting crop complete the same operation. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Alignment](../ui-design/alignment.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), and [Scale and Proportion](../ui-design/scale-proportion.md) to align amounts, dates, units, statuses, and route stages meaningfully.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence instrument → operation → resulting state → focused detail → return to the anchor. Alternate open editorial fields with contained financial vignettes instead of repeating dashboards.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Animate one finite transfer, route, card state, or balance change and stop on the useful result. Avoid rolling numbers, autonomous charts, or any motion that makes the financial state feel unstable.

### Developer Tools SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Present the product as an executable specimen using valid code, terminal, request and response, diff, graph, architecture, or playground material. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) to coordinate prose, code, and technical labels. Reject fake code, terminal cosplay, full-page IDE styling, Matrix motifs, and illegible technical wallpaper.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Let authentic syntax, output, and product chrome supply the technical texture while the page remains a website. Resolve [syntax and status color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [pane boundaries](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [layer depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners; dark mode is not the method.
- **Composition:** Pair cause and effect in the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md): command beside result, request beside response, or code beside product behavior. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Alignment](../ui-design/alignment.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), and [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md); expose guides only when they carry real structure.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence command/result split → open interval → request/response pair → system map → focused playground close. Change the technical artifact rather than repeating one card pattern.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Run command, progress, and result as one finite viewport sequence, then hold the output. Highlight only the affected path; never fake rapid typing or loop a process without resolution.

### Productivity & Collaboration SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Make one shared document, board, task, file, canvas, or thread the main visual plane, with participation attached to it. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) for artifact content, names, comments, and labels. Reject empty whiteboards, cursor swarms, unattached avatars, generic team photography, and constant celebration.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Build a calm working field punctuated by coordinated energy when people act. Use product-native cursors, selections, comments, reactions, or presence marks as atmosphere; resolve their [color relationships](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [containment](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), keep the shared artifact primary and tether every participant trace to its exact subject. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md), and [Balance](../ui-design/balance.md) to show one collective state rather than overlapping screenshot piles.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence shared overview → focused contribution → visible change → completed collective state → presence echo. Vary the artifact while preserving the artifact-plus-participation grammar.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Show one participant selecting, moving, editing, commenting, or approving, then settle on the shared result. Remove ambient traces before the focal relationship and never turn co-presence into perpetual motion.

### Workflow & Automation SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Make one trigger → steps or branches → result relationship the core graphic. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) for every node, port, status, and output label. Reject decorative wires, lightning shorthand, integration-logo wallpaper, fabricated steps, and flows without outcomes.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Create controlled momentum inside a stable modular system using real nodes, connectors, ports, and execution states. Resolve [state color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [node and connector boundaries](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [layer depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners; the process should feel executable, not magical.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), give one route priority and make every connector terminate at a real node. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md), and [Alignment](../ui-design/alignment.md) to subordinate branches and keep source and result visible.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence trigger and outcome → meaningful middle steps → branch detail → completed state → stripped closing path. Change the workflow content, not the diagram grammar.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Trace the actual execution order, activating only affected nodes, then stop on the result or status. Never animate every branch simultaneously or keep the graph alive after completion.

### Data & Analytics SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Treat one real, labeled visualization as the primary image and let its chart grammar organize surrounding details. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) for numerals, units, axes, legends, and annotations. Reject metric-card soup, fake data, unlabeled axes, three-dimensional charts, arbitrary multicolor display, and hover-only meaning.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Build a quiet analytical field from plot structure, gridlines, axes, annotations, and selective emphasis around the insight. Resolve [data color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [rules and containment](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners rather than assuming an analytics aesthetic.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), let one visualization lead and attach supporting values to their true relationships. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Alignment](../ui-design/alignment.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), and [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md) to group with space before adding containers.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence context → initial pattern → comparison or anomaly → focused annotation → stable conclusion. Alternate one expansive plot with quieter detail rather than repeating KPI panels.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Establish axes and context, reveal the relevant series, then land on the annotation or comparison. Hold the final chart as a complete static composition; keep simple data static.

### Marketing & Commerce SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Pair one real customer-facing output—storefront, campaign, email, message, review, advertisement, or product presentation—with the operator software that produced or measured it. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) across customer and operator planes. Reject dashboard-only openings, device piles, channel-logo clouds, fabricated revenue, and uncontrolled customer-brand collage.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Let approved customer creative supply energy while one coherent brand stage contains the variety. Resolve [canvas and accent relationships](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [frames](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners; do not recolor customer work to manufacture unity.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), keep output and operator state distinct but visibly paired: message beside campaign, storefront beside editor, or creative beside measurement. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md), and [Scale and Proportion](../ui-design/scale-proportion.md) to make one plane lead.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence complete output/operator pair → operator action → customer-facing result → lifecycle composite → closing output. Finish one channel relationship before introducing another.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Animate one finite lifecycle such as event → message → response or creative → channel → measured result, then leave both output and operator state inspectable.

### Security SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Art-direct for informed control, not threat theatre. Use one real architecture, topology, telemetry, or detect → contain → resolve relationship, with [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) governing technical labels. Reject code rain, glitch, hacker figures, locks, shields, endless scanning, alarm loops, and several competing threats.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Place one salient anomaly inside an otherwise stable field and return the field to calm after resolution. Resolve [alert-state color](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [system boundaries](../ui-design/border-divider.md), [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md), [perceptual priority](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), and [visual weight](../ui-design/balance.md) through their owners; security implies no dark or blue default.
- **Composition:** Build the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md) as one finite incident tableau or clear architecture map. Use [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Gestalt](../ui-design/gestalt.md), and [Alignment](../ui-design/alignment.md) to show protected subject, focal anomaly, and resolved state.
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence stable system overview → localized anomaly → response detail → calm resolved field → stable close. Do not sustain high visual alarm across the page.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Let the signal appear, show containment, and settle on the secure state. Motion may focus the anomaly but must stop when resolution is visible.

### Creator SaaS

- **Art Direction:** Make the approved work or creator the focal image and let product UI demonstrate one authentic creative act. Use [Typography and Readability](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) to frame the work without overpowering it. Reject stock “creative people,” fabricated portfolios, unrelated template mosaics, product-chrome dominance, and generic 3D spectacle.
- **Design the Atmosphere:** Derive the visual stage from the actual medium: cinematic framing for film, temporal waveform language for audio, or canvas behavior for design. Resolve [canvas relationships](../ui-design/color-discipline.md), [media edges](../ui-design/border-divider.md), and [depth](../ui-design/elevation.md) through their owners; keep varied work authentic rather than recolored.
- **Composition:** In the confirmed [Hero](../hero.md), keep the work largest and product chrome supporting. Show only enough editor, timeline, waveform, crop, comment, or export state to establish the software's role, using [Grid and Layout](../ui-design/grid-layout.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md), [Balance](../ui-design/balance.md), and [Scale and Proportion](../ui-design/scale-proportion.md).
- **Page Rhythm:** Through [Rhythm and Space](../ui-design/rhythm-space.md), sequence finished work → quiet tool crop → creative process → output gallery → final full work. Alternate immersive media fields with restrained product detail so the page feels curated, not mosaicked.
- **Viewport Reveal and [Motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md):** Demonstrate one edit, scrub, crop, arrangement, comment, export, or publish action and settle on inspectable work. The resting frame must preserve the finished work and the same creative relationship.

## Responsive Behavior

Apply [Responsive Adaptation](../responsive-adaptation.md) to recompose the selected visual method rather than shrink its desktop arrangement.

- Preserve one dominant artifact and its primary relationship. [Responsive Adaptation](../responsive-adaptation.md) uses priority from [Visual Hierarchy](../ui-design/visual-hierarchy.md) and size relationships from [Scale and Proportion](../ui-design/scale-proportion.md), removing secondary atmosphere before the focal artifact.
- Convert multi-plane compositions into an ordered pair or sequence: preserve the main route in flows, the key comparison in charts, the cause/result pair in developer artifacts, and output/operator pairing in commerce.
- Use an approved responsive product state or media crop. Keep [Canonical Components](../canonical/components.md), essential labels, and [readable typography](../ui-design/typography-readability.md) intact.
- Reduce overlaps, decorative motifs, secondary modules, traces, and ambient effects before changing the selected method.
- Preserve the complete static signature under [reduced motion](../ui-design/motion-transition.md), then verify the implemented result through [Accessibility Audit](../accessibility-audit.md).
