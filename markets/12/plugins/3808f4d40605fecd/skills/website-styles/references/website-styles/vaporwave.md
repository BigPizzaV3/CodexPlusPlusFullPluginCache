## Purpose

Create a dreamlike, internet-native website that makes commercial, technological, architectural, and popular-cultural memory feel familiar, artificial, distant, and newly strange.

Vaporwave is an internet-cultural primary direction, not a gradient palette, nostalgia pack, retro-futurist theme, or imitation operating system. Its identity comes from temporal displacement, recontextualized media, synthetic memory, idealized environments, and unresolved tension between attraction and estrangement within a conventional accessible website.

After the shared chat confirmation, use this profile only when Vaporwave was selected under confirmed delegation or preserved from the confirmed restatement. Apply it with the relevant Design intent skills and canonical Alpy package.

This profile defines semantic relationships and selection priorities. It does not define CSS, raw values, named typefaces, fixed colors, components, tokens, modes, breakpoints, assets, effects, cultural meaning, political position, or canonical interaction behavior.

## Identity

### Principle

Reframe familiar media so remembered promises feel desirable, artificial, distant, and open to reflection.

### Character

Vaporwave should feel:

- Nostalgic without claiming an accurate past.
- Dreamlike without losing orientation.
- Synthetic without becoming generic futurism.
- Attractive without requiring celebration.
- Uncanny without becoming horror.
- Ironic or critical only when confirmed by the project.
- Melancholic without becoming uniformly bleak.
- Layered without hiding interaction.
- Internet-native without reproducing obsolete usability.
- Ambiguous in cultural meaning and precise in function.

### Signature traits

Build the treatment through:

- A deliberate collision between different moments, media, environments, or cultural registers.
- Familiar commercial, technological, architectural, or popular-cultural material recontextualized through approved assets.
- One synthetic-memory relationship, mediated scene, non-place, or impossible recollection.
- Controlled repetition, isolation, cropping, scale shift, layering, sequencing, or interruption that changes how familiar material is understood.
- Tension between an idealized promise and distance, absence, obsolescence, artificiality, or excess.
- A stable canonical interface beneath atmospheric or collage-like expression.
- Clear provenance and truthful labeling for historical, commercial, cultural, altered, reconstructed, or generated material.
- Quiet functional regions that preserve orientation and allow the atmosphere to remain distinctive.

The result must remain recognizably Vaporwave without pink, purple, cyan, gradients, sunsets, horizon grids, palms, water, classical sculpture, Japanese text, anime, early-web interfaces, compact discs, obsolete devices, chrome, checkerboards, glitch, VHS damage, three-dimensional objects, or audio.

### Anti-goals

Do not reduce Vaporwave to:

- A neon or pastel gradient.
- A sunset, wireframe horizon, palm tree, statue, checkerboard, or floating-object composition.
- Random Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Greek, or Latin text used as texture.
- Anime, classical sculpture, obsolete software, old advertisements, and electronics assembled without coherent meaning.
- Glitch, scanlines, color separation, grain, compression, or VHS damage applied across the interface.
- An imitation browser, operating system, media player, shopping mall, or computer interface.
- Generic nostalgia for an era or culture the project has not established.
- Automatic claims of irony, anti-capitalism, melancholy, utopia, dystopia, or cultural critique.
- A mood board that obscures content, actions, evidence, or states.
- Unlicensed corporate marks, advertisements, characters, artworks, interfaces, recordings, or media samples.
