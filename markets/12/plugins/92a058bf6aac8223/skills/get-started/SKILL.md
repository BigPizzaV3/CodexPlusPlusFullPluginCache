# Getting started with Dibsly

Dibsly is a personal finance app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web. Once connected, ChatGPT reads the person's own Dibsly
account and can change parts of it with their confirmation.

Good first questions:

- "Coffee 4.50" — records an expense
- "How much have I spent this month compared with my plan?"
- "What payments are coming up?"
- "Show my portfolio and dividends."
- "How much tax do I owe on this year's dividends?"
- "How close am I to my savings goals?"
- "Who owes me money, and what is my next loan payment?"

Anything the app does can be done here too, after the person says yes: recording expenses, income and transfers, the
drafts from Apple Pay and bank notifications, editing or deleting operations, transfers and broker trades, importing a bank
statement, the month's plan, calendar payments (paid, skipped, moved, paused), goals, categories and accounts (new, renamed,
merged, deleted, ordered), brands' categories, debts and split bills, credits and their payments, currencies and settings, and
the sole proprietor's tax marks. Each change is shown first and saved only after the person says yes.
Each one is kept in the AI journal on the Dibsly website (my.dibslyapp.com → History → AI journal), where it can be
undone. Dibsly never moves money.
