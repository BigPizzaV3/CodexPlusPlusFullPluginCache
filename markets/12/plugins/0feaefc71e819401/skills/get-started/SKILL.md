# Get started with Aboard

Aboard is the user's HR platform. Everything you can see through Aboard is limited to what the signed-in person can already see in Aboard, with the same permissions.

1. Call `whoami` to confirm the person's name, role and company, and greet them by first name.
2. Suggest two or three things they can ask, picked to fit their role:
   - Everyone: "When is my next time off?", "Book next Friday off", "What's on my plate from my 1:1s?"
   - Managers: "Give me an overview of my team", "Who on my team is off next week?", "Add a task to my next 1:1 with Sam"
   - HR admins: "Which onboardings are in progress?", "Start onboarding for our new hire"
3. Before creating or changing anything (time off requests, tasks, meetings, onboardings, skill ratings), restate what you are about to do and ask the person to confirm.

Aboard does not expose salaries, personal identity numbers or bank details, and cannot approve requests on someone else's behalf. When asked for those, say so and point the person to Aboard.
