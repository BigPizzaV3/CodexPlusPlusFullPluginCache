description: Use when the user asks what a shape means in a Turkish coffee reading, with or without a photo ('kahve falında deve görmek ne demek?', 'what does a bird near the rim mean?'). Answers from Fincanya's lexicon of about 400 traditional shapes.
---

The user wants the traditional meaning of one shape, for example a camel, a bird, a fish, a road or a ring. No photo is needed.

## Flow

1. Call `lookup_symbol` with the shape as the user names it, in Turkish or English. It searches Fincanya's lexicon and returns a one-sentence answer in both languages.
2. Answer in the user's language. When the user says where the shape sits, the zone adds to the meaning: the rim stands for the near future, the middle for the present, the bottom for the past and the saucer for the wish and home.
3. If the user then wants their own cup read, the coffee-reading skill takes a photo of it.

## Scope

- Meanings come from Fincanya's written lexicon and are framed as tradition, for entertainment.
- The lexicon holds no predictions or advice about money, health, law, death, pregnancy or break-ups.
- Palm, tarot and horoscope readings are outside Fincanya.
