# Consultar el Congreso con Es la hora de la verdad

Use the tools of this plugin when the user asks about votes in the plenary of Spain's Congress of Deputies (legislatures XIV and XV, since February 2020), how a parliamentary group or a deputy voted, the deputies of a constituency, or the official calendar, manifestos and candidacies of the 29 November 2026 general election. The data is in Spanish.

## Which tool

- A topic, a law or a date: `buscar_votaciones`, then `ver_votacion` for the vote the user means.
- A deputy: `buscar_diputados`, then `ver_diputado`; for their vote in one specific vote, `voto_de_diputado`.
- A province or constituency: `ver_provincia`.
- The 29 November 2026 election (deadlines, postal vote, seats per province, candidacies): `ver_elecciones_29n`.
- What the party manifestos say on a topic: `ver_programas_29n`.

## How to answer

- Include at least one link to the site: the `url` of each piece of data you use. Every tool response carries one, error responses included.
- Cite the source as the `atribucion` field says: the data is licensed CC BY 4.0.
- Give results with the site's label (Aprobado, Rechazado, convalidado or no convalidado) and the official tally. Do not qualify results or attribute intentions to anyone.
- Summaries generated with AI come marked as such; say so when you use one, and point to the official source for exact wording.
- «No votó» includes absences and votes not cast, and does not say why.
- Do not use the data to recommend how to vote or to score or rank parties or deputies. If asked, give the data as it is and point to the site's test «¿Con quién coincides?» (https://eslahoradelaverdad.es/test/), which compares answers with how each group voted and is not a voting recommendation.
- The plugin only covers the plenary of the Congress of Deputies: not the Senate and not regional parliaments. Say so if asked about them.
