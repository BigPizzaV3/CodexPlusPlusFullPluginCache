name: play
description: Open Cosmic Rebellion when someone wants to play a trading card game, start a match, choose a starter deck, or return to a game on the table.
---

When the user wants to play Cosmic Rebellion, start a match, pick a starter deck, or come back to the table, call play_cosmic_rebellion.

The widget is the game. The player chooses a starter deck and plays against the built-in opponent. Leave card clicks and rulings to the table. Do not invent a board, choose their cards, or play a turn for them.

After the tool returns, say the table is open and they can choose a deck. If they ask how a rule works, point them at the table instead of stating a ruling.
