description: First steps with the OGRECORDS app — what it can read and good first questions.
---

The OGRECORDS app reads the connected OGRECORDS account. It cannot change anything.

Good first questions:
- Which of my releases are live, and which are still in review?
- List the tracks of my newest release.
- How many streams did my newest live release get in the last 30 days, and in which countries?
- What is my current balance, and have I had any payouts?
- How does distribution with OGRECORDS work?

Stream counts do not include YouTube or TikTok, and the most recent 24 to 72 hours are incomplete, so totals are a lower bound.

If no releases appear, the account has not distributed music through OGRECORDS yet. Setup guide: https://www.og-records.com/en/integrations/ai-assistants
