# Field Service Cloud Workflows

Use your Field Service Cloud records in ChatGPT or Codex to review crew priorities, investigate property concerns and prepare reports for your team or clients.

## What you can do

- **Operations Brief:** review today's crew plans, customer follow-up and work that needs your attention.
- **Operations Review:** review a manager's properties over a selected period, including customer updates, completed work and upcoming dates.
- **Workspace:** choose a manager in Operations or one jobsite in Property. Review service activity, issues, inspections and photos before deciding what belongs in a report.
- **Estimate Proposal:** create a customer proposal from an existing Extra, using saved scope/pricing and a standard or attached template.
- **Monthly Client Report:** prepare a jobsite's monthly Word and PDF report, with separate office review notes.
- **Translation:** read an individual saved note in another language while keeping its original text.

Workspace and report-file options depend on your assistant and connection. Word and PDF reports require file creation and document preview support, such as ChatGPT Work. Contact Janmar if an option is unavailable.

## Get connected

1. A company Admin enables **MCP access** in Field Service Cloud's Company Settings.
2. Install and enable Field Service Cloud Workflows using your assistant's available plugin options.
3. Open its **Field Service Cloud** connection and choose **Connect**.
4. Sign in with your own Field Service Cloud account, confirm your company and review the access request.
5. Start a new conversation with Field Service Cloud enabled.

Keep one Field Service Cloud connection enabled in the conversation. If you are using individual skills, choose Operations for briefs and internal reviews, Monthly Client Report for client reports, or Workspace for the interactive screen. Connector Instructions supports everyday questions.

## Try a request

- “Open the Field Service Cloud workspace.”
- “Give me my Operations Brief.”
- “Review my operations for [start date] through [end date].”
- “Show [jobsite name]'s work for [month/year].”
- “Prepare the [month/year] client report for [jobsite name].”

Name a manager when you need their crews and properties. For work history, give specific dates or use Workspace's date choices. Reviews cover up to 31 calendar days; a monthly client report covers a full calendar month. If a name or date is unclear, the assistant asks for the missing information.

## Review and prepare a report

In Workspace, choose Operations for a manager's crews and properties, or Property for one jobsite. Choose the period, review the work and select the service activity, issues, inspections and photos to include. The select-all control applies to matching records already loaded; check any additional pages before preparing the report.

Choose **Create report**, then **Draft with AI**. Expand and edit the report sections, or use **Changes to request** for a revision. Choose Word, PDF or both, generate the files and download them from the conversation. Check the final pages, photographs and captions before sharing them. Office or management checks stay in the separate review notes.

You can customize the report's headings, wording and photo preferences for your company. Submitted **Jobsite KPI** forms can appear in a monthly report when available; they are not required.

## Your records and access

The connection reads records your Field Service Cloud account is allowed to access. Relevant information is shared with your chosen assistant to answer your request. Choosing a manager does not give you additional permissions.

Preparing or editing a report does not change Field Service Cloud business records, approve work or send the report. A missing visit record does not prove the crew missed the work. Check the records and review the report before acting or sharing it.

## If something does not work

Check that the plugin is enabled, the connection says **Connected**, your company Admin has enabled MCP access, and you signed in to the intended account and company. Start a new conversation after installing or updating. If the wrong company appears or access has ended, reconnect with the intended account.

## Customer estimates

Ask: "Create a customer proposal from Extra [number] at [jobsite]." Or open Workspace, choose Estimates, select the jobsite and load an Extra. Review the saved pricing, choose what to include, and request Word, PDF or both. To use your own template, attach it in the conversation and enter its filename in Estimates. The assistant must open the attachment before using it. Review generated pages and unresolved currency, tax, prices and terms before sharing. This workflow does not save or send estimates.
