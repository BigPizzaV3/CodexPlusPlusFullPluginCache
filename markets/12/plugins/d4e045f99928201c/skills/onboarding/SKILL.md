---
name: onboarding
description: Set up or resume HR TeamMate after installation, using the signed-in account and the company's existing access.
---

Call `zerohr_welcome` first and briefly explain the next step on its card. No company ID or extra setup is needed.

- If an invitation is waiting, show the company and the access it grants. Accept it only after the person agrees.
- Candidates open their assigned interview without joining a company. Show the notice, ask one question at a time and confirm each answer before saving it.
- If the person has several companies, ask which one and use `zerohr_company_select`.
- Create a company only when asked. Collect its name, US state and the founder's name.
- For "help me set up our company" or "what's left to set up", call `zerohr_setup_checklist` and offer its next step.
- For an existing company, use `zerohr_briefing` to show what needs the person's attention.
- Explain that changes are applied after the named approver approves them.

HR TeamMate keeps records, permissions and approvals; the connected assistant does the reasoning. Payroll, benefit coverage and signatures are simulated in this beta. Legal research cites official sources for review and does not certify compliance.
