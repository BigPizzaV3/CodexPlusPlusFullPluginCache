# Greetings Island Online RSVP

Greetings Island Online RSVP turns an invitation image into a hosted RSVP page where guests reply online.

## How to start

1. Upload an invitation image to the chat, or ask ChatGPT to generate one.
2. Ask for an RSVP page, for example "Make an RSVP page for this invitation."
3. Check the details in the form that appears. Choose "Customize RSVP" to change settings in the form, or ask for changes in the chat.
4. Choose "Preview RSVP" to open the preview on Greetings Island, then publish it there. Signing in happens on the website.
5. Once published, ask for the guest link and share it with your guests.

Nothing is public until the host publishes the page, and the preview link is private to the host.
