# Customer Concentration Calculator

Answers: How much of my revenue depends on my biggest customers?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/customer-concentration

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yearly revenue from each of the largest customers | $ a year each | none, ask for it | One figure for each of the largest customers, at least two. Names are not needed. |
| Revenue from all other customers combined | $ a year | $0 | Everything not listed above, as one figure. Use 0 if every customer is listed. |

## Before you calculate

- List at least two customers.
- The revenue listed must add up to more than 0.

## Formula

1. Total revenue = every listed customer added up + revenue from all other customers
2. Each customer's share = that customer's revenue / total revenue x 100
3. Concentration = the largest customer's share
4. Top three share = the three largest customers' revenue added up / total revenue x 100
5. Revenue left if the largest customer leaves = total revenue - the largest customer's revenue

## Rounding

Give percentages to one decimal place and dollars to the nearest whole dollar.

## Worked example

Inputs: Yearly revenue from each of the largest customers $120,000, $80,000, $50,000; Revenue from all other customers combined $0.

1. Total revenue = $120,000 + $80,000 + $50,000 = $250,000
2. Largest customer's share = $120,000 / $250,000 x 100 = 48.0%
3. Revenue left if the largest customer leaves = $250,000 - $120,000 = $130,000

Result for these inputs:

- Largest customer's share: 48.0%
- Top three customers' share: 100.0%
- Total revenue: $250,000
- Revenue left if the largest customer leaves: $130,000

Rule of thumb for this result: Warning.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- The largest customer is under 25% of revenue: **Healthy.** The customer base is well spread. No single loss would be a disaster. Keep watching as the business grows, since one large new customer can change this quickly.
- The largest customer is 25% or more and under 50% of revenue: **Warning.** The business has real exposure to its largest customer. It is time to pursue new customers actively and to think through what happens if that customer's business changes.
- The largest customer is 50% or more of revenue: **Critical.** The business depends dangerously on one customer. If they leave, spend less or fail, more than half the revenue goes with them. Spreading the customer base is the first priority.

## Notes

- If only the largest customers are listed and the rest are left out, every share is overstated. Ask for the revenue from all other customers, or say plainly that the shares are of the customers listed only.
- The Playbook's own calculator takes two to ten customers and has no field for the rest. With the revenue from all other customers at 0, these figures are the Playbook's own calculator's.
