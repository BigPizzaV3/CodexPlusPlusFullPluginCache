# Customer Acquisition Cost Calculator

Answers: What does it cost me to win a new customer?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/customer-acquisition-cost

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing spend | $ | $0 | Advertising, promotion and marketing tools for the period. Use 0 if none. |
| Sales spend | $ | $0 | Sales salaries, commissions and sales tools for the same period. Use 0 if none. |
| New customers won | customers | none, ask for it | New customers gained in that period. |
| Average first purchase | $ | none, ask for it | What a new customer spends on their first purchase or job. |

## Before you calculate

- Marketing and sales spend must add up to more than 0.
- New customers won must be above 0.
- Average first purchase must be above 0.

## Formula

1. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) = (marketing spend + sales spend) / new customers won
2. CAC as a share of the first purchase = CAC / average first purchase x 100
3. Marketing cost per customer = marketing spend / new customers won, and the same for sales

## Rounding

Give CAC to the cent, other dollars to the nearest whole dollar and percentages to one decimal place.

## Worked example

Inputs: Marketing spend $10,000; Sales spend $8,000; New customers won 25; Average first purchase $1,200.

1. CAC = ($10,000 + $8,000) / 25 = $720.00
2. CAC as a share of the first purchase = $720.00 / $1,200 x 100 = 60.0%

Result for these inputs:

- Customer acquisition cost: $720.00
- CAC as a share of the first purchase: 60.0%
- Marketing cost per customer: $400
- Sales cost per customer: $320

Rule of thumb for this result: Elevated.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- CAC is under 50% of the first purchase: **Healthy.** More than half the cost of winning a customer comes back on their first purchase, before any repeat business.
- CAC is 50% to 100% of the first purchase: **Elevated.** Winning a customer takes most or all of their first purchase. That only works if customers come back and buy again at a good margin.
- CAC is over 100% of the first purchase: **Critical.** It costs more to win a customer than they pay the first time. The business depends on repeat purchases to recover it, or needs to win customers more cheaply.

## Notes

- Use the spend and the new customers for the same period, and count only customers who are genuinely new.
