# Revenue Per Employee Calculator

Answers: Is my team bringing in enough revenue for its size?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/service-business-benchmarks

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual revenue | $ a year | none, ask for it | Total sales for the year. |
| Full-time employees | people | none, ask for it | Full-time staff, with part-timers counted as fractions. |
| Industry | choose one | Other | The kind of business, which sets the range it is compared with. One of: Professional Services; Construction; Retail; Restaurant/Food; Technology/SaaS; Manufacturing; Healthcare; Other. |

## Before you calculate

- Annual revenue must be above 0.
- Full-time employees must be above 0.

## Formula

1. The Playbook's ranges, in revenue per employee a year: Professional Services $150,000 to $250,000; Construction $200,000 to $350,000; Retail $150,000 to $200,000; Restaurant/Food $55,000 to $75,000; Technology/SaaS $200,000 to $400,000; Manufacturing $200,000 to $300,000; Healthcare $100,000 to $200,000; Other $150,000 to $250,000
2. Revenue per employee = annual revenue / full-time employees
3. Middle of the industry's range = (low end + high end) / 2
4. Difference from the middle = revenue per employee - middle, and as a percentage of the middle
5. Revenue needed to reach the middle = middle x employees - annual revenue, or 0 if already there

## Rounding

Give dollars to the nearest whole dollar and percentages to one decimal place.

## Worked example

Inputs: Annual revenue $1,200,000; Full-time employees 8; Industry Other.

1. Revenue per employee = $1,200,000 / 8 = $150,000
2. Middle of the range = ($150,000 + $250,000) / 2 = $200,000
3. Difference = $150,000 - $200,000 = -$50,000

Result for these inputs:

- Revenue per employee: $150,000
- Industry range: $150,000 to $250,000
- Below the middle of the range by: $50,000 (25.0%)
- Revenue needed to reach the middle of the range: $400,000

Rule of thumb for this result: Within the range.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- Revenue per employee is above the top of the industry's range: **Above the range.** The team brings in solid revenue for its size, which leaves room for good wages, reinvestment and profit. Each new hire should keep it there.
- Revenue per employee is within the industry's range: **Within the range.** Middle of the road. It may be sustainable, with thin margins. Pricing, automation and roles that do not bring in revenue are the places to look.
- Revenue per employee is below the bottom of the industry's range: **Below the range.** It is hard to cover wages, benefits and overhead and still make a profit at this level. Revenue has to rise or costs have to come down.

## Notes

- The ranges are the Playbook's rough guide by industry. Real figures vary widely with the kind of work, the region and how much is subcontracted, so treat the comparison as a prompt for questions, not a verdict.
