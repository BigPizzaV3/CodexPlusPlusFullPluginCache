# ROAS Calculator

Answers: Are my ads making money?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/return-on-ad-spend

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ad spend | $ | none, ask for it | What was spent on the ads. |
| Revenue from the ads | $ | none, ask for it | Sales that came from those ads. |
| Profit margin on that revenue | % | 30% | The share of each sale left after the cost of delivering it, before the ads. |

## Before you calculate

- Ad spend must be above 0.
- Revenue from the ads must be above 0.
- Profit margin must be above 0.

## Formula

1. ROAS = revenue from the ads / ad spend
2. Profit from the ads = revenue from the ads x profit margin / 100 - ad spend
3. Break-even ROAS = 1 / (profit margin / 100). Below this, the ads lose money.
4. Ad cost for each dollar of revenue = ad spend / revenue from the ads
5. Profit for each dollar spent on ads = profit from the ads / ad spend

## Rounding

Give ROAS to one decimal place, the break-even ROAS to two, and per-dollar figures to the cent. Give other dollars to the nearest whole dollar.

## Worked example

Inputs: Ad spend $5,000; Revenue from the ads $20,000; Profit margin on that revenue 30%.

1. ROAS = $20,000 / $5,000 = 4.0
2. Break-even ROAS = 1 / (30 / 100) = 3.33
3. Profit from the ads = $20,000 x 30.0% - $5,000 = $1,000

Result for these inputs:

- ROAS: 4.0 to 1
- Break-even ROAS: 3.33 to 1
- Profit from the ads: $1,000
- Ad cost for each dollar of revenue: $0.25
- Profit for each dollar spent on ads: $0.20

Rule of thumb for this result: Just above break-even.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- ROAS is at least 1.5 times the break-even ROAS: **Well above break-even.** The ads make real profit after the cost of what was sold and the ads themselves. The question is whether spending more keeps the same return.
- ROAS is at or above break-even, but under 1.5 times it: **Just above break-even.** Profitable, but not by much. A small rise in ad costs or a dip in conversions could tip it into a loss. Improve conversion or targeting before spending more.
- ROAS is below the break-even ROAS: **Losing money on ads.** Every dollar spent on these ads loses money once the cost of what was sold is counted. Fix the targeting, the offer or the prices, or stop the spend.

## Notes

- The break-even point depends on the margin: at a 30% margin the ads must bring in about $3.33 for every $1 spent just to break even.
