# Payback Period Calculator

Answers: How long until an investment pays for itself?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/payback-period

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial investment | $ | none, ask for it | What the purchase or project costs up front. |
| Monthly net cash flow from it | $ a month | none, ask for it | Extra cash it brings in, or saves, each month after its own running costs. |

## Before you calculate

- Initial investment must be above 0.
- Monthly net cash flow must be above 0. If it is 0 or less, the investment never pays for itself.

## Formula

1. Payback period in months = initial investment / monthly net cash flow, and in years = months / 12
2. Net return after 1, 3 and 5 years = monthly net cash flow x 12, 36 and 60 - initial investment
3. ROI at each point = net return / initial investment x 100

## Rounding

Give months and years to one decimal place, dollars to the nearest whole dollar and percentages to one decimal place.

## Worked example

Inputs: Initial investment $25,000; Monthly net cash flow from it $3,000.

1. Payback period = $25,000 / $3,000 = 8.3 months
2. Net return after 1 year = $3,000 x 12 - $25,000 = $11,000
3. ROI after 1 year = $11,000 / $25,000 x 100 = 44.0%

Result for these inputs:

- Payback period: 8.3 months (0.7 years)
- Net return after 1 year: $11,000 (44.0%)
- Net return after 3 years: $83,000 (332.0%)
- Net return after 5 years: $155,000 (620.0%)

Rule of thumb for this result: Fast payback.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- Payback is under 12 months: **Fast payback.** The money comes back quickly, which lowers the risk and frees cash for the next opportunity. If it can be repeated at the same return, it deserves serious attention.
- Payback is 12 to 36 months: **Moderate payback.** Reasonable for most business investments, such as equipment, vehicles and major software. The question is whether it keeps earning well beyond the payback point.
- Payback is over 36 months: **Long payback.** The money is tied up a long time, and a lot can change in three years. The longer the payback, the more risk the business carries.

## Notes

- This is simple payback. It does not discount future cash, and it assumes the monthly cash flow stays the same.
