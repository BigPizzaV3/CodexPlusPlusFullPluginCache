# Break-Even Calculator

Answers: How many sales do I need to break even?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/break-even

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly fixed costs | $ a month | none, ask for it | Costs that do not change with sales: rent, salaries, insurance, loan payments. |
| Variable cost per unit | $ a unit | none, ask for it | What each unit sold costs to make or deliver: materials, direct labor, shipping, payment fees. |
| Selling price per unit | $ a unit | none, ask for it | What the customer pays for one unit. |
| Units sold a month now | units a month | optional, leave out if not known | Current sales volume. With it, the safety margin and the monthly profit can be worked out. |

## Before you calculate

- Monthly fixed costs must be above 0.
- Variable cost per unit must be above 0.
- Selling price per unit must be above 0.
- The selling price must be higher than the variable cost. Otherwise every sale loses money and there is no break-even point.

## Formula

1. Contribution margin per unit = selling price per unit - variable cost per unit
2. Contribution margin ratio = contribution margin per unit / selling price per unit x 100
3. Break-even units a month = monthly fixed costs / contribution margin per unit, rounded UP to a whole unit
4. Break-even revenue a month = break-even units x selling price per unit
5. If current units are given: safety margin = (current units - break-even units) / current units x 100
6. If current units are given: monthly profit = current units x contribution margin per unit - monthly fixed costs

## Rounding

Round the break-even units up to a whole unit before working out the break-even revenue. Give dollars to the nearest whole dollar and percentages to one decimal place.

## Worked example

Inputs: Monthly fixed costs $15,000; Variable cost per unit $25; Selling price per unit $75; Units sold a month now 400.

1. Contribution margin per unit = $75 - $25 = $50
2. Break-even units = $15,000 / $50 = 300.00, rounded up to 300
3. Break-even revenue = 300 x $75 = $22,500
4. Safety margin = (400 - 300) / 400 x 100 = 25.0%
5. Monthly profit = 400 x $50 - $15,000 = $5,000

Result for these inputs:

- Break-even units a month: 300
- Break-even revenue a month: $22,500
- Contribution margin per unit: $50
- Contribution margin ratio: 66.7%
- Safety margin: 25.0%
- Monthly profit at current sales: $5,000

Rule of thumb for this result: Healthy buffer above break-even.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- Current sales are above break-even and the safety margin is over 20%: **Healthy buffer above break-even.** A solid cushion against a drop in demand, a rise in costs or a slow season. It can go toward growth or cash reserves.
- Current sales are above break-even and the safety margin is 20% or less: **Close to break-even.** Profitable, but a modest dip in volume or a rise in costs could push the business into a loss. Raising prices, lowering variable costs or cutting fixed costs widens the margin.
- Current sales are at or below break-even: **Below break-even.** The business is losing money each month at this volume. It needs more volume, higher prices, lower variable costs or lower fixed costs.
- Current sales were not given: **Break-even calculated.** This is the least the business must sell each month to cover its fixed costs. With current sales, the safety margin can be worked out too.

## Notes

- A unit can be a product, a job, a billable hour or a customer a month, as long as the price and the variable cost are for the same thing.
- The answer assumes the price and the variable cost per unit stay the same at every volume.
