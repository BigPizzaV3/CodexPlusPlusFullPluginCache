# Overhead Rate Calculator

Answers: How much of my revenue goes to overhead, and what does overhead add to each hour or job?
Learn more: https://omnyra.ai/wiki/business/overhead

## Inputs

| Input | Unit | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly overhead | $ a month | none, ask for it | Costs that are not tied to a job: rent, office staff, insurance, vehicles, software, marketing. |
| Monthly direct labor | $ a month | none, ask for it | Wages and labor costs for the people who do the billable work. |
| Monthly revenue | $ a month | none, ask for it | Total sales in a typical month. |
| Number of employees | people | optional, leave out if not known | With it, overhead and revenue per employee can be worked out. |

## Before you calculate

- Monthly overhead must be above 0.
- Monthly direct labor must be above 0.
- Monthly revenue must be above 0.

## Formula

1. Overhead as a share of revenue = monthly overhead / monthly revenue x 100
2. Overhead rate on labor = monthly overhead / monthly direct labor x 100. Every dollar of direct labor carries this much overhead.
3. Revenue left after overhead = monthly revenue - monthly overhead
4. Estimated billable hours a month = monthly direct labor / 35. This assumes labor costs $35 an hour.
5. Overhead per estimated billable hour = monthly overhead / estimated billable hours
6. If the number of employees is given: overhead per employee = monthly overhead / employees, and revenue per employee = monthly revenue / employees

## Rounding

Give percentages to one decimal place, the overhead per billable hour to the cent, and other dollars to the nearest whole dollar. The Playbook's own calculator shows the overhead per billable hour rounded to the whole dollar.

## Worked example

Inputs: Monthly overhead $18,000; Monthly direct labor $35,000; Monthly revenue $85,000; Number of employees 8.

1. Overhead as a share of revenue = $18,000 / $85,000 x 100 = 21.2%
2. Overhead rate on labor = $18,000 / $35,000 x 100 = 51.4%
3. Estimated billable hours = $35,000 / 35 = 1,000
4. Overhead per estimated billable hour = $18,000 / 1,000 = $18.00

Result for these inputs:

- Overhead as a share of revenue: 21.2%
- Overhead rate on labor: 51.4%
- Revenue left after overhead: $67,000
- Overhead per estimated billable hour (labor assumed at $35 an hour): $18.00
- Overhead per employee a month: $2,250
- Revenue per employee a month: $10,625

Rule of thumb for this result: Healthy.

## The Playbook's rule of thumb

- Overhead is under 30% of revenue: **Healthy.** Overhead is lean for the revenue, which gives room on price and stronger margins. Check that the business is not short on the systems or support staff it needs to grow.
- Overhead is 30% to 50% of revenue: **Moderate.** A common range, with room to improve. Go through each overhead line and ask whether it is essential or only comfortable.
- Overhead is over 50% of revenue: **Critical.** More than half of every dollar goes to overhead before materials, direct labor or profit. Most jobs lose money once overhead is counted properly. Overhead has to come down, revenue has to grow without it, or prices have to rise.

## Notes

- The billable-hours figure is an estimate. It assumes direct labor costs $35 an hour, as the Playbook's own calculator does. If the owner knows their real billable hours, divide the monthly overhead by those instead and say that you did.
- To price a job so it carries its share of overhead, add the overhead rate on labor to the job's direct labor cost.
