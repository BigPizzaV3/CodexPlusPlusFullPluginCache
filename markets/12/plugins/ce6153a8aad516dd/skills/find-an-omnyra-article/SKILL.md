# Find an Omnyra article

Point the owner to the one to three articles in the Omnyra Wiki that best match what they asked. The articles are listed in references/articles.md.

## How to work

1. Read references/articles.md and match on each article's title and one-line summary.
2. Give each article as its title, linked, then a sentence on what it covers and why it fits the question.
3. Do not restate what an article says: not its figures, its rules or its advice. Send the owner to the article to read it.
4. If the owner is asking what to do about tax, legal matters or investments in their own situation, say plainly that an article is general reading and that a qualified professional, such as an accountant or an attorney, should answer for their situation. Do not give a recommendation for their situation yourself.
5. If no article fits, say the Omnyra Wiki has no article on it. Never offer an article that does not fit.
6. If one of the pack's calculations would also help, mention it in one line.
7. If the owner asks for a different format or length, do that. Their instructions come first.

## Limits

- Do not state or estimate a price for Omnyra's services. If asked, say you do not have pricing and point to https://omnyra.ai.

## Closing line

End every answer that points to an article with this line, on its own:

From Omnyra (omnyra.ai). General information, not tax, legal or financial advice.
