# CAR / 8D

Read [modes](../instructions/references/modes.md). For connected work read [connection](../instructions/references/connection.md), [change control](../instructions/references/change-control.md), [human decisions](../instructions/references/human-decisions.md) and [native content](references/native-content.md).

Identify the case, source issue and current stage. If connected, find likely matches, resolve ambiguity, then retrieve header, returned step UUIDs, actions and relevant evidence/links. A numeric D4 heading is not a step ID. Follow native filters and pagination. Give a short checkpoint and the next useful question; do not restart a completed interview.

Support D0 emergency response, D1 team, D2 problem definition, D3 containment, D4 causes, D5 corrective actions, D6 validation, D7 prevention and D8 learning/recognition. Place out-of-order answers into the right step. Separate immediate response, containment and systemic prevention.

Use [investigation research](../investigation-research/SKILL.md) for plausible mechanisms, specialist perspectives, consulted sources and discriminating checks. Use 5 Whys, Ishikawa, cause/effect or TRIZ where suitable. Do not force a single root cause, human-error explanation, five whys count or tool choice. Preserve disagreement and hypotheses needing tests.

Create real tracked actions with description, known owner, due date and correct CAR/step associations where supported. Keep task narrative separate from action status. D3/D5 tasks are not substituted by notes alone. Missing owners/dates remain unresolved, never invented.

At a useful milestone present exact writes and obtain approval under shared change control. Preserve nested content outside the approved patch. Do not complete steps, close CARs or record protected effectiveness judgements. A proposed CAR is not open; observe server transitions.

Standalone users receive a full useful draft with gaps, sources, actions and continuation state. Reports retrieved from saved records use current persisted content. Label unsaved proposals and draft status; do not fabricate approvals or an immutable revision history.

Example: a user supplies only containment progress. Place it in D3, check existing actions, and ask for the next missing fact. Do not treat completed containment as proof of root cause or effectiveness. If the CAR is locked/inaccessible, retain proposed work outside the database and explain the handoff.
