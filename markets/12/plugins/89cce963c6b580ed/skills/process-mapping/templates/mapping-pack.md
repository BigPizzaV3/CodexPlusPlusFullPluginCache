# Process mapping pack

Package/version; organisation label/verified ID if connected; scope; mode; state (working, stakeholder review, agreed, upload preview, uploaded verified, partly uploaded).

## Baseline
Retrieval time; completeness/visibility limits; existing record and parent IDs; available revision indicators; related records and interfaces.

## Hierarchy and process cards
Department > macro-process > procedure > work instruction. Stable local IDs for new drafts; actual IDs only when verified. Purpose, boundaries, inputs/outputs, roles, activities, handovers, controls, records and gaps.

## Flow, responsibilities and perspectives
Explicit relationship types; participant roles; actual feedback sources; disputed facts; variants; TOC assumptions/options; decisions and outstanding authority.

## Final agreement
Actual reviewers; agreed package version/scope; current versus future state; excluded/unresolved branches. Branch acceptance is not upload approval.

## Impact and upload preview
Existing/new matches; before/after fields and parents; affected children, processes and linked history; unresolved risks; exact supported operations; user-approved decisions; unsupported detail retained here.

## Receipt and continuation
Verified saved IDs/fields; unverified/failed/unattempted operations; outstanding app decisions; next action. No native map-versioning or transactional guarantee.
