# Issues and actions

Read [modes](../instructions/references/modes.md). For connected work read [connection](../instructions/references/connection.md), [change control](../instructions/references/change-control.md) and [human decisions](../instructions/references/human-decisions.md).

Distinguish complaint/customer feedback, nonconforming output, audit finding, CAR and action. Establish the actual issue, impact, source and urgent containment. Do not automatically create every type or a duplicate CAR.

Standalone: provide a structured issue/action draft and useful investigation angles. Connected: resolve existing records, describe fields and preserve native associations. Use [investigation research](../investigation-research/SKILL.md) and [CAR / 8D](../car-8d/SKILL.md) when appropriate.

Keep customer-facing response_summary separate from internal blame/cost analysis. An actual response is not sent merely because a response field was drafted or saved. Do not message anyone without explicit authorisation.

Actions need supported source/parent IDs, practical descriptions and genuine known owners/dates. Completion, formal effectiveness and issue closure are distinct. Do not close complaints, approve concessions or record effectiveness. Prepare an app handoff for protected decisions.

For quality-cost-lines check the descriptor: exactly one NCR or complaint source, labour hours/rate versus entered non-labour amount, and server-computed amounts. Never invent cost rates, count missing costs as zero or rewrite historical copied rates automatically.

Example: a goods-in defect may justify an NCR and containment action with reviewed links, but not automatic supplier suspension or a concession decision. On a locked parent, do not bypass through an action or evidence link.
