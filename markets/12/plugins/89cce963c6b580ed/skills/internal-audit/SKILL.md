# Guided internal audit

Read [modes](../instructions/references/modes.md) and [audit rules](references/audit-rules.md). For connected work also read [connection](../instructions/references/connection.md), [change control](../instructions/references/change-control.md) and [human decisions](../instructions/references/human-decisions.md).

Establish the context: real internal-auditor assistance, coached self-assessment or practice. Confirm purpose, scope/processes/sites, period, responsible human auditor, participants, selected standard/document edition and available criteria. Do not imply model independence, a physical visit or certification.

Develop a process-based plan with relevant risks, recent change, interfaces, timing, evidence access and sampling rationale. In standalone mode accept user-supplied procedures/history. In connected mode retrieve relevant authorised process records, earlier findings, CARs/actions, complaints and changes, showing how they alter the plan. Closed records do not prove effectiveness.

Confirm opening arrangements and explain how findings will be discussed. Follow actual work: ask one useful main question at a time, no more than three grouped questions. Use real examples; trace inputs, decisions, handovers, exceptions and outputs. Adapt to answers and contradictions rather than reading a clause questionnaire.

For each audit trail retain question, reply, participant, source, sampled example, reviewed evidence and open follow-up. Distinguish auditee statements, auditor-reported observations, record metadata and material actually examined by the model. Request evidence without demanding documents that the criteria do not require.

Maintain a separate criteria coverage matrix: planned, assessed-with-evidence, partly-assessed, not-assessed or proposed-not-applicable with rationale. Coverage is not conformity; unanswered does not mean nonconforming. Define denominators for any progress figure; do not invent compliance percentages.

Draft each nonconformity with the actual requirement, verifiable evidence and precise gap. Record conformity/strengths, observations and OFIs fairly. Use the agreed classification scheme; no invented major/minor rule. Obtain factual review, preserve disagreement and investigate unresolved matters. Do not soften a demonstrated failure into an OFI or upgrade a preference into a requirement.

Close with scope, criteria/editions, method, samples, responsible auditor, findings, strengths, limits/unassessed areas and follow-up. Distinguish correction, causal investigation, corrective action and effectiveness verification. Use [investigation research](../investigation-research/SKILL.md) for useful checks, never as a source of fictional findings.

For saving, map to actual audit headers, audit-findings, actions and supported links. Preserve approved Q&A/criteria/coverage detail through a labelled notes convention only if agreed; it does not update native question views. Report unsupported detail retained in the user-held pack. Propose standards mappings using real clause IDs; acceptance and audit locking remain app decisions. Do not automatically create a CAR/NCR for every finding.

Example: follow a recent purchase through supplier choice, receipt and an exception. If told all suppliers are approved, seek a real example of selection and exception control. If evidence is unavailable, leave that assessment open; do not invent either conformity or failure.

Export a standalone audit pack using [the template](templates/audit-pack.md). Pause with a concise checkpoint and evidence still needed. An incomplete remote review remains labelled incomplete.
