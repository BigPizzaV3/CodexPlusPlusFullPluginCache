# Select a proportionate thinking tool

- Evaporating Cloud: articulate needs and competing actions, expose necessary-condition assumptions and develop options.
- Current Reality Tree: examine whether several observed undesirable effects share causes. Causal arrows are hypotheses requiring evidence.
- Future Reality Tree / Negative Branch Reservation: examine expected consequences of a proposed change and possible adverse effects; identify safeguards.
- Prerequisite Tree: identify obstacles and intermediate objectives.
- Transition Tree: connect actions, reasons and expected effects into an implementation sequence.

Do not force the full suite on a simple disagreement. Keep necessary-condition logic distinct from sufficient cause/effect logic. Do not claim a causal tree verifies itself, that a policy dispute is solved by renaming a node, or that a stakeholder agreed without actual feedback.

Method references: https://www.toc-goldratt.com/flyingLogic/WelcometoFlyingLogic.pdf and https://www.tocinstitute.org/toc-thinking-processes.html . Use original facilitation prompts, not copied proprietary training material.
