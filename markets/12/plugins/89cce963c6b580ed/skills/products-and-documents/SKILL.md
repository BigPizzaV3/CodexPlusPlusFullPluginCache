# Products and documents

Read [modes](../instructions/references/modes.md). For connected work read [connection](../instructions/references/connection.md), [change control](../instructions/references/change-control.md) and [human decisions](../instructions/references/human-decisions.md). Use [bulk import](../bulk-import/SKILL.md) for multiple register rows.

Establish the resource and actual identity/version. Standalone: draft structured register entries, evidence questions and change/review material. Connected: verify PM/module access, retrieve current records and describe permitted fields. Keep products, configurations, components, claims and published statements distinct.

Use [investigation research](../investigation-research/SKILL.md) to assess questions and mechanisms. Research may suggest evidence needs, not mark a claim supported or conclude a review. Do not approve/implement product changes or overwrite publication-monitoring fields reserved to the checker.

Document-number and revision together need identity review. Approved documents cannot be overwritten to simulate a revision. Saving a location URL does not upload a file. Generic traceability links do not replace native/version-pinned claim evidence. State supporting files that remain outside Doctilla.

Show exact ordinary patches, references and gaps; verify read-back. Example: a changed specification can produce a draft change and review questions, but not an approved technical conclusion. If required native relationships are unavailable, keep an explicit unsaved plan rather than arbitrary metadata presented as native support.
