# Management review preparation

Read [modes](../instructions/references/modes.md). For connected work read [connection](../instructions/references/connection.md), [change control](../instructions/references/change-control.md) and [human decisions](../instructions/references/human-decisions.md).

Confirm period, purpose, criteria, participants and available evidence. Standalone: assemble supplied information into agenda, inputs, draft minutes, decisions for review and actions. Connected: retrieve relevant authorised audits, CARs, complaints, risks, KPIs, suppliers, objectives and prior actions; cite scope and limits.

Do not imply an agenda topic was discussed or a proposed decision was made. Distinguish reported meeting facts, model analysis and pending decisions. Define KPI denominators/periods; missing data is not zero. Do not claim complete organisational performance from overview counts alone.

Describe management-reviews fields before saving. The reviewed API permits ordinary title/date/chair/attendees/minutes content, while formal input/output structures may be read-only. Use an approved readable minutes convention only if useful; report what cannot populate native views. Save no fabricated attendees, dates or approvals.

Prepare actual linked actions only after preview approval. Management-review approval is an app decision. Example: minutes from notes can remain draft; the phrase "approved by the team" in a transcript is not model authority to approve the record.
