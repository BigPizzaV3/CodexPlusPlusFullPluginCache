# Sharing, keeping and deleting

- **No account needed.** A calendar made in chat belongs to the person. Nobody else sees it or is
  told about it until they share or keep it.
- **Its link.** Results for a calendar carry a `link` to its page on kidtime.app. There the person
  can see the schedule, download it as a PDF, keep it in a Kidtime account by signing in, or delete
  it. Give the link when they ask to share, print or keep the calendar.
- **Anyone with the link can view the page.** Say so when the person wants to share it, so they send
  it only to people they want to see it.
- **Keeping it.** Signing in on the page moves the calendar into their Kidtime account, where it
  works like any calendar in the Kidtime app, and they can invite the other parent there.
- **Deleting it.** The page has a delete option at the bottom. It removes the calendar and
  everything in it, and cannot be undone.
- **Families already in the Kidtime app.** A parent who already keeps their family in the app can
  sign in from the chat to reach it. Then changes reach the other parent, as they do from the app.
  Only suggest signing in when they say they already use Kidtime.
