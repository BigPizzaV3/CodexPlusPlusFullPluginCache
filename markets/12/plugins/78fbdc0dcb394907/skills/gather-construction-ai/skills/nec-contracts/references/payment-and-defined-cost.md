# NEC3/NEC4 Contract Reference

Detailed clause reference for the NEC Engineering and Construction Contract. This document covers the core clauses that are most relevant to commercial management and quantity surveying.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard form. Project-specific Z-clauses, secondary options, and contract data entries may modify these provisions. Always check the specific contract.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against NEC commentary, including the official neccontract.com FAQs and practitioner commentary specific to NEC3 (e.g. gmhplanning.co.uk's clause-by-clause guidance notes and Alistair Blakey's NEC3 "Clauses 60 to 64" commentary), confirming the clause 61.4 and 62.6 deemed-acceptance mechanisms exist in both NEC3 and NEC4 wording. Additionally cross-checked 2026-07 against the full GMH Planning / CECA NEC4 bulletin library (bulletins 1–66), NEC4 ECC Practice Note 1 (Oct 2017), the official NEC4 amendment schedules (Jan 2019, Oct 2020, Jan 2023) and GMH's NEC3→NEC4 clause-by-clause comparison — see `references/gmh-ceca-index.md` for the topic index and `docs/nec4-source-review.md` in the repository for the review record. Items that could only be verified against secondary sources are hedged in the text. Report suspected errors via the repository issue tracker.

## Core Clause 5: Payment

### Clause 50 — Assessing the Amount Due

**50.1** The PM assesses the amount due at each assessment date.

**50.2** The amount due is the Price for Work Done to Date, plus other amounts to be paid to the Contractor, less amounts to be paid by or retained from the Contractor.

**NEC4 change:** NEC4 requires the Contractor to submit an application for payment before each assessment date (NEC4 50.2). If the Contractor does not submit an application, the amount due at that assessment date is the lesser of the amount the PM assesses and the amount due at the previous assessment date — so a missed application can defer payment for the period's work.

**NEC4 50.9 — Defined Cost finalisation (Options C/D/E/F):** the Contractor may notify the PM that a part of Defined Cost is finalised and submit the records; the PM reviews within 13 weeks, may require further records (supplied within 4 weeks), and then has a final 4 weeks to accept or make their own assessment — **if the PM misses that final window, the Contractor's submitted Defined Cost is treated as correct (deemed acceptance)**. Use this to close out cost records progressively rather than leaving everything to the final account.

### Clause 51 — Payment

**51.1** The PM certifies a payment within one week of each assessment date.

**51.2** Each certified payment is made within three weeks of the assessment date, unless a different period is stated in the Contract Data. Interest is paid on late payment.

**Commercial significance:** Late payment triggers statutory interest. QSs should track assessment dates and payment periods carefully.

### Clause 53 (NEC4 only) — Final Assessment

NEC4 adds a contractual final-account mechanism with no NEC3 equivalent: the PM makes a final assessment of the amount due no later than **4 weeks after the Supervisor issues the Defects Certificate** (or 13 weeks after a termination certificate). If the PM does not, the **Contractor may issue its own final assessment** to the Client, payable within three weeks. The final assessment becomes **conclusive** unless referred to dispute resolution (W1/W2/W3) within the time allowed. QSs handling NEC4 final accounts must diarise these dates — conclusivity cuts both ways.
