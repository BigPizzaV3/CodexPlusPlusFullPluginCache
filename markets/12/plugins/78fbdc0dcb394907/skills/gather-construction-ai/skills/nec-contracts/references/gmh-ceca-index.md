# GMH Planning / CECA NEC Guidance — Further-Reading Index

Free practitioner guidance by GMH Planning (Glenn Hide) and CECA, including the monthly CECA
NEC4 bulletin series (1–66 and continuing), NEC4 Practice Note 1, official NEC amendment
schedules, and NEC3-era guides. Content is © GMH Planning / CECA — the entries below link to
the [gmhplanning.co.uk detail pages](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/) rather than
reproducing the documents. This library was reviewed against this plugin's NEC reference
content in July 2026; see `docs/nec4-source-review.md` in the repository for the review method
and the corrections that resulted.

## Compensation event assessment & the dividing date
- **[CECA Bulletin 18 – Clause 63.5 and Assessing a Compensation Event Against the Last Accepted Programme](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/clause-63-5-and-assessing-a-compensation-event-against-the-last-accepted-programme/)** — traces the NEC3→NEC4 (2017)→NEC4 (2019) evolution of clause 63.5's rule for assessing a CE against a stale Accepted Programme.
- **[2019 NEC4 Amendment to Clause 63.5](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/2019-nec4-amendment-to-clause-63-5/)** — explains the mechanics and rationale of the January 2019 clause 63.5 amendment, including the "programme mark-up" (not a revision) technique.
- **[NEC4 Practice Note 1 – Assessing Delays Due to Compensation Events](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-practice-note-1-rules-assessing-compensation-events-accepted-programme/)** — the official NEC4 practice note on assessing a CE's delay effect against the Accepted Programme current at the dividing date, including the fallback where that programme is stale, unaccepted, or missing.
- **[CECA Bulletin 51 – Assessing Compensation Events: Forecast or Actual Cost?](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/actual-of-forecast-defined-cost-to-assess-compensation-events/)** — why CEs are assessed on forecast rather than actual Defined Cost under clause 63.1, using the dividing date and clause 63.9's "competent and prompt" assumption.
- **[CECA Bulletin No.4 – Compensation Event Quotations and Use of Assumptions](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/ecc-compensation-event-quotations/)** — pricing CE quotations, PM vs Contractor assumptions under clauses 61.6/60.1(17), clause 63.8 risk allowances, and dispute-escalation practice.
- **[CECA Bulletin No.46 – Concurrency and NEC Contracts](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/concurrency-and-nec-contracts-ceca-bulletin-46/)** — NEC has no defined "concurrency" concept; overlapping delay events are resolved by chronological sequencing under clause 63.5/Practice Note 1.
- **[CECA Bulletin 63 – Dividing Date](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-explaining-the-dividing-date/)** — what the NEC4 dividing date is, the three clauses that use it (63.1, 63.5, X1.5), and how it fixes actual-vs-forecast assessment.
- **[CECA Bulletin 48 – Assessing Weather Delays Under NEC Contracts](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-assessing-weather-delays-as-compensation-events/)** — how the clause 60.1(13) one-in-ten-year threshold is proved, why impact assessment is subjective, and why weather CEs are unusually assessed on actual rather than forecast cost.
- **[CECA Bulletin 35 – Common Misconceptions Associated When Administering NEC Contracts](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-common-misconceptions/)** — ten widespread misunderstandings corrected, including early warning liability, quotation assumptions, X7 omission, deemed acceptance, and hindsight in CE assessment.
- **[CECA Bulletin 15 – Getting Compensation Events Implemented](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-bulletin-getting-compensation-events-implemented/)** — walks the CE lifecycle and the contractual meaning of "implemented" (agreed in cost/time terms, regardless of physical progress).
- **[How to REALLY Get Compensation Events Implemented](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/really-get-compensation-events-implemented/)** — 2016 practitioner workshop guidance on recurring failure points across the CE lifecycle, with suggested behaviours.
- **[Compensation Events – Good Practice Guide](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/compensation-events-good-practice-guide/)** — 2013 self-assessment checklist for how well a project team manages the NEC3 ECC compensation event process end to end.
- **[ECC Compensation Event Flowchart (NEC3)](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec3-ecc-compensation-event-flowchart/)** — one-page flowchart mapping the full NEC3 CE lifecycle with timescales, from trigger through to implementation.

- **[Engineering and Construction Short Contract (ECSC) – CECA Bulletin 32](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-engineering-and-construction-short-contract/)** — when and why to choose the ECSC over the full ECC, and a warning against picking it as an "easy option" for a complex project.
- **[NEC4 Engineering and Construction Short Subcontract (ECSS) High Level User Guide](https://gmhplanning.co.uk/nec-downloads/nec4-engineering-and-construction-short-subcontract-ecss-high-level-user-guide/)** — clause locator for the ECSC's subcontract counterpart, with the shortest CE time bar found in the family (3 weeks), and a Contract Data template that pegs subcontract reply periods to the main contract's own.
