# NEC3/NEC4 Contract Reference

Detailed clause reference for the NEC Engineering and Construction Contract. This document covers the core clauses that are most relevant to commercial management and quantity surveying.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard form. Project-specific Z-clauses, secondary options, and contract data entries may modify these provisions. Always check the specific contract.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against NEC commentary, including the official neccontract.com FAQs and practitioner commentary specific to NEC3 (e.g. gmhplanning.co.uk's clause-by-clause guidance notes and Alistair Blakey's NEC3 "Clauses 60 to 64" commentary), confirming the clause 61.4 and 62.6 deemed-acceptance mechanisms exist in both NEC3 and NEC4 wording. Additionally cross-checked 2026-07 against the full GMH Planning / CECA NEC4 bulletin library (bulletins 1–66), NEC4 ECC Practice Note 1 (Oct 2017), the official NEC4 amendment schedules (Jan 2019, Oct 2020, Jan 2023) and GMH's NEC3→NEC4 clause-by-clause comparison — see `references/gmh-ceca-index.md` for the topic index and `docs/nec4-source-review.md` in the repository for the review record. Items that could only be verified against secondary sources are hedged in the text. Report suspected errors via the repository issue tracker.

## Core Clause 7: Title

### Clause 70 — The Employer's Title

**70.1** Whatever title the Contractor has to Plant and Materials which is outside the Working Areas passes to the Employer if the Supervisor has marked it as for this contract.

**70.2** Whatever title the Contractor has to Plant and Materials passes to the Employer as they are brought within the Working Areas (and passes back to the Contractor if they are removed from the Working Areas with the PM's permission).

## Core Clause 8: Risks and Insurance

### Clause 80 — Employer's Risks

**80.1** The Employer's risks include:
- Claims, proceedings, and compensation due to the Employer's use or occupation of the site
- Loss of or damage to the works due to the Employer's risks (war, civil commotion, radioactive contamination, etc.)
- Loss of or damage to the Employer's property

These events, if they occur, are compensation events under clause 60.1(14).

### Clause 81 — The Contractor's Risks

**81.1** The Contractor's risks are all risks not listed as Employer's risks.

## Core Clause 9: Termination

### Clause 90 — Termination

**90.1** Either Party may terminate if a termination event has happened.

### Clause 91 — Reasons for Termination

Key grounds include:
- **R1-R10** — Insolvency events: either Party may terminate if the other becomes insolvent
- **R11-R15** — Contractor default (substantial failure to comply, failure to provide a bond, unaccepted subcontracting, substantial hindrance, substantial breach of health and safety) — Employer's reasons, available only after the PM has notified the default and it has gone unrectified for **four weeks**
- **R16** — Non-payment of a certified amount within **thirteen weeks** of when it was due — the Contractor's reason
- **R17-R20** — A prolonged instruction to stop work (no restart instruction within thirteen weeks); which Party may terminate depends on whose fault caused the stoppage (either Party where neither is at fault)
- **R21** — A prevention event that stops completion of the works — the Employer's reason
- **R22 (NEC4 only)** — the Contractor has committed a **Corrupt Act** — the Client's reason (NEC4 lists 22 reasons; NEC3 stops at R21)

**NEC4 narrowing:** NEC4 removed the Client's unrestricted right to terminate "for any reason" unless secondary option **X11 (termination by the Client)** is selected — a genuine NEC3→NEC4 risk-allocation shift. Under X11 the Contractor is paid Fee on the removed work, since the reason is outside its control.

### Clause 92 — Procedures on Termination

**92.1** The Contractor leaves the site and removes equipment. The full procedures P1–P4 (Client completes the works, use of Plant and Materials/Equipment, assignment of subcontract benefit) are selected by the Termination Table according to the reason.

### Clause 93 — Payment on Termination

The Termination Table maps reason → payment elements **A1–A4**: A1 (normal assessment plus Defined Cost of Plant and Materials in the Working Areas and other cost reasonably incurred, less retentions/unpaid advance-payment balance) always applies; A2 (forecast cost of removing Equipment); A3 (**deduction** of the Client's forecast additional cost of completing the works — applied when the Client terminates for Contractor default); A4 (**Fee percentage on the uncompleted work** — the Contractor's compensation for losing the job). A4 is paid **only** in narrow cases: Client insolvency (R1–10), Client non-payment (R16), Client default (R19), or Client termination under X11 — not on every Contractor-initiated termination. Once a termination certificate issues, the Contractor does no further work and, for most reasons, the Client need not make the next certified payment.
