# FIDIC 2017 Contract Reference

Detailed clause reference for the FIDIC Conditions of Contract (Second Edition, 2017). This document covers the core clauses most relevant to commercial management. References are to the Red Book unless otherwise noted.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard FIDIC 2017 form. Particular Conditions, special provisions, and employer-specific amendments may modify these provisions. Always check the specific contract.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against FIDIC 2017 secondary commentary from named firms and practitioner sources (e.g. Howard Kennedy's clause-by-clause commentary on Clause 14, CMS's guide to the FIDIC 2017 suite, Fenwick Elliott). ## Clause 13: Variations and Adjustments

### Sub-Clause 13.1 — Right to Vary

The Engineer may instruct a Variation at any time prior to Taking-Over. The Contractor shall not make any alteration or modification unless instructed. The Contractor must comply unless they promptly give notice that they cannot readily obtain the goods or that the variation will reduce the safety or suitability of the works.

A Variation may include:
- Changes to quantities, quality, form, character, kind, position, dimension, level, or sequence
- Omission of work (unless to be performed by others)
- Changes to the timing or order of work
- Additional work necessary for completion
- Changes to standards of testing or inspection

### Sub-Clause 13.3 — Variation Procedure

**13.3.1 — Variation by Instruction:** The Engineer issues a Variation instruction. The Contractor proceeds and the Variation is valued under clause 12.

**13.3.2 — Variation by Request for Proposal:** The Engineer requests a proposal from the Contractor before instructing. The Contractor responds with:
- Description of the proposed work
- Programme for the Variation
- Proposal for adjustment to the Contract Price and Time for Completion

The Engineer must then respond with consent or otherwise. If the Engineer does not accept the proposal, they may instruct the Variation anyway (valued under clause 12) or withdraw the request.

### Sub-Clause 12.3 — Valuation (Red Book)

Variations are valued:
1. At applicable rates and prices in the Contract — where the work is of similar character and under similar conditions
2. At new rates derived from Contract rates — where character is similar but conditions are different
3. At fair rates — where no applicable rates exist
4. As daywork — where instructed by the Engineer

For Yellow Book and Silver Book (lump sum contracts), Variations are valued as Cost Plus Profit unless the parties agree otherwise.

## Clause 14: Contract Price and Payment

### Sub-Clause 14.3 — Application for Interim Payment

The Contractor submits a monthly Statement to the Engineer, including:
- The estimated contract value of work executed
- Any amounts to which the Contractor considers themselves entitled
- Deductions for retention, advances, and other amounts

### Sub-Clause 14.6 — Interim Payment Certificate

The Engineer issues the IPC within **28 days** of receiving the Contractor's Statement.

The Engineer may withhold certification if:
- The Contractor has not submitted a compliant programme
- The Contractor has not submitted the performance security
- Other grounds stated in the contract

### Sub-Clause 14.7 — Payment

The Employer pays the amount certified within **56 days** of the Engineer receiving the Statement (i.e., 28 days after the IPC is due).

If the Employer fails to pay, the Contractor is entitled to financing charges (sub-clause 14.8).

### Sub-Clause 14.9 — Retention

Retention is deducted at the percentage stated in the Contract Data (typically 5%).
- First half released at Taking-Over
- Remainder released at expiry of the DNP (or latest DNP if sectional)
