# FIDIC 2017 Contract Reference

Detailed clause reference for the FIDIC Conditions of Contract (Second Edition, 2017). This document covers the core clauses most relevant to commercial management. References are to the Red Book unless otherwise noted.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard FIDIC 2017 form. Particular Conditions, special provisions, and employer-specific amendments may modify these provisions. Always check the specific contract.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against FIDIC 2017 secondary commentary from named firms and practitioner sources (e.g. Howard Kennedy's clause-by-clause commentary on Clause 14, CMS's guide to the FIDIC 2017 suite, Fenwick Elliott). ## Clause 20: Claims (2017 Edition)

### Sub-Clause 20.1 — Claims

Either party may make a claim. The procedure in 20.2 applies equally to Employer and Contractor claims.

### Sub-Clause 20.2 — Claims Procedure

**20.2.1 — Notice of Claim:** Within **28 days** of the claiming party becoming aware (or when it should have become aware) of the event. **Condition precedent** — no notice, no entitlement (subject to 20.2.5).

**20.2.2 — Engineer's Initial Response:** If the Engineer considers the Notice of Claim time-barred, it must give a Notice to that effect within **14 days**. If it does not, the Notice of Claim is deemed valid — although the other party may still contest its validity.

**20.2.3 — Contemporary Records:** The claiming party must keep contemporary records to substantiate the claim.

**20.2.4 — Fully Detailed Claim:** Within **84 days** of becoming aware. Must include:
- Detailed description of the event
- Contractual and/or legal basis
- Contemporary records
- Amount claimed and/or EOT requested
- Supporting calculations

If not submitted within 84 days, the Notice of Claim is **deemed to have lapsed** (subject to 20.2.5).

**20.2.5 — Agreement or Determination of the Claim:** The claim is agreed or determined under clause 3.7 — the Engineer has **42 days** to reach agreement, failing which a further **42 days** to issue a determination; no notice of determination in time = deemed determination **rejecting** the Claim. This sub-clause also allows a **late Notice of Claim or late fully detailed claim to be accepted** where the circumstances justify it — a significant softening of the time bar.

**20.2.6 — Claims of Continuing Effect:** Interim fully detailed claims at **monthly intervals**; final fully detailed claim within **28 days** after the end of the effects.

**20.2.7 — General Requirements:** General requirements applying to all claims.

## Clause 21: Disputes and Arbitration (2017)

### Sub-Clause 21.1 — Constitution of the DAAB

A standing DAAB of one or three members is appointed by the date stated in the Contract Data (28 days after the Commencement Date where no date is stated). The 2017 edition introduced the standing DAAB — a board appointed at the start of the contract, not just when a dispute arises.

The DAAB has two functions:
1. **Dispute avoidance** — informal assistance at the request of either party
2. **Dispute adjudication** — formal referral and binding decision

### Sub-Clause 21.4 — Obtaining the DAAB's Decision

Either party may refer a dispute to the DAAB. The DAAB gives its decision within **84 days** of referral.

The decision is binding on both parties, who must comply immediately. Either party may give notice of dissatisfaction within **28 days** — otherwise the decision becomes final and binding.

### Sub-Clause 21.5 — Amicable Settlement

After notice of dissatisfaction, the parties have **28 days** to attempt amicable settlement.

### Sub-Clause 21.6 — Arbitration

If no settlement, either party may commence arbitration. The arbitration is conducted under the ICC Arbitration Rules (or as stated in the Particular Conditions).
