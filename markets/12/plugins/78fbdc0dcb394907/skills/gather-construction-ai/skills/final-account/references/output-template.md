# Output Template — Final Account Position

Apply Pattern 8 for the `FA-[NNN]` ref (session-local; increment on a prior `FA` ref, else `001`).

```
FINAL ACCOUNT POSITION — [Project Name]
Ref: FA-[NNN]
As of: [today] | Contract: [family / Option, or "not confirmed"] | Currency: [GBP]

1. SUMMARY — three sentences: records-supported net, claimed net, and the negotiation gap
2. MEASURED WORK — Step 2 table + quantities-only annex (omit annex if empty)
3. COMPENSATION EVENTS / VARIATIONS — Step 3 AGREED and UNAGREED tables + evidence-grade key
4. DAYWORKS, PRELIMS & OTHER HEADS — Step 4
5. THE STATEMENT — Step 5 dual-total build-up + negotiation gap
6. OPEN RISKS — Step 6, always present
7. NEGOTIATION APPENDIX (INTERNAL ONLY) — Step 7
8. STANDING CAVEAT
```

The standing caveat, always, unmodified:

> This is a draft, internal final account position assembled for commercial review — **it is not
> the final account statement for issue to the other party, and not legal advice**. Compensation
> event statuses and dates are as recorded in `ce-register.md` or as supplied by the user this
> session: this skill has no visibility of the Project Manager's inbox and never assumes a
> status. The negotiation appendix (Section 7) is prepared for internal strategy only and must
> never be issued, attached, or paraphrased to the other party. Ref: FA-[NNN] is a session-local
> sequence number for cross-referencing.
