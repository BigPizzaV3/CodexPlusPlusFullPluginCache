# JCT Standard Building Contract 2016 — Clause Reference

Detailed clause reference for the JCT Standard Building Contract (SBC) 2016. This document covers the core clauses most relevant to commercial management and quantity surveying.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard form SBC 2016. JCT contracts are almost always amended by a schedule of amendments, which frequently inserts condition-precedent notice regimes — always check the contract as executed. JCT 2024 editions are now published — confirm which edition applies before citing clauses; see workflow.md for the Edition Differences (2016 → 2024) table. This document is contract-administration guidance, not legal advice.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against the official JCT SBC/DB/ICD/MW 2016 contents pages (jctltd.co.uk) and published commentary/case law, including named-firm and practitioner commentary (e.g. Ramskill Martin's "Back to Basics" series on Schedule 2 quotations and provisional sums, Fenwick Elliott, and contract-consultants.com) cross-checking Schedule 2 timescales, clause 4.22.1's exclusion of defined-work provisional sums, and clauses 2.28.5/2.28.6.1. Items that could only be verified against secondary sources are hedged in the text. Report suspected errors via the repository issue tracker.

## Section 5: Variations

### Clause 5.1 — Definition of a Variation

A Variation means:
- The alteration or modification of the design, quality, or quantity of the Works including:
  - Addition, omission, or substitution of any work
  - Alteration of the kind or standard of materials or goods
  - Removal from the site of work, materials, or goods (unless for non-compliance)
- The imposition, alteration, or omission of any obligation or restriction regarding:
  - Access to or use of the site
  - Limitations of working space or hours
  - The order of carrying out the work
  - The use of specified materials or goods

### Clause 5.3 and Schedule 2 — Variation Quotation

Under clause 5.3, an instruction may state that the Contractor is to provide a **Variation Quotation**. The procedure and timescales are set out in Schedule 2 (Variation and Acceleration Quotation Procedures), not in clause 5.3 itself.

The quotation shall comprise (Schedule 2):
- The value of the proposed adjustment to the Contract Sum, with supporting calculations
- Any adjustment to the time for completion
- The amount for any direct loss and/or expense
- A fair and reasonable amount for the cost of preparing the quotation
- Where the instruction so requires, information on additional resources and method

The quotation must be submitted within 21 days (of the later of receipt of the instruction and receipt of sufficient information), and remains open for acceptance for 7 days from its receipt.

If the quotation is not accepted, the Architect/CA must instruct whether the variation is to be carried out; if it is, it is valued under the Valuation Rules (clauses 5.6-5.10).

### Clauses 5.6-5.10 — The Valuation Rules

Variations are valued as follows (in order of preference):
1. Where the work is of similar character, executed under similar conditions and with no significant change in quantity — **contract rates and prices** in the Bills of Quantities (clause 5.6.1.1)
2. Where the work is of similar character but not under similar conditions and/or with a significant change in quantity — contract rates as a basis, with a fair allowance for the difference (clause 5.6.1.2)
3. Where the work is not of similar character — **fair rates and prices** (clause 5.6.1.3)
4. Where the valuation does not relate to additional, substituted or omitted work — a **fair valuation** (clause 5.10.1)
5. **Daywork** — where additional or substituted work cannot properly be valued by measurement (clause 5.7)

**Commercial significance:** The hierarchy is important. QSs should always start with contract rates and demonstrate why they are or are not applicable before moving to fair rates. Documentation of why conditions are "not similar" is essential for fair rate claims.
