# JCT Standard Building Contract 2016 — Clause Reference

Detailed clause reference for the JCT Standard Building Contract (SBC) 2016. This document covers the core clauses most relevant to commercial management and quantity surveying.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard form SBC 2016. JCT contracts are almost always amended by a schedule of amendments, which frequently inserts condition-precedent notice regimes — always check the contract as executed. JCT 2024 editions are now published — confirm which edition applies before citing clauses; see workflow.md for the Edition Differences (2016 → 2024) table. This document is contract-administration guidance, not legal advice.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against the official JCT SBC/DB/ICD/MW 2016 contents pages (jctltd.co.uk) and published commentary/case law, including named-firm and practitioner commentary (e.g. Ramskill Martin's "Back to Basics" series on Schedule 2 quotations and provisional sums, Fenwick Elliott, and contract-consultants.com) cross-checking Schedule 2 timescales, clause 4.22.1's exclusion of defined-work provisional sums, and clauses 2.28.5/2.28.6.1. Items that could only be verified against secondary sources are hedged in the text. Report suspected errors via the repository issue tracker.

## Section 4: Payment

### Clauses 4.8-4.9 — Due Dates, Interim Certificates and Valuations

SBC 2016 works from monthly **Interim Valuation Dates (IVDs)** stated in the Contract Particulars.

**4.8** The **due date** for each interim payment is **7 days** after the relevant Interim Valuation Date.

**4.9** The Architect/CA shall issue an **Interim Certificate** within **5 days** of the due date, stating the sum they consider due and the basis of calculation. The QS carries out the interim valuation, and the Gross Valuation (ascertained under clause 4.14) shall include:
- The total value of the work properly executed
- The total value of materials and goods on site
- The total value of materials and goods off site (subject to listed conditions)
- Variations properly valued
- Fluctuations
- Loss and/or expense ascertained

### Clauses 4.11-4.12 — Final Date for Payment and Pay Less Notices

**4.11** The **final date for payment** of each interim payment is **14 days from the due date** — not from the date of the Interim Certificate.

**4.12** (Pay Less Notice): If the Employer intends to pay less than the sum stated as due, they must give a notice specifying the amount they consider due and the basis for that amount, not later than **5 days** before the final date for payment.

**Commercial significance:** The pay less notice regime is a statutory requirement under the HGCRA. Failure to give a valid pay less notice means the full notified/certified amount becomes payable, even if the Employer disputes the valuation.

### Clauses 4.20-4.24 — Loss and/or Expense

**4.20** If the Contractor makes written application that they have incurred or are likely to incur direct loss and/or expense for which they would not be reimbursed by payment under any other provision, because the regular progress of the Works has been or is likely to be materially affected by any Relevant Matter, then the Architect/CA shall ascertain (or instruct the QS to ascertain) the amount. The application must be made as soon as this has become (or should have become) reasonably apparent, and must include — or be followed as soon as reasonably practicable by — the Contractor's **initial assessment** of the loss and expense incurred and likely to be incurred, together with such information as is reasonably necessary for ascertainment. The Contractor shall keep the assessment and information updated at monthly intervals until all information reasonably necessary has been supplied.

**4.21** The Architect/CA (or QS) shall notify the Contractor of the ascertained amount **within 28 days** of receipt of the Contractor's initial assessment and information, and **within 14 days** of each subsequent update.

**4.22** The Relevant Matters — the matters that entitle the Contractor to loss and/or expense. Note that not all Relevant Events are Relevant Matters — weather and force majeure give time but not money.

| # | Clause | Relevant Matter | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4.22.1 | Variations | Variations (and matters treated as Variations) under clause 5, excluding variations to provisional sums for defined work |
| 2 | 4.22.2 | Architect/CA instructions | Instructions including postponement under clause 3.15, expenditure of provisional sums, and opening up / testing where no defect found |
| 3 | 4.22.3 | Approximate Quantity | Execution of work for which an Approximate Quantity was not a reasonably accurate forecast of the quantity of work required |
| 4 | 4.22.4 | Contractor suspension | Suspension of the Contractor's obligations under clause 4.13 for non-payment (HGCRA) |
| 5 | 4.22.5 | Employer impediment, prevention or default | Any impediment, prevention or default by the Employer or any Employer's Persons — covers failure to give access and late supply of materials, goods, or information |

The amount ascertained is added to the Contract Sum (clause 4.23).

**Commercial significance:** Loss & expense is separate from variation valuation. It covers prolongation, disruption, and other consequential costs that are not captured in the variation valuation itself. The application must demonstrate:
1. A Relevant Matter has occurred
2. Regular progress has been materially affected
3. The Contractor has incurred direct loss and/or expense
4. The loss would not be reimbursed under other provisions

### Clause 4.26 — Final Certificate

**4.26** The Architect/CA shall issue the Final Certificate within 2 months of whichever occurs last:
- The end of the Rectification Period
- The date of issue of the Certificate of Making Good
- The date the Architect/CA sent a copy of the statement of the final adjustment of the Contract Sum under clause 4.25 to the Contractor

Its conclusive effect is governed by clause 1.9 (see above) — proceedings challenging it must be commenced within 28 days of issue.
