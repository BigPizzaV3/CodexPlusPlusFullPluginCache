# JCT Standard Building Contract 2016 — Clause Reference

Detailed clause reference for the JCT Standard Building Contract (SBC) 2016. This document covers the core clauses most relevant to commercial management and quantity surveying.

> **Note:** This reference covers the standard form SBC 2016. JCT contracts are almost always amended by a schedule of amendments, which frequently inserts condition-precedent notice regimes — always check the contract as executed. JCT 2024 editions are now published — confirm which edition applies before citing clauses; see workflow.md for the Edition Differences (2016 → 2024) table. This document is contract-administration guidance, not legal advice.

> **Provenance:** clause citations verified 2026-07 against the official JCT SBC/DB/ICD/MW 2016 contents pages (jctltd.co.uk) and published commentary/case law, including named-firm and practitioner commentary (e.g. Ramskill Martin's "Back to Basics" series on Schedule 2 quotations and provisional sums, Fenwick Elliott, and contract-consultants.com) cross-checking Schedule 2 timescales, clause 4.22.1's exclusion of defined-work provisional sums, and clauses 2.28.5/2.28.6.1. Items that could only be verified against secondary sources are hedged in the text. Report suspected errors via the repository issue tracker.

## Section 8: Termination

Section 8 deals with termination of the Contractor's employment under the contract:
- **By the Employer (clauses 8.4-8.6):** for specified defaults following notice — including failure to proceed regularly and diligently and suspension of the works (8.4), for insolvency (8.5), or for corruption (8.6).
- **By the Contractor (clauses 8.9-8.10):** for specified Employer defaults (including non-payment) or Employer insolvency.
- **By either party (clause 8.11):** for prolonged suspension of the works caused by neutral events such as force majeure.
- **Consequences of termination:** clauses 8.7-8.8 (Employer termination) and 8.12 (Contractor/neutral termination) govern completion of the works, the termination account, and payment.

## Section 9: Settlement of Disputes

### Clause 9.1 — Mediation

If a dispute or difference arises, either party may refer it to mediation. Mediation is voluntary — either party may withdraw at any time.

### Clause 9.2 — Adjudication

Either party has the right to refer any dispute to adjudication at any time. The adjudicator must reach a decision within 28 days of referral (or 42 days with the referring party's consent, or longer if both parties agree).

**Commercial significance:** Adjudication is the primary dispute resolution mechanism in construction. The adjudicator's decision is binding until the dispute is finally determined by arbitration or legal proceedings. Most adjudication decisions are complied with — they provide an effective interim remedy.

### Clauses 9.3-9.8 — Arbitration

Where the Contract Particulars state that arbitration applies, disputes are finally determined by arbitration under clauses 9.3-9.8; otherwise, by legal proceedings.
