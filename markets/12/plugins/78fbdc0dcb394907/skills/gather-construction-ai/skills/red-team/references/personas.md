# Opposing-PM personas

Loaded at Step 5 (The Opposing Response) once a persona is picked or defaulted.

The user may pick who is across the table (default: **letter-of-the-contract**):

- **reasonable** — accepts what is evidenced, queries PARTIAL heads, takes no style points.
- **letter-of-the-contract** — every procedural and evidential point available, formally taken.
- **nightmare** — a QS-turned-lawyer: everything above, plus what the draft *failed to
  reserve*, a global-claim characterisation where heads are blended, and a cross-examination of
  the quantum method.

The persona changes the letter's **tone, thoroughness, and ordering only** — verdicts, the
attack register, and the verdict tier are identical whichever persona is chosen, and the output
says so. A persona never licenses an attack the records and contract don't support.
