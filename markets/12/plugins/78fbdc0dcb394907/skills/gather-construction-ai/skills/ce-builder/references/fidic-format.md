# FIDIC Format — Notice of Claim (Clause 20.2)

Loaded at Step 6 when the contract profile (Pattern 13) names FIDIC, or the user confirms
FIDIC when no profile exists.

```
NOTICE OF CLAIM UNDER CLAUSE 20.2
Project: [Name]
Contract: FIDIC [Red/Yellow/Silver] Book 2017
Date: [today]

1. NOTICE (Clause 20.2.1)
   The Contractor hereby gives notice of a claim for [additional payment / extension of Time for Completion / both] arising from the following event or circumstance:

   [One paragraph describing the event]

2. CONTRACTUAL BASIS
   The Contractor's entitlement arises under sub-clause [X] which provides for [Cost / Cost Plus Profit / EOT / combination].

   The Contractor became aware of this event on [date]. This notice is given within the 28-day period required by clause 20.2.1 (deadline: [date]).

3. INITIAL PARTICULARS
   [Description of impact — this section will be expanded in the fully detailed Claim under clause 20.2.4, due by [date = awareness + 84 days]]

   Activities affected: [list]
   Estimated time impact: [X days / "not quantifiable from available records"]
   Estimated additional cost: [to be quantified in detailed claim]

4. CONTEMPORARY RECORDS
   See `evidence-schedule.md` for the shared
   table format.

5. NEXT STEPS
   The Contractor will submit the fully detailed Claim under clause 20.2.4 within 84 days of becoming aware (by [date]).
   [If continuing event] Interim fully detailed Claims will be submitted monthly under clause 20.2.6, with a final fully detailed Claim within 28 days after the end of the effects.

NOTES
- This notice is based on data recorded in Gather. It should be reviewed by the commercial team before formal submission to the Engineer.
- The detailed claim with full supporting calculations will follow.
- The Engineer must give notice within 14 days if it considers the Notice of Claim time-barred (clause 20.2.2), and a late Notice may still be accepted where circumstances justify it (clause 20.2.5) — so a missed deadline is serious but not always fatal.
- For FIDIC 1999 contracts, adapt to Sub-Clause 20.1 (28-day notice; fully detailed claim within 42 days) — this template follows the 2017 procedure.
```
