# Pattern 7: Common Event-to-Clause Mapping

When mapping site events to contract clauses, use this reference for the most common event categories:

| Event Category | NEC Clause | JCT Clause | FIDIC Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Access/possession delay** | 60.1(2) — Client not allowing access by the access date | 2.29.3 (deferment of possession) or 2.29.6 (impediment, prevention or default by the Employer) + 4.22 (Relevant Matters, L&E) | Sub-clause 2.1 (right of access) read with 8.5 (EOT) and 20.2 (claim) |
| **Instructions/changes** | 60.1(1) — PM instruction changing Scope/Works Information | Section 5 (variations) + 2.29.1 (Variations as Relevant Event) + 4.22.1 (Relevant Matter, L&E) | Sub-clause 13.1–13.3 (variations) |
| **Stopping work** | 60.1(4) — PM instruction to stop or not start work | 2.29.2 (Architect/CA instruction, including postponement under 3.15) | Sub-clause 8.9 (suspension) |
| **Third party / Others** | 60.1(5) — Client or Others not working within stated conditions | 2.29.6 (Employer's Persons) or 2.29.7 (statutory undertakers) depending on circumstances | Sub-clause 2.1 or 4.6 depending on circumstances |
| **Late information** | 60.1(6) — PM not replying within required period | 2.29.6 (Employer impediment/default — information not provided in due time) + 4.22.5 (Relevant Matter) | Sub-clause 1.9 (delayed drawings/instructions) |
| **Physical conditions** | 60.1(12) — Unforeseeable physical conditions | No direct Relevant Event (risk allocation depends on contract drafting) | Sub-clause 4.12 (unforeseeable physical conditions) — **not available in Silver Book** |
| **Weather** | 60.1(13) — weather exceeding the 1-in-10-year threshold (**both NEC3 and NEC4**) | 2.29.8 (exceptionally adverse weather, SBC 2016) — **time only, not a Relevant Matter** | Sub-clause 8.5 (exceptionally adverse climatic conditions) — **EOT only, not Cost** |

**Usage:** Skills should reference "see Pattern 7 for the clause mapping" rather than listing individual clauses inline. When the specific event does not fit a common category, consult the full contract knowledge skill (nec-contracts, jct-contracts, or fidic-contracts).
