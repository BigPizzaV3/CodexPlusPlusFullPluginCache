# Contract types

The plugin supports three major construction contract forms:

| Contract | Coverage | Key Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **NEC3/NEC4** | Full clause reference, all 19 CE grounds, early warnings, programme obligations | Compensation Events — 8-week notification |
| **JCT SBC/DB/ICD/MW** | Relevant Events, Relevant Matters, variations, EOT, loss & expense | Separate time (EOT) and cost (L&E) mechanisms |
| **FIDIC Red/Yellow/Silver** | Claims procedure, variations, unforeseeable conditions, DAAB | 28-day notice — condition precedent |

Contract knowledge is auto-invoked when relevant. Analytical skills like `$ce-builder`, `$events`, and `$delay-analysis` adapt their output to the contract type — using correct terminology, clause references, and notification timescales.
