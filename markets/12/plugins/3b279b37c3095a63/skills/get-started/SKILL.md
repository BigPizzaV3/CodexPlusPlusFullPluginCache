# Getting Started with QRTRAC

Welcome to QRTRAC on ChatGPT! QRTRAC is the enterprise dynamic QR code and smart link platform combining the best of Bitly and Flowcode.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Editable Dynamic QR Codes (Flowcode Alternative)**:
   Generate dynamic QR codes where you can change the target URL anytime after printing, without ever needing to reprint the physical code.

2. **White-Label Custom Domain Short Links (Bitly Alternative)**:
   Route scans and links through your own custom branded domain (e.g., `go.yourbrand.com`) with UTM parameter tracking and GPS click analytics.

3. **Interactive QR Widgets**:
   Get an instant live preview in chat, along with a link to open the interactive widget with scan simulation and real-time destination controls.

4. **Real-Time Scan Analytics**:
   Track scan counts, geographic locations (city, country, GPS coordinates with consent), device types, operating systems, and GA4 native sync.

5. **30+ Content Types**:
   Support for URLs, vCards / Digital Business Cards, PDF documents, Restaurant Menus, GS1 Digital Links, Anti-counterfeit Scan to Verify, Coupon redemption tracking, App Store smart routing, and WiFi.

6. **50% Nonprofit Discount Forever**:
   Verified 501(c)(3) and equivalent nonprofit organizations receive 50% off every plan, forever via [https://qrtrac.com/nonprofit-organizations/verify/](https://qrtrac.com/nonprofit-organizations/verify/).
