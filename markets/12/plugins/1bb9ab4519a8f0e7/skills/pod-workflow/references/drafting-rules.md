# Drafting profiles are not marketplace policy

These profiles preserve the publisher’s existing workflow. Some limits are deliberately stricter or more specific than a marketplace’s own requirements. Do not describe every profile value as an official marketplace rule, and do not claim a live policy check merely because server validation passed.

| Profile | Fields and plugin rules |
| --- | --- |
| Etsy PNG | Title at most 120 characters; exactly 13 unique tags, each exactly 3 words and at most 20 characters including spaces; description at most 700 characters; digital-download intent |
| Etsy Shirt | Title at most 120 characters; exactly 13 unique tags, each exactly 3 words and at most 20 characters including spaces; description at most 700 characters; physical-shirt intent |
| Merch by Amazon | Title at most 60 characters; bullet1 and bullet2 at most 230 characters each; complementary relevant keyword angles |
| TeePublic | Title preferably at most 60 characters; one main tag; exactly 15 supporting tags with exactly 2 words each |
| Redbubble | Title preferably at most 60 characters; exactly 15 tags with exactly 3 words each |

Tag relevance, truthful claims, buyer intent, independent writing, and safety screening remain the workflow’s responsibility. Deterministic format checks do not prove those judgments. Likewise a recommended title-length warning does not establish a marketplace violation.

Write a draft rather than inventing product facts. If no facts are confirmed, use general design-focused text; do not make up materials, sizing, fit, shipping, file contents, included licenses, print quality, charitable donations, or commercial outcomes. The design prompt’s final print specifications are instructions for future artwork, not a verified delivered file.

Do not add third-party brands to the plugin’s own name, imply marketplace affiliation, or use marketplace logos. Marketplace names here identify the supported listing formats. The workflow is independent of those services.
