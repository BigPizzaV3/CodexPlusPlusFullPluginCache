Aerospace/Defense – Moody's View

The global aerospace and defense sector carries a stable outlook with positive bias for defense primes, reflecting robust defense spending and steady commercial aircraft demand. Boeing's credit profile remains pressured by execution issues on the 737 MAX and 777X programs.

Company positioning: Boeing's Baa3 negative outlook reflects weaker positioning than A-rated peers; path to upgrade requires MAX production ramp and FCF normalization.
