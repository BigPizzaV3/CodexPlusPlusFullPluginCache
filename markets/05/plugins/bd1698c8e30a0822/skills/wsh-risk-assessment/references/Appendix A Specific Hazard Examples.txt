# Appendix A: NUS-Specific Hazard Examples Library

**Review Notes:**

* Activity statements should describe the task being performed.
* Hazard statements should describe the source of harm or exposure mechanism.
* Consequence statements should describe the injury, illness, damage, or adverse outcome resulting from the hazard.
* Most examples are well structured. Minor refinements have been made where hazards were expressed as consequences or where wording could be made more consistent.

## Chemical Laboratories

### Example 1

Activity: Dispensing concentrated sulphuric acid

Hazard: Exposure of the eyes to splashes of concentrated sulphuric acid during transfer

Potential Consequence:

* Severe chemical burns to the eyes
* Permanent vision impairment

### Example 2

Activity: Transferring volatile solvents

Hazard: Inhalation of acetone vapour during solvent transfer

Potential Consequence:

* Respiratory irritation
* Dizziness
* Headache

Hazard: Over-pressurisation resulting from a runaway exothermic reaction
