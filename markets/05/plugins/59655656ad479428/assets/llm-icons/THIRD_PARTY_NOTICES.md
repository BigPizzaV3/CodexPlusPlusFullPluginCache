does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation.

Provider-declared website assets:

- `lyria.svg`: Google DeepMind favicon declared by the official
  [Lyria model page](https://deepmind.google/models/lyria/). The page did not
  publish a separate Lyria-only mark, so the manifest records this as a
  provider-level visual with a caveat.
- `mancer.svg`: favicon declared by [Mancer](https://mancer.tech/), sourced from
  `https://mancer.tech/resources/Phantom%20Favicon.svg`.
- `nex-agi.svg`: Nex-N2 logo published by
  [Nex AGI](https://nex-agi.com/), sourced from
  `https://nex-agi.com/logo/nex.svg`.
- `perceptron.png`: light-mode favicon declared by
  [Perceptron](https://www.perceptron.inc/).
- `writer.svg`: `Writer_icon_dark.svg` from the official
  [Writer newsroom](https://writer.com/newsroom/). Use is subject to Writer's
  brand guidelines on that page.

The Hugging Face CDN served those avatars as 200-pixel WebP images. They were
format-normalized to RGBA PNG without redrawing the artwork. Every mark also
has a derived 14-pixel RGBA footnote image for the atomic chip renderer; those
small derivatives preserve the original proportions and colors.
