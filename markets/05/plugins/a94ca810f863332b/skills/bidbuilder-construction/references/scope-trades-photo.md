# Scope, Trade & Photo Reference

## Construction principles

Prioritize planning, preparation, documentation, quality workmanship,
communication, safety, and professional verification. Never recommend
shortcuts that compromise structural integrity, safety, or compliance.

## Common trade considerations

Demolition: labor, protection, dust control, disposal, access,
hazardous-material concerns. Drywall: substrate/framing condition, water
source, texture/finish matching, painting. Painting: preparation,
patching, sanding, caulking, priming, coats, protection, cleanup.
Flooring: subfloor, moisture, leveling, transitions, trim, furniture,
waste. Tile: substrate, waterproofing, drainage/slope, layout, movement,
trim. Cabinetry/countertops: measurements, wall/floor conditions,
selections, templates, sinks/appliances, lead times. Roofing: pitch,
layers, decking, flashing, ventilation, penetrations, access, weather.
Decks: footings, framing, decking, railings, stairs, utilities,
drainage, permits. Fences: property lines/survey, utilities, slope,
gates, access, permits/HOA. Plumbing/electrical/HVAC: identify scope and
coordination needs; recommend qualified/licensed trades when required.
Rental turnover/handyman: define each task; separate minor work from
permit-required or specialty-trade work.

## Hidden-condition awareness

Potential issues may include water damage, rot, mold indicators, pest
damage, improper prior work, structural concerns, electrical/plumbing
deficiencies, and code-related upgrades. Flag as potential unless
verified.

## Photo analysis

Report: - Project type + confidence - Visible materials - Visible
conditions - Potential scope - Risks - Missing information - Additional
photos/measurements needed - Priority follow-up questions - Pricing
categories - Estimator notes

Use "appears to be" or similar language when visual identification is
uncertain. Photos alone do not confirm concealed conditions.
