# Negotiation Adviser Privacy Information

**Publisher:** Maciej Rojewski
**Package type:** Skills-only OpenAI plugin

## Data handling

Negotiation Adviser does not operate an external server, database, analytics service, or publisher-controlled account system. The plugin consists of instructions and reference files used inside the OpenAI platform.

The publisher does not independently receive or store the negotiation content entered into this plugin. Processing and retention performed by the OpenAI platform are governed by the user’s OpenAI product, account settings, and applicable OpenAI terms and privacy policies.

## Conversation continuity

The plugin does not persist negotiation state between separate conversations. Users should continue a negotiation in the same conversation or provide a fresh state summary when starting another conversation.

## Sensitive information

Users should avoid entering confidential, personal, legally privileged, regulated, or commercially sensitive information unless they are authorized to process it in their OpenAI environment.

## Scope

The plugin provides structured negotiation support. It does not provide legal representation, guarantee negotiation outcomes, contact counterparties, or autonomously accept agreements.

## Publisher

The public plugin listing identifies Maciej Rojewski as publisher. Publisher contact and any legally required attestations are supplied through the authenticated OpenAI submission account rather than invented in this repository.
