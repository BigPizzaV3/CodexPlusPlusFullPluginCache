# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Brokerage Screenshot Parser is a skills-only plugin that provides instructions for parsing brokerage screenshots into structured data.

The plugin does not operate an external server, database, analytics service, advertising system, or user account system. The plugin developer does not independently collect, transmit, sell, or store screenshots or extracted data.

Your use of ChatGPT is governed by the OpenAI terms and privacy controls applicable to your account and workspace. Do not upload passwords, authentication codes, API keys, full account numbers, tax identifiers, or other credentials.

The plugin is not affiliated with or endorsed by E*TRADE, Robinhood, or any other brokerage.

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/sizheng/prof/issues.
