# Havan Festival Planner support

For help with a bug, incorrect date-source behavior, accessibility problem, policy concern, or culturally inappropriate output, use:

https://havan.dev/support

Include the festival, location/timezone, prompt, expected behavior, and observed behavior. Do not post private household, health, identity, or account information.

For urgent fire, medical, or safety situations, contact the appropriate local emergency service rather than relying on this plugin.
