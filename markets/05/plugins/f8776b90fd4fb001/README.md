# Andexnite Shopping

Official assisted-shopping plugin for [andexnite.cz](https://andexnite.cz/),
published by ANDEX FASHION s.r.o.

## What it does

- searches and compares live products;
- keeps Czech product names while explaining choices in Russian, Czech, or
  English;
- prepares and reviews a guest cart;
- opens the guest checkout without creating a store account;
- never submits the final binding order.

## Example prompts

- `Найди коричневую кожу толщиной около 2 мм до 1000 Kč.`
- `Сравни эти три товара и добавь лучший в корзину.`
- `Проверь стоимость доставки и открой checkout.`

## Safety boundary

The plugin can prepare a cart and, with explicit authorization, fill ordinary
checkout contact fields. It never enters payment-card data and never presses
the final order button.
