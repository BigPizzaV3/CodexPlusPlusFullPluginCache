# Turn Chaos Into Knowledge
1. Define users, decisions supported, source scope, sensitivity, freshness, and target system.
2. Inventory and deduplicate sources; preserve provenance, dates, ownership, and access constraints.
3. Extract concepts, entities, claims, decisions, actions, processes, questions, and relationships.
4. Design a shallow taxonomy around retrieval tasks; separate canonical records from commentary and archive.
5. Resolve duplicates without erasing contradictions; label confidence, stale material, and missing sources.
6. Return information architecture, canonical summaries, index, decision log, FAQs, gaps, maintenance rules, and migration plan.
