## Shared design implications

The references support story-led product demonstrations with motion graphics, varied visual scale, recurring subjects, and clear output reveals. They do not establish that every trailer should use identical fast cuts or a fixed number of title cards. Their landscape format and 56–73-second lengths also suggest offering a fuller launch film alongside a short social cutdown. Select exact duration according to the renderer's supported limits; custom composition can follow the intended longer form when available.
