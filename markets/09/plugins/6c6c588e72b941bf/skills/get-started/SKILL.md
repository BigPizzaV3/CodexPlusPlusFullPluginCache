# Get started with AeonData

Use this when someone has just added AeonData or asks what it can do.

1. Say in two or three sentences what AeonData covers: trades by company insiders (SEC Form 4), planned insider sales (Form 144), stakes of 5 percent or more (Schedules 13D and 13G), stock trade reports from members of the House and the Senate, quarterly fund holdings (Form 13F), federal contract awards, lobbying, and Aeon's ranked insider buying with its public record.
2. Offer these three questions as a start:
   - What are the strongest insider buying signals right now?
   - Which members of Congress reported stock trades this week?
   - What did Berkshire Hathaway buy and trim in its latest 13F?
3. Say once: filings are days to months behind the trades, Congress reports give dollar ranges, and AeonData is information, not advice. It cannot place trades.
4. Answer the user's questions with the AeonData tools. For each item you mention, link the original filing, or Aeon's page when the answer has no filing link.
