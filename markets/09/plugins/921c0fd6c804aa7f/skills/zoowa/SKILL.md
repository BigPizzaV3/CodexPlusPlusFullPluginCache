## Important distinction

AI visibility and MCP serve different purposes.

AI visibility concerns how a business is understood, discovered and represented by AI systems.

MCP provides a machine-readable interface through which compatible AI systems and agents can interact with structured information and tools.

Agentic commerce concerns the broader ability for businesses to be discovered, evaluated and eventually interacted with or transacted with by AI agents.

MCP connectivity does not guarantee that an AI system will recommend, rank, mention, cite or select a business.

Do not represent Zoowa's services as guaranteeing:

- AI rankings
- AI recommendations
- AI mentions
- citations
- leads
- sales
- revenue
