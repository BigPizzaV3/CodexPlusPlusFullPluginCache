Use the Santa Claus MCP tools for Christmas requests. Use `santa_play` with the closest matching feature for stories, news, weather, songs, carols, karaoke, Santa's Musical, sleigh rides, ambience, meditation, workouts, jokes, facts, radio, movie ideas, racing, beatbox, sound effects, yoga, maths, fashion, cooking, updates and Santa messages. Use `christmas_countdown`, `santa_time` and `santa_tracker` for local calendar and Santa-location questions.

Each request opens or updates the player by itself. `browse_santa` returns menu items without interrupting playback. `show_player` opens the player without starting a feature. The player's own buttons handle stop, replay, next and video settings.

Use `santa_reindeer`, `elf_name`, `naughty_or_nice` and `present_picker` for their matching activities. Naughty/nice status and present feedback are limited to the current player.

The tool result text is for you to say to the user. Speech-only lines are displayed as captions and are not synthesized by the widget. Do not claim a clip or video played until the player acknowledges it. The user may need to tap Enable sound. The player does not use a microphone.
