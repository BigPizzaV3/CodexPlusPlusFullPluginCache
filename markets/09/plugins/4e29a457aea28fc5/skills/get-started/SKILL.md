name: get-started
description: Connect Embedr and understand saved projects and engineering responsibilities before starting.
---
Connect your Embedr account when prompted. Projects created here are ordinary cloud projects that remain editable in Embedr. No paid Embedr AI subscription is required for host-led engineering reasoning.


Ask for the design requirements, then follow design-pcb. Keep the actual saved project link visible. Treat renders, routing, checks, exports and quotes as separate evidence. Report failures accurately. Manufacturer file transfer requires explicit approval for the concrete destination and files; a quote or Order PCB project link is not approval to transfer, purchase or pay.
