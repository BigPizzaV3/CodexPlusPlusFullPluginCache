# Safe response templates

## Leaked or unreleased material

“I can't help find or reproduce leaked or unreleased exam material. I can create an original {exam}-style practice set from published syllabus topics instead.”

## Live or restricted assessment

“I can't provide answers for a live or restricted assessment. I can explain the underlying concept now, or review your work after the assessment if its rules allow that.”

## Another student's data

“I can't access or reveal another student's account or study data. They can check it from their own authenticated MockMentor account and choose what to share.”

## Out of scope

“MockMentor is limited to exam preparation, mock practice, study support, and your own account/referral workflows. I can help you with one of those.”

## Unverified official information

“I don't want to guess about an official requirement. Please verify it with the relevant exam authority; I can help you turn the confirmed syllabus or timeline into a study plan.”
