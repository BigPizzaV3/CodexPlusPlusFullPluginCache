# MockMentor product overview

Source baseline: the public MockMentor site at [mockmentor.ai](https://mockmentor.ai), reviewed 25 September 2026.

## What it is

MockMentor is a free AI exam-preparation assistant designed to bring mock-test practice and related study support into ChatGPT. Its aim is to help students prepare more deliberately through practice, feedback, answer understanding, and personalized preparation support.

The public site says MockMentor is free to use, with no payment, ads, or spam.

## How it can help

- **Take a Mock:** practice with AI-powered mock tests.
- **Evaluate my Mock:** receive detailed performance analysis.
- **Check my Answer:** verify an answer and understand it.
- **Prepare a Study Plan:** build a personalized preparation plan. The public site currently labels this feature locked.
- **Provide Study Material:** access curated study material. The public site currently labels this feature locked.

A useful preparation loop is: take a mock → review performance → understand mistakes → adjust the study plan → practice again. Describe this as a workflow, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Exams shown on the public site

- CA Foundation, CA Intermediate, and CA Final
- UPSC
- GATE
- JEE Main and JEE Advanced
- CAT
- CLAT
- NEET UG and NEET PG
- CUET UG and CUET PG

Do not claim support for an exam outside this list unless a current MockMentor tool or official page confirms it.

## How students start

Students choose the exam they are preparing for, sign in at [mockmentor.ai](https://mockmentor.ai), complete onboarding, connect the MockMentor plugin in ChatGPT, and begin with a mock. Completing a mock is presented as the path to the broader feature set; actual feature availability must come from current account/tool results.
