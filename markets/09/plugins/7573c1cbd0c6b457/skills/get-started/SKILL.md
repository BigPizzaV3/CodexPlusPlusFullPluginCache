# Getting started with Precedentes

Precedentes searches Brazilian court decisions (STF, STJ and more than 70 other courts and agencies), laws, binding precedents and official gazettes.

- Ask in plain Portuguese, e.g. "Quais precedentes do STJ tratam de dano moral por negativação indevida?". Use `buscar_decisoes`.
- Before citing, open the decision with `decisao` or `inteiro_teor` and always show the `link_oficial` exactly as returned.
- To check citations in a text, use `verificar_citacoes`.
- For statistics, use `jurimetria` and always state the universe, period and criteria returned.
- Never cite a decision, number or excerpt that a tool did not return. Show contrary decisions and divergence when they exist.
- Drafts of legal briefs (`planejar_peca`, `redigir_peca`) are for the lawyer's review and signature; nothing is filed automatically.
