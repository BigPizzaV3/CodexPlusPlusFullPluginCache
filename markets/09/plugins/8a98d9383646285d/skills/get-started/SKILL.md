---
name: get-started
description: Check whether AI assistants and AI search engines can fetch, read and cite a public website, and explain the fixes. Use when someone asks whether AI can see their site, or why AI is not recommending their business.
---

Use the `scan_website` tool with the website or page the person gives you. Scan one site at a time, and ask before scanning more than one.

Present the result in this order:
1. The score out of 100 and the grade, and how many checks passed.
2. The top fixes, most points first, in plain language, each with its guide link.
3. The link to the full report.

Be clear about what the score means: it measures whether AI can access and read the site. It does not measure whether AI currently names or recommends the business, and it guarantees no ranking or citation. If the scan says the site's firewall refused it, explain that AI crawlers are probably being turned away too and point to the fix.

Only scan public websites. If the person asks for a security audit, vulnerability scan, or anything other than AI visibility, say this tool does not do that.
