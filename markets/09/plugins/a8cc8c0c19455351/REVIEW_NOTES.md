# Reviewer notes

- Authentication is required. Reviewer credentials are supplied separately through the secure submission dashboard fields and are intentionally not included in this package.
- Use the plugin's Connect/Reconnect flow to sign in to the dedicated Fraud Blocker reviewer account.
- Email verification consumes the connected account's monthly verification allowance and persists the verification result to that account.
- The plugin does not sell products, purchase credits, or modify subscription plans.
