# Mütalaa

Research Turkish law, legal rights and access to justice with source texts and
citations. Mütalaa finds legislation, statutes, codes, regulations, public court
decisions, case law and Turkish Revenue Administration (GİB) private tax rulings.

Use it for questions about rent and eviction, employment and dismissal,
consumer rights and refunds, contracts and debts, property, family and
inheritance, criminal law, administrative disputes, taxation and constitutional
rights. Research the legal basis of a right, duty, remedy, objection or appeal.
Turkish questions may use terms such as Türk hukuku, mevzuat, kanun, yasa,
yönetmelik, içtihat, mahkeme kararı or hak arama.

## Connect and research

Add the approved catalog listing through your host's supported plugin controls,
connect your existing Mütalaa account through OAuth and start a new chat. A ZIP
or GitHub release is not catalog approval. Development imports follow the host's
supported plugin-import flow; desktop success does not establish phone support.
The existing remote server is https://mutalaa.tr/mcp.

- Find the current wording of article 347 of the Turkish Code of Obligations.
- Find and read a Yargıtay decision about rent adjustment.
- Find a GİB private tax ruling about VAT withholding on business premises rent.

Five Turkish workflow skills and four Turkish reference files are bundled.
They load through the host's skill mechanism without MCP resource reads or
access to the user's filesystem. Explanations follow the user's language;
Turkish source terms, tool names and official identifiers retain their spelling.

## Sources and limits

Supported public court sources include Yargıtay, Danıştay, regional appellate
civil courts, first-instance civil courts, the kanun yararına bozma collection,
and the Constitutional Court (AYM), including constitutional review and
individual applications. A GİB private ruling is an administrative view, not a
court judgment. Criminal appellate and regional administrative court searches,
private UYAP files and foreign-law-only research are outside this release.

Mütalaa is a legal research aid, not a lawyer or a guarantee of complete,
historical or immediately updated coverage. Read relevant source texts before
relying on search results. Do not send identifying or confidential case details.
Anonymization is guided by model/client instructions, not an automatic server
sanitizer. OAuth, quotas and source availability still apply.
