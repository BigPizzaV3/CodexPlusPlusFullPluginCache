# Use marketing references

Call `search_marketing_intelligence` with a repository, focused query, and up to five results. Supported repositories cover email templates, competitor ads, scaling ads, brand identity, tactical case studies, campaigns, and growth playbooks. Select a relevant result and call `get_marketing_intelligence_preview` with its repository and ID.

Use sanitized text and any low-resolution visual as inspiration. Identify the relevant hook, structure, or audience insight and create original content grounded in the user's brand and verified product facts. Do not copy another brand's claims or imply a preview grants rights to its original assets.

Search and preview tools return results directly. Respect quota and access errors. No tool exports raw source HTML, original media, storage paths, private adaptation instructions, or the corpus. Do not attempt to retrieve those through workspace tools or invented operations.
