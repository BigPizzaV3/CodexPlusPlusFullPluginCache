# Primed Coaching Terminology

- **Daily Coaching Decision (DCD)**: The canonical, versioned decision record produced by Primed determining today's coaching stance and recommendation.
- **Planned Session**: The scheduled workout created by the athlete's training plan.
- **Adapted Session**: A planned session that has been adjusted by the coach based on recent evidence (e.g. fatigue, sleep debt, or recovery state).
- **Rest / Recovery Day**: A day with no prescribed strenuous training, allowing physiological adaptation.
- **Training State**:
  - `productive`: Training load is effectively stimulating fitness gains without excessive fatigue.
  - `maintaining`: Workload is sustaining current fitness levels.
  - `overloaded`: Acute fatigue accumulation exceeds recovery capacity; adaptation or rest is recommended.
  - `recovery`: Active reduction in volume/intensity to shed accumulated fatigue.
- **Recovery Status**:
  - `green`: Strong physiological recovery indicators.
  - `amber`: Moderate physiological stress or suboptimal sleep/recovery.
  - `red`: Significant fatigue accumulation or physiological strain.
