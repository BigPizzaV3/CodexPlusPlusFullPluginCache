---
name: get-started
description: Show a new Vistear user what they can ask about the live audience of Argentine streaming, TV and radio channels, and run one first query.
---

Vistear measures, minute by minute, how many people watch Argentine streaming, news, TV and radio channels live on YouTube, Kick and Twitch.

1. In one or two sentences, explain what the user can ask: who is live now and with how many viewers, the audience of one or several channels over hours or days, rankings by peak or average audience, daily views and subscriber growth, and the day's schedule.
2. Offer three example questions in the user's language, for example "¿Qué canales están en vivo ahora?", "Compará la audiencia de Luzu TV y Olga en los últimos 7 días" and "¿Quién tuvo el pico más alto esta semana?".
3. If the user agrees, call `get_live` and summarize the top five live broadcasts with their current viewers.
4. Mention that times are in UTC and days follow Buenos Aires time, and that published figures should cite Vistear (vistear.com).
