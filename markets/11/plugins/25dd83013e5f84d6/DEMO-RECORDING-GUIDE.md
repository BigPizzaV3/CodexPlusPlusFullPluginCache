# 1–2 Minute Demo Recording Guide

Record a single uninterrupted ChatGPT screen capture after the Jetopia plugin is connected. Keep the demo to about 100 seconds. Use the test records and clearly marked test contact details only. Do not show real guest information or pay a deposit.

| Time | Show | Enter this prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Jetopia app name and the connected MCP app in ChatGPT. Briefly introduce it: “Jetopia helps request private driver tours in Jeju. Quotes come from Jetopia’s pricing system, and staff review booking requests.” | — |
| 0:10–0:25 | Vehicle comparison card | “We are 4 people with 2 large suitcases, arriving at Jeju Airport on November 20, 2026. Which vehicle do you recommend?” |
| 0:25–0:43 | Quote card with total, deposit, and balance | “Please give me an exact quote for a full-day tour on November 20, 2026, for 4 people in a Staria, picked up at Jeju Airport. We will not visit Udo.” |
| 0:43–0:55 | Tour list or the assistant’s accurate no-match explanation for the nature preference | “What Jeju tour options can you show me? I especially enjoy nature and scenic places.” |
| 0:55–1:15 | Booking request result; keep the request number visible, hide contact details | “I want to submit the quote. My name is TEST. My phone is [test phone number], and my WhatsApp ID is [test WhatsApp ID]. I am staying at [test lodging]. I agree to the privacy notice and terms. Please submit the request.” |
| 1:15–1:28 | Status card | “Please check request [request number]. The phone number is [same test phone number].” |
| 1:28–1:40 | Scope boundary response | “Can you book me a hotel in Seoul?” |

End by showing that the Seoul hotel request was declined as outside Jetopia’s Jeju scope. Do not show a payment checkout or claim that the booking is confirmed immediately.
