# ClarioMoney

Connect your authorized ClarioMoney business account to search bookkeeping records, save invoice drafts and prepare supported changes for confirmation.

## Access and permissions

OAuth with PKCE and dynamic client registration. The user signs in, selects a business and approves permissions. Credentials and reviewer login details are not included in this package. Reviewer credentials must be provided in the private Review details section of the portal.

## Review environment

A dedicated business named CLARIOMONEY DEMO - DATOS FICTICIOS contains synthetic data only. Draft BOR2026/00001 totals EUR 121.00; a separate sample movement is EUR 121.00. The draft has not been issued or reconciled as paid. No real business memberships or banking credentials are supplied to the reviewer.

## Verification scope

The hosted OAuth/MCP endpoints were tested over HTTPS with a dedicated technical client, including permission denial and revocation. The recording shows the actual ClarioMoney UI with synthetic data. It is not a recording of a ChatGPT conversation or evidence of a completed OpenAI review. Complete the portal connection, tool scan and interactive cases before final submission.
