# PART ONE — THE DIAGNOSIS

The order of this diagnosis is canonical. **Ownership came first. Debt issuance came second.**

## 1.1 The first capture: enclosure

Long before corporate charters or double-entry bookkeeping, there was a simpler rule: finders keepers. Whoever arrived first claimed the land, the water, the trees, the animals, eventually the people. Force hardened into custom. Custom became law. Law learned to dress itself as morality.

The Earth began as commons. Game A emerged when pieces of that shared world were taken from the commons and recorded on private ledgers — first through violence, then through something more durable: language, accounting, and law.

Once ownership could be recorded outside human memory, the game became permanent.

**Money got enclosed too.** It began as a way to *measure* value. It became a thing to *own*. The scorekeeper joined the game as a player, and the instrument that was supposed to record who had what turned into one of the things worth having. Debt issuance made this worse. It did not cause it.

**Capitalism doesn't begin with markets. It begins with ownership positions that generate ongoing claims.**

Rewards moved away from those who did the work and toward those who controlled access. Resource owners collected profit. Landowners collected rent. Everyone else paid to live.

**Corporations** made capture immortal. A fictional person that can own, contract, and live forever. Capture no longer reset each generation. It compounded.

**Nations** made capture legal. Property rights, corporate charters, banking regulation, contract enforcement. Nations do not merely manage economies. They construct the game board.

## 1.2 The second capture: the ledger

If ownership captures space, credit captures time.

Money is not a thing. Money is credit — a number on a ledger, an accounting entry tracking claims on society's resources.

**Permanent credit**, created by governments. No repayment obligation. Circulates until deleted through taxation.

**Temporary credit**, created by banks. Borrowed into existence, repaid with interest, destroyed on repayment.

The same purchasing power. Completely different futures. Debt-based money reaches forward in time, converting future activity into present obligation.

Sovereign credit, meanwhile, flows overwhelmingly to ownership positions. The state doesn't capture the future. **Assets do.**

## 1.3 The cage and the key

**Everything is locked up behind private ownership, and money is the only key. But to get the key, you must sell your present — labour — and borrow your future — debt.**

## 1.4 The time-capture stack

Money isn't time. **Money is enforceable claims on other people's time.**

A billionaire has the same twenty-four hours as everyone else. What they have is other people's hours.

**Present time, through wages.** You sell your hours. The value they produce doesn't return whole. Profit, rent, interest, and tax take portions. The residual compounds.

**Future time, through debt.** Unborn hours pre-spent, sold forward to a bank that created the money with a keystroke.

**Past time, through inherited wealth.** The hours of dead generations, stored in titles and trusts, still extracting from the living.

Wealth in this system is a portfolio of enforceable demands on human hours.

## 1.5 The Copernican inversion

Capital sits at the centre of our economic universe. Everything orbits it.

But the actual sun is **Credit** — the ability to create purchasing power, authorise activity, make futures possible. Not credit as in debt. Credit as in *credo*: belief, trust, a shared promise.

**Productive capital is real and necessary.** Transformed matter, knowledge, infrastructure, tools, buildings. Group Ledgers hold capital. What Creditism rejects is not productive capital but the elevation of accumulated financial claims into the source and ruler of value.

We've been orbiting the wrong centre.

# PART TWO — THE ARCHITECTURE

## 2.1 The Three Reclaims

**Reclaim our Credit.** We create credit for the benefit of ourselves in communities, for our own purposes, from our own activities.

**Reclaim our Commons.** Common capital moves from private to communal ledgers. Ownership becomes stewardship, held by cooperatives, councils, communities, and commonwealth ventures.

**Reclaim our Purposes.** Communities choose their purposes, credit flows toward them, and the currency is deleted as it is spent. The purposes of credit creation can never again be captured for the barren purpose of money for money's sake.

## 2.2 Flow currency

The current intended design has three broad flow families:

- **Universal:** Personal Credit and Community Credit.
- **Earned contribution:** Personal Credit for verified work, learning, caregiving, and participation.
- **Bonus:** Personal Credit and Community Credit issued for recognized outcomes. Both belong to the current intended architecture; calculation and distribution protocols remain open.

Universal Income and contribution remain the two regular Personal Credit flows. Bonus sits alongside them. One outcome may support both Bonus PC for people and Bonus CC for groups through separately governed issuance. The grouping does not supply an issuance formula or prove the fairness of its calibration.

**Separate basis-recovery issuance is a bounded accounting event, not a fourth broad flow family.** Ordinary mature Exchange disposition is bounded by remaining recognized basis and realized buyer expenditure. Produced output follows the working cost-priority and recovery constraints below, regardless of how its sale is arranged. AYU Phase 2's Exchange instead transfers AYU Credit under its separate rule at `03`, *the two rails*.

### The deletion and transfer rule — canonical block

> **Scope: mature Creditism.** Both venues operate in Credit. AYU Phase 2 follows separate transitional rules at `03`, *the two rails*.
>
> **The Marketplace.** The primary production-and-consumption circuit: labor-only production, commissioned or made-to-order production, virtual input procurement, actual PC/CC-funded inputs, mixed production, and eligible consumption. Prices are exclusively in Credit. **The buyer's Credit deletes in full; it does not transfer to the producer.** Separately authorized labor recognition and eligible basis recovery are distinct issuance events. Production can begin by contract before an item exists, with production, delivery, acceptance, and settlement recorded under the applicable production-accounting rules.
>
> **The Exchange.** Disposition and reallocation of already-held goods and assets, including assets with zero remaining basis. Any personal or community account holder may list an eligible held thing at whatever price the market will bear. **The buyer's Credit deletes in full.** Seller Credit is issued separately, no more than the lesser of realized buyer expenditure and remaining recognized basis. A gifted bicycle with zero basis may sell at any negotiated price; no seller-side basis issuance occurs and its entire price deletes. A community may later dispose of a commissioned desk through this venue.
>
> **The venue test is functional.** Positive basis, a remaining beneficial sale claim, and whether an item has previously sold are not universal mature venue tests. The beneficial-claim distinction remains relevant to AYU's transitional rails. Choosing an Exchange transaction cannot bypass recognized production-cost priority for produced output. Ordinary later disposition does not charge completed historical production costs a second time.
>
> **Credit takes the form of the account holding it.** Personal accounts hold and receive Personal Credit. Community and group accounts hold and receive Community Credit. One measure, two forms. A sale between account types changes the form, and passing through an account never turns Community Credit into personal purchasing power.
>
> **The quantity arithmetic.** Any actual seller or basis-recovery Credit is issuance and enters the issuance side of the net-supply equation. **Net deletion for the sale = buyer Credit spent − actual separate seller/basis-recovery issuance.** The ordinary Exchange ceilings are settled; the working production-recovery constraints below also cannot issue more than buyer expenditure. A below-basis price does not guarantee full-basis reimbursement. Worker PC and Bonus issuance are recorded independently; a virtual revenue or cost entry creates no Credit.
>
> **Basis is remaining recognized eligible expenditure, not an unchanged original purchase price.** Recovery reduces remaining basis; the same expenditure cannot be recovered twice. A virtual acquisition or use cost alone creates no recoverable Credit basis or independent ownership. Opening basis for transition-era assets, allocation among outputs, improvements, depreciation, inventory, auctions, timing, and detailed posting remain open.
>
> **Credit is held until spent.** Personal Credit and Community Credit both persist. Periodic issuance adds to the balance. Neither lapses, resets, expires, nor accrues interest. **What persists is a balance, not a claim.** A held balance earns nothing, obliges nobody, and creates no claim on anyone's future output. It buys what is offered, on the terms of whoever offers it, and where supply is genuinely limited a larger balance still competes for it. This is what *Credit does not become capital* means: accumulating a balance is permitted, accumulating a claim is not.
>
> Creditism does not permit or recognise Credit lent at interest, pledged as collateral, invested for autonomous return, or inherited as concentrated financial power. Depending on the token architecture selected, some of those activities may remain technically possible outside the network, on systems AYU does not control. AYU does not recognise external loans, liens, collateral claims, interest arrangements, or token holdings as conferring membership, governance rights, productive-asset ownership, or any other in-network right. Whether such activity should be technically restricted remains open.

| | Capitalism: circulatory debt | Creditism: flow credit |
|---|---|---|
| How created | Banks create it as loans; requires debt | Created through existence and participation; no debt |
| Who controls | Private banks, central governments | Decentralised; flows directly to individuals |
| At point of use | Passes to next owner; accumulates through chains | Buyer Credit deletes in both venues; the mature Exchange separately issues seller Credit within basis and buyer-expenditure bounds |
| Hoardable as power | Yes — the core driver of inequality | Not recognised in-network |
| Inheritable as financial power | Yes — concentrates dynastic wealth | Not recognised in-network |
| Relation to debt | Is debt; interest compounds forever | Zero debt; interest-free, tax-free |
| Relation to labour | Workers sell labour to survive | Labour earns Credit; work is chosen |

## 2.3 Why Credit must not become capital

If credits could accumulate indefinitely and be lent, pledged, and inherited as power inside the system, they would become capital again. Within a generation or two the same story would re-run on different infrastructure. New ledger, same hierarchy.

A vote does not become capital because a vote is gone once cast. That property is what keeps democracies from collapsing into rule by accumulated political wealth.

**Deletion is what makes Credit democratic rather than dynastic.**

## 2.4 Personal Credit (PC)

Every person receives a baseline, called **Universal Income** in plain language. This is recognition that you have a right to life. Earth's resources, the infrastructure built by past generations, accumulated knowledge — this is your inheritance. It does not stop at any age and is not reduced by age.

Above the baseline, you earn more through participation: working, learning, creating, caregiving. Exceptional contribution earns Bonus.

Contribution recognition uses the **Standard Pay Scale**. The governed base recognizes the work at its demonstrated level. **Working proposal:** that base supplies the applicable Pay floor, with bounded negotiation above it. Total flows also differ because hours and contributions differ. **Equal categorically. Fair quantitatively.**

### The Standard Pay Scale — canonical block

> **The Standard Pay Scale is the public, democratically revisable schedule for recognizing verified contribution through Personal Credit issuance.** Verification and the public rule remain necessary.
>
> **Working proposal: the governed scale provides the applicable Pay floor, with bounded bilateral negotiation above it.** This applies to cooperative and Enterprise production and to individual proprietors. Negotiation cannot reduce recognition below the applicable floor and does not grant unrestricted PC issuance authority. Actual compensation is separately authorized PC issuance; the buyer's payment does not transfer into the worker's account.
>
> **Five factors set the base rate:** skill, responsibility, difficulty, risk, and experience. Experience means demonstrated proficiency, not years elapsed. Bargaining power and geography set nothing.
>
> **Recognition takes three forms.** Time-based rates where duration is the contribution: care, teaching, monitoring, ongoing maintenance. Fixed activity or Quest rates where the task is defined and completing it is the contribution. Milestone rates where the work is a project delivered in stages. The form follows the work, not preference. Where paying by the hour would reward taking longer, the work is priced by activity or milestone instead.
>
> **The scale prices the work. It does not price the outcome.** Outcomes are recognised through Bonus.

**Working proposal.** The scale keeps the three-level structure first proposed in the 2022 book: Personal or Amateur at 1 to 10 Credit, Professional at 10 to 20, Expert at 20 to 30. Those ranges apply per hour, per activity, or per milestone according to the work. They are not automatically hourly. The book's category menu carries forward as a starting menu, not a settled one. Every rate, band, boundary, and premium requires modeling, simulation, and AYU testing before adoption. This is lineage carried forward as a live foundation, not an archive record.

### Compensation authorization, price, and buyer feedback

**Open protocol.** Agreement on compensation and authority to issue Credit are distinct. The authorized issuer or approver, premium limits, contribution verification, collusion checks, dispute handling, and appeals remain open. The negotiated premium is contribution recognition, not a fourth regular Personal Credit flow.

**Base Pay, Pay premium, and sale premium are different.** The five governed factors provide equal base recognition for verified work at the same demonstrated level. An authorized negotiated Pay premium is the bounded working proposal above that base, explicitly superseding the blanket no-negotiation rule. A sale premium is realized only through an actual completed sale; an unsuccessful commission realizes no sale premium, while verified labor and authorized Pay premiums may still be recognized. No sale premium or negotiation itself authorizes PC issuance. Contractual disputes and performance failures may be addressed through applicable arbitration; verified reputation and future commissions may be affected. Detailed premium authorization and arbitration procedures remain open.

**Working proposal: compensation determines economic cost; the sale price does not determine compensation.** The applicable Standard Pay floor and any authorized above-floor negotiation establish compensation. That compensation becomes part of the economic cost of production and informs the producer's asking price. Buyer demand determines whether the offering sells and its actual clearing price. A sale below cost, or no sale, does not retroactively reduce recognized labor. Costs reflected in an asking price are not guaranteed to be recovered.

**Before work**, skill, demonstrated past proficiency, reputation, relationships, quoted compensation, and price inform buyer and contributor choices. Choosing workers with different authorized compensation levels changes production costs and can change the quoted price. **After work**, verified outcome information and customer feedback can affect reputation, future opportunities, and purpose-dependent Bonus assessment.

**The five base factors remain skill, responsibility, difficulty, risk, and experience.** Reputation is not a sixth factor; experience continues to mean demonstrated proficiency. The exact interaction of past performance, demonstrated proficiency, reputation, those five factors, above-floor premiums, and Bonus remains open. Feedback is evidence, not automatic issuance authority. Workers must not be automatically rewarded or penalized solely on unverified ratings, and lack of reviews does not justify recognition below the applicable floor. Verification, malicious-review protection, and appeals remain open.

**Transitional necessity multiplier.** During transition, roles that are essential and hard to keep continuously staffed may carry a published multiple of the standard rate, paid to everyone doing that work. The scale prices the work. The multiplier prices the shortage. It is not a fourth tier and not a permanent premium: as more people become willing and qualified, it steps down. What triggers the step-down is open. See `05` C1.

**The necessity multiplier and a negotiated premium do different jobs.** The published multiplier responds to a transitional shortage and applies under a governed rule to everyone doing the relevant work. The proposed premium permits bounded above-floor discovery without putting housing or Universal Income at stake. Their interaction remains open; no additive or multiplicative formula is specified here.

In mature Creditism, PC is not transferable, giftable, lendable, or usable to command another person. Only individuals spend PC, on the Marketplace and the Exchange. Eligible separate basis recovery follows the production or ordinary-disposition rules above and below; no buyer balance moves to the producer or seller. AYU Phase 2 transfer is described separately at `03`, *the two rails*.

### Personal Credit at death — canonical block

> **Personal Credit is not inheritable, and a person's remaining balance ends at their death.** The reason is fairness in the flow rather than management of the quantity. Nothing about how much Credit a person held should arrive as advantage to anyone else, and the system has no need of inherited Credit: every person's own issuance begins with their existence.
>
> **Things pass. Credit does not.** A person may name whoever they choose to receive what they owned. **A thing received that way carries a basis of zero**, exactly as a gift does, so its new holder has the use of it and nothing else: on resale they are credited nothing and the whole sale price deletes. Housing follows its own rule, which is continuity of occupancy rather than title — canonical block at `04`, *housing stewardship*.
>
> Community Credit is covered separately at 2.5: at death all of it goes to the three community accounts, whatever its origin.

**A person may buy a thing and give it away.** Only people can do this, and only with things bought using Personal Credit. The gift may go to anyone, a person or a group or a community. **A gifted thing carries no Credit basis.** Its new holder has the use of it and nothing else: if they later sell it in the Exchange, they are credited nothing and the whole sale price deletes. **Gifting carries limits**, because unlimited gifting is how favour becomes leverage. That there will be caps is settled; what they cap — total value, number of gifts, concentration by recipient, or some combination — is not, and like every parameter here it stays revisable. Nothing moves the other way — a community does not gift its inventory to a person. It lends it, which is what checkout is for.

*How the baker gets paid:* verified labor is recognized through actual PC issuance, separately from the buyer's payment. Under the working proposal, the applicable Pay floor and authorized above-floor negotiation establish compensation. That labor cost, materials, and other applicable costs inform the asking price; demand determines whether the product sells and its realized price. The buyer's Credit does not transfer to the baker, and including a cost in the quoted price does not guarantee its recovery. The proprietor input-recovery protocol remains open below.

## 2.5 Community Credit (CC)

While PC serves individuals, CC funds collective needs. It replaces taxation entirely.

Taxation is redistribution — taking from one person to give to another in a system that never distributed properly to begin with. Creditism skips step one.

CC flows to communities in proportion to their populations, at multiple scales. Citizens decide democratically how to allocate it. Every person also receives a personal CC allocation to direct toward projects and groups they support. **Only group accounts spend CC.**

### Personal Community Credit — canonical block

> **A person's Community Credit is theirs to direct.** They may hold it, direct it to a group or a project, delegate the choice to someone they trust to choose better, give it to another person, or wager it where wagering is permitted. It stays Community Credit through every one of those moves. **It never becomes personal spending power.** However many hands it passes through, it is spent at the end by a group or community account, on collective provision, and nothing flows back to whoever directed it.
>
> **Directing and delegating are different acts.** Project-directed unspent funds subject to return conditions are settled first, alongside other enforceable funding terms. General group-directed funding may carry different obligations. A returnable Regional grant to a Local Enterprise returns to the Regional source, not automatically to Local. Remaining unrestricted group balances return according to the governing community arrangements. Credit delegated is authority lent, and it can be recalled. Credit given away is given. Recovered CC inherits the restrictions of the expenditure that created its basis; the original agreement governs whether recovery is retained, redirected, or returned. Posthumous project-refund treatment remains open and creates no CC inheritance right.
>
> **At death, all Community Credit a person holds** goes to the three community accounts, whatever its origin. Issued, gifted, won, pooled, or otherwise acquired — the origin does not matter once it is held, because CC is not inheritable personal wealth. By default it divides among Local, Regional, and Global **in the same proportions used to issue CC across those three levels**, unless the holder made a permitted selection. What bounds a selection, how it is made, and when it takes effect are open.

**Personal Community Credit democratises minority backing.** A project most people will not fund can still be built if enough individuals choose to direct their equal shares toward it. This is one function venture capital currently performs, and under capitalism the minority able to perform it is the minority holding wealth. Here every person holds the same quantum of it. **It is one function, not the whole institution.** Specialist screening, staged release, monitoring, portfolio spread, and the discipline to stop a failing project are all elsewhere, and some of them are undesigned. Do not say personal CC replaces venture capital.

**People can also concentrate that power voluntarily.** Someone trusted to allocate well may end up directing a great deal of Credit belonging to other people. That is not a defect — good judgement earning trust is the mechanism working, and it produces **allocative authority without personal purchasing wealth**, which is a different thing from capital and should not be described as capital returning. What safeguards attach to it, if any, is open, and nothing here implies a numerical cap is required. Any that are eventually adopted are revisable institutional mechanics rather than constitutional rules. See `05` C2.

**This is why full Creditism has no Venture Creditism.** Community Credit already funds groups, projects, and ventures democratically, with an equal share directed by every person. Venture Creditism is an AYU mechanism, built because AYU operates inside capitalism without the complete Community Credit system. It is not part of the destination. See `03` §11.

### How communities direct Community Credit — canonical block

> **Creditism does not prescribe one procedure.** No Local, Regional, or Global community is told how it must decide where its Community Credit goes. Direct vote, elected representatives, participatory budgeting, specialist assessment panels, delegated voting, staged release against milestones, a standing draw ceiling for a group — all are available, none is required, and communities are meant to build, test, copy, and discard them. This is the same relationship capitalism has with corporate law, bankruptcy courts, and procurement rules: institutions built inside an architecture to solve recurring problems, differing between places, without anyone concluding the architecture is missing.
>
> **A draw ceiling is one such form.** A community may authorise a group to draw Community Credit up to an agreed limit, in tranches or against milestones, before the group has produced anything. This is not a loan and it creates no debt: the group holds no negative balance, owes nothing back, and pays no return. It is a community committing its own spending power to its own purposes ahead of the work.
>
> **What no procedure may do.** Whatever form a community chooses, it may not let Credit accumulate as a claim on anyone, pay a return to the authorising body, route Community Credit into personal spending power, convert a group's use of an asset into title, disenfranchise people the decision materially affects, or entrench itself against recall or revision by the members who made it. Authority must respect subsidiarity and affectedness. Flows stay auditable.
>
> **Projects stay visible and stoppable.** A funded project's outcomes and costs remain observable — in detail to those involved, in aggregate where personal privacy requires it under the block at 2.13. **There is no private accounting inside a public or group ledger.** Continuation remains reviewable, funding can be withdrawn through whatever procedure applies, no project may entrench the institution that funds it, and higher-tier recourse stays available. That is the floor. Review intervals, thresholds, who evaluates, what counts as failure, and what follows from it are institutional design choices and are meant to vary. Where a community abuses its authority, recourse runs to the Regional or Global scale. That recourse exists in principle; its procedure, standing, thresholds, and remedies are undesigned.

### Project failure — canonical block

> **A failed project is not a lost balance.** What it consumed and what it left behind are different things, and the crude sentence *the project cost 100,000 and failed* does not survive translation into Creditism.
>
> **Retained.** Durable assets bought with Community Credit sit on the Community Ledger. When the group disbands they return to availability, and they can be checked out by another group or listed in the Exchange and sold. **Nothing is stranded by rule.** Whether a particular asset is stranded in fact is the next category, and it turns on whether anyone else can use the thing.
>
> **Consumed.** Materials, energy, and ecological drawdown are gone. So are the years people spent. Those are the real cost and no accounting recovers them.
>
> **Stranded.** Specialized capital nobody else can use may retain little useful resource value. A reusable machine may remain valuable to another group; a purpose-built rig may have few alternative uses. Reuse value or realized Exchange price does not guarantee recovery of its acquisition cost. Separate Credit issuance is bounded by buyer expenditure and remaining eligible basis, which may already be zero.
>
> **Foregone.** The community has fewer useful things than it could have had. This is the discipline. A community that chooses badly does not go bankrupt — it gets less, year after year, relative to the path it could have taken. The loss is denominated in things, hours, and ecological headroom, not in Credit.
>
> **What does not happen is the part worth stating.** Recognition of the work is not contingent on the project's solvency. Contribution is recognised through issuance under the Standard Pay Scale, and Universal Income and housing do not depend on the venture succeeding, so a failed project does not take a contributor's means of living with it. **The design hypothesis that follows** — that this lets a society run more experiments than one where failure ruins the people who executed it — is plausible and unproven, and should be stated as a hypothesis rather than a result. What is not in doubt is that the discipline has to come from visibility rather than from ruin. Bankruptcy is loud. A community quietly getting less is not, and making that visible is what the ledgers are for.

**Actual worker pay is PC issuance.** A group does not transfer its CC balance to workers as wages. **Working proposal:** labor is nevertheless an economic cost in virtual production accounting. Separating the payer from the cost keeps labor visible without requiring a project to raise workers' PC compensation in advance.

### Production accounting — working proposal

**Three records stay distinct.** Real Credit accounts record balances, actual expenditure, issuance, deletion, and transfers where permitted. Virtual P&L records realized economic revenue and attributable costs. Resource accounting records actual materials, energy, labor time, ecological effects, capacity, and assets used or retained. A virtual entry is neither an actual payment nor evidence by itself of resource use.

**Enterprises operate through group accounts within the relevant community ledger and stewardship arrangements.** A governed Enterprise Input Allowance and virtual performance accounting work together to constrain continued activity. Exact technical nesting, expenditure and contribution-issuance authority, allowance calculation, thresholds, enforcement, replenishment, suspension, restoration, and review remain open.

**Labor-only Enterprises have inputs: labor is an input.** A design studio or care service records recognized labor costs, realized output prices, virtual revenue, virtual profit or loss, and continued productive authorization. An Enterprise cannot bypass virtual economic accountability by avoiding physical materials or by using actual CC instead of virtual procurement.

**Actual Marketplace sales generate virtual revenue at the realized selling price.** Recognized labor, authorized negotiated Pay premiums, consumed inputs, energy, applicable capital costs, and other production costs enter virtual costs. Virtual revenue minus virtual costs produces virtual profitability or loss. Virtual revenue and profit are not spendable CC. Worker PC, Bonus, and basis-recovery issuance are actual issuance events and count independently in the quantity arithmetic.

**Virtual procurement and authorized actual Credit expenditure can be combined.** An actual purchase may also enter virtual P&L as a cost, but it is one expenditure, not a second payment. Where Enterprise A supplies an input to Enterprise B, A can record virtual revenue and B records the corresponding input cost; those paired entries do not themselves require actual CC transfer. Actual Credit purchases remain possible under their own rules.

**Virtual limits and community authorization are distinct constraints.** Repeated output whose realized value does not support its economic costs can exhaust virtual ordering capacity. Exhaustion does not automatically withdraw actual CC spending authority: available CC may still be used under its authorization rules, including for inputs or productive assets. Accountability still applies, and the relevant community may separately withdraw use rights or operating authorization when continued operations are unjustified. Virtual loss creates no conventional debt and does not claw back already-issued worker PC. No automatic closure or revocation protocol is specified.

**Closure follows funding terms before unrestricted balances.** Returnable project-directed funds and other enforceable terms are settled first. Remaining unrestricted balances return under the governing community arrangements, ordinarily to the appropriate Local, Regional, or Global account. A returnable Regional grant is returned to Regional even where the Enterprise operates locally. Recovered CC retains the restrictions of its originating expenditure and follows that agreement. Assets remain community-held and subject to reassignment or applicable Exchange rules; prior recognized labor is not retroactively canceled. Posthumous refunds remain open, without CC inheritance rights.

**Both capital routes remain explicit.** Eligible authorized actual PC/CC expenditure can establish recoverable basis under an open recording protocol; Enterprise/group assets remain community-held and under use rights. Recording acquisition or use costs virtually creates no Credit basis or ownership. Capital expenditure must not be counted twice in virtual P&L. Once historical acquisition expenditure is fully recovered, that same expenditure is not continually charged again. Genuine subsequent maintenance, energy, labor, and other costs remain distinct, and resource use stays recorded. Allocation, depreciation, inventory, valuation, timing, and detailed posting remain open.

### Production basis recovery — working mechanism with confirmed constraints

> **Recognized labor and virtual production costs take priority over recovery of actual PC/CC input basis.** Production-cost priority applies to produced output regardless of how the sale was arranged or recorded. Choosing an Exchange transaction cannot bypass it. Ordinary later resale of an existing asset does not charge completed historical production costs a second time.
>
> **Eligible actual input expenditure may establish recoverable basis.** Separate recovery issuance for output sold is bounded by all three: remaining recognized basis; the portion attributable to that output, including applicable capital use; and the positive remainder of realized selling price after other attributable recognized production costs, including labor and virtual inputs. The basis portion being considered for recovery is not subtracted again as an additional “other” cost. These are ceilings, not a guarantee that the maximum will be issued.
>
> **Recovery reduces remaining basis, and no expenditure is recovered twice.** Basis-recovery issuance is not additional virtual sales revenue. Buyer Credit deletes; authorized recovery issuance is recorded separately in the quantity arithmetic. A virtual entry alone creates no basis.
>
> **Fully recovered acquisition expenditure has zero remaining basis.** A community-held oven fully recovered through restaurant sales has zero remaining basis on later Exchange disposition. Community ownership, another Enterprise using it, and continued productive usefulness do not reset that basis. Genuine subsequent costs remain distinguishable.
>
> **Allocation methods, depreciation, inventories, timing, authorization, and detailed posting remain open.** The confirmed cost priority and three constraints replace automatic recovery of an original input outlay; they do not settle every capital, mixed-input, refund, or sale protocol.

**Owner-supplied illustrations, not general Pay placements.** A carpenter spends 15 PC on wood, receives 30 PC for recognized labor, and sells the chair for 30 Credit. The buyer's 30 deletes. Recognized labor consumes the price's recovery capacity, leaving zero wood-basis recovery; the carpenter is 15 PC better off, from 30 issued minus 15 spent. An Exchange label cannot issue another 15 merely because the wood was bought. This example specifies one case, not a Standard Pay rate for carpenters.

For an output with 45 Credit of other attributable costs and 15 of attributable actual-Credit basis: sale at 30 allows zero recovery; at 50, at most 5; at 60, at most 15; at 75, still at most 15. The examples illustrate the confirmed ceilings and do not select a final payout or allocation protocol.

**Performance follows purpose.** Profitability can inform purpose-dependent Bonus, but it is not every Enterprise's sole purpose. Local, Regional, or Global communities may deliberately allocate bounded CC to socially useful work whose direct sales do not cover its costs. That is governed resource allocation, not necessarily an accounting defect. Preserve historical production and performance records. Longer-term analytics and possible retrospective Bonus remain future design work.

### Individual proprietors and one-person Enterprises

An individual proprietor remains distinct from a group Enterprise. A proprietor may incur actual PC input expenditure, receive separately authorized PC for verified labor, and undertake commissioned or listed production. Total quoted price normally reflects materials, recognized labor, and other applicable costs. Authorized compensation enters cost and informs the asking price; demand determines sale and realized price. Quoted costs are not guaranteed to be recovered.

The earlier automatic Exchange-based maker recovery route is superseded by the production-cost priority and bounded recovery mechanism above. Buyer Credit does not transfer to the worker or proprietor. Separate recovery is limited to eligible, attributable, remaining actual expenditure and residual price capacity; exact authorization, allocation, posting, mixed PC/CC, cancellation, and refund protocols remain open.

One-person Enterprises are permitted under governance and CC safeguards. Directing CC to a group in which the director is a member is subject to bounds preventing community spending power from becoming private spending power; exact caps remain open. A group account does not convert CC into PC by having one member.

## 2.6 Bonus

**The activity/outcome distinction is settled.** The Standard Pay Scale recognizes verified activity through its five base factors. Bonus recognizes verified outcomes. The same outcome must not be counted twice as base recognition and Bonus.

**Current intended Bonus architecture; calculation and distribution protocols open.** One successful outcome may generate both Bonus PC for people and Bonus CC for groups through separately governed issuance. Bonus CC is actual additional community spending capacity for expansion, equipment, machinery, infrastructure, and other authorized productive needs; it stays CC. Individuals, including proprietors, and group Enterprises remain distinct recipients. Allocations are intended to be mathematically bounded and fair across Local, Regional, and Global scales; formulas have not been supplied or validated, and capture resistance is not proven. The PC and CC calculations may differ. Recipient accounts, calculation, weighting, timing, authorization, distribution, and possible retrospective recognition remain open.

Assessment follows the purpose of the activity: quality and social or ecological outcomes, with virtual production performance where relevant. Verified outcomes and customer feedback may inform assessment; unverified ratings alone do not automatically reward or penalize workers. Virtual profitability is not a universal condition for Bonus. Friendly competition among comparable activities may support learning. Pools and rates are designed in the open, coding out exploits and coding in *imploits* — value that flows inward to the group rather than outward to an owner.

**Bonus CC is not automatically issued to cover operating losses or replenish an exhausted virtual allowance.** A community may separately support an unsuccessful but socially necessary activity through ordinary CC governance.

**Any actual Bonus PC or Bonus CC issuance counts in the Credit quantity arithmetic.** A virtual result, a review, or an evaluation score creates no Credit by itself. The detailed issuance and calibration arrangements remain open. Neither Bonus CC nor mature Community Credit is operational in AYU Phase 1; preparation and any later trial arrangements are described at `03`, *what AYU is calibrating*.

## 2.7 Monetary inflation and price inflation

### Canonical block

> **Creditism distinguishes monetary inflation from price inflation.**
>
> Monetary inflation is an increase in the total quantity of Credits outstanding. Price inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level. The two are connected. They are not identical.
>
> Credit issuance increases the quantity of Credits. Credit deletion decreases it.
>
> **Net Credit change = Credits issued − Credits deleted**
>
> If issuance and deletion balance, the Credit supply is stable. If issuance exceeds deletion, it inflates. If deletion exceeds issuance, it contracts.
>
> **Deletion can make monetary inflation impossible during any period in which total deletion equals or exceeds total issuance.** It cannot make all price increases impossible, because prices also respond to scarcity, productive capacity, popularity and fashion, shifting needs, local shortages, ecological limits, crisis conditions, demographic change, inventory, quality, geographic demand, and governance decisions.
>
> Creditism does not claim to abolish changing prices. It makes the quantity of currency an explicit, visible, and democratically governed design choice. That differs from capitalism, which structurally depends on recurrent monetary expansion over time through sovereign issuance and bank-created debt, although the supply can and does contract during particular periods. The effects appear downstream, through asset prices, consumer prices, debt burdens, and unequal purchasing power.
>
> The central design task is not simply to issue against production. It is to govern the relationship among issuance, deletion, available goods and services, ecological limits, inventories, demand, and prices.

**In capitalism the quantity of money is a byproduct** of private debt expansion and sovereign issuance. Capitalism structurally depends on recurrent monetary expansion over time, although the supply can and does contract during particular periods — deleveraging, credit contraction, crisis. The consequences appear downstream, through asset prices, consumer prices, debt burdens, and unequal purchasing power. **In Creditism the quantity of Credit is a deliberate design variable**, visible and governed.

Where real unused capacity exists — idle workers, vacant housing, underused skills, shuttered facilities — credits flow toward goods the economy could already produce but had no monetary demand for. Production rises to meet demand. Where bottlenecks exist, prices reveal the constraint.

**Prices become a signal about real limits, not a moral argument against giving people access to life.**

**Both mature venues sit inside this equation.** Buyer Credit deletes. Ordinary Exchange seller issuance is bounded by remaining recognized basis and buyer expenditure; produced-output recovery follows the additional cost-priority and attribution constraints above. **Net deletion for a sale = buyer Credit spent − actual separate seller/basis-recovery issuance.** Worker and Bonus issuance are independent events in total Credit issued. Virtual revenue, profit, and cost entries create no Credit; recovery creates no extra virtual sales revenue. Exact posting and payout protocols within the confirmed constraints remain open.

**The levers exist on both sides.** Personal Credit has three **issuance dials**: Universal Income, contribution, and Bonus. Any authorized negotiated contribution premium counts within contribution issuance; it is not a fourth regular PC flow. Community Credit has two channels: the equal per-person allocation, and the flows to community accounts at Local, Regional, and Global scale. The existing dials and channels remain adjustable. The proposed addition of Bonus CC must count any actual issuance in total Credit issued; its calibration and relationship to the existing CC channels remain open. Separate seller/basis-recovery Credit also counts as issuance, but follows remaining-basis, buyer-expenditure, and applicable production constraints rather than an adjustable issuance percentage, so it is **not** an issuance dial. On the other side, prices adjust how fast Credit deletes — a markup accelerates deletion, a markdown slows it — which allows the quantity outstanding to be held stable without touching the issuance rates at all. Bonus and participation volumes move smoothly in aggregate and feed prices directly. Balances persisting rather than lapsing does not change this arithmetic; it means the stock is what gets governed rather than the flow alone. **A balance ending at a holder's death leaves outstanding supply and counts on the deletion side of the equation.** That is bookkeeping and not an instrument: the quantity is governed by the dials above and by pricing, never by mortality. **Every figure anywhere in the pack is preliminary and requires modeling and simulation**, both for transition starting amounts and for steady-state rates.

Governing the issuance–deletion relationship at scale is an open design task. See `05`, C1.

## 2.8 The Three Forbidden Invisible P's

**Purposes.** *What is our activity for?* Hand this to a market and it answers "whatever pays." A school becomes a credentialing operation. A hospital becomes a chronic-care business. A song becomes a thirty-second hook.

**Pay.** *What is an hour of a human life worth?* The market sets it by what the buyer can get away with, given the seller's desperation. The home health aide sits near the bottom.

**Products.** *What is worth making?* "Worth" gets defined as profitable under extraction logic. Durable goods, slow food, beautiful buildings, cures rather than management, knowledge held in common — all fall into the blind spot.

**What replaces each:**

**Purposes** return to the people living the life.

**Pay** separates into Universal Income and transparent, democratically revisable recognition for verified contribution. The **Standard Pay Scale** uses skill, responsibility, difficulty, risk, and demonstrated experience for its governed base. **Working proposal:** that base is the applicable floor, with bounded bilateral negotiation above it. Neither the buyer nor the enterprise can use negotiation to remove the floor or issue unrestricted PC. Housing and baseline livelihood remain independent of the bargaining outcome.

**Products** get decided by a layered process — markets choose between competing offerings, Community Credit funds what communities decide is worth making but markets can't see.

**The market is bounded.** Communities choose public purposes, livelihood is guaranteed, and contribution recognition has a governed base. Markets help discover prices and choose among competing offerings. The proposed above-floor negotiation permits further discovery within published bounds whose exact design remains open.

## 2.9 Housing

Housing is a right, not a commodity.

On transition, homeowners become caretakers of their primary residence. Mortgages, insurance, and property taxes are eliminated. Equity in the primary residence converts to Personal Credit at market value less the mortgage owed. A person keeps up to three residences in total. A released vacation residence converts at what its holder put in, equity at purchase plus the principal they paid; a released rental converts at equity at purchase alone, because tenants paid its mortgage. Canonical block at `04` 5.1a.

Access works through a **stewardship rating system**, not a price system. A home carries a rating (1–10) reflecting quality and desirability. Each adult from the age of eighteen holds a **Housing Level**, beginning from caretaking, community contribution, and Personal Credit. Your level determines which rated homes are available to you. A person may also spend Credit to raise their level, directly or by improving the home they hold. The dwelling itself is never priced. What Credit buys is the qualification, and it deletes on use like any other purchase.

**One primary residence per person, plus up to two vacation stewardships.** The number is a response to scarcity and moves with availability. Ledgers are personal and never combine. Housing Levels stay individual too, but they stack for choice: adults who share a home qualify together for a better dwelling than either could take alone, because sharing uses less housing rather than more. Canonical block at `04` 2.5.

Planetary mobility is an aspiration of the mature system, subject to availability, ecological limits, local governance, and legitimate affectedness. When you select a new house, the previous one becomes available to someone else.

The housing layer is called **The Abodium**.

## 2.10 The Commons

Natural resources, land, and major infrastructure move onto shared ledgers as they are reclaimed, transferred, or placed under stewardship.

This is not government owning everything. It is collective stewardship.

Corporations and government agencies become member-run cooperatives and councils, with free lease to equipment and facilities, accountable to communities rather than shareholders. Unused assets already placed under the relevant community's stewardship may be made available under community rules, like a library.

**Private property still exists** — your home, belongings, creative works. What ends is speculative hoarding of land and resources.

## 2.11 Markets, prices, and pay

**The Marketplace** is the primary production-and-consumption circuit, including labor-only, commissioned, virtual-input, actual PC/CC-funded, and mixed production. Prices are exclusively in Credit, and buyer Credit deletes rather than transferring to the producer. Verified labor and eligible bounded basis recovery are separately authorized issuance.

**The mature Exchange** reallocates already-held goods and assets, including zero-basis assets, at negotiated market prices in Credit. Buyer Credit deletes; separate seller issuance cannot exceed remaining recognized basis or buyer expenditure. Completed historical production costs are not charged again on ordinary later disposition. Produced output cannot bypass cost priority through an Exchange label. AYU's transitional Exchange instead permits agreed combinations of value and transfers AYU Credit.

**The Standard Pay Scale** has five governed base factors and equal base recognition for the same verified work at the same demonstrated level. Its applicable floor plus authorized bounded above-floor negotiation is the working proposal. Compensation enters economic cost and informs asking price; demand determines realized price. Base Pay, authorized Pay premium, and realized sale premium stay distinct. Before-work discovery and after-work verified outcomes/feedback inform choices, reputation, future work, and purpose-dependent Bonus; reputation is not a sixth factor. Exact interactions and authorization remain open.

**The Balance** makes prices and Credit volume legible together. Realized prices need not cover economic costs. Worker livelihoods do not depend on an Enterprise covering them.

**Working production accounting** keeps actual expenditure, virtual P&L, and resource use distinct. Labor is an input even without materials. The Enterprise Input Allowance and performance accounting constrain continued activity; actual CC procurement does not avoid accountability. Virtual allowance exhaustion and a community decision withdrawing authority remain different events. Recovery of eligible actual input basis follows remaining basis, attributable basis, and positive residual selling-price capacity after other recognized production costs; recovered expenditure is never recovered twice. Virtual profit is not CC, and recovery is not extra virtual revenue.

## 2.12 The Ledger of Life

The economy is not beside the biosphere. It is inside it.

Standard economics treats ecological consequence as "externality" — a euphemism meaning *we know this matters, but it doesn't fit our model.* A ledger with no column for atmospheric stability or soil fertility will drive those toward zero automatically.

### Canonical block

> The Ledger of Life maps the planet's living systems: land, waterways and bodies, species, habitats, ecosystems, biomes, natural resources, ecological condition, and the existing ownership or stewardship claims attached to them. Mapping records what exists. It does not change legal ownership, does not make an asset available, and does not place it under stewardship. Mapping, ownership, availability, and stewardship are four separate fields. Local, Regional, Global, and Group Ledgers record only what those entities have actually reclaimed, been given, made available, operate, or steward.

**It does not put a price on nature.** It records what is taken, how it should be taken, what rates are sustainable, and what labour is owed to restore. It feeds the Quest Board.

Five mechanisms put ecology into the accounts:

1. **Resource accounting** — real use tracked at community and bioregional scale; issuance calibrated against tracked limits
2. **The Ledger of Life** — the open data layer
3. **Stewardship rather than ownership** — obligations built into the legal relationship
4. **Restoration as economic activity** — replanting, rewetting, regenerating earn full Personal Credit
5. **The biosphere at the table** — ecological consequence as primary input to major CC allocations, with representatives for affected ecosystems and proxies for future generations

**Intraction** is the regenerative counterpart to extraction.

## 2.13 The Visible Hand

Fifteen elements: The Ledger of Life · The Quest Board · Local, Regional & Global Ledgers · Group Ledgers · Personal Private Ledgers · Community Credit · Personal Credit · Bonus · The Marketplace · The Exchange · The Standard Pay Scale · The Balance · The Abodium · The Sphere · The Talking Stick

### Privacy — canonical block

> Public and group flows are auditable. Personal ledgers — identities, balances, possessions, and individual transaction details — remain private, accessible only to the individual or family, except where disclosure is voluntarily authorised or legally required. How aggregate transparency and personal privacy are technically reconciled remains open.

---

# PART THREE — WHAT CREDITISM IS NOT

**Not central planning.** Radically decentralised. Communities decide locally.

**Not state socialism.** Creditism differs from state-socialist systems centred on government ownership and centralised allocation: the state does not own the means of production and does not direct production quotas. It also differs from conventional market socialism, by separating access, contribution, commons stewardship, and Credit issuance into distinct distributed mechanisms rather than routing them all through firm ownership. Different categories, different failure modes.

**Not charity or dependency.** Everyone receives baseline credit as a birthright and earns more through contribution.

**Not anti-market.** Creditism creates genuine free markets by removing debt, rent extraction, and artificial scarcity.

**Not anti-innovation.** Removing capital gatekeeping and guaranteeing baseline security unleashes it.

**Not unlimited money printing.** Issuance is tied to real productive capacity and ecological limits, and offset by deletion.

**Not a compromise between left and right.** An attempt to preserve what each tradition saw clearly while avoiding what each became.

---

# PART FOUR — WHAT THE SYSTEM NO LONGER NEEDS

Interest-based money creation by banks. Profit-driven insurance. Tax collection bureaucracies. Landlordism and rent extraction. Stock markets and speculative finance. Most advertising machinery. Planned obsolescence. Bullshit jobs. Dynastic wealth.

**On war:** Creditism aims to remove the major economic incentives for war — resource capture, debt enforcement, and mass desperation. It does not claim to eliminate ideological, territorial, political, or interpersonal conflict. Conflict doesn't end because people become angels. The claim is narrower: the game stops rewarding it.

---

# PART FIVE — CAPTURE RESISTANCE

Capture is architecture, not just corruption.

**Distributed authority.** No single decision point controls issuance, housing allocation, or protocol rules.

**Transparent flows, protected privacy.** Corruption that once required accounting fraud becomes readable. Reconciliation with personal privacy remains open.

**Credit that cannot compound into power.** Deletes in the Marketplace. No interest rate to harvest, no asset class appreciating autonomously.

**Participation as default, gates as accountable.** Exclusion requires positive justification; inclusion does not.

**Exit must remain real.** Everything is open-source. A captured implementation can be forked.

---

# PART SIX — OPEN EDGES

**Transition at scale.** How a mixed Creditist/capitalist economy functions. How prices form across the boundary. How capital flight is managed.

**International coexistence.** Trade, currency exchange, legal recognition, migration.

**Large, long, uncertain projects.** Community Credit already selects and mobilizes collective provision at multiple scales, without a private investor-capital prerequisite. Verified labor is a source of separately authorized PC issuance, not wages raised in advance and transferred by the project. Its economic cost remains visible in virtual production accounting. Available actual CC, legitimate expenditure authority, draw limits, the working Input Allowance, and virtual performance accountability remain relevant. Communities may deliberately fund bounded socially necessary activity despite losses. Real capacity — people, skills, materials, energy, ecological headroom, facilities, time, and coordination — still binds. Institutional effectiveness, long-term capacity coordination, detailed accounting and authorization remain open; no universal investor-financing or long-project protocol is invented. See `05`, *open questions*.

**System resilience under correlated failure.** A drought, a sector collapse, a pandemic, a common supplier, a shared strategic mistake. Reserves, mutual aid between communities, Regional and Global response, prioritisation, emergency issuance, ecological rationing. Better understood as resilience and emergency coordination than as a missing financial instrument, and genuinely open.

**Cultural transition.** What happens to identity for people who built their self-understanding around accumulation, and how that gets supported without contempt.

**Pilot verification.** The core claims are design hypotheses supported by analogous evidence — Mincome, the Alaska Permanent Fund, Ostrom's commons research, the WIR's operation since 1934. None of these are Creditism. They are evidence the design is not crazy. Verification must come from actual pilots at increasing scale.

Full register in `05`, Section C.
