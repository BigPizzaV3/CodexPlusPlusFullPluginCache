## A3. Credit

**Credit / CREDIT** — the unit of exchange in mature Creditism. **Credit** in prose, **CREDIT** as display form. Plural **Credits** is correct. *Settled.*

### The deletion and transfer rule — canonical block

> **Scope: mature Creditism.** Both venues operate in Credit. AYU Phase 2 follows separate transitional rules at `03`, *the two rails*.
>
> **The Marketplace.** The primary production-and-consumption circuit: labor-only production, commissioned or made-to-order production, virtual input procurement, actual PC/CC-funded inputs, mixed production, and eligible consumption. Prices are exclusively in Credit. **The buyer's Credit deletes in full; it does not transfer to the producer.** Separately authorized labor recognition and eligible basis recovery are distinct issuance events. Production can begin by contract before an item exists, with production, delivery, acceptance, and settlement recorded under the applicable production-accounting rules.
>
> **The Exchange.** Disposition and reallocation of already-held goods and assets, including assets with zero remaining basis. Any personal or community account holder may list an eligible held thing at whatever price the market will bear. **The buyer's Credit deletes in full.** Seller Credit is issued separately, no more than the lesser of realized buyer expenditure and remaining recognized basis. A gifted bicycle with zero basis may sell at any negotiated price; no seller-side basis issuance occurs and its entire price deletes. A community may later dispose of a commissioned desk through this venue.
>
> **The venue test is functional.** Positive basis, a remaining beneficial sale claim, and whether an item has previously sold are not universal mature venue tests. The beneficial-claim distinction remains relevant to AYU's transitional rails. Choosing an Exchange transaction cannot bypass recognized production-cost priority for produced output. Ordinary later disposition does not charge completed historical production costs a second time.
>
> **Credit takes the form of the account holding it.** Personal accounts hold and receive Personal Credit. Community and group accounts hold and receive Community Credit. One measure, two forms. A sale between account types changes the form, and passing through an account never turns Community Credit into personal purchasing power.
>
> **The quantity arithmetic.** Any actual seller or basis-recovery Credit is issuance and enters the issuance side of the net-supply equation. **Net deletion for the sale = buyer Credit spent − actual separate seller/basis-recovery issuance.** The ordinary Exchange ceilings are settled; the working production-recovery constraints below also cannot issue more than buyer expenditure. A below-basis price does not guarantee full-basis reimbursement. Worker PC and Bonus issuance are recorded independently; a virtual revenue or cost entry creates no Credit.
>
> **Basis is remaining recognized eligible expenditure, not an unchanged original purchase price.** Recovery reduces remaining basis; the same expenditure cannot be recovered twice. A virtual acquisition or use cost alone creates no recoverable Credit basis or independent ownership. Opening basis for transition-era assets, allocation among outputs, improvements, depreciation, inventory, auctions, timing, and detailed posting remain open.
>
> **Credit is held until spent.** Personal Credit and Community Credit both persist. Periodic issuance adds to the balance. Neither lapses, resets, expires, nor accrues interest. **What persists is a balance, not a claim.** A held balance earns nothing, obliges nobody, and creates no claim on anyone's future output. It buys what is offered, on the terms of whoever offers it, and where supply is genuinely limited a larger balance still competes for it. This is what *Credit does not become capital* means: accumulating a balance is permitted, accumulating a claim is not.
>
> Creditism does not permit or recognise Credit lent at interest, pledged as collateral, invested for autonomous return, or inherited as concentrated financial power. Depending on the token architecture selected, some of those activities may remain technically possible outside the network, on systems AYU does not control. AYU does not recognise external loans, liens, collateral claims, interest arrangements, or token holdings as conferring membership, governance rights, productive-asset ownership, or any other in-network right. Whether such activity should be technically restricted remains open.

*Settled as principle. Operational mechanics open.*

> Do not say Credit "never circulates," "cannot accumulate," or "cannot be lent or collateralised" without this qualification. These are rules of what Creditism permits and recognises, not claims of technical impossibility on systems AYU does not control. Balances persist. Separate seller issuance is bounded by actual buyer expenditure and remaining recognized eligible basis, with production-cost priority for produced output.

**Personal Credit (PC)** — flows to people through the universal baseline, verified contribution, and authorized Bonus. Only individuals spend PC, on the Marketplace and the Exchange. Inside the system, PC is not transferable, giftable, lendable, or usable to command another person. Buyer Credit deletes; authorized worker PC and eligible seller/basis-recovery issuance are separate events. Balances persist until spent. **Personal Credit is not inheritable: a person's remaining balance ends at their death**, for fairness in the flow rather than to manage quantity, and the removal counts on the deletion side without becoming a lever. A thing left to someone carries zero basis, as a gift does. Canonical block: *Personal Credit at death*, at `02`. *Settled 4 Sep 2026; new production recovery is a working mechanism with confirmed constraints.*

**Community Credit (CC)** — democratic community spending capacity: equal personally directed allocations and flows to Local, Regional, and Global community accounts, with separately governed Bonus CC under the working proposal. **Only group/community accounts spend CC.** Enterprises operate through group accounts within community ledgers and stewardship arrangements, including permitted one-person Enterprises. CC never becomes private spending power. Balances persist; funding restrictions continue to apply. *Established community principles are settled; new Bonus and production mechanisms have open protocols.*

**Directing Community Credit** — a person may hold their share, direct it to a group or project, delegate the choice, give it to another person, or wager it where permitted. It remains CC. Returnable project-directed funds and other enforceable funding terms are settled first; remaining unrestricted balances follow governing community arrangements. A returnable Regional grant to a Local Enterprise returns to Regional. Recovered CC inherits the restrictions of the expenditure that established its basis. Delegated authority is recallable; a gift is given. At death, all CC held goes to the three community scales, in issuance proportions by default unless a permitted selection was made. Holder-selection bounds and posthumous project refunds remain open; no CC inheritance right is created. Self-member group direction is bounded to prevent private spending power; exact caps remain open. Canonical block at `02`, *Personal Community Credit*.

**Gifting (personal property)** — only a person may gift, and only a thing bought with Personal Credit, to anyone. Distinct from transferring Community Credit, which is covered above and carries no settled limit. A gifted thing carries no Credit basis: its holder has the use of it, and on resale is credited nothing while the full price deletes. **That caps apply is settled; what they cap is not** — total value, number, concentration by recipient, or a combination, all revisable. Community-held things are lent to members through checkout, never gifted to them. Personal Credit itself is never gifted. Canonical text at `02` 2.4 and `04` 2.6. *Settled in principle; the limit is open.*

**Bonus / Bonus Credit** — recognizes verified outcomes, distinct from Pay for verified activity. One outcome may support separately governed Bonus PC for people and Bonus CC for groups. Bonus CC is actual additional community spending capacity for authorized productive needs, not virtual profit and not an automatic loss subsidy or allowance replenishment. Allocations are intended to be mathematically bounded and fair across Local, Regional, and Global scales. Formulas have not been supplied or validated; capture resistance is not proven. Calculation, distribution, timing, and authorization remain open. Actual issuance counts in Credit quantity arithmetic. *Current intended architecture; working mechanisms, not operational AYU Phase 1 flows.*

**Imploit** — the inverse of an exploit. Where an exploit extracts value outward to an owner, an imploit returns it inward to the group that produced it. Bonus pools are designed to code out the first and code in the second. Coined by Eli Capracotta. Always gloss it on first use; it is not a word the reader will know. *Settled as a term of art.*

**Credit deletion** — removal of the Credit unit from circulation at the point of use. What prevents Credit becoming capital. *Settled as principle. Operational protocol open.*

**Credit persistence** — Personal Credit and Community Credit are stocks as well as flows. A balance is held until it is spent. Periodic issuance adds to it. Nothing lapses, resets, expires, or accrues interest, and there is no use-it-or-lose-it rule at any scale. A community can therefore save toward something large without any special mechanism. *Settled 19 Aug 2026.*

**Clearing** — a transaction completing with no onward claim left behind. In Creditism a purchase clears because the unit deletes. *Settled 31 July 2026.*

### Deletion and clearing — canonical block

> **Deletion describes the unit. Clearing describes the transaction. They are not synonyms and must not be used as though they were.**
>
> Use **deletion** for anything about quantity: supply, issuance, monetary inflation, and why Credit cannot become capital. Clearing has no arithmetic. The net-quantity pair is issued minus deleted.
>
> Use **clearing** for the purchase outcome in the two-event frame: the transaction completes and leaves no onward claim.
>
> **Bridge line: purchases clear because units delete.**
>
> *Deletes* leads in public writing. *Clearing* names the transaction outcome and belongs in sentences about a purchase. The unit is never the subject of *clears*.
>
> **For a general reader, deletion carries the explanation on its own, and the consequence must then be stated in ordinary language: the buyer's Credit does not transfer to the seller.** This is required, not optional. Dropping the word does not drop what the word was carrying. The preferred general-reader form states three facts and names no transaction: “You get the bread, your Credit deletes, and none of the Credit you spent goes to the baker.” Separately authorized issuance follows its own rules and can be explained when relevant. Where the transaction outcome does need naming for a reader who has not asked for mechanism, **completes** is available. It is ordinary English, carries no definition, and is not a third term of art.
>
> With a finance-literate reader, state the deletion mechanism before the word *clearing* appears. In banking, clearing means a buyer's payment settling into a seller's account, which is exactly what does not happen here. Conventional payment systems clear too. The difference is how a purchase clears, not whether it does: both clear, one transfers, one deletes.
>
> Never used of Credit: *burned*, *expires*, *retired*, *vanishes*, *evaporates*, *zeroed out*. *Destroyed* only when drawing the comparison to money-destruction in the banking literature. *Deleted* never applies to AYU Points.
>
> **A third sense of *clearing* exists in the pricing work and is unrelated to both of the above.** In market design, claims *clear* when competing requests for the same resource resolve together on a shared cycle. The boundary-pricing protocol uses the word that way throughout. It describes a scheduling rhythm, not what happens to a unit and not what happens to a purchase. No relationship between the senses should be inferred or constructed. If asked, say the word is doing different work in the two places and explain each on its own.

**Capital** — in Creditism, capital means productive capital: transformed resources, infrastructure, tools, buildings, knowledge. Group Ledgers hold capital. What Creditism rejects is not productive capital but the elevation of accumulated financial claims into the source and ruler of value. *Settled.*

> **This is a term of art, not a general definition.** The ordinary economic sense is broader and includes financial claims: stocks, bonds, balances, derivatives, expected income streams. Creditism narrows the word deliberately, because the argument turns on separating what is produced from what is claimed. When discussing capital as the world uses the term, say so. The narrow definition is not a denial that financial capital exists.

### Monetary inflation and price inflation — canonical block

> **Creditism distinguishes monetary inflation from price inflation.**
>
> Monetary inflation is an increase in the total quantity of Credits outstanding. Price inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level. The two are connected. They are not identical.
>
> Credit issuance increases the quantity of Credits. Credit deletion decreases it.
>
> **Net Credit change = Credits issued − Credits deleted**
>
> If issuance and deletion balance, the Credit supply is stable. If issuance exceeds deletion, it inflates. If deletion exceeds issuance, it contracts.
>
> **Deletion can make monetary inflation impossible during any period in which total deletion equals or exceeds total issuance.** It cannot make all price increases impossible, because prices also respond to scarcity, productive capacity, popularity and fashion, shifting needs, local shortages, ecological limits, crisis conditions, demographic change, inventory, quality, geographic demand, and governance decisions.
>
> Creditism does not claim to abolish changing prices. It makes the quantity of currency an explicit, visible, and democratically governed design choice. That differs from capitalism, which structurally depends on recurrent monetary expansion over time through sovereign issuance and bank-created debt, although the supply can and does contract during particular periods. The effects appear downstream, through asset prices, consumer prices, debt burdens, and unequal purchasing power.
>
> The central design task is not simply to issue against production. It is to govern the relationship among issuance, deletion, available goods and services, ecological limits, inventories, demand, and prices.

> **Terminology note.** In this pack's technical writing, "inflation" ordinarily means monetary inflation: an increase in the quantity of currency units. Price movement is the downstream effect, not the definition.
>
> **In public answers, do not silently redefine the question.** Most people asking "will Creditism cause inflation?" mean rising prices. Distinguish the two meanings, answer the one the questioner intends, and where that is unclear, explain both briefly. Answering the monetary question to someone who asked the price question reads as evasion, because it is.

### Credit form and transferability — canonical block

> **Working proposal:** AYU Credit will be the in-network exchange unit for AYU members. Depending on the technical architecture selected, the same unit may also be represented by a transferable token capable of moving to non-member wallets and trading externally.
>
> External transfer or market trading would not grant AYU membership, governance rights, verified participation status, or access to recorded in-network exchange. Those functions require an AYU account.
>
> Because Credits are issued freely through the network rather than sold as the price of membership, most people seeking their actual utility have reason to join rather than remain external holders.

> Do not say Credits are necessarily confined to the network. Say their **economic utility and recognised exchange system are centred inside AYU**, even if the token can technically leave it. The architecture is not decided; it is expected to be resolved as Phase 1 concludes.

### In-network reference value — canonical block

> **Working proposal:** AYU Credit is intended to use a diversified in-network reference value based on a basket of leading national currencies rather than a single national currency or a market price. The basket is intended to reduce single-currency volatility and to provide a widely understood conversion reference.
>
> It does not guarantee stable purchasing power, neutrality, or immunity from changes in the currencies composing it. A basket can still rise, fall, and lose purchasing power together with its components.
>
> The index is not a redemption guarantee and not a fixed external market price. Where an external market price exists, it may sit above or below the index. Composition, weighting, update process, and governance remain open. The index creates no external convertibility or redemption right.

### The ratchet — design hypothesis, not a financial claim

> **Design hypothesis.** As more goods and services become available inside the network, members may obtain more real value using Credits internally at the index reference than by selling them externally. A gap between an external price and internal purchasing power would create an arbitrage incentive to acquire Credits externally and use them inside. The design expects that pressure to move an external price toward the index over time. **No claim is made about price direction, and no advantage to holding is claimed or implied.** Whether the mechanism behaves as described is untested.
>
> **This is a hypothesis to be modeled and observed. It is not a forecast, a promise, or an offer.**
>
> **What bounds the formal entitlement route.** AYU Credit is transferable and balances can accumulate. The recorded member entitlement bounds formal surrender under that route through verified direct issuance and the applicable Early Member bonus; acquired balances do not enlarge that entitlement. An AYU Credit used in a Marketplace purchase deletes and cannot then be surrendered. An AYU Credit transferred in the Exchange remains outstanding in the recipient's account. A surrendered Credit is withdrawn and cannot then be spent. The same unit cannot be both deleted at purchase and surrendered for conversion. Broader transition treatment of holdings outside this route is under review; no additional conversion right is approved. The ratchet remains an untested hypothesis, not proof that every transition risk is resolved.
>
> **It bounds formal surrender under the entitlement, not every legitimate purchase.** Cross-boundary spending is intended to be generally possible under the common denomination and applicable settlement rules. What a balance can buy is subject to availability, not automatically restricted by the formal conversion entitlement. Broader above-entitlement transition treatment is under review, and no extra right is approved. No holding advantage or external price direction follows from this hypothesis. See *the two rails* and *across the boundary*, at `03`.

> **See `00`, Section 9.1.** This mechanism may never be presented as a reason to acquire, hold, or refrain from selling Credits. Never forecast a price. Never say Credits will become worth more.

### Member transition entitlement — canonical block

> **Working proposal.** At jurisdictional activation, a qualifying AYU member may convert AYU Credits into full Creditism Credit under a personal, non-transferable **transition entitlement**.
>
> **The entitlement belongs to the member, not to particular token units.**
>
> A member's entitlement consists of:
>
> 1. the cumulative AYU Credits **directly issued to that member** by the network under verified issuance rules, plus
> 2. a fixed additional **Early Member transition bonus**, under a common cutoff and qualification rule. The end of the Early Member designation at Phase 2 does not remove the member's verified participation history; the cutoff, qualification rule, and bonus amount remain open.
>
> Credits received from another member in exchange, or acquired from another holder, increase a member's balance. They do not increase the entitlement. The entitlement is generated by AYU's issuance record.
>
> At activation:
>
> **Credits converted = the lesser of the member's remaining entitlement and the AYU Credits they surrender for conversion.**
>
> The origin of the surrendered units does not matter. They may have been issued, received in exchange, or acquired from another holder. Issuance history sets the entitlement; available balance sets how much of it can be exercised. Token fungibility is preserved.
>
> Surrendered AYU Credits are withdrawn and replaced one to one with full Creditism Credit.
>
> **Credits above the entitlement do not convert in that activation.** They remain usable in the wider AYU network wherever AYU continues to operate. One jurisdiction adopting Creditism does not end AYU everywhere else, and activation should not become a forced liquidation event.
>
> The entitlement is **non-transferable, single-use, and consumed when exercised.** Every conversion is recorded in the global AYU membership ledger, so the same entitlement cannot be exercised again in another adopting jurisdiction.
>
> **The entitlement is calculated without reference to Founding Circle membership, Partner status, Patron status, or donation amount.** Founding Circle participants qualify for the Early Member bonus only because they are verified Early Members, under the same rule as everyone else. Their founding recognition is recorded in AYU Points.
>
> **AYU Points do not convert into Credit.** They are a participation record and create no redeemable claim. Points may verify membership and participation facts that bear on eligibility — historical verified Early Membership, for one. Ending the designation does not remove that history. No number of Points produces a corresponding number of Credits, at any rate, fixed or otherwise.
>
> Open: which issuance categories count, the Early Member bonus amount, the eligibility cutoff and qualification rule, residency and jurisdictional scope, treatment of inactive accounts, and the relationship to the universal starting allocation. All require modeling before Phase 3.

### Across the boundary — current intended architecture; settlement protocols open

> **One nominal Credit denomination.** AYU Credit and mature Credit have nominal 1:1 denomination. Their issuance, transfer, deletion, account, and jurisdictional rules may differ. Nominal correspondence is not an external market-price promise, unrestricted account conversion, or a right to particular goods.
>
> **Cross-boundary spending is intended to be generally possible in both directions**, subject to availability and settlement rules. Identify the applicable regime and venue. AYU Marketplace prices are only in AYU Credit and eligible purchases delete it. AYU Exchange permits agreed combinations of value and AYU Credit transfers. Mature Marketplace and Exchange prices are in Credit; buyer Credit deletes, with separately authorized labor or basis recovery where applicable.
>
> **Conversion and spending are different events.** A formal member transition entitlement limits surrender through that conversion route; it is not a universal ceiling on every legitimate cross-boundary purchase. Common denomination does not automatically convert balances or entitle a holder to goods. Contributor recognition, seller/basis-recovery issuance, cross-boundary balances, restrictions, settlement, and global quantity accounting require a detailed protocol. No unrecorded duplicate issuance or deletion is permitted.
>
> **National currencies do not all convert one unit to one Credit.** The intended architecture uses agreed currency-specific rates into the common Credit denomination, conceptually comparable to different national currencies entering the euro. AYU's proposed basket supplies an in-network unit reference. Currency rates, historical valuation windows, basket composition, and governance require agreement and modeling. Illustrations are not approved numerical conversion rates.
>
> **Open settlement design.** Cross-boundary availability, account eligibility, jurisdictional authority, treatment of transferred holdings, verification, basis, and reconciliation of issuance/deletion remain open. The common nominal denomination settles none of those procedures by itself.

**Transition status note.** Broader treatment of holdings above the formal entitlement is under architectural review. The existing entitlement remains in force; no additional conversion entitlement, valuation formula, or financial benefit has been approved. Formal conversion and legitimate cross-boundary spending are different events.

### The Standard Pay Scale — controlled vocabulary

The public, democratically revisable schedule recognizes verified activity through five base factors: skill, responsibility, difficulty, risk, and experience. Experience is demonstrated proficiency, not elapsed tenure. Equal base recognition is preserved; reputation is not a sixth factor. Pay survives as one of the Three Forbidden P's.

**Working proposal:** the governed scale is the applicable floor, with bounded authorized negotiation above it. This explicitly supersedes the blanket no-negotiation rule. Negotiation alone grants no PC issuance authority. Base Pay, authorized negotiated Pay premiums, and a sale premium realized through an actual sale are distinct. Verified labor and authorized Pay premiums may be recognized even where a commission produces no completed sale; the sale premium is not realized. Authorization, limits, verification, appeals, and detailed arbitration remain open.

Compensation enters production cost and informs the asking price. Demand determines whether output sells and its realized price; sale price does not set worker compensation, and quoted costs are not guaranteed recovery. Buyer Credit does not transfer to workers. Before work, skill, proficiency, reputation, relationships, compensation quotations, and price inform choices. After work, verified outcomes and customer feedback may affect reputation, opportunities, and Bonus; unverified ratings alone do not automatically reward or penalize workers. The precise interaction remains open.

Full canonical Pay block at `02`. Its three bands and starting menu are working figures, not occupation-specific rate assignments. The temporary necessity multiplier remains governed and public, not private bargaining power setting base Pay.

### Production basis recovery — working mechanism with confirmed constraints

> **Recognized labor and virtual production costs take priority over recovery of actual PC/CC input basis.** Production-cost priority applies to produced output regardless of how the sale was arranged or recorded. Choosing an Exchange transaction cannot bypass it. Ordinary later resale of an existing asset does not charge completed historical production costs a second time.
>
> **Eligible actual input expenditure may establish recoverable basis.** Separate recovery issuance for output sold is bounded by all three: remaining recognized basis; the portion attributable to that output, including applicable capital use; and the positive remainder of realized selling price after other attributable recognized production costs, including labor and virtual inputs. The basis portion being considered for recovery is not subtracted again as an additional “other” cost. These are ceilings, not a guarantee that the maximum will be issued.
>
> **Recovery reduces remaining basis, and no expenditure is recovered twice.** Basis-recovery issuance is not additional virtual sales revenue. Buyer Credit deletes; authorized recovery issuance is recorded separately in the quantity arithmetic. A virtual entry alone creates no basis.
>
> **Fully recovered acquisition expenditure has zero remaining basis.** A community-held oven fully recovered through restaurant sales has zero remaining basis on later Exchange disposition. Community ownership, another Enterprise using it, and continued productive usefulness do not reset that basis. Genuine subsequent costs remain distinguishable.
>
> **Allocation methods, depreciation, inventories, timing, authorization, and detailed posting remain open.** The confirmed cost priority and three constraints replace automatic recovery of an original input outlay; they do not settle every capital, mixed-input, refund, or sale protocol.

**Production accounting — working proposal.** Actual sales generate virtual revenue at realized price; recognized labor, authorized Pay premiums, consumed inputs, energy, and applicable capital costs enter virtual costs. Virtual profit or loss is not spendable CC. Paired inter-Enterprise revenue and input-cost entries do not require actual CC transfer; actual purchases remain possible under separate rules and are not paid twice because they also enter virtual cost. Real expenditure, virtual P&L, and resource consumption remain distinct.

Labor-only Enterprises have labor inputs and remain subject to virtual performance accountability. The governed Enterprise Input Allowance and virtual performance constrain continued activity; avoiding materials or using actual CC does not bypass accountability. Exhaustion does not automatically revoke actual CC spending authority or close the Enterprise. A separate community governance decision may withdraw authorization or use rights. Virtual loss is not conventional debt and does not claw back recognized worker PC. Allowance, enforcement, replenishment, restoration, review, technical nesting, expenditure authority, and capital protocols remain open.

Individual proprietors are distinct from group Enterprises. Their total quote normally reflects materials, recognized labor, and other applicable costs, but exact input-recovery, cancellation, and refund protocols remain open within the confirmed production-recovery constraints. Permitted one-person Enterprises retain CC and stewardship safeguards. Socially necessary activity may receive deliberately bounded community support despite losses. Historical performance stays recorded; longer-term analytics and retrospective Bonus are future design work.

### The Ledger of Life — canonical block

> The Ledger of Life maps the planet's living systems: land, waterways and bodies, species, habitats, ecosystems, biomes, natural resources, ecological condition, and the existing ownership or stewardship claims attached to them. Mapping records what exists. It does not change legal ownership, does not make an asset available, and does not place it under stewardship. Mapping, ownership, availability, and stewardship are four separate fields. Local, Regional, Global, and Group Ledgers record only what those entities have actually reclaimed, been given, made available, operate, or steward.

**It does not put a price on nature.** It records what is taken, how it should be taken, what rates are sustainable, and what labour is owed to restore. It feeds the Quest Board.

### Privacy — canonical block

> Public and group flows are auditable. Personal ledgers — identities, balances, possessions, and individual transaction details — remain private, accessible only to the individual or family, except where disclosure is voluntarily authorised or legally required. How aggregate transparency and personal privacy are technically reconciled remains open.

## A6. Governance and property

**Commons** — resources held and governed in common by a defined community. Not an unmanaged free-for-all. Has members, rules, stewards, accountability. *Settled.*

**Stewardship** — a legal and social relationship carrying obligations: maintain regenerative capacity, coordinate with neighbouring stewards, leave the land able to do what it was doing. It differs from unrestricted ownership because it carries enforceable obligations and does not create an unlimited, freely alienable claim over the underlying commons. A stewardship position may arise through transition, succession, selection, transfer agreement, or a payment mechanism without becoming conventional ownership. *Settled.*

**Subsidiarity** — decisions made at the smallest scale competent to make them well. *Settled.*

**Affectedness** — voice follows affectedness. *Settled.*

**Property categories** — five:

| Category | Status |
|---|---|
| **Personal property** — belongings, creative works, tools, savings | Protected. *Settled.* |
| **Stewardship homes** — primary residence, plus up to two vacation stewardships | Protected. Occupancy, maintenance, and succession settled — continuity of occupancy, not hereditary title. Three residences is the ceiling, per person, from age eighteen. Vacation figure is a working proposal. See `04` 2.5. |
| **Cooperative production assets** — workshops, facilities, co-op infrastructure | Listed on Community Ledgers, checked out under community-set usage rights and time limits. Not owned by the workforce. *Settled.* See `04` 2.6. |
| **Commons resources** — land, watersheds, atmosphere, digital infrastructure, knowledge | Stewarded democratically. *Settled.* |
| **Speculative and rentier claims** — absentee landlordism, derivatives, positions commanding others' future hours | The claim dissolves; the stake converts at equity contributed at purchase. Nothing is taken without conversion. *Settled as principle; mechanism open.* See `04` 5.1a. |

**Housing Level** — the qualification a person holds for a class of home, rated against the dwelling's own 1 to 10 rating. Begins from lived record: caretaking, contribution, and Personal Credit. **Stacks for choice, never on the ledger**: adults sharing a home qualify together for a better dwelling than either could take alone, while both levels stay exactly where they were. The level is what Credit buys.** *Working proposal. Rate, ceiling, and decay open. See `04` 2.5.*

**Councils, Clubs, Co-ops** — the three participatory forms within local, regional, and global communities. *Settled.*

## C0. Closed decisions — not open questions

*These are established principles or explicitly superseded decisions. A settled constraint does not close its implementation protocol. Sections C1 to C3 record the genuinely unresolved protocols and working parameters.*

**Stewardship home succession.** **Settled 25 Jul 2026 — continuity of occupancy, not hereditary title.** A surviving person who lived in the home may claim it by swapping their current residence for it, regardless of their own Housing Level. A household that moved out earlier has no claim. Vacation stewardship does not pass at all; it ends at death and the home returns to availability. Canonical block at `04` 2.5.

**Cooperative asset framing.** **Settled 25 Jul 2026.** Not "collectively owned." Listed on Community Ledgers and checked out under community-set usage rights and time limits, returning to availability when a group disbands. Canonical block at `04` 2.6.

**Credit persistence.** **Settled 19 Aug 2026.** PC and CC both persist until spent. Issuance adds to the balance. No lapse, reset, expiration, or interest, at any scale. This had been decided and had never reached a canon file, which is what made long-horizon funding look like it needed a mechanism it does not need: a community can save.

**The Exchange rule.** **Latest owner direction supersedes the original-price and universal venue formulations.** Mature Exchange disposes of already-held goods and assets, including zero-basis assets. Buyer Credit deletes; separate seller issuance is bounded by the lesser of buyer expenditure and remaining recognized basis. Produced output additionally obeys recognized production-cost priority and the three recovery constraints at A5, regardless of venue. Ordinary later asset resale does not charge completed production costs again. Net deletion is buyer expenditure minus actual separate issuance. Ceilings are settled, not guaranteed payouts; detailed posting and basis protocols remain open. Canonical block at A3 and `02`.

**Community sales and the Exchange.** Personal and community accounts may dispose of eligible already-held goods/assets through the same mature Exchange, including zero-basis assets; no separate community-sale channel. Production cannot bypass cost priority by using an Exchange arrangement. Latest functional venue clarification governs, while detailed eligibility, basis recording, and posting remain open. Community stewardship/use rights continue. Canonical asset block at `04`.

**Institutional freedom in directing Community Credit.** **Settled 19 Aug 2026.** No procedure is prescribed for how a Local, Regional, or Global community decides where its CC goes. Councils, direct vote, delegated vote, participatory budgeting, assessment panels, staged milestone release, and standing draw ceilings are all permitted forms and none is required. Members can always revise or recall the institutions they create. Higher-tier recourse exists in principle. The constraints every form must respect are listed at `02` 2.5. This is polycentric governance by design, not an undesigned mechanism.

**Savings conversion.** **Latest owner direction explicitly supersedes literal one-unit-of-any-national-currency-to-one-Credit parity.** The intended architecture uses a common Credit denomination and agreed currency-specific conversion rates, conceptually comparable to national currencies entering the euro. Exact rates, historical valuation windows, and governance require agreement and modeling. Eligible asset recognition remains governed by the property-transition principles; this change does not authorize speculative valuation benefits for excess AYU holdings. A conversion rate is not a purchasing-power guarantee. *Common denomination approved; conversion parameters open.*

**Pensions do not carry forward.** **Changed 1 Aug 2026, superseding the v10 position.** No past redistribution claim continues. Every person receives an initial allocation of 3,000 Credit per year of age, peaking at fifty and reducing by 3,000 for each year above it, additional to all conversions. Universal Income then flows for life. Canonical block at `04` 5.1b. *Working proposal.*

## C1. Monetary and mechanical

**Phase 2 technical architecture.** Whether Credit is represented by a transferable token, and on what infrastructure. Expected to resolve as Phase 1 concludes.

**Phase 2 issuance design.** Sequencing, rates, eligibility, and deletion conditions for in-network Credit.

**Issuance rates in mature Creditism.** Monthly Personal Credit, Community Credit per citizen at each scale, and calibration method. Figures in the essays and the book are illustrative.

**Governing the issuance–deletion relationship.** The central design task. How the relationship among issuance, deletion, available goods and services, ecological limits, inventories, demand, and prices is governed and adjusted is not specified.

**Deletion mechanics in operation.** How the Marketplace/Exchange boundary is defined, policed, and audited.

**Exchange mechanics.** Mature venue function, zero-basis eligibility, buyer deletion, remaining-basis and expenditure ceilings are settled. Produced output observes labor/virtual-cost priority and attributable-basis residual constraints, including on Exchange arrangements. Opening transition basis, allocation, depreciation, repair and improvements, inventory, auctions, timing, exact authorized seller issuance, and detailed posting remain open. Ordinary later resale does not re-charge completed production costs. The earlier maker's automatic input-recovery route is superseded; do not use it to promise reimbursement. *Protocols open within confirmed constraints.*

**The personal-property gifting limit.** That gifting a thing bought with Personal Credit carries caps is settled. Their structure is not: total value, number of gifts, concentration by recipient, exceptions for ordinary family transfers, or some combination. Too loose and gifts become leverage; too tight and ordinary generosity needs permission. **This is not the limit on transferring Community Credit**, which is separate and recorded at C2. *Structure open and revisable.*

**The issuance dials.** Three broad flow families: universal PC and CC, earned-contribution PC, and Bonus PC and CC. One outcome can generate separately governed Bonus PC and Bonus CC. Allocation settings and formulas remain open. Authorized seller/basis-recovery issuance also counts in quantity arithmetic but is bounded recovery, not a fourth broad flow family or adjustable issuance percentage. Prices influence deletion, not worker-compensation authorization. *Architecture recorded; calibration open.*

**Contribution verification.** **Settled:** qualifying contribution must be verifiable. **Open:** the verification methods. Do not present peer attestation, automated ledger observation, transactional-pattern detection, reputation scoring, AI verification, or any other specific verification architecture as current design unless explicitly adopted. The architecture must also resolve scale, privacy, contestability, anti-gaming, and appeal without turning verification into surveillance. *Methods open.*

**Persistent balances against capacity.** Because balances persist, a community can save toward a large project. Saving solves who may direct resources; it does not reserve the engineers, materials, or ecological headroom that will be needed when the spending happens. Whether the design needs any forward-capacity instrument, or whether pricing and the Quest Board handle it, is open. *Open.*

**Who measures productive capacity**, with what accountability and what appeal process.

**Price formation across the boundary** in a mixed Creditist/capitalist economy. Named unresolved in the published series and unresolved here.

**Basket-index specification.** Composition, weighting, update process, governance.

**External market price behaviour.** The ratchet is a design hypothesis. Not modeled, not observed, not guaranteed.

**Entitlement calculation.** Which issuance categories count toward a member's entitlement, the size of the Early Member bonus, the eligibility cutoff and qualification rule, and whether additional recognition scales with verified active tenure. Open.

**Jurisdictional scope.** Which members qualify at a given activation — residency, citizenship, presence, or another test — and how the entitlement interacts with the universal starting allocation every resident receives under full Creditism. Open.

**Global single-use enforcement.** The entitlement must be consumed when exercised and recorded globally so it cannot be used again at a second jurisdiction's activation. The recording infrastructure and its governance are undesigned.

**Entitlement top-up by purchase.** A member holding less than their entitlement could buy Credits to fill it. This may be acceptable, since the right was earned and the ceiling was already fixed. Whether to constrain it — by cutoff, balance snapshot, or averaging period — should follow modeling rather than precede it. Open.

**The AYU Credit layer after first activation.** The established entitlement still governs formal conversion. Broader treatment of holdings above it is under renewed architectural review; no new entitlement or valuation benefit is approved. AYU may continue outside adopting jurisdictions. Separate accounting within one nominal denomination, settlement, retained holdings, and any later sunset remain open. This review must not be described as a closed speculative-conversion case.

**Scarce-access allocation.** Where supply is genuinely limited, purchasing power decides who gets it, and balances can originate in participation or in purchase. This is the beachfront-home question, which exists in Creditism independently of any external market. External acquisition sharpens it rather than creating it. **The scarcity is in access, not only in goods.** A surgeon's hour, a place in a school, grid capacity, a slot at a shared facility, an expert's attention — `02` already prices these as real limits, and none of them answers to producing more. Allocation method for genuinely scarce access is open.

**Exit-price disclosure.** A member selling Credits externally receives the market price, not the in-network reference. Early on the two may differ substantially. This is a disclosure obligation rather than a design flaw: members should understand before selling that the two are not the same number.

**Early inventory pacing.** In-network goods and services will be thin at the start of Phase 2. How demand is paced against available inventory, and what liquidity provisions exist, is open.

**What verified participation history affects.** Points do not convert into Credit — that is settled, and the canonical block states it. The open question is narrower: whether participation history affects specific eligibility or access rules beyond Early Member status, and how.

**Standard Pay Scale figures.** The three bands carried forward from 2022 are starting parameters, not settled rates. Band boundaries, the category menu, premiums, and which recognition form applies to which kind of work all require modeling, simulation, and AYU testing. Nothing here may be stated as decided.

**The 2022 annual increase.** The book's category table raises rates by a fixed amount each year of participation. That is tenure. Canon defines experience as demonstrated proficiency rather than years elapsed. Both cannot stand. The figure carries forward as a modeling input and must either be re-derived as proficiency growth or dropped.

**The necessity multiplier step-down.** What makes the multiplier fall is undesigned. Under consideration: a published coverage ratio, review on a fixed cycle, automatic decline past a stated threshold. The ceiling on the multiple, who sets it, and the appeal path are all open. Without a stated trigger the multiplier becomes permanent by inertia, which is the failure mode to design against.

**The access ratio.** The tier spread is a ratio between pay rates, not between incomes. Universal Income sits beneath it, and housing carries no rent, mortgage, property tax, or insurance. What a person at the bottom band can actually access compared with a person at the top has not been computed. That figure answers the hierarchy objection better than the tiers do, and AYU modeling should produce it. **Do not state a ratio until one exists.**

**Tax treatment of Credit.** Credit received for contributed time and spent on goods carries tax consequences. They differ by jurisdiction, change with legislation, and sharpen wherever a transferable token exists. Common Planet takes no position and offers no guidance. **The answer is that a member consults a qualified tax professional in their own jurisdiction — say that and stop.** Do not characterise likely treatment, name a category, or reason about timing or basis, for any jurisdiction, however the question is framed and whoever is asking. Naming a treatment is tax advice whether or not it is labelled as such, and here it would also presume the external market `00` 9.1 guards. *Open, and outside the pack's authority to close. Counsel question. See `00` 9.0a.*

## C2. Governance

Voting thresholds and quorum. Protocol revision procedure. Contribution verification at scale without becoming surveillance. Appeal paths for contested contribution scores. Technical reconciliation of aggregate transparency with personal privacy. Protection of minorities against local majorities.

**Safeguards on transferring and aggregating Community Credit.** Exact gifting, wagering, pooling, delegation, concentration, disclosure, and conflict-of-interest protocols remain open and revisable. Separately, directing CC to a group in which the director is a member is subject to bounds preventing community spending capacity from becoming private spending power; exact caps remain open. Permitted one-person Enterprises do not bypass this. The personal held-CC death rule remains settled; holder selection and posthumous project-refund treatment remain open.

**Concentration of allocation authority.** Community Credit can be gifted, delegated, pooled, and wagered, so a person trusted to allocate well can end up directing a large volume of other people's shares. This creates no personal wealth — nothing flows back, and it never becomes spending power — but it is real power over what gets built. Delegated Credit is recallable; gifted Credit is not. Whether limits apply, whether they attach to the permanent form rather than the delegated one, and what disclosure is required are undesigned. Naming this matters: deleting Credit prevents concentration of *capital*, and does not by itself prevent concentration of *allocative authority*. Those are different things and the pack should not blur them. *Open.*

**Formal forecast weighting — closed, not required. Decided 21 Aug 2026.** Creditism does not need a protocol that raises a person's institutional weight because their past forecasts proved right. The feedback already exists and is voluntary: people watch who allocates well, delegate their Credit to them, and stop delegating to someone whose projects keep failing. That is sufficient at the architectural level, and encoding epistemic hierarchy into the protocol would make reputation another accumulable asset. Communities remain free to keep advisory forecasting records if they want. **Do not describe the absence of a formal mechanism as a gap.** *Closed.*

**Measuring affectedness.** Governance say is proportional to how much a decision affects you. That principle sits underneath subsidiarity and the whole three-communities model, and `01` §8 credits it to Kolahi. How affectedness is actually determined is undesigned: who measures it, on what evidence, on what timescale, and what happens when someone says a decision affects them more than the measure says it does. Every allocation of voting weight in the system depends on an answer. There isn't one yet. *Open, and the largest undesigned piece of the governance model.*

## C3. Property and transition

**Fiat baseline transition option.** *Working transition option:* a jurisdiction may introduce a fiat-denominated universal baseline before full Credit activation. This could reduce inequality and coercion immediately while Credit infrastructure is prepared. It may also expand the fiat supply, affect prices, or weaken the currency's exchange value. Those effects must be modeled rather than denied, and evaluated against effects on productive capacity, household security, imports, exchange rates, and eventual Credit conversion. *Possible, not mandatory.*

**Vacation residence limit.** Two per person alongside the one primary residence, per person and from age eighteen. The number is a working figure. *Working proposal.*

**How many residences the system can actually carry.** The three-residence ceiling is a scarcity response and moves with availability. Nothing yet establishes what availability is. The working hypothesis is that a great deal of housing is currently held as an appreciating asset rather than lived in, and that the stock is therefore larger than it appears. **That is a claim about the world and it is unverified.** Vacancy rates, second-home counts, institutional ownership shares, and short-stay conversion all bear on it. Until it is evidenced, the ceiling is a guess. *Open.*

**Housing Level bought with Credit.** Rate, ceiling, and whether a purchased level decays over time are all undesigned. So is what happens to the level when a person leaves an improved home: the improvement stays with the dwelling, and the level is what travels, but nothing specifies whether it can later be spent down or reallocated. *Open.*

**Purchased levels against genuinely scarce homes.** Where two people want the same dwelling and one can raise their Housing Level with Credit, the allocation contest acquires a purchasing dimension the rating system was meant to avoid. Related to the scarce-goods item above and not resolved by it. *Open.*

**Release timing for a retained vacation stewardship.** Whether the choice to release title and convert equity is a one-time election at transition or stays open afterwards. If it stays open it is an ongoing conversion channel rather than a transition event. *Open.*

**The initial allocation schedule.** The reasoning is recorded at `04` 5.1b: the climb recognises years already lived under the old system, the taper reflects a shorter remaining span, and the amount tracks the annual logic of Universal Income. **Confirmed by Remzi 1 Aug 2026.** What stays open is the arithmetic, not the reasoning: the 3,000 rate, the crossover at fifty, and how the two measures are weighted where they meet. *Working figures.*

The proposed annual ceiling's rate remains open; no numerical rate has been publicly specified. The former numerical ceiling is withdrawn from current public design. **Whether Venture Creditism can be described without investment vocabulary.** *Return*, *at risk*, and *principal* are the plain words for the mechanism and they are also the words the de-financialisation rules keep away from AYU Credit. Internal and member-facing use is settled; the public-facing vocabulary has not been chosen, and choosing wrong here costs more than choosing slowly. *Open.*

**The stacking schedule.** How much better a dwelling a shared household qualifies for, and how rating trades against bedrooms, is a working figure. The worked example at `04` 2.5 uses two adults at Level 5 reaching a 7-rated two-bedroom, a 6-rated three-bedroom, or a 5-rated four-bedroom. Whether the relationship holds at other levels or household sizes is undesigned. *Open.*

**Allocating genuinely scarce homes.** Where several equally qualified people want the same dwelling, the tiebreak is undesigned. Under consideration: ranked top-three preferences, queueing for specific homes, prior stewards holding first claim. *Open.*

**Large, long, uncertain projects.** **Rescoped 19 Aug 2026, superseding the v12 entry**, which grouped this with specialised financial functions and read as though Creditism had no way to fund large projects. It has one. Split three ways:

*Settled — no investor-capital prerequisite.* Projects of any scale depend on political authorization and real capacity rather than private financial capital. Verified labor is a source of authorized PC issuance, not a wage transfer requiring prior investor fundraising. CC provisions collective inputs through persistent personally directed and Local, Regional, and Global balances. **This does not erase authorization, available actual CC, governed draw limits, or working virtual operating constraints.** Labor-only production also records labor costs; using actual CC does not bypass virtual accountability. Communities may deliberately fund bounded socially useful loss-making activity. Do not impose a universal save-enough-Credit-first rule; opportunity cost remains things, hours, and ecological capacity.

*Settled — funding and selection.* Only group accounts spend CC. A project the majority will not fund can still be built if enough individuals direct their own shares. No private financial ownership is required at any point. Communities choose their own procedure.

*Settled — the review floor.* Project outcomes and costs stay observable, in aggregate where privacy requires. No private accounting inside a public or group ledger. Continuation reviewable, funding withdrawable, no entrenchment, recourse available. Canonical text at `02` 2.5.

*Closed — no universal mechanism required.* **Intertemporal binding.** There is no single Creditist rule for how binding a long project must be. Terms belong to the project and the institution authorising it, and can be staged, renewed, contested, renegotiated, or cancelled under the rules that project adopted. Institutions stay revisable, members retain ultimate authority, subsidiarity and affectedness apply. What varies is which commitment institutions work in which circumstances. Do not write this as a missing architecture-wide mechanism. **Half-built things.** Ordinary case-specific asset management: finish, pause, repurpose, transfer, dismantle, sell, or abandon, according to circumstance and governance. The retained/consumed/stranded/foregone frame supplies the accounting. No universal protocol required. **Formal forecast weighting.** Closed at C2.

*Open.* Which institutional forms actually coordinate large projects well. How real capacity is coordinated across years — pricing, the Quest Board, and ordinary planning may be sufficient, or communities may find forward-capacity arrangements useful; either way it is an institutional experiment rather than a missing financial instrument. Project-specific commitment and review mechanics. How specialised capital is valued when it returns to the ledger. System resilience under correlated failure, below.

**Failure and exit procedures.** "Exit must remain real" is stated. The mechanism is undesigned. What a failed project costs is settled in kind — retained, consumed, stranded, foregone, at `02` 2.5 — and the procedure for winding one up is not.

**System resilience under correlated failure.** *Reframed 21 Aug 2026 — this is resilience, emergency coordination, and mutual aid, not a specialised financial function, and there need not be one universal insurance analogue.* The line above treats one group failing while the rest of the system absorbs it. The harder case is many failing at once: a whole sector, a whole locality, a shared supplier, a common shock. Mondragon's federation absorbed Fagor because the rest of Mondragon was healthy — `04` Stage 2. Nothing in the design says what happens when it is not. Reserves, mutual-aid obligations between communities, and the point at which a Community Credit allocation is defended or allowed to fall are all unspecified. *Open.*

**Mobility against local limits.** The design promises planetary mobility. It also promises communities the right to govern local conditions. Those collide. Housing stock, ecological carrying capacity, infrastructure, water, schools, and clinics are local and finite. What happens when more people want to move somewhere than it can absorb, who decides, and whether a community may refuse, is undesigned. Distinct from the scarce-homes tiebreak above, which assumes the applicants are already local. *Open.*

**Jubilee scope and cross-border obligations.** Transition assumes some form of debt clearance. Which debts, and treated how, is unspecified. Secured against unsecured. Pensions and annuities, which are one party's liability and another party's security. Debts denominated in a foreign currency. Obligations to creditors outside the adopting jurisdiction, who have adopted nothing and have courts of their own. C0 settles how savings convert as *assets*, and `04` 5.1b ends pension obligations on the claim side. Nothing settles the liability side generally, and the asymmetry is conspicuous. *Open.*
