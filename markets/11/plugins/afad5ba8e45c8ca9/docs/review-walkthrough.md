2. Run the no-resume search case with a sample-data account; show supported

3. Demonstrate an earlier search remains readable after another search, then
   prepare a customer-selected job using the approved account budget.
4. Show both usable PDFs without real candidate details or capability URLs.
   Demonstrate accepted-request reuse and package-link refresh without a second

5. With explicit optional write consent and a sample customer report, record an
   agent-reported outcome. Do not visit or submit to an employer.
6. Exercise cross-account refusal, unsupported employer submission, and revoked
   or insufficient permission/budget behavior. Show safe reconnection/limits.
