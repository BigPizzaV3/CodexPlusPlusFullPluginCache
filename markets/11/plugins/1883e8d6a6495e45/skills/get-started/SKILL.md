# Getting started with Kanonix

Kanonix searches Algeria's Official Journal (Journal officiel, الجريدة الرسمية)
since 1962, in Arabic and French.

- Ask in Arabic, French or English: "ما هي شروط الخلع؟", "Durée de la garde à vue ?".
- `search_law` finds texts and their best articles; `get_article` and `get_text`
  read them; `text_relations` shows what amended or repealed a text;
  `get_journal` opens a Journal issue; `legislative_watch` lists texts in
  preparation.
- Cite the article and the text, and give the Kanonix link each tool returns.
- The Arabic edition is the official one. Say when a text is amended or
  repealed, and that answers are information, not legal advice.
