# SwingSmith · local plugin candidate 0.1.0

Connect an existing adult SwingSmith account to read saved analyses, compare compatible evidence from two selected reports, and find applicable source-backed practice guidance.

**This package is a review candidate.** Submission, approval and directory publication are separate states. During connection review, use the designated fictitious review account supplied privately by SwingSmith. Other accounts remain unavailable until customer-release checks are complete.

## What it can do

- Find your saved reports by date, club or camera view.
- Explain saved evidence, its source and its limitations.
- Compare two reports when their measurements are compatible.
- Find up to three applicable drills for a supported report finding.

It does not upload videos, run a new swing analysis, make purchases or save practice results. The initial drill mapping covers supported down-the-line early-extension findings; it is not a general coaching library. Automated source checks are identified separately from human approval. A lesson video's length is not a practice prescription.

## Connection and privacy

After an approved release, install through your host's plugin workflow and sign in on SwingSmith's first-party page. Declare adult eligibility and separately consent to share selected report evidence with ChatGPT or Codex. Never paste a password, email code or token into a conversation. This declaration is not verified age; junior access is unavailable.

Reports remain subject to your account's current and retained access. Unknown access is denied. The service shares bounded evidence and instructions, not raw video, signed media URLs or your account email in ordinary tool results. Existing analysis-processing and optional contribution choices remain separate. The host controls retention of its conversations.

To end future access, revoke the OAuth connection through your host and disconnect the plugin. Removing an installation does not necessarily revoke the grant; contact SwingSmith support if unsure. Account deletion or session revocation blocks future reads. Conversation history is managed separately by the host.
