# Bid language reference

These are drafting patterns, not verbatim AICP clauses or automatically applicable contract terms. Adapt them to the user's selected bid basis and approved scope.

## Firm bid

“This is a firm bid based on the production specifications, assumptions, and exclusions outlined herein. Changes to the approved creative, scope, schedule, or deliverables that result in additional costs will be submitted for client approval before those costs are incurred.”

## Provisional exceptions, only when intended

“The amounts included for [named categories and verified amounts] are provisional allowances and exceptions to the firm price, pending confirmation of creative requirements and vendor quotes. Any proposed adjustment will be presented for written approval before the affected work is authorized. All other included costs remain firm for the agreed scope.”

Do not use this exception merely because the producer has not obtained every supplier quote. If the allowance remains within the firm price, instead say the creative will be developed within that amount and any requested expansion of scope will be submitted for approval.

## Confirmed nonunion basis

“This bid is based on a nonunion production using nonunion labor rates and fringes. Incremental costs arising from union requirements are not included. If the production becomes subject to union requirements, the bid will require revision to reflect applicable rates, fringes, benefits, and any resulting changes to staffing, working conditions, or schedule. The revised costs and production plan will be submitted for client approval before the affected work proceeds.”

Adapt location and crew details only from current evidence. This clause describes the pricing assumption; it does not determine which labor obligations apply.

## Wardrobe

“Wardrobe is included at [verified amount or exact global reference]. The band's preferred stylist or wardrobe team has not yet been confirmed. Once selected, production will confirm the looks, fittings, staffing, and purchases or rentals with that team.”

Then specify whether this is a provisional exception or an allowance within the firm price. Do not leave its treatment ambiguous.

## VFX and custom fabrication

State the estimate's basis accurately: one initial conversation, a written quote, or an approved vendor scope. Identify the missing design/script/shot-count or fabrication requirements. Explain when pricing will be revisited and how changes will be approved. Never imply a vendor has committed to preliminary pricing.
