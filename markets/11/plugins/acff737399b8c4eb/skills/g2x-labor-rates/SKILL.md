# Research labor rates

1. Resolve the labor category and relevant filters, then inspect the current search, company or statistics mode schema.
2. Explain the category, filters, sample and units behind the returned rates. A ceiling rate is not a paid price or a proposed bid rate.
3. If lookup is unavailable, say it cannot finish through this connection. Do not diagnose missing entitlement from tool absence. Do not substitute award spending for labor rates.
