# Broad research and recovery

Generated by scripts/generate-action-room-research.mjs from the server's shared research policy. Protocol: broad_research_v1.

For any task requiring candidate discovery, comparison or suitability judgment, use action_room_research inside the existing task. Applicability follows the task's purpose, never an industry, keyword or list of example use cases. First read the user's objective, current task and report requirements; preserve their meaning in selection_criteria. Report recommendations guide investigation but do not establish facts about a candidate; Codex-added inferences are not confirmed facts either. Do not replace a requirement with an easier proxy such as industry membership, keyword presence or contact availability. Read the current ledger first; preserve its candidates, sources and rounds across resumptions. Classify a closed verification as bounded, and unrelated work as not_applicable with a reason. Document writing, coding or file conversion without candidate selection does not require candidate fields or fit_status. New Rooms require this classification before submission; do not ask the user to perform it.
For broad discovery, define the unit, scope, selection criteria, meaningful segments and at least three relevant source types. Use 100-200 distinct primary-unit candidates as an initial discovery target, never a cap or automatic stopping condition. Use accessible directories, official data/APIs and pagination, company-independent queries, synonyms, regions/languages within scope and related entities. Named-company searches are verification, not broad discovery. Keep small, regional and specialist alternatives in scope where relevant. Do not pad the list to reach a number.
Record sources actually read, dated facts tied to each candidate, unknowns, selection/exclusion reasons and the next useful action. Separate organization, person, product and channel counts; normalize identities and deduplicate mirrored sources. Check that the source describes this candidate's actual operations: keywords in privacy policies, illustrative examples or another legal entity's pages are not operational facts about this candidate. A source's existence or several evidence sentences do not establish suitability. Contact availability shows only reachability, not business fit or purchasing demand; lack of an email does not negate supported fit.
Immediately before selection, review which original requirement each confirmed fact supports and how. Keep this explanation in existing rationale and evidence, remaining uncertainty in unknowns, and the next proportionate action in next_action; use judgments for requirement-specific facts, excerpts, inference and counterevidence, and preserve readable stage explanations. Set only the fit judgment in attributes.fit_status: supported when evidence supports the current selection requirements, unconfirmed for inference or insufficient information, and not_fit when evidence shows a mismatch. eligible or priority=true requires supported plus the existing current evidence checks; the value alone is not proof. With insufficient public material, hold the candidate and continue feasible confirmation instead of automatically excluding it. Keep business fit separate from actual purchasing demand: a supported-fit candidate may be considered for a proposal before demand is confirmed, but record unknown demand and do not claim it exists. Use the current task's requirements for other attributes without inventing observations. Never backfill missing legacy fit_status as supported or treat an old decision as passing these checks.
Continue discovery while important segments remain unsearched or new eligible alternatives keep appearing. Before requesting comparable status, check every planned source type and segment, review all candidates, verify priority candidates in depth, and perform at least two genuinely different expansion rounds showing diminishing discovery (at most 5% new primary-unit candidates per round, with a floor of two). A documented complete bounded universe may justify fewer candidates; use source-linked universe evidence. If the market is genuinely scarce, record the exact searched scope, evidence and limitation instead of claiming statistical representativeness. Count or URL presence alone is not semantic validation: read the underlying material and explain the comparison.
Save partial/recheck status, limitations and next_steps when access or execution limits stop research. Approval/account waits affect only the dependent external action; continue independent authorized research. Early action on verified candidates may proceed within existing authority without claiming the whole research complete. Never repeat successful external effects. No background continuation is promised without a real host scheduler. Preserve the report, task order and business completion criteria; research readiness is not sales, supply or campaign success. Save the exact research mutation through the local research-checkpoint helper, then replay reporting after uncertainty. Never place credentials, private source URLs or raw sensitive records in the ledger.
Candidate decisions are proposals. Only a current server-owned semantic review plus a matching material receipt permits promotion. Preserve the server origin and map every criterion to its original requirement. Distinguish relevance, selection fit, demand and readiness. Required unknowns cannot be replaced by scores or counts. Review criteria first, then individual candidates using action_room_research action=review and patch_json={target:"criteria"|candidate_id}. This bounded reviewer compares the original task and report, criteria and independently read source excerpts; it never executes tasks or contacts anyone. Save the exact mutation before calling. Pending/failed/uncertain reviews are not passes. Save stage evidence and readable explanations with the decision-materials helper, then report its material_receipts in an ordinary save. Local receipts are host reports, not uploaded or server-read files. Upload only with authorization through existing artifact tools. Preserve old versions and continue independent work when evidence or access is missing.

## One owner and one record

Codex executes the research. The server owns the task's durable ledger and derives readiness from separate semantic and storage conditions. Action Room displays it. Do not create a second plan or executor. A spreadsheet/JSON export is a useful business deliverable when needed; preserve evidence and readable explanations for actual decision stages, not fabricated business activity. Record only necessary public business facts or non-sensitive summaries of authorized records. Credentials and signed/private URLs never belong here.

## Tool and exact retries

1. Read action_room_research with action=read, room_id and task_id. Page each collection (candidates, sources, rounds) using next_offset before changing existing records; default collection is candidates. All pages must have the same revision; reread if it changes. Sources and rounds have stable IDs; existing records merge by ID and are not deleted. Read the local research-checkpoint too. A prior uncertain mutation must be replayed before forming a new one.
2. Form a small patch (up to 100 sources, 100 candidates and 20 rounds per call). Save the exact MCP payload using local research-checkpoint --task <task_id>, with stdin {payload:{action:"save",room_id,task_id,binding_id,codex_session_id,mutation_id,expected_revision,patch_json}}. patch_json is the JSON-serialized patch below. Use stable UUID mutation_id and the latest server revision. No token or signed URL is needed.
3. Call action_room_research with that exact saved payload. Counts and readiness are derived server-side. A requested comparable assessment may remain researching with specific issues; save useful work, then address the gaps.
4. Pass {receipt:<exact successful response>} to research-checkpoint. A timeout is not acknowledgment. For a definitive error allowed by the helper, save {rejected:{mutation_id,code}}, read the latest revision, and form a new mutation. The report-artifact and external-effect checkpoints remain separate.
5. A save may prepare an independent later task without changing task order or completing it. Pause/achieved/exhausted goals prohibit new writes. Do not label business outcomes from research readiness. Readiness expires when source freshness expires; refresh the relevant evidence before submitting.

## Patch fields

All IDs below are stable short identifiers containing letters, digits, dots, dashes, underscores or colons. Arrays omitted from a patch keep previous records. To revise scope, include change_reason; keep existing references valid. Entity keys identify the real unit (company registration ID/canonical business domain, product ID or channel URL), not each page or contact. The unit string must match the primary candidates' kind. A person's parent_id may link their organization; people do not inflate organization counts.

- plan: {mode:"broad"|"bounded"|"not_applicable",purpose,scope,unit,subject,selection_criteria:[text],segments:[{id,label}],source_types:[text],initial_target:100,freshness_days:365,rationale,universe_size:null|integer,universe_source_ids:[source_id]}. Broad mode needs at least three relevant source types; mode is chosen by the task's purpose. Bounded/not_applicable require rationale. Use an explicit source-linked universe for small complete populations, including a user-authorized fixed scope described in rationale and available supporting public sources.
- sources: [{id,url,reference,title,publisher,source_type,observed_at:ISO-date,access:"read"|"unavailable",note}]. Supply a public HTTPS URL without credentials, or a non-sensitive reference label for an authorized provided/connected document (no private URL or local path); ordinary public page query parameters are allowed. Actually read sources before marking read. Duplicate URLs/mirrors are not independent evidence. For an explicitly authorized, already uploaded text/plain, text/html or application/json source, use artifact_id and permitted_excerpt plus a non-sensitive reference label; omit a private URL. The server verifies owner/task, original bytes and the exact permitted excerpt, retains only that excerpt, and does not copy the private original into the ledger. Binary/unreadable originals remain unconfirmed. Record inaccessible source attempts with a note and continue alternatives.
- candidates: [{id,entity_key,name,kind,parent_id:"",segment_ids:[id],decision:"unreviewed"|"eligible"|"held"|"excluded",priority:false,rationale,evidence:[{source_id,fact}],unknowns:[text],next_action,attributes:{fit_status:"supported"|"unconfirmed"|"not_fit",field:text}}]. New or updated eligible/priority decisions require supported, rationale and a current source-linked fact; priority also requires eligible and at least two distinct relevant facts. Unreviewed/held candidates and partial findings can be saved without these selection prerequisites. These are proposed decisions; eligible counts remain zero until a matching server review and material receipt exist. Existing omitted candidates are preserved, never automatically reassessed. Error strategy_codex_research_fit_status_invalid means use an allowed fit_status; strategy_codex_research_fit_status_required means confirm fit or explicitly hold the candidate without priority; strategy_codex_research_selection_evidence_incomplete means check rationale, current read evidence, and priority's eligible decision/two distinct facts. A rejected save changes nothing. These are structural checks, not proof of arbitrary text's truth or sufficiency; apply the shared policy above.
- rounds: [{id,strategy:"directory"|"discovery"|"targeted"|"expansion",source_ids:[id],segment_ids:[id],queries:[text],new_candidate_ids:[id],summary,observed_at:ISO-date}]. Use directories/endpoints/filters as query descriptions for bulk extraction. Record a candidate as new only once; follow-up verification uses an empty new_candidate_ids. Do not relabel named-company queries as discovery. Meaningful expansion changes the search route and checks missing types; repeated equivalent searches do not establish saturation.
- assessment: {status:"researching"|"comparable"|"partial"|"recheck"|"not_applicable",summary,limitations:[text],next_steps:[text],stop_reason,scarcity_reason:""}. For comparable use a factual stopping reason. Scarcity requires explicit searched scope and limitations plus genuine expansion evidence, never a shortcut around broad research. Partial/recheck must state remaining scope and next steps. Not-applicable work needs only plan and assessment, with empty research arrays; no business file is required.

## Purpose, criteria and independent review

Save review_contract with {stages:[{id,label}],origin_source_ids:[source_id],criteria:[{id,requirement_refs:["task"|"request"|"report"|"completion_criteria"|source_id],requirement,dimension:"relevance"|"selection"|"demand"|"readiness",required:boolean,applies_to:["*"|candidate_kind|segment_id],stage_id}]}. Use task-specific criteria and meaningful existing decision steps; stages are not new Action Room tasks. A changed contract requires change_reason. The server reads and versions the original protected request, report and immutable task; the caller cannot supply origin or reviews.

Candidates additionally carry judgments:[{criterion_id,status:"supported"|"unknown"|"contradicted",facts:[{source_id,entity_key,location,excerpt,fact}],explanation,inference,counterevidence,unknowns:[text],next_action}]. Each excerpt must concern this legal entity and actually support that requirement. Record absent purchase intent separately; do not make it mandatory unless the original task requires it.

Call action_room_research action=review with the same binding/mutation/revision fields and patch_json containing only {target:"criteria"} first, then {target:candidate_id}. Use research-checkpoint for these exact requests as well. A review_pending response is not acknowledgment: replay the same request. Calls are bounded to 6 source URLs, 12000 retained characters/source, 90000 input characters and 4096 output tokens, with two attempts/input, 512 calls/task and a USD 50 estimated task budget (USD 10 reserved per in-flight or unknown-usage call). The call records existing model-policy usage; no task executor or external action runs. Missing source access, timeout or invalid output is held/failed, never passed. A new mutation may retry a failed/uncertain call after two minutes; changing mutation IDs never bypasses the input/task budget. Resume uses saved provider results. The configured deployment budget may stop review; continue independent work and report the exact unmet condition.

## Local decision material custody

Run the local helper command decision-materials with the same --data-dir, --session, --project-key and --task identity options. Its stdin is {stage:{id,label},entries:[{origin,criteria,candidate,review,change_reason}]} using the exact server review, its captures and corresponding candidate version. Up to 100 entries share one stage summary and deduplicated original text. Pass only the public review summary/checks/captures, not raw prompts, logs or protected request JSON. The helper creates task-level ??/ and ????/, stable substage folders with the same pair, source text stored once by content, a versioned source manifest and readable HTML with checked relative links. It returns material_receipts and verified_local=true, uploaded=false. Replay exactly after interruption; changed bytes are rejected. On resume, run the same helper with {verify_receipts:[saved receipts]}; it reads/hashes every file and returns status=missing on loss or corruption. Save those returned receipts before acting, so the server withdraws promotion. A server cannot continuously inspect local disk. Do not invent future stages or external receipts.

Save returned material_receipts through ordinary action=save. Each is {stage_id,target,decision_version,status:"host_reported"|"missing",files:[{role:"evidence"|"explanation",relative_path,sha256,size_bytes,artifact_id?}]}. This is host-reported readback, not independent server access to local disk. Optional artifact_id must already belong to this task and have uploaded bytes; the server independently reads/hashes it. Local saving never uploads. Use existing artifact reserve/upload/complete tools only when authorized; preserve reservations and IDs. Web UI only enables download for verified uploads. Local originals/explanations remain accessible in the actual task folder. Saving documents never completes a business task.

Retain receipts for each applicable stage and the criteria review. Any purpose, criterion, candidate, evidence or policy change invalidates selection until a new review and matching materials exist. Old versions and submissions are retained; never backfill a historical explanation. Re-read the ledger and inspect review status/unknowns before acting on a selected candidate.

## Completion checks

For broad research: planned segments/source types searched, at least two read source types and publishers, actual broad discovery, every retained candidate reviewed or explicitly held/excluded with evidence, priority evidence checked, complete discovery lineage, freshness, and two different expansion rounds with diminishing new candidates. The 100-candidate initial target is not a cap: a source-linked complete universe or documented scarce market can justify fewer, while unmet coverage always requires further work. Execution/capacity limits mean partial research, never sufficient research. Provider listings, unsupported estimates and unknown purchase intent stay explicit. Do not claim statistical representativeness.
