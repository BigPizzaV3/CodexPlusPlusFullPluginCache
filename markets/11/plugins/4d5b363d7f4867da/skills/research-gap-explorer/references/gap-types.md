# Research gap taxonomy and evidence checks

Candidate gap types:
- **Population/context:** evidence in one demographic, institution, geography or setting may not generalize to another.
- **Outcome/time horizon:** short-term outcomes studied, longer-term effects or adverse outcomes poorly characterized.
- **Methods/measurement:** sampling bias, unvalidated instruments, inconsistent definitions, absent replication or missing baselines.
- **Contradictory evidence:** comparable studies reach different conclusions; investigate design and measurement before asserting disagreement.
- **Mechanistic/theoretical:** mechanisms or theoretical predictions not experimentally resolved.
- **Translation/implementation:** efficacy established in controlled conditions but real-world deployment, adoption or cost uncertain.

For each gap record (1) supporting papers/sections, (2) an explicit counter-search, (3) boundaries of searched material, (4) direct vs inferred support, (5) a falsifiable next question. When gaps refer to an under-studied group, prefer "few relevant records surfaced in this scoped search" over "no studies exist." Do not label an exploratory finding globally novel.
