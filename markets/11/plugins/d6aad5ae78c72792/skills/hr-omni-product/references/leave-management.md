# Leave Management

Verified 2026-09-10.

Leave Management replaces spreadsheet and email administration with employee requests, single-stage approval, entitlement tracking, calendars and reports.

## Setup and entitlement

- Employees are enrolled individually and need active employment plus a schedule.
- Annual allowance is set by an administrator. A current-period override can support cases such as mid-year starters.
- Carry-over can be configured per employee with a maximum and expiry.
- Companies may allow requests beyond balance.
- Individual schedules determine working days.
- Days off and configured public/non-working days do not consume allowance.
- Overlapping requests for one employee are rejected.
- There is no automatic accrual, automatic statutory calculation or automatic pro-rating.

## Leave types and approval

Leave types can define whether they reduce PTO, payment-entitlement percentage, manual or automatic approval, calendar visibility and half/full-day requests.

- One approver is sufficient. There is no multi-stage approval.
- Designated approvers cover their own employment department.
- Several approvers may cover one department.
- Requester and approver comments are supported.
- Submitted requests cannot be edited. Cancel and resubmit instead.
- Employees can cancel until the start date.

## Visibility and reporting

Approved normal leave is visible to enrolled employees in-app. Pending requests are in-app only. Hidden leave types remain hidden from ordinary employees and external calendar feeds.

Reports include PTO taken for a period, employee absence overview, annual balance/carry-over overview and the Bulgarian attendance report commonly called Form 76. Relevant reports export to Excel/CSV, not native PDF.

## Limits

No hourly leave, multi-stage approval, automatic accrual, payroll processing/synchronisation or mobile app. Migration and historical setup are manual.

Prices are time-sensitive. Check `sources.md` and current official pricing before quoting.
