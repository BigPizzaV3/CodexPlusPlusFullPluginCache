# Worklogs

Verified 2026-09-10.

Worklogs is trust-based time recording for working time and project allocation without invasive employee surveillance.

## Time records

Users can record date, start time, end time, optional project, optional note and whether the period is a break. Several non-overlapping records can exist on a day.

Two methods are supported:
- Manual entry, where fields remain editable.
- Running timer, where recorded start/end times are immutable but note, project and break status remain editable.

## Context and projects

- HR Omni never prefills worklogs.
- Public holidays and approved leave appear on the daily activity screen.
- Employee schedules provide expected-hours context.
- A progress view compares expected and recorded hours and displays overtime.
- Time can still be recorded on days off, public holidays or approved leave. The product informs rather than blocks.
- Projects support title, description and billable/non-billable status.
- Projects are available to all employees, not restricted by department.
- Do not claim clients, nested tasks, budgets, hourly rates or calculated project costs.

## Access, reporting and limits

Employees see/manage only their own entries. Authorised administrators can access reporting and individual employee records according to their access level.

There is no timesheet submit/approve/reject workflow and no period locking. Individual and project timesheet reports exist and relevant reports export to Excel/CSV.

Worklogs has no screenshots, location tracking, keystroke logging, QR check-ins or background monitoring. It is web based with no mobile app. Do not claim import, API, offline mode, reminders or payroll synchronisation unless current official sources verify them.
