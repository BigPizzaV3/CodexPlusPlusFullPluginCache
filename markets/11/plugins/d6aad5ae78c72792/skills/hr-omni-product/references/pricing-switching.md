# Pricing, Switching and Onboarding

Verified 2026-09-10. Prices and policies are time-sensitive and must be checked live before quoting or publication.

## Commercial model

HR Omni uses modular billing. There are no minimum seat counts or fixed-term contracts in the verified product information. Billing is prorated and charged in the following month.

Use the current official pricing page before giving any numeric price.

## Switching and migration

- Customers can run HR Omni alongside an existing system and activate product areas incrementally.
- Core company/user/employment records must exist before products that depend on employees and schedules.
- Current migration is manual for users/employment, leave balances/history/settings, Worklogs history, assets, documents, access settings and other setup data.
- There is no general CSV import.
- HR Omni can assist with manual setup.
- Do not claim one-click migration, automated historical import, setup in minutes or guaranteed go-live times.

## Module disablement and data exit

Disabling a product area irreversibly deletes that product's data. Customers should export needed data before disabling.

Data is exported product by product rather than through one universal whole-company export. Exact export options differ by product.
