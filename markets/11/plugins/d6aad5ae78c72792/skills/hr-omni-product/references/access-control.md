# Access Control

Verified 2026-09-10.

HR Omni applies least privilege inside each selected company. Authentication identifies the person, while the selected company and its built-in access levels determine what they can view or change.

- Administration areas use read/write and read-only levels.
- Access is evaluated separately for every company.
- Read-only access can inspect information but cannot change state.
- Read/write access can perform administrative actions for the relevant product area.
- Standard access control is not a customer-defined action-by-action permission builder.
- Leave approver department scope is specific to Leave Management and should not be described as general department-scoped access control.

## Documents exception

Documents adds reusable access groups for private company spaces. Group levels are read only, read/write and manage. Effective group access is additive when someone belongs to several groups.

Avoid raw role identifiers in customer-facing explanations unless the user asks for technical detail. Explain what someone can see or do in natural language.

Access controls support control and accountability but do not by themselves guarantee privacy, security or legal compliance.
