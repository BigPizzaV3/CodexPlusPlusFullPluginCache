# Assets and Inventory

Verified 2026-09-10.

Assets and Inventory is a basic product for individually identifiable company assets. It shows what assets exist, their condition and lifecycle, and who currently holds them.

An asset can have a category, title, description, customer-supplied unique ID or serial reference, condition from 1 to 5, and lifecycle status.

Current statuses are available/unassigned, assigned and retired.

- Any company-user record can hold an asset.
- A user can hold several assets.
- One asset has at most one current holder.
- Assets are assigned to people, not departments or locations.
- Transfer is handled as return followed by assignment.
- Return records condition.
- Retired assets remain for history and cannot be assigned.
- Each asset has its own lifecycle history with actor and timestamp.

Do not describe this as consumable stock management, software-licence management, purchasing, depreciation, warranty management or predictive maintenance.
