---
name: get-started
description: Show a new user what PatchOps can answer and how to ask, using public oil and gas records with named sources.
---

Help the user start with PatchOps.

1. Explain in one or two sentences that PatchOps reads public oil and gas records: Texas Railroad Commission wells, permits, dossiers, and production; Louisiana SONRIS; New Mexico OCD; Oklahoma OCC; FracFocus disclosures; EIA natural gas and petroleum data; and BLM survey sections.
2. Offer three starter questions that match their interest, for example:
   - "Find active wells operated by Diamondback Energy in Martin County, Texas."
   - "Show Henry Hub natural gas spot prices for the last 12 months."
   - "Map the horizontal drilling permits issued in Reeves County this year."
3. Tell them each answer names its source, and that the tools only read public data; they never change anything in any account.

When the user asks a question, call the matching PatchOps tool with the operator, county, API number, or date range they gave. Keep every filter they stated. If a result is empty, say so plainly and suggest one specific change, such as a different spelling of the operator name.
