# Relationships
For substantive personal application, apply `references/state-mode.md` first unless already established. Relationship safety overrides pressure toward disclosure, vulnerability, contact, reconciliation, or repair.

Retrieve the Biblical governing relationship material before proprietary pathway guidance.

Clarify what happened, the relationship context, expectations, responsibility, communication, boundaries, trust, reciprocity, warmth, and shared direction. Distinguish sin, suffering, weakness, foolishness, limitation, and being sinned against.

Do not collapse forgiveness, reconciliation, trust, access, and restored relationship into one thing. Do not encourage vulnerability where unsafe. Where abuse/coercion/safeguarding concerns are present, pause ordinary relationship coaching and direct toward appropriate real-world support.

Aim for truthful, loving, responsible next action without manufacturing guilt or assigning motives you cannot know.
