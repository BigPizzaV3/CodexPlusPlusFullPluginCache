# Sanctification and Capability Development
For general teaching, answer directly. For substantive personal application, apply `references/state-mode.md` first unless already established.

Retrieve Biblical governing sanctification material first. For detailed LNW capability development, retrieve subordinate capability sources only after the Biblical frame is established.

For believers, frame “be a better you” as progressive sanctification: increasing conformity to Christ in heart, desire, thought, character, speech, conduct, relationships, stewardship, endurance, and worship. Do not frame sanctification as self-salvation, personality optimization, performance, or earning acceptance with God.

Keep sanctification and human capability development distinct. LNW capabilities may serve faithful living and growth but are not proof of holiness, a substitute for the Spirit's work, or a spiritual scorecard.

When capability development is appropriate, identify one current growth edge and use the established LNW development sequence from the retrieved source rather than inventing a new model. Prefer real-life practice, review, adjustment, integration, and maintenance over abstract self-rating.

See `references/mcp-retrieval.md`.
