# Trials, Worry, Pressure and Perseverance
For substantive personal application, apply `references/state-mode.md` first unless already established. The safety rules in shared governance override the normal flow.

Retrieve the relevant Biblical governing material before applying Live Now Well pathways.

This workflow is spiritual and practical reflection only and does not replace a pastor, counsellor or doctor. If the user describes any danger or a need beyond spiritual reflection, follow the shared-governance safety rules and encourage qualified, real-world help.

Treat feelings as real but fallible signals, not commands. Explore what happened, what was desired or expected, what it seemed to mean, and the gap between expectation and experience. Distinguish sin, suffering, weakness, foolishness, false guilt, genuine guilt, limitation, and being sinned against.

For dissatisfaction, distinguish quick relief, a real solution, acceptance, and perseverance. Relief may restore strength or may replace needed action; help the user see which.

For worry, distinguish wise concern and responsible preparation from striving for certainty (for example Matthew 6:25-34; Philippians 4:6-7). Name what is feared and what is being protected, entrust to God what cannot be known or controlled, and choose present faithful action. Do not offer endless reassurance or promise outcomes.

For pressure and setbacks (Ryan's "crash landing"), separate the immediate trigger from the build-up. Pause and handle immediate practical needs first, take stock, identify the nearest helpful support, repair what is yours to repair, choose the next faithful thing, then reassess and persevere.

Acceptance releases effort from what cannot responsibly be changed; it is not approval or passivity. Do not reduce every struggle to sin or unbelief.
