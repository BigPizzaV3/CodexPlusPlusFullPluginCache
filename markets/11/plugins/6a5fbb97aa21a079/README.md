# Sovrano

**Shared sponsorship decisions, inside your AI.**

Sovrano gives brands, events, rights holders and agencies one governed workspace
for the same sponsorship situation. Your team keeps the Frame, evidence,
research, recommendation, approvals and outcome together instead of rebuilding
context in each conversation.

## What it feels like

1. **Frame.** Define what is being bought or sold, the objective, scope,
   constraints and success measures.
2. **Evaluate.** Combine recorded sponsorship evidence, transparent valuation
   and public research saved for safe reuse inside the workspace.
3. **Prepare.** Turn current evidence into a shortlist, budget, package,
   proposal assessment or negotiation position.
4. **Review.** Record what happened and carry approved lessons forward.

The current phase, completed work, blocker and next action come from the shared
record. A card or agent never approves or advances a phase on its own.

## Try asking

- "Start a shared evaluation of this sponsorship proposal and guide us through
  the current phase."
- "Research this sponsor, save the evidence for our team and tell us whether it
  applies to our situation."
- "Assess this package and fee, then prepare a negotiation position with every
  assumption labelled."
- "Show what changed since my colleague worked on this and what needs my
  decision."

## Good to know

- Teams share one workspace while roles and decision rights remain explicit.
- Recorded relationships, public sources, estimates, scenarios and agent
  judgment keep separate labels.
- Eligible public relationship signals enter the existing sponsorship-signal
  database with companion-agent provenance and pending-review status. Private
  workspace context does not enter that database.
- Exact previews and explicit human confirmation govern consequential changes.
- Contacting sponsors, publishing, accepting offers, making payments and legal
  advice remain outside Sovrano's actions.

## What the plugin connects to

The plugin connects to `https://sovrano.global/mcp-companion` over HTTPS. Normal
users sign in in the browser with an email code. Reviewer password access is a
separate test route.

Sovrano receives bounded workspace, situation, research and analysis inputs
needed for the selected task. Credentials, email codes and complete private
documents stay outside the tool inputs. The team controls optional sanitized
outcome contribution through a separate governed preview and consent.
