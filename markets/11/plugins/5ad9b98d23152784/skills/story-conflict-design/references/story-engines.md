# Document: Social conflicts and pains

Knowledge-base version (Givon / AI bloggers). Its purpose is to find strong social themes, conflicts and pains quickly, for character legends and for Reels/TikTok/Shorts scripts.

Pyaterochka is a Russian discount grocery chain.

## How to use this document

1. Choose one "conflict core": what breaks the hero's life.

2. Pin down the "trigger": the concrete event after which everything went downhill.

3. Fix the "low point": how the poverty, the loss of status or the reset to zero showed itself.

4. Define the "turn": what the hero decided to do differently, and why exactly now.

5. Add 3–5 "real details" (objects, places, documents, habits, phrases); they make the story believable.

6. Formulate the "promise of the path": why people should follow the hero, and what will change in 30/90/365 days.

## The rule that holds all the storytelling together

Every problem must lead to a reset to zero (poverty, debts, job loss, isolation, shame) and then to "rebuilding yourself from scratch". Without a low point there are no stakes. Without a path there is no series.

## The emotions that hook hardest

- Injustice: "I'm paying for other people's decisions."

- Shame and vulnerability: "I'm afraid to admit where I am now."

- Betrayal: "the person closest to me hurt me the most."

- Powerlessness before the system: debt collectors, courts, travel bans, debts.

- Hope: "I didn't die, I'm restarting."

- Anger as fuel: "I'll get my name back."

- Envy and contrast: "everyone is celebrating, and I'm on shift."

## Conflict library (cores)

### Debts and collectors (a financial trap)

- Essence: the hero lives "on the phone": debts, one or more loans, overdue payments, threats, pressure.

- Why it works: many people have loans or know someone in debt; the topic triggers a primal fear.

- How it leads to poverty: frozen accounts, deductions from wages, being unable to leave the country, working "just to make the payments".

- Details for realism: a screenshot of an "unknown number", a notice letter, a call at 07:30, a whispered conversation, a folder of documents, a travel ban, "payment due today".

- Path of rebuilding: a repayment plan → side gigs or a second job → a return to a skill or profession → the first small wins.

### Abuse and control (you were "cut down" to a convenient version)

- Essence: the partner restricts the hero's public presence, work, friends and money, and "breaks" the hero's personality.

- Why it works: a recognizable scenario, and a lot of abuse is hidden; people feel ashamed, but they empathize.

- How it leads to poverty: dependence on the partner, the loss of clients, work or reputation, cut-off contacts.

- Details: phrases like "don't embarrass me", "why do you need a blog", "I'll provide for you", "don't talk to them"; control over the phone.

- Path of rebuilding: get back your voice and social media → get back your profession → get back an income → get back your boundaries.

### Cheating, leaving or the partner's disappearance (betrayal at the peak of trust)

- Essence: "yesterday we were a family, today he disappeared, left or blocked me".

- Why it works: the highest emotional stakes and an instant before/after contrast.

- How it leads to poverty: the shared budget is gone, the debts and obligations remain, the home and the loans are in the hero's name.

- Details: keys on the table, an empty wardrobe, "don't look for me", tickets or a move, a stranger's number in the phone.

- Path of rebuilding: accept it → gather the documents → get a job → restore the professional identity.

### A business or loans registered in the hero's name (a legal setup)

- Essence: the hero is a "drop", a nominee front. A sole proprietorship or LLC (IP/OOO), contracts, loans or investors are all in the hero's name.

- Why it works: the fear of "I was dragged into this", a sense of being trapped and anger at the injustice.

- How it leads to poverty: courts, debts, reputation, blocked accounts, a travel ban.

- Details: a power of attorney, a signature, a company seal, calls from "investors", notices from the tax service (FNS) or the bank.

- Path of rebuilding: a lawyer → restructuring → evidence → a survival job in parallel.

### Serious illness of the hero or a parent (life "dropped out")

- Essence: a diagnosis, chemotherapy, long treatment, caring for a loved one; "everything stopped".

- Why it works: the strongest empathy; people know the pain of hospitals, queues and fear.

- How it leads to poverty: treatment, medicines, job loss, loans just to survive.

- Details: a patient wristband, a pharmacy receipt, a bag of medicines, "the test results are ready", a symptom diary.

- Path of rebuilding: rehabilitation → small steps (10 minutes of walking) → a return to the work or the skill → a new meaning.

### Injury, accident or sport (fractures, surgeries, rehabilitation)

- Essence: a fall (snowboard, scooter, car crash) → surgery → a month or months of pain and recovery.

- Why it works: the contrast "active life → cast and crutches" is visually gripping.

- How it leads to poverty: no work or side gigs, recovery costs, dependence on help.

- Details: a cast, crutches, stitches, exercises, a rehabilitation therapist, "the first steps without support", a sick-leave certificate.

- Path of rebuilding: recovery discipline → the psyche (the fear of it happening again) → getting back in shape → getting back to an income.

### Addiction: gambling

- Essence: the hero or the partner loses money, lies, takes out loans, destroys the family.

- Why it works: "gifts → debts → disappearance"; people know it from those close to them.

- How it leads to poverty: loans, selling possessions, debts, threats.

- Details: "bets", night calls, "I'll win it back", withdrawal, hidden transfers, selling the car.

- Path of rebuilding: admission → treatment or support groups → self-exclusion blocks → a financial plan → restoring trust and life.

### Addiction: drugs or alcohol (the fall and rehabilitation)

- Essence: a relapse, the loss of work, family and money, then rehabilitation and rebuilding.

- Why it works: hard truth, a strong "path" and respect for the effort.

- How it leads to poverty: dismissal, debts, a breakup, shame and isolation.

- Details: "1 day sober", a group, a diary, a relapse, apologies, simple everyday steps.

- Path of rebuilding: sobriety day by day → sport and a routine → a new profession or income → an honest blog without embellishment.

### Bullying, shame and appearance (a scar, excess weight, a defect)

- Essence: after an injury, surgery or weight gain, the hero feels "defective".

- Why it works: very many people live with this shame; the topic draws strong empathy.

- How it leads to poverty: withdrawal from public life, the loss of work that depended on the face, depression, isolation.

- Details: a scar or a plaster, "I don't film myself", "before" photos, clothes that "hide", a look in the mirror.

- Path of rebuilding: sport and self-care → acceptance → a new form of public presence → victory over shame.

### Double life («Двойная жизнь»): a job "to survive" by day, the dream in the evening

- Essence: the hero hates the day job but builds a different path in the evenings.

- Why it works: a mass experience; half the country lives like this or dreams of it.

- How it leads to poverty: a salary that goes "to zero", no prospects, fatigue, burnout.

- Details: a uniform or a badge, a 12-hour shift, the bus or the metro, the laptop at night, coffee at 23:40.

- Path of rebuilding: discipline of 60–90 minutes a day → skill growth → the first clients or orders → leaving "survival mode".

### Loss of status or profession (a reset to zero)

- Essence: the hero used to be "someone" (a stylist, an entrepreneur, an athlete) and became "no one" on paper.

- Why it works: the fear of falling and the hope of coming back are universal.

- How it leads to poverty: no clients, no name, no portfolio; the hero has to take a low-paid job.

- Details: old photos from shoots, "why did I give it up", an empty calendar, the first 3000₽ earned.

- Path of rebuilding: a portfolio from scratch → a content routine → the first case studies → the status returns.

### The system against the person (courts, bans, documents)

- Essence: the hero has hit a wall of bureaucracy, courts, the tax service (FNS), the bank or a travel ban.

- Why it works: the feeling of powerlessness before the system, plus interest in "how to climb out".

- How it leads to poverty: seizures, deductions, being unable to change jobs or leave, stress.

- Details: a summons or a court hearing, "account seized", queues, a talk with a lawyer, case numbers.

- Path of rebuilding: an action plan → legal steps → small wins → less anxiety.

### Orphanhood, a children's home or a hard childhood (a harsh starting point)

- Essence: the hero grew up without support and is used to surviving.

- Why it works: respect, empathy and belief in the hero's strength.

- How it leads to poverty: there is no support, so the hero pays for every mistake alone.

- Details: "I do everything myself", no financial "cushion", the fear of asking for help.

- Path of rebuilding: building support through skills, income and community; creating a "chosen family".

## Extra "plausibility boosters"

- Specifics instead of general words: not "problems", but "the collectors call at 08:10 and 19:30".

- Contrasting shots: "yesterday, hot-air balloons in Cappadocia; today, the till and a shift".

- Documents and objects: a badge, a work vest, a patient wristband, a cast, a court ruling, a receipt.

- The hero's speech: not perfect; sometimes tired, with pauses and things left unsaid.

- Micro-wins: "the first day without crutches", "the first client after the break", "the first order for 3000₽".

## Red lines (what to avoid so you don't lose trust)

- A face that is too perfect, or glamour, where there should be fatigue: in a Pyaterochka store, in a hospital, after a shift.

- Success that comes too fast: the path must be "slow and paid for".

- Too many "motivational slogans" without life and details.

- A tone that is too obviously "salesy": the audience comes for the story, not for a course.
