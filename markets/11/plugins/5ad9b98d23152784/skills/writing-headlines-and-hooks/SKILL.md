# Headlines, hooks and USPs

Deliver the text in the quantity and form the user asked for. Keep the given tone, platform,
audience and facts; with an open creative brief, offer genuinely different angles, not the same
words rearranged.

## What a good result looks like

- A headline quickly conveys a promise, intrigue, an event or a concrete benefit that fits the
  task.
- A hook matches the content that follows it and does not have to follow a sales formula.
- A unique selling proposition (USP) names a meaningful difference when the user has given a
  basis for that comparison.
- Add notes to the variants only when they help the user choose.

AIDA, PAS, BAB, a question, a contrast, specifics and a story can all serve as creative
supports. Choose them by the meaning of the request, and depart from them freely for the sake
of a more precise text.

Tie factual credentials, statistics, testimonials and comparisons to the user's data or the
project's materials. Keep a hypothetical or fictional context as such.
