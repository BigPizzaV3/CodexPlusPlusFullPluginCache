# Positioning and messages

Formulate positioning for a product, project, creator, character, series or campaign, at
exactly the scale of the request. Use the confirmed features from the materials; mark potential
insights about the audience and the market as hypotheses.

## Working system

1. Name the audience or the choice situation, if it is given.
2. State the tension or the task that the offer resolves.
3. Pick out a distinct promise and the way it is delivered.
4. Connect the promise to the available reasons to believe.
5. Build the hierarchy: the main idea, supporting messages and acceptable proof.

Match the strength of the promise to the available proof. Make options differ by strategy,
audience or the basis of value. When one result is needed, choose the strongest direction and
justify it. Include specific ads, posts and headlines to the extent needed to apply the
strategy.
