# Document: Viral script patterns (Reels/TikTok/Shorts)

Knowledge-base version. It holds ready formulas, structures and templates that fit any legend (Mila / Sergey / Angelina / Ksyusha / Sasha and so on).

Pattern names in «» are the names shown to users. Every other line in «» (a hook, caption, call to action, challenge or episode title) is a sample of what a Russian-speaking audience reads or hears, with an English gloss; write new ones in the user's language. Pyaterochka is a Russian discount grocery chain.

## What makes a script viral

- Contrast: "before/after" (best of all in the first 1–2 seconds).

- Stakes: the hero may lose, or has already lost, something important (money, name, health, freedom).

- Truth: not perfection, but "fatigue, screw-ups, shame, everyday details".

- Serialization: the story must continue tomorrow (a hook into the path, not a one-off video).

- Specifics: numbers, dates, objects, documents, real places.

- Retention: a micro-turn every 2–3 seconds (a new detail, shot or caption).

## Universal video structure (15–35 seconds)

0–2s: HOOK (a punchy line, a contrast, a screenshot, a face)
2–8s: CONTEXT (what happened, very briefly)
8–18s: DETAILS (2–3 concrete facts that make the story "real")
18–28s: TURN (what the hero decided to do, or what challenge they took on)
28–35s: CLIFFHANGER (what happens next + a soft CTA: «подпишись/следи/пиши в комменты», that is, follow / keep watching / write in the comments)

## Pattern 1 — «5 секунд: жизнь сломалась» (montage contrast)

- When to use: the start of a legend, the first approach, a fast viral piece.

- Format: 4–6 shots of 0.6–1.0 s each, with large captions on top.

- Hook text: «В начале 2025 — любовь. В конце — Пятёрочка.» (Early 2025: love. By the end: Pyaterochka.)

- Shots: (1) a proposal, a gift or a trip → (2) a wedding or a smile → (3) a document or a collectors' call → (4) the checkout or a uniform → (5) a laptop at night or the gym.

Caption template:

- «Сначала он строит идеальную жизнь» (First he builds the perfect life)

- «Потом дарит подарки» (Then he gives gifts)

- «Ты расслабляешься» (You relax)

- «А потом…» (And then…)

- «…и ты считаешь чужие долги» (…and you're counting someone else's debts)

## Pattern 2 — «Двойная жизнь» (by day / in the evening)

- When to use: ideal for Mila (cashier → stylist), and in general for every "ordinary" hero.

- Key: we show that the hero doesn't whine; the hero acts.

- Shots: uniform, checkout, scanner → metro → apartment and laptop → a fitting or a lookbook → going to sleep at 02:10.

- Emotion: recognition ("I live like this too").

Hooks:

- «Большинство живёт двойную жизнь. Я — тоже.» (Most people live a double life. So do I.)

- «Днём я улыбаюсь клиентам. Ночью — собираю себя заново.» (By day I smile at customers. At night I put myself back together.)

- «Мой день: 12 часов смена → 90 минут мечты.» (My day: a 12-hour shift → 90 minutes of the dream.)

## Pattern 3 — «Скрин/звонок как триггер»

- When to use: to give the stakes instantly. A screenshot is proof.

- Variants: a call from an "unknown number", a bank push notification, a court notice, a letter from an investor, a message from the ex.

- Important: 1 screenshot = 1 meaning. Don't overload it.

Structure:

- 0–2s: the screenshot or call + the caption «они снова звонят» (they're calling again)

- 2–8s: the hero's face (tired, no gloss) + 1 line

- 8–20s: 2 facts (what the debt is, why it's on you)

- 20–30s: «что делаю сегодня» (what I'm doing today): a small step

- 30–35s: «завтра покажу…» (tomorrow I'll show…)

## Pattern 4 — «До/после (тело/внешность/здоровье)»

- When to use: weight loss, rehabilitation, recovery after surgery or injury.

- Key: show the "price": pain, limitations, discipline.

- Visual: a timeline by dates + the same camera angle once a week.

Hooks:

- «Меня сломало. Я собираюсь обратно.» (It broke me. I'm putting myself back together.)

- «Я заново учусь ходить/улыбаться/жить.» (I'm learning to walk/smile/live again.)

- «Если ты думаешь, что поздно — посмотри на это.» (If you think it's too late, look at this.)

## Pattern 5 — «Реабилитация как сериал»

- Episode 1: the diagnosis or injury (without detailed medicine, but with emotion and a fact).

- Episode 2: the first day after the surgery or the cast (fear, pain).

- Episode 3: exercises (small steps).

- Episode 4: the first win (without crutches, without pills, without cravings).

- Episode 5: back to work (a job, content, the first order).

## Pattern 6 — «Срыв и честность»

- When to use: burnout, addiction, depression, crisis.

- Essence: not being "perfect", but being real.

- Important: breakdown → takeaway → plan for tomorrow. Don't get stuck in whining.

Hooks:

- «Сегодня я не вывезла.» (Today I couldn't handle it.)

- «Мне стыдно это показывать, но…» (I'm ashamed to show this, but…)

- «Я сорвалась. И вот что сделаю дальше.» (I broke down. And here's what I'll do next.)

## Pattern 7 — «Челлендж (публичный вызов)»

- When to use: launching the path. A challenge makes the series predictable and gripping.

- Examples: «30 дней — вернуть блог» (30 days to bring the blog back), «100 дней — выйти из долгов» (100 days to get out of debt), «60 дней — минус 7 кг» (60 days, minus 7 kg), «30 роликов за 30 дней» (30 videos in 30 days).

- Structure: goal → rule → countdown → a small daily report.

## Pattern 8 — «Работа в кадре: профессия как кино»

- When to use: Pyaterochka and the checkout, a warehouse, a courier, a salon, an office: any "ordinary" job.

- Essence: show "what it's really like" without humiliation, with dignity.

- Retention: ASMR details such as the scanner, the conveyor belt, the receipt, gloves, the uniform, the noise of the store.

Hooks:

- «12 часов смены. Никто не видит эту часть жизни.» (12 hours on shift. Nobody sees this part of life.)

- «Я здесь не навсегда. Я здесь — пока собираюсь.» (I'm not here forever. I'm here while I pull myself together.)

## Pattern 9 — «Возвращаю своё имя» (motivational, but without pathos)

- When to use: for Mila, it is the central slogan.

- Formula: pain → decision → action today → a request to "stay with me".

- Important: no "buy my course". Only the path and support.

## Pattern 10 — «Первый заработок/первый клиент/первое сотрудничество»

- When to use: to show growth and progress.

- Proof: a screenshot, a chat, an order or a delivery note (hide personal data).

- Emotion: "it's not the amount that matters, it's the fact that it's working again".

Hooks:

- «Первый клиент после того, как я всё потеряла.» (The first client after I lost everything.)

- «Это всего 3000₽. Но для меня это…» (It's only 3000₽. But for me it's…)

## Pattern 11 — «Как герой зарабатывает на ИИ» (without "content = money")

- Essence: show not "earned a lot", but "a path appeared".

- Focus: the process, discipline, the first results, reviews.

- Trigger: «если я смог(ла) с нуля — значит, возможно» (if I could do it from zero, it's possible).

Video template:

- Hook: «Я не блогер. Но у меня появился способ вернуться в профессию.» (I'm not a blogger. But I've found a way back into my profession.)

- 2 facts: what I can do or am learning (AI, editing, scripts).

- 1 proof: the first order or the first result.

- Cliffhanger: «завтра покажу, как делала это на телефоне» (tomorrow I'll show how I did it on my phone).

## Pattern 12 — «Скрытая правда про “успешный успех”»

- When to use: to tap into a mass pain and distance yourself from glossy perfection.

- Formula: myth → truth → personal example → conclusion.

- Example myths: "all rich people live easily", "a man will sort it out", "you just have to believe".

## Patterns for a series of 7 posts or videos (a mini-season)

- Day 1: «как я оказалась здесь» (how I ended up here); focus: contrast.

- Day 2: «какая моя реальность сегодня» (what my reality is today); focus: day and night.

- Day 3: «первое действие» (the first action); focus: a 7-day plan.

- Day 4: «первый факап/срыв» (the first screw-up or breakdown); focus: honesty.

- Day 5: «маленькая победа» (a small win); focus: proof.

- Day 6: «боль, которую не видно» (the pain nobody sees); focus: empathy.

- Day 7: «продолжение» (continuation); focus: what happens next.
