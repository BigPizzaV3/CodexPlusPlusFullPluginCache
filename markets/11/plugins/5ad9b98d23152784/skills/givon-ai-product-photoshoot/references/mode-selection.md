# Product Brief Selection

Use this reference only when the user's intended result is incomplete. Resolve the information
required to make composition, reference use and production scope executable.

## Decisions

- **Deliverable:** single product image, connected series, banner, carousel, ad variant,
  product-on-model visual or edit of an existing image.
- **Reference role:** product identity, style, composition, source image to edit, or none.
- **Visual focus:** full product, detail/material, product in use, person-product interaction,
  scene/context or conceptual treatment.
- **Composition:** crop, angle, product placement and space for text when relevant.
- **Variation:** which aspect should change between outputs: scene, crop, angle, hook or styling.

Infer these decisions from the request, attached media and prior context. Ask a concise labeled
question that collects the blocking missing decisions when materially different executable
directions remain equally plausible. An executable request proceeds directly to compatible-model
discovery and schema inspection.

## Fidelity

Treat user-supplied product identity, label, logo placement, color, material and geometry as
stable when the task calls for fidelity. For a creative or hypothetical product, follow the
world defined by the user.
