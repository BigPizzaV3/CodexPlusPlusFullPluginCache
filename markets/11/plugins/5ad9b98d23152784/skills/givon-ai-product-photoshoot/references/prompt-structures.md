# Product Prompt Structures

These are brief components, not model fields or hidden modes. Select a live model first, then
adapt wording with its family guidance and schema.

## Base brief

```text
Deliverable: <requested visual or series>
Product identity: <name/category and relevant visible details>
Reference role: <product identity / style / composition / source edit / none>
Purpose: <where or how the result will be used, when relevant>
Scene and interaction: <environment, props, person, action>
Composition: <crop, angle, placement, negative space>
Light and material: <concrete cues that affect the image>
Exact text: <quoted wording and placement, when requested>
Preserve: <details that stay stable>
Change: <requested transformation or variation>
```

Keep only components that affect the requested result.

## Common creative shapes

- **Clean product image:** product identity, surface/background, angle, crop and material light.
- **Lifestyle or in-use image:** product identity, plausible environment, interaction and a
  composition that keeps the product legible.
- **Product with person:** product identity, requested pose/action, crop and the detail that must
  remain visible.
- **Banner or vertical composition:** requested container, product placement, usable negative
  space and exact text when supplied.
- **Series:** one shared visual system plus a distinct purpose for every image.
- **Conceptual image:** one coherent physical or visual idea with the product still recognizable.
- **Edit/restyle:** exact source role, requested change and relevant preserved details.

Typed controls such as count, aspect, resolution, mask, references and quality belong in the
selected model's schema fields rather than the prose brief.
