# Marketplace card text

Produce exactly the fields the user needs: a title, a description, bullets, search phrases, an
FAQ, rich-content text or the full set. Take the current limits and rules of the specific
marketplace from verified materials when they affect the result.

## Meaning

The order of information follows the task and the format of the field. Buyers are usually
helped by:

- a clear name of the product and what it is for;
- meaningful benefits and usage scenarios;
- concrete specifications, composition or contents of the package;
- answers to important questions and objections.

Use keywords naturally and in proportion to the text. When there is a search keyword core,
keep its priority phrases; without one, write clear human text first of all.

## Facts and creative freedom

Keep the specifications, terms, certificates, reviews and numbers you were given. When the
request implies a hypothetical example or a creative concept, follow that context. For text
meant for publication, keep a missing required fact visibly separate from the finished wording,
so the user can fill it in quickly.

Before delivering, check that the result matches the requested fields, the marketplace's
limits, the tone and the source data.
