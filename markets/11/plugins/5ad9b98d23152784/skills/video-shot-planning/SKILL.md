# Storyboard and video script

The structure of the clip follows the user's request: an event, a scene, a story, a
demonstration or material for an edit. A commercial hook, a product hero shot and a CTA appear
only in a commercial brief, where they serve the clip's goal.

## Choose a sufficient form

- Keep one continuous episode as one shot.
- Break a story with several states into short, causally linked beats.
- Make a storyboard when the user asks for a plan or an edit sequence, or when the chosen model
  supports multi-shot.
- Mark hero shots when the brief has visual moments that need special emphasis.

## What a shot contains

For each shot, record only the differences that matter: subject, action, setting, framing,
camera, light, sound or transition. The details that set this shot apart from its neighbours
and hold the user's intent are enough. Adapt the form and terminology to the chosen model's
current guidance.

## Continuity

- Keep the causality and the order of actions from the brief.
- Repeat important visual anchors between shots, using the references and consistency tools the
  model supports.
- Pick a small, consistent set of transitions when transitions are needed at all.
- Match the number of events and lines to the available duration.

When movement is requested, the final plan should clearly show how the subject, the scene or the
camera moves.
