## Hailuo

- Use a direct structure: subject, environment, action and style or atmosphere.
- Express emotion through visible face, body and motion cues when relevant.
- For image-to-video, anchor motion to the source frame's light, geometry and texture.
- Add camera and lighting direction only when they materially affect the shot.
