# Campaign concepts and hypotheses

Turn the brief into distinct creative directions. A concept sets the idea mechanism and the
system of variants; finished copy, a script and a media plan remain separate results, produced
on request.

## Direction card

For each requested direction, fill in the useful fields:

- audience or situation;
- an observation, or a clearly marked hypothesis;
- the key promise;
- the creative mechanism or repeatable idea;
- examples of storylines, images or hooks;
- suitable formats and channels, if they are given;
- what must stay constant across variants;
- what exactly is being compared and which observable signal to look for;
- which facts or materials are still needed.

Make directions differ by strategy, audience or idea mechanism. Use past results, trends,
competitor data and forecasts only from the available materials. When there is no measurable
baseline, propose a testable hypothesis, an observable signal and a way to compare.

When one request asks for the whole package, add copy, story and visual details to the extent
needed to apply the concept. The main result remains the strategic direction and the test plan.
