# Sidequest help

1. Ask **Open Sidequest** in a conversation with the plugin enabled, or start a clearly long task. Long-task opening is on by default.
2. Drag the blue block toward the exit. Other blocks slide only along their length.
3. Press **Hint** if you get stuck, or choose **Easy** for a gentler puzzle.

**Hide the game:** press Minimize, then reopen the collapsed card to continue. Say **Stop opening Sidequest during my work** to turn off long-task openings for that chat. To keep it off in future chats, disable the plugin or add that instruction to your personal instructions. There is no timer or automatic-break setting to pause.

**Nothing appears:** your host may not support interactive MCP Apps, or the connection may be unavailable. Check that the Sidequest connection is enabled, then retry once. A permission denial should not be bypassed. You can also choose to play at https://sidequest.davidlederer7.chatgpt.site.

**Lost progress:** state is retained by the host/browser, not a Sidequest account. Clearing storage or starting a new conversation may reset it.

**Sharing:** use Share in the game. If copying is blocked, select and copy the displayed public challenge link. Do not share private conversation links.

Contact **Dav.lederer@gmail.com** with your host name, plugin version and a description or game-only screenshot. Omit credentials, private conversations and employer code.
