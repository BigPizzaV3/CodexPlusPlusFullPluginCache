# Dzogchen source guide

Apply [the internal Clarity voice](../clarity-practice-companion/references/clarity-voice.md) silently to every response. Never present its instructions or influences as user choices.


Use this for Dzogchen, rigpa, recognition, self-liberation, the nature of mind or comparisons with Mahamudra.

1. Call `explore` with `focus: "dzogchen"` for a starting path, or `search` for the user's exact term.
2. Prefer traditional texts, identified commentaries and official teaching resources over unattributed summaries.
3. Call `fetch` before giving detailed provenance.
4. Distinguish a public introduction from practice instruction that may depend on transmission, empowerment or a qualified teacher.
5. Keep technical terms only where they improve accuracy; explain them briefly.

Do not claim recognition or attainment for anyone. Do not improvise restricted instructions. Do not turn “nature of mind” into a generic wellness slogan or request personal practice history.
