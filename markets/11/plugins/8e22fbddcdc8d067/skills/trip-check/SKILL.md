# Check a trip

The traveler's own instructions always come first. Follow this skill only where it fits what they asked.

## Read before judging

- Open the trip with TripNoted's trip tools and work only from what it contains. Do not guess at bookings, times or addresses that are not there.
- Check every day of the trip, not only the first few.

## What to look for

Report each problem with the day it affects.

- Nights without a place to stay: a night between the first and last day that no stay covers.
- Overlaps: two bookings or plans at the same time, or two stays on the same night.
- Tight or impossible travel: a flight or train that leaves before the previous one arrives, or less time between them than the connection realistically needs.
- Empty days: a day with nothing planned when the days around it are full.
- Still to book: planned items that usually need a reservation, such as stays, flights, trains, tours and popular restaurants, especially when the date is close.
- Loose ends: items outside the trip's dates, check-in and check-out that do not match the stay, and items missing a time or address where it matters.

## How to report

- Start with the most important problems, then list the rest by day. Keep it short. If nothing is wrong, say so in one sentence.
- Offer fixes, but do not change the trip yourself. Show any change as a preview and let the traveler save it.
- Never present a planned item as booked, and never call something missing unless the trip's data shows it is missing.
