# Plan a trip with TripNoted

The traveler's own instructions always come first. Follow this skill only where it fits what they asked.

## Before drafting

- Find out only what you need: where, roughly how long, who is going and the pace they like. Ask one or two questions at most. If the request is clear enough, draft first and ask afterwards.
- Never invent dates. If the traveler has not given dates, leave them empty ("dates flexible") and set each item's day instead: 1 for the first day, 2 for the next. The card then shows the plan day by day. Keep the items of each day in the order they would happen.
- Never invent bookings. Something you propose is planned, not booked, until the traveler says they booked it.

## Shape the route

- Use one base (one place to stay) per region, for at least two nights. Change bases only when the region changes.
- Allow realistic travel time between places and between bases. Keep long transfers (more than about three hours) to one per day, and plan little else on that day.
- Plan two or three main things per day, grouped by area, with meals near what the traveler is doing. Keep the first and last days light.
- A trip card holds at most 40 items in total, including stays, transfers and ideas. For a long trip, keep the main stop of each day as an item and put smaller extras in that item's notes. If the trip still does not fit, offer to plan it in parts, such as one part per region, and save each part separately.
- Send the itinerary you propose as itinerary items (selected), on their dates when the trip has dates. Use ideas (suggestions) only for optional extras and alternatives beside that plan.

## Places

- Show the trip first, with places named as the traveler would know them. The trip card then looks up their addresses, map pins and photos itself and tells you the place_ref of each place it found, so do not look them up again or show the trip again for them. When you save the trip or show it again, keep those place_refs on their items.
- Never invent addresses, coordinates or photos. If a place cannot be found, keep its name and say so briefly.

## Show the trip

- Show the plan on a TripNoted trip card. The card shows the map, the days and the details, so reply in one or two sentences instead of repeating the itinerary.
- Show any change to a trip as a preview first. Save only when the traveler asks or selects Save.
