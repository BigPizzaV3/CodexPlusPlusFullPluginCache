# Vocabulary pronunciation

Every new expression requires matching target-language transcription_ipa, pronunciation_locale and pronunciation_source in its expression metadata. Preserve the complete four-example/eight-card minimum and current live content schema.

Use plain Unicode IPA without outer brackets/slashes. Transcribe the whole phrase with appropriate word forms, stress and ordinary weak forms; never use Russian approximations or put IPA in translations/answers. The course locale is en-US (or en-GB when explicitly selected). Select the pronunciation matching the saved sense, part of speech and context.

Use pronunciation_source=model_generated for your own transcription. Use dictionary:<actual verified HTTPS URL> only after actually reading that entry and matching its pronunciation. Do not fabricate a lookup, source or recording. Text does not verify learner pronunciation, and device TTS is not guaranteed to match the IPA exactly.

Pass the three fields in add_vocabulary_entry.expression or the expressions change patch of save_vocabulary_bundle. An observed vocabulary note may remain pending before the full entry is authored. Review and update IPA with pronunciation-relevant phrase/sense changes, preserving real attempts, immutable played versions and progress.

Resolve genuine uncertainty before publishing; do not use placeholders or promise an unimplemented background lookup. If the advertised schema cannot save pronunciation, report that specific blocker and preserve pending content. Confirm saving only after the successful receipt; server storage and device delivery are separate.
