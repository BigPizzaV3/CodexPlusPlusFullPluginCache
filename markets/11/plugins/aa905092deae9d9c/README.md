# Crosspublish for ChatGPT

Manage social media posts in your Crosspublish account from ChatGPT.

Connect the Crosspublish MCP server and sign in with your Crosspublish account. Posts and connected accounts use your active social set. Ask ChatGPT to review your queue or drafts, create or revise a post, add media, schedule a post, publish an existing post, or reply to a conversation. Confirm post content and timing before changes that affect publication. Immediate publication requires approval of the exact preview.

Crosspublish sends your prompts to your account through `https://crosspublish.com/api/mcp`. This can include post text, schedule times, selected social accounts, and media already uploaded to Crosspublish. Crosspublish handles this data under its [Privacy Policy](https://crosspublish.com/privacy). This plugin does not store data itself.

To add a file from your device, use the temporary Crosspublish upload link. The file must finish uploading before it can be attached to a post.
